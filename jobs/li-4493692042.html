Description

About League League is one of the fastest-growing technology companies in Canada and the leading healthcare experience platform. Getting healthcare is often the easy part — finishing it is where things fall apart: people book the appointment and skip the follow-up, fill the prescription and stop taking it, get the referral and never make the call. That gap costs health plans and health systems money, and it costs people their health. League closes that gap — identifying what each person needs to do next, clearing what’s in their way, and getting it done, for the 70 million+ people whose care already runs through our platform. Health plans and health systems trust us to do this at scale. Organizations like Manulife, SCAN, Geisinger, and Medibank on the payer side, and Baptist and Shoppers Drug Mart on the provider side.

The Role You will join our Team as a Senior Engineer (Full stack) working across Go and Node.js services to React interfaces for members and clinicians. You will deliver features end to end in a cross-functional team, help decide what gets built, and design with security, data residency and compliance in mind. You will report to the Engineering Manager, UI Services, and work in a remote-first, AI-native way.

What You Will Do

  • Design, build and ship full-stack features end to end: Go and Node.js backend services that meet data residency and regulatory standards, React and TypeScript interfaces for member-facing and clinician-facing products, and application data models in MongoDB and PostgreSQL that follow compliance best practices
  • Integrate features with ML and AI services, including the safety guardrails that keep their outputs appropriate for health and clinical contexts
  • Shape what gets built, not just how: contribute to product decisions with Product and Design, challenge requirements that do not serve users, and measure the impact of what you ship
  • Treat security, data residency and compliance as first-class concerns in every design and release, given the member and clinical data League handles
  • Collaborate and take ownership in a cross-functional team so features reach production smoothly and quickly, and take part in code reviews and help improve engineering standards

What You Bring

  • Production experience building and shipping full-stack software, with strong skills in Go and/or Node.js on the backend and React with TypeScript on the front end
  • Experience modelling and working with application data in MongoDB, and building and running services on a cloud platform such as GCP
  • Experience building software where security, privacy and regulatory requirements, such as HIPAA or PIPEDA, shape the design
  • Hands-on use of AI coding tools (Cursor or Claude Code) in your daily work, with sound judgment in reviewing AI-generated code and an understanding of what AI can and cannot do
  • Strong written and verbal communication, and experience working with Product and Design in cross-functional teams

  • Apply judgment and accountability when using AI by reviewing outputs for accuracy, bias, and quality before use

  • Identify opportunities to improve how work gets done from personal productivity to team-level workflows by leveraging AI effectively
  • Operate with strong data responsibility and security awareness, especially when working with sensitive or regulated information

How This Scales By Level

What We Look For
