***ONLY FOR CANDIDATES BASED IN POLAND***

Software Engineer, Traffic Infrastructure (Mid or Senior), Remote

You'll design and run the traffic routing platforms that carry global-scale traffic for a global traffic infrastructure provider. This is deep networking engineering in Go: Envoy data planes, Go control planes, eBPF and kernel-level tuning, on systems distributed across the world.

The essentials

  • 18,000 to 25,000 PLN per month, depending on level
  • Fully remote
  • Hiring at both Mid and Senior level

What you'll actually do

  • Design, build, deploy and optimise large-scale global traffic routing platforms
  • Develop high-performance distributed traffic infrastructure and networking software
  • Build and manage large-scale, geo-distributed systems
  • Lead complex migrations of legacy network systems and codebases in large environments
  • Develop core network routing tools

What you'll need

Essential

  • Proficiency in Go, with extensive experience designing and building distributed systems
  • Experience building and managing large-scale, geo-distributed networking software
  • Solid hands-on experience with Envoy Proxy (data plane) architecture alongside a Go control plane
  • Advanced knowledge of network protocols from L3 to L7, network infrastructure and observability
  • Strong understanding of the Linux network stack, practical eBPF experience, and hands-on socket and kernel performance tuning
  • At least 5 years of relevant experience for Mid, or 7 years for Senior

Nice to have

  • Practical Rust, specifically for developing and maintaining Envoy WASM filters
  • Working knowledge of C and/or C++ for low-level system integration and debugging

What's in it for you

  • Flexible working hours and a fully remote arrangement
  • Professional growth through internal training sessions and a training budget
  • Structured, hands-on onboarding to get you productive quickly
  • A team of engineers who care about the craft
  • The ability to move between projects over time
