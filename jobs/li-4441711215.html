About Gen-e2TM

While the market is still largely AI-augmenting delivery, we have reinvented the SDLC to be AI First. Leveraging our 16 years expertise in digital transformation, our approach is a game-changer in productivity and quality, with a strong collaboration between IA generative and our best Talents:

  • We now generate 95% of the entire product — code, documentation, infrastructure as code, and even design powered by GitHub Copilot.
  • The quality consistently exceeds the output of our best traditional engineering teams.
  • A product repository houses all product artefacts, giving AI full project context for higher-quality generation.
  • A library of rules and prompts defines coding standards, design principles, and security guidelines, ensuring enterprise-grade quality and scalability.

With Gen-e2TM, we deliver end-to-end products 2–3× faster than traditional approaches, while raising the bar for engineering excellence.

YOUR ROLE

At Palo IT, senior engineers are expected to be AI-Native by default . As a Senior AI-Native Engineer (Software) , you will lead the development of complex features and modules while guiding team members on technical decisions and AI-assisted development practices.

You will partner with Tech Leads, Architects, and cross-functional teams to deliver scalable solutions. You will apply Palo IT's Gen-E2 methodology to accelerate delivery and improve engineering quality.

Key Responsibilities

Software Engineering & Development (Senior Scope)

  • Lead the development of complex features, modules, and components.
  • Design high-performance, scalable solutions using AI-powered approaches.
  • Ensure code quality through testing, code reviews, and AI-assisted analysis.
  • Understand DevOps workflows and implement CI/CD automation pipelines .
  • Establish best practices on code quality and performance within project scope.

AI-Native Engineering (Core Expectation)

  • Use Generative AI coding tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Cursor) as a first-class engineering assistant for code generation, optimisation, and problem-solving.
  • Guide team members on effective use of AI tools and prompt engineering techniques.
  • Establish AI/GenAI best practices within project scope, including output validation and guardrails.
  • Leverage AI tools to troubleshoot and resolve technical issues efficiently.
  • Stay current with emerging AI technologies and development practices.

Collaboration & Technical Guidance

  • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers on technical skills and AI-assisted workflows.
  • Partner with Tech Leads and Architects to guide technical decisions within projects.
  • Collaborate with product managers and designers to deliver high-quality solutions.
  • Contribute to Gen-E2 practices , reusable accelerators, and internal playbooks.
  • Support presales and architecture discussions when required.

Must-Have Experience

  • 5+ years of hands-on software development experience
  • Deep technological expertise and a strong software development background.
  • Experience with solution architecture .
  • Team leadership experience.
  • Experience in developing scalable, reliable, and secure applications .
  • Preferred technologies include ReactJS for frontend development and NodeJS/Java/Python for backend development.
  • Strong understanding of common programming patterns and principles .
  • Demonstrated use of AI tools in day-to-day engineering work (not just theoretical exposure).

AI-Native & Gen-E2 Skills (Required)

  • Hands-on, daily use of AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot or equivalent).
  • Strong understanding of prompt engineering principles for optimal AI tool interaction.
  • Experience validating and controlling GenAI outputs (hallucination handling, code review of AI-generated code).
  • Critical thinking to validate and verify AI-generated code and solutions.

Good to Have

  • Experience with AI/ML services and frameworks .
  • Experience with cloud computing platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP).
  • Native mobile development (iOS, Android) or hybrid mobile development (Flutter, React Native).
  • Certification in Claude AI (Anthropic) is a plus.

What Success Looks Like at Palo IT

  • You lead development of complex features with AI-native practices.
  • You guide and mentor team members on technical skills and AI tools.
