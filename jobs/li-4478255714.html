Description

BestSafari.africa helps travellers find the right safari lodge for them, instead of the most heavily marketed one. A short quiz captures what matters to a traveller (priorities, budget, constraints, preferred region), and our scoring engine ranks a curated set of lodges across Africa to produce a personalised shortlist.

The product is live in production with real users. You'll be joining a small, dynamic, creative and motivated team that includes the founders. We are an early-stage start-up, with a strong team and patented technology, and the engineering decisions made over the next year will shape what the company becomes.

The role

We are hiring a full-stack engineer to take responsibility for the BestSafari.africa platform, end to end, and to help the team decide how, why and what to build next.

This is a development role with a seat at the table. You will be involved in business decisions. You will have responsibility of a live product, and agency to shape how it is built, and a direct line to the people deciding where the company goes. If you want to join the founding team and assist in making this a successful company and product, this is that job.

You inherit a working, documented system and a thorough handover.

What you'll do

•      Build and ship product. Own features across the Django API and the Next.js frontend, from the quiz and results flow to region pages and the blog.

•      Own the scoring and data. Maintain the recommendation engine and run lodge data refreshes, keeping results trustworthy and tested.

•      Run the platform. Deploys, environments (QA, staging, production), monitoring, incident response and cost, on AWS and Vercel.

•      Keep quality high. Reviews, tests, CI, and keeping the codebase and docs in a state the next person can pick up.

•      Work with the business. Turn what the team needs (content, SEO, bookings, partner integrations) into a plan and then into working software.

•      Shape priorities. Say what you think we should and should not build, and why.

What we're looking for

Must have

•     Several years (3-5+) of shipping production software, comfortable across backend and frontend.

•     Strong Python and Django (REST APIs, Postgres, background jobs), and TypeScript with React.

•     Good judgement about trade-offs: you can make a call with incomplete information and explain it to a non-technical teammate.

•     Comfort owning a system in production, including when something breaks.

•     Clear written communication. Enthusiastic and focused. Competent but not pedantic.

Nice to have

•     Next.js App Router experience.

•     AWS and Docker, CI/CD, Celery and Redis.

•     SEO, analytics or content-driven product experience.

Mindset

You like ownership, you ask "why" before "how", and you are proactive and creative, take initiative, rather than waiting for a developer spec.

Safari or travel experience is welcome but not required.
