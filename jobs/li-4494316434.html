This role works with Japanese clients in Japanese. We are looking for engineers with business-level Japanese (JLPT N1 or equivalent).

Turn real operational challenges at Japanese enterprises into production AI applications using React, Next.js, Python, and frontier LLMs.

■ Your Role

• Architect and build full-stack web applications and APIs from scratch based on business challenges uncovered with our consultants

• Embed AI workflows and autonomous agents (Claude / OpenAI / Gemini) directly into client operations

• Join client discovery and specification sessions in Japanese to align on workflows, edge cases, and UI prototypes

• Contribute to stack selection, code quality, and modern AI-assisted engineering practices within a lean team

■ What You'll Build

• AI-powered workflow automation tools and internal knowledge assistants

• Custom-built core systems for order processing, inventory, and customer management

• Data-driven e-commerce and revenue optimization platforms

• Client sectors: Printing, manufacturing, retail, food, and other foundational industries across Japan

■ Tech Stack

• Frontend: React, Next.js, Vite, TypeScript

• Backend: Python (FastAPI / Flask)

• Cloud & Infra: Firebase, Supabase, GCP, Vercel

• AI / LLMs: Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini

■ Team & Career Trajectory

All consultants and engineers work out of our Marunouchi office near Tokyo Station—no offshore handoffs. Our engineering team is international and uses English every day, while client engagements run in Japanese.

You will start as a hands-on product engineer and gradually take on upstream scoping, client leadership, and Project Management responsibilities tailored to your strengths and career goals.

■ Requirements

• 2+ years of professional experience building web applications with React or Next.js

• Experience designing and developing REST APIs, with solid fundamentals in Git and SQL / NoSQL databases

• Business-level Japanese (JLPT N1+ equivalent) with hands-on experience in client communication and Japanese documentation

• Valid work authorization in Japan

• Interest in expanding your scope into Project Management in the future

■ Preferred

• Production TypeScript experience

• Experience building LLM-integrated features or AI agents

• Direct client-facing development experience or team/project lead experience

■ Employment Terms

• Full-time permanent role (3-month probation under identical conditions)

• Marunouchi Office (3-min walk from Tokyo Station) / Hybrid remote & office

• Full flextime (no core hours); ~20 hours average monthly overtime (paid)

• Salary: Commensurate with experience and technical skills

• Weekends (Sat/Sun), Japanese national holidays, New Year holidays, and paid annual leave

• Full social insurance, commuting allowance, choice of work PC, free office drinks, training support, free book lending, side jobs allowed, stock options (under consideration)
