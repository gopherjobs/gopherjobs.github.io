Job Description

Main driver for this service is reach a consistent state of documentation, where we will have sufficiently mapped all devices within approx. 8.000 central offices ("Betriebsstelle"). With this mapping we shall achieve much improved cost efficiency within our energy and space management. This will directly benefit the central overview about technical equipment rack-slots including documentation of technology and energy supplies. This is a critical pillar for an overall energy management strategy and therefore contributing to the corporate objectives for Carbondioxide emission reduction.

It is the main enabler for:

  • Transparent Space Utilization, Energy Consumption and Energy planning.
  • Manage the technical places.
  • Localization of undocumented legacy systems.
  • Python, Angular, Kotlin/Java, Postgres.
  • GitLab, JIRA, Confluence, Tardis.
