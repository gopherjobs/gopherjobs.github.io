About The Role BigHand builds software that legal organisations rely on every day. We are looking for a Full-Stack Engineer to help us design and ship our agentic AI products after our acquisition of Ayora AI. Our goal is to build products that delight legal professionals, where clean user interfaces interact with cutting-edge agentic processes and enriched data, to provide intelligence before the point of decision.

This is a hands-on role for a Full-Stack Software Engineer who wants their work in production rather than as proof of concept. You will build the conventional and agentic AI experiences that showcase the product, contribute to how problems are framed as well as how they are solved, and take real ownership of the quality of what we release.

What You’ll Do

  • Build and ship features end to end, from interface through to backend services and data.
  • Design clean, minimal user experiences with close attention to detail, working alongside design and product.
  • Explore and apply agentic AI and LLM capabilities where they meaningfully improve the product and work safely and efficiently with agentic engineering systems.
  • Contribute to product and technical discussion and help refine requirements rather than simply consuming them.
  • Deliver consistently within a collaborative agile framework, with a track record of predictable, high-quality output.
  • Develop well-constructed solutions using the best tools available, systems thinking and a product-mindset.
  • Apply modern software development lifecycle practices, including code review, testing and continuous delivery.

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong TypeScript and React skills, with competency in at least one of our core backend languages (Python and/or Go) or a highly transferable equivalent.
  • At least three years of commercial work experience.
  • An analytical background, evidenced by a STEM degree in a technical subject. Physical sciences or mathematics are preferred.
  • Interest and practical experience with agentic AI and LLMs, and with the intersection between conventional UI and agentic processes.
  • Experience working safely and efficiently with agentic engineering systems.
  • Experience with design and UX, with a keen eye for detail and for minimalist, clean interfaces.
  • A solid understanding of modern software development lifecycle practices.
  • The ability to navigate typical problems independently, contribute to discussion and help shape technical requirements.
