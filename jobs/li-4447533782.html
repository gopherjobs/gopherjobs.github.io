About this position: We're looking for a curious, motivated Software Development Co-op student to join the Later Influence engineering team. This is an opportunity to work alongside experienced developers building software that powers influencer marketing for some of the world's leading brands and creators. As a member of the team, you'll contribute to real production software, participate in the full software development lifecycle, and gain hands-on experience with modern engineering practices. You'll be supported by experienced mentors while taking ownership of real production work that delivers value to our customers.

If you're passionate about software development, enjoy solving problems, and are eager to learn how high-performing development teams operate, we'd love to hear from you.

What you'll be doing:

  • Contribute to production features across our backend and frontend applications.
  • Collaborate with engineers, product owners, designers, and other cross-functional partners to deliver customer value.
  • Write clean, maintainable, tested, and well-documented code.
  • Participate in code reviews and learn engineering best practices.

  • Learn modern software architecture, cloud technologies, and scalable application design.

  • Take part in planning, retrospectives, and other Agile ceremonies.
  • Continuously learn from teammates while bringing your own ideas, curiosity, and creativity to the team.

Our approach to compensation: We take a market-based & data-driven approach to compensation.
