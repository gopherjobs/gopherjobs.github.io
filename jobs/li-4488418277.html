Mission description

We are looking for an experienced frontend and API developer with good technological understanding and knowledge of web and mobile-based solutions.

You will contribute to the development of modern digital products and services in agile, multidisciplinary product teams. The role involves working across frontend and backend, with a focus on backend development and APIs. At the same time, a good understanding of modern frontend technologies and the development of user-friendly digital solutions is desirable.

You will work on further developing and modernizing existing platform solutions, establishing new services and developing solutions that create better connections between customer data, products and digital channels.

  • Working with technologies such as Java, Kotlin, and modern front-end frameworks
  • Actively participate in agile and multidisciplinary product development teams
  • Contribute to automated testing, building and quality assurance of solutions
  • Collaborate closely with developers, designers and product owners
  • Contribute to improving performance, scalability, and user experience
