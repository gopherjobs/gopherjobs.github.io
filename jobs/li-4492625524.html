Description

Senior Software Developer (Backend) | Remote | USD $85,000 or local equivalent

A global nonprofit is hiring three Senior Software Developers to modernise the infrastructure that helps people find and connect scholarly research. You’ll help break a long-running Java platform into smaller services, build new features as the research metadata model evolves, and keep existing services reliable throughout the transition.

You’ll join a small, distributed team of senior engineers. You’ll help shape requirements, weigh architectural trade-offs, and own features from design through production.

What you’ll do

  • Build and improve backend services across established codebases and new projects.
  • Work with product and domain colleagues to turn open-ended requirements into clear, testable designs.
  • Review code and designs, improve tooling and documentation, and investigate production issues.

What you’ll bring

  • Around five years or more of hands-on software development experience, including maintaining and extending production systems.
  • Strong JVM experience with Java or Kotlin, plus experience designing microservices or distributed systems.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL and/or Elasticsearch or OpenSearch, along with Docker, Git, automated testing and CI/CD.
  • The ability to work independently, communicate clearly across time zones, and bring colleagues into technical decisions.

Experience with Clojure, AWS, performance tuning, or scholarly communications is welcome.

Contract and location

  • Full-time, fixed term, with a maximum term of three years.
  • Salary is USD $85,000 per year or the local equivalent, paid in local currency. Benefits include paid time off, unlimited sick time, and paid parental and medical leave.
  • The role requires at least two hours of overlap with colleagues in European time zones on weekdays and occasional travel, typically once or twice a year.
  • Existing work authorisation in your location is required. Visa sponsorship is unavailable.
