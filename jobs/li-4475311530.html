  • Establish reusable architecture patterns and engineering standards.
  • Collaborate with engineers, architects, and senior stakeholders on implementation.

Essential Skills

  • Strong cloud architecture and hands-on implementation experience.
  • Deep expertise in AWS, Azure, or GCP .
  • Strong cloud networking and Infrastructure as Code skills.
  • Cloud security, IAM, and resilience engineering.
  • Kubernetes/containers and distributed systems.
  • APIs, integrations, messaging, and event-driven architectures.
  • Experience shaping cloud strategy and influencing technical decisions.

Nice to Have

  • Telecom/MVNO or high-volume customer-facing platform experience.
