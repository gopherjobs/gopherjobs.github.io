-------------【 Job Description】-------------

■Recruitment Background

The company operates an industry-leading video platform holding over 50% market share in cloud video recording services. Over the decade since its founding, the organization has scaled rapidly from 3 to approximately 500 employees.

Faced with an impending labor shortage driven by the "80% Society" demographic shift, the company is advancing "Field AI Transformation (AX)" by integrating computer vision and AI, transforming video data into critical social infrastructure.

To support accelerated business expansion, the company restructured its Product Manager (PdM) roles into three clear functions starting this year. This reorganization aims to enable seamless, rapid cross-functional collaboration across Sales, Planning, Engineering, and Customer Success, driving agile product development.

Three Key Product Roles

■PdM (Product Manager) — Focus: "What to Build"

Engages directly with markets and clients to define product purpose and core value propositions, holding primary responsibility for overall product strategy.

■TechPdM (Technical Product Manager) — Focus: "How to Build"

Translates strategic product vision into viable technical roadmaps. Holds accountability for engineering requirements definition and technical feasibility, directly co-creating the product alongside development teams.

■POM (Product Operation Manager) — Focus: "How to Deliver & Scale"

Establishes sustainable operational systems to deliver completed products to market. Leads cross-functional coordination with Sales and CS while designing end-to-end launch workflows, logistics, field installations, and support infrastructure.

This hiring initiative targets the TechPdM role. The candidate will drive cross-functional projects to success from an engineering perspective, collaborating closely with Sales, Product Managers, Designers, and Customer Success. Anchored in deep frontend engineering expertise, the role leads user-facing product experiences through technology to accelerate the growth of the video platform.

■Role Mission

Aligning with overall corporate and product strategies, this role collaborates closely with Product Managers (PdMs) to lead the system development phase. Operating as a pivotal bridge between business goals and engineering implementation, the role balances technical leadership with hands-on development.

Serving as a TechPdM, the engineer will drive frontend development for mission-critical web products daily utilized by end-users, such as enterprise video management systems and MLOps platforms for AI developers. The scope extends beyond frontend implementation into upstream processes—including business requirements definition and functional specification—driving product value enhancement directly centered on user needs.

Offers extensive opportunities across advanced frontend domains, ranging from rich UI/UX experiences utilizing Canvas API and WebGL to architecting internal UI component libraries and low-code infrastructure.
