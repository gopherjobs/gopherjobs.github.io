Description

Job Description Are you excited about working with cutting-edge network technologies? Are you a developer who loves solving complex problems? Join the Network Operations Systems Group Our team develops innovative solutions crucial to deploying and maintaining Akamai's global network. Our network carries over 20% of the world's web traffic. We care about empowering Network Infrastructure, Operations and Engineering teams to work faster, reliably and more efficiently. We believe collaboration and continuous improvement lead to the best solutions.

Partner with the best You will be part of a diverse team that designs, develops and maintains high-reliability systems. As a Software Engineer II, you will have an opportunity to work and collaborate in an international environment with cross-functional teams. These solutions include distributed systems with both web and console interfaces.

As a Software Engineer II, you will be responsible for:

  • Analyzing, designing, and implementing high availability systems software
  • Supporting the full product life cycle - ideation, implementation, operation.
  • Improving the ability of our software systems for testing, deployment, monitoring and maintenance
  • Mentoring less experienced engineers to ensure quality solutions are implemented
  • Evaluating emerging trends and technologies related to network operations and automation

Do What You Love To be successful in this role you will:

  • Have a Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Computer Science, or related field
  • Have at least of 2 years development experience on Linux/ Unix platforms
  • Have at least of 2 years experience with programming in Python, Go and/or shell script
  • Have knowledge of common internet protocols such as TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS and/or TLS/SSL
  • Have experience with git/Stash and Perforce version control systems.
  • Demonstrate exceptional communication (written and verbal) and interpersonal abilities.
