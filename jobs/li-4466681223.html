The role!

As lead engineer, you will sit as part of the senior engineering leadership, reporting directly to the CTO/co-founder. You'll lead an incredibly talented & senior team focused on building the core platform, infrastructure - central to the product and organization.

This is a hands-on role, where you'll be directly contributing to the product & technical roadmap, defining architecture, building & shipping product, whilst empowering a highly autonomous team of engineers who know what they are doing. On the personnel side, you'll have ownership over the roadmap, sprint planning, delivery, optimizing the team & engaging with product & commercial stakeholders to deliver on super exciting, complex engineering challenges and launches.

Requirements:

  • 7+ Years of experience at least as a software engineer
  • 2+ Years as lead engineer, hands-on engineering manager or similar, whether it be a formal title or not
  • E2E ownership: Architecture, system design, development, deployment
  • Strong commercial and product sense: You have a high level overview and reasoning based on the commercial & product impact of what you are your team and building, not stuck in the details of the code
  • Startup pace, ownership, autonomy: You're comfortable in ambiguity, high pace, shipping fast
  • Experience mentoring, coaching, holding 1-1s, performance reviews, roadmap alignment on the personnel side, and you are comfortable
  • Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript, Python, Node.js, Next.js, AWS, IaaC and comfortable working with data stores and warehouses like MongoDB and BigQuery
  • AI enabled: using modern AI tools & LLMs to optimize your product engineering and delivery
