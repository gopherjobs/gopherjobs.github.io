Description

Be the bridge between Norwegian municipalities and a team that builds with AI every day.

As part of the department We are building software that powers the entire construction lifecycle from first sketch to final handover. Our solutions lead the Nordic market, and we're trusted by companies across the region to keep their projects running smoothly.

We are looking for a Product Engineer to join our team in Tønsberg with 5+ years of technical experience in software development and a good understanding of construction products, you can bring a customer and domain perspective to the way we build and test our software. The team is both developing and maintaining the ByggSøk application – a modern, cloud-based solution that simplifies and streamlines the entire building permit process for professionals in the construction industry, from planning to submission and communication with municipalities. We deliver about 50% of all digital building permit applications submitted to local authorities in Norway.

This is a product-focused role in a team that is increasingly built around AI. You will spend more of your time working with AI tools and agents than writing code by hand: turning domain knowledge and customer needs into well-defined solutions, directing AI to build, test and document them, and making sure what we deliver is correct, secure and actually useful for our users. You will be the person who knows ByggSøk and the Norwegian building permit domain inside out, and who speaks with our Norwegian customers, municipalities and stakeholders on the team's behalf.

The team is continuously improving collaboration, automation and integrations between professionals, municipalities and national services – helping shape the future of digital building permit solutions in Norway. The team runs on Scrum and uses Jira, Confluence, GitHub and Claude Code.

Your primary workplace will be in Tønsberg, where you'll collaborate with your immediate team, though we offer flexibility for remote work when appropriate. We place high value on collaboration, and you'll be working alongside talented professionals at all levels, both locally and globally.

Your Role

  • Own the understanding of ByggSøk – its functionality, users, regulations and integrations – and act as the link between customers, municipalities, business and the development team.
  • Shape the product: gather and clarify needs, write and refine requirements and user stories, and help prioritise the backlog.
  • Use AI tools and agents (such as Claude Code and Copilot) to prototype, implement, test, analyse and document solutions, and review and quality-assure what they produce.
  • Continuously find new ways to apply AI to make the team, the product and our customers' workflow more efficient.
  • Participate in Norwegian-language meetings, workshops and customer dialogue, and communicate clearly with the international team in English.
  • Work closely with developers and testers in Scrum, contributing to code and technical decisions where it makes sense.

What You Bring

  • Fluent Norwegian, ideally native level, both written and spoken – this is a must, as you will lead and attend meetings and talk with Norwegian customers and authorities daily
  • Good English communication skills, both written and verbal
  • Available to work on site in Tønsberg
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools in daily work, and a curiosity and drive to explore what they can do
  • 5+ years of technical experience in software development.
  • Strong ability to learn and understand project details and domain knowledge – experience from the construction industry, public sector, building permits (byggesak) or similar regulated domains is a strong advantage
  • Experience from product, solution, business analysis, consulting or software roles, working in an agile team
  • Technical understanding of modern web solutions – a background as a developer or experience with .NET, Angular/TypeScript or SQL is an advantage, but deep coding skills are not required
  • Familiarity with Jira, Confluence, GitHub is an advantage
  • A structured, curious and responsible way of working, with the ability to communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical people
