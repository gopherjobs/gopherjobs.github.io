Overview Viettel Cyber Security (VCS) is a leading cyber security firm, dedicated to protecting digital infrastructures worldwide while conducting in depth research and analysis for security solutions. Leveraging 100% in house development , VCS offers a comprehensive range of cutting-edge cyber security solutions, protecting governments, financial institutions, large corporations and SMEs against sophisticated cyber threats in the digital age.

VCS is internationally recognized for its Champion titles at Pwn2Own 2023 & 2024, consistent Top 5 global rankings, the discovery of400+ zero-day vulnerabilities, and multiple prestigious honors, including Gold – Best Cyber Security Company in Asia. Responsibilities

  • Plan and build the overall product architecture, ensuring alignment with the company’s strategy and business objectives.
  • Participate in researching, analyzing, and making decisions on technology solutions and software architecture suitable for high performance and scalability goals.
  • Guide and control code quality, instructing development engineers to adhere to clean code standards and best practices.
  • Research, evaluate, and apply new technologies (cloud, microservices, security, big data, DevSecOps, etc.) to enhance product quality and optimize system operations.
  • Participate in training and upskill the backend development team.
  • Collaborate closely with Product Owners (PO), Business Analysts (BA), and other stakeholders to ensure technical quality and solution direction align with product goals.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of backend development experience, with at least 1-2 years in a Technical Leader role.
  • Proficient in one of the following languages: Golang/Python/Java.
  • Experience in designing and implementing large-scale systems with high performance requirements (scalable, high-performance, real-time).
  • Strong knowledge of: OOP, Microservices architecture, Design patterns, CI/CD, Cloud (Docker, Kubernetes), Message Queue (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
  • Excellent logical thinking, analytical, and complex problem-solving skills.
  • Good communication, presentation, teamwork, and technical training skills.
  • Proactive, responsible, and highly self-motivated.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Information Security, or related IT fields.

Nice To Have

  • Experience developing products in Information Security, Fintech, or big data processing.
  • Experience handling performance tuning, query optimization, and system scaling challenges.
  • Good knowledge of System Security Architecture is a plus.
