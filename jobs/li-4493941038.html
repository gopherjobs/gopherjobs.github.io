What You’ll Get To Work On We are looking to hire an experienced Senior Platform Developer to join our engineering team. In this role, you will build and scale the core foundation that enables our product teams to ship quickly and reliably. You will take ownership of self-hosting and fine-tuning large distributed databases, managing infrastructure as code, and building internal tooling for teams across the organization. This is a role for someone with high autonomy who excels at self-prioritization and stays calm under pressure during incident response.

Role And Responsibilities

  • Design, deploy, and manage scalable infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (OpenTofu)
  • Own the self-hosting, scaling, and performance tweaking of large distributed database clusters
  • Build and maintain internal tooling and automation to empower engineering teams and streamline developer workflows
  • Conduct deep log investigations and root-cause analysis to troubleshoot complex performance and reliability issues
  • Participate in incident response, ensuring high availability and resilience across our platform
  • Conduct feasibility studies for upgrading and evolving core infrastructure and data systems
  • Leverage modern AI tooling to accelerate operational workflows, debugging, and development

Qualifications

  • Experience in a platform or SRE team
  • Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (OpenTofu / Terraform) and cloud-native environments (Amazon Web Services, Kubernetes)
  • Demonstrated experience managing, self-hosting, and tuning large distributed database clusters (such as PostgreSQL and Elasticsearch)
  • Strong log investigation, observability, and distributed systems debugging skills
  • Experience using modern AI tooling effectively in your engineering workflow
  • High autonomy, strong ability to self-prioritize, and a calm, methodical approach to incident response
  • Strong technical communication skills, with the ability to clearly explain architectural decisions and tradeoffs
  • Ability to mentor and support less experienced developers within your area of expertise

Good To Have

  • Experience developing backend APIs (Python 3, FastAPI, TypeScript) and internal developer tooling
  • Experience working with big data pipelines and message brokers (RabbitMQ)
  • Experience in cybersecurity
