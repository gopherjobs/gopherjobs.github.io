Description

Who You Will Work With As a Software Engineer within our Marketplace Technology EMEA team you will work within an engineering team partnering across technology and business functions.

Collaborating with engineers, architects, product leaders, business stakeholders, and external partners to deliver solutions that span multiple systems, technologies, and business processes.

You will join a team of engineers who embrace collaboration, continuous learning, and engineering excellence. We value curiosity, ownership, and a pragmatic approach to delivering business outcomes through technology. You will be reporting into engineering leadership, who will guide you to grow and build your career path.

Who We Are Looking For We are particularly interested in engineers who have experience working across multiple systems and technologies, designing end-to-end solutions, and leveraging modern AI capabilities both in software development practices and in business-facing solutions.

Qualifications Success in this role requires adaptability, strong engineering fundamentals, and the ability to quickly understand unfamiliar systems, processes, and business domains. Key Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Experience delivering production-grade software solutions across multiple systems and platforms
  • Strong understanding of end-to-end system design and business process flows
  • Experience contributing to solutions that span multiple applications, platforms, or business processes.
  • Experience with API-first architecture and service-oriented design principles
  • Understanding of cloud-native development, DevOps, CI/CD, and infrastructure automation
  • Uses AI-assisted engineering tools as a core part of the software development lifecycle to accelerate delivery, improve code quality, enhance decision-making, and drive engineering productivity.
  • Demonstrated ability to solve complex technical problems and work effectively in ambiguous environments .
  • Possesses a founder's mindset, proactively identifying opportunities, shaping solutions, and driving outcomes rather than operating as an order taker. Demonstrates ownership, curiosity, and the ability to influence direction across both business and technology stakeholders.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.

What You Will Work On As a Software Engineer on this team, you will design, build, and maintain solutions that improve how the business operates and how technology systems interact. You will work on a diverse portfolio of initiatives which may include:

  • Building integrations between enterprise platforms, SaaS applications, and internally developed systems
  • Designing and implementing automated business processes and workflows
  • Developing APIs, services, and event-driven solutions that enable data and process connectivity
  • Identifying opportunities to reduce manual effort through engineering and automation
  • Applying AI technologies to enhance business processes, decision support, and operational efficiency
  • Evaluating emerging technologies and recommending solutions to address evolving business needs
  • Improving reliability, scalability, observability, and maintainability across systems

Relocation support is not provided for this role. Applications will be accepted until 3rd October 2026. We encourage external candidates to apply as soon as possible, as the posting may close earlier if we receive a sufficient number of applications.
