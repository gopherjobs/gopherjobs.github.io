Conversational AI Engineer

We are looking for a Conversational AI Engineer to design, build, and deploy production-grade AI systems powered by Large Language Models (LLMs). The role combines conversational AI, agentic systems, backend engineering, model development, and speech technologies, with a strong focus on Arabic and regional dialects.

Responsibilities

* Design and develop conversational AI and agentic systems using frameworks such as LangGraph.

* Build LLM-powered applications using LLM APIs, prompt engineering, tool calling, structured outputs, RAG, and memory.

* Develop and integrate AI agents with backend services, databases, APIs, and enterprise systems.

* Design and implement scalable backend services and APIs using Python and FastAPI.

* Apply solid backend engineering concepts, including API design, authentication and authorization, error handling, validation, logging, caching, asynchronous processing, and service integration.

* Work with databases such as MongoDB and PostgreSQL for application data, conversation state, and AI-related data.

* Use Redis for caching, session/state management, queues, and other low-latency use cases.

* Work on LLM training and fine-tuning, including data preparation, evaluation, optimization, and experimentation.

* Deploy and operate AI models using model serving and inference technologies.

* Design scalable AI services with a focus on performance, reliability, scalability, and latency.

* Integrate Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS)technologies into conversational AI solutions.

* Evaluate LLMs and conversational systems using quality, accuracy, latency, and task-completion metrics.

* Collaborate with ML engineers, backend engineers, data scientists, and product teams to deliver end-to-end AI solutions.

AI & LLMs

* Strong experience in AI/ML and Generative AI.

* Hands-on experience with LLMs and LLM APIs.

* Experience building agentic applications using LangGrap or similar frameworks.

* Experience with LLM training, fine-tuning, and model evaluation.

* Good understanding of RAG, embeddings, vector search, tool calling, structured outputs, and conversational memory.

* Experience with model serving, inference, and production deployment.

Speech & Conversational AI

* Experience integrating Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems.

Arabic Language & Domain Knowledge

Strong knowledge of Arabic and Arabic dialects is required.

The candidate should understand the differences between Modern Standard Arabic and regional dialects, including dialect-specific vocabulary, expressions, pronunciation, code-switching, and conversational patterns.

Experience with Egyptian Arabic, Gulf Arabic, Levantine Arabic, or other Arabic dialects is highly valued.
