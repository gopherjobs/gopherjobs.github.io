Description

Job Description Ciklum is looking for a Senior Playful Engineer to join our team full-time in the Czech Republic.

We are a custom product engineering company that supports both multinational organizations and scaling startups to solve their most complex business challenges. With a global team of over 4,000 highly skilled developers, consultants, analysts and product owners, we engineer technology that redefines industries and shapes the way people live.

About The Role As a Senior Playful Engineer, become a part of a cross-functional development team engineering experiences of tomorrow. You will join a long-term strategic partnership with a leading Danish company — a globally loved brand creating products and experiences that inspire generations of children and families worldwide. Our team is integrating a proprietary programming language that will power a unified platform across the client's entire product ecosystem. The product is already on the market and actively used by end-users. You will be developing new releases, improving the architecture, enhancing features, and ensuring that everything works without a hitch — because when kids play, there is no room for bugs. You will work within a large cross-functional team of around 25 Ciklum engineers alongside 50+ client-side designers, project managers, testers, and architects — collaborating closely to deliver a seamless and joyful user experience.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop software features for a performance-critical, user-facing product
  • Rearchitect and create new product versions — improving existing functionality and introducing new capabilities
  • Integrate tech stacks across multiple client products onto a unified platform
  • Support and enhance the game flow to ensure a seamless, enjoyable, and bug-free user experience
  • Implement new features and game mechanics with a strong eye for quality and user interaction
  • Develop supporting tools, automation, and toolchains (linters, debuggers, build systems)
  • Analyze system constraints and optimize implementations for performance, memory, and concurrency
  • Collaborate closely with designers to translate product requirements into robust technical solutions
  • Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and mentor mid-level engineers
  • Troubleshoot and improve existing functionality across a large, complex codebase

Requirements

  • Strong Python skills — production-quality code, not just scripting
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills — you think before you code
  • Experience working within large, complex codebases
  • A genuine feel for user experience quality — you notice when something is off
  • Strong debugging skills and a zero-tolerance mindset for bugs
  • Solid knowledge of Git version control
  • Strong self-management — able to drive tasks from design to delivery independently
  • Proficient in verbal and written English communication
  • Passion for building high-quality, clean code

Desirable

  • Experience with C, C++, or Rust
  • Familiarity with compiler design, ASTs, type systems, or optimization techniques
  • Knowledge of embedded systems or low-level memory management
  • Experience with LLVM, GCC, or other compiler frameworks
  • Experience with Docker
  • Mobile platform experience (touch input, camera, GPS, Bluetooth, push notifications)
