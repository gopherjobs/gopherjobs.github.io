Description

We are seeking a motivated and collaborative individual to join our team as a Research Associate. This role offers an exciting opportunity to build a client-facing MVP prototype for the GreenAVM project. The post holder will work with the PI, Dr Yishuang Xu, in the School of Environment, Education and Development, and Co-I, Dr Khashayar Kazemzadeh, to develop a working prototype that wraps the existing GreenAVM model into a client-facing web application.

You will be responsible for:

  • Develop a web-based application (front-end and back-end) wrapping the existing AutoGluon valuation model for client-facing demonstrations
  • Integrate public data feeds (Land Registry, EPC Register) into the application to enable live or near-live property valuations
  • Implement SHAP-based explainability visualisations so that valuation outputs are interpretable by non-technical users
  • Deploy the prototype to a cloud environment (e.g., AWS, Azure) and ensure it is accessible for stakeholder demonstrations
  • Document the technical architecture, codebase, data pipelines, and deployment configuration, and provide a structured technical handover to support future development

We welcome candidates who bring diverse perspectives, experiences, and approaches to their work.
