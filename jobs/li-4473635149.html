About the Opportunity

We are seeking experienced Senior Software Engineers to support a project focused on evaluating and improving next-generation AI systems. You’ll apply your expertise in software engineering to assess AI-generated code and solve challenging real-world engineering problems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Evaluate AI-generated code for correctness, quality, and design.
  • Create challenging coding problems, reference solutions, and test cases .
  • Review AI reasoning related to architecture, debugging, and system design .
  • Provide detailed technical feedback to improve model performance.

Required Qualifications

  • At least 1 year of software engineering experience at a top technology company, including some experience within the past 7 years.
  • Strong knowledge of system design, software architecture, algorithms, and data structures .
  • Strong code review and debugging skills.
  • Ability to clearly explain technical decisions in writing.
  • Based in an English-speaking country .

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience evaluating complex software systems or AI-generated code.
  • Strong background in software architecture and system design .
  • Experience creating technical problems, solutions, and testing strategies.
