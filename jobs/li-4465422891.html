About XHawk.ai

XHawk is building the infrastructure for an AI agent economy

Software is shifting from tools to workers. For the past 40 years, enterprises bought software to help people work. Over the next decade, they’ll increasingly buy outcomes delivered by AI workers. We’re creating the infrastructure businesses need to discover, deploy, manage, and pay AI workers securely at enterprise scale.
