What You'll Do 🏡💻 You'll work across the AI lifecycle, from exploring new models and techniques through prototyping and evaluation to building and running production AI services, working closely with our agentic engineering approach.

  • Build and integrate AI and machine learning services into production systems
  • Develop agentic workflows that combine models, tools, data and business logic
  • Evaluate LLMs, SLMs and specialised machine learning models for specific use cases
  • Design retrieval, context and knowledge systems for AI applications
  • Review and validate code generated through AI-assisted and agentic engineering workflows
  • Work with software engineers, product teams and AI specialists to take ideas from concept to production
  • Monitor and improve AI systems once they are live in production

The must haves 💪

  • Strong Python skills (or equivalent experience in a language like C) and modern software engineering practices, including building APIs and services
  • A track record of shipping AI or software products into production, not just research or prototyping
  • Hands-on experience with LLMs or generative AI systems
  • Experience with AI agents, tool calling or orchestration frameworks, or with AI coding assistants/coding agents
  • Strong engineering judgement, comfortable reviewing, testing and challenging both human-written and AI-generated code: most of our code is written via AI coding agents such as Claude, so this sits at the centre of the role rather than at the edges
  • Experience running AI or ML systems in production, including cloud environments and CI/CD

The nice to haves ➕➕➕

  • Experience with RAG, embeddings, vector search or knowledge systems
  • Background in machine learning or applied data science
  • Model evaluation and benchmarking experience
  • Practical AI use cases in areas like finance, analytics or speech recognition (ASR)
  • Experience in customer experience or contact centre technology
  • Comfortable explaining technical ideas to both engineers and non-engineers

The location This role will be based in our Aarhus or Copenhagen office, with an expectation of 2-3 days/week onsite. English is our working language, so Danish isn’t required.
