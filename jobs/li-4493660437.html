Position: Home Gateway Engineering & Testing Engineer

Location: Santiago, Chile

About the role

We are looking for a Home Gateway Engineering & Testing Engineer to support the integration, validation and troubleshooting of Home Gateway (HG) and Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) platforms for a leading telecommunications service provider in Chile.

What you will do

  • Execute end-to-end testing and validation of new features, firmware releases and customer solutions in lab and production-like environments
  • Create and maintain test cases, and run functional, stress and performance validation
  • Investigate complex issues involving multiple components and services, supporting root cause analysis through log inspection, packet capture and network traffic analysis
  • Identify, report and follow up on defects through structured reporting
  • Work with development teams, vendors and customers to validate requirements and support solution readiness

What we are looking for

  • Experience in engineering, validation, testing or technical support within telecommunications, networking or connected-device environments
  • Hands-on experience troubleshooting and investigating issues in lab, pre-production or production-like environments
  • Familiarity with test execution, defect management and technical analysis
  • Ability to understand technical requirements and validate them through structured testing
  • Good working knowledge of Linux command line, SSH/Telnet and tools such as Wireshark or equivalent packet capture tools
  • Experience with Jira or similar defect management and test reporting tools
  • Strong analytical mindset, attention to detail and a proactive approach to problem solving
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work in cross-functional teams
  • Professional English and fluent Spanish

Technical areas you may work with

Exposure to some of the following is expected: Wi-Fi technologies, LAN/WAN connectivity, ACS and remote device management (TR-069), Internet services (VPN, DMZ, port forwarding, port mapping), QoS, voice services, Fixed Wireless Access (FWA), mesh and extender solutions, firmware upgrade and provisioning, device onboarding, factory reset and recovery procedures, and WebUI and user experience.

Nice to have

  • Previous experience with Home Gateway or broadband CPE platforms
  • ACS-based provisioning and device management
  • Broadband access technologies
  • API testing
  • Scripting or automation experience and automated validation frameworks

Previous Home Gateway or CPE experience is a strong plus, but we will also consider candidates from adjacent networking, telecommunications or connected-device domains who are keen to learn.
