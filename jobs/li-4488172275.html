Principal Engineer – Security Unicorn | London (Hybrid, 1–2 Days a Week) | Up to £175,000 + 15% Bonus

Most engineers are handed an architecture. As Principal Engineer here, you’ll decide what it should be.

This security unicorn has grown to 600 people and runs a platform that handles 15,000 transactions per second at around 100ms latency. Customers rely on it to protect them in real time, so there’s no room for downtime, shortcuts or slow responses. As the business keeps scaling, it needs a Principal Engineer to make sure the technology scales ahead of it.

Why this role stands out

  • You’ll set the direction. You’ll own the long-term technical vision for the entire platform, not just one part of it.
  • Your work protects people. Every system you design helps keep customers safe as threats happen.
  • The scale is real. You’ll be solving high-throughput, low-latency problems most engineers never get near.
  • You’ll have influence at the top. Product and executive leadership will rely on you as a trusted technical voice.

What you’ll take on

  • Defining the architecture and multi-year technical roadmap for the whole platform
  • Designing distributed systems that stay fast, secure and resilient as traffic grows
  • Leading the company’s toughest, highest-stakes engineering challenges yourself
  • Setting the engineering principles and standards every team works to
  • Guiding leadership through major technical decisions and trade-offs
  • Developing Staff and Senior Engineers into the next generation of technical leaders
  • Staying hands-on in design, prototyping and code where it makes the biggest difference

What you’ll bring

  • Extensive experience building and scaling large backend and distributed systems
  • Experience as a Principal, Staff or equivalent engineer, leading across multiple teams
  • Deep expertise in Go or another modern backend language
  • A proven record with high-throughput, low-latency, mission-critical systems
  • A security-first mindset and experience building systems where trust is everything
  • Exceptional system design skills and architectural judgement
  • The presence to influence senior stakeholders and bring engineers with you

The package

  • Base salary up to £175,000
  • 15% bonus
  • Hybrid working, with 1–2 days a week in the London office
  • Permanent right to work in the UK required, as visa sponsorship isn’t available

If you want to define how a fast-growing security platform is built and see your decisions protect customers at scale, get in touch for a confidential conversation.
