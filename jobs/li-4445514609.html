  • Backend development using TypeScript and Node.js.

  • Performance monitoring, troubleshooting, and system optimisation.
  • Secure, maintainable, and testable software development.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable, secure, and high-performance backend services and APIs that form the foundation of our iGaming SaaS platform.
  • Lead the design and implementation of distributed systems, microservices, and event-driven architectures to support platform growth and increasing transaction volumes.
  • Drive architectural decisions and contribute to the long-term technical roadmap, ensuring solutions align with business objectives and engineering best practices.
  • Enhance and evolve the functionality, performance, scalability, and reliability of existing platform components and services.
  • Design, implement, and maintain integrations with third-party partners, gaming providers, payment services, and external APIs.
  • Manage the development and continuous improvement of our Remote Gaming Server (RGS), ensuring compliance, security, performance, and operational excellence.
  • Identify system bottlenecks, performance issues, and reliability risks, implementing proactive solutions and optimisation strategies.
  • Conduct thorough code reviews and champion engineering excellence through coding standards, testing practices, and architectural governance.
  • Mentor and support Intermediate and Junior Developers through technical coaching, knowledge sharing, and career development guidance.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional Teams to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions and deliver high-quality software.
  • Work closely with DevOps teams to improve CI/CD pipelines, observability, infrastructure automation, and deployment processes.
  • Promote a culture of continuous improvement by evaluating emerging technologies, frameworks, and industry best practices.
  • Ensure systems are secure, maintainable, well-documented, and aligned with regulatory and operational requirements within the iGaming industry.
  • Participate in incident management, root cause analysis, and continuous reliability improvements for production systems.

  • A relevant Degree/Diploma or studying towards one (Advantageous).
  • At least 5 years of development experience in a commercial environment.
  • Strong proficiency in TypeScript and modern backend development practices.
  • Extensive experience designing and building RESTful APIs and service-oriented architectures.
  • Strong experience with databases (especially MySQL/Postgres’s) including performance tuning and optimisation.
  • Good working knowledge of caching and messaging systems such as Redis, RabbitMQ, and AMQP protocols.
  • Excellent knowledge of distributed systems, microservices architecture, event-driven design, and message-oriented middleware.
  • Experience designing and operating high-availability, fault-tolerant systems in production environments.
  • Strong understanding of software design principles, architectural patterns, and engineering best practices.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing strategies, and deployment automation.
  • Excellent troubleshooting, debugging, and performance optimisation skills.
  • Experience within the iGaming, FinTech, SaaS, or high-transaction platforms. (Advantageous).
  • Experience with containerisation and orchestration technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes. (Advantageous).
  • Experience with infrastructure automation and configuration management tools such as SaltStack, Terraform, Ansible, or equivalent. (Advantageous).
  • Understanding of cloud-native architectures and cloud platforms.
  • Experience with observability tools, monitoring platforms, and distributed tracing solutions.
  • Knowledge of security best practices for distributed systems and regulated environments.
