About The Role FADA is seeking a highly skilled, forward-thinking DevOps Engineer (Cloud, AI Infrastructure, Telemetry & Geospatial Systems) to architect, automate, and optimize mission-critical infrastructure powering advanced geospatial intelligence (GEOINT), Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR), and Earth Observation (EO) platforms.

This role plays a pivotal part in delivering secure, scalable, and resilient multi-cloud and hybrid environments across Azure and AWS for high-throughput imagery ingestion, raster/SAR/optical processing, AI/ML pipelines, and mission analytics. In alignment with industry standards, the role integrates AI-assisted operations and autonomous DevOps/SRE agents, InfluxDB time-series telemetry, and end-to-end observability to accelerate mission capability delivery while maintaining strict security, reliability, and governance.

Responsibilities

  • Multi-Cloud & Hybrid Architecture (Azure & AWS): Architect, deploy, and govern cloud-native and hybrid infrastructure across Azure and AWS for mission-critical GEOINT and imagery workflows—covering compute, object/block storage, networking, IAM/Entra ID, landing zones, and secure on-premise or air-gapped connectivity.
  • AI Infrastructure & DevOps/SRE Agents: Deploy, govern, and integrate AI tooling and autonomous/semi-autonomous DevOps and SRE agents (such as incident investigation, IaC/PR review, drift/compliance checking, and deployment risk workflows) with strict human-in-the-loop approvals, least-privilege guardrails, and full auditability.
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC) & DevOps: Implement and maintain modular IaC (Terraform, Bicep, Ansible) and GitOps workflows (ArgoCD or Flux) to automate provisioning of geospatial data pipelines, GPU compute clusters, AI inference/training environments, and distributed mission services.
  • Time-Series & Telemetry Engineering (InfluxDB & OTel): Design, scale, and maintain high-throughput telemetry and time-series pipelines using InfluxDB (and collectors such as Telegraf and OpenTelemetry) to capture infrastructure metrics, pipeline throughput, sensor/ground/platform telemetry, and real-time operational events.
  • End-to-End Observability & AIOps: Build and optimize observability stacks (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, InfluxDB, ELK/Loki, Azure Monitor, AWS CloudWatch) combining metrics, logs, traces, and anomaly detection to ensure proactive reliability across GEOINT workloads and mission platforms.
  • CI/CD & Python Automation: Architect resilient CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI) and build Python-based automation tools, CLI utilities, and API integrations for data ingestion, STAC/metadata workflows, telemetry automation, and automated verification.
  • Container & GPU Orchestration: Deploy and manage containerized workloads on Docker and Kubernetes (AKS and EKS), including GPU-accelerated node pools for computer vision/AI models, raster processing, and geospatial web services.
  • Performance & Scalability for EO/SAR Workloads: Tune storage, networking, caching, and distributed compute to handle bursty EO/SAR ingestion, large raster/COG datasets, and real-time analytics under strict SLAs.
  • Zero Trust Security & Mission Governance: Enforce Zero Trust architecture, network segmentation (VNet/VPC, firewalls, VPN/ExpressRoute/Direct Connect, service mesh), encryption at rest and in transit, secrets management, and compliance controls suited to sensitive defense and space/geospatial environments.
  • Incident Response, FinOps & Documentation: Lead root-cause analysis and incident response across dev, test, and operational environments; drive cloud/GPU FinOps optimization; and maintain architecture decision records (ADRs), playbooks, and operational runbooks.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with GEOINT engineers, AI/ML scientists, data engineers, and security teams to align infrastructure performance, telemetry, and automation with mission needs.

Basic Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Cloud/Systems Engineering, GIS/Geospatial Technology, or a related technical field.
  • Experience: 8–10+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, SRE, or Cloud Infrastructure roles.
  • Cloud Expertise (Azure & AWS): Deep practical experience with both Azure and AWS, including multi-account/multi-subscription landing zones, IAM/RBAC, networking, storage, and managed Kubernetes (AKS and EKS).
  • AI & DevOps Agents: Practical experience integrating AI/LLM-powered developer and operations agents, agentic tool/MCP workflows, or AIOps into CI/CD, IaC validation, or incident triage—including security, sandboxing, and governance controls.
  • Telemetry & InfluxDB: Hands-on experience with InfluxDB (time-series schema design, retention/downsampling policies, ingestion pipelines) alongside OpenTelemetry and industry-standard metrics, logs, and tracing stacks.
  • IaC & Automation: Strong proficiency in Python for operational tooling and APIs, paired with proven experience in Terraform (preferred), Bicep, or Ansible and Git-based workflows.
  • Containers & Orchestration: Solid mastery of Kubernetes architecture, workload autoscaling, ingress/egress, and GPU scheduling.
  • Geospatial Foundations: Hands-on experience supporting geospatial or imagery workflows (e.g., PostGIS, GeoTIFF/COG, WMTS/vector tiles, STAC catalogs, or raster processing pipelines).
  • Networking & Security: Strong command of DNS, routing, VPNs, load balancers, firewalls, Zero Trust principles, and enterprise security/compliance frameworks.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience building InfluxDB pipelines for high-frequency telemetry, hardware/satellite/ground-station metrics, or real-time operational analytics.
  • Experience deploying and governing AI agents (e.g., automated PR/IaC reviewers, SRE investigation agents, policy/compliance agents) with deterministic guardrails and audit trails.
  • Hands-on experience with Azure ML / AWS SageMaker, GPU clusters, and air-gapped, sovereign, or hybrid/on-premise deployments.
  • Experience integrating DevOps pipelines with EO/SAR processing engines, orthorectification/tiling/mosaicking workflows, or cloud EO platforms (e.g., SentinelHub, Google Earth Engine, OpenEO).
  • Experience with GitOps (ArgoCD or Flux), service mesh (e.g., Istio/Linkerd), and OpenTelemetry instrumentation across microservices and batch jobs.
  • Advanced Python for parallel geospatial/raster processing, metadata extraction, and automated infrastructure testing.
  • Background in defense, aerospace/space systems, ISR ecosystems, or high-assurance operational enclaves.
