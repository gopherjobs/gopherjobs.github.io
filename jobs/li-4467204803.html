About The Role You'll join a small, product-focused engineering team building the core platform that enables thousands of people to work with AI every day. The role spans the full stack, from polished React frontends to high-throughput backend systems that synchronize terabytes of client data and run millions of LLM calls per month.

What You'll Do

  • Build and ship high-quality full-stack features using TypeScript and React, with a strong focus on user experience.
  • Design and implement robust backend systems capable of handling massive data volumes and millions of API requests.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of features from concept and deployment through to ongoing operation, ensuring reliability and security.
  • Debug production incidents, identify root causes, and implement lasting solutions.
  • Synchronize and index large client datasets while managing fine-grained permissions.
  • Operate and optimize systems that process millions of LLM calls per month.
  • Architect and scale environments that spawn thousands of secure code sandboxes daily.
  • Build base infrastructure to help the integrations team onboard new systems quickly.
  • Mentor junior engineers and raise the bar through thorough, constructive code reviews.

What We're Looking For

  • 3 or more years of professional software engineering experience, ideally in the 3 to 5 year range.
  • Proven production experience with React, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL.
  • Strong backend skills at scale, including Python, tRPC, or comparable technologies.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and high-throughput distributed services.
  • Demonstrated full-stack application development, including clearly owned frontend projects.
  • Experience building scalable, secure, and reliable backend services handling high data or API volumes.
  • Strong product ownership with attention to UX and long-term product quality.
  • Experience integrating AI services, or a genuine interest in AI-integrated products.
  • Currently based in Munich, Germany.

Compensation & Benefits Salary range: 60,000 to 80,000 EUR annually. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

Location

Hybrid, based in Munich, Bavaria, Germany. Candidates must already live in Munich.
