Description

VinSmartFuture (formerly VinIT) is a technology subsidiary of Vingroup , leading the development of a comprehensive SuperApp and digital ecosystem that serves tens of millions of users across key sectors: payments, healthcare, education, mobility, commerce, and entertainment.

Our mission is to build the "Digital Core" - the foundational technology platform that powers Vingroup's unified digital operations, enabling smart management, data-driven decision-making, and scalable infrastructure on a national level.

Work Location:

  • Hanoi: TechnoPark Tower, Vinhomes Ocean Park, Hanoi
  • HCM: Vincom Dong Khoi, HCM

Job description:

  • Design, develop, and maintain high-performance backend systems serving large-scale products and platforms.
  • Build scalable Microservices, APIs, and distributed systems with high availability, reliability, and security standards.
  • Design system architecture, data models, and service integrations across multiple domains and platforms.
  • Develop and optimize real-time processing systems, event-driven architectures, and high-concurrency services.
  • Ensure system performance, scalability, observability, and operational excellence.
  • Collaborate closely with Product Owners, Mobile Engineers, Data Engineers, AI Engineers, and cross-functional teams to deliver product features.
  • Conduct code reviews, architecture reviews, and technical discussions to maintain engineering quality.
  • Troubleshoot production issues, perform root cause analysis, and implement long-term improvements.
  • Contribute to engineering best practices, technical standards, and platform architecture decisions.
  • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers and support team technical growth.

Requirement:

  • Minimum 8 years of hands-on experience developing backend systems with Golang .
  • Strong proficiency in Golang, including Goroutines, Channels, Context, Concurrency Patterns, and Performance Optimization.
  • Proven experience designing and developing large-scale Microservices architectures.
  • Strong experience with RESTful APIs and gRPC services.
  • Solid understanding of Distributed Systems and Event-Driven Architecture.
  • Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, MySQL, or equivalent relational databases.
  • Strong database design skills and experience optimizing complex SQL queries.
  • Experience with Redis and caching strategies.
  • Experience working with Message Brokers such as Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ, or similar technologies.
  • Experience deploying and operating services on AWS, GCP, or Azure.
  • Proficiency with Docker, CI/CD pipelines, and modern software development practices.
  • Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and ownership mindset.

  • Previous experience as a Technical Lead or Engineering Lead.
  • Experience in Mapping, Navigation, Mobility, Logistics, Fintech, IoT, AI Platforms, or other large-scale digital products.
  • Experience mentoring engineers and leading technical initiatives.
