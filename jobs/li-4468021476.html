💼 About the Role

As a

Platform / Infrastructure Engineer

, you will play a key role in this transformation. You will help us build the abstractions, tooling, and infrastructure foundations that allow our engineering teams to deploy and operate Otera reliably across multiple cloud environments.

We currently run primarily on Google Cloud Platform in Europe and Microsoft Azure in the UAE, while actively expanding our AWS footprint in the US. A major focus of the role will be helping us build the right abstractions so that our infrastructure becomes genuinely multi-cloud rather than simply replicated across providers.

You will work across cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes, networking, security, Infrastructure as Code, observability, internal tooling, and deployment platforms, while partnering closely with engineering and security teams.

🔥 Key Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure across GCP, AWS, and Azure.
  • Help evolve our existing infrastructure function into a mature Platform Engineering capability.
  • Build reusable infrastructure abstractions that support a truly multi-cloud architecture.
  • Maintain and evolve our Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and Helm.
  • Design, operate, and optimize managed Kubernetes environments, including GKE, AKS, and EKS.
  • Support containerized workloads and deployment workflows using Docker.
  • Design and maintain secure and reliable networking across cloud environments.
  • Improve connectivity between applications, cloud providers, internal services, and external systems.
  • Partner with our security function to continuously strengthen infrastructure security and cloud security practices.
  • Collaborate with development teams to improve CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, security scanning, deployment workflows, and developer experience.
  • Build and maintain internal platform tooling and support applications used across the organization.
  • Improve infrastructure observability, reliability, scalability, and performance through monitoring, logging, alerting, and automation.
  • Identify infrastructure bottlenecks and proactively recommend and implement improvements.
  • Support and improve our AWS deployment in the US while helping standardize infrastructure patterns across providers.
  • Support database and messaging infrastructure including PostgreSQL, Redis, and NATS JetStream.
  • Contribute to future infrastructure initiatives, including additional cloud environments and potentially on-premises or customer-hosted deployments.
