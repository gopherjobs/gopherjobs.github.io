What you will do

We’re looking for someone who enjoys owning the entire data lifecycle — from pulling raw data out of a source system all the way to the clean model powering the final dashboard. Because we’re a small, tight-knit team, there’s no tossing tickets over the fence to "another department" (mostly because there isn't one!). Instead, you get real ownership: our architecture is yours to shape, not just inherit and maintain.

Roughly 70% of your time will go into building brand-new things , with the remaining 30% dedicated to evolving what’s already running smoothly in production.

Here’s what’s on the roadmap:

  • End-to-end ingestion pipelines: Integrating new source systems and tables — taking them from initial configuration straight to the data warehouse.
  • Gold layer rebuild: Migrating and redesigning our Gold layer from Dataform to dbt , built from the ground up.
  • Batch & Streaming: Building batch processing jobs in Apache Beam / Dataflow , and helping us pioneer our streaming setup — it’s a fresh area for us with plenty of room to grow.
  • Smart orchestration: Crafting Airflow DAGs that are dynamically generated, event-driven, and equipped with self-triggering mechanisms for clean backfills.
  • Analytics engineering: Designing dbt models in BigQuery — dimensional modeling (facts & dimensions), SCD2 history tracking, and solid data quality tests.
  • True architectural ownership: You decide how a solution should work, rather than just blindly following specs from a ticket.

Our requirements

We’re looking for a seasoned engineer ( 5+ years of hands-on experience with production data ) who cares about clean, maintainable craftsmanship. Specifically, you’ll feel right at home if you have:

  • Production-grade Python: You write clean, readable code designed to be maintained by humans, not just to pass a one-off script run.
  • Advanced SQL: Window functions, MERGE statements, reading execution plans, and keeping an eye on query costs are second nature to you.
  • Orchestration with Airflow / Cloud Composer: Real production experience building and maintaining your own DAGs — our entire orchestration lives here.
  • Batch processing with Apache Beam & Dataflow: This is the backbone of our batch workloads (if you have a strong Apache Spark background and are happy to ramp up quickly on Beam, we’d still love to chat).
  • Messaging: Practical experience working with Google Pub/Sub .
  • BigQuery: Solid grasp of partitioning, clustering, and cost optimization strategies.
  • Real-world dbt experience: You’ve used dbt on an actual project — building incremental models, writing tests, and creating reusable macros.
  • Columnar data & Parquet: Strong understanding of schemas, data types, and Parquet internals — our platform relies on it end-to-end, from ingestion to the warehouse.
  • Data modeling & SCD2: Solid command of dimensional modeling and Slowly Changing Dimensions (Type 2) — in our setup, history tracking spans the entire data model, not just a single layer.
  • Cloud fundamentals: Hands-on experience with GCP (or strong AWS / Azure skills paired with eagerness to switch).
  • Languages: Fluent Polish (for business/data naming conventions and direct client communication) and solid English (for code, documentation, and technical work).

Nice-to-haves (bonus points if you’ve played with these):

  • Infrastructure & DevOps: Terraform / IaC, CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps, Cloud Build), Docker & Artifact Registry.
  • Cloud Security & Serverless: GCP IAM, Secret Manager, Service Accounts, Cloud Functions.
  • Data Ecosystem & Governance: Dataform, Dataplex, Analytics Hub, PII anonymization, and GDPR compliance.
  • Sources & Extra Tools: Oracle or MS SQL as source systems, Java, or DuckDB for snappy local development.
