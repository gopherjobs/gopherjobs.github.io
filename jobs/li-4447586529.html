Description

Job Description Ciklum is looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer to join our team in the Czech Republic.

We are a custom product engineering company that supports both multinational organizations and scaling startups to solve their most complex business challenges. With a global team of over 4,000 highly skilled developers, consultants, analysts and product owners, we engineer technology that redefines industries and shapes the way people live.

About The Role We are looking for a talented Senior DevOps Engineer to support our engineering work, with a strong focus on the underlying infrastructure that we need to get from source code to production, plus keep it happy once it's there. We need someone who understands software development, and enjoys working with developers on all the things necessary to improving, deploying, monitoring, and operating production services.

Responsibilities

  • Design, implement, and maintain highly available infrastructure on GCP and in Kubernetes
  • Develop effective tooling to accelerate onboarding of new services and build and release management
  • Own the project and deliverables
  • Build and implement monitoring and logging services
  • Work with application engineering to cultivate operational standards

Requirements

  • Strong experience with release pipelines, including blue/green deployments and multiple stages, in a > 100 node environment
  • Used infrastructure automation tooling including Terraform, Ansible, or similar
  • Software development experience in a language like Python or Go along with knowledge of shell scripting
  • Build and release experience with tools such as Github Actions, Codefresh, or similar
  • Experience with application performance monitoring tools such as Datadog, or similar
  • Hands on experience building automated Linux infrastructure on cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure
  • Experience working with EKS or equivalent kubernetes platforms and concept
