Description

About Oppizi

Oppizi is the global leader in offline performance marketing. We built the technology that tracks, scales, and optimizes offline channels: Flyering, Door to Door, Direct Mail, and Insert Media. Operating across 12 countries, we partner with category leaders like Uber, DoorDash, and Deliveroo to turn real world distribution into measurable, attributable growth.

Offline marketing was the last channel nobody could measure. We changed that, and we're now layering applied AI on top of a proven, revenue generating business.

The Role

As a Senior Product Engineer, you sit at the intersection of product intuition, user experience, and technical execution. You will own customer facing features end to end: discovery, system design, backend architecture, frontend polish, and production delivery.

You'll work on the tools our customers and field teams use every day, across our web platform, mobile apps, durable backend workflows, and our proprietary AI applications. This is a role for someone who wants to ship real products to real users, not maintain a slice of someone else's roadmap.

What you'll do:

  • Own product capabilities end to end.

Architect, build, and ship complete features across our web platform (React, Node.js) and mobile apps (React Native), from first commit to production monitoring.

  • Build durable, distributed workflows.

Model campaign operations, distribution tracking, and multi step business processes as Temporal workflows that survive retries, failures, and long running state.

  • Build applied AI experiences.

Integrate LLMs, agentic workflows, and intelligent automation directly into the product to remove operational toil and solve concrete customer problems. You'll extend our existing proprietary AI apps and help define what comes next.

  • Drive product discovery and iteration.

Partner closely with Product Managers, designers, and ops to shape requirements, run experiments, read the data, and deliver solutions users actually adopt.

  • Own what you ship in production.

Deploy services to AWS ECS, keep CI/CD pipelines fast and reliable, and proactively improve performance and observability.

  • Elevate the team.

Lead technical design reviews, mentor teammates, and continuously refine how we build for speed and quality.
