The role Building the data layer for physical and multimodal AI requires moving and processing petabyte-scale video, LiDAR, and sensor streams with zero room for infrastructure failure. This company has raised over $100M from leading global VCs and grown past 180 people in London, serving hundreds of the world's most advanced AI and robotics teams. The engineering culture is dense with top-tier researchers, quants, and systems engineers building mission-critical platform primitives from first principles.

You will join as a DevOps engineer embedded directly with the squads building the core platform. This is not an internal IT ticket-clearing desk or an afterthought support function; it is an infrastructure engineering role where you own reliability, scalability, and developer ergonomics across GCP and AWS. You will define how hundreds of services get deployed, observed, and scaled under heavy load, holding a high bar for automation and infrastructure-as-code.

The work

  • Architect, scale, and maintain multi-cloud Kubernetes clusters, networking, and high-throughput storage across GCP and AWS, treating infrastructure-as-code via Terraform or Pulumi as the absolute default.
  • Own and modernise end-to-end CI/CD pipelines, removing developer bottlenecks and enabling squads to ship reliably multiple times a day.
  • Define SLIs and SLOs for critical services handling petabyte-scale multimodal datasets, leading blameless postmortems to continuously improve system resilience.
  • Enforce platform observability standards, ensuring every service is fully instrumented with distributed tracing, structured logging, metrics, and actionable alerting before it hits production.
  • Partner directly with product and systems engineers to profile bottlenecks, harden service architectures, and tune system performance under peak load.

What You Bring

  • 4+ years of hands-on platform engineering, DevOps, or SRE experience maintaining mission-critical workloads in production.
  • Deep operational experience with Kubernetes, container orchestration, and multi-cloud infrastructure across AWS or GCP.
  • Production mastery of infrastructure-as-code using Terraform or Pulumi, alongside a track record of building automated CI/CD pipelines at scale.
  • Strong fundamentals in distributed systems observability (metrics, logs, tracing, alerting), Linux operating systems, networking, and databases.
  • Based in or able to relocate to London, working on-site four days a week.
