Senior Java & Cloud Engineer at BairesDev

As a Senior Java & Cloud Engineer, you will set the architectural direction for multi-cloud control plane services and drive long-term platform strategy. You will lead technical initiatives to ensure our core distributed systems are highly scalable, secure, and optimized for modern cloud environments.

What You'll Do

  • Establish the architectural direction for multi-cloud control plane services and define long-term platform strategies.
  • Design and implement robust, highly scalable, and performant distributed systems.
  • Leverage containerization and orchestration tools to optimize service deployment and scalability.
  • Mentor senior engineering team members and influence technical decisions across the wider organization.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional partners to ensure infrastructure capabilities align with business objectives.

What We Are Looking For

  • 5+ years of experience in Software Engineering, Cloud Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Proven expertise in Java development and designing distributed systems.
  • Strong hands-on experience with public cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure.
  • Solid understanding of Kubernetes and containerization using Docker.
  • Advanced proficiency in English.
