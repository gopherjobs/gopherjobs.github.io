Description

The climate crisis is the defining challenge of our time—but it’s also the greatest opportunity for innovation, and a challenge we’re proud to take on. At Overstory, we’re harnessing cutting-edge technology to enable a resilient electrical grid that keeps communities thriving as our world changes.

The grid is the backbone of life as we know it. It powers hospitals, keeps food fresh, and ensures communities stay connected. But extreme weather, aging infrastructure, and growing wildfire risks are putting this critical system under pressure. All of this combined makes the electric utility industry the greatest opportunity for tackling climate change.

One of the leading causes of catastrophic wildfires and power outages? Trees and brush coming into contact with power lines.

That’s where we help. At Overstory, we use AI and advanced satellite imagery to pinpoint and prioritize vegetation risks before they materialize. By giving utilities critical analysis on those risks, we’re helping prevent outages, reduce wildfire risks, and accelerate the transition to a safer, more resilient grid.

Our team spans the Americas and Europe, and we work with utility partners across the Americas and beyond. We’re outdoor enthusiasts, musicians, artists, athletes, parents, and adventurers. What unites us is a passion for solving complex problems, a commitment to climate action, and the belief that technology should be a force for good.

Join us to help us build a more resilient world together.

Role & Team As a Staff Site Reliability Engineer at Overstory, you will lead the technical direction of the cloud platform behind our products, data pipelines, and engineering workflows. Our AI and satellite-imagery analysis helps utilities find vegetation risk before it causes outages or wildfires, and that depends on infrastructure that is reliable, secure, and cost-aware at scale.

We run on Google Cloud, with GKE, Cloud Run, Terraform with Atlantis, Argo CD, and GitHub Actions, plus the orchestration and CPU/GPU compute behind our imagery and ML pipelines, including Dagster. You don't need experience with every one of these tools; we care more about how you design and run platforms than which tools you've used.

Reporting to our Director of Platform Engineering, you will be the team technical anchor. This is an individual contributor role: as a senior technical leader, you'll find the problems worth solving, drive architectural decisions, set the standards the team builds to, and mentor other engineers.

Time zone requirement: Europe (GMT/WET, CET,) and Eastern North America (NT, AT, ET)

What You'll Do

  • Set the technical direction for our cloud platform, including how we expand into new regions to meet customer data-residency requirements.
  • Help build our internal AI platform, which gives engineers a safe, cost-controlled way to run AI agents and deploy internal tools.
  • Build paved roads: self-service paths, reusable infrastructure modules, and shared delivery pipelines that let engineering teams ship safely through self service
  • Lead our reliability practice. Define SLOs and error budgets for the services and pipelines utilities depend on, shape incident response and on-call, and make sure every alert has an owner.
  • Make observability useful to the teams that rely on it: metrics, logs, and traces that help people find problems quickly, and fewer alerts that people act on.
  • Keep cloud and compute costs, including GPU, visible and under control, and help teams make cost-aware decisions.
  • Build security into the platform: least-privilege access, secrets management, and software supply-chain hygiene, in support of the security reviews our utility customers require.
  • Set production-readiness standards for new services, mentor engineers through design reviews, code reviews, and pairing, and partner with Product, Engineering, and Security leaders on what the platform should do next.

What Success Looks Like In Your First Year

  • Our utility-facing services and key pipelines have SLOs, and the team uses them to decide where to invest.
  • We have a repeatable, documented pattern for standing up a new region.
  • Development, staging, and production are cleanly separated, with alerts routed by environment and severity.
  • Common requests such as access, storage, and new services are self-service, and the team spends less of its week on tickets.
  • Cloud and GPU spend is visible to every team and broken down by service, so budgets are easy to track and audit.

Skills & Experience

  • Motivated by the chance to make the infrastructure behind wildfire and outage prevention reliable, secure, and efficient.
  • Extensive experience in SRE, platform, or infrastructure engineering, including time as the technical lead on systems other teams depend on.
  • Experience owning production infrastructure on a major cloud provider end to end, from design through operation, upgrades, and the trade-offs behind them. Google Cloud is a strong preference; deep experience on AWS is a reasonable substitute if you're ready to go deep on GCP.
  • Deep experience operating container platforms in production, ideally Kubernetes, including upgrades, autoscaling, workload isolation, and capacity planning.
  • Strong infrastructure-as-code and delivery practice: you've designed modules, pipelines, and deployment workflows that many teams share and trust.
  • Experience building reliability practices: SLOs and error budgets, alerting that people act on, incident response, and blameless postmortems.
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals. You write production-quality automation and tooling.
  • A track record of leading technical work across teams without formal authority: writing designs, building consensus, and setting standards that others adopt.
  • Clear written communication. We work remotely across time zones, so much of our work happens in writing.

Nice to have

  • Operating data orchestration or ML pipeline platforms, and running GPU workloads.
  • Multi-region architecture and data-residency requirements.
  • Security and compliance work for enterprise customers or in regulated industries.
  • Building internal platforms with AI tools or large language models, or using AI to change how your team works.
  • Geospatial or imagery data. This isn't required; you'll learn it here.

How We Work

  • Remote first. The team is spread across North America and Europe. We need several hours of daily overlap with U.S. Eastern and Central European time.
  • Support and on-call. SRE shares a weekly support rotation during business hours across ET and CET.
  • Written by default. We plan in Linear, document in Notion, and talk in Slack. Designs and decisions get written down.
  • AI-assisted engineering. We use AI coding tools every day, with guardrails, and we're building the platform that lets the rest of the company do the same.
