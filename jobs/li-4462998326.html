About the project:

As a Senior Fullstack Software Engineer (React, Node.js & Python) , you will play a pivotal role in designing, building, and scaling high-performance web applications across our frontend and backend ecosystems. Leveraging your expertise in React, Node.js, and Python, you will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to architect robust APIs, craft intuitive user interfaces, and optimize system reliability. You will drive engineering best practices, champion scalable architecture, and mentor team members to ensure the continuous delivery of high-quality software solutions.

What you’ll do:

  • Design, build, and maintain full stack features across front-end and back-end systems.
  • Develop and integrate scalable back-end services using Node.js, C#, and Python.
  • Work with relational and NoSQL data stores to support application needs.
  • Build and deploy cloud-native solutions on AWS, leveraging event-driven and serverless patterns.
  • Write and maintain automated tests to ensure code quality and reliability.
  • Manage infrastructure as code and contribute to CI/CD pipelines.

What makes you a great fit:

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 6+ Years of experience working in backend development with Node, C#, and Python.
  • 6+ Years of experience working with React.
  • 6+ Years of experience working with PostgreSQL, AWS DynamoDB, and SQL.
  • 4+ Years of experience working with AWS cloud services, including SQS, Lambda, ECS, CloudFront, ALB, and EventBridge.
  • 4+ Years of experience working with Jest and Playwright.
  • 4+ Years of experience working in Infrastructure as Code with Terraform, and version control/CI workflows with GitLab.
  • Advanced English Level is required for this role, as you will work with US clients. Effective communication in English is essential to deliver the best solutions to our clients and expand your horizons.
