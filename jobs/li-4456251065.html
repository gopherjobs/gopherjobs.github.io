Position Overview And Objective We are looking for a Senior Developer to join a small, dedicated team focused on leading complex design and development efforts for the next generation of Arctic Wolf’s parsing platform, a critical layer in our data pipeline that enables ingestion and normalization of diverse data sources.

Arctic Wolf processes trillions of events per day from a wide range of on-premise devices and cloud platforms. This data powers detection, analytics, and customer-facing workflows across the platform. As the volume, variety, and complexity of data continue to grow in a rapidly changing AI-amplified threat landscape, the need for scalable, adaptable data processing systems is increasingly critical.

This team will design and build the next generation of the parsing framework, improving how data is transformed, validated, and made usable across the pipeline.

Responsibilities

  • Own and deliver complex parsing platform initiatives with ambiguous requirements
  • Design and lead major components of the parsing framework
  • Improve scalability and flexibility across data workflows
  • Design and scale high-performance services in real-time data systems
  • Lead deployment of containerized services in production
  • Mentor engineers and guide technical execution

Experience & Requirements

  • 6–10 years of software engineering experience
  • Experience building complex distributed systems
  • Ability to navigate ambiguous problem spaces
  • Experience solving performance and scalability challenges in high-throughput, large-scale systems
  • Interested in working at the intersection of large-scale data systems and cybersecurity
  • Passionate about building high-quality, production-grade software
  • Experience building and deploying services in cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
  • Comfortable working with distributed systems and microservices architectures
  • Experience building high-performance, high-throughput systems
  • Familiarity with event-driven or stream processing systems (e.g., Kafka, Flink, Spark, Reddis, SQS, RabbitMQ, or other pub-sub systems)
  • Experience working with modern containerized environments (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Strong programming skills in one or more languages such as Golang, Python, or Java
  • Comfortable working in environments with continuous integration and deployment practices
