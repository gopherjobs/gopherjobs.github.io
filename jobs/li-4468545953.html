About the Role: We are seeking a Full Stack Builder to own the CRM Tasks product end-to-end. This role emphasizes complete product ownership - designing, building, shipping, and scaling features independently. You will operate with high autonomy, turning ideas into production-ready systems without relying on traditional cross-functional dependencies

What You’ll Do:

  • Own the full lifecycle: translate business requirements into scalable, production-grade solutions
  • Design and implement frontend and backend systems, APIs, and workflows
  • Rapidly prototype, validate with users, and iterate to production quality
  • Ensure performance, reliability, and scalability of systems
  • Monitor applications, maintain documentation, and improve engineering processes

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Proven experience shipping full stack production systems
  • Strong problem-solving and system design skills
  • Ability to work independently with high ownership
  • Experience with APIs, backend services, and modern frontend frameworks
  • Understanding of scalability, performance, and reliability trade-offs

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience building CRM or task/workflow management products
  • Familiarity with data pipelines, analytics, or monitoring tools
  • Strong frontend and UX sensibility
  • Portfolio or GitHub with deployed projects

Tech Stack: VueJS/NodeJS/GoLang

What Success Looks Like:

  • Delivers features from idea to production with minimal cycle time
  • Builds reliable, scalable systems used by customers
  • Makes sound trade-offs between speed, quality, and performance
  • Continuously improves product experience through iteration
