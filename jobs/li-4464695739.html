Staff AI Engineer at BairesDev

In this role, you'll lead backend AI features end to end, owning a component of a larger body of work alongside a Principal Engineer and a tech lead. Staying hands-on throughout, you'll build LangGraph flows and RAG pipelines while holding evaluation coverage on everything you ship. This is your opportunity to lead technically while remaining deeply hands-on, unblocking engineers in your pod and shaping how production LLM systems are built, maintained, and evolved over time.

What You'll Do

  • Lead and own backend AI features end to end, breaking down and delivering complex work.
  • Build and maintain LangGraph agent flows and RAG pipelines over structured and unstructured data.
  • Hold evaluation and tracing coverage on shipped AI behavior.
  • Stay hands-on across development phases, building alongside Principal and Senior engineers.
  • Unblock Senior engineers in the pod and surface blockers early.

What We Are Looking For

  • 8+ years of experience in Python.
  • Proven experience building, maintaining, and evolving production LLM systems, not just zero-to-one projects.
  • Hands-on experience with LangChain and LangGraph, including agent flows, tool and skill invocation, and state management.
  • Experience building RAG pipelines over structured and unstructured data.
  • Experience with LLM evaluation and tracing, including LangSmith, LLM-as-judge, and golden datasets.
  • 4+ years of experience with React/Next.js and TypeScript.
  • Experience with MCP server development against structured databases and data lakes.
  • Experience with streaming agent interactions, including LangGraph API and WebSockets.
  • Background with CI/CD pipelines and service observability, including logging, tracing, and SLOs.
  • Experience with distributed systems, microservices, and REST/GraphQL on AWS.
  • Strong prompt engineering skills, iterating against measured output.
  • Advanced proficiency in English.

Nice To Have

  • Experience with model gateway and routing such as Bedrock or LiteLLM.
  • Familiarity with agent-to-agent communication patterns (A2A).
  • Experience with vector databases such as Pinecone, FAISS, Qdrant, or pgvector.
  • Security mindset, including secure coding and AuthN/AuthZ practices.
  • Analytics and data visualization experience.
  • Experience with ServiceNow or enterprise workflow integration.
