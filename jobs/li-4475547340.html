Description

We’re looking for a senior product engineer to help us build better tools for legal research. You’ll work mainly on the backend, with a say in what we build and responsibility for taking it from an early idea to something customers use every day. That includes asking whether we’re solving the right problem, deciding what’s worth shipping first, and knowing when something needs more time.

About Pandektes Our mission is to rebuild legal research from the ground up. Lawyers still lose countless hours to fragmented sources, outdated systems, and databases that fight them at every turn. We're building the largest AI-powered legal database in the world to make that friction disappear.

We're a team of 45+ based in our Copenhagen HQ, Zurich, London, and Hamburg. We’re a small team with high trust and flat structure. We move fast, argue our positions, and ship. If your input is good, it gets implemented - often within hours, not sprints.

Pandektes has raised more than € 17.5 million and most recently closed a € 13.5 million Series A round (October 2026) led by Alstin Capital, PROfounders and Scale Capital. Pandektes already serves more than 450 customers across Denmark, Germany and Switzerland.

What We Value “How fast can we ship this?” comes up a lot at Pandektes. So does “we should take our time with this one.”

Knowing which question to ask is a big part of being an engineer here.

Knowing when it’s good enough We like getting things into customers’ hands quickly. That means making trade-offs, questioning scope, and accepting when something is good enough for now. There’s usually another approach to explore or another prompt to try. At some point, you have to ship.

We use AI throughout our development process. AI code reviews, feature flags, and remote coding agents running end-to-end tests help us move quickly with confidence. Deciding what to build, what to check, and when it’s ready still takes judgment.

We value people who can find a smaller version worth shipping, explain the trade-offs, and follow through once customers start using it.

Knowing when to slow down Some decisions deserve more time. Getting them right makes everything that follows easier. Getting them wrong means paying for the same mistake repeatedly.

Our default is to move quickly, so slowing down takes discipline. Sometimes it means pushing back when the rest of us want to get something out. We value someone who can explain why the extra work matters, challenge an approach, and help us decide where to spend our time.

You’ll have room to make those calls here, and teammates who will question them. We expect you to do the same for us.

Mess Around. Find Out. Share What You Learned. We enjoy trying things before we know whether they’ll work. A new tool, a different way to use an agent, an idea that might turn out to be useless. Finding out is part of the work.

Once a month, we get together to demo our setups, show what we’re experimenting with, and share what we’ve learned. Sometimes we invite people from outside the team to show us how they work. Those sessions have changed how we build software.

We value curiosity and the generosity to bring other people along. If you find something useful, show us. If an experiment fails, we’d like to hear about that too.

We try to watch this video once a month to keep this value top of mind.

The Experience You’ll Bring You’ve built and operated backend systems that real customers depend on. You can work through questions about data, APIs, performance, and security, and explain the consequences of your decisions.

Our stack includes TypeScript, NestJS, GraphQL, Postgres, and Prisma. We also work with OpenAI and Turbopuffer. Experience with these tools is useful; we’re especially interested in what you’ve built with them and the decisions you made along the way.

You enjoy working through a product problem while it’s still taking shape. You ask questions, share unfinished work, and help decide what’s worth building. You’re comfortable taking a feature from those early conversations through implementation, testing, and delivery.

What we offer You’ll join at a point where there’s still a lot to shape. We offer competitive pay and equity so you can share in the success you help create.

We are usually office based but we all work from home time to time. You’ll have your own Pleo card for the tools and work expenses you need, plus 25 vacation days a year, public holidays, and paid parental leave when your family grows.

We also make time to get together away from our usual work. Our last team trip was to Barcelona, and we’d love to have you along for the next one.
