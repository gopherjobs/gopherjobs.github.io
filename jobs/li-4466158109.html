About The Role Live Site Operations (LSO) is the team that keeps Shopify online. As an LSO Specialist, you sit at the center of two of the highest-leverage functions in the company: getting changes safely into production and driving the response when something goes wrong. This is active, hands-on work where you are constantly investigating, deploying, and decision-making, not just watching dashboards. You own the live site during your shift, and because our coverage is global and follow-the-sun, your shift is a critical link in 24/7 commerce, including the high-traffic weekend windows when merchants depend on us most.

This is a role for someone who stays calm under pressure, makes sharp decisions with incomplete information, and gets energy from being where the action is.

Key Responsibilities: As a Live Site Operations Specialist supporting Shopify’s e-commerce platform, you will be responsible for:

  • Supporting Global Deployment: Guide periodic code deployment for key areas to success, efficiently navigating any problems that may arise. Ensure safe deploys for the success of our customers, especially enterprise shops with high-scale requirements.
  • Responding to Incidents: Act as a first responder when production systems degrade, debugging, diagnosing, and understanding the technical details behind a problem, then working with owning teams to respond to and repair it. Help coordinate the response and drive incidents toward the fastest safe path to recovery.
  • Investigate and Strengthen Observability: Use our observability tooling as a launchpad for active investigation rather than passive monitoring, chasing down anomalies and improving the signal so problems surface sooner, while keeping the platform resilient for our global footprint and highest-scale enterprise customers under the most demanding traffic.
