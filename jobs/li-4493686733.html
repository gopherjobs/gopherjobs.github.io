Job Description Our Summer Internship is an 11-week programme designed to give you hands-on experience of working at Visa while developing your technical and professional skills. You will work alongside experienced professionals, contribute to meaningful projects and gain exposure to technologies and products operating at global scale.

Throughout the internship, you will complete an end-to-end project , take part in technical and professional development opportunities, learn through mentorship and Visa’s engineering community, and network with fellow interns and colleagues across the business. You will also have the opportunity to participate in a global social impact case challenge and hear directly from senior leaders through the Visa Executive Speaker Series .

Daily Responsibilities As a Software Engineering Intern, you will:

  • Work across product areas and gain exposure to software engineering at global scale.
  • Collaborate with engineers and cross-functional teams to develop and enhance software solutions.
  • Contribute across the development lifecycle, from design and build to testing, security and deployment.
  • Gain hands-on experience with Agile, Test-Driven Development and Continuous Integration and Delivery.
  • Build and improve robust, scalable and secure software in line with engineering best practices.
  • Explore AI and automation to improve code, systems and engineering processes.
  • Participate in code reviews and apply feedback to strengthen your technical skills.
  • Learn through hands-on experience, mentorship and the wider engineering community.

The internship is just the beginning of what we hope will be a lasting career at Visa. Strong performance during the programme may lead to the opportunity to secure a graduate role with Visa after completing your university studies. Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.

Qualifications

  • Pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree & graduating December 2026 or later.
  • Proficiency in at least one programming language or technology including, but not limited, to Java, C++, C# or python.
  • Knowledge of Spring, JavaScript, Angular or ML is a plus.
  • An undeniable passion for the software engineering discipline, examples of work you have completed outside of the core curriculum is a plus.
  • A strong desire to understand and leverage new tools and technologies and evolve their learning of the discipline.
  • Analytical abilities, including the ability to translate and communicate technical requirements.
