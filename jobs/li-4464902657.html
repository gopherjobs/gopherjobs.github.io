Senior Security Engineer at BairesDev

In this role, you'll drive supply chain and cloud-native security across hundreds of services, designing hardened container images and securing Kubernetes workloads at scale. Building Go-based security automation, you'll work directly with engineering teams through Git PR workflows, committing patches yourself rather than just flagging issues. This is your opportunity to be a strong coder first and a security expert second, applying sharp engineering judgment to distinguish good code from bad, AI-generated or otherwise.

What You'll Do

  • Design hardened container base images and secure Kubernetes workloads.
  • Build Go-based security automation tooling.
  • Drive vulnerability remediation with engineering teams via Git PR workflows, including committing patches directly.
  • Perform SCA, SAST, and DAST triage, taking vulnerabilities from discovery to patch.
  • Harden CI/CD pipelines, IaC templates, and Helm charts against supply chain risks.

What We Are Looking For

  • 5+ years of experience in software engineering with a security focus.
  • Strong proficiency in Go for security automation tooling.
  • Experience with container security, including hardened base images, Kubernetes runtime security, network policies, and admission controllers.
  • Experience with SCA and large-scale dependency vulnerability remediation, including SAST, DAST, and triage to patch.
  • Background in supply chain security, including CI/CD hardening, IaC templates, Helm charts, and third-party dependencies.
  • Experience with Terraform for provisioning security controls.
  • Experience with AWS cloud security, with Azure as a secondary platform, focused on container orchestration.
  • Experience applying AI to security, such as LLMs or MCP for automated vulnerability fixing or alert grouping, or a strong conceptual understanding of these approaches.
  • Advanced proficiency in English.

Nice To Have

  • Experience with Node.js development.
  • Familiarity with Artifactory or JFrog.
  • Experience with zero-CVE container vendors such as Chainguard or similar.
  • Knowledge of the OWASP Top 10.
  • Familiarity with supply chain frameworks such as SLSA.
  • Experience with FIPS compliance and cipher migration.
