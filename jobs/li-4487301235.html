Your Profile & DNA:

We are looking for an experienced Application Production Support Engineer to join our team in Amsterdam.

The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in production application support, Linux/Unix troubleshooting, Shell scripting, SQL, incident management, and application monitoring.

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Monitor and support production applications to ensure availability, stability, and performance.
  • Perform incident troubleshooting, root cause analysis (RCA), and problem management.
  • Handle application deployments, release activities, configuration changes, and production fixes.
  • Monitor application logs, batch jobs, APIs, interfaces, and system alerts.
  • Manage incidents and service requests using ServiceNow, JIRA, or similar ITSM tools.
  • Collaborate with development, infrastructure, database, security, and business teams to resolve production issues.
  • Automate repetitive operational activities using Shell, Python, or PowerShell.
  • Maintain operational documentation, SOPs, runbooks, and knowledge articles.
  • Participate in major incident management and post-incident reviews.
  • Support change management and ensure production changes follow operational controls

Qualification & Experience

  • A Bachelor's or Master's Degree or equivalent.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Linux/Unix troubleshooting .
  • Good experience with Shell scripting .
  • Strong knowledge of SQL and relational databases .
  • Proven experience in Application Production Support.
  • Experience in incident management and problem management.
  • Knowledge of Root Cause Analysis (RCA).
  • Experience with application monitoring and logging tools.
  • Understanding of REST APIs, microservices, batch processing, and application interfaces.
  • Experience with ServiceNow, JIRA, or similar incident management platforms.
  • Good troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Good command of spoken and written English.
  • Experience with one or more of the following Monitoring & Logging Tools: Splunk,Dynatrace,Grafana,AppDynamics,ELK Stack

Good-to-Have Skills

  • Knowledge of Python or PowerShell scripting.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD tools such as:
  • Jenkins
  • GitLab CI/CD
  • Azure DevOps
  • Knowledge of container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Exposure to cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure or AWS.
  • Understanding of ITIL practices and production change management.
  • Experience supporting enterprise-scale applications.
