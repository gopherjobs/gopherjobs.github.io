Full Stack QA Engineer | Tokyo | Hybrid | FinTech

Salary: 6,000,000 JPY~12,000,000 JPY depending on experience

Hybrid work: Twice a week in office minimum

We’re hiring a Full Stack QA Engineer to join a rapidly growing international FinTech company and help shape the next generation of financial services in Japan.

This is a great opportunity for a QA professional who enjoys test automation, modern technologies, and AI-driven quality engineering , and who wants to have a real impact in a large-scale product environment.

What You’ll Do

  • Design comprehensive test strategies, scenarios, and test cases across mobile, web, and API applications.
  • Drive test automation while also taking ownership of manual and exploratory testing.
  • Build and maintain automated test frameworks and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Analyze requirements and ensure strong functional and non-functional test coverage.
  • Identify, track, and resolve defects while continuously improving QA processes.
  • Act as a QA subject-matter expert and support junior engineers.
  • Collaborate closely with developers, product teams, and other stakeholders.
  • Leverage AI tools and LLM technologies to improve test design, automation, and exploratory testing.
  • Validate AI-powered applications, including testing for hallucinations, bias, drift, robustness, and performance.
  • Explore technologies such as OpenAI APIs, RAG, embeddings, and MCP integrations.

Tech Stack

Automation: Appium, Playwright, Selenium, Rest-Assured, Cucumber

CI/CD: GitHub Actions, Bitrise, Jenkins or similar

Architecture: Microservices, APIs

AI: OpenAI API, LLMs, RAG, embeddings, MCP

Cloud/Infrastructure: Docker, Kubernetes

Performance: Gatling, JMeter, wrk

What We’re Looking For

  • 3+ years of professional experience in QA automation.
  • Experience testing mobile apps, web applications, and/or APIs.
  • Hands-on experience with at least one automation framework such as Appium, Playwright/Selenium, or Rest-Assured.
  • Experience with Cucumber and CI/CD environments.
  • Strong understanding of test strategy, test case design, and QA methodologies.
  • Good knowledge of microservices and their testing challenges.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Professional experience using AI productivity tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, or similar.
  • Knowledge of OpenAI APIs and LLM integration.
  • Business-level English, Japanese is not required.

Nice to Have

  • ISTQB or equivalent QA certification.
  • FinTech, banking, or payments experience.
  • Agile development experience.
  • Performance testing experience.
  • Docker/Kubernetes experience.
  • Coding experience in web or mobile development.
  • Experience integrating AI into software development or QA workflows.

Why Join?

  • Work on products used at massive scale.
  • Join a highly international engineering environment.
  • Help build and improve next-generation financial technology.
  • Work with cutting-edge AI and automation technologies.
  • Opportunity to influence QA standards and processes.
  • Hybrid working environment in Tokyo.
  • Visa sponsorship / relocation support available.
