Staff Software Engineer — Card Issuing

Cards Engineering Lead

About Noah

Noah is building the financial infrastructure for a world where money moves as easily as information. Our platform connects traditional banking, payment networks and stablecoins, giving businesses and technology partners global payments, stablecoin payments, virtual accounts and digital wallets through a single set of modern APIs.

Fintechs, wallets, marketplaces and global businesses use Noah to move money across borders faster, cheaper and more transparently, with real-time settlement and compliance built in.

We're now expanding into card issuing, so customers and technology partners can spend directly from their financial accounts and stablecoin balances, anywhere cards are accepted.

The role

We're looking for an experienced, hands-on Staff Software Engineer to lead the technical delivery of our new card issuing product.

You'll work closely with our Head of Payments Partnerships & Expansion, Core Engineering and existing engineering teams to build a reliable, scalable card issuing platform within Noah's existing architecture and financial infrastructure.

What you'll do

  • Lead the technical implementation and delivery of Noah's card issuing platform spanning formal scheme certification, go-live execution, and ongoing operational maintenance.
  • Integrate with issuer processors, card networks and banking partners.
  • Build real-time card authorisation, lifecycle management and transaction processing services.
  • Implement clearing, reversals, refunds, disputes and card controls.
  • Integrate card processing with Noah's existing wallets, accounts, FX and ledger infrastructure.
  • Collaborate with Core Engineering on architectural decisions, ensuring solutions align with established architecture, standards and patterns.
  • Work with external partners on certification, testing and production readiness.

What we're looking for

Essential

  • Strong backend engineering experience, including technical leadership of complex production systems.
  • Direct experience with card issuing or issuer processing, including integrations with issuing processors and financial infrastructure.
  • Deep understanding of card authorisation, clearing, settlement and transaction lifecycles.
  • Strong distributed systems, API design and event-driven architecture skills.
  • Fluency with agentic software development workflows: routinely orchestrate coding agents in your IDE/CLI with repository context to implement, test and review changes, exercising engineering judgment and reviewing outputs—not merely copying and pasting from an AI assistant.

Nice to have

  • Visa or Mastercard certification and programme launch experience.
  • Experience & knowledge of PCI DCC rules
  • Experience with 3DS, tokenisation, VTS/MDES, fraud controls and digital wallet provisioning.
  • Experience with stablecoins, wallets or financial ledgers.
  • Experience with Go, TypeScript and AWS.
