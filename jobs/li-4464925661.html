We're currently seeking passionate individuals who are excited about the opportunity to contribute to an industry-shaping company while growing their own skills and careers. The Role

We're looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer to join our team in the APAC region. In this role, you'll work directly with enterprise and high-growth technology customers across APAC to architect and ship production systems on Telnyx's global network — spanning voice, messaging, AI, wireless, and edge compute .

This isn't about demoing products; it's about building real solutions that work at scale .

You'll sit at the intersection of software engineering, AI infrastructure, telecommunications, and customer success — working directly with customer engineering and technical leadership teams to understand requirements, architect solutions, build integrations, and take them from POC through production.

A major focus will be helping customers deploy real-time AI and communications workloads , including AI Voice Assistants, LLM inference, open-weight models, and applications requiring low latency, reliability, and regional data processing.

Responsibilities

  • Embed with customers to understand their technical requirements, AI use cases, and integration challenges
  • Architect and build custom production solutions using Telnyx's Voice, Messaging, Wireless, WebRTC, AI, and Edge Compute platforms
  • Build and deploy AI Voice Assistants, conversational AI, and LLM-powered applications
  • Deploy and operate LLM infrastructure, including open-weight models and LiteLLM or comparable gateways
  • Design model routing, fallback, observability, and cost strategies for production AI workloads
  • Build RAG pipelines, prompt architectures, evaluations, and model integrations
  • Deploy containerized services on Kubernetes and work with customer infrastructure teams on production environments
  • Troubleshoot complex issues across APIs, networking, SIP, WebRTC, AI inference, and customer infrastructure
  • Lead technical discovery, architecture sessions, POCs, pilots, and production launches from whiteboard to go-live
  • Own customer outcomes through deployment, stabilization, and handoff
  • Partner with Product and Engineering to turn customer feedback into product improvements
  • Create technical documentation, runbooks, and reference architectures
  • Help customers navigate data residency, security, privacy, and compliance requirements across APAC
