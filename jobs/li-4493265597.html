Role Purpose / Summary We are seeking an experienced, proactive Release Manager to coordinate the end-to-end release of a new government digital service supporting the implementation of Martyn's Law.

The service is being delivered through multiple agile multidisciplinary teams and includes public-facing digital journeys, integrations with Microsoft Dynamics 365, and operational case management capabilities.

The Release Manager will be responsible for translating the delivery roadmap into a coordinated, executable release strategy, ensuring that development, environments, testing, security assurance, service readiness and governance activities are planned and managed as an integrated whole.

This is a hands-on delivery coordination role, requiring someone who understands how to navigate complex technical and organisational dependencies, anticipates constraints and actively removes barriers to successful release.

Key Responsibilities

  • Release planning and coordination
  • Environment and testing coordination
  • Governance and service readiness
  • Delivery management and stakeholder engagement

Essential Experience

  • Ability to work independently, coordinate across organisational boundaries and drive timely decisions.
  • Confidence using Azure DevOps, Jira, ServiceNow or comparable delivery and IT service management tools.
  • Experience working with security assurance, penetration testing, performance testing and operational acceptance requirements.
  • Demonstrable experience with CABs, ITIL-aligned change management and service transition processes.
  • Practical experience coordinating multiple environments and integrated testing strategies.
  • Strong understanding of agile delivery, CI/CD, DevOps practices and modern software deployment approaches.
  • Significant experience managing releases within complex digital delivery programmes, preferably in UK government or other regulated environments.
  • Provide concise reporting and escalate emerging issues early, with practical options for resolution.
  • Facilitate release readiness meetings, technical coordination sessions and go/no-go reviews.
  • Maintain a clear, accurate view of release risks, blockers, decisions and dependencies.
  • Coordinate across supplier teams, SIA technical teams, business stakeholders and third-party providers.
  • Work closely with delivery managers and product managers to align feature readiness with release readiness.
  • Ensure releases comply with agreed technical, security and operational controls without introducing unnecessary process overhead.
  • Work with service management teams to ensure incident management, monitoring, support, operational ownership and business continuity arrangements are ready.
  • Coordinate the production of release documentation, change tickets, deployment plans, rollback procedures and operational handover evidence.
  • Understand and manage the requirements of change governance, Change Advisory Boards (CABs), release approvals and go/no-go decisions.
  • Coordinate release activities with SIA technology, security, operations and service management teams.
  • Manage dependencies on environment access, configuration, test data, deployment pipelines and external technical teams.
  • Ensure testing, remediation, retesting and release approval activities are appropriately reflected in delivery plans.
  • Coordinate the scheduling and sequencing of integration testing, system testing, UAT, operational acceptance testing (OAT), performance testing and IT Health Checks (ITHCs).
  • Work closely with engineering, platform, DevOps and testing teams to ensure environments are available, configured and fit for purpose.
  • Understand and coordinate the environment strategy across local development, integration, pre-production and production.
  • Establish effective release calendars, change windows, deployment schedules and escalation mechanisms.
  • Coordinate daily and weekly activities required to keep releases progressing, proactively identifying opportunities to reduce delays.
  • Maintain visibility of release dependencies, readiness criteria, constraints and critical-path activities.
  • Coordinate releases across multiple agile teams, shared technical services, integration points and operational stakeholders.
  • Develop and maintain an integrated release plan aligned with the product roadmap and delivery milestones.

Desirable Experience

  • Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365, Azure-based services, government service assessments, GDS Service Standard, public-facing transactional services and delivery involving multiple suppliers.

Expected outcomes Within the first four to six weeks, the successful candidate should have established:

  • Success measure: Releases move predictably through the delivery lifecycle, with minimal avoidable delay between technical completion, assurance, approval and production deployment.
  • An actively managed critical path, with clear ownership of blockers and escalation routes.
  • A practical coordination cadence with SIA and the delivery teams.
  • A release readiness checklist covering assurance, testing and operational governance.
  • A clear environment and deployment dependency map.
  • A credible integrated release plan and release calendar.
