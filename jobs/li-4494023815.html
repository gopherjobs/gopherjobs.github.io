Description

About This Opportunity Ericsson’s Early Career Program is an 18-month journey for you who are recently graduated or have up to five years of relevant experience. Through three rotations, including one international and market exposure, you will tackle challenges that matter, work alongside people who expect you to contribute, question, and move things forward.

The Innovate track is designed for you who are driven by building what’s next. You’ll rotate across Research, Product Development, Data, and AI contributing to solutions that don’t just support the business but move it forward.

This isn’t a programme where you sit on the sidelines. From day one, you’re hired into a role, trusted to contribute, supported to grow, and expected to make an impact. Find out more about the Early Career program here.

Your journey within the Innovate Program will start as AI Lead Engineer within Business Area Networks.

What You Will Do AI is not a side project in our R&D organization — it is changing how we design, build, verify and release products. We are looking for an AI Lead Engineer who wants to be at the front of that change from day one. Your mission is to investigate and transform how R&D works with the help of cutting-edge AI techniques. Concretely, that means:

  • Map and challenge existing R&D workflows and processes — from requirements and design through implementation, verification and release — and identify where AI creates real leverage.
  • Prototype and pilot solutions using modern AI techniques: large language models, agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented generation, code intelligence and automation of engineering toolchains.
  • Turn successful prototypes into adopted ways of working: measure the impact, document it, and drive towards large-scale AI development in the organization.
  • Act as a technical reference point for AI in your organization — sharing what works, being honest about what does not, and raising the AI literacy of the engineers around you.
  • Work across unit and country borders within RAN Services product development, regarding tooling, data and AI competence.

You will bring:

  • Technically oriented, with an AI-first mindset: your instinct is to ask what part of this problem AI should solve before doing it by hand.
  • Hands-on with AI/ML in practice — through studies, thesis work, internships or personal projects.
  • You can code (Python or similar) and you are comfortable with modern AI tooling.
  • Curious and structured: You can break a messy, undefined process into something you can measure and improve.
  • You take ownership and follow things through to an outcome, not just to an idea.
  • You communicate clearly and can convince experienced engineers to change how they work.
  • Fluent in English.
  • You have a global attitude and willingness to relocate internationally for one 6-month rotation.

Note on the title: “Lead” here refers to leading the AI agenda and technical direction in your area, not to line management. Formal leadership experience is not required.

In addition, you have:

  • MSc or PhD (or equivalent) in Computer Science, Engineering Physics, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, Applied Mathematics or related field.
  • Ability to develop strategies and prioritize activities.
  • Interest in experimentation and innovation with the ability to think creatively and be well adapted to change.
  • Knowledge of wireless communication, radio networks and AI & machine Learning.
  • Experience in software engineering or development with hands-on programming.
  • Solid networking, collaboration and communication skills and proficiency in English.
