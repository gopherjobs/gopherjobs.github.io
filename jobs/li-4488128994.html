Fractional Technical Lead – Python / React / AI

€4,000 per month | Fully Remote | 1–2 Days per Week | Contract

We're working with a growing, early-stage AI SaaS business looking for an experienced Fractional Technical Lead to take ownership of its platform and help drive the next stage of development.

The company is already generating revenue and building technology that helps businesses improve their visibility across AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini.

This is an excellent opportunity for a hands-on engineer who enjoys technical ownership, building AI-powered products and working directly with founders.

The Role

  • Take full technical ownership of an established AI SaaS platform.
  • Develop and improve LLM integrations, AI agents and automated workflows.
  • Build and maintain features using Python, Django, FastAPI, React and TypeScript.
  • Manage cloud infrastructure across Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Improve platform performance, reliability, security and operating costs.
  • Shape technical decisions and contribute directly to the product roadmap.

What We're Looking For

  • 7+ years' experience in full-stack software engineering.
  • Strong commercial experience with Python (Django/FastAPI) and React.
  • Hands-on GCP experience, or extensive AWS experience with a willingness to transition.
  • Proven experience deploying LLM-powered features into production.
  • Experience independently owning and maintaining live applications.
  • Strong communication skills and a proactive, commercially minded approach.

What's on Offer?

  • €4,000 per month for approximately 1–2 days per week.
  • Fully remote, with Central European working-hour overlap.
  • Flexible, month-to-month contract.
  • Opportunity to increase involvement as the business grows.
  • Genuine technical ownership and direct collaboration with the founders.
