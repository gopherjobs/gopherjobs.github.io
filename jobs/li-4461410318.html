About The Role The Identity & Access Management team owns our authentication and access control platform: the APIs, libraries, services, and data stores other engineering teams integrate with to control who can do what in their part of the product. Other teams write the access rules for their own features. This team builds the system they plug into. We are increasing our investment in this area, and the team is growing rapidly.

The role sits between platform engineering and product. Your team's systems sit in front of every dashboard, API, and piece of customer data, so reliability and security posture are yours to protect. Most of our roadmap is customer-driven, though: access control granularity, SSO and SCIM coverage, and service account scoping come up during procurement and renewal.

Qualifications You will

  • Partner with your product manager to assess customer needs, co-create the roadmap, and own delivery against it. Your product manager (PM) owns direction. You ground it in what is buildable and drive execution. Both of you bring empathy for the administrators who live in these systems every day, so you can tell which access control gaps are genuinely blocking customers.
  • Balance large investments alongside a steady cadence of customer requests while ensuring the team can deliver at a predictable pace.
  • Guide the team through architectural decisions and technical design in the evolution of our granular access control framework, which nearly every other team integrates with.
  • Hire and onboard engineers. Coach them through any growing pains, and evolve the team's norms and delivery cadence.
  • Monitor SLOs, SLAs, and health of the system in production and take timely action to meet our reliability goals.
  • Work with our security and compliance partners on reviews, audits, and the commitments we make to customers.

How You Will Start In the first month, you will learn our authentication and authorization systems, how customers manage access today, and where they get stuck. You will spend time meeting people and developing relationships on your team, sister teams, and cross-functional partners.

In 3 months, you will manage the team's day-to-day work. As you continue to ramp up on technical and execution strategy, you will partner with your manager and the team's PM to define the roadmap for our broader access control needs.

After 3 months, you will own execution, continue growing the team, and show progress on the more ambitious items on the roadmap. As your team ships, you will continue to evolve the norms and working agreements that keep it healthy and predictable.
