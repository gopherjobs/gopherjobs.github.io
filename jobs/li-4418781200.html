Description

We’re hiring a Technical Architect to lead the design and delivery of scalable,

secure embedded services across Sage’s platform. You’ll sit at the intersection of

engineering, architecture, and strategy - working with product, platform, and

partner teams to define solutions that are technically robust, cloud-native, and

aligned with our long-term vision.

💻/🏡 This is a hybrid role working 3 days a week in our Barcelona office.

You’ll be instrumental in helping Sage embed services into diverse ecosystems

by championing API-first thinking, cloud architecture best practices, and

scalable service design. This is a hands-on role ideal for someone who enjoys

both deep system-level work and strategic architectural leadership.

Meet the Team

You’ll report to a senior technology leader and work closely with solution

designers, product managers, cloud infrastructure specialists, and full-stack

engineering teams (primarily working in C# and React). You’ll also engage with

Sage Partners to ensure seamless third-party integrations.

How Success Will Be Measured

  • Delivery of scalable, secure, and maintainable service architectures
  • Alignment of solution designs with Sage’s enterprise architecture standards
  • Influence across engineering teams through architectural mentorship
  • Improvement in developer experience, service performance, and integration quality

Skills You’ll Gain

  • End-to-end architecture ownership in a large-scale cloud platform
  • Exposure to embedded services at enterprise level, used by external partners and internal teams
  • Broader impact through mentorship, code review, and technical leadership
  • Strategic engagement with senior stakeholders, balancing delivery and long-term vision

Snapshot of Your Day-to-Day

You’ll design core components of the Embedded Services Platform, guide

architecture decisions, write and review code (primarily in C#), and ensure

implementation quality across development teams. You’ll lead discussions with

product teams, participate in partner enablement, and champion architecture

governance while keeping a hands-on role.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in a Technical Architecture role, designing large-scale cloud-native enterprise systems
  • Proven ability to turn complex business needs into scalable technical solutions
  • Expertise in modern architectural styles: distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture, CQRS, Event Sourcing, CDC, etc.
  • Hands-on experience with C#, Java or a similar backend language
  • Strong background in designing REST APIs and data schemas at scale
  • Familiarity with public cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP), including container orchestration (e.g. Kubernetes, ECS, AKS, EKS)
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, including influencing across tech and non-tech teams
  • Experience providing mentorship and architectural guidance in agile product delivery settings
  • Fluency in English (Spanish is a plus)

Tech Stack You’ll Work With

  • Languages & Frameworks: C# (primary), .NET Core
  • Architectural Patterns: Event-Driven Architecture, CQRS, Clean
  • Architecture, API-first, Distributed Systems
  • Integration: REST APIs, Publish/Subscribe, Distributed Tracing
  • DevOps & Tooling: Git, CI/CD pipelines, code reviews, observability tooling
