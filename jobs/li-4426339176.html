Description

Overview We are searching for Full Stack Engineer (React, Java) for 18-month project to join us.

As part of our platform, we are developing a rewards system that encourages local shopping. Users will be able to collect points when visiting selected partner businesses and redeem them for discounts or benefits. This solution helps build lasting relationships between customers and local companies while also supporting the growth of the regional economy.

If modern technologies unleash your creativity and you thrive in an environment of learning and collaboration, this is the project for you.

  • Continuously optimize code quality, development processes, and system performance

  • Collaborate closely with teammates and contribute to shared technical knowledge
