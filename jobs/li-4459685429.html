Cloud Engineer

Location: Netherlands (hybrid)

Language: English-speaking environment

Are you a Cloud Engineer based in Europe and ready for your next career step?

Would you like to relocate to the Netherlands and design & implement secure, scalable GCP cloud infrastructure while accelerating your growth through structured, high-level training?

At Xccelerated , we are looking for Medior Cloud Engineers who want to work on real client projects, deepen their cloud and infrastructure expertise, and grow quickly in an international environment.

🔧 What you’ll work on

As a Cloud Engineer, you will focus on building and maintaining modern cloud platforms with a strong emphasis on infrastructure, automation, and scalability.

You will:

  • Design and implement secure, scalable GCP cloud infrastructure
  • Build and maintain infrastructure using Terraform (IaC) / Cloudformation
  • Set up and manage landing zones and cloud environments
  • Develop and optimize CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows
  • Work closely with engineering teams to support cloud-native applications
  • Apply software engineering practices (python)

👩 💻 Who we’re looking for

You are a Cloud Engineer who enjoys building scalable, secure cloud platforms and has a strong foundation in both infrastructure and software engineering. You like working hands-on, solving complex problems, and continuously improving systems through automation.

You bring:

  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Cloud, IT, or a related field
  • 4–5 years of experience in Cloud Engineering, Infrastructure, Platform Engineering, or DevOps
  • Experience in GCP (or AWS) cloud services
  • Solid experience with Infrastructure as Code , preferably Terraform
  • Experience designing and building scalable cloud infrastructure and landing zones
  • Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines , automation, and cloud-native development practices
  • Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Strong software engineering skills (preferably in Python or Go)
  • Understanding of cloud security, networking, and best practices
  • Willingness to relocate to the Netherlands and work in an international, English-speaking environment

🎁 What we offer

At Xccelerated, we offer more than a job — we offer a career accelerator in Cloud Engineering .

  • Competitive salary based on experience
  • 25 vacation days
  • Weekly technical training, mentoring, and innovation days
  • Company laptop and modern tooling
  • Great lunches, excellent coffee, and a well-stocked snack bar
  • Hybrid working model and an international team

🌍 About Xccelerated

Xccelerated (part of the Xebia Group ) is a tech accelerator focused on developing the next generation of Cloud, Data & AI professionals .

You’ll work four days per week on real client projects and spend one day per week on structured training, coaching, and innovation . After one year, you’ll have the opportunity to join the client organization directly .
