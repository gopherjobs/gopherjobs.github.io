ROLE PURPOSE Underwriting & Pricing decides which risks Homeprotect takes on and what we charge for them, one of the company's most strategically important and technically demanding domains, and one whose output, the price and terms of a quote, ripples through nearly every other part of the business: the journey that presents it to a customer, the policy created from it, and the numbers reported on afterwards.

We're looking for a Staff Engineer to shape the technical direction of this domain as its platform modernises, and to work across the boundary with the teams that depend on it, so pricing and underwriting changes can be made quickly, safely and with confidence across the business, not just within one team's codebase. At this level, you'll operate with a high degree of autonomy, defining technical direction rather than executing someone else's, and your influence will extend beyond a single team: you'll be the person other engineering teams turn to when a change touches pricing, quoting or underwriting logic.

Success in this role means Underwriting & Pricing's architecture and technical standards let the business change pricing and risk logic quickly and safely, and that other teams depending on the domain can build against it with confidence.

Key Responsibilities Technical Direction & Architecture

  • Define and own the technical direction and architecture for the Underwriting & Pricing domain: the pricing and rating engines, underwriting rules, risk appetite logic and quote calculation services.
  • Make architectural decisions that affect multiple teams or systems, and document them clearly enough that others can act on them independently.
  • Identify and drive the resolution of significant technical risk or complexity before it becomes a business problem, particularly around the accuracy and auditability of pricing and underwriting decisions.
  • Lead the technical design of complex, cross-team initiatives from first principles through to delivery.

Cross-Domain Technical Leadership

  • Act as the technical point of contact for other engineering teams whose systems depend on pricing, quoting or underwriting output.
  • Influence architecture and engineering standards beyond the immediate team, helping the wider engineering organisation make sound decisions wherever they touch this domain.
  • Represent Underwriting & Pricing in cross-team or organisation-wide technical discussions and decisions.

Product & Business Partnership

  • Partner with the domain's Product Manager, Engineering Manager, and Actuarial and Commercial colleagues, to translate pricing and risk strategy into a technical direction that can support it.
  • Help stakeholders understand technical trade-offs and constraints, and shape pragmatic solutions that balance commercial ambition with engineering rigour.

Mentoring & Continuous Improvement

  • Develop senior engineers and emerging technical leaders within the team.
  • Drive improvements to engineering practices, testing and operational rigour appropriate to a domain where correctness has a direct commercial consequence.
  • Contribute to Homeprotect's wider engineering standards and architecture as the organisation's product engineering model matures.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & EXPERIENCE Essential

  • Proven experience as a Staff Engineer or equivalent, owning technical direction across multiple teams, platforms or a significant business capability.
  • Strong track record of defining architecture and engineering standards independently, and communicating them clearly through documentation, design and discussion.
  • Experience influencing engineering decisions across teams without relying on formal authority.
  • Comfortable working with rules-heavy, calculation-intensive systems, where correctness, auditability and rigorous testing matter as much as delivery speed.
  • Strong hands-on experience in at least one modern backend language, such as C# or Java (or a comparable object-oriented language).
  • Deep, hands-on understanding of modern software engineering practices: continuous integration and deployment, automated testing, observability and cloud-native development.
  • Experience operating production systems, in a public cloud environment such as Azure or GCP, that need to be reliable, secure, performant and auditable.
  • Excellent communication skills, able to build trust and drive alignment with both engineers and non-technical stakeholders, including senior stakeholders.

Desirable

  • Experience in general insurance, financial services or another regulated industry.
  • Understanding of pricing, underwriting, actuarial or risk modelling concepts.
  • Familiarity with Python, useful for working closely with Actuarial and data science colleagues on pricing and risk models.
  • Experience building or evolving a platform or set of services consumed by multiple other engineering teams.
  • Exposure to event-driven or distributed systems.
  • Experience using AI-assisted engineering tools to improve engineering productivity.
