Description

Ilir Sela started Slice with the belief that local pizzerias deserve all of the advantages of major franchises without compromising their independence. Starting with his family’s pizzerias, we now empower over tens of thousands of restaurants with the technology, services, and collective power that owners need to better serve their digitally minded customers and build lasting businesses. We’re growing and adding more talent to help fulfil this valuable mission. That’s where you come in.

This position is open to applicants across all of the UK on a remote basis, with the physical office located in Belfast, Northern Ireland.

There are several openings within our Services team so we welcome applications from candidates seeking associate, intermediate or senior level positions.

What You'll Do As a Platform Engineer on the DevEx Deploy & Run team, you'll build and evolve the platform that powers engineering across Slice. You'll work closely with product engineering teams to improve developer experience while raising the bar for reliability, security, and operational excellence.

You Will

  • Build and maintain our Internal Developer Platform, making it easier for engineers to deploy, operate, and troubleshoot their services.
  • Create self-service tooling and automation that eliminate repetitive work and reduce operational overhead.
  • Design and implement GitOps workflows using ArgoCD to provide safe, repeatable, and scalable application delivery.
  • Improve our Kubernetes platform by developing reusable patterns, templates, and guardrails that make the right thing the easy thing.
  • Build internal applications and developer tooling in Python that simplify infrastructure management and operational workflows.
  • Enhance CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, improving deployment speed, consistency, and developer productivity.
  • Use generative AI to help build and maintain internal services, including agentic services
  • Improve observability by leveraging Datadog dashboards, metrics, logging, tracing, and alerting to help teams operate confidently in production.
  • Partner with product engineering teams to understand pain points and continuously improve the developer experience.
  • Reduce cognitive load for engineers by standardizing workflows, automating infrastructure operations, and providing well-documented platform capabilities.
  • Champion engineering best practices around reliability, security, automation, and platform adoption.

Our Technology Our platform is constantly evolving, but some of the technologies you'll work with include:

  • Kubernetes
  • ArgoCD & GitOps
  • Python
  • GitHub & GitHub Actions
  • Datadog
  • AWS
  • Generative AI (Claude Code & AI Agents)
  • Terraform
  • Docker

More important than experience with every individual tool is a passion for building platforms that make other engineers more productive.

Who You Are We're looking for engineers who enjoy building platforms rather than products—people who love automation, reducing complexity, and enabling others to move faster.

The Ideal Candidate Has Platform mindset You think in terms of reusable systems instead of one-off fixes. You enjoy creating platforms, guardrails, and automation that improve the experience of dozens or hundreds of engineers.

Strong Automation Skills You're comfortable writing production-quality software—particularly in Python—to automate infrastructure, build internal tooling, and eliminate manual processes.

Kubernetes Experience You have experience operating or building on Kubernetes and understand modern cloud-native deployment patterns.

GitOps & CI/CD You understand GitOps principles and have experience with tools like ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, or similar deployment and automation platforms.

Operational excellence You care deeply about reliability, observability, and operational simplicity. Monitoring, logging, metrics, and incident response are part of how you build software—not an afterthought.

Strong Communication Skills You are clear and concise in both written and oral communication whether it be providing status updates or defining requirements in RFC/PRD documents or ticketing system.

Collaborative enabler You enjoy partnering with engineering teams, understanding their pain points, and building solutions that improve their day-to-day experience.

Ownership mindset You take ownership of problems from discovery through implementation. You look beyond immediate fixes and seek scalable, long-term improvements.

Nice to have: Experience in product engineering. Having shipped customer-facing features yourself gives you firsthand empathy for the friction platform tooling can remove, and helps you design capabilities engineers actually want to adopt. What Success Looks Like In this role, success isn't measured by the number of customer-facing features you ship—it's measured by how much easier you've made it for every engineer at Slice to build, deploy, and operate software.

You'll Know You're Succeeding When

  • Deployments become faster and more reliable.
  • Engineers spend less time wrestling with infrastructure.
  • Platform capabilities are easy to discover and adopt.
  • Operational toil is replaced with automation.
  • Best practices become the default through well-designed guardrails.
  • Proficient in the use of generative AI tools.
  • Teams can confidently move from idea to production with minimal friction.
