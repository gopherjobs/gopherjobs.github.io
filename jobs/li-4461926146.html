About the Opportunity: In this role, you will join the team developing Ericsson's 5G Core portfolio, contributing to a carrier-grade, cloud-native, high-performance Packet Core network. This work supports communication providers in automating their networks and optimizing resources to deliver differentiated services for consumer and enterprise use cases.

What You Will Do:

  • Assist in the development and testing of high-quality microservices, following cloud-native architecture principles
  • Participate in Continuous Integration processes for our products
  • Contribute to design documentation, including component descriptions and use case modeling
  • Support troubleshooting efforts for issues assigned to your team
  • Actively participate in all Agile team activities: daily stand-ups, sprint planning, demos, feature grooming, and retrospectives

What You Will Bring:

  • Programming experience in Golang and/or C++
  • Familiarity with AI and automation concepts
  • Knowledge of integration technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, REST, Helm, Jenkins, Spinnaker, JSON, and GitLab
  • Familiarity with tools such as Git, Artifactory, Maven, Gradle, and databases (e.g., Neo4J)
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English

Eligibility requirements:

  • 2.7+ GPA or equivalent , Uploading an unofficial copy of your transcript
  • Actively enrolled in an accredited undergraduate, master’s, or PhD on a full-time basis at a four-year college or university at the time of the co-op
  • Be available to work 40 hours per week
