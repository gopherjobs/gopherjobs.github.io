Description

Company Description Space Inch is a digital product agency that builds thoughtful, well-crafted software products for US-based companies, focusing on scalable, long-term solutions. The team partners with a small number of clients at a time, emphasizing enduring relationships and products that evolve and stand the test of time. Founded in 2013 as a mobile gaming studio, Space Inch applies its experience in moving fast and making strong product decisions to all current work. The company maintains a people-first, product-driven mindset, prioritizing high-quality software and a collaborative team environment. As a remote-first organization with teams in Europe, the USA, and Latin America, Space Inch is engineering-driven, values ownership and trust, and aims to be a place where people can build lasting careers.

Role Description The Engineering Manager (Hands-On / Java) – Colombia is a full-time, remote role responsible for leading and mentoring a distributed engineering team while actively contributing to the Java codebase. This role oversees end-to-end software development, from technical design and implementation to integration and deployment, ensuring high-quality, maintainable solutions for client products. Day-to-day responsibilities include coordinating project work, facilitating technical decisions, conducting code reviews, and aligning engineering efforts with product and business priorities. The Engineering Manager collaborates closely with cross-functional partners, manages timelines and deliverables, and fosters a culture of ownership, continuous improvement, and strong engineering practices within the team.

Qualifications

  • Candidates should possess strong Engineering Management and Team Leadership skills to guide, mentor, and support a distributed development team.
  • Candidates should possess solid Software Development experience, with hands-on expertise in Java and modern backend engineering practices.
  • Candidates should possess effective Project Management abilities to plan work, manage timelines, and coordinate with cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Candidates should possess experience with Integration of systems and services, including APIs and third-party platforms.
  • Relevant skills and qualifications include experience in remote, international teams; familiarity with agile methodologies; strong communication and collaboration abilities; and a track record of delivering reliable, scalable software.
  • A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience, is preferred.
