NOCTRA · FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEER

Where our system meets the real world

------- Please read the full role description before applying. --------

LOCATION, PLEASE READ FIRST

This is a field facing role, so where you are based matters.

You must be based in Norway, ideally in the Oslo area and just outside it, which is where we are based. Trondheim and Tromsø, and their surrounding areas, are our other operating regions, and being based in or near any of these is a real advantage and, for this role, effectively a requirement.

These are the regions where our first pilots will happen, across the High North and the maritime domain, so you will install, integrate and support our systems on site there.

Given the defence context of the work, you should also be eligible for, and willing to undergo, Norwegian security clearance.

ABOUT NOCTRA

Noctra builds sovereign, AI native software for intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance in the European Arctic and the maritime domain. We are pre seed, based in Oslo, closing our first funding round now, and in conversation with a number of relevant actors, with our first pilots approaching.

Our platform, IRONLIGHT, takes in sensor and drone feeds, applies AI on the spot to build a clear operational picture, and runs fully offline in degraded environments. We are close to having a working system. What we need now is someone who can help take it out of the lab, keep developing it, and make it work and stay working where it actually matters.

THE ROLE

You are the person on the ground where our system meets the real world. When we run a pilot with a defence or maritime partner, you deploy the system into their environment, get it working with their sensors and their setup, keep it running, train their operators, and bring what you learn straight back to the engineering team.

This role is not only about deploying the system in the field. You will also help develop it further and maintain it as it goes into real operational use, so that what runs in front of an operator keeps improving and stays reliable.

The role calls for real experience with pilot projects: preparing them, executing them on site, and integrating our software into the systems a partner already has in place. You are half engineer and half field operator, and you are trusted with the relationship at the technical level.

What you would own:

Deploying and integrating our system at partner sites, including vessels and remote locations. Hooking it into the partner's existing sensors and networks, in degraded and offline conditions. Keeping it running in the field, troubleshooting on the spot, and handling the unexpected. Training operators and being their technical point of contact during the pilot. Capturing feedback and field data and feeding it straight back to the product team.

WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR

A strong systems and deployment engineer, comfortable with Linux, networking and real time systems. Someone with proven experience running and delivering pilot or field deployment projects end to end, and integrating software into existing operational systems. Calm and resourceful in the field, able to solve problems alone without a finished playbook. Customer facing and trusted, able to train operators and represent us on site. Comfortable with travel across the High North and the maritime domain, with demanding conditions, and with founder pace.

Backgrounds we especially value: defence, military, maritime, aviation, or ISR field experience; hands on deployment and support of systems in the field rather than only in an office; and familiarity with sensors, real time pipelines, or computer vision.

HOW THIS ROLE CAN WORK

We want to make this work for the right person, so the role can be task based, part time, or full time, depending on what suits you and your situation. Tell us what you would prefer.

COMPENSATION

In the initial phase, compensation is equity only, as founding team equity with standard vesting. We cannot offer salary until the pre seed round closes. Once it does, this moves to a market salary. You will need to be in a position to carry this phase, and you will need to believe in what we are building.

WHAT THIS IS

Early stage, technically demanding, and close to real operations. High trust and high ownership. A chance to own how our system is delivered, developed and supported in the field from day one.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

A backlog driven engineering role with a finished structure waiting for you. A remote only desk job. A narrow, isolated coding position.

HOW TO APPLY

Send a short message, not a standard cover letter. Tell us which region in Norway you are based in, the most hands on field or deployment work you have done, and why this role fits you.
