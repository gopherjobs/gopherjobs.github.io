Senior Backend Engineer

Salary: £80,000 - £110,000 + Equity

Location: UK Remote (quarterly onsite week, expenses covered)

Positions: Multiple

We're working with a venture-backed software company operating at the intersection of AI, enterprise security and data governance. Their platform helps large organisations gain visibility and control over how emerging AI technologies are used across the business, enabling employees to innovate safely while ensuring sensitive information is protected. Following strong commercial growth and increasing demand from enterprise customers, they're expanding their engineering team and hiring multiple Backend Engineers.

With a highly technical culture and a team of around 40 people in the UK, this is an opportunity to join an engineering-led business where you'll have direct influence on product development, architecture and platform scalability.

Senior Backend Engineer - The Role

You'll be responsible for building and evolving the backend systems that power a modern SaaS platform, working across microservices, integrations, APIs and large-scale data processing. This is a hands-on role with significant ownership, where engineers are trusted to take features from design through to production.

Senior Backend Engineer - Requirements

  • 3-5+ years of commercial software engineering experience using Java and Spring Boot.
  • Experience building scalable backend services, APIs and distributed systems.
  • Strong understanding of event-driven architectures, asynchronous processing and microservices.
  • Experience working within AWS, Azure or GCP environments.
  • Ability to deliver reliable, well-engineered software within a fast-moving product environment.
  • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

Senior Backend Engineer - Nice to Have

  • Experience within cybersecurity, identity, governance or enterprise SaaS environments.
  • Knowledge of technologies such as OAuth, OIDC, SAML, SCIM, Entra ID or Okta.
  • Previous startup or scale-up experience.
  • An interest in AI and modern engineering tooling.

This is an excellent opportunity to join a rapidly growing security technology company operating at the forefront of AI adoption and enterprise security. You'll be working alongside a highly technical engineering leadership team, helping shape a product already gaining significant traction with enterprise customers, while benefiting from a flexible remote-first culture and meaningful equity participation.
