Overview

We are seeking a TRIPS Application QA Tester to support a large-scale Core Deposit Replacement program within the financial services industry. This role will focus on manual testing activities across critical banking and payment processing applications, ensuring quality throughout the software delivery lifecycle.

The ideal candidate has strong experience with manual testing, test management tools such as Tosca, qTest, Zephyr, or Xray , and experience supporting financial services platforms. Experience with TRIPS, payment processing, or treasury management applications is highly preferred.

Responsibilities

  • Create and execute test plans, test cases, and test scripts.
  • Lead manual testing efforts across assigned workstreams.
  • Track, document, and manage defects through resolution.
  • Coordinate testing activities with business, technology, and project teams.
  • Support system, integration, regression, and user acceptance testing.
  • Validate business requirements and ensure adequate test coverage.
  • Communicate testing status, risks, and issues to stakeholders.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of software testing or quality assurance experience.
  • Hands-on experience with Tosca and qTest or similar tools such as Zephyr or Xray .
  • Strong experience with manual testing methodologies and defect management.
  • Experience working on large-scale enterprise technology initiatives.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with the TRIPS application .
  • Payment processing, treasury management, or banking application experience.
  • Financial services industry experience.
  • Experience supporting core banking modernization or transformation programs.
  • Experience working in Agile environments.

Key Skills

Manual Testing, QA Testing, Tosca, qTest, Zephyr, Xray, Test Planning, Defect Tracking, UAT, SIT, Regression Testing, Financial Services, Payment Processing, Treasury Management, Agile Delivery.
