  • Build integrations with platforms such as Okta, SentinelOne, Zscaler, and other security technologies through REST APIs.

  • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and embed security best practices throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • Collaborate with Cybersecurity, Infrastructure, and Data Science teams to develop new automation solutions.
  • Ensure applications comply with secure development, auditing, logging, and data protection standards.
  • Participate in innovation initiatives that accelerate the responsible adoption of Artificial Intelligence across NOV.
