Company Description

OZAV is a full-stack Banking as a Service platform for stablecoins, enabling companies to launch and operate financial products without needing licenses, banking partners, or blockchain expertise. Through a single composable API, OZAV offers accounts and payments, branded Visa cards, embedded multi-chain wallets with custody, and stablecoin on/off ramps connected to local payment rails in LATAM.

The platform also supports cross-border crypto processing and embeds KYC, KYB, AML, and compliance directly into transaction flows.

Companies use OZAV to manage global cash flow, issue payouts, access liquidity, reconcile international revenue, and hold stablecoins for treasury and settlement. Built for fintechs and other innovators connecting LATAM to the world, OZAV powers both human-driven and AI-driven financial operations with programmable, neutral infrastructure.

Role Description As a Software Engineer at OZAV, you will design, build, and maintain end-to-end features across our stack.

You will collaborate closely with product, design, and compliance teams to translate business and regulatory requirements into secure, scalable APIs and user experiences. Daily work includes implementing new functionality, refactoring and optimizing existing code, writing automated tests, and reviewing peer code to maintain quality and reliability.

You will help integrate third-party services, improve performance of mission-critical payments and wallet flows, and contribute to architectural decisions for a distributed, high-availability system. This is a full-time, hybrid role based in São Paulo, SP, with a mix of on-site collaboration and work-from-home flexibility.
