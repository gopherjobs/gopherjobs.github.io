About The Client A major Brazilian financial institution specializing in investment banking, wealth management, and corporate lending. The customer develops secure digital banking platforms and financial solutions to serve a diverse global client base.

What You’ll Do

  • Design, develop, and implement prioritized backend features using Java and Quarkus.
  • Apply engineering best practices to maintain technical and functional quality throughout the development lifecycle.
  • Build solutions that support flexibility, scalability, availability, resilience, and reliability.
  • Contribute to the definition and evolution of microservices and event-driven architectures.
  • Participate in technical, architectural, and product-evolution discussions.
  • Provide technical analysis and recommendations to support architectural decisions.
  • Design and implement integrations between internal and external services using REST APIs, messaging platforms, and event hubs.
  • Develop and maintain distributed applications in public cloud environments, primarily AWS.
  • Create and maintain unit, service, contract, and integration tests.
  • Contribute to technical standards, engineering best practices, and the continuous improvement of development processes.
  • Support observability through effective logging, health checks, metrics, monitoring, and alerting.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams and clients to solve complex technical problems.
  • Participate in Agile and DevOps delivery practices.
  • Share knowledge and provide technical guidance and mentorship to less experienced team members.

What You Bring

  • Senior-level experience in developing web applications and microservices using Java.
  • Strong proven experience with Java 8 through Java 21.
  • Hands-on experience developing applications using Quarkus.
  • Proven experience in developing and deploying applications in public cloud environments, preferably AWS.
  • Strong understanding of microservices and event-driven architecture patterns.
  • Experience designing and developing distributed applications and service integrations.
  • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and orchestration.
  • Experience creating unit, service, contract, and integration tests using tools such as JUnit and Postman/Newman.
  • Experience with relational and non-relational databases, including MySQL/MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Amazon RDS, or DynamoDB.
  • Experience with caching and data-indexing solutions.
  • Experience with messaging and event platforms such as AWS SQS, AWS SNS, RabbitMQ, and Kafka.
  • Experience implementing observability practices, including logging, health checks, metrics, and monitoring.
  • Hands-on experience with monitoring tools such as Datadog.
  • Experience with Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery pipelines using tools such as Jenkins.
  • Strong knowledge of software engineering and design best practices, including Design Patterns, SOLID, Clean Code, and 12-Factor App principles.
  • Experience with serverless services, particularly AWS Lambda.
  • Experience working in Agile and DevOps environments.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
  • The ability to collaborate effectively with technical, product, and client stakeholders.
