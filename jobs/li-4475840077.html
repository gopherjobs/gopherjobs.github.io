The Role SimplePractice is hiring for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our team in a remote role. The ideal candidate will have expert-level knowledge in Ruby on Rails and extensive backend development experience. This role will be instrumental in implementing a wide variety of planned features and service enhancements to support our growing platform.

As one of the first hires in Mexico, you will play a pivotal role in helping us build a team of software developers who are passionate about what they do.
