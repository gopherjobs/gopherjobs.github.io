About Us We are the independent expert in assurance and risk management. Driven by our purpose, to safeguard life, property, and the environment, we empower our customers and their stakeholders with facts and reliable insights so that critical decisions can be made with confidence.

As a trusted voice for many of the world’s most successful organizations, we use our knowledge to advance safety and performance, set industry benchmarks, and inspire and invent solutions to tackle global transformations.

About The Role DNV is seeking two fixed-term , senior level Full Stack Developers based in Mexico to accelerate development of the next generation of Cascade Facility Ratings (CFR), a business application supporting facility-rating workflows. You will work across a modern web application, delivering backend services, user-facing features, and integrations as part of a distributed product team.

The work will contribute to a demo release and an initial production-grade release. Our teams are small, collaborative, and quality-focused, so your technical judgment, communication, and contribution will matter from the start.

  • Core contributor developing and maintaining backend services and APIs using C# and ASP.NET Core.
  • Create and extend web user interfaces using Vue framework, HTML, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript/Typescript.
  • Translate product requirements into maintainable domain, application, persistence, API, and UI changes in a layered Clean Architecture solution.
  • Integrate frontend and backend services, including REST APIs, OpenAPI-based contracts, and generated TypeScript clients.
  • Design, write, and maintain automated tests, including unit tests; contribute to integration, UI, and end-to-end coverage as appropriate.
  • Apply secure coding practices throughout design, implementation, peer review, and defect remediation.
  • Use AI-assisted development tools thoughtfully to improve delivery effectiveness while validating outputs, protecting sensitive information, and maintaining engineering accountability.
