Description

Zatlas is the payments automation platform for hospitality. We sit between hotels, their OTA partners and their acquirers, and recover the revenue that falls through the cracks. We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer in São Paulo to build the payment and integration systems that hotels rely on to collect their revenue.

Our stack

Backend: Kotlin and Java with Spring

Database: PostgreSQL

Cloud: AWS

Observability: Datadog

Frontend: React (fullstack experience is a plus, not required)

Tooling AI-assisted development (e.g., Claude Code) is part of our daily workflow

Kotlin is not required: strong Java engineers pick it up fast.

Why this role, and why now

Zatlas recovers the revenue that falls through the cracks between hotels, their OTA partners and their acquirers: virtual credit cards (VCCs) that never get charged, cancellation and no-show fees that go uncollected, and payment methods that turn out to be invalid. Our clients include some of the largest hotel groups in Latin America, and we are expanding globally.

We are backed by Kaszek and Picus Capital and have grown 5x year over year. You will join as a senior individual contributor who owns backend systems end to end, from design to production. You are not tied to one squad: you will work across Integrations and Finance as priorities shift, in a small, all-senior team where every person has outsized impact.

What you will own

  • Payments and financial flows

Design, build and run scalable, resilient backend services that move real money for our hotel clients.

Correctness in financial flows: charging, routing across acquirers, reconciliation, settlements and disputes.

  • Partner integrations

Build and maintain integrations with external partners: OTAs, channel managers, PMSs, acquirers and payment processors.

Work directly with partners' technical teams to get integrations live and keep them healthy.

  • Architecture and engineering quality

Take part in architecture decisions, choosing the simple, boring solution first and adding complexity only when the problem demands it.

Keep code quality, performance, security and observability high, including on-call ownership of what you ship.

  • Product and team

Work with Product, Operations and Account Management to understand the hotel's problem before writing code.

Mentor teammates and help raise the engineering bar as the team grows.

Who we are looking for

We hire senior engineers who ship pragmatically and own outcomes, not just tickets.

MUST HAVE

6+ years building backend systems in production, currently at a senior level.

Solid experience with Java or Kotlin on the JVM, ideally with Spring, and with relational databases (PostgreSQL or similar).

Strong CS fundamentals: data structures, algorithmic complexity and distributed systems design.

Hands-on experience with integrations with external partners (APIs, webhooks, async messaging) and with the failure modes that come with them.

Pragmatism. You pick the simplest solution that works, avoid over-engineering, and ship within a timebox.

Ownership. You have taken something from zero to production and been accountable for it.

Clear, concise communication, in writing and in conversation, including disagreeing with a real argument.

Advanced or fluent English.

STRONG PLUS

Payments, fintech or high-scale platforms (e.g., Mercado Livre / Mercado Pago, Nubank, Stone, PagBank, PicPay, iFood).

Growth inside the same company (promotions, broader scope) and tenure that shows commitment.

Startups or tech-driven companies where you built before you scaled.

Intermediate Spanish (many of our clients are in Mexico).

Interest in growing into technical leadership over time.

Frontend experience with React.

PROBABLY NOT FOR YOU IF

You want to stay in one narrow area. Here you move across Integrations and Finance as priorities shift.

You reach for the most sophisticated architecture first, rather than the simplest thing that works.

You prefer to hand off what you ship rather than own it in production, on-call included.

You are looking for a fully remote role. We are together in the office every Tuesday and Thursday.

How we work

We work hard and keep it sustainable: focused days, rare weekends, and 2-month OKR cycles that make priorities and impact visible. The engineering team is flat and all-senior, with direct access to the founders. We pick the simple, boring solution first, ship within a timebox, and own what we build all the way to production.
