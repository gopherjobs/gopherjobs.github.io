Description

We're partnering with an innovative software company whose platform supports thousands of users worldwide in managing complex business processes, customer operations, and data-driven decision making. With continued growth and ambitious product plans ahead, they're looking to strengthen their engineering team with the addition of a Full-Stack Software Engineer.

The Role

As part of a highly collaborative and distributed engineering team, you'll play a key role in developing new features, improving existing functionality, and helping to scale a platform relied upon by businesses across the globe.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain modern web applications.
  • Develop and enhance backend services, APIs, and integrations.
  • Deliver high-quality frontend experiences with a focus on usability and performance.
  • Work closely with Product, Design, QA, and fellow Engineers throughout the development lifecycle.
  • Contribute to technical discussions, system design, and architectural improvements.
  • Continuously improve application reliability, scalability, and performance.

Technology

  • TypeScript
  • Vue.js
  • Node.js
  • MongoDB
  • Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Storybook
  • BigQuery
  • Firebase

About You

You'll ideally have:

  • Strong commercial experience with JavaScript and TypeScript.
  • Experience working with modern frontend frameworks such as Vue.js or React.
  • Solid backend development skills using Node.js.
  • Experience designing and building APIs.
  • A track record of developing scalable, maintainable software.
  • A proactive approach to problem-solving and continuous improvement.

What's In It For You?

  • Fully remote working environment.
  • A genuinely collaborative international team.
  • The opportunity to take ownership and influence technical direction.
  • Exposure to modern cloud-native technologies and large-scale systems.
  • The chance to work on a product making a real impact for customers globally.

If you enjoy tackling complex technical challenges, working with modern technologies, and building products that are used at scale, we'd love to hear from you.
