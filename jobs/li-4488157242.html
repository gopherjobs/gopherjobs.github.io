Job Family: Research and Development

Job Function: Software Engineering

About The Role We are seeking a Senior Full-Stack Engineer with extensive experience in cloud-native microservices and modern frontend architectures to help build high-performance, AI-enabled Outbound Contact Center channels. In this role, you will lead the design and implementation of enterprise-scale systems using API-first design principles, AI SDLC practices, and distributed system patterns. You will work within an autonomous team covering end-to-end delivery—from architectural design to containerized deployment and production troubleshooting.

Key Responsibilities Architecture & Solutions Engineering

  • Core Domain Development: Architect and implement Predictive, Progressive, and Preview outbound systems using Go-based microservices and API-first design.
  • Management & Admin Interfaces: Develop configuration and administration management interfaces built on Node.js.
  • Frontend Engineering: Build responsive, high-performance web applications using JavaScript/TypeScript alongside React or Angular frameworks.
  • Backend Systems: Design, build, and deploy enterprise microservices capable of handling high TPS with ultra-low latency, high resiliency, and scalability.

Technical Leadership & Quality Focus

  • Domain SME: Serve as a domain Subject Matter Expert (SME), drive key architecture decisions, and perform rigorous code reviews across Agile/Scrum teams.
  • Quality & Performance: Enforce high quality standards through static analysis, load & performance profiling, and system tuning.
  • AI Integration: Utilize AI coding assistants (e.g., Codex, Cursor, Claude) and prompt engineering to optimize workflows across assessment, planning, code generation, testing, and deployment.

Operations & Observability

  • DevOps & Delivery: Operate within a self-sufficient team handling containerized deployments using Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm (Argo CD experience is a plus).
  • Observability & Incident Management: Debug complex production issues, conduct root-cause analysis (RCA), and maintain operational reliability using Grafana, Prometheus, and telemetry tools.

Preferred Candidate Profile

  • Education: B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech / M.C.A. in Computer Science or an equivalent technical field.
  • Total Experience: 12+ years of overall experience architecting, designing, developing, deploying, and troubleshooting cloud API-based microservices (Cloud Native & Hybrid) and frontends.
  • Backend Expertise: 10+ years of core backend engineering experience.
  • Full-Stack Expertise: 5+ years of full-stack web application development experience.
  • AI SDLC Practices: 1+ years utilizing AI Agents & Skills for planning, code generation, testing, deployment, and troubleshooting.

Technical Skills & Competencies

  • Backend: Strong proficiency in Go, Java, or Node.js.
  • Frontend: JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Angular, and SDKs.
  • Databases & Streaming: Kafka, Redis, MariaDB / MySQL.
  • Architecture: Microservices architecture, distributed systems design, and RESTful APIs.
  • Cloud & DevOps: Hands-on experience with Azure or GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, CI/CD pipelines, and continuous delivery patterns (Argo CD preferred).
  • Domain Expertise: Strong background in Telecommunications or Contact Centers (Outbound Contact Center background preferred).

Experience 12+ Years of Experience
