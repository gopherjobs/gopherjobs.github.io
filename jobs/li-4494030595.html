Description

About Verily Health Inc. At the intersection of technology, data science, and healthcare, Verily Health transforms complex health data into trusted intelligence, enabling health solutions that are more personal, predictive, and precise.

Verily’s flagship Governed Health Intelligence platform, Verily Pre, enables healthcare organizations to responsibly govern, model, and securely activate their protected health data at scale, deploying intelligence with the scientific rigor, oversight, and auditability needed for healthcare.

Description As a member of the Developer Platform (Foundations) team, you'll own the tools our engineers use every day: Okta, Opal, GitHub, Jira and Slack. You'll automate how they're run, keep them secure and compliant as we grow in regulated markets like FedRAMP, and make them easy to use. IT handles day-to-day identity operations, such as onboarding and offboarding. You'll own administration and new features.

Responsibilities

  • Own Okta administration and build new identity features: app integrations, policies, groups and federation.
  • Administer GitHub, Jira, Slack and Opal, including permissions, workflows, Actions runners and access requests.
  • Automate these tools with APIs and scripts, so changes are reviewed and auditable.
  • Run access reviews and gather audit evidence for FedRAMP, FISMA and SOC 2.
  • Work with IT on the ops-to-engineering handoff, and take escalations.
  • Write runbooks and design docs, and recommend when we should adopt or retire a tool.

Qualifications Minimum Qualifications

  • BA/BS degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent practical experience in software engineering and [4–6] years of experience in software, DevOps or platform engineering.
  • Hands-on Okta administration (SSO, SAML/OIDC, SCIM, groups and policies) or similar enterprise identity provider experience, GitHub organization administration, including writing and troubleshooting GitHub Actions.
  • Go or Python, and experience integrating tools through REST APIs.
  • Working knowledge of least privilege, access reviews and audit logging.
  • Clear written and oral communication, especially in runbooks and design documents.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Okta certification (Okta Certified Professional or Administrator)
  • Experience with Opal or another just-in-time access tool
  • Experience with Jira and Slack administration and automation
  • Experience with GCP and compliance frameworks such as FedRAMP, FISMA or SOC 2
  • Experience with Terraform and managing SaaS configuration as code
  • Experience with Atlantis or similar Terraform automation
