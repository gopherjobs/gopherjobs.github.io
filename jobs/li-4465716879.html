Description

DocuSketch is a leading software solution provider for the restoration, property insurance, and real estate industries in North America. Our AI-empowered digital platforms enable restoration companies, adjusters, and insurers to efficiently document, manage, and process property claims. Driven by a loyal customer base, DocuSketch has become one of the fastest-growing technology companies in the proptech sector.

Our headquarters are in Potsdam, Germany. While we have strong hubs in Germany and Serbia, our team is truly borderless, with employees distributed across Europe, the US, and Canada. We are currently undergoing an exciting transformation in how we build software. We aren't just adopting AI; we are building small, hyper-autonomous squads that design agentic workflows and human-in-the-loop systems to multiply their output, combining AI-orchestrated engineering with deep, foundational product expertise.

About the Role: We are looking for a product-minded builder, not just a ticket-taker.

As a Senior Full-Stack Engineer, you will be a core member of a small, fully autonomous, "mini-startup" squad. We believe that small, highly aligned teams build the best products. Your squad will own a specific product domain end-to-end—from brainstorming the initial user experience to fast-paced prototyping, shipping, and tackling complex production challenges.

You will leverage advanced AI agents daily to supercharge your output. By using AI to rapidly build prototypes, write boilerplate, and architect complex microservices (Java/Spring Boot) and user interfaces (Angular) all the way to our mobile application (KMP), you will free yourself up to focus on what really matters: solving real customer problems and delivering measurable business outcomes.

What You’ll Do

  • Own the Product Journey : Operate with a true builder mindset. Work tightly with your small squad to deeply understand customer pain points, prototype solutions, and ship high-value features end-to-end.
  • Full-Stack Execution: Architect and deliver robust experiences across the stack. You will build intuitive, user-centered frontend interfaces (Angular) and design scalable, reliable backend microservices (Java/Spring Boot).
  • Agent System Design & Evals: Design autonomous agent loops—managing tool permissions, failure recovery, and knowing when not to delegate to an agent. Build robust eval frameworks and measurement systems to systematically test, score, and distrust model outputs before they touch production.
  • Navigate & Modernize: Confidently dive into legacy codebases when necessary, debugging and modernizing infrastructure to ensure our core platform remains stable and performant as we scale.
  • Champion Quality & Collaboration: Act as a technical leader within your squad. Uphold rigorous engineering standards through thoughtful code reviews, automated testing, and a focus on long-term maintainability.

What You Bring The Builder Mindset:

  • Product Empathy: You care deeply about the user experience and the why behind the software you build. You measure success by user outcomes, not just lines of code deployed.
  • Small-Team Player: You thrive in tight-knit, highly autonomous, cross-functional squads where collaboration and shared ownership are key.
  • Startup Mentality & Collaboration: You move fast without working alone. You make your reasoning reviewable, pull in a second pair of eyes before expensive decisions, and treat your squad's outcome as your own. In a three-person squad, your judgment is a third of the team's judgment—we hire accordingly.
  • Outcome-Oriented: You focus on delivering measurable business value and are comfortable navigating ambiguity to find the right solution.

Technical & AI Foundations:

  • Proven Experience: 6+ years of software engineering experience with a strong track record of shipping full-stack products.
  • Backend & Frontend Mastery: Deep proficiency in Java/Spring Boot (microservices, RESTful APIs) and strong skills in Angular.
  • Agentic Execution & Evals: Practical experience orchestrating agents and designing feedback loops. You know how to build evals that hold non-deterministic code accountable, treating model outputs with healthy, systematic distrust.
  • Systems Thinking: Strong high-level architectural judgment. As agents take over raw code generation, your ability to reason about system boundaries, state management, edge cases, and long-term technical direction is what actually scales.
  • Modern Engineering: Deep experience with CI/CD, automated testing, and agile methodologies.
  • Language: Excellent communication skills in English to collaborate seamlessly across our international teams.

Nice to Have

  • Mobile Experience: Knowledge of Kotlin or cross-platform mobile development.
  • Domain Expertise: Background in InsurTech, PropTech, or property restoration.

Why Join DocuSketch?

  • Build the Future of Engineering: Don’t just use AI—define how it’s used to build products. You’ll be at the forefront of the next era of AI-orchestrated software development.
  • Small Squads, Massive Impact: Your work won't get lost in corporate bureaucracy. You will operate in a small, fast-moving squad that ships high-value features directly impacting thousands of professionals across North America.
  • Startup Speed, Market Leader Stability: Enjoy the high-energy, fast-prototyping environment of a startup, backed by the financial security of a profitable, industry-leading PropTech company.
  • True Flexibility: Work from where you’re most productive. We offer fully remote options across Europe (with hubs in Germany and Serbia) and a diverse, international culture.
