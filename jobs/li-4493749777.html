Description

About Sharesource: We are a BCorp company whose purpose is to democratise opportunities for individuals from developing countries. With our brand and culture empowering our partners to attract and keep the most qualified members, we share the knowledge to unleash potential and grow scaling teams for Australian and Canadian businesses.

https://www.sharesource.com.au/

What are we looking for? We are looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer based in the Philippines. Our engineers are spread across Australia, the Philippines, and the USA, but we work as one team: we share the same goals, the same codebase, and the same ways of working, and everyone has a voice wherever they are. You'll take features from idea to production across our whole platform, the React front end, the Python API behind it, and the AWS services it runs on. Working in a product-led team alongside product managers, you'll help shape what we build as well as build it.

Our software development lifecycle is agent-led. You'll work day to day with AI coding agents such as Claude Code, which shifts more of your effort earlier in the process, more time thinking through the problem and system design, and less time writing routine boilerplate. In practice, that means better architecture, faster delivery, and more thorough automated testing and deployment.

What are you expected to do?

  • Build features end to end using React and TypeScript, Python and FastAPI, and PostgreSQL and PostGIS on AWS.
  • Design clear REST APIs, including APIs that customers and partners integrate with.
  • Write automated front-end and back-end tests with every change and ship through CI/CD.
  • Work in an agile, product-led team alongside product managers.
  • Help shape how the team works, designs, and builds by bringing ideas, challenging existing practices, and helping grow the team's capability. Contribute to how the team works rather than simply delivering assigned tasks.
  • Use AI coding agents such as Claude Code to design, build, and test faster.
  • Communicate clearly in fluent English with colleagues across Australia, the Philippines, and the USA.

You'll be a great fit if:

  • You have 5+ years of production experience building full-stack web applications using JavaScript/TypeScript (ReactJS) on the frontend and Python (FastAPI) on the backend.
  • You have experience with micro-frontends built with React and TypeScript.
  • You have experience with Python and FastAPI on AWS, with PostgreSQL and PostGIS, using API Gateway, Lambda, ECS Fargate, S3, CloudFront, SNS/SQS, EventBridge, and RDS.
  • You have an understanding of event-driven architecture built on Kafka, Lambda, and containers.
  • You are familiar with testing and observability tools such as Jest, React Testing Library, Playwright, pytest, and Datadog.
  • You have exposure to Databricks lakehouse, model serving, and MLflow, using foundation models from Anthropic, Google, and others.

We will be absolutely thrilled if you also have the following:

  • You have experience integrating systems and products through AI, including LLM APIs, agents, and MCP.
  • You have experience with event-driven architectures using Kafka.
  • You have experience with infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform and Serverless Framework.
  • You have experience with GIS and mapping technologies such as PostGIS, Mapbox, and ArcGIS.
  • You have experience with Databricks or Datadog.
