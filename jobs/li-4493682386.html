Job description:

About the role

We’re looking for an experienced Program Engineering Lead for a long-term, full-time remote engagement (6+ months) on a large enterprise transformation programme.

You’ll own the engineering system that turns approved designs and architecture into secure, testable, deployable software. You’ll set engineering standards and controls across multiple delivery squads, strengthen technical execution, and make sure engineering evidence supports every release decision.

Key responsibilities

  • Own the programme engineering operating model, standards and ways of working across all delivery squads
  • Drive engineering execution from approved design through build, integration, deployment and operational evidence
  • Establish and govern SDLC / DevSecOps controls: source control, code review, CI/CD, environments, security, deployment, observability and rollback
  • Drive adoption of BDD, Definition of Ready and Definition of Done as engineering controls, working with Product, Architecture and Testing
  • Own technical delivery readiness, identifying capability, environment, tooling, automation and integration gaps early
  • Coordinate Technical Leads and engineering communities so decisions and patterns are consistent across squads
  • Drive quality at source: code quality, automated unit/component/contract/integration testing and technical debt visibility
  • Manage cross-squad engineering and integration risks in partnership with Architecture
  • Improve productivity using reliable metrics: flow, automation, build/deployment health and defect/rework signals
  • Industrialise AI-assisted engineering practices while keeping human approvals and engineering controls in place

Key deliverables

  • Programme engineering standards and playbook
  • SDLC / DevSecOps control model
  • Engineering readiness view across squads
  • CI/CD, environments and tooling readiness
  • Engineering quality and automation evidence
  • Cross-squad integration risk view
  • Engineering metrics and improvement backlog
  • Technical release-readiness input

Requirements

  • 10+ years leading engineering delivery within large, complex enterprise programmes
  • Proven responsibility across 5–7 concurrent engineering workstreams involving complex integrations, APIs, data dependencies, shared platforms, legacy systems and coordinated releases
  • Deep, current knowledge of modern SDLC, DevSecOps, CI/CD, automated testing and engineering quality controls
  • Strong practical understanding of AI-assisted and agentic engineering, including eval-driven testing, guardrails, observability and human approval controls
  • Ability to translate end-to-end architecture and cross-system dependencies into executable plans, integration milestones and evidence-based release decisions
  • Fluent English; strong stakeholder communication with architects, security, platform/SRE and delivery leads

Working hours & location

  • Candidates based in Latin America (e.g. Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Peru) are preferred, for overlap with US time zones
  • Candidates in Poland or Ukraine are welcome if they can work until 10–11 PM local time

What we offer

  • Long-term, full-time remote contract (6+ months)
  • Senior ownership of engineering across a large-scale programme
