About the Opportunity

On behalf of our client, a technology company specializing in cloud computing, GPU-powered applications, and real-time streaming technologies, Artifact Systems is seeking a Full Stack Software Engineer to join our client's engineering team.

Our client develops innovative solutions that enable users to access high-performance computing resources and run demanding applications directly through their browsers, without being limited by their local hardware.

We are looking for an experienced software engineer who enjoys building products end to end, understands software architecture, and is comfortable working across different layers of complex systems.

This is an opportunity to contribute to technically challenging products where performance, scalability, and user experience matter.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs.
  • Build and enhance modern frontend applications using JavaScript and React.
  • Contribute to software architecture and system design decisions.
  • Develop and improve cloud-based services, including machine lifecycle management and session orchestration.
  • Work on browser-based application streaming experiences and related integrations.
  • Collaborate with the engineering team to improve system performance, reliability, and scalability.
  • Take ownership of features throughout the software development lifecycle, from design to production.
  • Investigate and resolve complex technical issues across different layers of the application.
  • Contribute to code quality, technical improvements, and engineering best practices.

Requirements

  • 4+ years of professional experience developing production web services and frontend applications.
  • Strong hands-on backend development experience with Ruby or Python (at least one is required).
  • Strong proficiency in JavaScript and React.
  • Solid understanding of software architecture, system design, and scalable application development.
  • Ability to understand how backend services, frontend applications, databases, and infrastructure components interact.
  • Experience designing, developing, and integrating APIs.
  • Familiarity with Linux environments, deployment processes, and production systems.
  • Ability to analyze and troubleshoot complex technical problems across different layers of an application.
  • Strong problem-solving skills, technical ownership, and an end-to-end engineering mindset.
  • Good command of written and spoken English.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively within a dynamic engineering team.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with AWS, GCP, or other cloud platforms.
  • Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD processes.
  • Experience with C++, Rust, or Go.
  • Knowledge of WebRTC, video encoding, or real-time streaming technologies.
  • Familiarity with GPU-based applications or remote desktop technologies.
  • Experience with OpenTelemetry.
  • Interest in networking, performance optimization, and low-latency systems.

Why This Opportunity?

  • Work on innovative products at the intersection of cloud computing, GPU technologies, and real-time streaming.
  • Contribute to meaningful software architecture and engineering decisions.
  • Solve complex technical challenges across the full technology stack.
  • Collaborate with an experienced engineering team and take ownership of impactful features.
  • Help build scalable products used in performance-intensive computing environments.
