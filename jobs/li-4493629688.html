About The Role We are looking for a Senior Agentic AI Engineer with extensive experience designing, developing, and deploying production-ready AI agents and multi-agent architectures.

You will play a key role in building intelligent systems that combine LLMs, advanced reasoning, model orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and engineering intelligence.

The ideal candidate has a strong software engineering background, hands-on experience with LangGraph, agent communication protocols, model training, and MLOps, and the ability to transform complex technical requirements into robust AI solutions.

Responsibilities Agentic AI Architecture & Multi-Agent Systems

  • Design, develop, and deploy multi-agent orchestration architectures using LangGraph, including planning, routing, reasoning loops, state management, and human-in-the-loop approvals.
  • Build Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) workflows to coordinate specialized agents and improve decision-making.
  • Implement Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and Mixture-of-Thoughts (MoT) strategies to optimize model performance, latency, and cost.
  • Develop secure agent interoperability using MCP, A2A, and ACP protocols.
  • Implement agent memory, context management, tool calling, approval workflows, and traceability.

LLMs, RAG & Model Development

  • Build advanced RAG pipelines, including document extraction, embeddings, vectorization, and hybrid search.
  • Work with vector databases such as pgvector, Qdrant, Weaviate, or Milvus.
  • Fine-tune and deploy open-weight language models using LoRA, QLoRA, PyTorch, and distributed training frameworks.
  • Develop and optimize Small Language Models (SLMs) for efficient cloud, on-premise, and edge deployment.
  • Apply model training techniques including supervised fine-tuning, RLHF, DPO, distillation, and quantization.
  • Explore multimodal AI solutions combining text, images, 3D data, and engineering documentation.

Engineering Intelligence & Simulation

  • Integrate AI agents with CAD/BIM data, digital twins, and simulation environments.
  • Develop hybrid AI solutions combining rules-based reasoning and machine learning.
  • Implement optimization algorithms, graph search, constraint solving, and explainable decision-making.
  • Explore world foundation models, including NVIDIA Cosmos, for simulation, synthetic data generation, and physical reasoning.

MLOps, AgentOps & Deployment

  • Build and maintain scalable MLOps and AgentOps pipelines, including CI/CD, versioning, monitoring, and model evaluation.
  • Implement observability and tracing using LangSmith, Langfuse, or similar tools.
  • Establish AI guardrails, reliability mechanisms, and performance evaluation frameworks.
  • Deploy secure AI solutions using Docker, Kubernetes, Linux, GPU cloud infrastructure, and private on-premise environments.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary engineering teams to deliver reliable, production-ready AI solutions.
