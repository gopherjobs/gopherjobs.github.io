About The Role As a Senior Software Development Engineer, you will design and lead robust APIs, data models, and background services while contributing to modern, user-friendly web interfaces. You will own features end to end — from design through launch—working closely with peers and cross-functional partners to clarify requirements, assess risks, and make trade offs that support long-term quality.

Responsibilities:

  • Design and deliver scalable, high-quality technical solutions aligned with business and product needs.
  • Apply advanced technical expertise to solve complex engineering problems and improve system performance, reliability, and maintainability.
  • Contribute to technical architecture by evaluating design options, defining technical approaches, and supporting long-term product direction.
  • Lead complex technical initiatives from planning through implementation, deployment, and ongoing support.
  • Identify opportunities to modernize systems, improve engineering practices, and introduce innovative technical solutions.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to clarify requirements, assess feasibility, and ensure successful delivery.
  • Provide technical guidance, code reviews, and mentorship to strengthen engineering quality and team capability.
  • Proactively identify technical risks, dependencies, and implementation challenges, and drive effective mitigation plans.
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and recommend practical improvements that enhance product quality and team efficiency.
  • Other duties which are assigned by the Employer or authorized person of the Employer.

  • 6+ years of experience in software development engineering.

Our Approach to Flexible Work With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote.
