Building the conversation between investors and companies

More than 200 investor meetings a year — and at many listed companies, they are still being scheduled over email.

Shared Research produces equity research on Japanese companies for institutional investors in Japan and abroad, and builds IR One, an IR communication platform. What we make is software that removes the friction sitting between listed companies and their investors, one piece at a time.

We're looking for a Web Application Engineer to build it with us.

Why we're hiring: writing code got faster. The bottleneck moved downstream.

AI has made code noticeably faster to produce. That's true of our own work — implementation takes a fraction of the time it used to, and the range of what we can build has widened.

What hasn't changed is the step where someone decides "this is ready to ship." That still depends on human judgment. To be straightforward about it: our bottleneck right now is code review.

So we are looking less for someone who writes quickly, and more for someone who reads deeply.

This role comes with a lot of autonomy — which is exactly why we need someone we can hand things to.

What you'll be working on

  • IR One — an IR communication platform that handles meeting scheduling, dialogue and information sharing between listed companies and institutional investors, end to end
  • Research report editing system — the core system our analysts use to produce and publish research
  • Anonima — an anonymous communication service for corporate clients
