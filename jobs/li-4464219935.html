Are you ready to make your mark? About The Role In this role, you will design and build the backend services and capabilities that enable users to create, configure, test and deploy.

You will work closely with frontend engineers, AI/ML engineers, product managers, UX

designers, and platform teams to bring Agent Studio capabilities from concept through

production. You will develop the services behind agent configuration, orchestration, integrations,

testing, deployment, and lifecycle management while ensuring the platform meets enterprise

expectations for performance, reliability, security, and scalability.

This is a great opportunity for an experienced backend engineer who enjoys solving complex

technical problems, building extensible products, and creating intuitive ways for users.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs
  • Build capabilities that enable users to create, configure, test and deploy.
  • Build APIs and service interfaces that enable intuitive user experiences and reliable interaction between frontend and backend services.
  • Develop mechanisms for managing configurations, versions, state, context, tools, permissions, and other runtime components.
  • Build services that enable to securely interact with Intermedia products, enterprise

applications, APIs, data sources, and external services.

  • Collaborate closely with frontend engineers and UX teams to translate complex capabilities into intuitive product experiences.
  • Work with product managers to translate customer needs and requirements into scalable technical solutions.
  • Contribute to technical design decisions for agent orchestration, configuration

management, workflow execution, integrations, and supporting backend services.

  • Build capabilities that allow users to test, evaluate, troubleshoot, and refine before and after deployment.
  • Develop reliable mechanisms for handling execution, errors, retries, timeouts, and other runtime conditions.
  • Design appropriate controls for authentication, authorization, permissions, and secure

access to tools, models, services, and data.

  • Design for scalability, reliability, performance, observability, and fault tolerance across services.
  • Write high-quality, readable, testable code that follows established engineering standards

and best practices.

  • Participate in code and design reviews and contribute to strong engineering and quality

practices across the team.

  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues across backend services, workflows and integrations.
  • Build monitoring, logging, testing, and observability into production services to support

reliable agent execution and product operations.

  • Collaborate with DevOps, and infrastructure teams to support reliable deployment and operation.
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and engineering approaches related backend engineering, and distributed systems.
