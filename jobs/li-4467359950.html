Qinematic Space is a dynamic and innovative company committed to revolutionizing the field of computer vision and human movement analysis. We focus on developing cutting-edge solutions that bridge technology with real-world applications to improve health and performance for individuals and organizations. Our small team thrives on collaboration, innovation, and a passion for transforming lives through technology. Join us in shaping the future of human motion analysis and healthtech/fitech.

Role Description

This is a contract, remote role for a Full Stack Engineer with experience and expertise in computer vision and skaletal tracking.

As a key member of our team, you will develop, test, and maintain both front-end and back-end components of our software solutions. You will collaborate with cross-disciplinary teams to integrate computer vision technology into our platforms, design scalable architectures, and ensure optimal performance. Additionally, you will contribute to improving user experiences and participate in product development cycles.

This job role requires a self-employed person or organisation to be registered in Sweden.

Business language is English. Developer languages are mostly C langauges and Vue.

This is a start-up venture, with all the ups and downs that come with being a start-up. So only people with a passion for impact through health technology should consider this role.
