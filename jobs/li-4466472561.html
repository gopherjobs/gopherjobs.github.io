Description

At HMS Industrial Networks, we value the dedicated contributions of our employees, and we recognize that it is our people that make HMS successful. We work hard to maintain our vibrant, innovative, and welcoming culture, and we strive to support our staff as they grow and develop both personally and professionally.

About The Team You'll join a dynamic agile software development team at HMS Delft, The Netherlands, where we develop innovative software solutions for industrial diagnostics and cloud products. Our mission is to design, implement, and maintain reliable software that helps customers monitor, diagnose, and optimize their industrial operations.

Working in an international environment, you will collaborate closely with colleagues across different HMS locations and product teams. Our team develops both embedded software for industrial devices for diagnostics and cloud-based solutions for remote access. As HMS continues to expand its Cloud platform, built on AWS and leveraging technologies such as Java, Spring, and Angular, you'll have the opportunity to contribute to software that connects industrial edge devices with modern cloud services.

Role And Responsibilities As a Software Engineer , you will contribute to the development of software for our industrial diagnostics products and cloud-connected solutions. You will be involved throughout the full software development lifecycle, from requirements analysis and implementation to testing and maintenance.

Your Responsibilities Include

  • Designing, developing, and maintaining embedded software for industrial diagnostics products.
  • Contributing to the development of cloud-based applications and services on the Ewon Cloud platform using Java, Spring, and Angular.
  • Implementing and executing unit, integration, and E2E tests to ensure software quality and reliability.
  • Collaborating with product owners, architects, and fellow engineers to define and deliver new features.
  • Writing clean, maintainable code following software engineering best practices.
  • Participating in code reviews and technical discussions to continuously improve the quality of our products.
  • Troubleshooting, debugging, and resolving issues across embedded and cloud-based systems.
  • Contributing to the design and evolution of software architectures for both embedded and cloud solutions.
  • Expanding the use of AI in our development processes.

About You You are a software engineer with a strong problem-solving mindset and a passion for developing reliable, high-quality software. You enjoy working on technical challenges across different domains and are motivated by delivering solutions that create value for customers.

You combine solid technical skills with a pragmatic and collaborative approach. You are eager to learn, take ownership of your work, and contribute to a team environment where knowledge sharing and continuous improvement are valued.

Only candidates currently based in the Netherlands will be considered. Required Experience

  • Bachelors or Masters degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electronics, or equivalent experience.
  • Several years of experience in software development.
  • Strong proficiency in Java development & in-depth experience with the Spring framework.
  • Knowledge of software design principles, testing methodologies, and debugging techniques.
  • Experience with version control systems such as Git, ideally with GitLab.
  • Familiarity with networking concepts and communication protocols.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative international environment.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Angular is a huge plus.
  • Experience with AI agentic workflows.
  • Knowledge of Cloud Infrastructure & Services (e.g., AWS concepts like EC2, S3, Docker, Kubernetes) to understand deployment environments.
  • Experience with testing frameworks (e.g. Robot for E2E testing, JUnit, Mockito for Java; Jasmine, Karma, Jest for Angular).
  • Experience with RabbitMQ or other messaging queues.
  • Experience developing REST APIs and distributed systems.
  • Familiarity with Linux-based development environments.
  • Experience with embedded software development using C++.
  • Understanding of software architecture and system design principles.
  • Knowledge of industrial communication protocols and connectivity solutions (Profinet, Ethernet/IP and Profibus).
