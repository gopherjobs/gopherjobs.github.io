About Us Sauce Labs is the world’s largest full-lifecycle, test automation platform, and the company behind Selenium. Trusted by 80% of the world’s top ten largest financial institutions and over 300,000 enterprise users, Sauce Labs provides the only AI platform capable of turning business intent into autonomous testing and quality assurance. With a proprietary dataset of 8.7 billion test runs, Sauce Labs empowers the Fortune 2000 to bridge the gap between AI-driven code generation and enterprise-grade software quality. Learn more at http://saucelabs.com.

Release Assurance at the Speed of AI | Meet the new Sauce Labs About The Team We’re a small advanced tech team that takes ideas from “someone had a hunch” to “customers are using it”—usually in days, not sprints. This is a high ownership team: talking to whoever has the problem, deciding what’s worth building, cutting scope, shipping, and then deciding whether to double down or move on.

Required Skills Deep fluency in at least one modern general-purpose language—and the ability to pick up others quickly. We work across TypeScript/Node, Go, and Python, and you’ll touch more than one. We care far more that you know one language inside out—its type system, concurrency model, testing story, etc.—than that you’ve used our specific stack. An ideal candidate would be comfortable:

  • Reading and modifying an unfamiliar codebase without a guided tour
  • Writing and maintaining tests that hold up in CI, not just on your laptop
  • Publishing and versioning something other people depend on — a CLI, an SDK, a package
  • Debugging across a boundary you don’t own (a third-party API, someone else’s service, a CI runner)
