About The Role Software Developer - 8 month FTC Hybrid working - Andover office At Simplyhealth, our purpose is to improve access to healthcare for all in the UK. As a certified B Corp with more than 150 years of heritage, we're committed to making healthcare more affordable, accessible and inclusive for everyone.

We’re looking for a Software Developer to join our Tech community and play a key role in designing, building, testing and maintaining high-quality software products that support our customers, colleagues, and business goals. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on delivering solutions, working collaboratively as part of a cross-functional delivery team.

Your Responsibilities Will Be Software Delivery

  • Design, build, test, and maintain software solutions that meet customer, colleague, and business needs.
  • Translate user stories and acceptance criteria into clear technical tasks and deliverable solutions.
  • Break down work, assess technical feasibility, and provide realistic estimates.
  • Produce well-structured, reliable, secure, and maintainable code.

Quality & Technical Standards

  • Ensure code is well tested, using test-first approaches where appropriate.
  • Contribute to code reviews, pairing, and constructive technical feedback.
  • Consider performance, security, scalability, and maintainability as part of day-to-day development.
  • Apply engineering best practices and follow agreed technology standards.

Collaboration & Ownership

  • Work closely with developers, quality engineers, product managers, tech leads, platform engineers, and stakeholders to deliver fit-for-purpose solutions.
  • Communicate progress, risks, blockers, and technical decisions clearly and early.
  • Take ownership of work from initial understanding through to delivery.
  • Work collaboratively, supporting shared ownership of outcomes rather than working in isolation.
  • Show curiosity and willingness to learn when working with unfamiliar technologies, tools, or parts of the system.
  • Look beyond assigned tasks to identify gaps, risks, dependencies, and opportunities to support the team or improve delivery.
  • Take a thoughtful problem-solving approach, using documentation, team knowledge, engineering judgement, and appropriate tools to understand problems and shape practical outcomes.

Coaching & Continuous Improvement

  • Support other developers through coaching, pairing, code reviews, and knowledge sharing.
  • Share learning and good practice within the team and wider engineering community.
  • Keep up to date with relevant tools, technologies, and engineering approaches.
  • Look for opportunities to improve engineering quality, team processes, and delivery effectiveness.

About You Essential requirements:

  • Strong experience designing, building, testing, and maintaining software in a professional engineering environment.
  • Hands-on experience with backend development using Go or a similar modern programming language.
  • Experience with frontend development using React, Next.js, TypeScript, or similar technologies.
  • Experience building and consuming APIs and working with service-based architectures.
  • Strong experience writing automated tests as part of day-to-day development, including unit, integration, and API-level testing where appropriate.
  • Good understanding of software engineering principles, design patterns, and maintainable code practices.
  • Experience using Git and working with code review practices in a collaborative team.
  • Understanding of secure development practices, performance considerations, and operational reliability.
  • Experience working in an Agile delivery team, contributing to planning, estimation, reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical decisions clearly and work effectively with others.

Desirable

  • Commercial experience developing services in Go.
  • Strong experience with React, Next.js, and TypeScript in production environments.
  • Experience using CI/CD pipelines and deployment tooling to support safe and reliable delivery.
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms, containerisation, or infrastructure-as-code tooling.
  • Experience coaching, pairing, or supporting other developers.
  • Experience working across multiple systems, services, or technology stacks.
