The role This company is building the orchestration layer for global supply chains, using agentic AI to coordinate work across a complex web of suppliers, factories, and logistics partners. They're a small team, but they've already secured contracts above $500K ACV with major Fortune 250 manufacturers, backed by significant seed funding from leading investors. This isn't just another enterprise SaaS play; it's about solving one of the hardest coordination problems in the global economy.

You'll be one of the first product engineers in their new London office, joining a team of eight to build the application layer around their AI core. This role is 0-to-1 product ownership: you'll take product surfaces from a blank page to what planners at these large companies use daily. It's not ML research or maintaining an existing codebase; it's about defining and building the user experience that unlocks the power of their core AI.

The work

  • Own product surfaces end-to-end, from initial concept to daily use by enterprise customers.
  • Design and build the application layer around an agentic AI core, solving complex coordination problems.
  • Integrate with messy enterprise systems (ERPs, TMS, supplier portals) that lack clean APIs.
  • Make critical product decisions, balancing scope, user needs, and shipping velocity.
  • Ship features from a blank page to production, without waiting for detailed specs.

What You Bring

  • At least two years of full-time, hands-on product engineering experience. Time spent founding a company without building the product yourself does not count.
  • Experience as a founding or early product engineer at a product-led startup, owning features from concept to production.
  • Comfortable across the full application stack: API design, data modeling, and user interfaces, with the ability to integrate with complex enterprise systems.
  • You make product calls yourself: deciding what to build, what to cut, and what's good enough to ship.
  • Based in the UK, Germany, or France and ready to relocate to London. Sponsorship is available.
