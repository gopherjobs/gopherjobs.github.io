Description

Job Description Are you ready to push the boundaries of AI-agentic development and automation? Are you excited about contributing to our distributed edge systems at scale? Join our Edge Application and Edge Computing Team! We are responsible for developing and maintaining critical software applications, which optimize our customers' Web and Media content. Our distributed systems need to operate securely and reliably at scale, across thousands of servers geolocated globally.

Partner with the best Implement software applications and AI-native workflows. You'll work on highly scalable systems across thousands of production servers. Contribute to Akamai's compute platform using programming, testing, and debugging skills to ensure quality and performance.

As a Software Engineer II, you will be responsible for:

  • Collaborating with the product engineering team and/or working groups.
  • Designing, implementing and maintaining software, workflows or tooling supporting customers' products and internal processes.
  • Leveraging AI tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude and Gemini as a core part of your daily engineering tasks.
  • Triaging and analysis of customer escalations related to product operations and behaviour, including the identification, mitigation, and resolution of product bugs
  • Working closely with local and remote peers and collaborating with global teams on various technical topics.
  • Fostering a culture that strives for consistent, iterative improvement to our processes, strategies, tools and frameworks.

Do What You Love To be successful in this role you will:

  • Have at least 3 years of professional experience and a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field/experience.
  • Demonstrated interest and experience in leveraging AI tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Claude, Gemini) for AI-assisted coding, debugging and automations.
  • Have experience developing and monitoring backend applications, understanding of system limits and impacts on performance/scale.
  • Programming experience with Java, relational and non-relational databases and containerization technologies.
  • Be enthusiastic about automation, and building tools to streamline development processes and reduce manual toil.
  • Have expertise with scripting languages in a Linux/Unix environment (e.g.: shell, Bash, Python, Ruby).
