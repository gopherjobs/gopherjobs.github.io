General Summary As a Product Security Engineer focused on AI-assisted vulnerability research and exploit mitigation, you will play a critical role in securing software systems by identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities using our AI infrastructure. This role is ideal for someone who is passionate about software security, has a deep understanding of C/C++ internals, and enjoys building and applying tools to uncover subtle flaws before attackers do, using state of the art AI capabilities.

Your primary focus will be on designing, deploying and operating AI centric tools for analyzing C/C++ code to detect memory safety issues, logic errors, insecure coding patterns and other security flaws. You will also work closely with development teams to integrate and fine-tune sanitizers (such as ASan, UBSan, and MSan) and other compiler-based instrumentation to proactively catch bugs during development and testing.

A part of your role will involve evaluating and implementing exploit mitigation strategies and ensuring they are effectively deployed across the software stack. You may also monitor external security incidents (e.g., CVEs, threat reports, zero-days) to identify detection gaps in current tooling and processes.

This is a hands-on, engineering-focused role that blends security research, AI and non-AI tool development, and collaborative problem-solving. You’ll work alongside developers, security engineers, and incident responders to ensure that vulnerabilities are not only found, but also understood, mitigated, and prevented in the future.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong proficiency in C and C++, with deep understanding of memory management and low-level programming.
  • Proficiency with static analysis tools (e.g., CodeQL, Klocwork, Coverity, Helix QAC, Parasoft, Clang Static Analyzer).
  • Experience with embedded device security architectures.
  • Strong understanding of common vulnerability classes (e.g., buffer overflows, UAF, race conditions).
  • Proficiency with AI-assisted vulnerability detection methodologies and tooling.
  • Hands-on experience with fuzzing frameworks (e.g., AFL++, libFuzzer, Honggfuzz).
  • Excellent written communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of symbolic execution, taint analysis, or dynamic binary instrumentation.
  • Exposure to LLVM Compiler, particularly writing passes and Clang Static Analysis checkers.
  • Experience in working with external security researchers.
