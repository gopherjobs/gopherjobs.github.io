About The Role Platform is the integration layer every Nextlane product connects through: the APIs, gateways and integration services that link dealers' management systems (DMS) with car makers (OEMs), partners and our cloud products.

As a Senior or Staff Software Engineer in Platform , you will design, build and run that layer - and help rebuild hundreds of OEM integrations on it, with AI doing much of the work under human review.

Your Mission Make the platform something every team builds on, not around: stable and secure, one way in, and a new integration in weeks instead of months.This is a senior software engineering role first, with AI on top. You will write, review and ship code, own it in production, and make AI tools work on real, long-lived codebases - not demos.You will work with Platform Product, our architects, Developer Experience, QA, Security and the product teams that use the platform, across several European countries.

Your Responsibilities As a key member of the team, you will be responsible for:

The integration layer

  • Design, build and run the platform's APIs and integration services, behind one front door: our API gateway.
  • Build OEM and partner integrations on our new integration platform - each one a reviewed recipe, not a project of its own - and move existing ones onto

it, domain by domain.

  • Own what you build in production: monitoring, on-call, fixing what breaks.

Contracts and access

  • Design versioned contracts for the core entities - customer and vehicle first, then workshop and parts - one shape, whatever the DMS.
  • Apply one API policy - versioning, deprecation, error codes - so partners keep working when things change.
  • Help build one sign-in and permission model: who may call what, for which dealer.

Stable and safe

  • Add SLOs, alerts and contract tests to the top APIs; release in stages, with rollback.
  • Build security in: no secrets in code, scans in every pipeline, vulnerabilities fixed on time.
  • Lead incidents when needed; write the post-mortem and see its actions through.

AI In How We Build

  • Make AI coding assistants and agents (Claude Code or similar) work on our codebases: repository instructions, conventions, architecture maps, tools.
  • Grow our AI assembly line for integrations - design, build and test, deploy -with a person approving the contract and the release.
  • Bring AI code review and AI-built tests into the pipeline, with our Developer Experience team.
  • Work inside a product team when it needs it, pairing on its code, so the AI tools keep working after you move on.
  • Keep AI governed and measured: what data goes where, human review of every change; adoption, lead time and change failure rate, reported honestly.

Shared knowledge and the bar

  • Keep designs, runbooks and post-mortems open and AI-readable, so anyone can ask what broke last time and what fixed it.
  • Review code and designs, coach engineers, write short guides.
  • At Staff level: set technical direction beyond the team, with our architects; lead migrations other teams depend on; help design how AI agents use the

platform safely - identities, limits, audit.
