Role Overview:

Amagi's Customer Experience team delivers 40 to 50 linear channels every week to OTT, FAST, cable and smart-TV platforms around the world, and every delivery is quality-checked before it goes live. Today much of that QA is done by people watching streams for hours and checking EPG and metadata by hand. As an Onboarding Automation Engineer, you will design and build, from scratch, the automated testing system that does this work: a scalable, API-driven platform that checks video, audio, subtitles, ads, EPG and metadata the way a viewer would experience them.

This is a software development role, not a manual testing role. You will build the system that the QA team uses, and you will own its adoption. You will sit within the QA team, work with Python, AWS, FFmpeg, OpenCV and AI models, and partner with delivery engineers, product and engineering. Within your first six months, the goal is a fully functioning system that tests video, SSAI, EPG and metadata for at least four tier-1 platforms.

What will you own?

1/ Automated Delivery QA Platform

  • Design and build, from the ground up, an API-driven automated testing system that validates every linear channel delivery before it goes live.
  • Define the test catalogue and pass/fail metrics with the QA team, and turn the requirements of close to 600 distribution platforms into configurable, reusable test cases, starting with tier-1 platforms such as Samsung TV Plus, LG Channels and Roku.
  • Build pre-QA checks that catch stream, configuration and metadata errors before a delivery reaches QC, raising the first-time QA pass rate.
  • Make the system easy for the QA and delivery teams to adopt: self-service test runs, clear reports, and integration with the tools they already use, such as Jira and Slack.

2/ Video, Audio, and Ad Validation

  • Detect visual defects the way a viewer sees them: black or frozen frames, pixelation and macroblocking, visible interlacing and other on-screen artefacts, using OpenCV and vision models rather than relying only on stream metadata.
  • Build accurate, cost-effective detection of audio-video sync drift, where PTS timestamps alone cannot be trusted, along with missing or silent audio, channel layout and loudness issues.
  • Validate closed captions and subtitles: presence, on-screen position, and timing against the audio.
  • Validate SSAI and monetisation: SCTE-35 markers, ad break signalling, and whether ad replacement actually happens in the delivered stream.
  • Check HLS and DASH streams against each platform's requirements: renditions, bitrates, codecs, resolution and manifest structure.

3/ EPG and Metadata Validation

  • Automate EPG and metadata checks across XMLTV, JSON and platform-specific formats: schedules, ratings, languages and required fields.
  • Use OCR and vision models to validate artwork, for example whether an image carries the required title treatment, and whether titles and descriptions match what is actually playing.
  • Bring existing validation scripts, such as the Roku EPG framework and the pre-QA checks on the delivery board, into the common platform.

4/ Scalable, Cost-Efficient Infrastructure

  • Architect the system on AWS so it scales out with delivery volume (40 to 50 channels a week and growing) and scales back when there is nothing to test.
  • Process many concurrent streams efficiently, choosing between CPU, GPU, OpenCV and hosted AI models (for example through Amazon Bedrock) based on accuracy and cost per test.
  • Containerise and deploy services with Docker, Kubernetes or serverless components, managed as infrastructure as code, and integrate them into CI/CD pipelines (Git, Jenkins).
  • Add monitoring, alerting and logging so that failures in the platform itself are found and fixed quickly.

5/ Quality, Performance, and Reliability

  • Measure the accuracy of every automated check against human QA results, and drive down false positives and false negatives so the QA team trusts the system.
  • Analyse recurring delivery and QC failures, and turn them into new tests and pre-checks.
  • Keep historical reports and artefacts (HTML, JSON, recordings) for audit and debugging.
  • Own the stability of the platform, maintaining 99.9% uptime.

6/ Collaboration and Reporting

  • Work with the QA lead and delivery engineers to prioritise test cases by business impact and platform.
  • Track progress against a 30-60-90 day plan and give regular updates to your manager.
  • Document the system and its APIs so other teams can add tests and plug it into their own workflows.

What are we looking for?

  • 2-5 years of software development experience, with a track record of building backend systems or automation frameworks from scratch, not only writing test scripts.
  • Strong hands-on programming experience in Python, including services, APIs and concurrent processing.
  • Hands-on experience with video and audio analysis tools such as FFmpeg/FFprobe, MediaInfo and OpenCV.
  • Working knowledge of streaming technologies: HLS, DASH, SRT, codecs, bitrates and adaptive renditions.
  • Familiarity with server-side ad insertion (SSAI) and SCTE-35, or the ability to learn them quickly.
  • Experience designing scalable, cost-efficient systems on AWS (EC2 Auto Scaling, ECS/EKS, Lambda, S3, SQS), and familiarity with AWS Media Services.
  • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar), Git and Jenkins or equivalent CI/CD tools.
  • Experience using AI/ML models, OCR, vision models or LLM APIs in production, and the judgement to know when they are accurate and cheap enough to use.
  • Experience with REST APIs, JSON/XML schema validation, and a relational database such as PostgreSQL.
  • Experience with monitoring and observability tools.
  • The ability to reason about a problem from first principles, for example how to detect audio-video sync issues from the viewer's side, and propose a practical, cost-effective solution.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to give regular status updates.
  • Ownership mindset and the ability to work independently on a greenfield system in a fast-paced environment.
  • Customer-focused mindset: you understand that a defect your system misses reaches a customer's viewers.
  • Good to have: experience with broadcast, OTT or FAST delivery, EPG formats such as XMLTV, smart-TV platforms or device test labs, and test frameworks such as Pytest or Robot Framework.
