About The Role Join an early-stage AI company building software that connects AI agents with real-world laboratory systems. You will own critical infrastructure for coordinating experiments, connecting agents and instruments, and capturing reliable data across physical and software systems.

What You'll Do

  • Build systems that schedule and coordinate experiments across distributed laboratories.
  • Connect AI agents to experiments and instruments through robust agent infrastructure.
  • Design data infrastructure to capture system state, actions, measurements, failures, and provenance.
  • Architect distributed systems that reliably coordinate software, devices, and experiments.
  • Develop APIs and developer tools for new workflows and integrations.
  • Debug and optimize systems across software, hardware, and physical environments.

What We're Looking For

  • At least 3 years of experience building production-grade software systems end to end.
  • Strong proficiency in Python or Rust, with solid software engineering fundamentals.
  • Production backend or distributed-systems experience, including APIs, networking, and asynchronous communication.
  • Experience building software as an engineer at an early-stage startup.
  • Ability to take ownership in ambiguous environments and debug across software and hardware layers.
  • Experience with robotics, physical AI, hardware integration, lab automation, embedded systems, ML infrastructure, data infrastructure, or orchestration systems is a plus. Biology experience is not required.

Compensation & Benefits Base salary: $100,000 to $250,000 USD annually. Visa sponsorship is not available.

Location

On-site in Munich, Germany. Other listed work locations include Zurich, Switzerland, and San Francisco, United States.
