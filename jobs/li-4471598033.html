🌍 About Hopae

At Hopae , we're building the world's most trusted digital identity platform — private, secure, and built for real life. We believe identity should be in your control — not locked behind outdated systems or centralized gatekeepers.

Our product, Hopae Connect, lets businesses verify real people across borders through a single API — no need to build country-by-country integrations themselves. Our technology powers instant, privacy-first verification across fintech, credentials, supply chain, and gaming — for IDV providers, e-signature platforms, and enterprises who need identity to just work, everywhere.

Backed by top global investors, Hopae operates across Seoul, Paris, Luxembourg, and San Francisco , bringing together exceptional talent from over 10 nationalities. We move fast, think globally, and build products that define the future of identity.

🎯 What You'll Do

Hopae connects businesses to government eIDs and identity systems worldwide through a single platform — across fintech, credentials, supply chain, and gaming. As a Software Engineer at Hopae, you own real problems end to end, across the full stack, in a team where the mission moves fast and the scope is broad by design.

This is not a role where you pick a lane and stay in it. You'll work across backend, integrations, infrastructure, and tooling — whatever the product needs. If you thrive in environments where ownership is total and the work is never the same twice, this is for you.

  • Build and scale the core verification platform — API architecture, verification lifecycle, async processing, event delivery, and production reliability
  • Connect and operate external identity providers — building adapters, normalization layers, health monitoring, and the tooling that makes adding the next provider faster than the last
  • Build the infrastructure that takes integrated capabilities to proven in production — developer tooling, E2E test infrastructure, reference environments, and go-live systems
  • Own quality end to end — you write the tests, you monitor production, you fix what breaks
  • Investigate failures independently, identify root causes, and fix the system rather than the symptom
  • Work directly with Product, partners, and customers when solving a problem requires it — you're not behind a ticket queue

🧠 Who You Are

  • Strong backend fundamentals — comfortable with APIs, distributed systems, async processing, and production reliability
  • Full stack in practice — you go where the problem is, whether that's backend, infrastructure, tooling, or frontend when needed
  • You own quality: testing, observability, and operational readiness are part of how you work, not someone else's job
  • High ownership, low ego — you drive ambiguous problems to resolution without waiting to be told exactly what to do
  • Thrives in a fast-moving environment where the scope is broad and the mission evolves
