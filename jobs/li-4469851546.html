About Doit

We are focused on reinventing how people spend, save, invest, exchange, travel, and more. Our objective is to build tools that will help people get more from their money and and become an expert at ease. We believe AI will help make finance smart, assessible and safe.

We are looking for the most talented and driven people we can find. We are looking for people who work for their passion, not counting hours. Who loves building great next-generation products, not status quo. Who cares about redefining how everyone around us can get the best financial applications, not for an exclusive few. We have teams working around the world, with over 20 nationalities and growing from our offices and remotely. Join us and build a better future for our society.

In 2019, we built the first mobile-first, insurance platform, enabling insurance to be accessible online by millions in the region. Today, it's the leading insurance platform in Southeast Asia.

The Role

We are looking for full stack engineers to build fast across Doit's AI Neobank app.

This role is for product-minded engineers who can move across frontend, backend, internal tools and customer journeys to turn ideas into working products quickly.

What You'll Own

  • Build and ship full product features across frontend, backend, APIs and databases.
  • Create customer and internal workflows for onboarding, accounts, payments, rewards, travel, FX, support automation, KYC and risk.
  • Turn ambiguous product problems into working systems with clean UX and reliable backend logic.
  • Work closely with product, design, mobile, backend and AI engineers to launch fast and improve after release.
  • Build internal tools and automation that reduce manual work and help teams operate at scale.
  • Own your work beyond release: monitor, debug, learn and improve.

What We're Looking For

  • Strong full stack engineering experience with real shipped products.
  • Comfortable across frontend, backend, APIs, databases and production debugging.
  • Good frontend execution using React, Next.js, Vue or similar modern stack.
  • Good backend fundamentals around APIs, data models, authentication, integrations and reliability.
  • Strong product sense and ability to simplify messy workflows.
  • Able to work fast with ambiguity and own outcomes end-to-end.

This Role Is Not For

  • Engineers who only want to work on one narrow layer.
  • People who need complete specs before starting.
  • Developers who ship UI without understanding backend or operations impact.
  • Candidates who avoid production support.
  • People who make vague claims without showing real shipped work.

The Bar

We are looking for engineers with real technology career highlights. A strong highlight is not just working at a famous company. A strong highlight means you personally helped build, ship or scale a serious product, platform or system.

Location

This role is remote, but candidates must be based in China . We are hiring for this market specifically, so applicants should already be based in China .

Language

English is our main working language across global teams. Strong English communication is required.

Interview Process

Our process is designed to move fast:

1. Online assessment or practical task

2. Technical interview

3. CEO / final round

For strong candidates, we aim to complete the process and make an offer within 1 week from the start of the interview process. Candidates who complete assessments quickly will be prioritized.
