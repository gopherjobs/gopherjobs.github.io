About the role

When a guest pays for their stay, the payment has to just work. Nobody notices a payment that succeeds, and everybody notices one that fails. At Mews, the Fintech Core team builds the payments platform behind hotels around the world, and Acquiring is the heart of it: the part that actually processes card and alternative payments.

As a Product Builder, you own the whole thing. You find the problems worth solving, design the solution, write the production code and check whether it moved the metric.

What you'll be doing

  • Own payment initiatives from the first question to production: discovery, design, code, launch and measurement.
  • Make payments more reliable and faster for hotels and their guests, and prove it with data.
  • Build and run backend services that process real money at scale, with testing, monitoring and safe rollouts built in.
  • Talk to hotels, support teams and partners to understand where payments break and why.
  • Use AI tools and agents as your default way of building, and improve them as you go.
  • Make your own trade-off decisions, and explain them clearly to the people around you.

What we're looking for

  • Solid backend engineering experience, shipping and running production services where accuracy really matters.
  • A track record of owning outcomes, not just tasks: you've chosen what to build, shipped it and measured the result.
  • Hands-on use of AI tools like Claude Code or Cursor in your daily work, with examples you're proud of.
  • Experience with payments, fintech or other financial systems is a big plus, but not a must if your foundation is strong.
  • Curiosity about the people using what you build, and the habit of talking to them.
  • Bonus points for startup or 0-to-1 experience, or products you've shipped on your own.

AI Fluency Level 3: In this role, AI is a core part of how you build, not an add-on. You have redesigned how you or your team works with tools like Claude, Cursor or agent frameworks, and you have built approaches that other engineers now follow. You also question what the AI gives you: you check the code and the payment logic it produces and know when it is wrong, which matters most in a domain where correctness comes first.

For more information on AI fluency at Mews, please refer to "AI Fluency at Mews: A Comprehensive Guide for Candidates" on Confluence.
