We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer for the LN Cloud Automation Team, which works in a hybrid‑remote setup from our office in Barneveld, the Netherlands, 3-5 days per week. This team is responsible for deploying and managing LN CE in the AWS Cloud. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will develop new functionality for Infor LN Cloud Automation together with a talented team. In this key role, you will work closely with other teams such as Engineers, Architects, Cloud‑Ops Engineers, and Support to successfully develop, maintain, and test new and existing software. Infor LN provides customers in the discrete manufacturing industry with extensive capabilities to optimize their business processes and work more efficiently. More information about Infor LN can be found here: https://www.infor.com/solutions/erp/ln
