To be considered for this position, you must have an advanced level of English & Spanish

At GBS Group, we help entrepreneurs and executives start, run, and manage their businesses in the United States through Start-Up Services, Financial & Tax Advisory, and operational support solutions. As we continue scaling our internal capabilities, we are investing heavily in automation, AI-driven workflows, and internal platforms that improve how we operate, deliver, and scale. We are building a high-leverage internal systems layer powered by automation and AI—and this role is critical to that effort.

This role owns the design, development, deployment, security, and maintenance of internal tools, automation systems, and AI-driven workflows. AI is expected to be a core part of how this work is done, from development and testing to deployment and ongoing improvement. It combines internal product development, platform engineering, DevOps, and applied AI systems to improve operational efficiency and scale business processes. You will also be responsible for the security, reliability, and integrity of these systems, ensuring safe handling of data, access, and system interactions across all automation and AI workflows.

This is a high-ownership, builder role for someone who thinks beyond code and focuses on driving real business impact through systems.

Key Responsibilities

Internal Tools & Platforms

  • Build and evolve internal tools that support core business operations
  • Design systems that improve operational efficiency and scalability
  • Treat internal tools as products with long-term ownership

Automation & System Orchestration

  • Design and implement automation across workflows, systems, and data pipelines
  • Connect APIs, third-party tools, and internal systems into cohesive workflows
  • Continuously identify and execute high-impact automation opportunities

AI Systems & Workflows

  • Integrate AI tools, copilots, and agent-based processes into day-to-day operations
  • Design and manage AI-driven workflows that are reliable and production-ready
  • Establish guardrails for AI usage (data exposure, prompt injection risks, etc.)

Infrastructure, DevOps & Reliability

  • Own CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, and environment management
  • Maintain infrastructure reliability, monitoring, and observability
  • Troubleshoot system failures, integration issues, and performance bottlenecks

Security & System Integrity

  • Implement and maintain security best practices across internal tools and AI workflows
  • Manage authentication, authorization, and access controls (RBAC, secrets, API keys)
  • Ensure secure handling of sensitive data across integrations and AI systems
  • Monitor for vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and potential abuse
  • Protect systems against risks specific to AI-enabled environments

Cross-Functional Impact

  • Work closely with operations and leadership to translate business needs into systems
  • Improve how teams operate by reducing manual work and increasing leverage

What Makes Someone Successful Here

  • Thinks like a product builder, not just a developer
