Description

Our customers never think about infrastructure. That's the point.

At Phocas, we've spent 20+ years building financial planning and analytics products that are powerful yet simple to use. We've grown to nearly 300 people across the globe, and our Product & Technology team in Christchurch is at the heart of it. We move fast, learn from our mistakes, and use AI to speed up how we work.

Behind our products sits a multi-region AWS SaaS platform serving mid-market customers across the US, Canada, UK, Europe and Australia: established Windows and SQL Server workloads running alongside containerised and serverless services. We're looking for a Platform Manager to lead the team that keeps it running, keeps it secure, and keeps it moving forward.

What you'll do

  • Lead the platform team, owning the availability, performance, security and cost of our AWS platform.
  • Lead the team's adoption of AI tools and agentic workflows, with sensible checks on what they produce.
  • Turn strategy into a clear, sequenced roadmap, and see it through to measurable outcomes.
  • Keep AWS spend visible and efficient as we grow, from attribution and rightsizing to commitment planning.
  • Set the standard for infrastructure as code, CI/CD, monitoring, logging and access control, favouring simple, supportable solutions.
  • Get hands-on when it counts: lead incident response and root cause analysis, and know the architecture first-hand.
  • Build a high-trust team and work closely with neighbouring teams like Engineering Enablement.

What you'll bring

  • Extensive experience running large, complex platforms, including several years leading teams.
  • Recent hands-on engineering experience in high-scale, 24/7 operations.
  • Deep AWS fundamentals: networking, compute, storage, resilience, capacity management and security, including IAM and least-privilege design.
  • A track record managing AWS costs at scale, including attribution, forecasting and optimisation.
  • Hands-on experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform/Terragrunt) and running CI/CD pipelines.
  • Comfort operating a mixed estate, Windows/IIS and self-managed SQL Server on EC2 alongside ECS, Lambda and AWS Batch, and modernising it incrementally where it delivers real benefit.
  • Practical experience embedding AI tools into engineering or operations workflows.
  • Familiarity with our stack is a bonus: Terraform/Terragrunt with Atlantis, GitHub Actions, Octopus Deploy, Elastic, HashiCorp Vault.
  • A pragmatic, outcomes-focused approach and clear, straightforward communication.

Why work at Phocas?

  • The people: when we ask what people love most about working here, it's always the same answer
  • Real ownership: this is a platform that matters, serving customers across five regions, and you'll shape how it evolves
  • AI-forward culture: we use AI to improve how we build and operate, and this role is at the centre of that
  • 10% PD time: one day a fortnight dedicated to learning and development, no questions asked
  • Sunny Sydenham office: spacious, central, with parking for cars, bikes, and scooters
  • Benefits: Southern Cross health insurance, life, TPD and income protection, extra paid parental leave, and more
  • Flexibility: hybrid working and flexible hours
