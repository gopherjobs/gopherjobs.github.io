The IZTECH Geothermal Energy Research and Application Center (GEOCEN) is a multidisciplinary research center at Izmir Institute of Technology (IZTECH), dedicated to geothermal energy research, geosciences, innovative technologies, and sustainable subsurface resource management. Our research activities include geothermal exploration, reservoir characterization, hydrogeology, geochemistry, water resources management, disaster risk management, geological and numerical modeling, and GIS-based spatial data analysis . We also integrate field investigations, drone-based surveying, remote sensing technologies, and geospatial monitoring to support geoscientific research and environmental assessments. Through interdisciplinary research, innovative technologies, and national and international collaborations, GEOCEN contributes to scientific innovation, disaster resilience, and sustainable energy and water resources management.

Role Description

The Modeling Laboratory at IZTECH GEOCEN is seeking two motivated undergraduate or master's students to help establish and coordinate a new GIS Database Working Group .

We particularly welcome students from City and Regional Planning and Civil Engineering at IZTECH who are interested in geosciences, GIS technologies, geological modeling, and interdisciplinary research.

The initial focus will be on developing a campus-based geoscientific GIS database , integrating geological, hydrogeological, environmental, and other spatial datasets. Students will collaborate with researchers while gaining practical experience in GIS applications, geological modeling, and scientific data management.

Key Activities:

  • Developing and organizing geoscientific GIS databases for the IZTECH campus.
  • Collecting, processing, mapping, and analyzing geological, hydrogeological, and environmental data.
  • Supporting spatial analysis, digital mapping, and 3D geological modeling applications using ArcGIS Pro and Leapfrog Energy .
  • Integrating field observations, remote sensing, and drone-derived datasets into geospatial workflows.
  • Contributing to related geoscience research activities and helping establish the GIS working group.

Research & Development Opportunities

  • Technical Training: Gain practical experience in GIS, spatial data analysis, 3D geological modeling, remote sensing, and drone-based mapping through training and guidance from GEOCEN researchers.
  • Hands-on Research: Work with real geoscientific datasets and participate in interdisciplinary field, laboratory, and digital modeling activities.
  • Scientific Development: Depending on research outcomes and individual contributions, activities may lead to research projects, conference presentations, and peer-reviewed publications.
  • Collaborative Learning: Help establish the working group, with the potential to develop into a broader IZTECH GIS & Geosciences Community in the future.

Priority will be given to students with previous GIS experience (QGIS, ArcGIS, etc.). However, motivated students eager to learn are also encouraged to apply. Participation is voluntary and flexible , allowing students to contribute according to their academic schedules and availability.
