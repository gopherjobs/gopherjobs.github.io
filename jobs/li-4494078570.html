Role: Google Cloud Infrastructure Engineer

Type: Contract

Location: Colombia / Chile / Argentina / Costa Rica -Remote

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
  • 6+ years of experience architecting and deploying scalable, secure infrastructure on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including expertise with IaC (Infrastructure as Code), DevOps, and CI/CD processes.
  • Experience designing and implementing cloud enterprise solutions, including a proven track record in Professional Services or Technical Consulting, and demonstrated project management, communication, collaboration, and negotiation skills.
  • Experience coding in one or more general purpose languages (e.g., Python, Java, Go, C, or C++) including data structures, algorithms, and software design.
  • Ability to communicate in English and Portuguese or Spanish fluently to engage with local stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Expertise in orchestrating containerized workloads via Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), GCE, GCVE, and GCS.
  • Extensive knowledge of IaC for Landing Zones and designing Infrastructure
  • Foundations, including high-availability networking solutions (e.g., VPC, Cloud Firewall, Interconnect, VPN).
  • Expertise with Identity and Access Management (IAM), Observability (logging and monitoring), Resource Management, Cost Optimization (FinOps), and standard IT security practices.
  • Experience in large Data Center Exit and Public Cloud migration to GCP.
  • Expertise in leveraging AI agents to automate, expedite, and de-risk complex infrastructure migrations.

Desired Certifications (almost a requirement for the Professional Services team):

  • Google Professional Cloud Architect.
  • Google Professional Cloud Network Engineer.
  • Google Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer.
