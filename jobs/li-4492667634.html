About the Role

Our client is a global financial technology company providing software for employee share incentive and deferred compensation plans. Its platform supports the entire plan lifecycle, including grants, vesting, exercises, accounting, and reporting.

They are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join their distributed engineering team and support the platform during Australian business hours.

You will work on a mature Python/Django application with approximately 700,000 lines of code across 70 applications, alongside newer Go services. The role combines building new product features with maintaining production systems and supporting clients when technical issues arise.

This position is suited to an experienced engineer who enjoys developing new functionality, troubleshooting complex production issues, and taking ownership of systems that clients rely on.

Key Responsibilities

Product Development

  • Design, develop, and deliver new features while extending existing functionality in a Python 3.13 and Django 5.2 application, as well as newer Go services.
  • Work with the wider engineering team to deliver items from the product backlog.
  • Use AI coding tools and agents as part of the development, testing, and debugging process while maintaining high code quality.
  • Participate in planning, technical design discussions, and peer code reviews.
  • Write well-tested, maintainable code.
  • Improve platform performance and reliability.

Production Support

  • Monitor production systems during Sydney business hours using Sentry and AWS monitoring and alerting tools.
  • Investigate and resolve production issues, following peer-reviewed change processes and AWS deployment workflows.
  • Identify when issues require a rollback or escalation.
  • Investigate root causes and implement appropriate fixes, tests, monitoring, and documentation.
  • Prepare clear daily handovers for the wider engineering team.
  • Progress toward independent end-to-end production release ownership following a structured 3–4 month onboarding period. A second senior on-call engineer will remain available for emergency sign-offs.

Client Support and Communication

  • Act as a technical point of contact for client administrators and the client management team during Australian business hours.
  • Explain technical issues and resolutions clearly to non-technical finance and HR users.
  • Participate in client calls when technical input is required.

Requirements

  • 7+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong experience building and maintaining large, complex production web applications.
  • Proven ability to design and deliver substantial features within an established software product.
  • Strong Python 3.x and SQL/PostgreSQL skills.
  • Strong Django experience, ideally with modern Django 4 or 5.
  • Experience investigating production issues using logs, stack traces, and tools such as Sentry.
  • Experience profiling Python applications, analyzing slow SQL queries using tools such as EXPLAIN, and optimizing ORM access patterns, background jobs, and reports.
  • Strong understanding of AI-assisted software development, including different AI coding workflows, their strengths and limitations, and the ability to manage parallel workflows using Git worktrees.
  • Ability to maintain code quality through clear specifications, thorough testing, and careful code reviews.
  • Experience with Git, peer code reviews, CI/CD, and automated testing.
  • Sound technical judgement when evaluating risks, prioritizing fixes, and deciding when to escalate issues.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with experience communicating directly with clients.
  • Good judgement when handling sensitive financial and personal information.
  • Willingness to complete background and security screening.
  • Ability to consistently work Australian business hours with reliable home internet and backup power.

Beneficial Experience

The following experience is advantageous but not required:

  • Go (Golang) development for microservices.
  • AWS infrastructure, release pipelines, caching strategies, and observability tools.
  • Pandas and NumPy, particularly for financial reporting or data transformation.
  • Experience in financial services, payroll, equity compensation, or another regulated industry.
  • Experience working with distributed teams across international time zones.
