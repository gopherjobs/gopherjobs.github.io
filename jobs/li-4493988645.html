Job Description This Software Engineer role is part of Visa's Product Reliability Engineering team supporting the Data Streaming Platform. The team manages Kafka as a critical platform and combines software development with site reliability engineering responsibilities. We are looking for a software developer with strong Python skills, hands-on problem-solving ability, and an interest in Kafka and Generative AI. The engineer will first build practical knowledge of the platform and its production support model, then contribute to the development of automation and agentic applications.

  • Design, develop, test, and deploy reliable software solutions and automation using Python.
  • Build agentic and Generative AI applications using concepts and frameworks such as large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, LangChain, and LangGraph.
  • Develop and integrate APIs and backend services; contribute to user interface development when required.
  • Learn and support the Kafka platform, including its reliability, performance, scalability, and operational processes.
  • Develop products, tools, and processes that reduce manual effort and improve day-to-day platform operations.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues, analyze root causes, and implement sustainable fixes.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business and operational requirements into effective technical solutions.
  • Communicate clearly through technical documentation, reviews, and stakeholder discussions.

This is a hybrid position. Expectations of days in office will be confirmed by your hiring manager.
