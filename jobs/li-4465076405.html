Role Overview:

We are looking for a Cloud AWS Engineer to join one of our biggest clients, a leading organization focused on delivering innovative digital platforms and cloud-native solutions that support large-scale business operations.

If you are looking for an opportunity to drive cloud transformation initiatives, work with modern AWS technologies, and contribute to the development of scalable and secure platforms, we have the ideal project for your career.

Join a talented team and help shape the future of cloud engineering and DevOps excellence.

100% Full-remote

What you will do:

• Manage and optimize development, testing, acceptance, and production environments on AWS.

• Design and implement cloud-native and serverless solutions using AWS services and Infrastructure as Code.

• Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, monitoring, and logging capabilities.

• Support development teams with troubleshooting, automation initiatives, security best practices, and platform improvements.

What you are like:

• Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technologies, or a related field.

• More than 7 years of experience in software development, cloud engineering, or DevOps environments.

• Strong experience with AWS services including EC2, ECS, EKS, Lambda, IAM, RDS, S3, API Gateway, CloudFormation, and EventBridge.

• Hands-on experience with Terraform, CloudFormation, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD tools (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins), and Agile/Scrum methodologies.

• Experience with .NET/C#, REST APIs, microservices, Python, TypeScript, serverless architectures, cloud migrations, and security-by-design principles.
