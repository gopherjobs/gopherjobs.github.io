About ALTAVE

Protecting lives and resources with technology : this is ALTAVE's purpose.

We are a technology company with international presence in the oil & gas, mining, security, and artificial intelligence sectors. We develop advanced AI and computer vision solutions for automation, monitoring, and decision-making in critical environments.

We are looking for a Computer Vision Developer who is passionate about technology and innovation to join our team and help drive the development of our artificial intelligence solutions.

We are looking for someone who doesn't just train AI models, but who wants to turn data and algorithms into a scalable ecosystem through MLOps and cutting-edge engineering, ensuring high performance, security, and real business impact.

Description

At Altave, you will work on developing computer vision solutions with real impact on the lives of people and companies. Your work will range from proposing new detection algorithms to creating datasets and automating training pipelines.

We value professionals with a solid grasp of classic and modern AI algorithms — especially Deep Learning — who write clean, testable, and maintainable code.

You will also have the chance to explore the use of LLMs as a productivity tool, supporting data curation and validating detections generated by models.

This position is based on-site in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. Employment will be structured through an Employer of Record (EOR) with our local partner in Saudi Arabia.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and integrate computer vision solutions for different real-world scenarios
  • Propose and test new detection and segmentation algorithms
  • Automate training, validation, and inference pipelines for models
  • Generate and annotate datasets for training, proactively requesting images from clients and internal teams and sourcing public datasets
  • Read and apply knowledge from state-of-the-art scientific papers
  • Use LLMs to assist with dataset generation and result validation
  • Ensure code quality through best practices, testing, and peer review
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of development and experimentation processes
