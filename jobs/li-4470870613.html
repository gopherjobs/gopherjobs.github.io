Overview

We are supporting one of the largest organisations in Europe on a major network transformation and automation programme. We are seeking an experienced Telecommunications DevOps Engineer to join a long-term initiative focused on network orchestration, automation, and cloud-native telecom environments.

This is a design and development-focused role where you will be responsible for building, enhancing, and automating telecom network services and orchestration solutions within a large-scale carrier environment.

Candidates with experience of Nokia Orchestration Center (NoRC) are highly preferred. However, engineers with strong experience across other telecom orchestration platforms and network automation environments will also be considered.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop and maintain telecom orchestration and automation solutions
  • Build and enhance automated workflows for network service provisioning and lifecycle management
  • Develop integrations between orchestration platforms, network functions, OSS/BSS systems and cloud environments
  • Collaborate with architecture, engineering and operations teams to improve automation capabilities across the network estate
  • Create, test and deploy infrastructure and application automation solutions
  • Support CI/CD implementation and DevOps best practices within telecom environments
  • Contribute to cloud-native networking initiatives including containerised and Kubernetes-based deployments
  • Drive improvements in network reliability, scalability, efficiency and service delivery processes
