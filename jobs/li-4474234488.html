About BIT:

BIT (formerly Matrixport) is a global digital asset financial services and infrastructure group. Headquartered in Singapore and founded in 2019, BIT bridges traditional finance and digital assets through governance-driven financial services and technology.

The firm manages over US7 billion in assets and facilitates more than US7 billion in monthly trading volume. BIT offers services including custody, trading, asset and wealth management, liquidity and financing solutions, and tokenised real-world assets (RWA), serving institutional and professional investors globally.

BIT Group entities maintain a licensed and regulated footprint across Singapore, Hong Kong, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, the United States and Bhutan.

For more information, visit www.bit.com

Why Join Us:

At BIT, we tackle complex problems as a team. We encourage openness and promote transparency, respect, and inclusivity. Every team member is valued and has a voice that can be heard. We are always in search of intellectually curious and entrepreneurial individuals who are keen on making an impact in the crypto ecosystem and in building a better product for the next one billion users.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Lead a distributed team of software engineers across different countries, supporting the systems and services of the company's asset management business.
  • Serve as the technical lead for the entire business unit, developing a deep understanding of product and operational needs to plan system and team development, and define the technical roadmap and engineering standards.
  • Oversee the overall delivery quality and efficiency of the team, while balancing long-term initiatives.
  • Participate in the solution design and review of key modules, and develop well-controlled execution plans for high-complexity or high-risk problems.
  • Set goals, conduct performance reviews, and lead recruitment and training for the engineering groups within the department.
  • Understand requirements from business, product, operations, and risk management, and adjust engineering resource allocation accordingly.
  • Establish development processes and engineering standards suited to the AI era, and lead the team in pragmatically using AI to improve overall delivery quality and efficiency.
  • Represent the team in communication with business, operations, risk management, and other departments, and coordinate with other engineering teams.

Job Requirements:

  • 6+ years of backend development experience, including 3+ years managing technical teams.
  • Expert in Golang or Rust, with experience developing and maintaining large-scale applications. Familiar with scripting and tooling languages such as Python, Bash, and SQL.
  • In-depth knowledge of MySQL transaction isolation mechanisms and their common patterns in real-world application development.
  • Familiar with standard cloud services such as AWS, including EC2, EBS, S3, RDS, VPC, and ELB.
  • Extensive experience designing, maintaining, and iterating on distributed systems. Deep understanding of the complexities of distributed systems in areas such as transactional operations, data consistency, testability, and observability, with mature methodologies for addressing them.

Preferred:

  • Experience developing financial services such as asset management, trading, or payments. Familiarity with derivatives, structured products, and DeFi products.
  • Experience managing cross-regional or remote teams.
