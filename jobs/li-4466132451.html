Full Stack Engineer (Clojure/ClojureScript) at BairesDev

In this role, you'll take full ownership of features across the stack for a retail AI outfitting platform, spanning database design, backend API development, and the frontend interfaces colleagues rely on daily. Joining a team of experienced Clojure engineers, you'll build services that connect with LLM APIs, coordinate multi-step AI workflows, and support semantic search across large-scale product catalogs. This is your opportunity to work independently at a high level of technical ownership, contributing to a platform that serves hundreds of enterprise retailers and 100M+ monthly shoppers.

What You'll Do:

- Take end-to-end ownership of features spanning database schema, backend logic, and frontend interfaces.

- Develop backend services connecting to LLM APIs to power AI-driven functionality.

- Coordinate multi-step AI workflows using orchestration tooling.

- Support retrieval and search pipelines enabling semantic search across the product catalog.

- Make independent architectural and technical decisions within a fast-paced, ownership-driven team.

What we are looking for:

- 8+ years of experience in software engineering.

- Solid command of Clojure.

- Practical experience with ClojureScript.

- Background in PostgreSQL and SQL schema design.

- Experience working with LLM APIs in production settings, primarily calling and consuming them.

- Familiarity with REST API design principles.

- Comfort within the functional programming ecosystem, including tools like shadow-cljs, re-frame, and reagent.

- Exposure to prompt engineering or working with non-deterministic model outputs.

- Advanced proficiency in English.
