Minimum Qualifications

  • Meet one of the following (completed within the past 3 years or expected to be completed within the next 12 months):
  • A Master's degree with 0 years of work experience; or
  • A Bachelor's degree with 2 years of work experience.
  • Qualifying education and work experience should be in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in at least one of the following programming or scripting languages: C/C++, Python, or Java, with evidence of recent (within the 12 months) coursework, projects, or work experience.
  • Experience in quality assurance (QA), testing, and/or debugging in an academic, project-based, or work related setting.
