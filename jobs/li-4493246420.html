Our client is a technology company based in Tokyo providing software engineering and technology solutions to enterprise customers, with particular strength in the financial industry.

About the Positions

Middle Software Engineer

As a Middle Software Engineer, you will develop and improve software systems, APIs, databases, and applications while collaborating closely with other engineers.

You will contribute to implementation, testing, troubleshooting, and technical documentation. Depending on your experience and interests, you may also participate in system design and architecture discussions.

This position offers the opportunity to deepen your technical expertise through hands-on development and collaboration with senior engineers.

Senior Software Engineer

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will take a leading role in the architecture, development, and technical direction of large-scale and complex systems.

You will lead technical decisions across backend, frontend, and infrastructure while continuing to work directly on challenging development tasks.

The role includes mentoring junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, design reviews, technical guidance, and coaching.

You will work closely with Product Managers, business teams, client-side engineers, and other stakeholders to evaluate technical feasibility, explain architectural trade-offs, and deliver high-quality systems.
