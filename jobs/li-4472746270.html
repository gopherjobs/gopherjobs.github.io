Description

About Platform Engineering Platform Engineering (platformengineering.org) is home to the world's largest community of platform engineers. Through events like PlatformCon, which brings together over 35,000+ participants every year, we connect thousands of professionals, share best practices, and help shape the future of how teams build and scale internal developer platforms.

We're looking for a Senior AI Automation & Systems Engineer to own the technical infrastructure behind our content and design operation, from planning and research, through AI-assisted production and quality control, to publishing and distribution.

This position is fully remote and is open to applicants based in Europe.

What You Will Do

  • Take technical ownership of our content and design factory: tooling selection, automation pipelines, quality management, and agent orchestration that let a small team produce at scale.
  • Design and development (structuring, prompting, QA) of complex multistep content and design creation workflows and interfaces as skills in model harnesses and external content workflow tools (e.g., AirOps, n8n).
  • Think through the user journey and create UI integrations into existing solutions (e.g., Supabase databases) or vibe-code and maintain UI and interfaces for workflows, databases, etc. (search, export data, import to other workflows).
  • Keep our stack modular and replaceable, evaluating tools continuously and making changes before we become dependent on the wrong ones.
  • Reduce manual overhead systematically by building automations and agent workflows that replace repetitive processes.
  • Work with leadership as a peer on architectural decisions and infrastructure direction, not just as an implementer.

What We're Looking For

  • Strong technical background: database schemas, REST/GraphQL API integrations, RAG/vector databases design/implementation, and automation pipelines, end-to-end and without hand-holding.
  • Fluency with AI as a development tool. You use Claude Code, Cursor, or similar and actively think about where agents can replace manual work.
  • Understanding of professional content creation (i.e., structure, tonality, narrative and ability to differentiate between great and ok content pieces).
  • Commercial mindset and strong prioritization: you know which systems move the needle and focus there.
  • Own a roadmap and hold architecture conversations with leadership.
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals; you don't need to be a traditional full-stack engineer, but you should be comfortable writing, reviewing and maintaining production code.

Why You'll Love Working With Us

  • Your work will directly enable a community of 35,000+ platform engineers worldwide.
  • Real ownership of core infrastructure, with the autonomy to shape it.
  • Fully remote across Europe, strong async culture, low-ego team.
  • Competitive salary and room to grow as our ambitions scale.

If this sounds like a fit, we would love to hear from you!
