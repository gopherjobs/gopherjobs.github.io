Qualifications

  • 4+ years experience in development of C++ PC/Console titles, 2+ years experience with Unreal Engine;
  • Solid fundamental C++ skills, algorithms and data structures, memory management, multithreading, debugging skills;

  • Experience with Unreal Procedural Content Generation is a solid plus;
  • Ability to self-manage small to mid-sized tasks and take responsibility for feature delivery;
  • Experience with version control tools (Git, Perforce) and collaborative development;
  • Clear communicator with attention to detail and polish;
  • Good communication skills in English, and ability to work with remote teams.
