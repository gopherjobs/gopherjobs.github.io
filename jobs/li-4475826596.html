Description

We are looking for an Engineering Manager to join our globally distributed team! The ideal candidate brings strong engineering leadership experience, deep technical expertise in modern web technologies, and a proven ability to balance strategic decision-making with day-to-day execution

Founded in 2004, Redux provides Managed Cloud Services and Managed Teams. With nearly two decades of experience, our work is powered by a highly skilled team of DevOps specialists, developers, systems architects, and accounting and admin professionals based across North America, Latin America, and the Asia-Pacific region.

Our people-first, founder-led operations ensure that each client receives unmatched white-glove treatment. We tailor our processes and services to meet each client’s specific needs — from shared Slack workspaces to flexible pricing models.

Technical Environment

  • Frontend: React
  • Backend: Node.js
  • Database: PostgreSQL
  • Search: Typesense
  • Cloud Platform: Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Infrastructure: Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
  • Security & Compliance: SOC 2 Type II
  • Development Model: AI-assisted and agentic engineering workflows

What You’ll Do

Lead the Team and Own Staffing

  • Directly manage, coach and develop the Engineering team, including the Platform Engineer, with clear expectations and candid feedback.
  • Own all Engineering recruiting and hiring, from defining roles and evaluating candidates through selection and onboarding.
  • Own performance management, including improvement plans and termination decisions when necessary.
  • Manage external software vendors and Engineering contractors, including performance, delivery, cost and contract coordination.

Deliver Software and Work Hands On

  • Spend approximately half of the role contributing technically through design, coding, code review, troubleshooting and production support.
  • Own Engineering execution from planning through production, including commitments, dependencies, testing and follow-through.
  • Own platform architecture with senior engineers, balancing delivery with maintainability, security, scalability and technical debt.
  • Partner with the Director of Product on scope and tradeoffs, communicate risks early and represent Engineering in occasional technical conversations with customers and suppliers.

Operate the Platform Securely

  • Own uptime, observability, incident response, root-cause follow-up, backup and recovery, release safety and production change controls.
  • Ensure cloud infrastructure and integrations are reliable, secure and delivered through infrastructure as code.
  • Own the reliability and technical operation of production data pipelines and analytics platforms.
  • Own Engineering's SOC 2 technical controls, audit evidence and remediation.

Advance AI-Enabled Engineering

  • Advance AI-assisted development and agentic engineering workflows, with clear standards for reviewing, testing, securing, documenting and owning AI-generated software.

What You Bring

  • Demonstrated success managing software engineers, including recruiting, coaching, performance management, termination decisions and oversight of external vendors or contractors.
  • Recent hands-on experience building and operating production SaaS applications using Node.js, React and relational databases.
  • Experience owning SaaS architecture and cloud operations, including infrastructure as code, CI/CD, observability, incident response and reliability.
  • Experience operating production data pipelines and analytics platforms.
  • Working knowledge of secure software development and the technical controls required by SOC 2 or a comparable compliance program.
  • Practical experience with AI-assisted development and agentic engineering workflows, with sound judgment about review and production readiness.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with the ability to communicate with engineers, business leaders, customers and suppliers in a fully remote startup.

Preferred Experience

  • Leadership experience in a small or midsize SaaS company with a distributed Engineering team.
  • Experience with Google Cloud Platform, PostgreSQL or Typesense.
  • Experience with payment or accounting integrations, inventory systems, procurement software or healthcare technology.
  • Experience improving a mature monolith while continuing to deliver customer value.
