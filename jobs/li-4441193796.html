How you'll make an impact

  • take end-to-end ownership of our backend services to deliver scalable, reliable systems powering our mobile experience
  • architect and implement robust backend solutions with Kotlin, Spring Boot, AWS, and Kotlin-based AWS Lambdas ensuring long-term performance and maintainability
  • ensure reliability and operability of your backend services by optimizing monitoring, logging, metrics, alarming, and overall service health
  • collaborate with our MaPS platform team to deploy and operate workloads on Kubernetes using modern, developer-friendly infrastructure
  • develop and document clean, efficient APIs for seamless integration with other teams and products across our ecosystem
  • act as a mentor and sparring partner for Android and cross-functional team members on backend topics to boost collaboration and knowledge sharing
  • broaden your skill set by exploring Android or iOS development and gaining insight into the full product vertical
  • play a key role in shaping technical vision, aligning architecture decisions, and continuously improving processes for greater product impact

What you'll need

  • at least five years of hands-on experience as a backend or software engineer, ideally in JVM-based backend systems
  • fluent in Kotlin and experienced with Spring Boot, having built and operated production-grade backend services
  • solid track record working with AWS cloud technologies (including Lambda and container-based workloads), plus familiarity with cloud-native patterns like IAM, S3, and messaging services
  • interested in Kubernetes, understanding concepts like deployments, observability, and containerized workloads
  • strong commitment to code quality, maintainable architectures, testing and long-term system ownership
  • enthusiasm for monitoring, transparency, and building systems that are easy to operate
  • proven strength in mentoring colleagues, building cross-team relationships, and actively promoting knowledge sharing in diverse environments
  • strong communicator who translates complex technical topics for product managers, designers, and fellow engineers
