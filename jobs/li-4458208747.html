Description

About the job:

  • Massive Scale: Design, build, and optimize systems that handle a huge distributed scale of ~120 million offer events daily. You will be working on one of the largest services at Allegro.
  • Applied AI & Machine Learning: Move beyond simple chatbots. You will work with real AI use cases, integrating ML models and AI agents to analyze images (e.g., detecting counterfeit products and supporting brand protection processes) and read the context of offers to make automated decisions.
  • Meaningful Business Impact: Build and maintain essential tools that ensure platform safety and compliance. Your work in automating offer and product verification will directly prevent legal and Terms & Conditions breaches and protect users.
  • Modern Tech Stack: Work primarily in Kotlin to build our backend services. While the backend is our core, having an interest in frontend technologies (like React, Angular, or Vue.js) is a great plus, as we value full-stack capabilities.
  • Cutting-Edge AI Infrastructure: Be part of the initiative to evaluate and build infrastructure for open-source AI models (open weights) to optimize costs. You will also help develop "AI Evals" to track model performance and prevent regressions at scale.

Join the Offer Verification team! You will have a chance to build tools that validate all merchant listings against the company policies and law regulations. Your team works with the Data Science and Machine Learning teams, utilizing AI to make real-time decisions. High performance, engineering best practices, and a great atmosphere in the team guaranteed!
