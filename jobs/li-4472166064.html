Role Overview The new talent will play a critical role in designing and developing backend systems that support IoT devices, leveraging modern DevOps practices. Works with cutting-edge technologies in Azure cloud infrastructure and a microservices architecture, building solutions that protect the lives and homes of our customers.

Team Overview The team is responsible for the end-to-end mobile experience that allows customers to configure, monitor, and control their alarm system. Their mission is to provide a seamless, secure, and intuitive experience for managing both security and smart home capabilities directly from the app.

Key user journeys include:

  • Arming and disarming the alarm system, including automation scenarios.
  • Managing smart door locks, including locking, unlocking, pairing, and removing devices such as Yale Doorman and Danalock.
  • Enabling and disabling smart plugs, as well as configuring related automation flows.
  • Managing core system settings, including room names, areas, and overall system organization.

The team owns the customer-facing experience across these journeys, ensuring reliability, ease of use, and a connected smart home ecosystem
