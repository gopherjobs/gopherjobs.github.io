About The Role We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Staff Engineer specializing in Ruby to join our client's dynamic team on a full-time basis. This remote role offers the flexibility to work from anywhere, providing an excellent opportunity for professionals looking to contribute to innovative projects in a flexible environment. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining scalable Ruby-based systems, ensuring high performance and reliability. Collaborating with cross-functional teams, including product managers, developers, and operations, the Staff Ruby Engineer will play a critical role in delivering high-quality software solutions that meet business objectives and technical standards.

Qualifications

  • Proficiency in Ruby programming language and Ruby on Rails framework.
  • Experience developing RESTful APIs and working with JSON-based services.
  • Strong understanding of database design principles and SQL optimization techniques.
  • Familiarity with testing frameworks such as RSpec or Minitest to ensure code quality.
  • Hands-on experience working with cloud platforms like AWS or similar services.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field is preferred.

Responsibilities The Staff Ruby Engineer will be responsible for a variety of technical and collaborative tasks, including:

  • Developing and maintaining Ruby on Rails applications and microservices to support business needs.
  • Optimizing application performance, scalability, and security to handle increasing user loads.
  • Collaborating with product managers and stakeholders to define technical requirements and project scope.
  • Writing clean, maintainable, and well-tested code to ensure long-term sustainability of applications.
  • Debugging and resolving complex issues in production environments promptly.
  • Participating in code reviews to uphold coding standards and best practices.
  • Implementing new features and enhancements based on evolving business requirements.
  • Ensuring seamless integration of applications with third-party services and APIs.
