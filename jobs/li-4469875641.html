We are partnering with a global leading financial services company in their search for an AI Engineer to design, build and deploy production-ready Generative AI solutions.

This is a hands-on role focused on turning AI concepts into scalable business solutions, working with Python, GCP, Vertex AI, OpenAI and Anthropic Claude.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and deploy GenAI applications including RAG, chatbots, copilots and document intelligence.
  • Develop LLM workflows and integrations using OpenAI and/or Claude APIs.
  • Build solutions on GCP, including Vertex AI, BigQuery and Cloud Run.
  • Work with structured/unstructured data, embeddings, vector databases and semantic search.
  • Implement CI/CD, monitoring, testing and production controls.
  • Ensure solutions meet Responsible AI, GDPR, security and regulatory requirements.
  • Work with Technology, Data Risk and Compliance teams to deliver practical AI solutions.

What you will bring

  • Proven experience delivering AI/GenAI solutions into production.
  • Strong Python development experience (production‑grade code).
  • Strong experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), ideally Vertex AI.
  • Hands‑on experience with ChatGPT / OpenAI APIs and/or Anthropic Claude.
  • Experience integrating AI services via APIs and building backend services.
  • Familiarity with modern software engineering practices (version control, testing, CI/CD).
  • Generative AI & LLMs
  • Practical experience with prompt engineering, system prompts and evaluation techniques.
  • Experience implementing RAG architectures, vector databases, and semantic search.
  • Experience working with structured and unstructured data
  • Financial services experience (highly desirable) or experience working in a regulated environment.
  • Pragmatic problem‑solver focused on business value, not just models
  • Strong communication skills and a pragmatic, delivery-focused approach.

Candidates must be based in the UK. Hold a British/EU Passport or Indefinite Leave to Remain. Sponsorship cannot be offered for this role.
