Description

What You’ll Do

  • Design, develop, and sustain Python-based services and platform capabilities for an internal AI platform
  • Build reusable foundations that enable multiple products across the company to create and operate AI agents
  • Collaborate closely with engineers and product partners across Mexico, the United States, and India
  • Participate in technical design discussions, code reviews, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement of engineering practices
  • Contribute to a reliable, scalable, secure, and maintainable platform for AI-powered products

What You’ll Bring

  • 5+ years of experience developing production software with Python
  • Solid understanding of cloud platforms and cloud-based application architecture; experience with Azure, AWS, or GCP
  • Good conceptual understanding of large language models and how to integrate LLM APIs into software applications
  • vely for development, review, testing, and team productivity.
  • Strong problem-solving, analytical thinking, debugging, collaboration, and mentorship skills.
  • Ability to learn new technologies and business domains quickly with strong attention to detail.

What Could Set You Apart

  • Hands-on AI or LLM development experience is a plus
  • Familiarity with PydanticAI is a plus
