About The Team The Core Platform team is part of the Internal Platform area at Docplanner. We build and maintain the core infrastructure that all Docplanner products run on. Our focus is availability, scalability, security and cost. Our goal is that product teams can ship their applications without having to deal with infrastructure complexity.

About The Role We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to join the Core Platform team. You will design, build and operate the Kubernetes platform that product teams use every day. You will work on a shared platform that serves the marketplace and several practice management systems across the Docplanner Group.

The challenges

  • Standardization. Teams across Docplanner build and run their services in different ways. You will help define one standard way to build, deploy and operate a service, and make it simple enough that teams adopt it without help from us.
  • Security by default. Every service that runs on the platform should be secure from the first deployment, without extra work from its team. You will harden the platform and its processes while keeping delivery fast for developers.
  • Availability. All Docplanner products depend on the platform. You will keep it highly available while it grows and changes, including during upgrades and incidents.
  • Developer autonomy. Product teams should be able to operate their own services without waiting for the platform team. You will replace manual requests with self-service and automation.
  • Cost efficiency. The platform should use its resources well. You will help teams see what their services cost and reduce waste.

What You Will Do

  • Build, monitor and maintain Kubernetes clusters in several regions.
  • Design and run continuous delivery with a GitOps approach.
  • Keep disaster recovery procedures current, tested and documented.
  • Automate service deployments so that they are highly available.
  • Troubleshoot critical issues and improve system performance.
  • Support your teammates and share what you know.

What You Need Must have

  • Deep Kubernetes experience in production. You understand the control plane, scheduling, autoscaling of workloads and clusters, and how a cluster heals itself.
  • AWS experience with EKS, VPC networking and IAM. You can design for high availability across zones and you know where the costs are.
  • Infrastructure as code with Terraform, used by a team through pull requests and pipelines.
  • GitOps delivery with Argo CD or a similar tool, and application packaging with Helm.
  • Network troubleshooting in Kubernetes and in the cloud: pod networking, DNS, load balancers, IP address planning.
  • Secrets management and least-privilege access in Kubernetes and in the cloud.
  • Backup and disaster recovery design, with clear recovery time and recovery point targets.
  • Using AI coding agents in your daily infrastructure work.
  • Professional working English, spoken and written. You can discuss technical topics, write documentation and take part in meetings in English.

Nice to have

  • Managing cloud resources through the Kubernetes API, for example with Crossplane.
  • Policy as code and network policies to enforce security in the cluster.
  • Cost-aware autoscaling of nodes and workloads.
  • Experience with different deployment strategies.
  • Writing your own tooling and automation in Go or Bash.
  • Building monitoring and alerting for a platform.

We value a growth mindset. Nobody meets every requirement, so apply if you are willing to learn the rest.
