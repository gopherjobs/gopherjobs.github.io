👋What are our wishes? We are looking for a Full Stack Developer with strong Cloud-Native Development and Cloud Architecture experience to design, develop, and deploy scalable, secure, and high-performing digital solutions.

The role covers both front-end and back-end development , cloud infrastructure, AWS/Azure/GCP , Docker, Kubernetes, Serverless , CI/CD, DevOps, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), REST APIs, Microservices , and SQL/NoSQL databases.

You will work with cross-functional teams to develop modern cloud-native applications, integrate systems and APIs, optimize application performance, implement security best practices, and support continuous delivery and technical improvement.
