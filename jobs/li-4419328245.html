Description

Company Founded by CPAs, tax attorneys, and engineers, Taxbit is the leading innovator automating global tax reporting for the digital economy. Taxbit's AI-enabled platform streamlines compliance related to digital assets, payments, and other financial transactions. Its unique combination of deep subject matter expertise and AI-forward technology have made it a fast-growing disruptor trusted by the world's leading fintechs, marketplaces, digital asset players, Big 4 accounting firms, traditional enterprises, and government agencies. Backed by top-tier Silicon Valley VCs, Taxbit's team is located across offices in San Francisco, New York, Seattle, Salt Lake City, London, Madrid, and Singapore.

Opportunity for Impact Taxbit is seeking a Forward Deployed Engineer to be a critical technical partner in our growth. This role is a unique hybrid, split evenly between client-facing technical leadership and core platform engineering, perfect for someone who is passionate about helping the world's largest enterprises solve complex problems and navigate the complex frontier of digital asset compliance through cutting-edge software.

This role requires professional proficiency in English. This role is based in Madrid. Candidates must be legally authorized to work in Spain or another European Union country without company-sponsored visa support (e.g., holders of a valid Residency and Work Visa or Permanent Resident status).

You will spend roughly half your time working directly with clients: providing deep technical guidance during the sales process, helping close deals, and owning the integration experience from start to finish. You will become the company's expert on our integration process, from backend data pipelines to frontend application integrations, and will develop and own the demos that bring our product to life.

The other half of your time will be spent as a key contributor to the core Taxbit platform. You will leverage the direct client insights from your field experience to identify problems, then design, build, and deliver the scalable platform solutions and new features our clients need, directly shaping our product roadmap.

We welcome individuals who are humble, hungry, and excited to tackle some of the industry's biggest challenges at the intersection of technology and client success!

Role And Responsibilities Client-Facing Engineering (Pre-Sales & Post-Sales)

  • Act as the trusted technical advisor to our most strategic clients, from individual developers to CTOs.
  • Provide expert technical guidance to our sales team and potential clients during the pre-sales cycle to help design solutions and close deals.
  • Develop, own, and maintain our suite of high-quality demos and technical documentation for use in pre- and post-sales client engagements.
  • Become a deep expert on our client integration process, mastering both backend data integration challenges and frontend application integrations.
  • Lead the post-sales technical integration, owning client problems end-to-end and building modern solutions and proofs-of-concept for complex, client-facing problems.

Core Software Engineering

  • Be the voice of the customer to Product and Engineering, synthesizing complex field-level challenges and opportunities into actionable product roadmap initiatives.
  • Contribute directly to the core Taxbit platform by building modern, scalable solutions to complex platform-level problems, including new features and enhanced integration capabilities.
  • Write well-designed, well-tested, readable, and maintainable code for both client-facing tools and the core Taxbit platform.
  • Partner with designers, product managers, sales, and other engineers to solve the right problems for customers and the platform.
  • Participate in code reviews to maintain code quality, distribute knowledge, and help team velocity.
