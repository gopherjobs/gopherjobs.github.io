Description

We are looking for a highly technical, hands-on Fullstack Developer who is comfortable going deep into complex systems, solving ambiguous problems, and owning technical outcomes end-to-end.

This is a hardcore engineering role. We are looking for someone who can independently investigate problems, make sound technical decisions, challenge assumptions, and take full responsibility for the quality and reliability of what they ship.

You will work extensively with TypeScript, React, Node.js/NestJS, distributed systems, event-driven architecture, real-time communication, and AI-assisted engineering workflows.

AI is an important part of how we work, but AI does not replace engineering judgment. You should be able to use AI aggressively to increase your productivity while still understanding, reviewing, testing, challenging, and taking ownership of the final solution.

What you'll do:

End-to-End Technical Ownership

  • Own features and technical problems

from requirement clarification through production

.

  • Understand requirements deeply before implementation; identify ambiguity, edge cases, dependencies, and technical risks.
  • Design, implement, test, deploy, monitor, and troubleshoot end-to-end features.
  • Take responsibility for production quality, reliability, performance, and maintainability.
  • Investigate unfamiliar systems and technical problems independently rather than waiting for detailed instructions.
  • Drive problems to resolution, including coordinating with other engineers when dependencies or cross-service issues are involved.

Hardcore Engineering

  • Build and evolve scalable services, APIs, and system architecture.
  • Design robust backend solutions involving concurrency, distributed systems, event-driven architecture, and real-time communication.
  • Work hands-on across the stack, with strong emphasis on backend engineering.
  • Investigate complex production issues using logs, metrics, traces, monitoring, debugging, and other observability tools.
  • Identify root causes rather than treating symptoms.
  • Make pragmatic engineering decisions balancing speed, maintainability, scalability, performance, reliability, and technical debt.
  • Improve engineering practices, tooling, automation, testing, and development workflows.

Communication & Engineering Agency

  • Proactively communicate technical decisions, progress, blockers, risks, and dependencies.
  • Ask clarifying questions when requirements are unclear instead of making assumptions and proceeding blindly.
  • Challenge unclear or technically risky requirements when necessary.
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Design, Engineering, and international stakeholders.
  • Work effectively in a distributed/remote environment with a high degree of independence.
  • Keep stakeholders informed without requiring constant follow-up.
  • Explain complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

AI-Native Engineering

  • Use AI tools and coding agents extensively throughout the engineering lifecycle: coding, architecture, debugging, investigation, testing, documentation, and code review.
  • Break down complex problems into well-scoped tasks that can be delegated to AI agents.
  • Manage multiple AI-assisted tasks or agents in parallel while preserving requirements, context, dependencies, and technical direction.
  • Maintain persistent context using structured documentation, project instructions, CLAUDE.md-style files, or equivalent approaches.
  • Review, test, challenge, and validate AI-generated code and recommendations before accepting or shipping them.
  • Detect incorrect assumptions, hallucinations, incomplete implementations, regressions, security issues, and edge cases in AI-generated output.
  • Know when to delegate to AI and when a problem requires direct engineering investigation and reasoning.
  • Continuously improve how AI is integrated into engineering workflows.
