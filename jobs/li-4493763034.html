Description

At trivago you will find those who aren't afraid of change but rather embrace it, turning every challenge into a pathway for growth. Join trivago, work with a great team, and grow with us!

Join us in making a difference As a Senior Platform Engineer in our Hybrid Platform team, you'll help shape the Kubernetes platform that runs trivago across Google Cloud and our own Düsseldorf datacenter — so on-prem feels like just another GCP region. You'll apply a software engineering mindset to infrastructure and take care of everything from bare-metal provisioning and CI runner fleets to the gateway that gives our engineers safe access to AI tools. Your work makes it easier for every engineer at trivago to ship with confidence. You'll be a trusted partner for product, data and ML teams, shape design decisions with real autonomy, work on the most challenging performance problems in our stack, and mentor others as the platform grows. trivago is a place where ambition and support go hand in hand — we hold high standards and genuinely invest in each other's growth. Ready to build the platform others build on? Apply today.

How you’ll make an impact:

  • Design, build and automate our hybrid Kubernetes platform across GKE and on-prem Rancher/RKE2, KubeVirt and MAAS bare metal — using Terraform, ArgoCD and progressive rollouts to keep change safe.
  • Use data to guide architecture decisions, from evaluating node operating systems and CI runner technologies to rightsizing nodes and workloads with our in-house tooling.
  • Investigate and solve complex performance problems — profiling bottlenecks, tuning systems and building tooling in Go or Rust — and guide initiatives that make the platform more reliable, faster and more cost-efficient for everyone who builds on it.
  • Define SLIs, alerting and runbooks with Prometheus and Grafana, run blameless RCAs together with the team when incidents happen, and, if you choose, join our paid on-call rotation.
  • Work closely with product, data, ML and developer experience teams to shape the platform roadmap and OKRs together, including secure, self-service access to AI tools through our MCP gateway.
  • Share what you know: mentor other engineers, run knowledge-sharing sessions in our guilds, and present our work internally and at industry events.

What you'll need to thrive:

  • Experience running Kubernetes in production across cloud and on-premises environments — for example, you've upgraded clusters, debugged networking or scheduling issues and been part of incident response. Experience with GKE, Rancher/RKE2, MAAS or KubeVirt is a plus, not a must.
  • Experience with infrastructure-as-code and automation (for example Terraform, GitOps and CI/CD platforms such as GitHub Actions).
  • Coding experience, ideally in Go or Rust — strong skills in another language plus the interest to learn one of them work too. You enjoy digging into problems, especially performance ones, and investigate methodically from first symptom to root cause, backing your fixes with measurements and clear design docs so others can build on them.
  • A genuine learning mindset — you approach challenges with curiosity, seek out feedback, and continuously look for ways to develop and grow, and you enjoy helping others grow too by mentoring, presenting and sharing what you've learned.
  • A performance mindset — you set ambitious goals for yourself and follow through on them, seeking feedback and support along the way.
  • An open and curious mindset toward AI tools and automation — you actively explore how AI can enhance your work, from agent-assisted engineering to the platforms that make AI tools safely available to others, and you're excited to grow alongside evolving technologies.

Stand out with: Experience with Nix/NixOS, Linux internals or networking (DNS, Istio, Tailscale).
