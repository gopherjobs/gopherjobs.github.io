Description

AI ENGINEER (FULL STACK)

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Philippines-based AI Engineer who can build and maintain complete, production-ready applications for a South African technology company.

This is a hands-on software engineering role—not a data science, research, or prototype-only position.

You will work on solutions ranging from new full-stack applications and AI-powered workflow automations to integrations with existing platforms and systems.

You will own your projects from architecture and development through deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

• Design, build, and deploy complete applications, including backend services, application programming interfaces, databases, and user-facing interfaces.

• Integrate applications with internal systems, third-party platforms, and external data sources.

• Build generative AI capabilities such as retrieval-augmented generation, structured outputs, tool use, streaming, prompt management, and model orchestration.

• Create workflow automations using n8n, custom pipelines, or event-driven architectures.

• Evaluate different large language models based on quality, cost, privacy, and response time.

• Set up logging, error tracking, monitoring, and alerts.

• Investigate production issues and continuously improve application reliability.

• Write secure, tested, maintainable, and well-documented code.

• Use AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or similar platforms as part of your development workflow.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

• At least 2 years of professional experience building and shipping full-stack applications.

• Experience maintaining real production systems—not only proofs of concept or demonstrations.

• Strong backend engineering skills using Python, Node.js with TypeScript, or a comparable language.

• Solid frontend development experience using React, Next.js, or an equivalent framework.

• Hands-on experience integrating generative AI or large language models into production applications.

• Experience working with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, open-source models, or similar technologies.

• Working knowledge of relational or non-relational databases, application programming interface design, and cloud infrastructure.

• Experience with Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, or a similar cloud platform.

• Proficiency with Git, continuous integration and continuous delivery pipelines, Docker, and modern deployment practices.

• Clear spoken and written English, including the ability to explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders.

• Comfortable working remotely from the Philippines and collaborating during agreed overlap hours with the South African team.

• A portfolio or examples of full-stack or generative AI applications you have helped take into production.

HELPFUL, BUT NOT REQUIRED

• Experience with n8n, Make, Zapier, or custom workflow orchestration.

• Familiarity with vector databases such as Pinecone, Chroma, FAISS, or Qdrant.

• Experience with retrieval-augmented generation architectures, model evaluation, or prompt management at scale.

• Experience using multiple AI agents within a software development workflow.

• Background in consulting, regulated industries, or working across different business sectors.

WHAT WE VALUE

• Ownership—you follow through from idea to production and beyond.

• Pragmatism—you balance speed with sound engineering decisions.

• Curiosity—you continue learning as AI tools and practices evolve.

• Clear communication—you make technical decisions understandable.

• A builder’s mindset—you create, test, learn, and continuously improve.

HOW TO APPLY

Please submit your résumé and links to relevant projects or technical work.

Be ready to discuss one or two applications you personally helped take into production, your specific contribution, the architecture used, how generative AI was integrated, and how you managed reliability, security, cost, or performance trade-offs.
