Description & Requirements Who we are looking for Do you want to lead the technical delivery of next-generation AI agent solutions for global organizations?

Join Deloitte’s growing Agentic AI practice and help turn ambitious AI concepts into secure, scalable and production-ready enterprise solutions.

We are looking for an experienced technical leader who combines hands-on engineering judgement with the ability to guide delivery teams and advise senior business and technology stakeholders.

You will be successful in this role if you have:

  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems or a related technical field.
  • 7+ years of hands-on experience in software engineering, systems integration, data engineering, AI/ML implementation, automation or enterprise technology delivery.
  • Experience leading technical workstreams, solution components or delivery teams in client-facing projects.
  • Strong understanding of generative AI, large language models, agentic AI, AI orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases, prompt design, guardrails and evaluation methods.
  • Strong Python skills and practical experience delivering backend and API-based solutions.
  • Experience with REST and/or GraphQL APIs, system integrations, data flows and enterprise application landscapes.
  • Experience with at least one major cloud platform: Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud Platform.
  • Experience with structured and unstructured data, document ingestion, embedding pipelines, vector stores and modern data platforms.
  • Ability to review and challenge solution architectures, technical designs, integration patterns and non-functional requirements.
  • Understanding of Git, code review, testing, CI/CD, DevOps or MLOps practices and production-readiness considerations.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into technical scope, implementation plans, estimates, risks and delivery milestones.
  • Clear communication, stakeholder management, problem-solving and coaching skills.
  • Ability to guide teams while remaining close enough to implementation details to ensure technical quality.
  • EU Work Permit

Your future role As an Agentic AI Technical Lead , you will own the technical direction and delivery of AI agent solutions across architecture, implementation, integrations, data, engineering quality and production readiness.

You will act as a trusted technical advisor to clients while leading teams and workstreams through complex delivery challenges.

In this role, you will:

  • Lead technical workstreams and solution components across local and international client engagements.
  • Own implementation approaches for AI workflows, tool calling, routing, orchestration, backend services, APIs, data layers and enterprise integrations.
  • Shape AI agent solution designs together with architects, product owners, technical leads and client stakeholders.
  • Make and document key design decisions across workflows, integrations, orchestration platforms, cloud services, data flows and enterprise connectivity.
  • Oversee the implementation of retrieval-augmented generation, prompt orchestration, guardrails, evaluation, monitoring and logging patterns.
  • Guide teams through architecture, performance, scalability, observability, security, privacy, reliability and production-readiness decisions.
  • Review implementation plans, code quality, documentation, integration design, testing approaches and deployment readiness.
  • Balance business value, technical feasibility, maintainability, scalability, risk and delivery effort.
  • Identify and resolve technical risks, blockers, dependencies and quality issues before they affect delivery outcomes.
  • Coordinate technical dependencies across Deloitte teams, client IT, platform teams and technology partners.
  • Work directly with client stakeholders to understand requirements, validate assumptions and explain solution options clearly.
  • Translate business priorities into technical scope, milestones, delivery plans and implementation risks.
  • Facilitate technical workshops, architecture discussions, demonstrations and solution walkthroughs with business and IT stakeholders.
  • Lead, coach and develop Consultants and Senior Consultants across software engineering, integration, data, AI and platform delivery.
  • Set technical priorities, assign responsibilities, review progress and support the escalation of delivery issues.
  • Develop reusable methods, accelerators, templates, playbooks and knowledge assets for Agentic AI delivery.
  • Contribute to Deloitte’s Agentic AI offering, internal capability building, proposals, demonstrations, estimates and solution narratives.
