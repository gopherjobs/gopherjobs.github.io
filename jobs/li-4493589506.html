About Us

Automation Consulting Pty Ltd is an Australian-based AI and automation consultancy helping businesses streamline operations with smart, scalable solutions.

About our Client

Our client is a dynamic Australian FinTech startup revolutionising payments. Their platform empowers businesses with cutting-edge payment solutions while offering valuable rewards. Launched in 2019, they are one of Australia’s fastest growing fintech companies.

Role Overview

We’re seeking an engineer who has grown into product thinking to join our client’s product and engineering team. This is a hybrid role, roughly 60% hands-on engineering and 40% product: you’ll help shape what gets built, not just build it.

Important: this role is with our client (an Australian FinTech), not with Automation Consulting. The work is payment platform development and does not include GenAI development.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend:React, TypeScript
  • Backend: Node.js, serverless (AWS Lambda, ECS)
  • Infrastructure: AWS, Terraform
  • Databases: PostgreSQL
  • Observability and analytics: Datadog, Amplitude, Mixpanel
  • AI tooling:agentic coding tools (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor)

Key Responsibilties

Product (around 40%)

  • Take part in discovery with Product Managers and stakeholders to understand user problems across the platform
  • Define how features should behave, including user flows, edge cases and success metrics
  • Use funnel and conversion data to find drop-off points, propose improvements and validate them through experiments such as A/B tests
  • Prioritise what to build, what to cut and what to validate first
  • Explain trade-offs and delivery options clearly to non-technical stakeholders

Engineering (around 60%)

  • Own features end to end, with a strong front-end focus on UX-heavy flows
  • Ship production code using agentic, AI-assisted development, delegating work across front-end, back-end and analytics agents, with the judgement to decide what is genuinely production-ready
  • Work across multiple parts of the platform, collaborating closely with the backend and serverless layers
  • Instrument features with analytics to measure funnel and conversion performance
  • Contribute to code reviews, architecture discussions and the team’s engineering standards

Requirement

  • 5+ years of full-stack web experience with a clear front-end lean (React, TypeScript)
  • An engineering background with growing product ownership: you’ve shaped what to build, not just how to build it
  • A track record of owning features end to end, from discovery to production
  • Hands-on experience with agentic, AI-assisted development beyond tests and documentation, with sound judgement on checking its output
  • Product mindset: comfortable using analytics tools (e.g. Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog) to measure funnels, and familiar with how A/B tests are set up and read
  • Experience with AWS and Terraform, and knowledge of PostgreSQL
  • Comfortable working in a space that’s still being defined, and helping to define it
  • Strong spoken and written English, including explaining technical topics to non-technical people
  • Comfortable working Australian business hours, ideally with experience working with Australian companies or clients
  • Proven ability to work effectively in distributed teams

Nice to have

  • Experience with growth, activation or onboarding flows
  • Experience in fintech or payments
  • Experience as a tech lead or in a green-field, no-playbook setting
