🚀 Senior DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

📍 London (Onsite)

💰 Up to £130K + Equity

We’re partnered with a fast-growing AI company building computer vision products deployed across a large and rapidly expanding fleet of connected devices.

They’re looking for a strong DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer to build the software, tooling and infrastructure powering both their cloud platform and edge estate.

This isn't just maintaining Kubernetes and Terraform — you'll be writing production software and building infrastructure systems across AWS, Kubernetes and IoT.

What You’ll Be Doing 👇

  • Build production-grade software for their internal infrastructure platform
  • Scale and evolve AWS & Kubernetes environments
  • Build provisioning and remote management systems for large fleets of edge devices
  • Develop CI/CD, deployment and automation tooling
  • Build observability and telemetry across cloud and edge infrastructure
  • Improve reliability, security and developer experience as the platform scales

Tech ⚙️

AWS • Kubernetes / EKS • Terraform / Pulumi • Python / Go • Grafana • Prometheus • IoT / Edge

What They’re Looking For 🧠

  • Strong Python or Go engineering skills
  • Production experience with AWS & Kubernetes
  • Strong Infrastructure-as-Code experience
  • Experience building CI/CD, automation and observability systems
  • Comfortable owning production infrastructure end-to-end
  • Edge / IoT experience is a bonus, not essential

Why It’s Interesting 💡

  • Much more engineering-heavy than a traditional DevOps role
  • Infrastructure spanning cloud + physical edge devices
  • Real-world AI systems operating at significant scale
  • Small engineering team with high ownership and architectural influence
  • Strong commercial traction, significant backing and meaningful equity

If you enjoy writing software as much as building infrastructure and want something more technically interesting than another cloud-only DevOps role, this is worth a conversation.
