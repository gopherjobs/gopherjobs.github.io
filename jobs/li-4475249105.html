Description

Personio's intelligent HR platform helps small and medium-sized organizations unlock the power of people by making complicated, time-consuming tasks simple and efficient. Our team of 1,500 Personios is building user-friendly products that delight our 15,000+ customers and their 1.5 million employees. Ready to make an impact from day one? This role requires 2 days a week in our Munich or Dublin office .

Join us to shape the future of software in the underserved and high-impact HR technology industry. Your work will have a direct and tangible impact on customers, offering ownership and the chance to make a meaningful difference. As we prepare for significant growth, you'll face exciting challenges and have the opportunity to influence our path toward becoming one of the world's leading tech companies.

As a Senior Staff Software Engineer (Technical Lead) in Developer Experience (DX) , you'll set organization-wide technical direction for developer productivity across Personio's entire engineering stack with an AI first mindset - from backend services in Kotlin and Java, through protos, gRPC, Kafka and GraphQL, to CI/CD and build systems, across our frontend and Node.js, React, TypeScript and PHP ecosystems . You'll be a technical multiplier: not managing people, but shaping how hundreds of engineers build, test, and ship software every day.

This is a hands-on, deeply technical role. You'll pair architectural judgment with the credibility to drive change across teams that don't report to you — reducing friction across the full software development lifecycle (SDLC) , scaling CI/CD and build infrastructure , defining and improving testing capabilities , and bringing AI-powered tooling safely into everyday engineering work. It’s not enough to just have opinions on a good developer experience, you’ll need the judgement and experience to drive adoption of those standards.

What You’ll Do Set Technical Strategy for Developer Experience

  • Own the technical strategy for DX: simplify the stack and data flow, and architect how CI/CD, build orchestration, testing frameworks, and underlying tooling evolve together.
  • Act as a technical multiplier — aligning disparate engineering groups, establishing company-wide best practices, and driving complex, multi-team initiatives without direct authority.
  • Mentor senior and staff-level engineers across DX and partner teams, raising the technical bar org-wide.

Scale CI/CD, Build, Test, and Release Systems

  • Own the technical direction for CI/CD pipelines, build orchestration, and release automation across a polyglot codebase (Kotlin, Java, TypeScript, PHP) to increase developer productivity.
  • Streamline the local development inner loop, testing frameworks (including end-to-end testing), and CI feedback cycles to accelerate how quickly engineers can ship safely.
  • Evaluate and evolve our GitHub-based workflows, tooling, and stacks to keep pace with engineering growth.

Backend Platform: Microservices, Protos, gRPC, Kafka

  • Provide technical leadership on the backbone of service-to-service communication: proto/gRPC contracts, API evolution, and Kafka-based eventing patterns that underpin our microservices architecture.
  • Partner with backend platform and product-domain teams to define standards for API design, versioning, backward compatibility, and resilience.

Frontend DX, Frameworks & AI Productivity Tools

  • Provide technical direction for the frontend developer experience: Node.js tooling, React and TypeScript frameworks, shared component and build tooling, and the developer-facing surface of our frontend platform.
  • Ensure frontend and backend DX investments are coherent, shared standards for testing, tooling, and release practices regardless of language or stack.
  • Lead the safe, scaled integration of AI-powered developer tools, internal engineering copilots, and intelligent triage or automation systems into the SDLC.
  • Evaluate emerging AI tooling and frameworks, and set guardrails and adoption patterns so teams can use them confidently and safely.

Cross-Team Leadership & Influence

  • Work closely with engineering leaders, EMs, and staff engineers across Personio to align priorities and manage dependencies across many services and teams.
  • Communicate technical strategy, trade-offs, and progress clearly to both engineering and leadership audiences.
  • Represent DX in architecture reviews and technical decision-making forums, keeping solutions pragmatic and adoptable at scale.

What You Need To Succeed Technical Depth & Breadth

  • 8+ years of software engineering experience with a deep focus on developer tooling and productivity, with experience in platform engineering or infrastructure a bonus.
  • Expert, hands-on proficiency across core languages relevant to our stack: Kotlin, Java, TypeScript.
  • Production experience with microservices architectures, including protos, gRPC, and Kafka-based eventing.
  • Comfortable operating in cloud-native environments (Kubernetes, AWS) and with Infrastructure as Code (e.g., Terraform).
  • Working proficiency with Node.js, React, and TypeScript, and an understanding of the frontend developer experience — tooling, build performance, and framework evolution.

CI/CD, Testing & Build Systems Expertise

  • Hands-on mastery of build orchestration and CI/CD platforms (e.g., GitHub Actions), including designing pipelines that scale to hundreds or thousands of engineers.
  • Experience with testing strategy, including end-to-end testing frameworks, and improving feedback-cycle speed without sacrificing safety.

AI-First Mindset

  • Practical experience with, or strong point of view on, AI-powered developer tooling (like Claude Code, Codex, Cubic) and how to roll these out safely at scale.
  • Experience building or rolling out internal AI agents or developer-facing AI tooling.

Collaboration & Influence

  • Proven track record of driving complex, multi-team technical initiatives without direct authority.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills; able to move fluidly between deep technical discussion and leadership-level framing.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with build systems (e.g., Bazel, Nx) at scale.
  • Experience with PHP in a production environment.
  • Contributions to open-source developer tooling or CI/CD ecosystems.
  • Experience in a fast-growing, multi-stack engineering organization.
