  • Create scripts and solutions to facilitate automation and efficient data processing
  • Ensure systems are optimized for cloud compute infrastructure
  • Work within project requirements to develop systems that align with the overall vision
  • Assist in data acquisition and processing projects
  • Debug and optimize technical issues in graphics pipelines and models

  • 3+ years of experience in film, TV, or game production, working closely with modelers, effects technical directors, riggers and animators

  • Experience in extracting and managing large volumes of data from Maya
  • Deep experience in designing, maintaining and evolving computer graphics pipelines
  • Strong understanding of procedural principles, style languages, and complexity
  • Proven ability to work within cross-functional teams on productions and projects
  • Inclusive mindset and excels at collaboration

  • Competence with modern cloud compute infrastructure
  • Passion for innovation and improving production processes
