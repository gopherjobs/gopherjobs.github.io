Senior Golang Developer (Remote | Good English) I. Company Description:

Asian Technology Solutions (ATS) Vietnam is a trusted regional consulting and technology partner, helping organizations navigate digital transformation, operational complexity, and evolving security challenges with confidence. With a strong commitment to innovation, integrity, excellence, and collaboration, ATS Vietnam delivers integrated solutions across Talent Solutions, IT Consulting, Technology Services, Cyber Security, and Fraud Prevention.

With a growing presence across Asia-Pacific and the Middle East, ATS Vietnam combines deep regional market expertise with global best practices to help businesses build high- performing teams, modernize technology ecosystems, strengthen cyber resilience, and accelerate sustainable growth.

II. Job Overview:

We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Golang Engineer to join our remote team and build scalable backend systems and distributed platforms. You will work on complex, high- performance systems and collaborate with international teams throughout the development lifecycle.

III. Key Responsibilities:

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and distributed systems using Golang.
  • Build and improve event-driven architectures and scalable APIs.
  • Ensure system reliability, performance, scalability, and fault tolerance.
  • Debug, troubleshoot, and optimize systems in production environments.
  • Apply best practices for concurrency, resilience, and distributed system design.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional and international teams to deliver complex technical
  • solutions.

• Leverage AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot to improve development productivity.

  • Experience designing and building event-driven architectures.
  • Strong experience debugging and troubleshooting production systems.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot or similar.
  • Good English communication skills, both written and spoken.
