Role Overview:

We are seeking a  Senior Director of Software Engineering to lead the architecture, development, and global scale-out of our Security Service Edge (SSE) platform.

This role is responsible for building and evolving our cloud-native security platform that delivers comprehensive protection across users, devices, applications, data, and network traffic — spanning branch, hybrid, multi-cloud, and internet environments.

The ideal candidate is a seasoned engineering leader with deep expertise in distributed systems, cloud-native architecture, and enterprise security platforms. You will shape the long-term technical strategy, drive execution excellence, and build a high-performing engineering organization in India that supports a globally deployed SaaS platform.

Key Responsibilities: •    Define and execute the long-term engineering roadmap for the SSE platform aligned with business objectives.

•    Build, mentor, and scale high-performing engineering teams.

•    Establish strong architectural governance, engineering best practices, and quality standards.

•    Champion secure-by-design principles across application code, infrastructure, and CI/CD pipelines.

•    Partner closely with Product Management to evolve core SSE capabilities including Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA), Secure Web Gateway (SWG), CASB, policy engines, traffic inspection, analytics, and identity integrations.

•    Recruit and retain top engineering talent in cloud, distributed systems, and cybersecurity domains.

•    Foster a culture of ownership, innovation, operational rigor, and continuous improvement.

 

Required Skills & Experience: •     15+ years in software development and 5+ years in engineering leadership •    Proven experience building and scaling SaaS engineering teams.

•    Strong track record of delivering complex, cloud-native security or networking products.

•     Deep understanding of SSE/SASE architecture principles and zero-trust frameworks. •    Strong background in distributed systems, microservices architectures, and hyperscale environments (AWS, Azure, GCP).

•    Experience with identity systems (SSO, MFA, OAuth, SAML) , policy engines, and traffic inspection frameworks.

•    Familiarity with DevOps best practices, CI/CD pipelines, observability, reliability engineering, and automation.

•    Proficiency in modern programming languages such as Go and Python.
