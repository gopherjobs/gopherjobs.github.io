Software Engineer – Payments

UK | Remote or Hybrid

Northreach is partnering with a growing payments technology business to recruit a Software Engineer to help develop secure, scalable and highly reliable payment products.

This is an opportunity to work on systems responsible for moving money, processing transactions and connecting businesses with banks, card schemes and payment providers.

You will join a collaborative engineering team and take ownership of software from initial design through to production. The role will suit someone who enjoys solving complex technical problems and understands the importance of reliability, security and accuracy when working with financial transactions.

The role

You will design, build and improve the services that support payment processing, transaction management, reconciliation and integrations with external financial institutions.

You will work closely with engineering, product, operations and compliance teams to translate business requirements into reliable technical solutions.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Designing, developing and maintaining secure payment services and APIs.
  • Building scalable backend systems capable of processing high volumes of financial transactions.
  • Developing integrations with banks, payment gateways, acquirers, card schemes and third-party providers.
  • Supporting payment authorisation, settlement, reconciliation, refunds and exception-management processes.
  • Writing clean, well-tested and maintainable production code.
  • Contributing to technical design, architecture and engineering standards.
  • Monitoring production systems and investigating performance, reliability or payment-processing issues.
  • Building appropriate safeguards around financial data, transaction accuracy and system security.
  • Participating in code reviews and supporting the development of other engineers.
  • Working with product and operational teams to understand customer requirements and improve the payment experience.
  • Identifying opportunities to improve automation, platform performance and development processes.
  • Taking ownership of features throughout the full software-development lifecycle.

What we’re looking for

You will have commercial software-engineering experience and a track record of building reliable backend applications, APIs or distributed systems.

You will also bring:

  • Strong experience with at least one modern backend language such as Java, Kotlin, Go, Python, C# or TypeScript.
  • Experience designing and developing RESTful APIs and microservices.
  • An understanding of distributed systems, asynchronous processing and event-driven architecture.
  • Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
  • Knowledge of relational databases and data-modelling principles.
  • Experience with automated testing, continuous integration and deployment.
  • A strong understanding of software security, reliability and observability.
  • The ability to investigate complex technical issues and communicate solutions clearly.
  • A collaborative, pragmatic and ownership-focused approach.
  • The confidence to work in a fast-moving environment where priorities may evolve.

Desirable experience

  • Previous experience within payments, fintech, banking or another regulated financial-services environment.
  • Knowledge of card payments, bank transfers, payment gateways or acquiring.
  • Experience with payment authorisation, clearing, settlement or reconciliation.
  • Understanding of ledgers, transaction lifecycles and financial-data integrity.
  • Experience with Kafka or another message-broker technology.
  • Experience using Kubernetes, Docker and infrastructure-as-code tools.
  • Knowledge of PCI DSS or other payment-security requirements.
  • Experience building systems where availability, latency and transaction accuracy are business-critical.

The opportunity

This role offers the opportunity to solve meaningful engineering problems within a complex and rapidly evolving industry.

You will contribute to systems that process real financial transactions, influence technical decisions and help build payment infrastructure that is secure, scalable and dependable.
