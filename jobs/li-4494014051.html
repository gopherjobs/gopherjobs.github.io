Description

Trackunit is a fast-growing company and a leading supplier of IoT solutions, operating out of many offices worldwide. Trackunit specializes in digital solutions that connect machines, people, and systems in the global construction industry. The construction industry is the least digitalized industry of all. This lack of digitalization has left construction far behind a global economy on the rise and placed the industry as one of the least productive industries too.

We are looking for a Backend or Fullstack Software Engineer who is curious about building with AI, to build features that enable customers to act on their machines remotely. You work side by side with Product, Design, and our firmware team, and your work ends up on real machines in the field, as we move into an agentic development lifecycle. Whether you have a couple of years of experience or a few more, we would like to see your application.

Send a command, change a machine. Customers use remote commands to change settings on a machine and to verify the outcome. You build the Java code that powers that, work with the firmware team through APIs, test end to end on real hardware and devices, and help integrate the feature into our MCP server so customers can use their own AI tools with it.

With locations in the hearts of London, ON and Kitchener, ON we offer you a flexible setup with the possibility to work from one of our locations or remotely from anywhere in Ontario. No relocation assistance is offered for this position.

The base salary hiring range for this role is $80,000–$110,000 CAD per year , depending on experience and qualifications.

Once in the role, successful candidates will have the opportunity to progress through the full salary range based on performance and development over time. This range reflects base salary only and does not include additional elements of our total rewards offering, such as our remote work policy, health benefits, retirement savings, and other rewards.

What's in it for you?

  • Build with AI every day. The team works in an agentic development lifecycle, so AI is part of your daily work.
  • See your work reach a machine. Your work does not stay on a screen: you see the physical result on real machines.
  • Learn the business, not just the code. Working with product, design and firmware colleagues, you learn who our customers are and what they need to succeed.
  • Shape the product. Remote commands are in beta and still being broadened, so your ideas and your work directly influence how the feature takes shape on its way to general availability.

Who are you ideally? You are a Backend or Fullstack engineer who cares that what you build works on a real machine, and who is excited about how AI is changing software development.

  • Hands-on experience building backend services in Java. Java is our team's stack: you will use it every day to build the configuration behind remote commands and the APIs that connect to our firmware team.
  • You are using AI in your development, or are keen to: some hands-on use, at work or on your own, and a real interest in AI development.
  • Experience with databases and SQL, and the design sense to shape the services and data around them and explain your choices.
  • You work well with others: you learn the problem and the customer with a product manager and a designer, keep them updated on where you are, accept feedback and stay comfortable when things change quickly.
  • Nice to have: experience with an agentic workflow, Kafka or other streaming or messaging, and experience working with hardware teams or in IoT. Domain knowledge can be learned.

AI is part of how our team builds software, and we are moving into an agentic development lifecycle. Working with an agentic workflow is a big plus, but not required, since it is still so new; curiosity and a willingness to learn matter more than any specific tool.

Don't meet every single requirement? No worries, this is what we're looking for ideally. If you're excited about this role but your background doesn't align perfectly with every point above, we encourage you to apply.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Build the Java code that lets customers create remote commands and send them to devices, and ensure they work.
  • Integrate remote commands into our MCP server so customers can use their AI tools with the feature.
  • Work with the firmware team through APIs and test end to end on real hardware and devices, not only in software.
  • Make command creation a general, configurable feature in the UI, replacing low-level configuration done by technicians and support staff today.
  • Work with product managers and designers to break down new requests, covering risks and design considerations, and keep them updated on status.
