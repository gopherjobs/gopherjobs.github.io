As one of the fastest-growing software companies in the country, AlertMedia is looking for a Sr. Software Engineer with full-stack experience. This role is an opportunity to use a wide variety of design and technical web skills to help us develop and support features for the AlertMedia product suite. As an AI-forward company, we actively use modern AI tools such as Claude, Codex, and other emerging technologies to help our engineers work smarter, move faster, and build better products.

Who you are: You thrive in a collaborative engineering environment that plays to everyone's strengths with a willingness to work across the entire stack. You’ve developed scalable production web applications in Python and/or Elixir . You care about the quality of your work, the impactful product you help build, and the team you help build it with. You have AWS (or other cloud) experience and are willing to learn from people with both more and less experience than you. You are a user of AI-assisted development tools that improve engineering workflows, code quality, and team productivity.

What you get to do every day:

  • Design and develop features for the AlertMedia product suite
  • Learn the AlertMedia platform and tools, and its uses for our customers
  • Work with Python, Elixir, JavaScript, Next.js, React and other web technologies
  • Use AI-enabled tools such as Claude, Codex, and other emerging platforms to support development, problem-solving, documentation, and productivity
  • Work in an Agile based environment using tools like Git, JIRA, Jenkins, and Slack
  • Contribute to the technology lead that AlertMedia has over other solutions through innovative development and by helping contribute new ideas related to leveraging technology in our product line
  • Use strong verbal and written communication skills in collaboration with other engineering and product teams
  • Contribute to AlertMedia’s culture, values and vision for the future

What you bring to the role:

  • 6-8 years of experience building scalable web applications, Elixir/Python/Django/JavaScript tech stack preferred
  • Experience with Linux-based containers using tools like Docker or Kubernetes
  • Experience with Amazon Web Services (or other cloud services) is required
  • PostgreSQL, Redis, and other storage technologies experience required

  • The ability and desire to work in a fast-paced challenging environment
  • Outstanding communication skills and ability to effectively work cross-functionally in a fast-paced challenging environment
  • Strong English proficiency (written and oral) is required
