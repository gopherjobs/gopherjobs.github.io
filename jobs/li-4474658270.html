Description

About The Team Breakneck Studio – an incubator and accelerator scaling digital and e-commerce brands at high velocity. It's home to our Co-Found program, where ambitious founders take products from zero to market in months, not years. This is where you get hands-on, zero-to-scale experience you won't find in a regular job

We are a fast-scaling travel startup with strong product-market fit ($1.46M ARR and growing) building Marlow AI — an AI-assisted trip planning experience that hands off to a real human advisor to lock in the details and book the trip accurately. We are two-sided: travelers get a consumer product that plans and books real trips, and advisors get a SaaS-style subscription with certification and qualified leads baked into the offer.

Currently, we are looking for a Founding Engineer — someone who can own features end-to-end, from database schema through the server function layer, the UI, and the tracking that tells us whether it worked, and ship them without needing a hand-off. This is one of the first technical hires on the team: your fingerprints will be on the architecture, the integrations, and the decisions that shape how we scale from here.

Get ready to

  • Own features end-to-end: schema design → server function → UI/UX → event tracking → ship to production, using our stack of React 19, TanStack Start, Supabase (Postgres + RLS), and Cloudflare Workers
  • Work directly in the Marlow AI trip-planning flow and the advisor handoff/booking flow, across both the traveler-facing and advisor-facing surfaces
  • Design and evolve Postgres schemas with Supabase, including RLS policies and GRANTs that actually hold up under real traffic
  • Integrate and maintain third-party APIs — flights (Kiwi Tequila), hotels (Hotelston SOAP), payments (Primer, PayPal Marketplace), subscriptions/leads (Kilo Launchpad) — including auth, webhooks, and signature verification
  • Build and refine LLM-powered tooling inside Marlow's orchestration layer (tool-calling, streaming, prompt iteration) across OpenAI, Anthropic, and OpenRouter
  • Instrument features against our unified event schema (view_item, add_to_cart, initiate_checkout, add_payment_info, payment_complete) so product and growth decisions are backed by real data
  • Ship on Cloudflare's edge runtime and reason clearly about SSR vs. client boundaries in TanStack Start
  • Write tests where they earn their keep, and work alongside AI-agent dogfooding
  • Take ownership of ambiguous problems, make a call, and move — this is a one-engineer-per-feature environment, not a hand-off pipeline
  • Help shape technical direction as we grow — you won't be handed a spec and a backlog forever; as the team grows, expect a voice in architecture calls and, over time, a hand in shaping the engineering team itself

We expect you to

  • Have 3–7 years shipping production web apps, including at least one seed / Series A or B startup as an early hire (employee #2–15)
  • Be comfortable being the only engineer on a feature, from DB migration to UI/UX, with no one else to lean on
  • Have personally shipped something using Supabase, Postgres RLS, or pgvector — and understand why RLS-plus-GRANT is non-negotiable, not optional hardening
  • Be TypeScript-native: you write it and read it without flinching, not "I've used TS before"
  • Have hands-on experience with at least one meta-framework (Next.js, Remix, TanStack Start, SvelteKit) and understand SSR vs. client boundaries
  • Have integrated at least 10 third-party APIs, including auth, webhooks, and signature verification — payment processors, comms platforms, legacy SOAP vendors, or similar
  • Have strong communication skills and be comfortable working directly with founders and cross-functional stakeholders

Bonus points for

  • LLM tool-calling experience: function calling, streaming, prompt versioning
  • Experience on edge/Workers runtimes
  • SQL beyond CRUD: window functions, CTEs, triggers
  • Exposure to supervised fine-tuning for AI
