Key Responsibilities

  • Under the guidance of a mentor, participate in requirements analysis and technical solution design for assigned product areas, and drive solutions through to implementation. Take ownership of module development, testing, deployment, and post-launch troubleshooting, with accountability for code quality and delivery outcomes.
  • Manage project tasks and priorities effectively, ensuring smooth delivery throughout the development lifecycle. Collaborate closely with Product, QA, Operations, and other cross-functional teams, proactively communicating progress, identifying and escalating risks, and ensuring high-quality, on-time delivery.

Strong foundation in computer science fundamentals, including data structures and algorithms, operating systems, computer networks, and databases.
