Job Description As a Product Engineer, you will contribute to the delivery of reliable, scalable applications that support our clients and business priorities. Working within a collaborative development environment, you will apply sound engineering practices to solve technical challenges and help create integrated solutions from concept through implementation.

What You'll Be Doing

  • You will participate in the research, design, development, integration, testing and deployment of software application.
  • You will need to design, develop and support RESTful APIs and system integration.
  • You will conduct technical analysis and design of application module & database.
  • You will need to ensure developed modules comply with established architecture standards, coding standards and quality requirement.
  • You will participate in client meetings, requirement gathering sessions, solution reviews and technical workshops to support project delivery and system integration.
  • You will be reporting to the Head of software development.

Qualifications What you'll bring to the team

  • Must have a Degree / Advanced Diploma in Computer Science or equivalent. We will assess candidates for the appropriate role level based on their relevant experience, technical expertise and professional qualifications.
  • At least 4 years of experience in software development, system design and application support.
  • Proficiency in modern front-end technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and jQuery and Angular.
  • Proficiency in back-end development using PHP and MySQL. Knowledge or experience in C# and .NET technologies.
  • Hands-on experience designing/ developing, documenting, testing and supporting RESTful APIs using XML/JSON.
  • Experience designing, implementing and securing solutions using cloud services such as AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
  • Experience with CodeIgniter, and MVC architecture
  • Experience with Agile, SCRUM, Software Development Life Cycle and software design concepts.
  • Proficiency in CI/CD tools, e.g. Jenkins, Github.
