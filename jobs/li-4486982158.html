Position Overview And Objective The Developer will contribute to our Engineering organization by designing and building next-generation security operations automation platforms that improve detection reliability, incident response speed, and operational efficiency. This role combines advanced backend engineering, cloud-native architecture, cybersecurity automation, and applied artificial intelligence to develop intelligent systems that strengthen Arctic Wolf’s security operations capabilities.

As a Developer, you will help define architectural direction, mentor engineers, and drive innovation across automation frameworks, detection pipelines, and artificial intelligence-powered security workflows.

Responsibilities As a Developer at Arctic Wolf, you will:

  • Deliver well-scoped backend features for real-time threat analysis services with minimal supervision.
  • Design, develop, test, and maintain microservices as part of an agile engineering team.
  • Write clean, reliable production code with automated tests and clear handling of edge cases.
  • Deploy containerized services using CI/CD, AWS, Kubernetes/EKS, and Infrastructure-as-Code practices.
  • Participate in code reviews, design discussions, and operational support for owned services.
  • Continuously learn, improve, and contribute to team engineering standards.

Required Qualifications

  • Minimum 1 year of professional software development experience.
  • Proficiency in Python and Golang programming, with the ability to develop and maintain production-quality applications.
  • Hands-on experience working with Kubernetes (Amazon EKS preferred) for container orchestration and deployment.
  • Exposure to Generative AI technologies, including frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative engineering environment.
  • Interest in building innovative solutions at the intersection of AI, cloud technologies, and scalable software systems.
