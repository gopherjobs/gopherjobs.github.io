Job Summary We are seeking a passionate, intellectually curious, and technically skilled Domain Consultant/Solutions Engineer to join our growing DevOps Domain Consulting team. In this role, you will be the trusted technical advisor and subject matter expert to our customers, with a specific focus on Application-Level and Agentic AI secrets management.

This role involves working closely with the sales team, product management, and engineering teams to better position, demonstrate, and evaluate Idira solutions to customers. Your mission is to help customers secure machine identities and secrets across their DevOps pipelines, cloud-native applications, AI Agents and hybrid infrastructure. Idira Domain Consultants work with their business unit sales counterparts and are responsible for establishing the value of Idira solutions to prospective and current customers through demos, presentations, meetings, and Proof of Concepts (POCs). The Domain Consultant helps create successful and satisfied customers by building relationships and technical trust with customers and is seen internally and externally, as a technical expert on Idira products in their category, as well as an industry expert in identity and secrets management.
