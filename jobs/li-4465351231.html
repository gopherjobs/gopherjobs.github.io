Description

We are looking for a Senior Java Developer to support software delivery and technical support activities in the insurance technology sector. This role focuses on diagnosing complex application issues, contributing to software fixes, supporting integration workstreams and ensuring reliable service for enterprise IT environments.

JOB DETAILS

Portugal | Remote | Insurance Technology

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Analyze technical incidents, application issues and support requests, proposing appropriate and sustainable solutions.

- Contribute to the preparation of functional and technical specifications for software corrections, enhancements and support activities.

- Ensure quality in the handling of bug reports, change requests and technical assistance requests.

- Escalate risks, alerts and operational progress to the relevant service management stakeholders.

- Support the resolution of software fixes in coordination with development, research and integration teams.

- Investigate application logs, production behavior and performance indicators to support root cause analysis and service continuity.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field.

- Senior experience in Java development, with a strong background in application maintenance, technical support or software delivery environments.

- Solid ability to work with complex enterprise applications, incident resolution and production-oriented debugging.

- Collaborative, responsible and solution-oriented mindset, with the ability to communicate clearly with technical and service stakeholders.

- Interest in building domain knowledge within the insurance technology sector through structured onboarding and continuous learning.

TECHNICAL SKILLS

- Java 11 and the Spring ecosystem.

- Java EE concepts and enterprise application development.

- Monolithic application architecture and maintenance.

- REST APIs and systems integration.

- Relational databases, including Oracle, SQL Server and PostgreSQL.

- SQL query writing and optimization, including execution plans and indexing.

- Maven, Git, Jenkins and JUnit.

- HTML5, CSS and JavaScript.

- Agile methodologies, including Scrum or SAFe.

- Ticket management and incident tracking tools, such as Jira or equivalent.

- Application servers, including WebSphere, JBoss, WildFly or Tomcat.

- Heap dump and thread dump analysis for memory and performance diagnostics.

- Application log analysis and production debugging practices.

WHAT WE OFFER

- Private health insurance from your first day, with optional family extension.

- Continuous training and certification support to strengthen long-term professional growth.

- Flexibility whenever the role and context allow it, supporting sustainable performance and daily balance.

- Team moments, shared milestones and proximity with leadership when it matters most.

- Upskilling opportunities in quality management, information security, AI governance and cybersecurity best practices, where relevant to your role and development path.
