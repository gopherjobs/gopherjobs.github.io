Purpose of the role In 18 months, you own backend services that customers use every day, and you are ready for the next level rather than waiting for it. This is a hands-on backend role in one of our product teams for someone early in their career who is already shipping code professionally and wants to get senior fast. It is not a role for someone who wants to sit at this level for years.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Building, testing and shipping backend services that power products our customers use every day
  • Writing clear, well-tested code that you and your teammates will be happy to maintain
  • Reviewing other engineers' code and learning from theirs
  • Pairing with senior engineers on the harder problems and levelling up along the way
  • Taking ownership of what you do not yet know, asking good questions and seeking feedback
  • Using AI tools as part of your daily workflow (and helping the team get better at using them!)

What You Will Need To Be Successful

  • Professional coding experience - You have been actively developing software in a professional setting. We do not mind which language: our stack is mostly Python and you will pick it up quickly
  • Solid fundamentals - Data structures, basic algorithms, object-oriented programming, testing and version control
  • Growth mindset - You treat gaps in your knowledge as opportunities, not weaknesses and go and find what you do not know, rather than waiting to be told
  • Hunger to progress - You want to be a Senior Engineer, you want to get there as fast as you reasonably can and you are up for putting in the work
  • Good communication - You can explain what you are working on, ask for help when you are stuck, and give useful feedback in code reviews
  • You work AI-first - You will use AI daily here, and we mean daily. You do not need to arrive an expert, but you do need to arrive curious, experiment fast, and take ownership of getting good quickly. People who wait to be trained will find this uncomfortable
