Role Overview As a Staff Engineer (Backend) in the Platform team, you set the technical direction for the shared foundations that every Ajaib product runs on: identity, core services, API standards, observability and developer tooling. You treat the platform as a product whose customers are other engineers, solve the hardest cross-team problems, and make engineers across the company stronger. You stay hands-on where it matters most.

  • Define the technical direction and architecture for platform systems that span many teams
  • Lead the design of scalable, secure, multi-tenant services and review critical designs from other teams
  • Set API and contract standards across the company: versioning, backward compatibility, deprecation and documentation
  • Lead the architecture of identity and access: authentication, authorization, token handling and service-to-service security
  • Design for reliability: service level objectives, rate limiting, isolation between consumers, graceful degradation and disaster recovery
  • Build the golden paths that make good engineering the easy path: service templates, shared libraries, CI/CD standards and observability defaults
  • Plan and lead large migrations across many teams, with clear timelines, tooling and support
  • Identify technical risks early, weigh trade-offs and drive decisions that fit business and regulatory needs
  • Lead investigations and root cause analysis for complex production incidents, and drive lasting improvements
  • Partner with Engineering Managers, Product, Security, Infrastructure and Architecture on cross-team initiatives
  • Mentor Senior Engineers and help grow technical leadership across the company
  • Stay hands-on with implementation for critical components and complex problems

Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
  • Experience: 8+ years of professional software engineering, with a proven record of designing and running platform or shared services used by many teams, and leading technical work across teams
  • Languages: Highly proficient in Java, Go or another modern backend language
  • System Design & Architecture: Strong expertise in microservices, event-driven architectures, multi-tenant design, reliability engineering, observability and performance optimization
  • Security: Deep knowledge of authentication and authorization (Keycloak / Ory Stack, OAuth2, OpenID Connect, JWT, mTLS) and secure system design
  • Data & Messaging: Deep practical experience with Kafka, Redis, and relational and NoSQL databases, including query tuning and data consistency
  • Infrastructure: Experience designing and operating applications on cloud platforms (GCP, AWS) and Kubernetes
  • Mindset: Product mindset toward internal users. Strong communication, with the ability to influence technical decisions without direct authority. Experience in fintech or another regulated environment is a plus
