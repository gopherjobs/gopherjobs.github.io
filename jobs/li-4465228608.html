Senior Software Engineer

HRCap, Inc. • Seoul, South Korea • fulltime
Back to Home Apply
Remote: No • Relocation: Yes

Description

About the Job

Our client is an elite, high-growth global technology enterprise specializing in hyper-scale digital platforms, AI-driven developer tooling, and cross-border creator economies. We are seeking an autonomous, highly technical Senior Software Engineer to anchor our core engineering hub in Seoul, South Korea . In this mid-to-senior level role, you will lead the architectural design, development, and scaling of critical backend systems and user-facing infrastructure handling millions of daily active users.

Why Join Us

  • Global Scale & Modern Stack: Drive the technical vision for applications operating at a massive, global scale utilizing microservices, Kubernetes orchestration, and cloud-native architectures.
  • Seoul-Based Global Hub: Work from the heart of Seoul’s bustling tech district (Teheran-ro/Gangnam) in a progressive, tech-forward corporate environment that blends the speed of Silicon Valley with the precision of Korean tech innovation.
  • Elite Leadership Velocity: Take ownership of entire engineering epic tracks, mentor junior developers through active code reviews, and fast-track your path toward Engineering Management or Staff Architecture.

Responsibilities

  • System Architecture & Development: Design, write, and deploy robust, highly scalable, and secure full-stack or backend applications (typically using Java, Go, or Python/Node.js) capable of handling massive concurrency.
  • Cross-Border Architectural Integration: Lead the localization, API refactoring, and integration of global codebases to ensure flawless performance and zero latency for both Western and APAC infrastructure.
  • Technical Mentorship & Code Quality: Establish high engineering standards by conducting meticulous peer code reviews, optimizing continuous integration (CI/CD) pipelines, and enforcing rigorous unit/integration testing methodologies.
  • System Optimization & Scaling: Proactively identify and resolve systemic bottlenecks, optimize database querying frameworks, and engineer microservices to scale seamlessly across global cloud infrastructures (AWS/GCP).
  • Cross-Functional Communication: Serve as the technical liaison between global product managers and localized development pods, translating high-level business goals into scalable technical blueprints in both English and Korean.

Requirements

  • Education & Engineering Literacy: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a highly related technical discipline.
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of proven professional experience as a full-time software engineer, with a track record of building and shipping production-grade distributed systems.
  • Language Fluency: Professional or native-level verbal and written proficiency in both Korean and English is mandatory. You must be entirely comfortable running technical sprint planning in English and mentoring localized teams in Korean.
  • Core Technical Mastery: Deep, production-tested expertise in at least one core backend ecosystem (e.g., Java/Spring Boot, Go, Python/Django, or Node.js/TypeScript) paired with expert command of data structures, system design patterns, and algorithms.
  • Database & Cloud Ecosystems: Strong hands-on experience designing schemas and optimizing relational (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and non-relational (Redis, MongoDB) databases within cloud-native environments (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
  • Location Comfort: Must be currently residing in South Korea or fully authorized to work in Seoul without requiring immediate initial relocation/visa processing (or eligible for standard F-series/E-series visa transfers).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience working in a globally distributed engineering team or a multinational tech enterprise.
  • Hands-on expertise with containerization and orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes) and stream-processing infrastructure (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
  • Practical familiarity with front-end architectures (React, Vue.js, or TypeScript) to seamlessly support full-stack engineering tracks.

Job Details

  • Employment Type: Full-Time, Permanent
  • Location: Seoul, South Korea (Hybrid)
  • Salary Range: ₩85,000,000 – ₩120,000,000 KRW base structure + Performance Bonus, Equity/Stock Options, Corporate Health, and Wellness Perks.
