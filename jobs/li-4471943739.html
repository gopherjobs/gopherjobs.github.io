Description

How you can make your mark

Make your mark as software developer at Boskalis by taking complete responsibility for software development for our Survey department!

Boskalis is a world-class maritime services provider and represents the leading edge of solutions for major maritime challenges. Technical innovations are crucial for offering efficient and sustainable solutions to these challenges. With projects worldwide and a fleet of 600 vessels and equipment, Boskalis has a lot to offer you as a software developer in the field of technical software and systems. In this role, you will primarily work on software used by hydrographic surveyors. These surveyors, work for all divisions within Boskalis: dredging & inland infrastructure, offshore energy, towage, and salvage. This means you will encounter a wide range of software-related issues.

Your Responsibilities As Software Developer Based on the needs of the Survey department, you'll identify the software that needs to be developed. You'll inventory and analyze the required functionality and then be responsible for designing, building, testing, and documenting the software.

The software solutions are often state-of-the-art and require you to have a good understanding of data processing and visualization. A certain level of mathematical knowledge will also be useful in this role. Whether it's existing or newly developed technology, you're up to the task. Developments are moving rapidly, and Boskalis is eager to innovate, so we expect you to be flexible and up-to-date on the latest trends in technical software development.

Your Qualities And Experience You can move ahead as a software developer if you have:

  • A Bachelor's degree in technology or computer science.
  • Preferably 5-10 years of relevant work experience in a similar role where you independently developed (technical) software.
  • Knowledge of applied development methods, languages, and tools, such as Linux and C++. - Demonstrable experience with object-oriented information analysis, software design, development, and documentation.
  • Excellent command of both Dutch and English.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Comfortable working abroad for short periods.

Knowledge and experience with one or more of the following is a plus:

  • Dredging processes and equipment or the maritime sector.
  • Hydrography and/or survey instrumentation, equipment, and methods.
  • Data processing and visualization.
  • GIS system and CAD systems.

You communicate effortlessly at various levels and are capable of thinking outside the box. Working under pressure is no problem for you; the software you develop is often crucial for delivering survey data on time, making it essential to ensure this process runs smoothly and quickly.
