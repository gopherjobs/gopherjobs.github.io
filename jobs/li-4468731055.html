The team builds and deploys novel AI-enabled solutions that directly improve productivity and increase delivery quality for our clients. About The Role We are seeking a strong Software Engineer with Python expertise to support and build Generative AI and agentic AI solutions on Microsoft Azure . This role is ideal for someone who has hands-on experience building production-grade AI applications using the Microsoft Agent Framework , modern backend engineering practices, and secure cloud-native architectures.

This role covers both the AI-enabled automation of service delivery processes, i.e. tax and immigration services, as well as the use of AI capabilities to streamline functional / back-office processes (e.g. Finance, HR etc.).

The ideal candidate has experience designing and deploying agentic systems , building evaluation harnesses , integrating tools and workflows, and implementing Responsible AI and security controls for enterprise use cases.
