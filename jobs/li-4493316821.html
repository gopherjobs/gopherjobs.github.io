The role This company builds the multimodal data and evaluation platform powering model development for hundreds of leading AI teams, from autonomous vehicles to frontier robotics. Backed by top-tier global venture firms with significant growth funding, they provide the essential infrastructure that bridges raw visual and sensory data with production AI performance.

You will join the in-house ML team sitting firmly on the engineering side, owning the architecture, deployment, and reliability of vision and multimodal models in production. This is not a role for prompt wrapping or tweaking hyperparameters in notebooks. It is a systems-first software engineering seat where you will solve hard GPU utilization, latency, and throughput bottlenecks to deliver real-time model inference and closed-loop data feedback at massive scale.

The work

  • Architect and run low-latency serving infrastructure for heavy vision and multimodal models, delivering real-time interactive predictions directly into high-throughput annotation workflows.
  • Build scalable offline inference pipelines processing millions of hours of egocentric video for robotics customers, balancing GPU cluster costs, throughput, and fault tolerance.
  • Productionise agentic quality-control and active learning workflows, transforming fragile research prototypes into resilient, self-healing platform features.
  • Design dynamic model integration layers that gracefully handle rapid, customer-defined ontology shifts without requiring bespoke one-off code.
  • Profile and optimise end-to-end model serving performance across PyTorch, CUDA, and Kubernetes environments on AWS and GCP.

What You Bring

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience with deep expertise building and running production ML systems or model-backed distributed infrastructure at scale.
  • Hands-on mastery of deep learning model serving and inference optimisation, including GPU utilisation, dynamic batching, and latency-throughput-cost trade-offs.
  • Proven track record designing and operating scalable distributed systems on Kubernetes and cloud infrastructure (GCP/AWS) using Python and PyTorch.
  • Demonstrated ownership taking experimental research prototypes and re-architecting them into dependable, maintainable platform features.
  • Based in or able to relocate to London, working on-site in the office at least four days a week.
