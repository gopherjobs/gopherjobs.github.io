👋 At NeuralTrust, we're looking for a Site Reliability Engineer to help us scale our platform and take our technology to the next level. If you're passionate about building robust backend systems and working at the intersection of AI and security — join us!

🚀 About NeuralTrust

We’re a Barcelona-based startup building the future of AI and Agents security . Our SaaS platform enables large organizations to integrate generative AI into their products and services in a secure, reliable, and measurable way .

Backed by leading European investors and collaborating with top Spanish universities, NeuralTrust operates at the intersection of AI innovation, cybersecurity, and enterprise transformation .

As generative AI reshapes industries, security and trust become board-level priorities. At NeuralTrust, we ensure organizations can adopt AI confidently and responsibly .

  • Design, build, and maintain highly available, scalable, and secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Automate deployments, CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring systems.
  • Ensure platform reliability, uptime, and performance across environments.
  • Troubleshoot production issues and lead incident response efforts.

⭐️ About You

We run on cloud-native infrastructure with Kubernetes and modern observability stacks. While we don’t expect you to know everything, we’re looking for a strong foundation and a hacker mentality.

  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure—GCP preferred).
  • Strong background in Linux systems, networking, and distributed systems.
  • Proficiency in Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform or similar).
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and containerized workloads (Helm, Kubectl, etc).
  • Familiarity with monitoring and alerting systems (Prometheus, Grafana, etc.).
  • Solid scripting / automation skills (Python, Bash, or similar).
  • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and GitOps workflows.
  • Security-first mindset in infrastructure and operations.
  • Team player: you enjoy sharing knowledge, supporting your teammates, and learning together.
