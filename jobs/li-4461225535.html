Description

🚀 Capitole Consulting keeps growing and we want to do it with you!

We are looking for a Software Engineer with more than 5 years of professional experience , to join an international project and collaborate with one of our leading clients in the Automotive industry .

We have an exciting opportunity to design, develop and operate modern cloud-native applications in an international environment. The role covers the full software development lifecycle , combining backend , frontend , cloud , DevOps and microservices technologies to deliver scalable and high-quality enterprise solutions.

🎯 RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Design, develop and maintain cloud-native applications using modern technologies.
  • Develop backend services and frontend applications in a microservices architecture .
  • Build and maintain REST APIs , asynchronous integrations and event-driven solutions.
  • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines , containerized deployments and cloud infrastructure.
  • Ensure software quality through testing , code reviews and security best practices.
  • Collaborate with product, architecture and business teams to deliver scalable solutions.
  • Participate in architectural decisions and promote engineering best practices.

🛠️ REQUIREMENTS:

  • +5 years of experience as a Software Engineer.
  • Strong experience with Node.js, TypeScript and NestJS.
  • At least 3 years of experience with Angular .
  • Experience designing microservices, REST APIs and distributed systems .
  • Solid knowledge of PostgreSQL, MongoDB/DocumentDB and data modeling.
  • Experience with Kafka , Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Experience working with AWS cloud environments.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving and communication skills .

⭐NICE TO HAVE:

  • Experience in large-scale enterprise environments.
  • Knowledge of GitOps, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI and developer productivity initiatives.
  • Experience improving CI/CD, observability and platform engineering .
  • Experience mentoring developers and promoting engineering best practices.
