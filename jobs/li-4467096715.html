About Doctolib

Doctolib Connect is a secure medical communication app designed for healthcare professionals.

More than 1M healthcare professionals use Doctolib Connect to discuss and coordinate patient care with colleagues both within and beyond their organizational walls. Today, Doctolib Connect is the largest medical network in Europe. 

Since 2013, Doctolib has been striving for a healthier world by providing new-generation technology. With more than 2,800 employees across France, Italy, Germany and the Netherlands, Doctolib improves the daily lives of healthcare professionals and serves more than 80 million Europeans, offering a fast, frictionless, and secure journey for all their care needs.

 

What you'll do

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join the Doctolib Connect team in Amsterdam, Netherlands .

As a Backend Engineer , your mission will be to work on secure medical communication solutions that enable healthcare professionals to discuss and coordinate patient care with colleagues both within and beyond their organizational walls. You will be working in a feature team developing APIs and backend services that power Europe's largest medical network with over 1M healthcare professionals.

Working in the tech team at Doctolib involves building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. We work in feature teams in an agile environment, while collaborating with product, design, and business teams.

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Building, owning and maintaining Doctolib Connect's backend providing APIs for our mobile and web clients using Java 17, Spring Boot, Akka and more
  • Managing and monitoring our cloud infrastructure at AWS, implementing "infrastructure-as-code" (Terraform) solutions
  • Ensuring our users' data is safe and secure by keeping security and privacy front and center in everything you do
  • Collaborating with peers working on Android, iOS, web and Doctolib's Rails backend to build an exceptional experience for healthcare professionals
  • Managing the full development lifecycle, including stakeholder communication and production issue debugging

About our tech environment

  • Our solutions are built on a single fully cloud-native platform that supports web and mobile app interfaces, multiple languages, and is adapted to the country and healthcare specialty requirements. To address these challenges, we are modularizing our platform run in a distributed architecture through reusable components.
  • Our stack is composed of Rails, TypeScript, Java, Python, Kotlin, Swift, and React Native.
  • We leverage AI ethically across our products to empower patients and health professionals. Discover our AI vision here and learn about our first AI hackathon here!
