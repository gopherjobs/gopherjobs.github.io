Blockchain/Web3 Senior Backend Developer (Golang)

Location: Remote (Malaysia, Singapore, HongKong or Taiwan preferred)

NodeReal is a One-Stop Infrastructure and Solution Provider for the High-Speed Blockchain Era, targeting to provide world-class infrastructure and dramatically optimize web3/blockchain development tools and services.

We are a globally distributed startup with 100+ employees. Back in 2022, we raised $16M (Series A) led by Sky9 Capital. We enable Web3 projects to build on many blockchains, supporting 200+ DApp projects and powering >50% of the Top 50 Projects on the BNB Chain and Aptos.

Web3 is all about the community builders, and ours consists of 16K+ developers. Some of our well-known customers are CoinMarketCap, PancakeSwap, 1inch, Galxe, Certik, TheGraph, Trust Wallet, and many more.

Our mission is to make Web3 Real — and we hope you can be part of this. Are you excited by this? Join us to make us the AWS of Web3!

Responsibilities

  • Build high-quality, high-performance Web3 infrastructure to power a range of Web3 products.
  • Design and implement scalable backend services and APIs (e.g., RPC gateways, indexing, and data services) that serve high-throughput, low-latency Web3 workloads.
  • Develop and optimize blockchain data pipelines, including node interaction, event/log parsing, and on-chain data extraction across multiple chains.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues related to system performance, reliability, and scalability in distributed, high-concurrency environments.
  • Conduct thorough code reviews and collaborate with other developers to maintain high code quality and engineering standards.
  • Monitor, profile, and continuously improve service performance, stability, and security.
  • Contribute to technical documentation, design specifications, and best practices.
  • Leverage AI agents and AI-assisted coding workflows to improve development velocity and code quality.

Requirements

  • Hands-on experience with Web3 projects (e.g., smart contracts, blockchain protocols, on-chain infrastructure).
  • Solid Golang foundation with proven production experience.
  • Practical experience with AI agents and AI-assisted coding workflows.
  • Experience with distributed systems, concurrent programming, and network protocols.
  • Strong logical thinking, a collaborative mindset, and a fast learning curve.
  • Strong communication skills; fluency in English is required to coordinate effectively with overseas partners and stakeholders.

Good to have

  • Prior experience with blockchain infrastructure such as RPC services, node operations, indexers, or archive/data nodes.
  • Deep understanding of blockchain concepts, including consensus algorithms (e.g., PoS, PoA), state transition models, EVM internals, and cryptography.
  • Familiarity with performance profiling and optimization techniques for high-throughput, low-latency systems.
  • Experience contributing to open-source projects, especially in the blockchain space.
  • Familiarity with cloud infrastructure, containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and observability tooling.
  • Experience with trading algorithms or quantitative systems.
