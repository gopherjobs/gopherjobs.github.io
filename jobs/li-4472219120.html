Hungry, Humble, Honest, with Heart.

The Opportunity

Are you an enthusiastic early-career engineer passionate about building secure AI infrastructure from the ground up? Join Nutanix to help develop our DataHub platform—an enterprise data hub and identity middleware engine positioned between raw infrastructure/SaaS platforms and downstream search, retrieval, and AI application clients. This is your chance to work with cutting-edge technology, automating data ingestion, sensitive data classification, security governance, and identity-aware retrieval. Be at the forefront of enterprise data innovation in a collaborative environment that fosters hands-on growth and mentorship.

About The Team At Nutanix, you'll join the DataHub team, a geo-dispersed group of talented professionals dedicated to pushing boundaries and delivering trusted enterprise data solutions. Our team's diverse skill sets enable us to solve complex challenges in enterprise search, data access, and governance while supporting one another in a dynamic atmosphere.

You will report to the Technical Director, who is committed to mentoring junior team members and fostering an inclusive environment. The work setup is hybrid, requiring you to be in the office at our Vancouver office three days a week for valuable in-person collaboration. There are very mild travel requirements for this position.

Your Role

  • Design and construct enterprise-grade connectors for varied data sources.
  • Implement robust access control policies to eliminate unauthorized data exposure during search queries.
  • Create and refine CPU-optimized and GPU based text classification rules and metadata tagging to detect and safeguard sensitive information (PII, PHI, financial records, credentials) using Regular Expressions (Regex) and Named Entity Recognition (NER).
  • Develop and write clean unit and integration tests for document extraction, classification, and ingestion pipelines.
  • Partner with senior engineering staff to design, test, and enhance enterprise search and retrieval pipelines.

What You Will Bring

  • Education & Experience: You recently completed a Bachelor's/Master's degree in Computer Science or a related Engineering field with 3-6 years of relevant work experience
  • Programming Languages: Strong proficiency in Python, Golang, or modern backend engineering technologies.
  • Architecture Awareness: Exposure to vector databases, enterprise storage solutions, REST APIs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, and identity-aware search/retrieval systems.
  • Core Engineering Concepts: Solid foundation in data structures, enterprise-grade security standards, and Git-based version control.
  • Text Classification & NLP (Good to Have): Familiarity with regex pattern matching, entity recognition libraries (e.g., spaCy, MedSpaCy, NLTK), and techniques for validating sensitive data types.
  • Mindset: Self-motivated, growth-oriented, and eager to be mentored by seasoned technical leaders.
