Engineering Culture

At Tightknit we are committed to only hiring *“Hell yeah!”*s and not “ meh ”s. We believe an awesome culture is a natural byproduct of an awesome team.

Here Are Qualities We Strive For

  • Extreme Ownership: Take pride in your work, own features completely, and fix issues even when they fall outside your exact domain.
  • Strong Opinions, Weakly Held: We want opinionated people who will push back on plans they disagree with, but who will commit fully once a decision is made.
  • Thick Skin, Not Thick Skulls: We welcome debate without taking it personally, knowing that critique is a gift.
  • Be a Pioneer: You are comfortable buildings systems around you from scratch.
  • Hustle with Purpose, not Polish: Stay scrappy. Iterate quickly.
  • No Energy Vampires: We have a strict "We > Me" mentality and will not tolerate high-blame mentalities, territorial behavior, or oversized egos.
  • Kaizen (改善): Leave things better than you found them; make incremental improvements.
  • Use AI to disrupt yourself: Otherwise your job, or your company, may not exist soon.

You should be able to speak to how you value the impact of community ( being a member of a community group is a major plus ).

Compatibility Check

SuperDay (typically 1 day) - paid work trial, designed as a mutual compatibility check so you can see what it’s like to work with us (and we can learn what it’s like to work with you). This is a pragmatic test of fit that ensures everyone is genuinely excited about the partnership. It is not designed as a technical test.

SuperDay

The final stage of our process is what we call a SuperDay. This is typically one paid full day of work with us, which we can flexibly arrange around the candidate's schedule. The goal is a mutual compatibility check so all of us can get a real sense of what it’d be like to work together. The SuperDay may be done remotely or in person if nearby one of our co-working spaces. We are not able to bypass this stage so if the candidate is not interested in doing this final step, unfortunately we will have to part ways and the candidate will no longer be considered for the role.

💡We can be very flexible with scheduling! For example, we can split the SuperDay into half-days, do it in the evenings, or schedule it on a weekend as needed.

The candidate will be working on mock tasks similar to the real-world day-to-day work someone in this role does at Tightknit. They will also have the chance to meet a few of their potential direct team members, and if they haven’t already, our founders. This gives the candidate a chance to show off their skills, and for us to see the quality, speed and communication of the candidate. Overall, the candidate should aim to spend at least 80% of their time and energy on the task and less than 20% on meeting people, as we will base our decision primarily on the output of the day.

We pay all candidates a flat rate of $500 USD for their SuperDay efforts. On rare occasions, if we have to cancel a scheduled SuperDay arrangement because we have filled the role with another candidate who was further advanced in the process, we will pay a $250 USD term fee to the candidate for their efforts up until that point. If the candidate is unable to accept payment for the SuperDay, we will donate the amount to an agreed upon charity of their choice.

As a part of the SuperDay arrangement, you will be asked to sign an NDA / CLA and may be provided with a mocked and/or siloed codebase.

Your First Two Months at Tightknit

Week 1–2

  • Complete your SuperDay.
  • Join the Slack customer community and see how real users think.
  • Form strong opinions about our architecture, product gaps, and velocity constraints.
  • Release a small to medium-sized feature. Improve something meaningful.
  • Ship production code.

Weeks 3–4

  • Own a scoped but consequential project end-to-end.
  • Propose improvements to our systems (engineering, AI workflows, APIs, performance, etc.).
  • Start shaping how engineering should operate here — not just participating in it.

Month 2

  • Lead a cross-cutting initiative (integration, refactor, reliability improvement, new surface area).
  • Have direct customer conversations.
  • Make at least one architectural decision that meaningfully improves our trajectory.

By the end of two months, success looks like this:

  • You’ve taken full ownership of at least one initiative.
  • You’ve earned trust through action and follow-through.
  • You’ve helped us move faster without lowering our standards.
  • You’re clearly operating as a founder-level multiplier — not waiting to be told what to do, but defining what should exist next.
  • You wake up excited to come work alongside this team!
