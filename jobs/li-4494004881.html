General Summary The Senior Software Engineer is responsible for designing, building and operating the software that powers AI agents across the ConnectWise platform. Working within the AI Agents team, this role applies strong software engineering fundamentals to integrate large language models, tools and data into reliable, secure and scalable production services that deliver thoughtful experiences for our partners. This role works in partnership with ML engineers, product managers and platform teams to bring agentic capabilities from prototype to production.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities

  • Designs, develops, tests and maintains backend services and APIs that power AI agents and LLM-based features.
  • Builds agent capabilities such as tool and function calling, workflow orchestration, memory and context management, and integrations with internal and third-party systems.
  • Implements retrieval-augmented generation components, including data ingestion, embeddings, vector search and prompt pipelines.
  • Contributes to evaluation, testing and observability of agents, including automated tests, eval harnesses, logging, tracing and monitoring.
  • Ensures production readiness of services through performance tuning, reliability engineering, security and cost optimization.
  • Participates in design reviews and code reviews, and upholds engineering best practices and standards.
  • Collaborates with cross-functional teams to translate product requirements into well-scoped technical deliverables.
  • Mentors junior engineers and shares knowledge across the team.
  • Researches new tools, frameworks and techniques in the AI agent space and documents findings.

Knowledge, Skills, And/or Abilities Required

  • Ability to work independently on features and projects with limited supervision.
  • Strong programming skills in Python, working knowledge of another language (e.g. TypeScript, Java, Go or C#) is a plus.
  • Solid understanding of software engineering fundamentals: data structures, algorithms, object-oriented and service-oriented design, and testing.
  • Experience building RESTful or gRPC APIs and microservices, with frameworks such as FastAPI, Flask or similar.
  • Practical experience with LLM APIs (e.g. OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock) and agent frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, Semantic Kernel or similar.
  • Familiarity with retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases and prompt engineering; awareness of protocols such as Model Context Protocol (MCP) is a plus.
  • Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Proficient in SQL and comfortable working with relational and NoSQL data stores.
  • Knowledge of at least one cloud platform (e.g. AWS or Azure) and its relevant services.
  • Proficiency in version control systems such as Git.
  • Ability to break down and communicate technical concepts clearly to audiences of varying technical understanding.

Educational/Vocational/Previous Experience Recommendations

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field required; Master's degree preferred.
  • 5+ years of relevant software engineering experience.
  • Experience building and shipping production software on cloud computing platforms.
  • Hands-on experience building LLM-powered applications or AI agents is strongly preferred.
  • Experience deploying, monitoring and iterating services in production.
