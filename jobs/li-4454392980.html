Description

At Similarweb, we're revolutionizing the way businesses interact with the digital world by revealing to them everything that happens online. In 2021, we went public on the New York Stock Exchange, and we haven’t stopped growing since! We’re looking for an Experienced Full Stack Developer to design, code and manage Similarweb’s large-scale and highly available services, using cutting edge technologies & design.

This role will report to Team Manager, R&D.

  • Determine feasibility by performing risk management and evaluating tradeoffs.
  • Lead decision-making & design of APIs, mechanisms, and abstractions.
  • Build new features and directly impact the future of our product end to end (frontend, backend, tests, monitoring, etc.) and cross platforms.
  • Write high-quality, functional code – backend and frontend.

  • Perform code reviews, evaluate implementations, and provide feedback.
  • Have lots of fun!

Here’s What You’ll Need

  • B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Computer Science or a related field;
  • 4+ years of backend engineering and hands-on experience with web technologies;
  • At least 2 years of experience in developing front-end/mobile (Well-known frameworks- React, Vue, etc. - an advantage);
  • Infrastructure: Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP or Azure), containerization (Docker), and orchestration (Kubernetes, Terraform, Helm, Argo).
  • Experience in Typescript;
  • Experience in Node.js and at least one large framework (Express, NestJS etc.)
  • Experience with at least one large UI framework (react, angular, vue);
  • Experience with databases;
  • Experience in architecture and design of production-grade web services;
  • Comfortable taking challenges and learning new technologies;
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to provide constant dialog between teams;
  • High proficiency in both written and spoken English;
