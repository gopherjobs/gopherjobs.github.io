Sedna Introduction

At Sedna, we’re building the future with robotics. Our work is in integrating robotics and automation into industries like construction and mining. If you’re passionate about cutting-edge technology, solving complex problems, and building systems that push the limits of what is possible in robotics and automation, Sedna is where your skills can make a global impact.

We are a US based company with strong South African roots. Our business started in Joburg in 2005 and later moved to the USA. We are now building out our engineering office’s in Cape Town (Century City) and Joburg (Greenstone Hill) to support our US operations and customers.

Job Overview

As a Senior UI Engineer, you will design and build intuitive, high-performance user interfaces for Sedna's web and desktop applications, including 3D visualization and signal-processing tools used across Sedna's automation and robotics deployments.

You will work closely with backend engineers, QA, and product stakeholders to deliver secure, scalable, and visually polished software.

Key Responsibilities

UI / Frontend Development:

  • Design and develop scalable, high-performance user interfaces for web and desktop applications.
  • Build responsive, accessible UI components and design systems using modern frontend frameworks (e.g., React, Angular, or similar).
  • Contribute to 3D visualization interfaces and processing pipeline front-ends (e.g., using Godot, OpenGL, Unity, or similar).
  • Build and maintain integrations with Web APIs to enable service communication.
  • Implement secure authentication and authorization flows (OAuth) within the UI layer.
  • Apply cybersecurity best practices to ensure application security and data integrity within front-end code.
  • Collaborate with backend and QA teams to troubleshoot, test, and deploy solutions while maintaining high code quality.

Design & UX Collaboration:

  • Translate wireframes, mock-ups, and product requirements into functional, polished interfaces.
  • Champion consistent design patterns and component reuse across Sedna's software products.
  • Optimize UI performance and cross-platform / cross-browser compatibility.

Quality & Process:

  • Participate in code reviews and maintain high standards of code quality and maintainability.
  • Support automated and manual testing of UI components in collaboration with the QA team.
  • Assist with triage and resolution of defects related to UI/UX issues.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science , Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent professional experience).
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in UI / frontend software engineering.
  • Strong experience with modern JavaScript/TypeScript frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue).
  • Experience building and consuming Web APIs.
  • Experience with OAuth or similar secure authentication protocols.
  • Knowledge of 3D frameworks/libraries (e.g., OpenGL, Unity, or similar) advantageous.
  • Experience with C# / .NET or similar backend-adjacent technologies advantageous.
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and Kubernetes advantageous.
  • Strong understanding of responsive design, accessibility, and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Willingness and ability to travel internationally (USA) as required.

Skills & Competencies

  • Attention to detail
  • Design sensibility and user-centred thinking
  • Time management
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Proactive and collaborative approach to teamwork
