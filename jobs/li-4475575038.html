Description

Job Title: Quality Assurance Engineer (VR, Mobile & Desktop Applications)

Contract Duration: Remote (UK), 6-Month Contract

Remuneration: £43.27 Hourly Rate

We're seeking an experienced Quality Assurance Engineer to support the testing and release of a large-scale consumer application across VR, mobile, and desktop platforms. This role is ideal for someone with a strong QA background in gaming, VR/AR, or complex consumer-facing products who enjoys hands-on testing, identifying issues, and helping deliver stable releases.

The Role

As a dedicated QA owner, you'll be responsible for executing test plans, validating builds, identifying defects, and working closely with engineering teams to ensure high product quality across multiple platforms. You'll play a critical role in supporting weekly releases and maintaining a seamless user experience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute smoke, regression, and release testing across VR, mobile, and desktop platforms.
  • Validate fixes before and after release deployment.
  • Perform end-to-end testing of core user journeys and application functionality.
  • Investigate and reproduce crashes, performance issues, and stability problems.
  • Create detailed bug reports with clear reproduction steps and supporting evidence.
  • Maintain test plans, test cases, and release checklists.
  • Support bug triage activities, including severity assessment and issue prioritisation.
  • Identify opportunities for test automation and process improvements.
  • Collaborate closely with Engineering, Product, and Release teams to ensure quality standards are achieved.

Required Skills & Experience

  • QA experience within gaming, VR/AR, mobile, or large-scale consumer applications.
  • Experience executing regression testing and release validation against fixed delivery schedules.
  • Hands-on testing experience on Android devices, including build installation and log analysis using ADB/Logcat.
  • Strong defect management and bug-reporting skills.
  • Experience creating and maintaining test plans and test cases.
  • Ability to independently investigate issues and communicate technical findings clearly.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to work effectively under release deadlines.

Desirable Experience

  • VR application testing experience.
  • Knowledge of Unity and Unity log analysis.
  • Multiplayer, social platform, or user-generated content (UGC) testing experience.
  • Cloud-streaming application or gaming experience.
  • Performance and memory profiling tools.
  • Python, scripting, or test automation experience.
  • Experience working within live-service release environments.

Why Apply?

  • Work on innovative consumer technology spanning VR, mobile, and desktop platforms.
  • Gain exposure to emerging technologies and next-generation digital experiences.
  • Enjoy high ownership and autonomy within the QA process.
  • Partner closely with engineering teams and have a direct impact on release quality.
  • Join a collaborative, fast-paced environment focused on delivering exceptional user experiences.
