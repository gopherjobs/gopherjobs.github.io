Description

A hands-on AI engineering role building agentic systems for the fast growing nuclear energy sector.

About Us

Tegro is a technology development, commercial and delivery partner for Deployable Energy’s Unity micro reactor. Based in Singapore, we originate, develop and deliver modular nuclear power projects for ports, maritime, logistics, data centres and industry.

About the Opportunity

The company is hiring an AI Engineer to build the agentic and software layers behind its commercial and financial operations. You will design, build and deploy AI agents and applications across the end to end enterprise requirements of the business.

The role suits an engineer with production experience building LLM-based applications who wants to apply agentic AI to advance business outcomes across a variety of use cases and department requirements.

Key Responsibilities

Agentic Systems & AI Applications

·        Design, build and deploy LLM-powered agents and agentic workflows (planning, tool use and multi-step automation) for finance, commercial and project teams.

·        Build retrieval (RAG) and knowledge systems over financial models, contracts, project data and company documents.

·        Develop applications including capital management and investor reporting tools, commercial pipeline and CRM tools, project development and delivery tracking, and document processing and extraction.

Data, Integration & Infrastructure

·        Build data pipelines, APIs and integrations connecting financial models, CRMs, document stores and reporting systems.

·        Build internal applications and dashboards that put agent outputs in front of users.

·        Deploy and maintain AI services in production on cloud infrastructure (Azure).

Evaluation & Reliability

·        Build evaluation, monitoring and guardrails so outputs are accurate and reliable, particularly where financial data is involved.

Product & Stakeholder Collaboration

·        Work with the Commercial & Product Lead to identify use cases, scope requirements and prioritise the build.

·        Document systems and support the team to adopt and use them.

The Successful Applicant

·        Degree in computer science, software engineering, or a related discipline.

·        2–6 years’ experience as an AI engineer, ML engineer or software engineer, including building and deploying LLM-based applications in production.

·        Experience building agentic systems in a corporate or business setting.

·        Strong Python; TypeScript or front-end experience is advantageous.

·        Hands-on experience with LLM APIs, agent frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex or equivalent), tool use and MCP, RAG, vector databases and prompt engineering.

·        Backend and deployment experience: APIs (FastAPI or similar), SQL, Docker and cloud platforms (Azure preferred).

·        Experience with evaluation and observability for LLM systems.

·        Comfortable working with structured and unstructured data.

·        Exposure to finance, energy, industrial or infrastructure is advantageous but not required.

·        Able to communicate with both technical and business stakeholders.

What's on Offer

·        Full-time, office-based role in Singapore, starting Q1 2027.

·        Visa sponsorship may be considered for the right candidate.

·        Build agentic systems from the ground up as part of a small team.

·        Direct work with leadership on commercial and technology strategy.
