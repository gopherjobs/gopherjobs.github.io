Role Description: We are looking for a proactive Test Engineer II to join our CS SES testing team in Amsterdam. In this role, you will work across cross-functional squads to test Pega workflows from an end-user perspective, with Customer Service agents as your primary focus. You will embed directly into Agile teams to drive risk-based testing, advocate for early testability, and build deterministic automated checks. If you are passionate about combining strong backend/API testing with low-code front-end applications and cutting-edge AI testing methodologies, this role offers an exciting opportunity to make a real impact on our operations.

Key Job Responsibilities and Duties:

  • Independently execute test design, risk-based testing, and quality practices from initial design to release within cross-functional Agile squads.
  • Guide teams to make features testable early, advocating for isolated verification, shift-left testing, and sustainable root cause analysis.
  • Build and maintain deterministic, fast-feedback test automation for APIs and backend services using tools like Rest Assured, Playwright, or Postman.
  • Test and validate end-to-end Pega customer service workflows from an agent perspective while integrating non-functional checks (performance, accessibility, security, or resiliency).
  • Explore, implement, and test AI features and LLM models within testing workflows (e.g., using Claude Code or similar frameworks).
  • Coach and mentor less experienced test engineers on testing best practices, testability, and continuous quality improvements.
  • Collaborate closely with cross-functional squads and partner teams to communicate quality trade-offs clearly to both technical and business stakeholders.

Qualifications & Skills:

  • Strong experience in test design, execution, and test automation across the full software delivery lifecycle in Agile environments.
  • Proven hands-on experience in backend and API testing alongside front-end testing for low-code platforms,
  • Practical experience with relevant test automation tools and frameworks (e.g., Playwright, Rest Assured, Postman).
  • Demonstrated understanding of testing AI/LLM models and practical experience using AI tools (e.g., Claude Code) to enhance testing practices.
  • Solid understanding of risk-based testing, exploratory testing, non-functional testing, and observability/troubleshooting techniques.
  • Strong coaching, communication, and stakeholder management skills with a dedicated customer and end-user mindset.
  • Familiarity with Pega product architecture and workflows would be good to have.
