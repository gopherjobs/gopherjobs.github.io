💻 Role The Financial Platform team at Whatnot is fundamental to the company's global operations. Our systems are at the heart of every platform transaction, determining earnings, fees, and costs dynamically across a global marketplace; understanding and charging tax and cross-border duties; and accurately managing returns, refunds, and seller payouts at scale. Each month, we are responsible for billions of dollars in transaction volume, making us one of the most trusted and essential systems in the business.

As a Software Engineer On This Team, You Will

  • Design, build, and own reliable financial systems that support billions of dollars in transaction volume.
  • Develop scalable infrastructure that enables Whatnot to launch new markets, products, and ways for buyers and sellers to transact.
  • Make thoughtful technical trade-offs across correctness, reliability, speed, and scalability in systems where accuracy is critical.
  • Own projects end-to-end, from understanding the problem and designing the solution through launch and iteration.
  • Partner across engineering, product, finance, and operations to solve complex financial and marketplace problems.
  • Help shape the architecture and technical direction of Whatnot’s financial platform as the business scales.

We offer flexibility to work from home or from one of our global office hubs, and we value in-person time for planning, problem-solving, and connection.
