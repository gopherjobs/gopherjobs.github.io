About the Position

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will take a leading role in the architecture, development, and technical direction of large-scale and complex software systems.

You will lead technical decisions across backend, frontend, and infrastructure while continuing to work directly on challenging development tasks.

The role also includes technical leadership and mentoring responsibilities. You will support junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, design reviews, technical guidance, and coaching.

You will work closely with Product Managers, business teams, client-side engineers, and other stakeholders to evaluate technical feasibility, explain architectural trade-offs, and deliver high-quality systems.
