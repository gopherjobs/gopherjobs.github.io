Description

We are looking for an experienced and driven Delivery Lead to guide the rollout and adoption of our modern, cloud-native monitoring, logging, and tracing stack (focusing on tools such as OpenTelemetry, Grafana, and Kubernetes in an AWS environment).

Your main responsibility goes beyond supporting platform engineers with technical delivery — you will drive change, coordinate cross-functional dependencies, and ensure that solutions are successfully adopted into the daily workflows of engineering teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Drive Delivery & Adoption: Lead initiatives from platform integration through to full operational adoption within development teams.
  • Cross-Functional Coordination: Map dependencies, align priorities, and remove blockers between platform teams, SREs, and product development teams.
  • Stakeholder Management & Decision Making: Keep technical and business stakeholders aligned, challenge assumptions when necessary, and facilitate decision-making during conflicting priorities.
  • Operational Readiness: Ensure new observability solutions are delivered with clear processes, documentation, and operational readiness for production environments.
  • Project Leadership Experience: 5–8 years of experience as a Project Manager, Delivery Lead, or Engineering Program Manager within complex software or cloud environments.
  • Technical Background: Hands-on experience in software development or infrastructure. Prior coding experience in Java and a solid understanding of AWS environments.
  • Cloud & Observability Domain Knowledge: Strong conceptual understanding of cloud architecture (AWS, Kubernetes) and the observability ecosystem (e.g., OpenTelemetry, Grafana, Datadog).
  • Structured & Pragmatic Mindset: Outcome-driven rather than process-oriented, with proven skills in managing complex cross-team dependencies.
  • Strong Communicator: Confident in facilitating technical discussions, navigating trade-offs, and building consensus between engineers and leadership.
  • Background within the automotive or telecom sector.

Nice to Have

  • Prior experience within Telecommunications or large-scale connected vehicle / IoT services.
  • Working knowledge of Swedish (English is the primary working language).

To apply for this position you are required to have a work permit in Sweden. We encourage all relevant candidates to apply! The recruitment is ongoing, apply today!
