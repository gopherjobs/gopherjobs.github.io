Truth is, we’re just as much a technology business. Throughout our almost 30-year history, we’ve consistently been recognised as a great place to work; in 2026, Capital One was officially ranked 9th in the UK’s Best Workplaces list. Hear from our team about what it’s like working at Capital One UK.

Using technology to build the future of finance. That’s one good thing. Our Tech teams continuously drive innovation to deliver a positive impact for millions of customers every single day. And that’s what makes a career in technology here like nowhere else. Our Graduate programme offers three different rotations within our core engineering teams, ensuring you gain a broad range of experience to support your growth into a well-rounded full-stack software engineer.

What You’ll Need We don’t mind what degree background or grades you have, but we want to see:

  • You have a strong foundation of coding skills (in any language).
  • You have the flexibility and aptitude for the learning it takes to master lots of different tools and technologies.
  • Your approach to coding and ability to solve complex technical problems.
  • How you communicate and collaborate with others.
  • Your enthusiasm for tech and Capital One.

The Recruitment Process Step 1: Apply! We don’t screen CV’s so there’s no need to tailor your CV or attach a cover letter. If you meet the eligibility criteria you will be sent online testing to complete, assessing the core skills required for the role, and a one-way video interview, looking at your motivations for the role and some examples where you’ve shown the soft skills we’re looking for.

Step 2: Virtual assessment day, consisting of a pair programming exercise and a presentation and strengths based interview.

Step 3: In-person final interview with one of our Senior Software Engineering Managers, and the opportunity to look around our office.

Where And How You'll Work This is a permanent position based either in our Nottingham office. We embrace a hybrid approach to work. You'll spend three days a week in the office (Tuesday-Thursday), with Monday and Friday being optional work-from-home days.

Our offices are designed to inspire and support you. At our Nottingham head office, you'll find an on-site gym, restaurant and dedicated Learning Loft. Our London office boasts a rooftop running track and coffee bar.

What’s In It For You We invest in the growth of our people alongside our business. You will have extensive opportunities to learn and develop your career, including access to up to £5,000 in funding for external qualifications.

Benefits In addition to career development, our comprehensive benefits package is designed to grow with you, ensuring your wellbeing is prioritised at every stage of your career:

  • Wealth & Future: Competitive pension and performance-based bonus schemes
  • Holidays: Start with 25 days of annual leave, increasing with tenure. Need more? You have the option to buy 5 additional days.
  • Health: Access premium care through our private medical insurance.
  • Family Support: We champion your milestones with enhanced parental leave.
