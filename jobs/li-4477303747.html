The Project and the Role Description

At Nexer Czech Republic, we are looking for a Senior AI-Assisted Software Engineer to join an international team delivering enterprise platforms for workflow automation, pricing, tender management, and system integration.

We are seeking a hands-on engineer who can turn complex business and technical challenges into reliable production solutions. You'll work across the full delivery lifecycle while leveraging AI-assisted engineering tools such as GitHub Copilot to accelerate development, improve quality, and drive innovation.
