About the Company

Join a fast-growing AI startup that's transforming the way AI models are built through an innovative AI data platform. The company provides end-to-end solutions for AI data collection, annotation, and management, helping enterprises develop high-quality AI models faster and more efficiently.

Their platform supports technologies such as AI-OCR, object detection, Large Language Models (LLMs), and other cutting-edge AI applications. With clients ranging from major Japanese enterprises to international organizations, the company is rapidly expanding across Japan, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Europe.

If you're passionate about AI, cloud technologies, and building products with real-world impact, this is an excellent opportunity to work on meaningful projects in a fast-paced startup environment.

What You'll Do

As a Full-Stack Engineer, you'll take ownership of developing and enhancing the company's AI data platform across the entire technology stack.

Your responsibilities include:

  • Develop and maintain web and mobile application features
  • Design and build scalable backend APIs and database architecture
  • Design, deploy, and optimize cloud infrastructure
  • Improve the UI/UX of AI annotation and data collection tools
  • Build and maintain DevOps/MLOps environments and CI/CD pipelines
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Business, and Engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions
