Description

Are you passionate about modern commerce architectures, headless ecosystems, and guiding enterprise clients through complex digital transformations?

At Vaimo , we help global brands drive digital growth through cutting-edge customer experiences and robust engineering. We are looking for a Technical Architect based in Latin America to serve as the strategic technical anchor across our client lifecycle - from early discovery and solution design through to delivery and long-term architectural governance.

In this role, you will bridge client business vision with technical execution. You will design scalable architectures, conduct technical discovery, evaluate modern platforms, and guide distributed engineering teams to deliver high-performing solutions.

What You Will Do

  • Solution Architecture & Discovery: Lead technical discovery sessions with clients to translate complex business needs into clear, scalable, and resilient architectural designs (Conceptual Solution Designs, integration blueprints, and API specifications).
  • Client Advisory & Technical Leadership: Act as a trusted technical advisor to stakeholders, clearly articulating architectural decisions, managing technical risk, and evaluating third-party platforms and integrations.
  • Engineering Guidance & Best Practices: Collaborate closely with development teams by establishing delivery standards, conducting senior-level code reviews, defining technical guidelines, and ensuring overall architectural health.
  • Pre-Sales & Feasibility: Support pre-discovery estimations, feasibility assessments, and technical demonstrations for prospective clients and upcoming feature initiatives.
  • Continuous Improvement: Steer Proof of Concepts (PoCs) to validate new technologies, foster reusable engineering patterns, and contribute to internal architectural discussion forums.

What We Look For

  • Architectural Experience: Proven experience designing and architecting scalable enterprise web or digital commerce solutions.
  • Modern Commerce Paradigms: Hands-on architectural familiarity with either composable/headless commerce stacks or enterprise platforms (e.g., Adobe Commerce, commercetools, or similar enterprise eCommerce engines).
  • Modular Ecosystem Exposure: Solid understanding of modern MACH/composable architectures, including APIs, headless CMS platforms (e.g., Contentful, Contentstack, Strapi, Sanity), search engines (e.g., Algolia, Klevu), or modern front-end frameworks (e.g., React/Next.js, Vue/Nuxt.js). You are not expected to be an expert in every single tool - we value architectural depth and adaptability.
  • Consultative Communication: Excellent ability to explain complex technical concepts, trade-offs, and architecture diagrams to both non-technical stakeholders and engineering teams.
  • Collaborative Leadership: Demonstrated strength in mentoring engineers, defining quality standards, and fostering psychological safety and continuous learning within technical teams.
  • Language & Location: Professional working proficiency in written and spoken English and Spanish, comfortable collaborating across international time zones from Latin America. Spanish is mandatory.

Value-Add / Nice to Have

  • AI-Assisted Engineering: Hands-on experience with agentic coding tools, such as Claude Code, Cursor, or GitHub Copilot, in real delivery work. You know where these tools save time and where their output needs close human review.
  • Context Engineering: Experience setting up shared project context for AI tools, such as CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md files, reusable prompts, and coding guidelines, mcps, skills, gems etc., so that AI output follows the team’s architecture and standards.
  • AI Governance and Enablement: A clear understanding of the risks of AI in client projects: data privacy (GDPR, LGPD), prompt injection, unsafe tool access for AI agents, licence and IP concerns, and client rules about which code or data may go to an AI provider. You can turn this knowledge into practical guardrails and help engineers use AI tools safely and with good judgement.

What We Value

  • Passion for architectural cleanliness, performance optimization, and reliable software design.
  • Pragmatic decision-making - balancing ideal architecture with client timelines and business goals.
  • Curiosity to experiment with emerging commerce and front-end technologies.

How We Work

  • Fully remote flexibility within Latin America.
  • Collaborative, multicultural teams across global time zones.
  • A culture centered on ownership, transparent communication, and continuous professional growth.
