Job Description If you are Senior Agile Engineer, looking for an opportunity to grow, Copeland has an exciting opportunity for you! Based in Xi'an, you will responsible for developing high-quality software that is aligned with user needs and business goals, and supporting the team's technology capability developing & mentoring junior Engineers.

As Senior Agile Engineer, You Will

  • Develop high-quality software design and code.
  • Design and implement cloud-native solutions with 24/7 availability, demonstrating infrastructure-as-code (IaC) standard methodologies for efficient and scalable deployments.
  • Use serverless computing on Azure to optimize scalability, performance, and cost-efficiency.
  • Implement production code daily, following security standard methodologies and integrating efficient rollback mechanisms for quick issue resolution.
  • Develop APIs and protocols to enable seamless integration with websites, third-party partners, mobile applications, and IoT devices.
  • Create automated unit and behavioral tests to ensure software reliability, functionality, and quality throughout the development lifecycle.
  • Support and develop agile engineers by providing technical advice, coaching, and educational opportunities.
  • Collaborate with internal and remote teams to fix and improve products.

Required Education, Experience, & Skills

  • 5 years’ experience in software development with exposure to multiple programming languages (e.g., Java, TypeScript, C#, HTML, XAML, PowerShell, SQL, Swift, etc.)
  • Proven experience developing and deploying software on major cloud-native platforms (Microsoft Azure, AWS, GCP, etc.)
  • Ability to translate business requirements into clear, executable technical designs
  • Proficiency in both relational databases (e.g., SQL Server, MySQL) and NoSQL databases
  • Track record of shipping at least one commercially used software product
  • Comfortably using automation for the development, testing, and deployment of software products
  • Capability to independently participate in product support (e.g., on-call rotations)
  • Solid understanding of agile development methodologies, with the ability to teach and promote agile concepts within the team
  • Excellent communication skills, able to explain technically complicated and abstract topics to business and product teams effectively
  • Good written English skills
