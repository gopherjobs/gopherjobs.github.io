Description

The Opportunity Joining the Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP) team as an MTS-2 Software Engineer, you will work on the platform that enables secure, scalable, and production-grade AI agent workloads on enterprise infrastructure.

Your work on the NKP Platform team focuses on building capabilities to run untrusted agent code and tools in strongly isolated environments, close to enterprise data, with low latency and high resource efficiency. The role spans distributed control-plane and data-plane services, worker-pool orchestration, actor or application lifecycle management, Linux-based isolation, networking, persistent state, and observability.

This is deep systems and platform engineering work. You will be expected to own features end to end—from architecture and implementation through testing, reliability, and production readiness—and collaborate across NKP, Kubernetes, storage, networking, security, and AI teams.

About The Team NKP is a complete, enterprise-grade, production-ready platform based on Kubernetes that serves modern applications. By taking the best of the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) ecosystem, but providing it in an integrated and lifecycle-managed package, NKP delivers all the functionality necessary in production with a dramatically lowered TCO. NKP provides simplified Kubernetes operations, seamless application mobility, and integrated security and compliance. Platform engineers and IT admins can use NKP to keep all cloud native resources up-to-date with upgrades, patching, maintenance, and security. The technology and community in this industry are growing fast, new applications and solutions are coming to the market every day, new standards are becoming mature at full speed.

You will report to the Senior Engineering Manager, who adopts a collaborative leadership style that encourages team involvement in decision-making and values open discussions. The manager emphasizes the importance of strong communication and problem-solving skills, aiming to build a supportive environment for both personal and professional development.

This role does not require any travel, allowing the new hire to focus on their responsibilities within the local team while also collaborating effectively with their US counterparts through virtual meetings and communication tools.

What You Will Do

  • Design and implement highly available distributed services for agent execution, including control-plane APIs, worker pools, schedulers, routing, and lifecycle management.
  • Develop Linux and Kubernetes-native components for application execution, including process isolation, namespaces, cgroups, seccomp, gVisor, microVMs, and container runtimes.
  • Integrate NKP with Nutanix infrastructure, including AHV, AOS/NUS, CSI-backed storage, Nutanix Files or Objects, Flow networking, and existing observability capabilities.
  • Build durable state and snapshot workflows so agents can survive failures, be resumed later, and move between workers when required.
  • Establish performance, scale, and reliability benchmarks for high-density agent execution and make data-driven architecture decisions.
  • Contribute to technical designs, code reviews, test strategy, operational runbooks, and production readiness reviews.
  • Work with open-source communities and upstream projects where appropriate, while ensuring the solution meets Nutanix enterprise requirements.

Key Must-Have Competencies

  • Experience: Up to 3 years of software engineering experience, including significant ownership of distributed systems, cloud platforms, operating-system components, or infrastructure products.
  • Distributed Systems: Strong understanding of service decomposition, consistency, concurrency, failure detection, retries, idempotency, leader election, replication, partitioning, and graceful degradation.
  • Linux Fundamentals: Deep knowledge of Linux processes, scheduling, memory, filesystems, namespaces, cgroups, capabilities, system calls, networking, security boundaries, and performance troubleshooting.
  • Systems Programming: Strong proficiency in Go, C++, Rust, or Python, with the ability to write maintainable, production-quality systems code. Go is especially valuable for Kubernetes and platform development.
  • Kubernetes: Strong experience with Kubernetes architecture and internals, controllers, operators, CRDs, scheduling, admission, networking, storage, and debugging cluster-level behavior.
  • Containers and Isolation: Hands-on knowledge of OCI containers, containerd or Docker, runc, rootless containers, seccomp, Linux capabilities, user namespaces.
  • Platform and API Design: Proven ability to design clear, versioned APIs and scalable services with well-defined contracts, lifecycle semantics, compatibility expectations, and operational controls.
  • Storage and State: Understanding of persistent volumes, snapshots, checkpoints, object storage, distributed filesystems, and the trade-offs among durability, latency, consistency, and cost.
  • Networking and Security: Fundamentals in TCP/IP, DNS, TLS, service-to-service communication, network policy, egress control, workload identity, and secure handling of credentials.
  • Product Engineering: Track record of delivering long-lived production software, making pragmatic trade-offs, and collaborating effectively across engineering, product, security, and quality teams.
  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Nice-to-Have

  • Experience building Kubernetes operators, node agents, schedulers, runtimes, or other cluster-level infrastructure.
  • Experience with Cloud Hypervisor, KVM, or other virtualization technologies.
  • Familiarity with worker-pool architectures, or high-density execution systems.
  • Experience with suspend/resume, checkpoint/restore, snapshotting, live migration, or stateful workload mobility.
  • Familiarity with Kubernetes Cluster API, KubeVirt, container orchestration, or multi-cluster management.
  • Contributions to Kubernetes, CNCF, Linux, container-runtime, virtualization, or systems-oriented open-source projects.
