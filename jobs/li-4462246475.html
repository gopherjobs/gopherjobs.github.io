Full Stack Java & Angular Engineer | Mid & Senior

💼 Permanent Contract

💰 Mid-level: up to €35k | Senior: up to €42k

We’re looking for Full Stack Java & Angular Engineers to join a highly skilled international engineering team working on the next evolution of a suite of business-critical applications.

The existing platform is successfully running in production and is now entering a major new phase involving new development, application modernisation and refactoring, scalability improvements and multiple technical streams .

This is an opportunity to work on complex, data-intensive applications using a modern Java, Angular, AWS and microservices stack .

What you'll be doing

  • Develop backend services using Java & Spring Boot
  • Build frontend applications with Angular & TypeScript
  • Design and integrate REST APIs
  • Work with SQL and NoSQL databases
  • Contribute to microservices, modernisation and refactoring
  • Participate in user story refinement, estimates and technical solution design
  • Work across coding, testing, build and deployment activities
  • Improve application performance, scalability and reliability
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex application issues
  • Collaborate with engineers, architects and international stakeholders
  • Support existing production applications alongside new development
  • Participate in a shared 24/7 on-call rotation for critical P1/P2 production incidents once fully onboarded

Technical Requirements

  • Strong experience with Java & Spring Boot
  • Solid hands-on experience with Angular & TypeScript
  • Experience with REST APIs, SQL/NoSQL and microservices
  • Hands-on experience with AWS – S3, Aurora, EKS and Route 53
  • Experience with Docker & Kubernetes
  • Familiarity with Terraform, Gradle and Liquibase
  • Experience with Git/Bitbucket, Jira & Confluence
  • Understanding of modern CI/CD and software engineering practices
  • Strong written and spoken English

Nice to have: Python, AWS Glue/Athena, Snowflake, Power BI, SAML/OIDC and secure coding practices (OWASP).

Experience: typically 3+ years for Mid-level and 5+ years for Senior-level .

What we're looking for

Beyond the technology stack, we’re looking for engineers who are curious, proactive and passionate about what they build .

For Senior profiles in particular, we value people who can step back, understand the bigger technical picture, challenge solutions and take ownership of complex topics — rather than simply waiting for predefined tasks.

You should be comfortable working independently while being an active part of an international engineering team.

Working Model

  • 🇵🇹 Portugal-based candidates only
  • 🏠 Remote from Portugal
  • 🕘 European working hours (CET/CEST)
  • 📞 Shared 24/7 on-call rotation for critical production incidents
  • ✈️ Occasional travel within Europe
  • Candidates must already have the legal right to work in Portugal. Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.
