Description

Do you want to build the platform our product teams run on and turn infrastructure into a self-service product that developers enjoy using? Then we have a skilled and ambitious team who would love to meet you!

Continia develops add-on solutions for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. Our solutions for accounts payable automation, e-documents, expense management and bank integration help more than 20,000 customers worldwide automate everyday finance processes, delivered through a global network of more than 1,800 reselling partners.

We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to join our Infrastructure team. Our platform is built on an Azure Landing Zone and our own Rust-based Internal Developer Platform (IDP). The IDP lets product teams provision their infrastructure from a simple manifest. It already runs in production, and now we want to take it further.

About your role

You will share ownership of the platform architecture with your team and work closely with our product teams.

You will:

  • Develop and evolve the IDP, built on event-driven, CQRS and event sourcing principles
  • Design and maintain our Azure Landing Zone, networking and Bicep modules
  • Automate identity and access management with Entra ID, PIM and Azure RBAC
  • Build security and compliance into the platform as part of our ISO 27001 work
  • Build observability, monitoring, reliability and FinOps guardrails (such as Azure Policy and cost visibility) into the platform
  • Develop CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps
  • Explore AI-assisted workflows, such as coding agents and MCP servers, that help developers use the platform

About you

You enjoy deep technical work and building things other people use. You like to discuss architecture openly, and you document your work. You also:

  • Have 5+ years of experience in software development or platform engineering, including production systems on Azure
  • Have hands-on experience with Azure Landing Zone, Azure networking and Infrastructure as Code, preferably Bicep
  • Have solid backend skills and experience with APIs and event-driven or distributed systems
  • Have experience with observability, troubleshooting production systems, and ensuring high availability
  • Ideally know Rust. If you are a strong Go or C# engineer who wants to work in Rust, we would also like to hear from you
  • Have a security mindset and experience with CI/CD, containers and identity management
  • Communicate fluently in English. Danish is a plus.

If this sounds like you but you don’t check every box, we still encourage you to apply - we value more curiosity, growth and potential than a perfect match on paper.

What we offer

  • Shared ownership of a platform in production, with room to shape where it goes next
  • Flexible working hours, both at the office and from home
  • A competitive compensation package, subsidized lunch and an active employee association
  • A culture based on trust, responsibility and collaboration
  • Company events where you meet colleagues from all our locations

This is a full-time position, based at our Aalborg office. We work hybrid with 3 office days a week.
