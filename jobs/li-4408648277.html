At Halter, we know how much farmers care - for their animals and their pasture. But the tools they rely on haven’t kept up, and we’re on a mission to change that. Halter helps farmers make smarter decisions - fast. We reckon good pasture management is the key to more productive and sustainable farms. More grass. Healthier soil. Happier animals. And more time back for the people doing the work.

About The Role We're looking for standout Senior Engineers with a deep product instinct: people who care as much about the problem as the solution. You'll take on challenges that genuinely haven't been solved before. We don't mind whether your strengths sit on the frontend, backend, full stack, or on the platform side that gives every other engineer leverage. What matters is that you ship robust, thoughtful work, lift the bar on how we build, and use AI tools well.

The kind of problems our teams solve Halter is one system: hardware, software, and AI, running live on real farms. Our engineering teams own different slices of that system, but the problems all rhyme:

  • Turning thousands of collars and billions of sensor events into a single, calm decision in a farmer's hand.
  • Modelling the physical world (animals, pasture, terrain) accurately enough to act on in real time.
  • Building mobile and web experiences that hold up at 5am whilst out in a paddock in the rain.
  • Designing platforms and pipelines that scale with our incredible pace of growth without the seams showing.
  • Engineering against constrained hardware, flaky rural connectivity, and the messiness of the physical world, and shipping with pace despite it.

Every team here ships software that has to work in a paddock, not just a browser tab.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build, and deliver features and systems end to end, from the surfaces farmers touch to the services and infrastructure behind them.
  • Make smart architectural calls, then back them up by writing the code.
  • Work closely with engineering, design, and product to ship high-impact features quickly.
  • Support and mentor other engineers through design conversations, code reviews, and generous context sharing.
  • Contribute to the reliability and performance of our systems, especially when things get tricky. Every team owns its services end-to-end, with an on-call rotation.
  • Get out on farm. Deep domain knowledge matters, and you'll be expected to develop it.

You'll be great if you:

  • Have designed and delivered complex systems in production and have the experience (and scars) to show for it.
  • Think like a product engineer: you care about the customer experience, not just clean code.
  • Know how to use AI tools well to lift team output and focus on the harder stuff.
  • Have experience supporting and mentoring other engineers.
  • Are comfortable in fast-moving, ambiguous environments where adaptability beats perfection.
  • Have worked in cloud environments. AWS is a bonus.
  • A degree in software engineering (or equivalent) is nice to have, but not essential.

Depending on where you sit on the stack, some of these will land harder than others:

  • Frontend / Mobile: strong UX intuition, modern frontend frameworks (React, React Native a bonus), an eye for performance and polish on real devices.
  • Backend: building and scaling REST APIs, distributed systems, and event-driven services. Comfort with both relational and non-relational databases. Node.js, Kotlin, Go, or systems that integrate with hardware/IoT a bonus.
  • Full stack: all of the above, end to end, across web, mobile, and the services that power them.
  • Platform: you love building tooling that makes the whole company move faster, including the AI tooling that's reshaping how engineers work. Strong AWS and Terraform at scale, with experience across CI/CD, observability, and on-call tooling.

Our Office First Approach There’s a reason you visit your friends in person, live with your family and don’t do dinners over Zoom. Humans are wired for connection. We believe a world-class, in-person office culture is the best way for high-performing teams.

Being office first is a core pillar of our culture. We believe in-person connections are key to driving your own growth, learning, impact, and building genuine long-lasting relationships. Strong relationships make it easier to disagree, give feedback, and do meaningful and aligned work. We don’t like having heaps of rules or policies, but this means having strong, trusted relationships is critical.

We’re office first, not office only. This means working from the office everyday is our default setting, but we flex when we need to. We have a high-trust culture, so everyone is trusted to do what’s best for Halter.

Our office vibe is something special, it’s hard to describe until you’re here, but people at Halter who have come from fully remote or hybrid companies say they could never go back - the high energy and spectacular people they are now surrounded by everyday makes work so enjoyable. Your growth, your learning and your impact is truly unlimited here, and a big part of that comes from being together solving problems, innovating, building context, and constantly learning from each other.
