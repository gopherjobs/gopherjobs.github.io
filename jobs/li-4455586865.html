  • Collaborate with Product Owners and Business Analysts to understand requirements and translate them into technical solutions.

Qualifications What We're Looking for

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Minimum 3 years of software engineering experience, including full stack application development.
  • Experience with:
  • JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, and CSS
  • Front-end frameworks such as React, Angular, or Vue
  • Java backend development
  • RESTful API development
  • ORM technologies such as Hibernate or JPA
  • Good understanding of Agile methodologies, CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, and test automation.
  • Experience with source control and development tools such as Git, Bitbucket, Maven, and Jenkins.
  • Experience with a non-relational / noSQL database such as Elasticsearch, MongoDB, or DynamoDB
  • Experience deploying code in AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud environments
