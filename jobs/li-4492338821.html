The Product

Auth0 is a developer-friendly identity platform that simplifies authentication and authorization for applications.Designed by developers for developers, we make access to applications safe, secure, and seamless for the more than 100 million daily logins around the world. Our modern approach to identity enables this Tier-Ø global service to deliver convenience, privacy, and security so customers can focus on innovation. Know more about our product at https://auth0.com/ .

The Core Identity organization

Core Identity is the Auth0 Engineering organization entrusted with the very heart of our platform: the authentication pipeline, user sessions, and the foundational concepts the rest of the product is built on. We are also the stewards of the platform’s technical health, performance, and efficiency. We are hiring for the Protocols team. The team owns and builds the core authentication and authorization protocols at Auth0, which carry millions of logins every day for customers around the world, and we are also building the protocols that will power the agentic era.

The Role

This is an opportunity to solve complex distributed systems challenges at a global scale. As a Senior Software Engineer on the Protocols team, you will design, build, and operate the services that implement identity standards at global scale. You will primarily work with our Node.js backend (TypeScript/JavaScript), with data stored in MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Redis, running on multi-cloud infrastructure (AWS and Azure).

We use the latest AI technologies and practices in our daily work, from AI coding assistants to AI-assisted design, testing, and operations. We're looking for an engineer who is comfortable using these tools and eager to find new ways they can help us build better software.

If you enjoy simplifying complex problems and building resilient, secure software, we encourage you to apply.

What you will do:

  • Join a fast paced and agile team spread remotely across Central Europe and US or Canada EST.
  • Build innovative features and standards that extend the capabilities of Auth0’s platform to help organizations securely innovate around the world.
  • Take ownership of the technical quality, security, reliability, and scalability of our systems. You'll drive architectural improvements and advocate for engineering best practices.
  • Use AI tools and practices across the development lifecycle and help the team keep improving how we use them.
  • Work in a highly collaborative and cross-functional environment, working with talented engineers and partners across Product, Security, Design, Architecture and QA to deliver features that delight our customers.
  • Deepen or gain expertise in identity, security, and modern cloud technologies (AWS, Azure) while working on distributed systems at scale.
  • Mentor other engineers and contribute to our culture of technical excellence and continuous improvement.
  • Participate in an on-call rotation to ensure our critical services remain healthy and reliable.
  • Work hands-on with new and evolving identity specifications, and share implementation feedback that helps shape them.

What you will bring:

  • 4+ years of professional software development experience, or equivalent.
  • Proficiency in building backend services with Node.js (JavaScript or TypeScript).
  • Experience designing, building, and operating distributed systems in a cloud environment (e.g., AWS, Azure).
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools (e.g., AI coding assistants) in your day-to-day engineering.
  • A strong commitment to quality, with experience in various testing strategies (e.g., unit, integration, end-to-end).
  • A product-oriented mindset, with the ability to understand customer needs and work collaboratively to find effective solutions.

Nice to have:

  • Experience in the identity and access management (IAM) domain.
  • Interest or experience in AI agents or emerging agentic protocols.
  • Knowledge of security engineering principles and application security best practices.
  • Experience working effectively in a distributed, remote-first team.
