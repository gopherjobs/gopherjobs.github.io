Role Summary

We are seeking a Senior Java Developer with hands-on experience in enterprise-grade financial applications and payment integration solutions. The ideal candidate will be responsible for building high-performing, scalable, and resilient applications. You will play a key role in designing and delivering solutions for mission-critical systems within the banking and payments space. This position involves contributing across the full software development lifecycle — from requirement analysis and technical design to development, testing, and deployment — while ensuring compliance with industry standards for performance, security, and reliability.

What You’ll Do

  • Participate in requirement-gathering sessions with product teams and assess the technical feasibility of features and integrations.
  • Take ownership of design and solutioning for modules assigned within a sprint.
  • Drive technical design sessions and peer reviews to ensure alignment with architectural standards.
  • Deliver robust and secure solutions aligned with ISO 20022, SWIFT, SEPA, and other payment formats.
  • Ensure code quality through code reviews, static analysis tools, and adherence to test-driven development.
  • Work closely with QA and DevOps to ensure smooth CI/CD and release processes.
  • Investigate production issues and perform RCA for defects and escalations.
  • Continuously improve the system through innovation, refactoring, and adoption of emerging technologies.

What You Bring

Must-Haves

  • 5-8 years of experience in Java/JEE development with expertise in Core Java, Java EE (8+), multithreading, memory management, and performance tuning.
  • Strong proficiency in Spring ecosystem (Spring Boot, Data, Security) and messaging systems (IBM MQ, RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka) in secure, HA environments.
  • Hands-on experience with relational (PostgreSQL, Oracle, DB2) and NoSQL databases (MongoDB), plus SSL/TLS and secure data flows.
  • Skilled in containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions), and observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Dynatrace).
  • Deep understanding of financial messaging standards (ISO 20022, SWIFT, MT/MX messages, SEPA, NACHA) and payment flows (RTGS, real-time, cross-border), with compliance and security expertise.
  • Proven experience in banking/fintech implementations, including reconciliation, settlement, and reference data management.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience working with APIs for real-time and batch payment processing.
  • Experience in secure system integration, including mutual TLS and client certificate management.
  • Hands-on experience in developing adapters or extensions for products like VolPay or similar integration platforms.
  • Knowledge of PaaS platforms (e.g., AWS MSK, Azure Service Bus, Red Hat OpenShift).
  • Previous contributions to open-source projects or developer communities.
  • Sun/Oracle Certified Java Programmer/Developer is a plus.
