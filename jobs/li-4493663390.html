Description

About The Role And Team Uber One is Uber's membership program and one of the company's biggest growth engines. One subscription gives members savings and perks on rides, food delivery, groceries and more, plus partner benefits, and it's available in markets around the world. Uber One recently passed 50 million members , and those members now drive about half of Uber's Gross Bookings across Mobility and Delivery. Members spend more, come back more often and use more of Uber's services than non-members, which is why membership sits at the center of Uber's platform strategy.

Behind every member's experience are systems that decide which benefits they get, what they pay, how their membership renews, and what they see in the app. All of it has to be correct in every market, currency and product, for tens of millions of people, every day.

As a Senior Software Engineer on the Catalyst team in Membership, you'll build the platform behind how Uber One is configured, launched and evolved. Catalyst's systems let product, operations and engineering teams create and safely roll out new benefits, offers and member experiences in any market, without writing code for each launch. You'll work on high-traffic, low-latency services where correctness matters directly to members and to the business. You'll also own your work end to end, from the first design doc to production support.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build and operate highly available backend services that serve Uber One membership and benefit data at global scale.
  • Build platform capabilities that let teams launch and change membership offers safely and quickly, including validation, approvals and gradual rollouts.
  • Turn ambiguous product and operational requirements into clear technical designs and deliver them with engineers across Membership.
  • Raise the team's engineering quality through design reviews, code reviews, testing, observability and on-call ownership.
  • Mentor engineers and help shape the team's technical direction and roadmap.

Basic Qualifications

  • 6+ years of professional experience in backend software engineering.
  • Proficiency in Java or Go, with experience building and operating production services.
  • Experience designing data models and working with relational or NoSQL databases (e.g. MySQL, PostgreSQL, Cassandra).
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience building high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems serving millions of users.
  • Experience building configuration, rules or experimentation platforms, or internal tools used by non-engineering teams.
  • Experience with subscriptions, payments, pricing or promotions systems where correctness is critical.
  • Experience with caching, event streaming (e.g. Kafka) and safe deployment practices such as feature flags and staged rollouts.
  • Experience leading multi-quarter projects across teams, from design through launch and operations.
