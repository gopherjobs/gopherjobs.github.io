Description

We're looking for a Gemini Enterprise Engineer to design, implement, and support enterprise-grade Generative AI solutions using Google Cloud Platform (GCP) . You'll work closely with business stakeholders, developers, cloud engineers, and security teams to deliver secure, scalable AI-powered applications that transform business processes.

Responsibilities

  • Design, configure, and deploy Gemini Enterprise solutions based on business requirements.
  • Develop AI-powered enterprise search, knowledge discovery, workflow automation, and AI agent solutions.
  • Integrate Gemini Enterprise with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, databases, APIs, cloud storage, and other enterprise systems.
  • Build secure data ingestion, indexing, retrieval, and search experiences.
  • Implement Identity and Access Management (IAM), role-based access controls, and AI governance best practices.
  • Monitor application performance, troubleshoot issues, and optimize AI search quality and user experience.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, develop technical solutions, and deliver Proofs of Concept (POCs).
  • Create technical documentation and provide training to administrators and end users.
