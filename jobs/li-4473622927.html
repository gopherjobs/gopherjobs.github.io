About the Role

As a Full Stack Developer, you'll be responsible for improving and maintaining our Reseller platform. You'll be involved in the product lifecycle from ideation to deployment to maintenance, making sure we build a robust and scalable solution. You'll work closely with the Product and Customer Success teams to build features that provide direct value to our partners, while ensuring they are scalable and meet our quality standards. You will be the driving force behind backend tasks in a multidisciplinary team.
