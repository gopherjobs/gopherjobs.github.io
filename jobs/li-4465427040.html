Job Description:

Title:  AI Engineer

Type:  Contract, 6 months + possibility of extension 

Location:  Toronto preferred (remote flexibility for a strong candidate)

Mode: Onsite

Pay Range:  $64 - $80/hr (based on experience)

Role Brief: We are seeking an AI Engineer to build and deploy production-grade applications powered by Generative AI. The role is focused on GenAI application engineering rather than developing AI models from scratch . The engineer will work with LLMs, RAG, AI agents, APIs, and cloud technologies to deliver AI solutions end-to-end, from architecture and development through deployment and optimization.

Top Must-Haves

  1. 6+ years of Software/Application Engineering experience , including 1-2+ years building GenAI applications in production.

  2. Strong Python development experience with hands-on expertise building APIs, microservices, and production applications .

  3. Proven experience developing GenAI solutions , particularly RAG, LLM pipelines, and Agentic AI .

  4. Strong AWS/cloud experience , Bedrock, Lambda, EKS, or Fargate , and experience deploying cloud-native applications.

  5. Hands-on experience with LLM frameworks and models , such as Lang Chain, LlamaIndex, GPT, Claude, Gemini, or LLaMA .

  6. Experience with AI application architecture and deployment , including Docker, Terraform, CI/CD, monitoring, and production optimization .
