Description

Signicat is a pan-European digital identity company that makes it easy and safe for organisations to know who they are interacting with online. Using our proprietary technology, we help companies and public organisations securely verify the identities of people and businesses, authenticate users, and enable compliant electronic document signing, so their customers can access services and complete online processes with confidence.

Today, Signicat employs over 530 people across 20 offices in 15 European countries. We work with leading banks, fintechs, telcos, public authorities, healthcare providers, and enterprises across retail and mobility to deliver trusted digital identity and onboarding solutions at scale.

Joining Signicat means contributing to a mission that has a real impact on how society works and trusting each other in an increasingly digital world.

About The Role Join our team behind ReadID, the market leader in mobile identity verification. As a Java Cloud & DevOps Engineer, you will contribute to building, scaling, and maintaining our high-throughput SaaS platform. You will bridge backend software engineering and modern cloud operations, helping deliver continuous improvements across public and sovereign clouds on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and/or AWS with Kubernetes.

About The Team You will be joining a multidisciplinary agile team with an informal, open, and positive atmosphere with a culture of mutual help and support. The team is located in the heart of Enschede in a modern workplace offering a comfortable working setup. There is a friendly, social vibe, with activities during and outside of work. A significant part of the team usually works from the office, which helps integration and getting up to speed quickly.

What you will do

  • Backend Engineering: Develop, test, and maintain features for the ReadID SaaS backend with a focus on reliability, performance, and security.
  • Cloud & Containerization: Deploy, configure, and monitor containerized services running on Kubernetes (GKE) and cloud infrastructure.
  • CI/CD & Deployments: Maintain and improve CI/CD pipelines to support smooth, automated releases and reliable deployments.
  • Clean Code & Quality Engineering: Write clean, modular, and well-tested code (Unit, Integration tests) adhering to team standards and best practices.
  • Quality Assurance: Collaborate on release testing and validation to ensure seamless interoperability across mobile platforms and international identity documents.
  • DevOps Collaboration: Actively participate in infrastructure automation, observability, troubleshooting, and continuous platform improvements.
  • Agile Teamwork: Work closely with cross-functional team members, participating actively in sprints, code reviews, and knowledge-sharing sessions.

What we expect

  • Education: BSc or MSc degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Java Foundation: Solid working experience with server-side Java engineering (e.g., Java 17+, Spring / Spring Boot, or Jakarta EE).
  • Cloud & Container Basics: Practical experience or hands-on exposure to Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud environments (GCP or AWS), with an eagerness to deepen your cloud expertise.
  • APIs & Databases: Understanding of RESTful APIs, JSON/XML, and relational databases (such as PostgreSQL or MySQL).
  • Security Awareness: Understanding of core application security principles, secure coding practices, and OWASP fundamentals.
  • Mindset & Drive: A curious, problem-solving mindset with a willingness to learn, ask questions, and take ownership of tasks.
  • Communication: Good professional proficiency in English (both verbal and written).
  • Site Reliability & Support: Take part in periodic 24/7 on-call shifts to support incident management and platform reliability.
  • Location: Located within commuting distance of Enschede (or willing to relocate), comfortable working from the office at least 2 days a week (most colleagues prefer ~4 days).

Nice to Have

  • Exposure to Observability & Logging tools (e.g., ELK Stack, Prometheus, Grafana, Cloud Monitoring).
  • Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (e.g., Terraform) or GitOps concepts.
  • Basic knowledge of frontend web technologies (JavaScript, TypeScript, Angular).
  • Relevant cloud or Kubernetes certifications (e.g., Google Cloud Associate Cloud Engineer or CKAD/CKA).
