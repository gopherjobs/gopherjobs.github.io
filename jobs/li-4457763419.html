Description

This position collaborates with business stakeholders, solution engineers, architects and Agile teams to deliver Guidewire configuration and integration solutions that support complex insurance initiatives. The role serves as a subject‑matter resource for the Guidewire platform, providing system‑level perspectives and implementing technical solutions aligned with business and architectural objectives. It focuses on optimizing Guidewire configuration, building reliable integrations using APIs and messaging frameworks, and improving development and deployment processes through CI/CD and DevOps practices. The position ensures successful technology delivery across lines of business and the enterprise, performing work independently with limited guidance or direction.

Essential Job Functions:

  • Codes instructions for computer programs, objects, and components across multiple applications; configures packaged software to deliver required system functionality
  • Provides advanced technical expertise in solution implementation and application functionality, ensuring alignment with architectural standards and business requirements.
  • Reviews and validates technical implementations of team members to ensure quality, consistency, and adherence to best‑practice architecture principles
  • Develops and maintains application components with a focus on performance, reusability, maintainability, and efficient use of system and storage resources
  • Analyzes existing automated and manual processes and implements system enhancements to improve efficiency and reliability.
  • Researches, develops, and implements automation techniques to improve development efficiency and system sustainability.
  • Documents and implements application changes following installation standards, coding conventions, and enterprise guidelines.
  • Analyzes and supports system requirements related to security, backup, recovery, restart, controls, and data integrity, recommending appropriate technical solutions.
  • Analyzes and documents requirements for data modeling and data conversion to support application functionality and integrations.
  • Performs quality checks on code, configuration, related components, and documentation; delivers production‑ready working code incrementally each sprint
