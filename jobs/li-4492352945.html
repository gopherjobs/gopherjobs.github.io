Key Responsibilities & Objectives

Your Key Responsibilities

  • Full-Stack Architecture: Architect, implement, and maintain the end-to-end stack—from device kernel and hardware abstraction layer (data acquisition) to cloud microservices (training, ingestion, inference) and the Android application (diagnostics, actionable user alerts).
  • AI Development: Design and build AI features on Fairphone Android on full ML lifecycle.
  • Frontend & Diagnostics App: Take ownership of the user-facing Android components utilizing modern Android development practices (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose) to deliver intuitive diagnostic interfaces and transparent device health insights.
  • MLOps & CI/CD: Clean, manage, and curate datasets; version, test, package, and deploy models into production environments with automated validation and monitoring.
  • Quality, Testing & Reliability: Conduct comprehensive unit and integration testing across edge cases, thermal limits, and battery constraints to ensure rock-solid stability and low system overhead.
  • Collaboration: Partner closely with cross-functional teams (Embedded, OS Longevity, Product, Quality, and Open Source teams) to define, ship, and continuously refine intelligent features.
  • Ecosystem & Standards: Ensure full compatibility with the Android SDK, Android framework, carrier certification requirements, and Google Mobile Services (GMS) compliance.

Your Key Objectives

  • Lead the strategic AI development for Fairphone devices.
  • Architect, implement, and maintain a robust end-to-end intelligence pipeline—spanning on-device data acquisition (kernel/HAL/framework), cloud ingestion, model serving, and user-facing Android applications.
  • Design, deploy, and optimize AI models throughout the full ML lifecycle.
  • Establish continuous MLOps and CI/CD pipelines where AI models are treated as first-class, versioned, and monitored software artifacts.
  • Champion ethical AI practices, data privacy, and open source collaboration within the mobile device ecosystem.

  • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or equivalent practical experience.
