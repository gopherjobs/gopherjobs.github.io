DevOps Engineer (Good English)

About SmartOSC

SmartOSC is a leading digital transformation and digital commerce agency headquartered in Vietnam, with offices across Asia-Pacific, Europe and North America. We help global brands, retailers and financial institutions build, scale and run their digital platforms, from eCommerce and mobile apps to cloud and banking solutions.

About the Role

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer with strong English communication skills to join our HCM team. You will work directly with international clients and cross-functional teams to design, automate and operate reliable, secure and scalable infrastructure for enterprise-grade digital products.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, build and maintain CI/CD pipelines to automate build, test and deployment processes
  • Provision and manage cloud and/or on-premise infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation…)
  • Deploy, operate and optimize containerized applications on Docker and Kubernetes
  • Set up and maintain monitoring, logging and alerting systems (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK/EFK, CloudWatch…) to ensure system availability and performance
  • Implement security best practices: access control, secrets management, vulnerability scanning and compliance requirements
  • Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure and deployment issues, and perform root cause analysis
  • Collaborate with developers, QA and project managers to improve release processes and system reliability
  • Communicate directly with clients in English: clarify technical requirements, report status and propose solutions
  • Write and maintain technical documentation (architecture, runbooks, operating procedures)

Requirements

Must-have

  • 2+ years of experience in a DevOps, SRE or System Engineer role
  • Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure or GCP)
  • Solid experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production environments
  • Proficiency with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions or Azure DevOps
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform, Ansible or equivalent)
  • Strong Linux administration skills and scripting ability (Bash, Python or similar)
  • Good understanding of networking fundamentals (DNS, load balancing, VPN, firewall, TCP/IP)
  • Good English communication skills (speaking and writing) , with the ability to work directly with international clients and team members

Nice-to-have

  • Cloud certifications (AWS Solutions Architect / DevOps Engineer, CKA/CKAD, Azure Administrator…)
  • Experience with on-premise infrastructure or hybrid cloud environments
  • Knowledge of GitOps tools (ArgoCD, Flux) and service mesh (Istio)
  • Experience in banking, fintech, or eCommerce projects with high security and availability requirements
  • Familiarity with DevSecOps practices and tools (SonarQube, Trivy, Vault)

What We Offer

  • 13th-month salary and performance bonus
  • Opportunity to work on large-scale projects with global clients
  • Full social, health and unemployment insurance per Vietnamese Labour Law, plus premium healthcare insurance
  • Sponsorship for professional certifications and training programs
  • Young, dynamic and international working environment
  • Team building, company trips and regular engagement activities
  • Working hours: Monday to Friday
