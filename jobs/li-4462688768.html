Description

We help our Clients define their transformation journey enabled by the latest technology - generating insights to illuminate innovative ideas, creating valuable experiences for customers, achieving huge cost savings or embracing new collaborative mindsets.

We are looking for:

Data Solutions Engineer (rola projektowa) Your future role:

  • Review and analyse business and functional requirements,
  • Design test scenarios and test cases based on project documentation and business processes,
  • Execute functional UI level tests within Microsoft Dynamics 365 environments,
  • Execute integration and API tests,
  • Identify, report and monitor defects to ensure timely resolution,
  • Report on the progress of assigned testing activities,
  • Collaborate closely with analysts and developers to ensure prompt and effective defect resolution,
  • Support end-to-end testing activities across business processes,
  • Prepare and maintain test documentation and testing evidence,
  • Contribute to maintaining high quality standards throughout the software delivery lifecycle.

Apply if you have:

  • At least 2 years of professional experience in software testing,
  • Experience in testing Microsoft Dynamics 365 products,
  • Knowledge of German language enabling effective communication with stakeholders,
  • Very good command of English, both written and spoken,
  • Practical knowledge of test management and defect tracking tools such as HP ALM, Jira, Azure DevOps or similar,
  • Understanding of end-to-end business processes,
  • Experience working in Agile and/or Waterfall software delivery methodologies,
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills,
  • Ability to work independently and proactively,
  • Strong organizational and communication skills,
  • Ability to work effectively within international teams,
  • Demonstrated knowledge of new technologies and AI-powered tools in your daily work (e.g. task automation, information analysis, content creation),
  • Availability 5 days a week.
  • The ability to operate as a sole proprietor or business entity within Poland (due to the project-based nature of the employment).

Nice to have:

  • ISTQB Certification,
  • Experience in large-scale ERP implementation or transformation projects.

By joining us you gain:
