Description

We are bsport. The place to be! bsport is an all-in-one platform combining boutique fitness and advanced technology. Our platform helps our partners manage their bookings, payroll, marketing and more, to streamline operations and boost their commercial success.

  • We’ve built a community of over 7 million users 🤝

  • Grown our team to more than 200 employees 🚀

Join us to write the next chapter of your career!

About The Role As a Senior Backend Engineer in our Community & Engagement team, you’ll help build and evolve the backend systems powering our marketing and communications capabilities, used by thousands of studios worldwide. Your team’s focus areas will include:

  • Driving initiatives that bring bsport into the world of agentic AI
  • Maintaining and evolving our marketing suite, including our Campaigns tool and workflow automation capabilities
  • Building and scaling our SMS, email and push notification communication channels, as well as future integrations with new channels
  • Helping our studios gain a better understanding of their community by improving the foundations of our member data
  • Contributing to the re-architecture of core functionalities within bsport, from our member segmentation tools to our communications stack

🎯 What Will Be Your Impact As a Senior Backend Engineer, you will:

  • Design, build and maintain scalable and reliable backend systems powering our marketing, communication and member data products
  • Take ownership of complex technical projects, from architecture and implementation to deployment and continuous improvement
  • Contribute to the re-architecture of core backend functionalities, helping us improve scalability, maintainability and performance
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Front-end Engineers and other teams to turn business requirements into robust technical solutions
  • Drive technical excellence through code reviews, best practices and thoughtful engineering decisions
  • Help shape the technical direction of the team and mentor other engineers through your technical expertise and experience

🌟You Would Be Great Fit If You Have

  • Experience building AI agentic solutions to manage complex tasks, especially with LangChain suite.
  • You have substantial experience in any current Object-Oriented backend language (familiarity with Python and Django is a plus)
  • You have experience designing and building scalable backend systems and APIs
  • You take ownership and pride in building exceptional products
  • You’re detail-oriented, organized, and an effective communicator
  • You thrive in dynamic, fast-paced environments and enjoy being challenged
  • You have a strong understanding of software architecture, code quality and engineering best practices
  • You are comfortable working on complex technical problems and making pragmatic engineering decisions
  • You are professionally proficient in English
