Synthesia is the world’s leading AI video platform for business, used by over 90% of the Fortune 100. Founded in 2017, the company is headquartered in London, with offices and teams across Europe and the US.

About The Role The Developer Platform team builds internal tools and platform that improve the velocity, quality and developer experience of Synthesia's engineering teams. They own developer productivity (from code commit to production), developer insights and SDLC metrics, developer infrastructure (CI/CD systems), AI-assisted QA/testing and AI-assisted development. AI Adoption has grown to 90%+ employees with 100%~ adoption in engineering in the last 8 months. You'll have a big surface area and real ownership from day one into making Synthesia's engineering go faster but with quality in this day and age of AI-driven development.

  • You will work end-to-end owning the architectural decisions that govern how features are built, released safely to customers, and supported long-term. You'll mentor engineers and embed technical practice through code review and design partnership.
  • You will lead projects that span quarters, breaking down complex problems into milestones that can be validated iteratively. You'll operate with high autonomy: defining success criteria, calling trade-offs, and knowing when to escalate to leadership.
  • You will partner directly with leadership to shape the roadmap and technical strategy. You'll have the skills to push back on requirements, propose alternatives, and influence what we build and when.
  • You will own the operational health of systems you build and you'll use data to drive decisions about iteration, maintenance, and deprecation.
  • You will be accountable for the technical direction of your area. You'll anticipate 12+ month technical challenges, propose solutions that scale, and build the engineering capabilities that will let us execute ahead of the curve.

What We're Looking For

  • At least seven (7) years of experience as a software engineer, with at least 2-3 years working on senior/staff level
  • Proven hands-on experience building and operating developer platforms, CI/CD systems, observability/metrics infrastructure, code quality automation, testing infrastructure and/or developer tooling.
  • You have previously shipped infrastructure or tooling that measurably improved developer velocity or reduced cognitive load.
  • Previous experience leading the adoption of new AI-powered developer tools or capabilities across an engineering organization.
  • You have operated in a high-performing engineering organization (scale-up or established) where you've seen how infrastructure choices directly impact team velocity and product quality. You understand what it means to build for other engineers.
  • Experience integrating third-party services (APIs, SaaS tools, external platforms) into internal systems, with attention to reliability, security, and user experience. You understand the operational constraints of running other people's code/services at scale.
  • Clear understanding that platform and infrastructure work directly enables commercial outcomes.
  • Previous experience owning large technical areas end-to-end.
