Description

We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Platform Engineer to drive the design, evolution, and reliability of our platform ecosystem. In this role, you will take ownership of technical decisions , guide architectural direction, and coordinate technical work across engineering teams to ensure scalable, secure, and efficient solutions.

You will act as a bridge between engineering, product, and operations, balancing hands-on engineering with technical leadership and collaboration .

Your role: Technical Leadership & Decision-Making

  • Own and drive architecture and design decisions for platform services and infrastructure.
  • Define and enforce engineering standards, best practices, and design patterns.
  • Evaluate and select technologies, tools, and frameworks aligned with business goals.
  • Provide technical guidance and mentorship to engineers across the team.
  • Lead design reviews and ensure solutions meet scalability, performance, and reliability requirements.

Team Coordination & Delivery

  • Coordinate technical tasks across multiple engineers and teams to ensure consistent progress and alignment.
  • Break down high-level initiatives into actionable engineering work items.
  • Act as a technical point of contact for cross-team initiatives and dependencies.
  • Facilitate collaboration between development, QA, DevOps, and product teams.
  • Support delivery planning and help remove technical blockers.

Platform Engineering & Operations

  • Design and maintain robust platform infrastructure (cloud, on-prem, hybrid).
  • Build and improve CI/CD pipelines and automation frameworks.
  • Ensure high availability, observability, and performance of platform services.
  • Drive infrastructure-as-code (IaC) and configuration management adoption.
  • Implement monitoring, alerting, and incident response processes.

Reliability, Security & Compliance

  • Ensure systems meet security, compliance, and governance standards.
  • Champion SRE principles including SLAs, SLOs, and error budgets.
  • Lead root cause analysis and continuous improvement after incidents.
  • Improve system resilience and disaster recovery capabilities.

Your Background:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience).
  • 5–10+ years of experience in platform engineering, DevOps, or infrastructure roles.
  • Proven experience in making and owning architectural decisions.
  • Strong proficiency in cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP).
  • Experience with:
  • Containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Bicep, CloudFormation, etc.)
  • CI/CD tools (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, etc.)
  • Strong scripting or programming skills (Python, Go, Bash, etc.).
  • Experience working in Agile/DevOps environments.
