What the role is about

No playbook exists. You'll write it.

You don't build from a distance. You sit with the client, understand their world, and ship solutions in the room. You're the person who turns a messy whiteboard session on Monday into a working prototype by Friday and make the client feel like they built it with you, not for them.

Here's what that looks like day to day:

  • You orchestrate agents to build what teams used to build by hand: faster, sharper, at a scale that wasn't possible before
  • You shape fast-paced AI breakthrough projects from PoC to production-ready MVP, ensuring they generate real business value
  • You build data pipelines, run ML workflows, develop and deploy web services on cloud environments (preferably Azure)
  • You support integration with client systems and make sure what we ship actually holds up in the real world
  • You work embedded in one of our squads: close to the action, with short decision paths and real ownership
  • You bring AI to organizations that need it most: corporates, Mittelstand, scale-ups, NGOs, and the public sector
  • You help shape who we are – internally and externally – as a key contributor in a fast-growing company

What you bring

Engineering depth:

  • 3+ years of professional experience building scalable, production-grade software
  • Hands-on ML in production: model training, deployment, monitoring; proficient with TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn
  • Strong Python and SQL: solid foundations, clean code, good engineering instincts
  • Cloud and web experience: at least one cloud platform (preferably Azure), secure web app setup, PostgreSQL
  • You run multiple Claude Code instances in parallel and feel frustrated when you can't
  • You've created proper evals and let AI agents cook for 30+ minutes

Forward Deployed mindset:

  • You're comfortable in a boardroom and a terminal on the same day and you switch between the two without losing a beat

  • You translate between business and technology fluently – in both directions
  • Comfort with ambiguity: vague requirements are your starting point, not a blocker
  • German from C1 for confident client communication

Don't meet every point? Apply anyway.

Travel: This role requires being on-site with clients around 50% of the time. You're comfortable with that – and ideally, you see it as a feature, not a bug.
