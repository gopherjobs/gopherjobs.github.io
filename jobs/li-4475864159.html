What they're looking for

  • Strong backend skills in Go, or in Rust or Java with an interest in moving to Go
  • Hands-on experience building distributed systems
  • Experience with messaging or streaming tools such as Kafka, Google Pub/Sub or RabbitMQ
  • Experience building and supporting production systems
