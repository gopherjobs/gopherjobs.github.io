JOB DESCRIPTION & REQUIREMENTS

Department/Reporting Line:

IC (Individual Contributor) role — works closely and daily with Product, Frontend, and Engineering team members from the Client. Not a people-management or architecture-only role.

Job Purpose / Summary:

Hands-on backend developer responsible for building, shipping, and maintaining production APIs and services for a live multi-tenant SaaS product. Owns features end-to-end and stays close to production, not just writing code but ensuring it runs correctly for real users.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design, build, and maintain backend services and APIs in a live multi-tenant product.
  • Own features end-to-end: data model, business logic, integrations, tests, and rollout.
  • Integrate with third-party systems (REST APIs, auth, webhooks, retries, mapping messy payloads).
  • Write and review SQL and schema migrations; keep data correct under real traffic.
  • Debug production issues and follow through to a fix, not just log a ticket.
  • Collaborate with frontend and product on contracts, edge cases, and delivery timelines.
  • Review code, give clear feedback, and keep the team unblocked.
  • Stay reachable on team channels during working hours, respond promptly.

Required Qualifications/Skills:

  • 4+ years of professional backend development, shipping production systems.
  • Strong Python (production experience).
  • Solid SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL or similar).
  • Experience with APIs, background jobs/message queues, external integrations.
  • Comfortable working in an existing codebase without breaking tenants/flows.
  • Clear written and spoken English.
  • Reliable availability during team's working hours (small team — same-day response needed when something is stuck in production).

Preferred Qualifications (Optional):

  • SQLAlchemy (or similar ORM), database migrations
  • Node.js / Express
  • Redis, object storage (S3 or similar)
  • RabbitMQ or another message queue
  • Docker
  • ERP, marketplace, or payments/accounting APIs
  • Multi-tenant SaaS experience

Work Environment/Location:

Remote/offshore (Vietnam-based via AST), working hours aligned to overlap with Mind Master Solutions team (Singapore). Reachable on Slack/chat, able to join calls same-day when needed.

Company Values/Culture Note:

Small, tight-knit team culture — direct communication, high responsiveness expected, no tolerance for delayed replies that block others.
