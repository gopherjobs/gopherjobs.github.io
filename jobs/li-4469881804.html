As a member of a data engineering team , you will be responsible for the design, development, integration, delivery, maintenance and support of data pipelines and applications. You'll bring experience managing and supporting Postgres and SQL Server databases , along with experience building data ingestion pipelines on the Databricks platform . We're looking for a self-motivated professional with experience designing and architecting technical solutions and the ability to quickly learn new technologies and programming languages. You will propose, develop, and support customer-facing applications, delivering hands-on solutions that meet or exceed customer expectations.

How Your Day-to-day Work Will Look Like

  • Architect, design, and deliver high-quality code independently, following industry best practices.
  • Understand and make well-reasoned design decisions and tradeoffs in your work.
  • Diagnose and fix production issues, communicating with the development lead to raise issues and anticipate potential barriers.
  • Build enterprise applications at scale using expertise in computer science fundamentals, developing scalable, high-availability, high-performance components across front-end and back-end architectures.
  • Seek empirical evidence through proof of concepts, tests, and external research; participate in special projects as assigned.

This Would Make You The Ideal Candidate

  • Bachelor's degree in a related field or equivalent experience.
  • Demonstrated experience administering RDS Postgres and Aurora databases, with an understanding of Microsoft SQL Server database administration.
  • Experience with the Databricks platform, including Unity Catalog, notebooks, and pipeline creation.
  • 3 years of software development experience and/or commensurate skills building commercial applications with modern software engineering principles and practices.
  • Demonstrated record of contributing successfully in distributed software delivery teams using Agile/Scrum and DevSecOps practices.
  • Proficiency with cloud technologies (AWS, Serverless, Event-Driven Architecture, SOA, Microservices, Microfrontend, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code) and other modern technologies.
  • Proficiency with professional software engineering practices across the full software development life cycle, including coding standards, architecture/design patterns, code reviews, source control management, build processes, testing, and operations.
