Why This Role Matters ICEYE's Flood Solutions exist to help people make better decisions when floods happen. We strive to provide the most accurate information before, during and after flood events. As a Engineering Lead, you will lead one of our flood product teams: its people, its delivery and the health of the systems it builds and runs in production. The team supports two products, Flood Rapid Impact (FRI) and Flood Insights (FI). It is a cross-functional team of software engineers, ML engineers, geospatial data scientists and hydroinformatics engineers, all reporting to you. Within the team, the hydroinformatics engineers lead the analytics and own the analytical quality of the products. The team builds and runs the flood analysis platform: a Python backends on PostgreSQL/PostGIS, a map-heavy React UIs, Argo Workflows based analysis pipelines, and event-driven services that turn satellite flood observations into data customers rely on. You will work in tandem with the Product Manager. They own what we build and why; you own how the team builds and delivers it, and the people who do. You stay hands-on, which keeps your technical judgement credible and your feedback specific, and your success is measured through what the team achieves. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.

ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.

ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.

Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

Your Day-to-day Responsibilities

  • People & Team Leadership: Lead the people in your team through 1:1s, clear expectations, timely feedback and coaching. Own hiring, onboarding, growth and performance conversations, including the hard ones, and help grow the next technical and team leaders.
  • Iterative Product Delivery: Plan and run the team's work together with the team. Turn priorities into small, testable increments that ship end to end, keep work in progress low, and make delivery of the team's quarterly goals predictable and visible. Follow progress from data and re-plan early with Product when scope or dates move.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work with the Product Manager as a pair: they own priorities and the product outcome, you own the delivery plan and the trade-offs behind it. Partner daily with Product and Operations , bring engineers and hydrologists together on shared acceptance criteria, and manage the team's dependencies on other teams.
  • Operational Excellence & Reliability: The team runs what it builds. Own production quality and operational readiness, run the team's on-call rotation, make sure the team has the telemetry to know how its services are doing at any time, and plan the capacity needed to run what it has next to building new things.
  • Pragmatic Architecture & Evolution: Make or guide the team's technical decisions with reversible, incremental patterns, together with our Staff Engineers. Own how the team's analytical methods are validated, and raise the team's engineering bar while balancing speed, risk and quality.
  • Hands-on Engineering: Stay a strong technical contributor in the team's code, set the example in code reviews and pairing, and stay close enough to the details to guide technical trade-offs.

Must Haves What we’re looking for

  • Domain experience: professional experience in geospatial, Earth observation, Earth science or hydrology
  • People leadership: experience with 1:1s, coaching, feedback, and supporting growth and performance, including addressing performance issues directly and kindly
  • Engineering: senior-level experience designing, delivering and running cloud native production software, designing for resilience and learning from incidents
  • Hands-on stack: strong Python (FastAPI/async) and PostgreSQL, and enough TypeScript/React to review and contribute to the UI
  • Delivery: experience planning and running a team’s delivery, breaking down ambiguous work, managing dependencies and making progress visible, with a focus on finishing over starting
  • Science partnership: experience working with scientists as peers, understanding how analytical methods are validated before production, and explaining trade-offs clearly to scientists, engineers and Product
  • Office collaboration: you welcome working 3 days per week in the Espoo office and thrive in direct collaboration

Nice To Haves

  • Experience with SAR, optical or other remote sensing data, ideally building products on it end to end
  • Experience with flood mapping, water surface elevation or DEMs, or with flood or hydrological modelling in production
  • Experience with raster and vector data in production (for example rasterio, GeoPandas, PostGIS) and geospatial standards such as STAC, COGs, OGC or tile serving
  • Experience running ML models in production, including MLOps
  • Familiarity with AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker and CI/CD, and planning infrastructure changes together with platform teams
  • Experience with event-driven systems and long-running pipelines: queues, retries, idempotency and workflow orchestration such as Argo Workflows, Airflow or Dagster
  • Use of tooling, including AI-assisted workflows where permitted, to speed up routine work while keeping humans accountable for quality
