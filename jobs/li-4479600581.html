What you'll do

We are seeking an experienced MLOps Engineer to join our team and drive the design, implementation, and optimization of our machine learning lifecycle and deployment platform. This role requires deep expertise in MLOps, continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD/CT) for machine learning models, cloud infrastructure orchestration, model monitoring, and applied Large Language Model (LLM) operationalization (LLMOps).

This position is an individual contributor role reporting to Machine Learning Engineering Manager.

Responsibility

  • Architect, build, and maintain scalable MLOps platform capabilities to automate model training, evaluation, registration, deployment, and monitoring
  • Implement CI/CD/CT (Continuous Integration, Continuous Deployment, Continuous Training) pipelines to ensure seamless, reliable model deployment and automated retraining workflows
  • Establish model monitoring, observability, and alerting frameworks to track data drift, concept drift, latency, throughput, and overall prediction quality in production
  • Operationalize Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI systems into production environments, including LLMOps workflows, prompt engineering platforms, vector databases, and evaluation frameworks
  • Design and maintain model registries, metadata tracking, and feature stores (e.g., Feast, MLflow) to ensure auditability, governance, and reusability across ML teams
  • Provision, scale, and optimize Kubernetes-based machine learning inference and training clusters for high availability, low latency, and cost efficiency
  • Collaborate closely with data scientists, ML engineers, and software teams to streamline model handoffs and accelerate time-to-production for AI capabilities
  • Implement automated data quality, model testing, and guardrail frameworks to enforce responsible AI and data governance practices
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex production incidents across model serving infrastructure and data streams

What you bring

Basic

  • 2+ years of experience in MLOps, DevOps, or Data Engineering with a focus on machine learning infrastructure
  • Experience with Python and shell scripting for automation and infrastructure engineering
  • Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm)
  • Experience building CI/CD/CT pipelines using GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins for ML model deployment
  • Experience with MLOps frameworks, model registries, and experiment tracking tools (e.g., MLflow, Kubeflow, BentoML, Triton Inference Server, Ray)
  • Experience operationalizing LLMs and GenAI solutions (e.g., OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, open-source models) via APIs or self-hosted inference frameworks (vLLM, TGI), including guardrails, vector stores, and evaluation pipelines
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible
  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and microservice/serverless architectures
  • Experience with system monitoring (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog) and ML monitoring frameworks (Evidently, Arize, or Fiddler)
  • Experience with version control (Git), SQL, and database optimization techniques

Preferred

  • Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work with complex, ambiguous requirements
  • Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate across multidisciplinary teams
