Description

Location: Praha, Czechia

Thales people architect identity management and data protection solutions at the heart of digital security. Business and governments rely on us to bring trust to the billions of digital interactions they have with people. Our technologies and services help banks exchange funds, people cross borders, energy become smarter and much more. More than 30,000 organizations already rely on us to verify the identities of people and things, grant access to digital services, analyze vast quantities of information and encrypt data to make the connected world more secure.

Thales in the Czech Republic employs over 400 people from 45 different nationalities. A total of 15 teams work on projects for government agencies, banking, mobile services and the Internet Of Things (IoT) technology. At the core of our business is the development of software which we configure and embed in a multitude of different devices and form factors. These include many kinds of payment cards, SIM cards, travel passes, secure eBanking devices, authentication tokens, machine identification modules (MIM), and secure ID documents including ePassports, eID and eHealth cards, as well as eDriving licenses. Because of the international environment surrounding us every day, it comes as no surprise that English is our official corporate language.

The Partner Technical Leader provides technical leadership for partner connectivity, ensuring that integrations are secure, resilient, scalable, and aligned with the evolution of the D1 platform.

Mission

  • Technical lead for the Connectivity squad for connections with our partners (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple, Google, etc.).
  • Define and evolve the partner connectivity architecture, with a focus on security, resilience, performance, and multi-tenancy.
  • Anticipate technological developments and partner requirements, assess their impact, and propose adaptation roadmaps for the D1 platform.
  • Drive technical connectivity choices and standards, and support the connectivity squad in implementing them.
  • From an engineering perspective, oversee technical partner integration and certification topics through to production deployment.

Deliverables

  • Documented and maintained architectures, patterns, and partner security/connectivity requirements.
  • Technical roadmap, impact analyses, and migration plans associated with partner changes.
  • Technical documentation and evidence required for certifications, including tracking of risks and technical debt.

Interactions

  • Work daily with the squad’s developers. Act as the technical leader for the partner scope.
  • Coordinate technically with partners and internal teams : Architecture, Security, Integrator, CRE, SRE, and Product teams.
  • Report functionally to the Technical Project Manager, who prioritizes the backlog and arbitrates the squad activity. Provide regular updates on risks, dependencies, decisions, and deadlines.
