Senior Full Stack Engineer – Java / TypeScript / Vue

Remote | Contract until End of March 2027 | Likely Extension

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Full Stack Engineer to join a major technology programme within a highly regulated, high-volume environment .

This is a remote contract running until the end of March 2027 , with a strong likelihood of extension.

The Role

You’ll join a cross-functional engineering team responsible for designing, building and enhancing business-critical digital platforms.

We’re looking for a genuinely full-stack engineer with particularly strong Java backend development experience , combined with modern frontend engineering using TypeScript and Vue.js .

The platform operates within a highly regulated, high-traffic environment, so experience building reliable, scalable and secure software is essential.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Design, build and maintain scalable backend services and APIs using Java .
  • Develop modern frontend applications using TypeScript and Vue.js .
  • Work across the full software development lifecycle, from technical design through to deployment and production support.
  • Design and integrate RESTful APIs and services.
  • Produce clean, maintainable and well-tested production code.
  • Contribute to architectural and technical design decisions.
  • Work closely with Product, Engineering, QA, Platform and other technical teams.
  • Troubleshoot complex technical and production issues.
  • Build solutions with security, scalability, resilience and regulatory compliance at their core.
  • Participate in code reviews and drive engineering best practice.
  • Provide technical guidance and mentoring to other engineers where required.

Essential Experience

  • Strong commercial Java backend engineering experience.
  • Strong frontend development experience using TypeScript .
  • Commercial experience with Vue.js .
  • Proven experience operating at Senior Full Stack Engineer / Senior Software Engineer level.
  • Strong experience designing and integrating APIs.
  • Experience developing scalable, high-availability applications.
  • Strong understanding of automated testing and modern software engineering practices.
  • Experience with CI/CD and modern deployment practices.
  • Comfortable taking ownership of technical delivery within Agile, multidisciplinary teams.

Industry Experience

Previous experience within iGaming, gambling, betting, lottery or online gaming is highly desirable .

Alternatively, we are interested in engineers with strong experience in other highly regulated, high-volume environments , including:

  • Financial Services / FinTech
  • Payments
  • Banking
  • Insurance
  • Government
  • Other regulated consumer technology businesses

You must be comfortable working in environments where regulation, security, resilience, data protection and auditability are critical considerations.
