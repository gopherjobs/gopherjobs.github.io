Founding Software Engineer – Agentic Systems

London | Hybrid | Competitive Salary + Equity

We're building next-generation AI systems and are looking for a strong software engineer with experience building agentic products to join the team at an exciting stage of growth.

This is not a prompt engineering role . You'll be designing and building production software around LLMs working on agents that can reason, use tools, execute tasks and operate reliably in real-world environments.

What you'll do

  • Build and ship agentic AI systems into production
  • Design backend architectures and scalable services
  • Work across agent orchestration, tool use, evaluation and reliability
  • Integrate LLMs into robust software systems
  • Make decisions around data, state, permissions, observability and testing
  • Experiment quickly and own problems from idea through to deployment

What we're looking for

  • Strong software engineering fundamentals
  • Several years' experience building production software
  • Experience building LLM-powered or agentic systems
  • Strong programming ability in Python, Rust, C++, Java or similar
  • Good understanding of system design and architecture
  • Experience with tools/frameworks such as LangGraph, MCP, Google ADK or similar
  • Pragmatic, high-agency approach to solving difficult problems

We're much more interested in how you think and what you've built than whether you've used a particular framework.

What you'll get

  • Competitive salary + meaningful equity
  • 7% pension, private healthcare & dental
  • Relocation support
  • High autonomy and genuine ownership
  • Work alongside a highly technical engineering team
  • The chance to build agentic systems from the ground up
