The Role Mode.Inc is looking for a Senior Backend Engineer with strong Go expertise to join the team taking ownership of our newly acquired VPN product and the additional apps we will be acquiring.

You will help take technical ownership of an existing production VPN following its acquisition by Mode.Inc. Your initial focus will be understanding and stabilizing the existing backend and Go networking engine, closing technical and security gaps, and establishing the engineering foundations needed to evolve the product confidently to a consumer VPN with 10M+ installs, 1,000+ servers in 45+ countries, and users in some of the most restrictive network environments in the world. That engine, a Go library cross-compiled for Android and iOS that embeds a userspace TCP/IP stack and drives the Shadowsocks and WireGuard clients, is the core of the product, and you will be the engineer who understands it, hardens it, and extends it.

We are a user-facing product-led team, and we are looking for someone who is passionate about building private, secure, and resilient systems that people depend on every day.

As an engineer, you'll be accountable for taking over, stabilizing, and evolving an existing backend: nimbly experimenting with solutions, building high-quality software, and measuring results. You'll own the account, subscription, and server-selection APIs, the WireGuard peer and configuration pipeline, and the integrations with payments, analytics, and attribution providers. You'll establish automated testing, API documentation, and CI/CD where they don't exist yet, close the security gaps identified during due diligence, and use agentic AI coding tools as a core part of your daily workflow. You'll work closely with the mobile frontend engineer, DevOps/network engineer, product managers, operations, and several stakeholders to define and execute according to our strategic roadmap to accelerate our future growth.
