About The Role And Team OpsEng team is part of bsport's Platform organization, alongside SRE. We build and connect the internal tools, SDKs and automations that remove friction, simplify customer data migrations, and help every team at bsport work better and faster.

If SRE builds the house, OpsEng equips it giving each team the right custom tools to do their job well.

🎯 What Will Be Your Impact

  • Own the architecture and delivery of client onboarding and data migration pipelines.
  • Build and scale the application deployment pipeline for bsport's branded mobile apps.
  • Design and maintain internal tooling and shared libraries — Django admin actions, feature flags, reusable SDKs — that other engineering teams depend on to move faster.
  • Drive technical decisions with an eye toward scalability, maintainability, and cross-team impact; your work will be felt across the whole engineering org, not just your squad.
  • Raise the bar on internal developer experience: code quality, testing practices, and CI/CD pipelines.

🌟You would be a great fit if

  • You have +5 years substantial experience with Python.
  • You've worked with Django at a meaningful level.
  • You have hands-on experience with GitLab CI/CD, Kubernetes, and AWS.
  • Experience designing internal developer tooling or platform-layer libraries.
  • You communicate well, in English, with both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • You want to lead across boundaries, and scale your impact to the organization.
