Description

Kevel is transforming the retail media space with its cutting-edge, AI-driven ad tech infrastructure APIs that power the Retail Media Cloud™. This groundbreaking solution combines the power of AI insights with API-based technology, allowing multi-brand retailers to build dynamic, customizable ad platforms while maintaining full control of their first-party data. With Kevel, retailers can deliver personalized shopper experiences, optimize ad targeting, and unlock predictive insights to stay ahead in an ever-evolving market.

JOB OVERVIEW : Kevel Console is Kevel's omnichannel advertiser self-serve platform. It lets media owners, especially retail and commerce media networks, hand campaign management and reporting directly to their advertisers, across onsite and offsite channels such as Meta, Google and Adform. As a Senior Fullstack Engineer on the Console team, you'll help modernize the systems behind the product, improve its reliability and robustness, and build the new features that win and grow customers. Learn more about Console: https://dev.kevel.com/docs/introduction-to-kevel-console .
