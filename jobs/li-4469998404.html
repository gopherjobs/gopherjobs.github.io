Description

Responsibilities Responsibilities & Qualifications

  • Involved in the analysis of unstructured and semi-structured data, including latent semantic indexing (LSI), entity identification and tagging, complex event processing (CEP), and the application of analysis algorithms on distributed, clustered, and cloud-based high-performance infrastructures.
  • Exercises creativity in applying non-traditional approaches to large-scale analysis of unstructured data in support of high-value use cases visualized through multi-dimensional interfaces.
  • Handle processing and index requests against high-volume collections of data and high-velocity data streams.
  • Has the ability to make discoveries in the world of big data.
  • Requires strong technical and computational skills - engineering, physics, mathematics, coupled with the ability to code design, develop, and deploy sophisticated applications using advanced unstructured and semi-structured data analysis techniques and utilizing high-performance computing environments.
  • Has the ability to utilize advance tools and computational skills to interpret, connect, predict and make discoveries in complex data and deliver recommendations for business and analytic decisions.
  • Experience with software development, either an open-source enterprise software development stack (Java/Linux/Ruby/Python) or a Windows development stack (.NET, C#, C++).
  • Experience with data transport and transformation APIs and technologies such as JSON, XML, XSLT, JDBC, SOAP and REST.
  • Experience with Cloud-based data analysis tools including Hadoop and Mahout, Acumulo , Hive, Impala, Pig, and similar.
  • Perform Data Manager - Senior duties for the DIA CIO Infrastructure Services program in accordance with approved technical, security, quality, configuration, and change-control processes.
  • Coordinate assigned activities with Government representatives, program leadership, engineering, operations, cybersecurity, service owners, and other mission partners.
  • Maintain accurate technical, operational, and status documentation; identify risks, issues, dependencies, and corrective actions affecting assigned work.

Required Qualifications

  • U.S. citizenship and an active TS/SCI clearance are required . A polygraph will be required for the position, and the employee must obtain and maintain all customer-mandated system and facility access.
  • Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution in an area applicable to the position plus at least 6 years of relevant experience.
  • OR Qualifying education and experience combinations: HS/GED plus 10 years; associate plus 8 years; bachelor plus 6 years; master plus 4 years; PhD plus 2 years.
  • Demonstrated senior-level proficiency performing Data Manager - Senior responsibilities in classified or mission-critical information technology environments.
  • Considered a recognized authority within the company.
  • Works on unusually complex technical problems and provides solutions which are highly innovative.
  • Strong written and oral communication skills and the ability to work independently and as part of an integrated technical team.
  • No legacy DoD 8570 baseline is identified for this position. If privileged-access or cybersecurity duties are assigned, the employee must satisfy the Government-designated DoD 8140 work-role qualification before performing those duties.

Desired Qualifications

  • One or more of the following certifications are desired: DAMA CDMP, Security+, ITIL 4 Foundation.
  • Current training and hands-on experience with the tools, platforms, and mission processes used by the assigned service lane are preferred.

Overview We are seeking a Data Manager - Senior to join our team supporting the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) CIO Infrastructure Services (CIS) contract in the National Capital Region (NCR). The DIA CIO Infrastructure Services program provides secure enterprise infrastructure engineering, operations, and technology-refresh support across classified environments. This senior Data / Storage position performs engineering escalation and change-window support and supports reliable, secure, and mission-focused delivery in coordination with Government leads, program leadership, service lanes, and technical teams.
