Description

Job Description Do you enjoy collaborating with teams to solve complex challenges? Do you have a passion for cutting edge technologies? Be part of our SRE team, improving reliability and scalability! Our mission is to support, maintain, and scale our Managed Kubernetes products.

Partner with the best You will work to ensure uninterrupted operations from the beginning to the end of a software's life cycle for Linode Kubernetes engine ( LKE ) & other Akamai Compute Cloud native offerings.

You Will Be Responsible For

  • Ensuring end-to-end reliability, scalability, and performance of Managed Kubernetes offerings while developing, maintaining, automating, troubleshooting, and proactively resolving production incidents.
  • Automating processes, ensuring stability, monitoring releases, deploying successfully, collaborating across teams, benchmarking performance, and shaping product and service evolution.
  • Performing proactive measures such as capacity planning, performance tuning and implementing infrastructure as code.
  • Collaborating with development teams to design and deploy new features and services that align with organizational reliability and performance goals.
  • Building software tools and systems to automate analytical tasks and workflows to increase efficiency and reliability.
  • Maximizing reliability, security, and performance by prioritizing Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and adopting a proactive engineering approach.
  • Helping to identify areas for new technology investments.

Do What You Love To be successful in this role you will:

  • Have extensive industrial experience and bachelor's degree in computer science or related engineering field
  • Obtain certifications like Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) or Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD) to enhance professional credibility.
  • Possess experience with Internet protocols (DNS/HTTP/TLS/TCP)
  • Demonstrate experience with coding in one or more of the following languages (python, golang, SQL)
  • Have experience with configuration management tools such as Ansible, terraform, and Salt.
  • Have experience troubleshooting Unix & Linux issues.
