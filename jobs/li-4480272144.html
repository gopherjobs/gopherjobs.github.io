What You’ll Do

  • Design, implement, and maintain cloud infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code
  • Support the platform architecture across private networking, containers, edge and CDN services, managed storage, IAM, and security boundaries
  • Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines, including federated identity, access controls, and separation between read and write operations
  • Implement platform observability, monitoring, alerting, and operational dashboards
  • Establish secrets-management and rotation processes
  • Develop disaster-recovery, resilience, and business-continuity capabilities
  • Build and maintain serverless functions and infrastructure automation using Python
  • Establish security guardrails and operational standards for a multi-tenant development platform
  • Collaborate with AI engineering teams to integrate agent capabilities and automated deployment workflows
  • Support ephemeral cloud environments used for AI-powered application development
  • Improve production readiness through documented, reviewable, and reversible infrastructure changes
  • Troubleshoot complex platform issues and conduct root-cause analysis
  • Take end-to-end ownership of platform stability, reliability, and operational performance
  • Identify opportunities to reduce deployment friction and improve the developer experience
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of platform architecture, tooling, security, and operations

What You Bring

  • Experience designing, implementing, and operating cloud-native infrastructure within a public cloud environment
  • Hands-on experience using Infrastructure as Code in production environments
  • Experience with Go or Go-based infrastructure tooling
  • Understanding of cloud networking, private networks, firewalls, containers, edge and CDN services, managed storage, and IAM
  • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, preferably using GitHub Actions
  • Knowledge of federated identity, authorization policies, least-privilege access, and secrets management
  • Experience implementing observability, monitoring, alerting, and disaster-recovery strategies
  • Proficiency in Python for infrastructure automation, serverless functions, or supporting services
  • Experience supporting multi-environment architectures and production-grade platforms
  • Understanding of platform security, resilience, scalability, and operational reliability
  • Strong troubleshooting and root-cause analysis skills
  • Strong written communication and technical documentation skills
  • Professional English proficiency
  • Ability to work effectively in ambiguous environments, make sound technical decisions, and take ownership of outcomes

Nice to have

  • Experience operating multi-tenant platforms for internal enterprise users
  • Knowledge of cloud-security guardrails and software supply-chain security
  • Experience with on-call operations, incident management, and production reliability
  • Familiarity with AI-powered development platforms
  • Exposure to agent-based engineering and deployment workflows
  • Experience designing infrastructure to reduce blast radius and isolate failures
  • Knowledge of controlled deployment, rollback, and change-management practices
  • Experience supporting developer platforms or internal platform-engineering products
