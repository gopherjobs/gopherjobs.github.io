Freelance BE heavy Full-stack engineer

For the ML Platform Experience team of a global client, we are looking for a freelance (ZZP) full-stack engineer.

Stack: Python / Java / Scala / JS / TS. Affinity with traditional Machine Learning or Generative AI is very welcome.

About the Team

The ML Platform Experience team is an important part of our internal AI, ML Platform. The team owns integrational points for this whole platform and our customers, including:

  • AI/ML Registry with built-in Governance and Compliance capabilities
  • AI/ML Observability pipelines
  • Platform UI/UX

Role Description

The role of a Software Engineer II in the ML Platform Experience team is backend role with occasional frontend tasks and it will require enough understanding of AI and ML builders needs from the get go.

You will:

  • Support and develop AI Registry with flexible asset schemas
  • Support and develop AI Observability pipelines
  • Ensure frontend developers have all relevant information through these backend endpoints

Key Job Responsibilities and Duties

  • Design, build, and maintain AI, ML Platform Registry and Observability pipelines to improve platform integration, simplify governance, and automate compliance.
  • Design and maintain low-latency services that guarantee performance even when traffic scales to hundreds of thousands of requests per second.
  • Maintain critical system uptime, troubleshoot effectively, profile production applications for bottlenecks, and lead incident management.
  • Take initiative to address the larger technical needs of the product you work on and always keep the customer at the center of everything you do.
  • Drive successful technical and business-related initiatives that improve productivity, performance, and quality.

Role Qualifications and Requirements

Professional Experience

  • Professional experience 3+ years as a Software Engineer designing, building, and operating production services and distributed systems.
  • Proven ability to contribute to technically complex projects with cross-team dependencies and meaningful business impact.

Programming & Software Engineering

  • Solid skills in server-side programming, must have: Python and Java, Scala is a pre with good understanding of concurrent programming and low-latency systems.
  • JavaScript/TypeScript is a strong plus.
  • Ability to understand business context, explain complex technical concepts clearly, and make pragmatic technical and architectural decisions.

Performance & Reliability

  • Knowledge of performance optimization, including profiling, load testing, and tuning services to manage hardware (CPU/GPU/memory) constraints.
  • Proven experience implementing robust observability (metrics, logging, and alerting) and maintaining scheduled workloads (batch predictions, maintenance, etc.) in a production cloud environment.

Cloud Infrastructure & Platform Engineering

  • Hands-on experience with cloud-native infrastructure components, particularly container orchestration (Kubernetes/Docker), Infrastructure as Code, and managing critical cloud primitives in a Linux-based environment.
  • Experience with platform-level governance and access control with a good understanding of quotas, tenancy, permissions, and safe enterprise access patterns for shared AI infrastructure.

AI/ML & MLOps

  • Expertise in modern MLOps practices including model versioning, automated CI/CD, and advanced deployment strategies (e.g., shadow, canary, traffic shifting) for high-reliability services is a strong plus.
  • Previous experience as an engineer in an ML/AI infrastructure team is a strong plus.

Compliance & Governance

  • Experience working with and implementing Regulatory Compliance requirements (e.g. such as GDPR, DMA, SOx, and the EU AI Act) is a strong plus.

Communication & Collaboration

  • Strong communication and collaboration skills to effectively partner with AI, ML practitioners and engineers, supporting the development of platform standards and tooling improvements.
