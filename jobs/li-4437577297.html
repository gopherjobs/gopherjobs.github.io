What you will do: The Role's Key Responsibilities and Tasks

As an eDOer, you will have clear objectives, great challenges and a clear overview of how your work contributes to the global company project and its customers. As an Engineering Manager in the Customer eXperience vertical you will be in charge of:

  • Working with top-notch tech stack including Java 17, BigData (Kafka, BigTable, Redis, PostgreSQL), LLMs, Datamesh, Docker, Kubernetes, Google Cloud, Sonarqube and Datadog
  • Collaborating with Product, Business, Architecture, Data Science to shape the technical strategy and evolution of the Payments domain, define the technical strategy for your modules and ensure their optimal performance
  • Helping your team translate functional specifications into high-performance logical technical design and code and split the work into smaller tasks that add quick value to customers
  • Promoting our standards of technical excellence, innovation and continuous improvement
  • Leading a high performing cross functional agile team of 7-10 experienced software engineers and developers
  • Taking an active role in finding and hiring the best talent available, in collaboration with our recruiters, and onboard new joiners into our culture of high performance, collaboration and growth
  • Managing performance, setting goals and prioritising work for your team, balancing the capacity of the team
  • Establish a sense of identity, engagement and belonging in your team, and improve team dynamics - in collaboration with your dedicated agile coach
  • Act as a mentor, helping others develop their skills and professional career
