Job Title: Software Engineer

Location: Central London | 5 days a week in office

Pivotal Partners is exclusively partnered with a fast-growing cybersecurity company building innovative cloud security technology to help businesses detect and respond to threats before they cause damage.

Following a recent $30M Series A, we're looking to grow our engineering team with product-minded engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges and taking ownership from start to finish. You'll join a small, high-performing team working on some of the most challenging problems in cloud security.

What you'll do:

  • Build and develop core product features across cloud security, threat detection and automated attack response.
  • Take ownership of projects end to end, from initial ideas through to development and delivery.
  • Work closely with the founders, engineers and customers to shape product direction.
  • Solve complex technical challenges, including detecting threats from AI agents and identifying attacks before they happen.
  • Help develop new capabilities as we expand beyond threat detection into automated security response.

Requirements:

  • Around 3-4+ years of software engineering experience, with scope for more senior candidates.
  • Strong backend engineering experience; open to Python, Ruby, Node or other languages.
  • Product-minded approach, with experience owning projects and delivering solutions end to end.
  • Background in technically demanding environments such as cybersecurity, fintech, developer tooling, gaming or cloud infrastructure.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-ownership environment and collaborating closely with customers.
  • Based in London or willing to relocate , with a commitment to working in the office five days a week.
