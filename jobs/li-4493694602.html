About The Team Join the team behind Asset Management , a relatively new, greenfield product at Paylocity designed to help organizations manage IT assets throughout the employee lifecycle - from assigning equipment during onboarding to managing, purchasing, and recovering assets. The team is made up of experienced senior engineers and owns the product end to end, with a strong focus on quality, thoughtful technical design, and a “you build it, you run it” mindset.

The product is now entering its next stage of growth, with a focus on expanding the Asset Management ecosystem through integrations with external device-management and IT service platforms. As a Senior Software Engineer, you’ll work alongside experienced engineers to shape these new capabilities, contribute to technical decisions, and take ownership across the full delivery lifecycle, from solution design and implementation through deployment and production. This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys building rather than simply maintaining, wants meaningful technical ownership, and is excited to influence how a growing product evolves.

Position Overview This entry-level position is designed for recent graduates or early-career developers looking to launch their technical journey. Core Responsibilities

  • Write clean, documented code under guidance from senior engineers

  • Follow team coding standards and best practices
  • Participate actively in code reviews and technical discussions
  • Document solutions and decisions clearly
  • Collaborate effectively within cross-functional teams

Team Collaboration

  • Work closely with experienced engineers for guidance
  • Ask clarifying questions to ensure understanding
  • Participate in team meetings and planning sessions
  • Share knowledge and learn from peers
  • Adapt to feedback and improve continuously
  • Follow established team processes and workflows

Growth & Development

  • Receive dedicated mentoring from senior engineers
  • Participate in regular code reviews and feedback sessions
  • Leverage training resources and workshops and then demonstrate applied learnings

Education And Experience

Technical Skills
