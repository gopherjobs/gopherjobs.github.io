Senior Technical Architect | LLM Guidance Service | £600/day inside IR35 | Remote

Most government architecture seats are a replatform with the answer already decided.

This one is a working LLM proof of concept that has to become a real service, and the architecture for that hasn't been drawn yet. If you've taken a proof of concept through governance and into a build phase, this is your seat.

THE ROLE

A central government department is building an LLM solution that improves the speed, access and accuracy of guidance for its operational staff. The proof of concept exists. The job is the future state architecture, the approvals that let it be built, and a design that holds up once real caseworkers are relying on the answer.

Two seats, SFIA 5, and it's remote with ad hoc travel.

THE SPECIALISM — this is the seat

  • Designing future state architecture and taking it through governance approvals

  • MongoDB, and it's essential rather than desirable
  • Presenting architecture design to technical governance forums
  • Technical authoring, particularly design and specification documents
