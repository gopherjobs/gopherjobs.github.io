General Summary Qualcomm, a leading technology innovator, pushes the boundaries of possibility to enable next-generation experiences and drive digital transformation, creating a smarter, connected future for all.

As a Qualcomm Build System Engineer , you will design, develop and operate services and methods used for cutting edge software development.

We are seeking additional engineers working with enabling software development using methodologies such as the build system Bazel and the build engine Buildbarn. Today, we have a state-of-the-art Bazel-based build system, and a mono-repository based integration flow. We build ~10Gb of source code and execute tests, including on target hardware, in under 10 minutes for every commit. Your job is to further improve upon this state including diagnose and optimization of performance bottlenecks, introducing new testing strategies and frameworks to further increase confidence, and investigate health metrics and other ways to gain insight into build system optimizations.

Your work includes architecting the build system for products at the magnitude of several hundred to a thousand developers. You will not only operate services such as Zuul and Buildbarn running on for example Kubernetes on prem and AWS infrastructure. You will also push, adapt and impact those products in the open-source community. You will be doing this in a team, using DevOps principles.
