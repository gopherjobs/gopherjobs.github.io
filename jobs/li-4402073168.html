Job Description As a Senior/Staff Backend Engineer, you'll own critical parts of the platform that banks and fintechs use to make real-time fraud, credit, and compliance decisions. You'll join a small, senior team where your technical decisions have direct impact.

What you'll do

  • Own features end to end: turn an idea or customer problem into a design, build it, ship it safely (GitHub Actions, feature flags with LaunchDarkly), and keep it running well in production.
  • Build and scale the Java services at the core of our decisioning platform, where latency and reliability directly affect our customers.
  • Work on real-time streaming and data systems (Kafka, Flink, ClickHouse, Databricks) handling high event volumes with tight latency requirements.
  • Build reliable processes with workflow orchestration (e.g. Temporal).
  • Make technical decisions, not just implement them: propose architecture, weigh trade-offs, and write design docs.
  • Work directly with product, frontend, and customer-facing teams to understand the problem before writing code.
  • Own production: monitoring, performance tuning, and fixing root causes, not symptoms.
  • Raise the engineering bar through code reviews, knowledge sharing, and helping others grow.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years of backend development, with deep expertise in Java and its ecosystem.
  • A track record of taking work from initial concept to production with minimal hand-holding. You're comfortable with ambiguity and know when to ask questions and when to make a call.
  • Solid experience building and operating low-latency, high-scale distributed systems in production.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS, microservices, and containers (Docker, Kubernetes).
  • Experience with event streaming and data systems such as Kafka, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, and Redis.
  • Good understanding of API design and security best practices.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills. We work async a lot, and Claude is part of how we work every day, connected to Slack, Notion, Linear, GitHub, etc.

Nice To Have (not Required)

  • Experience with Flink, Temporal, or Databricks.
  • Experience in fintech, fraud prevention, risk, or payments.
  • A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
  • Experience using AI coding agents (e.g. Claude Code) in day-to-day development.
