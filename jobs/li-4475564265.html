Etraveli Group is a leading global flight technology provider, specializing in flight sales and offering flight content delivery and fintech products. We are here to solve complexity, by connecting millions of flights and travelers across the globe, from search and selection to trip and beyond.

Our B2B portfolio includes Precision, our specialist in travel fintech; Tripstack, our B2B Flights as a Service Provider and world leader in virtual interlining, serving as the gateway to Etraveli Group’s flight content and fulfillment services; Wenrix, the AI Infrastructure for profitable growth in air; and Flygresor.se, Sweden’s leading flight comparison site. The Role

As Etraveli Group rapidly expands its agentic AI ecosystem, we are looking for a hands-on engineer to serve as the core technical muscle for our AI Optimization team. In this role, you will rapidly build, test, and evaluate agentic MVPs using Python, APIs, and LLM frameworks. You will design rigorous evaluation pipelines to prove accuracy, latency, and ROI, ensuring all agents align with company principles. Once validated, you will act as the key technical stakeholder translating prototypes into clean specifications for our core Technology team to scale.

Responsibilities Rapid Prototyping & MVP Engineering

  • Build functional end-to-end agents using Python, LLM frameworks, APIs, and databases.
  • Spin up infrastructure (vector/relational databases, docker containers) for real-world
  • testing.
  • Select architectural patterns (API wrappers, lightweight RAG, function calling).
  • Iterate based on business feedback and discard when concepts prove unviable.

Data Rigor & Technical Evaluation

  • Design evaluation pipelines to benchmark accuracy, hallucination rates, latency, and cost.
  • Build ground-truth labeled evaluation sets with business owners to validate production
  • readiness.
  • Analyze discrepancies between agent outputs and human decisions during pilots.
  • Support business stakeholders in taking responsibility for prompts and business logic
  • Continuously test new AI models and tools against internal benchmarks.

Feasibility & Technical Discovery

  • Assess operational workflows with strategy teams to determine agentic feasibility and
  • scope difficulty.
  • Evaluate new agent architectures and AI tooling to recommend core platform adoption.

Handoff & Platform Stakeholder

  • Serve as the primary technical stakeholder to the core technology AI platform team.
  • Translate validated MVPs into clear technical specs, architecture diagrams, and data
  • flows.
  • Partner with AI platform team to ensure seamless absorption of MVP logic into internal
  • SDKs.

Requirements Must Have

  • 3+ years software engineering experience with strong Python and rapid prototyping skills.
  • Hands-on experience with LLM orchestration (tool use, RAG, structured outputs via
  • LangChain, LlamaIndex, LiteLLM, or APIs).
  • Proficiency in API design, SQL, and relational/vector databases.
  • Experience using evaluation frameworks (Ragas, DeepEval, LangSmith) to benchmark
  • performance.
  • Agile, pragmatic mindset for fast experimentation outside traditional sprint cycles.

Ability to convert complex business logic into concise specs for engineering teams.

Nice to Have

  • Background as a Forward-Deployed Engineer, Solutions Architect, or similar fast-delivery
  • role.
  • Familiarity with modern deployment stacks (FastAPI, Docker, serverless).
  • Domain experience in travel tech, airline distribution, or payments.
  • Basic product management understanding (ROI tracking, KPIs, user validation).
