Remote: No • Relocation: Yes

Description

Job Responsibilities

  • Define and drive the overall technical architecture and engineering direction for software systems.
  • Work closely with engineering and business stakeholders to translate requirements into practical technical solutions.
  • Provide technical guidance throughout the development lifecycle and ensure engineering best practices are followed.
  • Design scalable and maintainable systems with a strong focus on performance, security, reliability, and scalability .
  • Support the adoption of modern architecture approaches, including Microservices, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), and Clean Architecture .
  • Oversee system integration, deployment practices, monitoring, and technical issue resolution.
  • Identify technical risks and recommend appropriate solutions or improvements.
  • Conduct technical reviews and support continuous improvement of development standards.
  • Mentor engineers and provide technical guidance to the development team.

Requirements

  • 3–5 years of experience in a Technical Architect, Solution Architect, Technical Lead, or similar role.
  • Strong hands-on software development background across both backend and frontend technologies.
  • Solid experience with Node.js/NestJS and React/Next.js .
  • Good understanding of modern software architecture and distributed systems.
  • Hands-on experience with cloud-based environments , preferably GCP.
  • Familiarity with Kubernetes, containerized environments, and modern deployment practices .
  • Good knowledge of relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL ; exposure to NoSQL databases is a plus.
  • Strong understanding of system performance, security, scalability, and reliability.
  • Good troubleshooting and problem-solving skills in complex technical environments.
  • Previous experience in mentoring or providing technical guidance to engineering teams.
  • Good English communication skills , with the ability to collaborate effectively with different stakeholders.

  • Premium healthcare insurance for employees and eligible dependents.

  • Lunch support, drinks, and snacks at the office.
  • Annual health check-up program.

  • Training and professional development sponsorship.
  • Collaborative and international working environment with opportunities to work on modern technologies and large-scale systems.
