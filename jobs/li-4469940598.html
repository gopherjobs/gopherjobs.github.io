  • 5+ years of experience with C# and .NET Core (preferably version 6)
  • 4+ years working with SQL Server, including stored procedures and performance tuning
  • 3+ years of experience with Azure cloud services (Logic Apps, Functions, Web Apps, or similar)
  • Demonstrated autonomy, proactivity, and ownership
  • 3+ years of experience using Azure DevOps for task management or pipelines (strong experience with deployment pipelines, CI/CD, and Git workflows)
  • 2+ years of involvement in solution design, architecture discussions, or technical decision-making
  • Experience debugging production issues and ensuring system stability
  • Experience building applications from scratch
  • 3+ years of experience working with legacy codebases

Nice to Have
