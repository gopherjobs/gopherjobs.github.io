WHAT YOU WOULD ACTUALLY BE BUILDING

Our client is building the AI system of action for UK lettings and estate agencies. It sits on top of the agency's CRM and runs the operational work: prospecting, viewings, maintenance, compliance and arrears.

A lot of that work runs through their voice agent, which picks up the phone when the branch can't. Every call it handles has to turn into real action in the agency's systems, reliably, across every agency on the platform.

The platform is built to run agents, not just respond to them. That is the part you would own.

WHY THE ARCHITECTURE LOOKS LIKE IT DOES

A voice agent handling live calls needs backend systems that respond in real time and don't drop work. That is why this is event driven microservices, deployed from a monorepo onto Kubernetes on Google Cloud.

The product runs on agentic pipelines. You will know from experience what breaks in those at scale, and how to build systems that hold up when they do.
