Description

The Opportunity

Surevine builds secure, scalable, collaboration solutions for the most security conscious organisations, enabling them to work together on their most highly sensitive information.

We’re looking for a Junior Software Engineer early in their career who wants to grow quickly by working on real production systems. You’ll be paired with an experienced engineer from day one, and your development will be overseen by our Head of Engineering, who’s passionate about helping people grow early in their careers.

We hire software engineers who are adaptable, curious, and committed to creating excellent engineering solutions, both independently and as part of a team. You don’t need to know everything yet; we care as much about how you learn as what you already know.

Engineering in Surevine

You’ll get hands-on experience across the software lifecycle; cloud engineering, test-driven development, AI-assisted development, experimentation, prototyping and automation. We don’t hire engineering zealots or rigid process followers. We look for adaptable people who want to learn how to weigh up the context and choose the right tools and approaches for the job.

We actively use AI tools to accelerate our development, improve code quality and solve complex problems, and we’ll support you to use them as well. Alongside that, we’ll help you to build strong engineering fundamentals: we expect you to understand, question and be able to explain any code an AI tool writes for you.

We want you to have (or be keen to learn) an understanding of how engineering decisions affect security. Most importantly, we want you to be honest about what you know and what you don’t know and to know how to work with your “don’t know”. Asking for help early is a strength here, not a weakness.

We’re a distributed company and always have been, so you'll communicate and collaborate through video calls, chat and written documentation. We value clear communication and welcome different communication styles and preferences.

We look at problems from all angles, but we don’t sit around staring at them. Sometimes the best way to learn is to build an MVP and put it in the hands of a real person.

The tools we use

We don’t expect you to know most of these. You’ll work with some of them in your first weeks and pick up others over time, with support from the team. What matters more is that you’re curious and are open-minded about using the best technology for them problem, in front of you.

  • Typescript, React, Next.js
  • AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, ECS, GCP
  • GitLab CI/CD, Pulumi/Terraform
  • Python, Langchain, AI/ML integration
  • Postgres, MongoDB, Redis, Neo4j, Elastic
  • Java, Spring Frameworks
  • Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor or other LLM-powered development assistants

What you will be doing

We recognise that engineers have different working styles and strengths. Below are the key parts of the role and we'll work with you to find approaches that play to your strengths while achieving our shared goals.

  • Build and ship features in our live production systems, with design guidance from senior engineers, growing into contributing design decisions as your experience develops
  • Take part in code reviews, both having your code reviewed and reviewing others’, contribute to documentation and knowledge sharing across the team
  • Consider the user at every step; from understanding the need through to designing and delivering the solution
  • Work alongside experienced colleagues on systems in live operation, including helping to support them when things go wrong
  • Experiment with AI-powered development tools and practices, and share what you learn with the team
  • Bring fresh eyes to how we work. We want your questions and ideas about how the team delivers
  • Develop your skills through pairing, mentoring, structured learning and time for experimentation. We encourage trying new approaches while also valuing depth and mastery

About you

We value diverse thinking styles and backgrounds. You don't need to match every point below. If you meet most of the essentials and you’re excited by the role – we’d encourage you to apply.

Essential

  • A degree in Computer Science or a related subject, or equivalent practical experience
  • Some professional software development experience, typically 1-3 years (placements and internships count)
  • Working knowledge of at least one language we use, such as JavaScript/TypeScript, Python or Java
  • An interest in security and awareness of secure coding basics, such as common vulnerabilities and how to avoid them
  • Clear written and verbal communication, and comfort working in a remote team

Nice to have

  • Experience with a cloud platform (AWS or GCP) and containers (Docker)
  • Exposure to CI/CD pipelines or infrastructure as code
  • Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code, Copilot or Cursor
  • Personal projects, open source contributions or anything else you have built because you wanted to

Working at Surevine

We're committed to building an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives and working styles strengthen our team. We:

  • Make reasonable adjustments throughout the application process and your employment
  • Support different communication and collaboration preferences
  • Offer flexible working arrangements
  • Value both collaborative work and focused individual contribution time
  • Provide clear expectations, structured onboarding, and ongoing mentorship
  • Give you protected learning time and a learning budget
  • Bring the Engineering team together in person bi-monthly (at the least)

If you need any accommodations during the application process or have questions about how we work, please let us know.
