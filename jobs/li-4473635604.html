Job Description

As a Senior Software Developer with Java, Angular and DevOps skills, you will be responsible for designing, implementing and maintaining back-end, front-end and DevOps solutions for a wide range of business challenges. This includes technologies such as Java, Spring ecosystem, PostgreSQL, Angular and TypeScript, Docker, Kubernetes. You will work closely with our product owner and cross-functional teams to understand their needs. Your expertise will help us improve our products and services, optimise the customer experience and increase operational efficiency.

Responsibilities and Duties:

  • You will create software solution designs and take care of their documentation.
  • You will define standards for system testing/test automation and take care of the installation and configuration of software for complex products and services.
  • You will support team members during error analysis through to error correction and also provide technical support for other teams for the products and services you support.
  • You will work closely with other teams in an international environment and have the opportunity to lead projects of strategic relevance.

Qualifications

Must-have requirements:

  • Very experienced with current Java 21+ and Maven (6+ years)
  • Very experienced with the whole Spring ecosystem (Spring Boot, Spring Data, Spring Batch, Spring Security, Hibernate and JPA) (6+ years)
  • Very experienced with Angular and TypeScript (6+ years)
  • Experience with relational Databases (PostgreSQL)
  • Experience with version control (GitLab, GitLab Actions)
  • Experience with DevOps cycle (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Experience with Test Driven Development, Unit and Integration Tests
