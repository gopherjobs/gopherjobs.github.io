Summary

At Remote Crew, we're partnering with a global sleep health company looking for a Lead Software Engineer to join its growing Porto based engineering team. You'll split your time between shaping technical architecture and writing production code inside a Scrum team. This is an individual contributor role for someone who wants real influence over how products get built, not just a seat in design meetings.

Salary: up to €85k / year

Location: Porto, Portugal.

What You'll Do

  • Turn Product Requirements Documents into clear Technical Design Documents
  • Define architecture, assess feasibility, and estimate effort for new features
  • Design scalable, secure microservices running on AWS
  • Embed inside one of four Scrum teams as an active developer
  • Write production code in Java and Kotlin, with occasional Angular work
  • Help your team break architecture plans into user stories and sprint tasks
  • Review code and mentor developers to raise the technical bar
  • Partner directly with Product Managers to evaluate trade offs before building
  • Work with relational (PostgreSQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB) databases
  • Support the team's move away from legacy MongoDB toward PostgreSQL

What We're Looking For

  • Strong hands on experience building scalable microservices in Java
  • Comfort learning Kotlin on the job if you haven't used it yet
  • Proven experience with distributed systems and service boundary decisions
  • Working knowledge of PostgreSQL and MongoDB
  • A track record of writing technical design documents or RFCs
  • Experience working in a Scrum or Agile environment
  • Ability to explain technical trade offs to non technical stakeholders
  • Comfort mentoring developers through code review
  • Some exposure to frontend frameworks, ideally Angular
  • You must live within about an hour of Porto

Nice to Have

  • Experience with AWS and Kubernetes
  • CI/CD pipeline experience
  • Background in healthcare tech, regulated environments, or SaaS
  • Familiarity with sleep diagnostics or medical devices

Why Work With Us

  • Your work directly improves how millions of people sleep and live
  • The company is growing fast, around 80% in sales this year, creating new architecture and leadership roles regularly
  • No mandatory office days. Fully remote, with the team coming together only occasionally
  • You'll split your time evenly between architecture and hands on coding, not just handing off designs to others
  • Clear career paths are being built for both individual contributors and managers

If this sounds like your next move, we'd love to hear from you.
