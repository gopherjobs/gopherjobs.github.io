Recruitment Background

In recent years, the data services market has expanded rapidly with a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of approximately 18.15%, driving an increasingly high demand for Data Engineers in the AI era.

In this growth domain, the company is redefining data infrastructure from a software perspective and promoting the "software productization of data infrastructure." Consequently, the design skills, engineering capabilities, and quality-driven mindset cultivated as a Software Engineer are critical to advancing data engineering.

In this recruitment, the company seeks individuals who can leverage their experience in "design, quality, and development operations" gained as a Software Engineer to build and operate data platforms and pipelines.
