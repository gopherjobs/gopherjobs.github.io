Description

Introduction:

We're hiring an Engineering Team Lead / Tech Lead (m/f/d) in Munich (hybrid, 3 days on-site / 2 days remote) to own the architecture of Peter Park's event-driven, cloud-native SaaS platform — a product used by millions of drivers across Europe.

You are the highest technical authority in your squad: a hands-on architect who sets and defends the technical direction while staying close enough to the codebase to maintain deep credibility. Whether you're coming from a Senior/Staff Engineer track or an Engineering Manager background, we care about impact — not titles.

Your Objectives & Role:

  • Orchestrate Architecture: Lead architectural discussions and guide your squad through complex trade-offs. Own the scalability, reliability, and cost-efficiency of our distributed, event-driven infrastructure on AWS.
  • Lead by Example: Contribute production-grade code, tackle core features, and run peer code reviews (MRs) that elevate the engineering standard.
  • Develop Engineers: Mentor and grow software engineers through continuous feedback, psychological safety, and clear career progression. Shield your team from operational noise so they can do their best work.
  • Bridge Strategy & Execution: Translate business goals into actionable technical objectives. Partner closely with Product Managers to balance feature delivery with technical debt reduction.
  • Drive Ownership: Foster an entrepreneurial mindset. Help engineers understand the "why" behind architectural decisions and keep customer impact front of mind.

What You Bring:

  • Hands-On Leadership: You're an established Engineering Manager, Staff Engineer, or Senior Engineer ready to own a team. Disciplinary management experience is a plus, not a requirement.
  • Distributed Systems Experience: You've designed, scaled, and owned distributed, event-driven systems in a SaaS or IoT environment. You confidently defend trade-offs around consistency, throughput, and cost.
  • Technical Depth: Fluency in Node.js / TypeScript (our primary backend stack) or our IoT stack (C++, Python) . You understand AWS cloud architectures well enough to review a senior engineer's work and push it further.
  • Agile Fluency: Proficiency with tools like Linear or Jira. You use data to drive team velocity and release frequency.
  • People-First Mindset: High emotional intelligence, proactive communication, and the ability to motivate a diverse team with kindness and clarity.
  • Stakeholder Communication: Comfortable navigating from engineer-level technical detail to C-level strategic conversations.
  • Language: Excellent English (C1+) is mandatory. German is a strong plus.
