Forward Deployed Engineer | AI-native vertical SaaS | London (in person) | £150K base + meaningful equity | Visa sponsorship + relocation

I'm hiring two Forward Deployed Engineers for a seed-stage AI company in London that is automating a roughly $300bn industry that still runs largely by hand.

The company

They build AI-native software for Testing, Inspection & Certification (TIC). TIC is how products and systems get checked against safety and regulatory standards. Their customers are certification bodies, notified bodies and inspection companies. These firms still work across Word files, PDFs, spreadsheets and email. The product covers scheduling, technical review, conformity assessment, field inspection and document intelligence.

  • Live in production at some of the largest enterprises in the industry
  • Customer demand is ahead of what the team can build, which is why these roles exist
  • Their seed round was heavily oversubscribed and co-led by top-tier European and US VCs
  • Most of the engineering team are olympiad medallists in maths, physics or informatics

The role

This is hands-on, customer-facing engineering. You'll sit with customers, learn how their inspection and certification work actually gets done, and turn it into production software. You own delivery from the first workflow conversation to shipped code. You'll work alongside the commercial team and a Deployment Strategist on pitches and rollouts.

What you'll do

  • Work directly with technical and non-technical stakeholders on real workflows
  • Turn messy, real-world processes into clear, working software
  • Ship end to end with React/TypeScript frontends and Python backends
  • Build LLMs, agents and model-backed APIs into customer workflows

What they're looking for

  • An exceptional engineering background, ideally from a top tech company, AI lab, trading firm or fast-scaling startup (a big logo helps but isn't required)
  • Strong, production-grade TypeScript and React (or an equivalent framework)
  • Comfortable building backend services in Python
  • Experience shipping AI features into production products
  • Able to reason about complex workflows, well beyond CRUD apps
  • Personable, and good at making complex things easy for domain experts to understand

Strong fits include FDEs or solutions engineers at AI-native companies, early engineers at vertical SaaS startups, and engineers from labs or trading firms who want more ownership and more time with customers.

Why it's worth a look

  • A huge, under-served market where AI makes a real difference
  • You own the whole loop, from customer discovery to shipped product
  • Applied AI in regulated workflows, not demos
  • Close work with the commercial team, a good route into technical leadership or founding roles
  • Full visa sponsorship and relocation from anywhere, with flights covered
