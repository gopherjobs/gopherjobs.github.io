Help us shape the future of health technology! At Top Doctors Group , we work to improve the way people access healthcare. Through technology, we connect patients, healthcare professionals, and clinics to provide a more accessible, efficient, and human experience.

Today, we operate in 10 countries and develop digital solutions that help thousands of healthcare professionals grow their activity and millions of people find the care they need.

💡 Why is this role exciting? Because you will have a direct impact on the development of a complex, cloud-native platform supporting digital health and social care.

iWantGreatCare , part of Top Doctors Group, is a platform that combines Node.js and TypeScript microservices , specialized background workers, event-driven workflows, and applications built with Vue.js and Nuxt.js . It runs on Google Cloud Platform , using technologies such as Pub/Sub, Docker, Kubernetes, MongoDB, BigQuery, ETLs, and modern CI/CD practices.

As a Fullstack Developer , you will design, build, and evolve scalable backend services, distributed workflows, and user-facing applications. You will work closely with Engineering, Product, Design, Operations, Integrations, and DevOps to deliver reliable solutions with a meaningful real-world impact.

⚙️ About The Platform Our platform has a distributed, cloud-native architecture composed of:

  • Microservices and workers: Node.js and TypeScript services, with specialized background workers communicating through GCP Pub/Sub queues and REST APIs.
  • Cloud infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform, Docker, Kubernetes, and managed services supporting scalability and resilience.
  • Data: MongoDB, BigQuery, ETL processes, and integrations with other products and systems.
  • Frontend applications: Administrative and user-facing applications developed with Vue.js and Nuxt.js.
  • DevOps and observability: CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, monitoring, logging, and alerting.

  • Conduct detailed code reviews and help maintain high standards of code quality.
  • Share knowledge and contribute to the continuous improvement of the team’s engineering practices.
