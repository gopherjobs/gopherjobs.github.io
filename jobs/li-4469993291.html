Description

Who We Are We’re on a mission to transform how fleets operate, using cutting-edge camera technology, AI, machine learning and telematics to make roads safer and businesses smarter.

As an award-winning SaaS company in a high-growth phase, we’re scaling fast and expanding into new markets worldwide. Our technology gives fleet operators real-time visibility, reduces risk, improves efficiency, and helps set new safety standards across the industry.

Join a global, ambitious team and be part of what's next!

What You'll Do As a QA Engineer, you will help ensure the quality, reliability and usability of our SaaS platform across web, mobile and API services.

Responsibilities:

  • Analyse requirements to develop manual and automation tests for web apps, mobile apps and APIs.
  • Work closely with developers and product teams to understand features, identify risks and build quality into the development process.
  • Develop and maintain reliable automated test suites for functional, regression and end-to-end testing.
  • Integrate and execute automated tests within a CI/CD environment.
  • Perform manual and automated testing on new releases to ensure they meet defined quality standards.
  • Investigate test failures and defects and provide clear, meaningful bug reports.
  • Continuously improve test coverage, automation and QA processes.

Essential What we’re looking for

  • Bachelor’s degree in software engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • 3+ years’ experience in software QA engineering, testing web and mobile applications across multiple platforms and devices.
  • Strong demonstrable test automation experience using frameworks such as Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, Appium or other similar tools.
  • Experience with API and integration testing, including REST APIs, JSON and authentication.
  • Strong understanding of functional, integration, system, regression and exploratory testing.
  • Experience of working in an agile/scrum development environment.
  • Proficiency in QA and software development tools including Jira, Git, Xray and Postman.
  • Experience with at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, JavaScript/TypeScript or Java.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Strong attention to detail, with a disciplined and methodical approach to testing.
  • Strong organisational skills and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • A proactive mindset that is always looking to drive innovation and efficiencies in the QA process

Advantageous

  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure.
  • Familiarity with QA/test processes for embedded devices
  • Performance/load testing experience using JMeter or similar tools.

Why join CameraMatics?

  • Competitive salary and benefits
  • Hybrid working model: 2 days onsite (Dublin or Waterford Office) and 3 days remote
  • Opportunity to be creative and play a key role in the success of a high growth start-up
  • A collaborative, ambitious team that moves fast and takes quality seriously
  • Opportunity to grow with the company as we expand across international markets
