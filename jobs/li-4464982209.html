Role Overview:

The client seeks an Engineering Expert to provide specialized technical guidance for system architecture decisions, troubleshooting complex issues, and optimizing performance across distributed systems. The role requires deep expertise in multiple engineering domains to support critical infrastructure and product development initiatives.

Key Responsibilities:

Required Skills & Qualifications:

• Experience with distributed systems, microservices, or large-scale backend infrastructure.

• Proficiency in at least one programming language such as Python, Java, Go, or Rust.

• Strong problem-solving skills with a track record of resolving complex technical challenges.

• Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).

• Ability to write clear technical documentation and communicate architectural decisions effectively.

The position provides exposure to cutting-edge technologies and the opportunity to shape technical strategy for high-impact projects.
