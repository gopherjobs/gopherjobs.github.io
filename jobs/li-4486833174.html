Role Overview

As a Senior Product Engineer, you will lead the technical development of proprietary AI products designed for major financial institutions.

You will be responsible for translating complex and sometimes abstract business requirements into technical architecture and product functionality, rapidly moving from concept and prototyping to production implementation.

A major focus of the position is building robust commercial systems capable of handling high-load and real-time processing, while ensuring product quality, scalability, reliability, and user experience.

Rather than limiting AI initiatives to experimentation or PoCs, you will help integrate AI and Generative AI into production systems that deliver measurable business value and improve customer operations.
