About The Role We are looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer to help build and deliver workflow software for the college admissions application process. Our product supports students, families, counselors, and admissions consultants across key workflows including school selection, Common App material preparation and submission tracking, essay review, application milestone management, and client-facing service operations.

This role sits at the intersection of software engineering, product thinking, and customer engagement. You will work directly with users to understand their real needs, translate ambiguous workflow problems into technical solutions, and build reliable product experiences that improve the admissions journey.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain software systems that support college admissions workflows, including school research, application planning, Common App process management, essay review, and document tracking.
  • Work directly with customers and internal service teams to understand their pain points, operational processes, and underlying business needs.
  • Translate user requirements into product features, workflow improvements, internal tools, and scalable technical solutions.
  • Rapidly prototype, validate, and iterate on product ideas based on real user feedback.
  • Collaborate with product, design, operations, and customer-facing teams to define priorities and deliver high-impact solutions.
  • Own projects end to end, from problem discovery and technical design to implementation, deployment, and post-launch iteration.
  • Identify opportunities to automate repetitive workflows, improve service quality, and create a smoother application experience for users.
