Key Responsibilities:

  • Design, develop, and maintain cloud services connecting IoT devices with business systems.
  • Build and enhance integrations across products, applications, and platforms.
  • Develop APIs and data exchange solutions.
  • Integrate data from chargers, BMS, controllers, and other connected devices.
  • Design scalable data flow architectures in cloud and microservices environments.
  • Collaborate with firmware, hardware, and product teams.
  • Support telemetry, fleet management, and analytics solutions.
  • Troubleshoot integration and data communication issues.

**The ideal candidate for this position will have

:**

  • 3+ years of experience as a Cloud Developer, Backend Developer, or Software Engineer.
  • Experience building cloud-native applications.
  • Strong knowledge of REST APIs and system integrations.
  • Hands-on experience with microservices architecture.
  • Proficiency in Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud Platform.
  • Experience with SQL and/or NoSQL databases.
  • Knowledge of real-time data processing systems.
  • Good English communication skills.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with IoT projects.
  • Knowledge of industrial communication protocols.
  • Experience with MQTT, WebSockets, or event-driven architectures.
  • Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Experience in industrial, automotive, energy, or telematics sectors.
  • Basic knowledge of BLE, Bluetooth, USB, or device communication.
  • Experience with telemetry and fleet management platforms.
