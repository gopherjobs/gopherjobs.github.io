Why This Role Matters The Engineering team at Vonage is a group of talented engineers working on complex challenges associated with high-performance distributed systems and processing large volumes of data in real time.

We are looking for a Staff Software Engineer to join our Fraud Prevention engineering team. As a senior technical leader, you will act as a technical anchor for the platform, shaping the architecture and evolution of systems that process millions of events in real time to protect Vonage customers from fraud.

This role combines deep hands-on engineering with technical leadership. You will drive architectural decisions, guide the technical direction of the team, mentor engineers, and help scale both our platform and engineering practices as the business continues to grow.

We are a team that embraces the future: we have a strong and growing adoption of AI across our engineering workflows, and we actively leverage AI agents in our day-to-day work - from accelerating development cycles to enhancing fraud detection intelligence. If you are excited about working in an environment where AI is not a buzzword but a real part of how we build and operate, you will feel right at home.

What You Will Do

  • Provide technical leadership and long-term architectural vision for Vonage's Fraud Prevention Platform.
  • Lead the design and evolution of high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems processing millions of fraud detection events in real time.
  • Lead performance optimisation initiatives and resolve the most complex system-level challenges.
  • Shape the long-term technical roadmap for the Fraud Prevention Platform, balancing business priorities, technical debt, scalability, and operational excellence.
  • Drive and own significant architectural decisions, ensuring scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability across the platform.

  • Define and evolve coding standards, testing strategies, observability practices, and operational excellence across the organisation.
  • Guide the team through technical planning, solution design, and delivery of strategic initiatives.
  • Take part in on-calls for our production services.
