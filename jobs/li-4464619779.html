Description

Our energy client is looking for a Senior Application Developer to support the development of enterprise-scale automation platforms used to manage the lifecycle of network infrastructure across both IT and Operational Technology (OT) environments. TCJP00022624

Initial contract goes until Oct 1, 2027 with chances of extension. 3 days/week onsite in Calgary.

Description This role will focus on building tools that support firmware upgrades, configuration management, and operational workflows for technologies such as Cisco routers and switches, Versa SD-WAN appliances, and cellular modem platforms.

The successful candidate will work closely with experienced Network Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) and leverage AI-assisted development practices to deliver high-quality, scalable solutions that improve reliability, security, and operational efficiency.

Manage very complex or multiple projects and is considered a SME and has the ability to influence others. Oversees projects and ensures alignment with business goals. Leads a work group or project team. Develop and document software based on a defined set of design and performance requirements Adhere to processes defined by the Technical Lead and Solution Architect Provide estimates of work effort Proactively provide ideas to improve proposed solutions Actively participate in application design, database design, schema layout and data loading Actively participate in code walkthroughs/inspections as per the software development process and Quality Management Plan Prepare sample data and unit test plans Troubleshoot and corrects complex bugs or issues (including performance issues) Develop and execute unit and integration tests as per the project plan Assist in the set up and execution of demonstrations as required Align all work effort with the project schedule/work plan

Key Accountabilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain automation solutions supporting firmware upgrades and network lifecycle management
  • Collaborate with network SMEs to translate operational processes into reliable, repeatable automation workflows
  • Develop end-to-end automation including pre-upgrade validation, execution, verification, and rollback mechanisms
  • Build user-facing tools and APIs to support operator-driven workflows
  • Implement audit, logging, and error-handling capabilities to support operational transparency and compliance
  • Integrate automation solutions with enterprise platforms such as monitoring, inventory, and service management systems
  • Leverage modern development practices, including AI-assisted tooling, to accelerate delivery
  • Contribute to reusable frameworks and automation patterns

Must Have

  • 5+ years of professional software development experience
  • Strong proficiency in Typescript, UI Frameworks (ie: React), Python
  • Strong proficiency in AWS Cloud Services, CI/CD and Cloudformation or other infrastructure as code platforms.
  • Experience developing automation tools, backend services, or workflow-based systems
  • Experience working with APIs, scripting, and system integrations

Preferred

  • Experience in infrastructure, network, or systems environments
  • Familiarity with automation or orchestration frameworks
  • Experience working in operational or regulated environments
  • Exposure to AI-assisted development tools or methodologies
