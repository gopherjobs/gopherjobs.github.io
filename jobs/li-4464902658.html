Senior Backend Engineer (Go) – Auth0 at BairesDev

In this role, you'll build Go backend services that integrate deeply with Auth0, handling authentication and authorization flows that keep systems secure at scale. Working with enterprise SSO and OIDC token flows, you'll design solutions where identity is central, not an afterthought. This is your opportunity to apply strong Go backend expertise to identity infrastructure, building services that other systems and teams depend on to establish trust.

What You'll Do

  • Build and maintain Go backend services integrating with Auth0.
  • Implement enterprise SSO integrations using SAML.
  • Handle OIDC authentication and token flows.
  • Design PostgreSQL relational data models and schemas.

What We Are Looking For

  • 5+ years of experience in backend software engineering.
  • Strong proficiency in Go.
  • Hands-on experience with Auth0.
  • Experience with enterprise SSO integration using SAML.
  • Background with OIDC authentication and token flows.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL relational data modeling and schema design.
  • Upper-intermediate English proficiency.

Nice To Have

  • Experience migrating a legacy authorization model to an externalized auth system.
  • Familiarity with SCIM user provisioning.
