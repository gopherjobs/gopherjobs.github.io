MuleSoft Engineer at BairesDev

As a MuleSoft Engineer, you will design and implement robust integration solutions using the Anypoint Platform to drive API-led connectivity. You will act as a vital link between disparate systems and business requirements, ensuring seamless data flow and architectural consistency across the enterprise ecosystem.

What You'll Do

  • Architect and develop scalable APIs and integration flows using MuleSoft Anypoint Platform.
  • Create sophisticated data mapping and transformation logic utilizing DataWeave.
  • Deploy and manage integration applications within CloudHub environments to ensure high availability.
  • Implement reusable integration patterns and best practices for REST and SOAP services.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to define technical specifications and connectivity strategies.
  • Maintain the health and performance of the integration layer through proactive monitoring and optimization.

What We Are Looking For

  • 4+ years of experience in Integration Development, Software Engineering, or Middleware.
  • Proven expertise in designing and developing integration solutions using MuleSoft Anypoint Platform.
  • Proficiency in DataWeave for complex data transformations and API-led connectivity design.
  • Hands-on experience with CloudHub or CloudHub 2.0 for deploying and managing Mule applications.
  • Strong understanding of integration patterns and REST/SOAP web services.
  • Advanced proficiency in English.
