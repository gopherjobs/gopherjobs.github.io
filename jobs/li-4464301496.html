🚀 Technical Lead (TypeScript & Python)

We’re looking for a Technical Lead to take ownership of the transition from an early-stage platform to a robust, production-ready environment.

You’ll be a key technical decision-maker, driving the backend architecture, production readiness, AI workload, and engineering standards while working closely with the domain team.

🛠️ What you’ll do

  • Lead the transition to a robust production platform , owning the architecture and key technical decisions.
  • Make the final backend technology decision across TypeScript and Python , and drive its implementation.
  • Build production infrastructure from the ground up, including CI/CD, staging environments, PostgreSQL, deployments and cloud infrastructure .
  • Own the generation platform , including prompt discipline, provider abstraction and cost management.
  • Lead the technical approach to AI/LLM workloads , ensuring reliability, scalability and maintainability.
  • Establish rigorous standards for AI-assisted development, engineering practices and code reviews .
  • Partner closely with the domain team, bringing strong technical judgement, critical thinking and pragmatic decision-making.

🎯 What we’re looking for

  • 8+ years of experience building and shipping web services.
  • 2+ years of experience leading engineering teams or acting as a technical lead.
  • Strong production experience with TypeScript and Node.js .
  • Solid experience with PostgreSQL .
  • Python and FastAPI experience is a strong plus.
  • Proven experience shipping LLM-powered features , including structured outputs, retries and schema validation.
  • Experience building CI/CD pipelines and cloud deployments from scratch .
  • Strong hands-on experience with AWS .
  • Excellent communication, critical thinking and technical decision-making skills.
  • Experience with eLearning platforms is a big plus.
