💻 Backend Developer (TypeScript and Node.js)

We’re looking for a Backend Developer to help expand our core platform, support the full product catalogue, and build new backend services for the domain team.

You’ll work on core backend functionality, AI-powered services, asynchronous processing and production data infrastructure, contributing to a platform designed to scale reliably.

🛠️ What you’ll do

  • Implement template contracts and core generation services .
  • Build and ship a complete audio pipeline for text-to-speech assets .
  • Design and manage asynchronous workloads, queues and background jobs .
  • Own PostgreSQL data models , ensuring they are scalable, reliable and well structured.
  • Handle production database migrations safely and efficiently.
  • Integrate and maintain LLM-powered backend services , focusing on reliability and structured data.
  • Collaborate closely with the domain team and other engineers to turn product requirements into robust technical solutions.
  • Apply design thinking principles to solve problems pragmatically and build maintainable systems.

🎯 What we’re looking for

  • 4+ years of backend development experience.
  • Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript and Node.js .
  • Experience with Python and FastAPI is a strong plus.
  • Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL and relational data modelling , including experience with JSONB .
  • Proven experience integrating LLM APIs , including schema validation and token usage/cost logging.
  • Solid understanding of asynchronous processing, queues and idempotent jobs .
  • Experience working with AWS cloud infrastructure .
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a pragmatic approach to software design.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.

🌟 Nice to have

  • Experience working with eLearning platforms .
  • Experience building AI-powered products or content generation systems .
  • Experience working with text-to-speech or audio processing pipelines .

If you enjoy building scalable backend systems, working with modern AI technologies and solving complex engineering problems , we’d love to hear from you.
