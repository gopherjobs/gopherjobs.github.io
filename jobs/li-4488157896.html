10,000 transactions a second, answered in around 50ms. Few engineers get to work at that kind of scale, and fewer still get to own a real piece of it.

This AI company is valued at more than $10bn, has 500+ people and is growing fast. Its products have genuine real-world impact, and the platform behind them is scaling quickly. They’re hiring Senior Software Engineers to build and own the backend services that keep it fast and reliable.

Why you’ll want this one

  • Real ownership. You’ll take services from design to production and own how they run.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build and scale high-throughput, low-latency backend services, mainly in Go

  • Track down bottlenecks and make systems faster, leaner and more resilient
  • Make key technical decisions within your team and see them through to production
  • Improve observability, testing and reliability across the services you own
  • Mentor other engineers and raise the bar through code reviews and design discussions

What you’ll bring

  • 5+ years’ backend software engineering experience
  • Strong experience building distributed systems in production
  • Hands-on experience with Kafka, Google Pub/Sub, RabbitMQ or similar
  • Strong coding skills in Go, or in Rust or Java
  • A solid understanding of concurrency, performance and system design
  • A track record owning services in high-volume production environments

The package

  • Base salary up to £140,000

  • Permanent right to work in the UK required, as visa sponsorship isn’t available
