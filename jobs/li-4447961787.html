Description

Looking for

An engineer who would rather solve the last mile than avoid it. You will embed with bank engineering and operations teams to get Triple's AI agents live inside their infrastructure.

About Triple

Triple is building the intelligence layer for financial services. From our roots in merchant transaction data, we’ve expanded to solve one of the industry's biggest challenges: dispute and fraud management. We are deploying AI agents to fully automate these workflows for top-tier card issuers.

With $10M in funding and a global footprint spanning over 60 banks and fintechs (including Airwallex, Qonto, N26, and Trade Republic), we are a fast-moving, experienced team. Join us to help shape the future of banking operations.

What you will do

  • Own the technical delivery of AI agent deployments at banks, from first scoping conversation to stable production
  • Build the integration layer for each client, whether the deployment is API-based, in a private cloud, or on-premise
  • Work directly with bank engineering, security, and operations teams
  • Turn every client-specific solution into a reusable component, so the next deployment is faster than the last
  • Tune agent behavior to each client's operational reality, then measure whether it reduced their workload
  • Carry what you learn in the field back to product and engineering, because you will see the gaps first

What you need

  • 4+ years of experience as a software engineer, forward deployed engineer, or solutions engineer
  • English proficiency required
  • Strong TypeScript and SQL, and comfort working across the stack when a deployment needs it
  • Experience integrating with enterprise systems in financial services, payments, or another regulated industry
  • Comfort with different deployment models, including API-based, private cloud, and on-premise
  • Comfort in front of clients: you can run a technical working session with a bank's engineers
  • A pragmatic instinct for what to build now, and what to make reusable
  • Ownership mindset: you define the solution as much as you build it, and you do not wait to be unblocked
  • Understanding of payments, disputes, or chargeback workflows is a strong plus, and genuine curiosity about them is required

Conditions

100% remote. Competitive salary, and equity. We are an AI-native company: every engineer here works with coding agents daily, and you will have the AI tools you need without usage limits. We build client-led, which means you will sit close to the banks using what you ship. Travel to client sites as needed. The chance to shape how the world's largest banks run operations with AI.

Join Triple and help us build the intelligence platform for banking operations.
