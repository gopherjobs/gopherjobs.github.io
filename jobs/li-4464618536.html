WHAT CHALLENGE WILL YOU BE TAKING ON? You will join a systems development team in the aeronautical sector (air navigation). You will be involved in all phases of the project’s development (DevOps): requirements definition, system design, software/hardware development, validation, commissioning and maintenance. The team has experience in similar development projects, which will facilitate your learning and the transfer of knowledge.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Defining system requirements and software architecture.
  • Implementing the system (languages: Java, C++ and Python) following CI/CD processes (tools: GitLab, Jenkins,
  • Robot Framework) and generating releases (Docker).
  • Deployment: Harbour.
  • Preparing and updating the necessary documentation.

WHAT DO WE NEED IN OUR TEAM? For this role, we are looking for computer science graduates with knowledge of:

  • C++ or Java
  • Python
  • Linux
