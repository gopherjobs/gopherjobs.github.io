As a Staff Fullstack Engineer, you will lead the design, build, and testing of SentinelOne's next-generation security orchestration and automation (SOAR) solution, a brand-new product offering built to extend the value of our cybersecurity platform. You will bring deep front-end and back-end expertise to drive this product from technical design through delivery, integrating it with our existing platform and collaborating closely with product, security, and other engineering teams. This is an opportunity to leverage modern technologies and help revolutionize how we defend against threats.

What Will You Do?

Primary responsibilities include:

  • Lead the design, development, and testing of SentinelOne's next generation security orchestration and automation (SOAR) tools, taking features from technical design through delivery with minimal guidance.
  • Integrate the SOAR solution with our existing product offering to extend the value of SentinelOne's cybersecurity platform, working through moderately complex technical challenges independently.
  • Collaborate closely with cross functional teams, including product, security, and other engineering teams, acting as a technical resource for the SOAR product area.
  • Work across the full stack, including React, TypeScript, Python (FastAPI with SQLModel), and NodeJS for the front end and services layer, Codemirror and Reactflow for the product's workflow building experience, and PostgreSQL, Docker, Kubernetes, Redis, AWS, and GCP for the underlying infrastructure.

What skills and knowledge should you bring?

Ideal candidates will have:

  • 6 or more years of professional full stack or front end engineering experience
  • Extensive coding experience in TypeScript using React, with the depth to independently lead design and implementation of complex front end systems.
  • Previous coding experience building backend APIs in Python - advantage.
  • Ability to mentor and act as a technical resource for colleagues with less experience.
  • Experience with Codemirror or Reactflow is preferred.
