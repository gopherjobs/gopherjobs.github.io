About the Role

We are looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer to take ownership of the technical delivery, deployment, and production support of our Document AI and LLM-powered solutions , including Document Parse, Information Extract, and Solar LLMs.

This is a highly hands-on, customer-facing engineering role at the intersection of cloud engineering, software development, DevOps, AI, and technical delivery . You will work directly with customers to take solutions from proof of concept (POC) through production , ensuring deployments are reliable, integrations work as expected, and document-processing pipelines achieve the required accuracy.

You will collaborate closely with a small, senior U.S.-based delivery team and partner with Product and Engineering teams in Korea. Your work will have a direct impact on our ability to successfully deploy solutions, resolve complex customer challenges, and scale our customer base.

What You'll Do

  • Own customer deployments from POC to production. Provision environments, configure services, integrate APIs, and deploy solutions across SaaS, AWS, Azure, and containerized environments.
  • Troubleshoot complex issues end-to-end. Investigate problems across APIs, integrations, services, classification, extraction, OCR outputs, and processing pipelines. Resolve issues directly or isolate a clear root cause for Product and Engineering.
  • Optimize Document AI pipelines. Configure and continuously refine Document Parse and Information Extract workflows, including schema design and extraction configurations, to achieve customer accuracy requirements.
  • Operate and support production environments. Monitor, debug, patch, upgrade, and troubleshoot deployed systems while prioritizing availability, reliability, and customer experience.
  • Build for scale. Turn recurring deployment and troubleshooting patterns into reusable automation, templates, technical documentation, runbooks, and engineering practices.
  • Partner with customers and internal teams. Translate customer requirements and technical issues into actionable engineering solutions while communicating clearly throughout implementation and support.
  • Support cloud marketplace and partner initiatives. Assist with AWS/Azure Marketplace listings and technical partner enablement when needed.
  • Influence the product. Bring field insights back to Product and Engineering and help identify opportunities to improve deployment, usability, reliability, and scalability.

What We're Looking For

Must-Have Qualifications

  • 3–5 years of hands-on experience with AWS and/or Azure , including cloud infrastructure, deployment, and operations.
  • Strong understanding of APIs, integrations, and distributed/cloud-based applications .
  • High proficiency in at least one programming language. Python or TypeScript preferred; Go, Java, or C++ are also welcome.
  • Strong working knowledge of Linux, command-line tools, and Bash scripting .
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, Docker Compose, and container orchestration .
  • Proven ability to independently investigate and solve ambiguous, complex technical problems with minimal supervision.
  • Strong debugging and root-cause analysis skills across applications, infrastructure, integrations, and data-processing pipelines.
  • Clear written and verbal technical communication, with the confidence to work directly with customers during implementations and troubleshooting.
  • Strong AI fluency and practical experience using AI-assisted development tools to accelerate research, engineering, debugging, and delivery while maintaining full ownership of the quality and reliability of your work.
  • A continuous-learning mindset with an interest in experimenting with new technologies, tools, and engineering approaches.

Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Experience with LLMs, AI-powered workflows, AI agents, Document AI, OCR, or data-extraction pipelines .
  • Experience with DevOps, CI/CD, deployment automation, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) .
  • Experience with AWS Marketplace and/or Azure Marketplace listings or operations.
  • Experience monitoring, debugging, patching, and upgrading live production systems .
  • Experience translating customer or field feedback into meaningful product or engineering improvements .
  • Exposure to additional cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Oracle Cloud, or similar technologies .

Who Will Thrive in This Role

You are an engineer who enjoys getting close to both the technology and the customer problem . You are comfortable moving between writing code, deploying containers, inspecting logs, troubleshooting APIs, tuning AI extraction pipelines, and explaining technical findings to customers and engineering teams.

You don't need a perfectly defined problem before getting started. You can investigate unfamiliar systems, form hypotheses, test solutions, identify root causes, and drive issues through resolution.

Most importantly, you combine strong engineering fundamentals with curiosity about AI, cloud infrastructure, automation, and production systems —and you take ownership of getting solutions successfully into customers' hands.
