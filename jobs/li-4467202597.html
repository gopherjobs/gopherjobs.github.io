Job Description

  • Development and optimization of systems: Expanding backend and frontend architecture of portals based on large datasets.
  • Database management and tuning: Designing schemas, query optimization, and maintaining performance of PostgreSQL and MySQL databases with millions of records. SOLR/Elasticsearch (full-text, faceted search).
  • AI integration in daily work: Actively and effectively using AI tools to speed up code writing and automate tasks.
  • API / automation design: Creating secure, scalable, and efficient endpoints for external clients and internal integrations.
  • Ensuring code quality: Writing tests independently (unit/integration) and creating and updating technical documentation.

Desired Skills & Experience

  • Full-stack experience: Practical, project-backed knowledge of a chosen backend ecosystem (Python, Node.js, PHP) and a frontend framework (e.g., React, Vue) or plain, efficient JS.
  • Databases & Search at an Advanced level: knowledge of Postgres (indexing, query optimization) and experience with Elasticsearch.
  • AI-Driven Development: Ability to effectively use AI assistants to boost personal productivity (while maintaining critical thinking and verifying code).
  • Knowledge of best practices: Experience with containerization (Docker), writing automated tests, and documenting architecture.
  • High level of autonomy: Ability to map general business needs to concrete technical solutions without detailed specifications.
  • Product thinking: Understanding architectural consequences (performance, security, data pagination) for publicly or commercially available systems.
  • Communication skills: Ability to ask the business the right clarifying questions before starting to code.
