About the Role

We are looking for Senior Software Engineers with strong experience in Python or TypeScript/JavaScript/Golang and hands-on exposure to preference data collection and/or rubric creation .

This role is ideal for engineers who combine strong software engineering fundamentals with excellent judgment, attention to quality, and the ability to turn complex technical work into clear, actionable outcomes.

You will work on cutting-edge AI/LLM projects , supporting data generation, collection, evaluation, and engineering workflows.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, refine, and iterate on rubrics and evaluation criteria for preference data.
  • Review and label data while maintaining a high quality bar and identifying subtle issues.
  • Build and maintain pipelines and infrastructure supporting data generation, collection, and evaluation.
  • Analyze findings and communicate them through clear technical write-ups, updates, and recommendations.

Must-Have Technical Skills

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Strong proficiency in Python , particularly building and maintaining pipelines/infrastructure
  • OR strong experience with TypeScript / JavaScript / Golang .
  • Hands-on experience with preference data collection and/or rubric creation .
  • Experience working with large and complex codebases is highly relevant. Experience with codebases such as VSCode is a plus .
  • Strong understanding of software engineering practices, debugging, code quality, and scalable development workflows.
  • Ability to evaluate technical/data quality and clearly explain why something is good or needs improvement .
