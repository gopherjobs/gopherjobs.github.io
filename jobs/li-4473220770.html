🚀 We’re Hiring: AI Project Manager / Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

📍 Location: Japan

📅 Contract Duration: 1 Year

🔹 Project Management Responsibilities

  • Lead and manage end-to-end delivery of AI/ML projects from initiation to closure.
  • Define project scope, goals, milestones, timelines, and budgets in collaboration with stakeholders.
  • Develop and maintain detailed project plans, risk registers, and status reports.
  • Coordinate cross-functional teams including Data Scientists, AI Engineers, Developers, and Business Analysts.
  • Facilitate Agile/Scrum ceremonies including sprint planning, stand-ups, reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Identify, assess, and proactively mitigate project risks and dependencies.
  • Manage stakeholder communication and expectations across technical and business teams, including C-suite stakeholders.
  • Track and report KPIs, OKRs, project health metrics, and delivery progress using dashboards and reporting tools.
  • Ensure projects comply with governance, compliance, security, and data privacy standards.

🔹 Forward Deployed Engineer Responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary technical point of contact for clients during AI solution deployment.
  • Lead on-site and remote deployment of AI/ML models, platforms, and integrations.
  • Conduct technical discovery sessions and workshops with clients to gather requirements.
  • Oversee system integration, configuration, testing, and deployment within client environments.
  • Provide technical guidance and troubleshooting during and after deployment.
  • Develop technical documentation, user guides, training materials, and deployment playbooks.
  • Collaborate with Product and Engineering teams to communicate client feedback and drive product improvements.
  • Ensure smooth handover and knowledge transfer to client teams after deployment.
  • Support pre-sales activities including demos, Proofs of Concept (PoCs), and solution scoping.

🔹 What We’re Looking For

  • Experience in AI/ML project management, technical delivery, software engineering, solutions engineering, or a similar role .
  • Strong understanding of AI/ML solutions and software development lifecycles.
  • Experience working directly with clients and translating business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Strong project planning, stakeholder management, communication, and problem-solving skills.
  • Hands-on technical understanding of APIs, system integrations, cloud platforms, data pipelines, and AI/ML systems .
  • Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies .
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with business, product, engineering, and AI/ML teams.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, client-facing environment.
  • Willingness to work with clients and technical teams in Japan .
