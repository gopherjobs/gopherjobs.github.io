Key Responsibilities

  • Support Kubernetes and the services deployed on it, including Airflow, AI tools, and MCP servers.
  • Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines and GitHub setup.
  • Manage AWS infrastructure, including IAM roles, policies, and permissions across multiple accounts.
  • Implement and maintain secrets management.
  • Partner with engineers and analysts to productionize work and support reliable operation at scale.
  • Help shape infrastructure approaches for high-load and high-volume workloads.

Required Skills, Knowledge And Expertise

  • Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes and AWS.
  • Experience supporting services and deployment pipelines in a production environment.
  • Experience with infrastructure in a high-load or high-volume B2C environment, or a similarly demanding setting.
  • Experience managing distributed infrastructure across multiple cloud accounts is a plus.
  • Familiarity with Airflow, GitHub, CI/CD, and secrets management is valuable.
