Description

Senior Software Engineer (Full Stack, AI-native regtech)

Central London, hybrid (2 days p/w onsite)

What you'll be doing

The product is B2B compliance software. It takes messy, unstructured company data and turns it into regulatory reporting for financial services firms and large corporates in the UK, Europe and the US. AI agents do most of the collecting, checking and validating, and people handle the exceptions. Your job is to make that reliable.

  • AI ingestion of documents and public data, mapped to regulatory requirements
  • Growing the rules engine that sits behind the reporting
  • Data infrastructure and controls built to audit standard
  • Contributing to the product's AI agent and integrations

There's no tech lead, so the architecture calls are yours. You'll work in a small squad that takes on one big customer problem at a time, and you'll spend real time with customers.

Where you'll be

In the London office two days a week and remote the rest. It's a small, flat team and you'll report into the leadership team. The business has patient backing and is heading towards profitability, so there's no runway clock ticking. As the team grows, there's a route into engineering management.

Required

  • Full-Stack Product Engineer who wants to shape the solution, not just build to a spec
  • A senior engineer who self-manages and owns features end to end
  • You'd rather shape the solution than build to a spec
  • Comfortable directing AI coding agents and reviewing what they produce
  • Happy in front of customers

Preferred

  • Full-stack TypeScript across React/Next.js, Node.js and AWS Lambda
  • Experience in data-heavy, compliance or enterprise software rather than consumer apps
