Your Profile & DNA:

We are looking for an experienced AI Platform Engineer , who will turn AI best practices into reusable platform services, SDKs, templates, developer journeys, architectural blueprints, and governance patterns . You will help ensure AI solutions are scalable, secure, compliant, and easy to adopt .

  • Define and maintain AI architecture standards and reference architectures .
  • Translate emerging AI patterns into enterprise-ready standards and reusable capabilities .
  • Build shared AI services covering security, access management, observability, and scalability .
  • Develop SDKs, templates, accelerators, and reference implementations for ML, GenAI, AI agents, and AI systems.
  • Improve the AI developer experience and drive adoption across domains.
  • Design cloud-native solutions , CI/CD pipelines, and platform automation.
  • Integrate AI services, AKS, and supporting platforms .
  • Collaborate with architecture, security, risk, data governance, and regulatory teams .

Qualification & Experience

  • A Bachelor's or Master's Degree or equivalent.
  • Strong Python software engineering skills, including design patterns, packaging, and coding best practices.
  • Strong Azure knowledge, including networking and IAM / Entra ID.
  • Experience with DevOps, CI/CD, automated testing, and deployment strategies .
  • Knowledge of Kubernetes reliability engineering , observability, scalability, and availability.
  • Experience with ML lifecycle, MLOps, LLMOps, Generative AI, AI Agents, and RAG .
  • Understanding of AI evaluation, monitoring, security, governance, and architecture .
  • Strong systems-thinking, communication, collaboration, and developer experience mindset .

Technology

Azure, Azure DevOps, Python, AKS/Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, Databricks/Unity Catalog, MLflow, Arize Phoenix, Grafana, Prometheus, Azure Monitor, Nexus, and Twistlock.
