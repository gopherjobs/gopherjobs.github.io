Description

Your next opportunity starts here.

At Aubay Portugal, we work daily with some of the largest organisations across sectors such as banking, insurance, telecommunications and energy, helping to build and evolve critical systems that support millions of users. We have been in Portugal since 2007 and are part of an international group with a presence in several European countries. More than technology, we build close relationships and deliver real-impact projects.

What will you do?

  • Develop and maintain robust software services and APIs using Python frameworks, preferably FastAPI, with a focus on data processing and deployment.
  • Design and manage secure cloud infrastructure from a software engineering perspective, handling core networking components like WAF and CDN.
  • Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform for deploying and orchestrating scalable services with Kubernetes (AKS and Flux) and Docker.
  • Build and maintain end-to-end data pipelines, ensuring efficient data flow across real-time (Kafka/Kinesis) and batch architectures.
  • Contribute to event-driven architectures by working with event emitters, transformations, and GitOps tooling, leveraging Kafka for producers and consumers.

What are we looking for?

At Aubay, our focus is on professionals who bring both technical expertise and the right mindset:

  • Proven professional experience of at least 5 years as a Senior Python Developer, specifically within data-focused roles and retail domain projects.
  • Expertise in Python development, including building web services with frameworks like FastAPI/ASGI, and strong experience with PostgreSQL.
  • Solid experience with cloud infrastructure engineering (Azure, AWS, GCP – particularly networking, WAF, CDN) and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform).
  • Hands-on experience with containerization and orchestration technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes (AKS and Flux).
  • Strong understanding of data engineering concepts, including data modeling methodologies, and practical experience with tools such as Databricks, Snowflake, Airflow, Kafka, or Kinesis.
  • Alignment with our corporate culture and a commitment to integrity and ethics are highly valued;
  • Strong customer focus, teamwork, task prioritisation, and daily commitment are highly appreciated.
  • Excellent communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills, with fluency in English.

What will you find here?

  • A close-knit and informal environment, where direct communication is preferred and people are valued;
