Description

Westwood Talents is hiring expert Software Engineering Evaluators for a remote, hourly contract role reviewing and assessing AI-generated work products for real-world quality.

Role Overview

Selected professionals will apply deep software engineering expertise to review AI-generated work products, including slides, spreadsheets, and documents, and grade them for real-world quality.

Key Responsibilities

  • Evaluate AI-generated artifacts for usability in a software engineering context.
  • Identify factual, aesthetic, and presentation errors.
  • Provide clear, structured written feedback.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of relevant professional experience in Software Engineering at a top firm in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand.
  • Native or professional fluency in English.
  • High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace (PowerPoint, Excel, Word).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Advanced degree (Master's or higher) from a reputable institution.

Location

Open to candidates with experience at firms in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.

Compensation and Engagement Terms

  • Rate: $100-$150 per hour.
  • Fully remote, flexible schedule.
  • Engagement as an independent contractor.
  • Payments issued weekly via Stripe or Wise, based on services rendered.
  • Project duration may be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on business needs and performance.
  • This role does not involve access to confidential or proprietary information from any employer, client, or institution.
  • Sponsorship for H-1B or STEM OPT candidates cannot be supported at this time.

Westwood Talents considers all qualified applicants without regard to legally protected characteristics and provides reasonable accommodations upon request.
