Description

Shine is the financial copilot for entrepreneurs and small business owners. Founded by serial entrepreneurs Rico Andersen and Martin Hegelund, Shine is a leading European fintech unicorn on a mission to restore the joy of running a business, by ending wasted time on financial admin. Shine offers a connected solution for invoicing, accounting, payroll, business accounts, payments, and financing, meaning business owners can focus their energy on growing a healthy business, not held back by manual admin.

Part of something bigger Today we're part of Cegid, a European leader in cloud software for finance and accounting. Together we're building Europe's leading financial copilot for small businesses and their accountants.

We're a multicultural team working from France, Germany, Denmark and the Netherlands, contributing to a wider European network that spans Spain, Portugal.

The ERP Engineering Team at Shine

We're a fast-growing engineering team on a mission to build the best ERP for Small Businesses in Europe. We work in a domain-based structure, where each domain has real ownership over its area — from architecture to quality and technical roadmap.

Your Role as a Domain Engineer As a Domain Engineer, you're the technical reference for the product: you define architecture direction, ensure engineering standards, and work closely with team managers and Senior Engineers to make sure technical decisions serve the business goals. This is a senior IC role, focused on cross-cutting technical impact, not people management.

We're modernizing our stack and expanding into new markets, which means real problems around scale, multi-tenancy, and domain complexity — not greenfield, but not paralyzing legacy either.

What You'll Do

  • Own the target architecture of your domain and the realistic path to get there.
  • Ensure reliability, scalability, security, and compliance are design constraints from day one, not afterthoughts.
  • Maintain living architecture documentation (C4 model diagrams, ADRs) as part of the engineering workflow.
  • Set and calibrate design review thresholds, delegating to senior engineers as their judgment grows.
  • Spend roughly half your time embedded with engineering teams, and half on cross-engineering work — standards, the Golden Path, and the Architecture Forum.
  • Grow the architectural judgment of senior engineers, so fewer decisions need to come through you over time.
  • Partner with Engineering Directors, Engineering Managers, and Product to surface technical constraints and trade-offs with clarity.

📍 Job located in Porto with possibility of two remote working days per week. About You

  • Scalable systems experience: You have significant hands-on experience designing and evolving systems at scale, ideally within multi-tenant SaaS or platform environments.
  • Architectural ownership: You take end-to-end ownership of architecture decisions. You ensure your reasoning is well-documented so others can easily understand the context and trade-offs later.
  • Influence without authority: You are comfortable operating with technical authority and guiding direction without needing formal line-management authority.
  • Cross-functional communication: You can translate complex technical trade-offs into clear, legible concepts for Product managers and business leadership.
  • Empowerment over centralization: You have a genuine interest in developing other engineers' judgment rather than centralizing decision-making around yourself.
  • Tech stack & domain experience: You are comfortable with .NET, Angular, SQL Server, Azure, and modern CI/CD practices. Experience with financial, accounting, or ERP systems—as well as European regulatory compliance—is a strong differentiator.
