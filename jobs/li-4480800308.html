About The Opportunity The Mid-Level AI Software Engineer is responsible for the autonomous development of medium-complexity features and solutions, contributing actively to technical decision-making and code quality. You will have the ability to integrate AI into product workflows with minimal supervision, possessing a deep understanding of the impact of your architectural choices. You will act as a key bridge between business requirements and technical implementation, collaborating closely with Data and AI Engineering teams to deliver robust, production-grade solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and deliver full-stack features of medium complexity autonomously.
  • Participate actively in architectural discussions and propose well-founded technical solutions.
  • Conduct code reviews, providing constructive feedback to peers and contributing to the team's overall technical growth.
  • Design, implement, and optimize integrations with LLM provider APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini).
  • Apply prompt engineering and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) techniques to product features.
  • Model and optimize relational and NoSQL databases for mid-scale solutions.
  • Implement CI/CD pipelines and contribute to the team’s DevOps culture.
  • Ensure high-quality deliverables through automated testing (unit, integration, and E2E).
  • Document technical decisions and implemented architectures.
  • Collaborate with Product, Data, and AI Engineering teams to define and execute solutions.
  • Identify and propose improvements to development processes and software quality.
  • Monitor applications in production, proactively identifying and resolving incidents.
  • Maintain the codebase with high standards, applying design principles with discernment regarding maintainability and scalability.

Preferred Qualifications & Experience

  • Solid experience in full-stack development within production-grade projects.
  • Proficiency in back-end programming languages (Python, Node.js, Java, Go, etc.) paired with TypeScript and modern front-end frameworks (React, Vue, or Angular).
  • Ability to write maintainable, testable code autonomously, justifying design decisions based on trade-offs involving readability, coupling, and maintenance costs.
  • Proven experience in refactoring and evolving legacy code safely, supported by robust test suites.
  • Experience integrating LLMs into real-world applications (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, etc.).
  • Experience with prompt engineering and RAG applied to product contexts.
  • Intermediate knowledge of relational and NoSQL database modeling and optimization.
  • Experience with Docker and basic container orchestration.
  • Experience with cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure) in a professional capacity.
  • Experience with CI/CD and deployment pipeline automation.
  • Intermediate/Advanced/Fluent English.

Nice to haves

  • Public cloud certification (AWS, Azure, GCP) at the Foundational/Associate level or equivalent.
  • Experience with Kubernetes.
  • Knowledge of MLOps and model lifecycle management.
  • Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, SQS, etc.).
  • Knowledge of security practices for AI applications (e.g., prompt injection prevention, guardrails).
  • Experience with monitoring and observability tools (Datadog, Grafana, etc.).
  • Degree in IT, Information Systems, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
