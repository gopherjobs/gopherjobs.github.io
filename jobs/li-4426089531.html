Description

Help us run and evolve a mission-critical SIP platform used by business customers every day. In this role, you ensure stability and availability while actively driving automation and modernization of our voice services. You’ll work in a DevOps setup with real ownership and impact — from operations to continuous improvement. What you can expect: -High-impact role on a critical VoIP platform (SIP Core / Ribbon) -Modern DevOps environment with automation and continuous improvement -Collaboration across network, architecture, and operations teams -Agile setup (SAFe) with real influence in PI Planning and execution -Room to shape and improve systems — not just operate them.

What impact will you have?

  • Operate and improve SIP-based telecommunication backend systems
  • Ensure high availability, stability, and performance of voice services
  • Monitor systems proactively and prevent incidents
  • Troubleshoot complex issues and perform root cause analysis
  • Test and deploy updates, fixes, and improvements
  • Drive automation, monitoring, and operational efficiency
  • Contribute to capacity planning and system health checks
  • Maintain and improve documentation and runbooks
  • Participate in on-call rotation (Engineer on Duty)

Must-haves

  • Experience operating SIP/VoIP platforms (ideally Ribbon SIP Core)
  • Strong knowledge of SIP, VoIP protocols, and secure transport (RTP, SRTP, TLS)
  • Solid networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, routing, switching, IPSec)
  • Experience with Linux systems and system operations
  • Hands-on mindset in troubleshooting and problem-solving
  • Good English skills (C1)

Nice-to-haves

  • Automation & DevOps tools (e.g., Jenkins, Git, Terraform, Ansible)
  • Scripting skills (Python, Bash)
  • Experience with Kubernetes / cloud environments
  • Familiarity with monitoring tools and ITSM / ITIL
  • Experience working in agile setups
  • German language skills
