Technical Product Manager, Series B, APIs/SDKs, London Hybrid

Series B indoor mapping and location platform. Live in 5,000+ venues across 35 countries, powering location for 25 million people. Customers include Fortune 100 companies, the US Department of Homeland Security, major US airlines and airports, and leading healthcare systems.

We're hiring a Senior Technical Product Manager to own the Location product. It's a machine learning–driven positioning and mapping platform used through APIs, SDKs and mobile apps. You'll work directly with the CEO/CPO, an engineer who built the original technology.

The package

  • £100k to £115k base plus performance bonus
  • Hybrid: 2 days a week in the Fulham, London office (Tuesdays and Thursdays)

What you'll do

  • Own the technical roadmap for the Location squad: positioning, mapping, navigation and geofencing
  • Work hand in hand with engineering on architecture trade-offs, API and SDK design, and delivery
  • Turn enterprise client feedback into clear requirements, user stories and mockups
  • Demo the platform and present to technical and executive stakeholders at Fortune 100 clients
  • Start as a senior IC, with a path to managing people as the team grows

Must have experience

  • 6 to 8 years as a Product Manager in B2B SaaS, with a strong technical focus
  • Hands-on ownership of APIs, SDKs, developer platforms or mobile products (iOS/Android)
  • An engineering or computer science background, or you can go deep with engineers on how things actually work
  • You've taken a product from 0 to 1 in a startup or scaleup
  • Confident with enterprise clients and C-level stakeholders
  • Bonus: experience with geospatial, mapping, indoor positioning, BLE, IoT or smart buildings

Must haves

  • Based in or near London, or willing to relocate, and able to be in the office Tuesdays and Thursdays
