micro1 is looking for QA Engineers with hands-on experience testing 2D design, animation, illustration, or other creative applications. You’ll use your QA and creative software expertise to evaluate real-world workflows and help improve the quality and reliability of AI systems.

Scope of Work

  1. Test 2D and creative applications using real-world workflows.

  2. Identify bugs, usability issues, inconsistencies, and unexpected behavior.

  3. Create and execute test cases for different features and workflows.

  4. Reproduce issues and document clear steps, expected results, and actual results.

  5. Test edge cases and more complex workflows used by designers, illustrators, or animators.

  6. Provide clear feedback on application functionality and user experience.

  7. Work with the team to improve testing quality and coverage.

  1. Professional experience in QA, software testing, or application testing.

  2. Hands-on experience testing 2D, design, animation, illustration, or other creative software.

  3. Familiarity with tools such as Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Sketch, After Effects, Animate, or similar creative applications.

  4. Experience with manual and functional testing, test cases, and bug reporting.

  5. Strong attention to detail and ability to identify visual and functional issues.

  6. Good problem-solving and analytical skills.

  7. Clear written and verbal communication skills.
