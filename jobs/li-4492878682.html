Description

About DwellFi DwellFi is a California-based startup building an AI-native platform that is transforming how private markets operate. We simplify and automate complex financial workflows across Private Equity, Venture Capital, Real Estate and Private Credit.

Our platform combines AI-powered document intelligence, intelligent agents, deterministic business rules and workflow orchestration to automate processes such as capital calls, distributions, NAV reconciliations, fund accounting and investor reporting.

As we scale, reliable, secure and cost-efficient infrastructure is critical to delivering an enterprise-grade platform to institutional customers.

What You'll Do As an AI Platform Engineer, you'll architect and build the systems that power our platform. You'll own the end-to-end development of scalable backend infrastructure and AI agents that analyze data, automate workflows, and generate insights for our users in Private Asset Management space.

Who You Are

  • You have a strong foundation in backend engineering, paired with a genuine passion for building AI-driven products.
  • You think like an entrepreneur, not just an individual contributor, and must own features end-to-end, from market insight and concept validation to development, deployment, and scaling it into revenue-generating products.
  • You are deeply curious about AI, consistently exploring emerging technologies and translating them into practical, high-impact features

Your Responsibilities

  • Build and scale FastAPI-based systems integrating AI agents with complex data workflows
  • Develop and orchestrate multi-agent systems leveraging LLMs
  • Manage PostgreSQL data systems with a focus on performance, integrity, and security
  • Implement async processing and scalable workflows (e.g., Celery)
  • Design robust prompt strategies and production-grade agent architectures
  • Write clean, testable, and well-documented Python code
  • Continuously experiment with and adopt cutting-edge AI tools and frameworks

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent hands-on experience)
  • 2+ years of backend development experience in Python (or strong experience in another language with the ability to ramp quickly)
  • Strong understanding of API design and SQL/PostgreSQL schema design
  • Experience with background job frameworks like Celery or similar tools
  • Hands-on experience with LLMs (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic) and frameworks like LangChain or RAG
  • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP)
  • Solid understanding of AI/ML or agent-based architectures
  • Strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills
