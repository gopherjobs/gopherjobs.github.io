Description

Multiverse Computing Multiverse is a well-funded, fast-growing deep-tech company founded in 2019. We are the largest quantum software company in the EU and have been recognized by CB Insights (2023 and 2025) as one of the 100 most promising AI companies in the world.

With 180+ employees and growing, our team is fully multicultural and international. We deliver hyper-efficient software for companies seeking a competitive edge through quantum computing and artificial intelligence.

Our products, including CompactifAI, Foundry and Sentinel AI, address critical needs across various industries:

  • CompactifAI is a groundbreaking compression tool for foundational AI models based on Tensor Networks. It enables the compression of large AI systems—such as language models—to make them significantly more efficient and portable.
  • Foundry is our AI stack offering, bringing together AI applications, fine-tuning, compression, synthetic data generation, RAG, AI agents and inference.
  • Sentinel AI is our AI-native governance stack, currently in development. It makes sure AI activity stays secure and within the required policies. It will run both as SaaS and in customer on-prem environments, and integrates with Foundry.

You’ll be working alongside world-leading experts to develop solutions that tackle real-world challenges. We’re looking for passionate individuals eager to grow in an ethics-driven environment that values sustainability and diversity.

We’re committed to building a truly inclusive culture—come and join us.

As an Engineering Manager you will

  • Lead the Sentinel AI team of machine learning and software engineers, taking end-to-end ownership of its roadmap, delivery, quality and customer outcomes.
  • Turn AI governance, security and regulatory requirements into a clear product and engineering roadmap, working with stakeholders and customers.
  • Drive Sentinel AI's capabilities across AI governance and AI security, including policy enforcement, monitoring of AI activity and agentic red teaming.
  • Ensure Sentinel AI is properly integrated into Foundry, working with the teams that own its components.
  • Own Luminary, our external enterprise LLM benchmarking product, and our internal model evaluation platform, keeping both current with the latest evaluation frameworks.
  • Own the KPIs and SLAs for Sentinel AI across SaaS and on-prem deployments.
  • Stay hands-on as a player-coach: build, prototype, and write and review code and designs alongside the team, while setting engineering standards for architecture, quality, security and delivery.
  • Drive proofs of concept and innovation across product lines, leveraging AI to optimise workstreams and the way our teams work.
  • Hire, mentor and grow engineers, set clear expectations and build a high-ownership, high-performance team.
  • Work with product, legal, compliance and engineering teams to keep Multiverse Computing's offerings, including Sentinel AI, aligned with the EU AI Act and other emerging regulatory frameworks.
  • Partner with product, presales, sales, marketing and support teams to ensure a streamlined go-to-market for Sentinel AI and Foundry.
  • Collaborate with the CompactifAI, model development, DevOps and QA teams to deliver integrated, reusable solutions across the organisation.

Required Qualifications:

  • Master's degree or higher in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, Data Science, Operations Research or a related field
  • 8+ years of experience in software or machine learning engineering, including 3+ years leading engineering teams
  • Proven experience leading teams that combine machine learning and software engineers, with an ownership mindset: you take accountability for outcomes and act without waiting to be asked
  • Experience in AI governance, AI gateways and AI security, including agentic red teaming and adversarial testing of LLM-based systems
  • Knowledge of the EU AI Act and other regulatory frameworks (e.g., GDPR, ISO 27001)
  • Strong understanding of machine learning models and LLMs, of how to evaluate them, and of the AI stack around them (fine-tuning, RAG, agents, inference)
  • Experience building or owning model evaluation products against realistic, real-world scenarios
  • Strong hands-on engineering skills (Python preferred): you still build, prototype and review code for secure, scalable, cloud-native systems delivered as SaaS and on-prem
  • Track record of driving proofs of concept and innovation, and of applying AI to improve products and ways of working
  • Track record of delivering against KPIs and SLAs and of shipping quality products on time
  • Experience hiring, coaching and retaining engineers, and building a strong team culture
  • Excellent communication skills, including with legal, compliance, customer and executive stakeholders, both in-person and through online resources
  • Experience designing and running benchmarking pipelines for LLMs or other AI models, with metrics for quality, efficiency and performance, is a plus.
