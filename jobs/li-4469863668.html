Description

At AstroKube, our mission is to enable the world to create and maintain reliable digital products.

To accomplish it, we build AstroKube Platform, which unifies infrastructure context, reusable components and repeatable workflows under permissions that control what humans and AI agents can run.

We also build AstroKube Agents, a team of specialist agents (platform engineering, release engineering, SRE, security, FinOps) that do operational work with varying degrees of engineering supervision.

To complement our products, we deliver Cloud Native services.

What do we offer?

The conditions are:

  • Salary EUR 24-45K
  • Hybrid work, combine in-person at our offices in Ciudad Real and remote
  • Flexible hours as long as they fit with the projects you are working on

What will you do?

You will have the following responsibilities:

  • You will design and build AI agents and AI-native systems that automate platform engineering and operations work. You will achieve this using LLM APIs, agent frameworks and protocols such as MCP, or the technology you apply based on the problem.
  • You will participate in the design and implementation of Cloud Native platforms and Release Engineering processes.
  • You will develop Infrastructure as Code modules and automate operational processes on Cloud Native platforms. You will achieve this using Terraform modules, Pulumi modules, Kubernetes operators, or the technology you apply based on the problem.
  • You will deploy Cloud Native platforms (almost always based on Kubernetes) and then help migrate applications to this new platform. In addition, you will participate in the platform's operations: "you build it, you run it!"
  • You will develop automatic tests to validate architectures and Cloud platforms.
  • You will participate in the ideation and development of tools and functionalities to create self-service Cloud platforms.

What are we searching for?

We are looking for someone with a great desire to learn, a good team player, and enthusiasm for open source.

Our requirements are the following:

  • You have a builder mindset.
  • You have curiosity for technology, especially for AI and Cloud.
  • You want to outgrow this role. We would rather hire someone who will grow past this job description than someone who will sit comfortably inside it.
  • You have an AI native mindset. You have not only adopted AI into your workflow, you use it to keep growing your own knowledge and abilities.
  • You are comfortable building AI agents for yourself and for others.
  • You must be able to communicate your ideas clearly and especially be able to listen to others.
  • You learn fast and on your own. You are comfortable being dropped into a technology you have never used and finding your way.
  • You can speak fluently in both Spanish and English.

Our daily work involves the following areas and we value any experience you have in them.

  • Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Data platforms (Databricks, Kubeflow)
  • Kubernetes and the Cloud Native ecosystem
  • CI/CD and release engineering (GitHub Actions)
  • Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, OpenTofu, Pulumi)
  • High Level Programming (Go, Python, TypeScript, SQL)
  • Systems Programming (Rust, C)
