What You'll Do

  • Stakeholder in the overall management of 5G NTN RAN solution, own the design and development of RAN applications (on top of SMO/RIC framework) for the 5G LEO ground network based on use cases and requirements from system engineering and other stakeholders
  • Lead DevOps oriented engineering efforts to integrate/deploy/manage RAN applications
  • Support end to end integration of operational workflows of NTN ground network

The day-to-day

  • Participate in system design/development/integration efforts of various NTN orchestration and resource management applications in a modern satellite ground network
  • Apply your knowledge of NTN / 5G /O-RAN framework and overcome restrictions imposed by standard interfaces for D2D use cases
  • Drive and contribute in the design/development of NTN RAN applications on public cloud
  • Integrate orchestration, telemetry, fault, metrics, and other OAM features required to manage RAN applications and overall constellation resources

What You'll Need

  • Minimum 10 to 12 years of software development experiences with Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, related field, or equivalent work experience
  • At least 2-3 years of people leadership experience leading small R&D teams
  • Very good understanding of 5G NR standard and O-RAN framework
  • Experience using AI assisted coding tools for production grade software development and testing
  • Experience defining and developing RESTful interfaces
  • Hands on with modern mainstream languages such as Go, Python, Rust and C++
  • Excellent knowledge of Linux operating system and development environments, GitOps
  • In-dept knowledge of cloud-native technologies, including Kubernetes (K8s), CaaS, PaaS, with hands on knowledge of containerized application development.
  • Experience deployment and managing software applications in private, public (GCP preferred), or hybrid cloud environments.
  • Strong deployment, troubleshooting, automation expertise
  • Ability to work with distributed remote agile teams and vendors with a high degree of self-motivation and ability to work independently
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills to drive interaction with peer engineers, vendors and project manager
  • Ability to travel up to 10%

What Will Help You On The Job

  • Experience developing RAN resource optimization applications for TN/NTN networks
  • Understanding of LEO NTN network and associated RAN challenges
  • Experience in microservices-based, stateless, cloud-native architecture definitions, considering auto-scaling, high availability and elasticity
  • Experience deploying and monitoring applications in Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE)
  • Experience in CI-CD automation and tools such as Argo, Jenkins, Ansible, etc.
  • Knowledge of container networking fundamentals and K8s orchestration
  • Experience applying machine learning & statistical techniques such as clustering, classification, regression, forecasting, & feature engineering, to real-world problems
  • Experience with network management frameworks utilizing SNMP, Netconf, YANG, and RESTful APIs
  • Strong analytical and communication skills
