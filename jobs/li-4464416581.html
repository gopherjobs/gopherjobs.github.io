About AIRoA The AI Robot Association (AIRoA) is launching a groundbreaking initiative: collecting one million hours of humanoid robot operation data with hundreds of robots, and leveraging it to train the world's most powerful Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.

What makes AIRoA unique is not only the unprecedented scale of real-world data and humanoid platforms, but also our commitment to making everything open and accessible. We are building a shared "robot data ecosystem" where datasets, trained models, and benchmarks are available to everyone. Researchers around the world will be able to evaluate their models on standardized humanoid robots through our open evaluation platform.

About This Role You will own the web platform that enables operators to teleoperate robots in home and living environments, along with the operator- and resident-facing applications. You will design, build, and maintain them across their full lifecycle. Human operators remotely drive humanoids in residential settings through these applications, and you also own the integration that hands the resulting data off to the ML/VLA and data-platform teams.

The platform spans a Go backend built on Domain-Driven Design and Clean Architecture, two Next.js/React applications (one for operators and one for residents), low-latency video, audio, and ROS telemetry transport, and the AWS infrastructure that runs it across development, staging, and production. You will work end-to-end, from real-time transport and access control to the operator UX that shapes the quality of the collected data.

Team & Development Style

  • Work starts from requests raised by the strategy team and on-site operators, and you take it from there: organizing requirements, then design, development, testing, and ongoing operations. You see features through from conception to production, not just a single stage
  • We work in Scrum (sprints and story-point estimation)
  • We align on design documents before implementing, and changes are reviewed and then delivered continuously via CI/CD
  • You will build alongside people from a range of specialties, including operators, VLA researchers, and robotics engineers

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and implement teleoperation systems (real-time control, low-latency video/audio/telemetry transport, and operator-facing UI)
  • Work closely with robot operators, using real-world feedback to continuously improve the operator experience, session reliability, and data quality
  • Develop and operate the web applications and internal tools that support data collection, such as mission/task management, robot and operator management, and monitoring dashboards
  • Develop configuration-management systems for metadata such as robot configurations, task definitions, and operator assignments
  • Design and implement APIs for data interactions between internal services and external data sources
  • Integrate with the core platform (robot and data infrastructure), collaborating with the VLA and Data/ML platform teams on data ingestion and workflow management
  • Ensure secure communication through robust authentication and access control (including role-based access and service-to-service authentication)
  • Operate the platform across development, staging, and production (Infrastructure as Code, database migrations, and observability)

AIRoAについて 一般社団法人 AI Robot Association（AIRoA）は、数百台のヒューマノイドロボットによって100万時間規模の稼働データを収集し、それを用いて世界最高水準の Vision-Language-Action（VLA）モデルを学習するという、前例のない取り組みを進めています。

AIRoAの特徴は、実世界データとヒューマノイド基盤の規模だけでなく、すべてをオープンにするという姿勢にあります。データセット・学習済みモデル・ベンチマークを誰もが利用できる「ロボットデータのエコシステム」を構築し、世界中の研究者が標準化されたヒューマノイド上で自らのモデルを評価できる、オープンな評価基盤を目指しています。

このポジションについて あなたには、オペレーターが家庭・生活環境のロボットを遠隔操作（テレオペレーション）するのを支えるWebプラットフォームと、オペレーター・居住者向けアプリケーションの開発・運用保守を担っていただきます。オペレーターが住環境のヒューマノイドを円滑に遠隔操作できるよう、操作を支えるアプリケーション群を継続的に開発・改善するとともに、そこで得られたデータをML/VLAチームやデータ基盤チームへ受け渡す連携部分も担う、ソフトウェアの中核レイヤーです。

このプラットフォームは、Domain-Driven Design と Clean Architecture に基づく Go 製バックエンド、オペレーター向け／居住者（resident）向けの2つの Next.js/React アプリケーション、低遅延の映像・音声・ROSテレメトリ伝送、そしてそれらを開発・ステージング・本番環境で動かす AWS インフラにまたがります。リアルタイム通信やアクセス制御といった基盤から、収集データの品質を左右するオペレーターUXまで、エンドツーエンドで関わっていただきます。
