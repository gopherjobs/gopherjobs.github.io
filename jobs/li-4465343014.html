  • About the project
  • As we continue to grow, we are looking to add a Sr. Software Engineer to our team. If you are passionate about building scalable B2B SaaS solutions and thrive in a fast-paced, cloud-native environment, this is a great opportunity for you.
  • This role sits within our Engineering organization and owns application and service development end-to-end — from architecture through implementation, testing, and deployment. You will be a hands-on backend-leaning full-stack engineer, building microservices and modern front-end components while mentoring other engineers along the way.
  • This is a technically deep, execution-focused role where strong engineering fundamentals, cloud fluency, and cross-team collaboration are as important as raw coding skill (role is approximately 70% hands-on development, 30% mentoring).
  • What you'll do
  • Develop applications and services primarily using React and .NET, hosted on Azure or AWS PaaS offerings.
  • Design architecture through diagrams and technical design documents.
  • Build and maintain microservices that are scalable, highly available, and cost-optimized.
  • Design and implement APIs within a modern software architecture.
  • Create event-driven solutions where events trigger actions across system components.
  • Use container and provisioning technologies such as Docker and Terraform.
  • Integrate security practices into ongoing development work.
  • Work with NoSQL databases and distributed storage systems.
  • Implement monitoring and observability tooling to track system performance and resource utilization.
  • Interpret functional/non-functional requirements and deliver reliable software solutions independently.
  • Mentor team members, providing regular feedback on architecture, documentation, and code quality.
  • Estimate and plan work through user stories and tasks.
  • Participate in interviewing, recruiting, and hiring as the team grows.
  • What makes you a great fit
  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 6+ years of experience as a Full-Stack Developer / Software Engineer (approx. 60–65% backend, 35–40% front end).
  • Strong experience with C# and .NET 6 or later.
  • Senior-level experience with ASPX Pages and React.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker and Terraform.
  • Cloud experience with AWS and/or Azure (deep experience with either is a strong plus).
  • Experience developing and implementing microservices in production environments.
  • Background building and consuming APIs in modern software architectures.
  • Experience with event-driven architecture.
  • Experience integrating security into the development lifecycle.
  • Experience with decentralized/NoSQL databases and distributed storage.
  • Experience implementing monitoring and observability tools.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
  • Very strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Advanced English Level is required for this role, as you will work with US clients. Effective communication in English is essential to deliver the best solutions to our clients and expand your horizons.
