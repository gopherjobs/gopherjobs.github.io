Guided by a shared purpose of helping people love where they live, we have a diversified portfolio of brands and solutions across different countries in Latin America, covering all phases of the housing journey. We also have a Technology Hub in Portugal. We develop technology and innovation to transform and enhance the overall living experience.

With the support of a world-class team of investors and advisors, including Kaszek, Qualcomm, General Atlantic, and SoftBank, Grupo QuintoAndar is currently valued at over USD 5.1 billion and continues to grow year over year.

Location & Remote Work for technology and remote-first roles Our technology team operates under a "remote-first" model, which means we work from home and can live anywhere in Brazil. We also offer the option of working from our São Paulo offices or partner coworking spaces, up to twice a week.

About the Team Productivity Platform is part of QuintoAndar's Tech Platform line. We build the platform that makes our 650+ Engineers more productive: CI/CD, our internal CLI, our Internal Developer Platform (Backstage), experimentation infrastructure, engineering metrics and a growing set of AI-assisted developer workflows.

We are a software engineering team, not an operations team. We don't just keep systems running; we build the products other engineers use every day to take code from commit to production.

Why now AI changed how our engineers work. They now run several coding agents in parallel, and the amount of code we submit doubled in the last six months. Every one of those changes still has to be tested, reviewed and deployed safely. Our job is to make the platform ready for that agentic scale: faster pipelines, a more robust release platform, and new ways to review code with quality.

What you will do As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design, build and run parts of the platform used by our 650+ Engineers, working closely with Staff Engineers on the team's technical direction.

  • Build and evolve features of our CI/CD and release platform (Woodpecker, ArgoCD and more) so it stays fast and reliable as load keeps growing.
  • Develop tools that help engineers review code faster and with higher quality.
  • Improve our Internal Developer Platform (Backstage) and internal CLI so engineers find what they need in one place.
  • Use engineering productivity metrics to find bottlenecks in your area and ship improvements.
  • Own the services you build in production, including taking part in the team's on-call (fireman) rotation.
  • Help teams follow best practices through tools, documentation and automation.
