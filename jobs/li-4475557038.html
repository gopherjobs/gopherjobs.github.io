IT Test Analyst | Life & Pensions | Mostly Remote | €350/Day

  • Multi-year transformation programme
  • Life400
  • Dublin based

A leading Irish Life & Pensions organisation is embarking on a multi-year transformation programme, modernising core systems, evolving critical business platforms and delivering new products and services across the business.

This is an excellent opportunity for a self-sufficient IT Test Analyst with strong Insurance and Life & Pensions experience to join an established technology team and play a key role in delivering business-critical system change.

You’ll work closely with Business Analysts, Developers, Testers and business stakeholders, taking ownership across the full testing lifecycle — from requirements and test design through to defect management, regression testing and release.

With a significant pipeline of transformation and product initiatives, this role offers the opportunity to work across complex systems, contribute to continuous improvement and make a tangible impact within a major Irish financial services organisation.

Why You’ll Love This Job

  • Mostly remote, with the flexibility of working from home and occasional in-office collaboration.
  • Excellent contract longevity.
  • Take on new challenges, work within a major Irish Life & Pensions organisation on business-critical transformation projects.
  • Join a collaborative, experienced technology team with strong exposure to Insurance, Life & Pensions and core systems.

What You’ll Do

  • Define testing strategies, create test cases and execute testing activities.
  • Identify, document and manage defects through to resolution.
  • Assess business and customer impact of system changes and identify risks.

What You Bring

  • 3+ years' experience as a Test Analyst within the insurance sector.
  • Life 400 experience
