Description

There are NO limits to your career: come shape the future and be part of a truly unique global culture at OutSystems! As a Lead Developer at OutSystems, you are a technical Thought Leader and a senior individual contributor who drives organizational acceleration and high-impact Expert Services. Your mission is to translate visionary Agentic AI concepts into scalable, production-ready solutions, defining the global standards for technical excellence and innovative automation that scale across multiple delivery teams. You are the "North Star" for Agentic Systems and engineering acceleration within the organization.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own the technical delivery of the organization’s most complex strategic projects, ensuring peak performance and security.
  • Lead Expert Services, solving mission-critical issues and turning ambiguous needs into governed, scalable architectures.
  • Govern the lifecycle of Agentic Systems, integrating platform tools with industry-standard versioning and automated pipelines.
  • Define CI/CD release strategies, incorporating version control and feature toggling to optimize the practice’s delivery engine.
  • Drive technical thought leadership, sharing architectural blueprints and acceleration standards utilized by all delivery teams.
  • Institutionalize Strategic Prompt Engineering, focusing on chain-of-thought, and non-deterministic output governance.
  • Lead technical discovery and sizing for pre-sales, supporting executive-level sessions on technical strategy and business value.
  • Leverage emerging AI technologies (LLMs, Vector DBs, Agents) to boost global developer productivity and system intelligence.
  • Architect massive-scale AI orchestration, designing the tool-calling, memory, and reasoning structures for autonomous agents.
  • Partner with Product and R&D as a strategic liaison, aligning field requirements with the platform’s future roadmap.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Soft Skills: Strategic influence, innovation mindset, expert-level communication for executive stakeholders, and the ability to lead and mentor senior engineers.
  • Technical Skills: 8+ years in software development. Mastery of Agentic Development, including agent orchestration, memory management, tool-calling, and non-deterministic governance. Expertise in the OutSystems Agentic Platform and associated stacks (.NET, CSS, JS, SQL). Mastery of CI/CD lifecycles, feature toggling, and version control management for shared libraries and code.
  • Education/Experience: 8–10 years of experience; proven track record of leading technical teams and delivering "Expert Services" in high-pressure enterprise environments.
  • Certifications: OutSystems Expert Developer + Agentic AI Specialist certifications.
