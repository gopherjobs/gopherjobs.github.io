Description

Job Title: Software Engineer / Python Developer

Type - Contract

Location - Remote

Job Summary

We are looking for a skilled and collaborative Software Engineer to contribute to the development, enhancement, and deployment of new and existing software products. The ideal candidate will have strong Python development experience, a passion for writing high-quality and maintainable code, and the ability to work effectively within a cross-functional product and engineering team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, test, and deploy scalable software applications and backend solutions.
  • Write clean, efficient, maintainable, and high-quality Python code following industry best practices.
  • Contribute to the development and enhancement of new and existing products and features.
  • Participate in code reviews, paired programming, testing, debugging, and technical discussions.
  • Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and other stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Take ownership of technical and product-related decisions and contribute to the overall product roadmap.
  • Develop and maintain cloud-based applications and data-driven solutions.
  • Work with containerization and orchestration technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Identify opportunities to improve application performance, scalability, reliability, and development processes.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies and recommend appropriate tools and approaches for different technical requirements.
  • Follow an iterative development approach, experiment with new ideas, and continuously improve engineering practices.
  • Troubleshoot production issues and support applications through deployment and ongoing maintenance.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Strong professional experience with Python development.
  • Hands-on experience with one or more Python frameworks such as Django or Flask .
  • Experience working with AWS, Azure, or other cloud platforms .
  • Experience with data engineering/workflow tools such as Kedro and Databricks .
  • Strong understanding of Docker and Kubernetes .
  • Experience working with relational databases such as MySQL or PostgreSQL .
  • Familiarity with software development lifecycle, CI/CD, version control, testing, and deployment practices.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience developing cloud-native and distributed applications.
  • Understanding of data pipelines, APIs, microservices, and scalable backend architectures.
  • Knowledge of Agile/Scrum development methodologies.
  • Ability to evaluate and adopt modern technologies based on business and technical needs.
  • Strong communication, collaboration, and stakeholder-management skills.
  • A continuous-learning mindset with enthusiasm for experimenting and improving existing processes.
