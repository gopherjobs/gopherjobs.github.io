About Us Maplesoft Group is currently seeking a Senior Developer (Guidewire) for a permanent opportunity with our client. This role is ideal for a hands-on senior developer who thrives in a collaborative environment, can work independently, and delivers high-quality technical solutions while contributing to modernization initiatives across the organization.

________________________________________

Position Summary The Senior Developer will be responsible for designing, developing, enhancing, and maintaining Guidewire applications within a cloud-based environment. The successful candidate will hit the ground running, contribute to code reviews and technical investigations, support junior and senior developers, and take ownership of delivering quality code across a variety of mini-projects, refactoring initiatives, and ongoing application enhancements. This role offers strong opportunities for career growth toward an Expert-level technical position.

________________________________________

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop, enhance, and maintain Guidewire applications using Gosu and Java.
  • Work within Guidewire Cloud environments to deliver high-quality, scalable solutions.
  • Conduct code reviews and provide constructive technical guidance to development team members.
  • Investigate existing code, troubleshoot complex issues, and recommend effective solutions.
  • Participate in refactoring initiatives and smaller modernization projects.
  • Collaborate with junior and senior developers through day-to-day technical mentorship and knowledge sharing.
  • Contribute to technical discussions and ensure consistent development best practices.
  • Use GitHub for source control and collaborative software development.
  • Work with Informatica and related enterprise integration technologies where required.
  • Take ownership of development deliverables while working independently with minimal supervision.

________________________________________

Required Qualifications

  • 5–7 years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Guidewire development.
  • Proficiency with Gosu and Java.
  • Experience working with Guidewire Cloud.
  • Experience using GitHub for version control and collaborative development.
  • Strong ability to understand existing codebases, investigate issues, and perform code reviews.
  • Ability to work independently and deliver quality solutions with minimal direction.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Willingness to obtain or maintain up-to-date Guidewire certifications within the first 3 months of employment if needed.

________________________________________

Preferred Qualifications

  • Commercial insurance industry experience.
  • Experience with Informatica.
  • Guidewire certifications that are current and up to date.
  • Previous experience providing mentorship or technical leadership to development teams.
  • Solution design experience within Guidewire environments.
  • Experience contributing to modernization or enterprise refactoring initiatives.
