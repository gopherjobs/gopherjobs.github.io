TITLE

Senior Associate Software Engineer

Who We Are Duck Creek Technologies is the intelligent solutions provider defining the future of the property and casualty (P&C) insurance industry. Our modern SaaS platform enables insurers to run agile, cloud-based operations and deliver better customer experiences.

Duck Creek is a flexible-first company, offering employees autonomy in how they manage their work. As part of our Flying Together approach, we are thoughtfully increasing opportunities for in-person collaboration by focusing on hiring in designated locations. This role is aligned to a specific location, and you’ll partner with your leader to participate in meaningful in-person moments that support team connection and collaboration.

About Vista Duck Creek Technologies is a portfolio company of Vista Equity Partners, a global investment firm that partners with mission-critical enterprise software and technology-enabled businesses to drive growth and operational excellence. As part of a Vista portfolio company, you’ll work in an environment backed by deep expertise, long-term investment, and a strong commitment to building market-leading businesses and meaningful careers.

Position Overview As our Senior Associate Software Engineer in our Delivery and Support team, you’ll deliver customer implementation projects, configuring, integrating and extending the platform for each customer’s needs. Through handover and go-live you’ll own application and production support for customer go live. This role sits alongside our core product engineering team rather than within it. You’ll work closely with Delivery, Product, Engineering and customer stakeholders to resolve issues quickly, configure and extend the platform for each customer’s needs, and feed what you learn back into the product. It suits a hands-on-engineer who enjoys solving real problems that directly affect customers

What You’ll Do

  • Deliver customer implementation projects end to end. You’ll configure, integrate and extend the platform to meet customer requirements, working within the core product’s architecture and standards
  • Work with Delivery, Product and customer stakeholders to understand requirements, estimate work and turn them into maintainable solutions delivered to agreed timelines
  • Lead the technical handover from implementation into live service, making sure environments, integrations and documentation are ready for go-live
  • Own issues from first report to resolution, including root-cause analysis, fixes, clear communication with customers and internal stakeholders, and steps to stop them happening again
  • Work with Delivery, Product and core Engineering to turn customer requirements into maintainable solutions and pass recurring issues and product gaps back to the product team
  • Build quality into delivery through unit and integration testing, troubleshooting, debugging, and maintaining reliable and performant services
  • Make delivery faster and more repeatable through reusable configurations, CI/CD, deployment automation and better runbooks and monitoring
  • Act in accordance with and as a good steward of Duck Creek Technologies mission, vision, and core values:
  • Mission: To empower insurers to reimagine the future of insurance
  • Vision: To transform insurance technology, helping insurers be smarter, faster, and more efficient, and ultimately provide the best protection for people and businesses
  • Values: We Prioritize Respect, We Listen, We Care, We Add Value, and We Lead

What You’ve Done

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher or equivalent additional years of experience; applicable functional experience in the discipline or a closely related function
  • At least 5 years of professional Java engineering experience, with strong experience developing backend web applications.
  • Proficiency with Spring Boot, REST APIs, JPA, and Hibernate, together with experience working with relational databases such as MySQL
  • Experience delivering customer-facing implementations or integrations in a SaaS or platform environment.
  • Experiencing supporting systems, with strong troubleshooting and debugging skills
  • Knowledge of microservices architecture and software engineering best practices, including unit and integration testing and maintaining high code quality
  • A proactive, pragmatic approach to problem-solving, with strong communication and collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a distributed team
  • Experience with AWS, Apache Camel, Docker, Kubernetes, or other relevant cloud and DevOps technologies is beneficial
  • Experience in the insurance industry or working with insurance-related software is beneficial

Skills, Knowledge, Abilities, And Behaviors

  • Applied knowledge of Software Engineering practices, processes, tools, and operating standards
  • Sound analytical judgment and structured problem-solving capability
  • Ability to plan, prioritize, and deliver work against agreed timelines
  • Clear written and verbal communication for technical and business audiences
  • Stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration
  • Attention to detail and commitment to high-quality work products
  • Ability to interpret data, identify trends, and convert findings into action
  • Process improvement mindset with practical change management awareness
  • Understanding of governance, risk, controls, and policy compliance
  • Customer, partner, or internal service orientation appropriate to the function
  • Adaptability in a dynamic global software company environment.
  • Ownership mindset, accountability, and reliable follow-through
  • Professional discretion when handling confidential or sensitive information
  • Ability to learn new systems, tools, methods, and business processes quickly Inclusive behaviors that support collaboration, trust, and constructive feedback

LOCATION & WAYS OF WORKING

Duck Creek is a flexible-first company, offering employees autonomy in how they manage their day-to-day work. As part of our Flying Together approach, we are thoughtfully increasing opportunities for in-person collaboration by focusing on hiring in designated locations.

This role is aligned to a specific location, and you are expected to reside within reasonable proximity to that location. You will partner with your leader to participate in meaningful in-person moments that support team connection, collaboration, and business priorities.

As our Flying Together approach continues to evolve, you may be expected to transition to a hybrid work arrangement, including working from a designated office location on a schedule determined by your leader and organizational needs.
