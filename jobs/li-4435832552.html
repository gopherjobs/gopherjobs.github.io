Description & Requirements Who we are looking for At Deloitte, we have passion for innovation. For our fast-growing Engineering practice in Deloitte EMEA, we are looking for a detail-oriented Integration Architect focusing on solutioning, designing and implementing APIs and Agent integrations.

Respecting each other's time, we look for seasoned individuals with a bit (5y+) of experience solving complex problems using various integration styles and patterns.

We value integrity and transparency, and we don’t use Copilot for screening CVs – so don’t bother polishing it.

If you believe you have the right mix of:

  • passion for solving problems and creating added value
  • ownership to get things done
  • critical thinking to do things once (or twice at most 😊)
  • business acumen to interact not only with IT teams but also to read (or invent) business requirement
  • experience in various integration solutions such as iPaaS, microservices, APIs. Events streams, etc etc.
  • have valid work permit

and you are:

  • team player able to work without the team
  • able to work in various methodologies (Waterfall, Agile, SAFe)
  • effective in leveraging AI in daily duties.

Apply! Share problems you solved, integrations you built, innovations you created, and experience you gained.
