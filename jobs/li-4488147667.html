About The Opportunity Role Overview

Build high-performance AI-enabled contact center channels (Outbound) with enterprise-scale microservices and responsive frontends using API-first principles and leveraging AI SDLC.

Team Charter

  • Core Domain: Architect and implement Predictive, Progressive, and Preview outbound systems using Go microservices and API-first design.
  • Administration & Configuration: Develop management interfaces and services built on Node.js.
  • Full-Stack Autonomy & Deployment: Operate within a self-sufficient team covering end-to-end delivery; knowledge of Argo CD is a plus.
  • Operations & Observability: Provide customer support and troubleshooting using Grafana and telemetry tools.

Education & Qualification

  • B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech / M.C.A. in Computer Science or equivalent.

Experience Requirements

  • Total Experience: 12+ years in architecting, designing, developing, deploying, and troubleshooting cloud API-based microservices (Cloud-Native and Hybrid) & Front End.
  • Backend Development: 12+ years of core backend development.
  • Full-Stack Experience: 5+ years in full-stack application development.
  • AI SDLC Experience: 1+ years using Agents & Skills to perform assessment, planning, implementation, testing, deployment, documentation, and troubleshooting.
  • Platform & DevOps Architecture (Optional): 2+ years of experience in platform/system architecture.

Responsibilities

  • Backend Architecture & Solution Design: Design, build, and deploy enterprise-scale microservices handling high TPS with ultra-low latency, high resiliency, scalability, observability, and serviceability.
  • Frontend Development: Build user interfaces using JavaScript/TypeScript, React/Angular.
  • Technical Leadership & Collaboration: Serve as a technical domain expert, lead system design decisions, review code, and collaborate in Agile/Scrum cross-functional teams.
  • Operations & Quality Assurance: Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve issues; manage containerized deployments (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm).

About The Requirement Technical Skills & Competencies

  • Backend: Strong proficiency in Go, Java, Node.js
  • Frontend: JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Angular, SDKs
  • Data Streaming & Databases: Kafka, Redis, MariaDB, MySQL
  • Architecture & Web Services: Microservices architecture, distributed systems design, RESTful APIs
  • Cloud & DevOps: Hands-on experience with Azure or GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, CI/CD pipelines, and continuous delivery patterns
  • AI Tools & Engineering: Advanced proficiency with AI coding assistants (Codex, Cursor, Claude), prompt engineering, Agentic-based development for planning, assessing, code generation, troubleshooting, and AI-assisted workflow optimization
  • Quality Focus: Static Analysis, Load & Performance Profiling & Tuning
  • Observability & Serviceability: Experience with Grafana/Prometheus, System Telemetry
  • Customer Care: Debugging & troubleshooting customer issues, root-cause analysis, production troubleshooting, and incident resolution

Domain Expertise

  • Strong domain background in Telecommunications or Contact Centers (Outbound Contact Center Preferred).
