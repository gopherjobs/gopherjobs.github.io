Description

Job Description The PACE is fast. The expectations are high. The contribution matters. Working at TYTAN is more than a job, this is a mission. We push boundaries, think big, and hold ourselves to the highest standard. Our mission and values guide everything we do, and we're looking for people who are driven by ownership, embrace challenges, and are energized by meaningful work. If you're motivated by impact and thrive in dynamic environments, you'll fit right in.

At TYTAN , we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect European sovereignty.

We are looking for a working student who wants hands-on experience with real data infrastructure. Your developments will enable engineers to get valuable insights from the product operations in a way which is efficient, scalable and reliable. You can expect a lot of ambitious prototyping, participation in system design rounds and hands-on flight testing operations.

Your roadmap to make an impact

  • Build and test the tooling that creates, collects and aggregates our data, working alongside engineers in a small, hands-on team
  • Develop and test pipelines for data creation, collection and aggregation, from devices in the field to storage
  • Write Python tools for ingesting, validating, converting and processing sensor data, logs and video
  • Improve and automate our DataOps workflows: data versioning, quality checks, metadata and dataset management
  • Containerize tools with Docker and help run them reliably on Linux hosts and edge devices
  • Support MLOps tasks such as preparing training datasets, tracking experiments and automating evaluation runs
  • Write tests and documentation so your tools can be used and maintained by the whole team

Job Requirements

  • Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s programme in Computer Science, Data Science, Electrical Engineering or a related field
  • Solid Python skills: you write clean scripts and small tools, ideally using libraries such as pandas or NumPy
  • Comfortable working on Linux: command line, shell basics and remote machines via SSH
  • Confident with Git: branching, pull requests and collaborating in a shared codebase
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, including building images and running containers
  • A working understanding of cloud infrastructure and object storage such as S3, plus networking basics (IP addressing, ports, DNS and how data moves between devices and servers)
  • A drive to make systems work end to end, and fluent English

Nice to have

  • First exposure to ML workflows, data versioning or orchestration tools
  • Interest in robotics, embedded systems or ROS 2
  • German language skills
  • Interest in aerospace or defence, and in working close to real hardware and field operations
