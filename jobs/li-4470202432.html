Description

Hungry, Humble, Honest, with Heart

The Opportunity Nutanix has disrupted the multi-billion-dollar virtualization market by pioneering the first converged compute and storage virtualization appliance. We are now onto our next leg of making hybrid multi-cloud infinitely simple and cost-effective. In this role, you will help build the next-generation platform that gives enterprises the ability to encapsulate infrastructure and applications as code, optimizing capacity and automating operational tasks at scale.

About the Team You will be joining the Nutanix Cloud Manager engineering team. We are a group of builders dedicated to streamlining management, deployment, and scaling of applications through self-service and centralized role-based IT governance. We operate in a culture of high technical mentorship and autonomy, working closely to drive financial accountability and unify security operations with intelligent analysis.

Your Role

  • Improve performance and scalability of internal back-end components across a variety of languages including C/C++, Go, Python, and Java.
  • Diagnose and debug deep, insidious issues within massively parallel systems and async network code.
  • Build systems management software to manage the application life cycle in the software-defined scale-out data center.
  • Collaborate with product managers, designers, and support engineers to deliver robust, web-scale solutions.

What You Will Bring

  • BS/MS in Computer Science or Engineering with 7-10 years of experience in building high-performance, fault-tolerant distributed applications.
  • Familiarity with OS internals, distributed data management, and web-scale systems.
  • Strong proficiency in Linux and experience in at least one of the following: GoLang, C, C++, Java, or Python.
  • Working experience with storage, networking, virtualization (Nutanix, VMware, KVM), and/or cloud technologies (AWS, Azure, GCP).
  • Experience with database systems (SQL, Document-based) and messaging technologies (NATS, Kafka, Pulsar).
