About Nexus Mods Established in 2001, Nexus Mods is the largest video game modding platform on the internet - a household name in the PC gaming ecosystem.

  • 77M+ registered members
  • 900K+ mods for 5,000+ games
  • 24B+ mod downloads

We’re currently focused on product modernisation, driving growth and building infrastructure for the next 10 years of community-powered modding.

Our Role We’re looking for Principal Backend Engineer to join our Backend Engineering team. You will act as the technical lead within a cross functional pod building Nexus Mods’ core services.

This is a hands-on role focused on shaping technical decisions, guiding the pod’s engineering work, supporting the team technically, and ensuring delivery is robust, scalable, and aligned with our technical direction.

This is a chance to work on challenges most companies never encounter:

  • Massive scale serving millions of users and requests.
  • Complex mod compatibility, dependencies, and versioning.
  • A deeply engaged and passionate global modding community.

From discovery and experimentation to delivery and scale, you’ll play a key role in shaping systems that:

  • Help millions of gamers discover, download, and manage mods effortlessly.
  • Empower creators to publish and manage their work with confidence.
  • Power high traffic APIs and distributed systems at scale.
  • Support both web and desktop applications.
  • Enable rapid experimentation and iteration on new features.
  • Ensure the platform evolves to support the next decade of growth.

Role Responsibilities

  • Own technical direction within your pod (architecture, design, tooling, trade-offs).
  • Build and scale backend services and APIs serving millions of users.
  • Ensure systems are performant, reliable, and maintainable.
  • Align pod decisions with wider platform architecture.
  • Support and unblock engineers to help teammates work efficiently.
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Design, and other disciplines.
  • Drive engineering standards across code quality, testing, and delivery.
  • Contribute to discovery, prototyping, and iterative delivery.
  • Identify and tackle technical debt and scalability challenges.

Role Requirements Requirements:

  • Extensive backend engineering experience, ideally with gaming or tech.
  • Deep experience with at least one backend language (Ruby, PHP, Java, Go, Node.js, etc.)
  • Strong system design and architecture skills.
  • Experience building and scaling APIs (REST and/or GraphQL).
  • Solid understanding of distributed systems and event driven architectures.
  • Experience with relational databases (Postgres/MySQL) and NoSQL (Redis/ElasticSeach).
  • Experience with CI/CD, Docker, and Kubernetes.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to lead through influence.
  • Comfortable balancing speed, quality, and pragmatism.

Bonus Skills

  • Experience with authentication, payments, or search systems.
  • Background in high scale platforms or UGC.
  • Understanding of gaming ecosystems (e.g. multiplayer services, content distribution, or digital marketplaces).
  • Experience building tools for creators, modding communities, or user-generated content platforms.
  • Passion for gaming, modding, or creator ecosystems.
