The Opportunity - Senior Software Engineer II, Platform & Enablement (Remote - CANADA) Wrapbook is looking for a Senior Software Engineer II to join PANDA , our platform and technical enablement team. PANDA owns the cross-cutting systems every product team at Wrapbook relies on: the Approval Workflow Engine (AWE), permissions for customers and internal employees, authentication (Auth0/MFA), and notifications. This isn't just a feature team — it's platform infrastructure with deep technical complexity and broad cross-team impact, and the systems you build will be relied on company-wide.

At the Senior II level, you'll be a senior expert and standard-setter — owning complex, multi-stakeholder work, raising the technical bar across the team, and proactively driving team success beyond your own deliverables.

What You'll Do

  • Own and evolve cross-cutting platform systems — approval workflows, permissions, authentication, and notifications — designing and improving complex architectures with an eye toward simplicity, scalability, and backward compatibility
  • Align multiple stakeholders to a plan, empowering contributions from inside and outside the team and function; serve as a trusted partner with business stakeholders and cross-functional teams
  • Lead complex, multi-stakeholder initiatives, coordinating multiple concurrent efforts and rescuing at-risk projects when needed
  • Set the code quality standard for the team — author code reviewed primarily for strategic alignment, not fundamentals
  • Advocate for and design tech investment projects at the team level, framing them against roadmap priorities
  • Create AI-forward workflows that meaningfully accelerate the team and set the standard for how AI is used and shared broadly
  • Develop teammates with visible multiplier impact — build a continuous learning environment and recognize contributions at all levels
  • Lead and participate in technical interviews for IC and lead engineering positions
  • Participate in an on-call rotation, responding to production issues and partnering with the team to resolve them quickly

What You'll Have

  • You're a full-stack engineer with a strong technical background building production web applications and APIs in Ruby on Rails
  • Architectural depth — you design and improve complex architectures, optimize for simplicity, and set the code quality standard; you understand the risks of changing shared infrastructure and can make deliberate tech debt tradeoffs with business context
  • Multi-stakeholder leadership — you've led complex initiatives across multiple teams, built stakeholder buy-in, and rescued at-risk projects
  • High ownership mentality — self-directed and solution-oriented; you independently prioritize competing short- and long-term goals, balancing inbound requests with self-directed work
  • Experience on a platform, infrastructure, or enablement team — you've built systems that other teams consume and you're comfortable balancing "ship our roadmap" with "unblock everyone else." Bonus points if that experience includes approval workflows, role-based access control (RBAC), or identity/auth products (Auth0, Okta, session management) in a multi-tenant SaaS product
  • Domain and competitive knowledge — you build expertise over time, use data to inform decisions, and align your work with business strategy and roadmap priorities
  • A force-multiplier mindset — you develop teammates with visible multiplier impact, foster a continuous learning environment, and use AI with strong oversight and judgment to build tooling and workflows that address common team pain points
  • Experience with agentic AI tooling, orchestration frameworks, and AI-augmented workflows
  • Experience with technical documentation and planning
  • Excellent communication skills — you match depth and format to audience and medium, synthesize complex input concisely, and are a trusted partner with business stakeholders
  • Comfortable working in two-week cycles with Agile planning
  • Strong SQL skills; PostgreSQL experience is a plus
  • Customer-focused with attention to detail and a commitment to quality
