Project Description

Full Description

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Backend Developer to join our growing engineering team at Hiredbuddy. In this entry-level role, you will work remotely alongside experienced developers to design, build, and maintain the server-side logic, databases, and APIs that power our platform. You will have the opportunity to learn from senior engineers, contribute to real-world projects, and grow your skills in a supportive environment.

As a Backend Developer, you will be responsible for writing clean, maintainable, and efficient code using modern programming languages and frameworks. You will collaborate with front-end developers, product managers, and QA testers to ensure seamless integration and optimal performance of our applications. Your daily tasks may include developing RESTful APIs, optimizing database queries, implementing authentication and authorization mechanisms, and troubleshooting existing systems. You will also participate in code reviews, write unit tests, and document your work to ensure high-quality deliverables.

We value continuous learning and encourage you to stay updated with industry trends and best practices. You will have access to a mentorship program, internal workshops, and a dedicated budget for professional development. Our remote culture promotes work-life balance, open communication, and cross-functional collaboration.

To succeed in this role, you should have a foundational understanding of backend development concepts, including experience with at least one programming language such as Python, Java, or Node.js. Familiarity with relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and web frameworks (e.g., Django, Spring, Express) is a plus. You should be comfortable with version control systems like Git and have a basic grasp of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud). We are looking for someone who is eager to learn, pays attention to detail, and can communicate effectively in a remote team setting.

If you are passionate about building robust and scalable systems and want to kick-start your career at a company that invests in your growth, we would love to hear from you. Apply today to join Hiredbuddy and make a meaningful impact!

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience)
  • 0-2 years of experience in backend development (internships or academic projects count)
  • Proficiency in at least one backend programming language (Python, Java, Node.js, etc.)
  • Basic understanding of RESTful APIs and web services
  • Familiarity with SQL and relational databases
  • Knowledge of version control (Git) and collaborative workflows
  • Strong problem-solving and debugging skills
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work independently and manage time effectively in a remote environment
  • A portfolio of personal projects or contributions to open-source is a plus

Benefits

  • Professional development budget for courses and certifications
  • Company-provided laptop and equipment
  • Collaborative and supportive team culture
