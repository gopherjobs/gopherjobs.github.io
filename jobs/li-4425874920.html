💻 Role The International Engineering team at Whatnot builds the foundational systems that power our global expansion. Every new market introduces different payment methods, tax requirements, shipping constraints, consumer protections, and operational challenges, creating complex technical problems with direct business impact.

As a Senior Backend Engineer based in Poland, you'll work with a small, high-impact team across Poland and the US to design and build highly scalable distributed systems that support millions of transactions across multiple countries and regulatory environments. You'll partner closely with product, operations, payments, logistics, and platform teams, with an initial focus on Germany, France, and the UK.

The Team Owns Critical Operational And Transactional Systems Across

  • Cross-border transaction infrastructure
  • Tax and regulatory compliance
  • International post-purchase experiences
  • Marketplace operational tooling and automation

Your work will span core backend infrastructure and product-facing systems that directly improve buyer trust, seller success, and Whatnot's ability to scale globally.

👋 You Required

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Strong backend engineering experience in distributed systems or large-scale platforms
  • Experience designing, operating, and scaling reliable production services
  • Experience working with relational databases and event-driven architectures
  • Ability to drive projects independently from ambiguous problem statements to production launch
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate across engineering, product, operations, and business stakeholders

Preferred

  • Strong proficiency in Python
  • Experience with payments, fintech, e-commerce, logistics, marketplace platforms, or compliance systems
  • Experience building operational platforms, workflow systems, or transaction processing infrastructure
  • Experience working in rapidly growing product organizations
