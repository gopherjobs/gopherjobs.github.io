About The Role Kinaxis Maestro manages and predicts what is needed to effectively balance supply and demand for some of the world’s most complex supply chains. We are seeking a Software Platform Architect to lead the evolution of our Maestro platform toa cloud‑native architecture with experience designing database engines, storage engines, distributed databases, query execution systems, or other large-scale data infrastructure platforms.

As part of the Product R&D organization, you will define and drive the architectural foundations that enable massive scale, elastic performance, and cost‑efficient execution across Kinaxis’ next‑generation backend platform that underpins Maestro.

This role is central to Kinaxis’ platform modernization — re‑architecting core data, query, and execution capabilities to operate natively in the cloud while preserving the performance, reliability, and customer trust that define our platform.

What you will do

You operate comfortably across the full architecture lifecycle - from conceptual design and deep technical exploration to hands‑on implementation and production hardening. You are driven by complex systems problems and understand how to balance performance, scalability, cost, and operational resilience in real‑world cloud environments.

You will design and evolve highly scalable, distributed, cloud‑native architectures that power core platform capabilities. You think naturally in terms of data flow, consistency models, latency trade‑offs, fault tolerance, and system boundaries, and you continuously push for simpler, more robust, and more performant designs.

You will contribute to the architectural direction for the backend platform, shaping how large‑scale data processing, query execution, and platform services are built and evolved. This includes setting standards for cloud‑native design, guiding decisions around distributed systems patterns, and ensuring architectures are both extensible and production‑ready.

You will act as a technical authority across Product R&D, influencing architectural decisions that span cloud infrastructure, core platform services, and data systems. You will mentor senior engineers, elevate architectural rigor across teams, and foster a culture where architects are deeply accountable for the systems they design.

You are a pragmatic, hands‑on architect. You write code, validate assumptions through prototypes, and work closely with engineering teams to ensure designs translate into reliable, scalable implementations. You value clarity, simplicity, and execution over theoretical perfection.

You are a strong collaborator and communicator, able to explain complex architectural concepts to engineers, product leaders, and executives alike. You bring structure to ambiguity and guide teams toward solutions that align technical excellence with business outcomes.
