The Opportunity We’re Planview, the only Value Stream Management company that transforms traditional businesses into high-performing tech companies. Our Value Stream Management (VSM) platform has enabled our customers to dramatically reduce their time to market and increase the velocity of software development.

We’re seeking an Intermediate Software Engineer to join our team in Vancouver, BC. In this role, working closely with our senior team members, you will design, implement, and test features for a generative AI platform for our products. These features will allow users to interact with an AI to identify management concerns and suggest possible actions to address the concerns.

You will be collaborating with multiple teams within Planview including Product and Data Science. Multiple Planview teams will provide a variety of management data including OKR's, product portfolios,and engineering work tickets. The ideal candidate enjoys developing new features with AI for AI-based products and collaborating with other teams.

What You'll Do Bring AI to life in a variety of Planview products by supporting those teams with an exciting new agentic platform. Collaborate with the Product team to enable AI-based customer use cases. Work with other development teams as stakeholders. Develop and deploy scalable services using a modern tech stack
