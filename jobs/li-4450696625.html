Description

About The Role And Team Uber's Deployment Engine team is the backbone of how the world's largest ride-sharing and delivery platform safely ships innovation at scale impacting ~ 150 million customers. Our deployment platform enforces policies and gathers real-time signals to ensure that every change to Uber's production systems rolls out incrementally and automatically reverts if those signals hint at trouble.

Our mission is simple: make production deployments stress-free for every engineer at Uber. We do this by building intelligent guardrails that continuously evolve alongside new workload types, deployment patterns, and scale demands. With ~1M rollout operations per week across our deployment systems , orchestrating changes safely is a genuinely hard problem and one we're deeply passionate about solving. The stakes are real: our systems directly impact 150 million+ monthly active platform consumers globally, 7 million+ active drivers and couriers and services in 70+ countries and 10,000+ cities. Our Platform Makes High-precision, Data-driven Decisions To Detect Potential Incidents And Regressions Early And Automatically, Dramatically Reducing The Risk Of Outages. We Power Capabilities Like

  • Automatic rollbacks of bad deployments
  • Emergency deploy policies and system lockdowns during active incidents
  • Incident mitigation tooling that keeps Uber's services available and reliable

If you're energized by wrestling with distributed systems edge cases, using cutting edge technologies and infrastructure challenges, the kind that keep a global business running, you'll thrive here. You'll work on a critical platform that enables Uber to innovate rapidly without compromising safety or reliability, making our customers happy and our services world-class.
