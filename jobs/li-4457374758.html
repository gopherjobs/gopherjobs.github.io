Excellent opportunity to work REMOTELY with a U.S.-based company. Candidates living in Mexico, or all LATAM are welcome to apply. About The Company Bydrec, Inc. is a California-based company that connects top Tech talent from Latin America with U.S. companies looking to expand their development teams. Learn more at bydrec.com.

Position Summary BYDREC is looking for a Software Test Engineer to join our client’s team and help ensure the quality, reliability, and functionality of web/SaaS and mobile applications.

This is a manual-testing-first role for a technically capable QA professional who is comfortable working across functional, exploratory, regression, and integration testing, while also using Python for test scripts, data validation, and automation .

The ideal candidate is hands-on, detail-oriented, comfortable investigating complex issues, and able to clearly document and communicate defects. Experience using modern AI coding tools such as Claude, Codex, or similar agentic coding tools is required .

Responsibilities

  • Perform hands-on manual testing of web, SaaS, and mobile applications.
  • Develop and execute test plans, test cases, and test scenarios.
  • Conduct functional, regression, exploratory, and integration testing.
  • Reproduce, investigate, and document software defects with clear and actionable details.
  • Validate application behavior across different environments and use cases.
  • Write and maintain Python scripts for test automation, data validation, and other QA activities.
  • Collaborate with developers, product managers, and other stakeholders to identify and resolve quality issues.
  • Support automation efforts and contribute to improving testing efficiency and coverage.
  • Use API testing tools and technologies to validate backend services and integrations.
  • Analyze test results and communicate findings effectively.
  • Leverage AI-powered coding and development tools to improve testing, scripting, and automation workflows.

Qualifications Required:

  • Hands-on experience in manual software testing, particularly with web/SaaS and mobile applications.
  • Strong understanding of software testing methodologies and QA best practices.
  • Experience with:

  • Regression testing
  • Exploratory testing
  • Bug reproduction
  • Defect reporting and documentation
  • Working knowledge of Python for scripting, automation, or data validation.
  • Experience working with or understanding REST APIs.
  • Experience with agentic coding / AI-powered development tools, such as Claude, Codex, or similar technologies.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to communicate technical findings clearly and effectively.
  • Intermediate to Advanced English proficiency. English proficiency B2+ or C1
