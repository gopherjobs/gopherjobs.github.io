Description

Role Description The Architect at PM Corner Thailand is a part-time remote role focused on shaping and guiding the technical and architectural direction of community platforms and related initiatives. On a day-to-day basis, the Architect will design and document system architectures, collaborate with software developers to ensure solutions align with community needs, and oversee the integration of various tools and services used by members. The role involves reviewing technical proposals, advising on best practices in architectural design, and helping coordinate small projects that enhance digital experiences for the community. The Architect will also work closely with organizers to translate product and community requirements into scalable, maintainable technical solutions.

Qualifications

  • Strong skills in Architecture and Architectural Design, with experience defining system structures and technical standards.
  • Hands-on experience in Software Development and Integration, including collaborating with developers to implement and connect systems.
  • Proficiency in Project Management, with the ability to plan, prioritize, and coordinate part-time initiatives in a remote environment.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail in technical documentation and solution design.
  • Effective communication skills and the ability to collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders in a virtual community setting.
  • Experience with modern web or cloud technologies and architectural patterns (e.g., microservices, APIs, distributed systems) is beneficial.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Prior experience supporting product management communities, startups, or technology-driven organizations is an advantage.
