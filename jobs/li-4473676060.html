Description

Institution ALBA is the Spanish synchrotron light source, a large research infrastructure operating thirteen beamlines, complementary facilities, and an Electron Microscopy Center, this last in partnership with other institutions. Currently constructing multiple more beamlines, expanding the Electron Microscopy Center, and integrating advanced data analytics, it sums up a wide range of infrastructures geared toward finding solutions to societal challenges.

Located in Cerdanyola del Vallès (Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain), it is funded by the Spanish Government (Ministerio de Ciencia e Innovación) and the Catalan Government (Generalitat de Catalunya, Departament de Recerca i Universitats). The synchrotron light produced by 3 GeV electrons is used by thousands of researchers to analyse and understand the properties and functionality of matter, spanning a wide variety of fields, such as catalytic research, health, energy production/storage, environmental research, communication technologies, or cultural heritage.

Our highly motivated staff works in a multidisciplinary work environment with an atmosphere formed by lived diversity, inclusion and respect for our colleagues. One of the goals of our gender equality plan is to reach parity within the Consortium’s different divisions and offices, and several actions have been taken to encourage applications from women mostly in scientific, engineer and technical job positions.

Located close to Barcelona in a natural park and well connected by car and public transportation, with excellent connectivity to the world and an employer who understands the importance of life-work balance.

Facing an upgrade to ALBA II, a 4th generation facility, which will increase dramatically brightness and coherent flux, ALBA is in a phase of growth and dynamics. Being a part of the ALBA team will promote your career and will give you the opportunities to explore new territories.

Tasks Responsibilities The Scientific Data Management (SDM) section supports the facility’s scientific data processing and management needs, ensures data compliance for future implementations, and aligns activities with current data policy and FAIR principles. SDM collaborates closely with other Computing Division groups responsible for beamline control, data acquisition, databases, and IT systems infrastructure. The section primarily supports the Experiments Division, while also providing occasional assistance to other divisions in the following areas:

  • Scientific data processing/analysis software
  • Data pipelines development and optimization
  • Data formats, data analysis, data reduction after experiment acquisition
  • Post-processing visualization tools and workflow engines
  • Data governance

We are offering two positions with complementary areas of focus:

Scientific Software Engineer: Responsible for developing data processing, analysis, and visualization software for imaging beamlines . This role involves close collaboration with scientists and Computing Division colleagues to ensure reliable processing, storage, and delivery of experimental data and metadata.

Data Management and Infrastructure Engineer: Responsible for developing, integrating, and maintaining scientific data services, including the data catalogue, associated databases, and the VISA data-analysis platform across all beamlines. Responsibilities include incident resolution, user support for data access and processing, coordination with other sections, and contributions to data standards, including ALBA’s participation on the NeXus committee.

Both positions will undertake the following functions, with different emphases according to their respective profiles:

 Develop scientific data processing and analysis software, applying automated testing, packaging, continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD), configuration management, logging and monitoring.

 Develop and optimize data pipelines for High Performance Computing (HPC), integrating them with data-analysis platforms and supporting services.

 Perform data formatting, analysis and reduction following experimental data acquisition, including instrument-specific data reduction and compression.

 Develop post-processing visualization tools and workflow engines to support scientific analysis.

 Ensure the integration of data and metadata into the ALBA data catalogue using the required ontologies, in accordance with FAIR principles, including the development and maintenance of supporting data-management software and services.

 Perform any other duties consistent with the position’s category, responsibilities, professional level, and functional scope, as reasonably assigned by Management to ensure proper operation.

The two positions require fluent English speaking and writing. We will consider candidates with a basic level of English, provided they engage with the language support provided by the consortium to reach full proficiency.
