Description

Summary Our team is growing, and we’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to help shape the future of technology services for Property & Casualty (P&C) carriers. This is a customer facing role focused on implementing AI powered solutions and configuring our platform with Python, MySQL and automation tools. You’ll collaborate closely with customers to translate business needs into technical outcomes, ensuring our AI enhanced systems deliver performance, reliability and impact.

If you love solving complex problems, thrive in AI driven environments, and enjoy working closely with customers to deliver secure, scalable solutions, we’d like to chat with you.

This is a fully remote based role, however candidates must be located in Argentina or Brazil . This is a long-term Independent Contractor position.

Note: please submit your resume in English .

What You'll Do

  • Work directly with customers to understand business needs and translate them into AI enhanced technical solutions
  • Configure and extend our platform using Python, MySQL and AI APIs to deliver intelligent functionality
  • Partner with product management to launch secure, user friendly features that integrate automation, predictive analytics and natural language capabilities
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to define AI driven requirements, manage expectations and drive on time delivery
  • Thrive in an Agile environment with Scrum, automated testing, continuous integration, code reviews and version control
  • Deliver projects on time based on the defined Statement of Work and communicate progress effectively with customers
  • Own the creation and maintenance of validation tests focused on ensuring accuracy and integrity of AI powered workflows
  • Leverage AI throughout the implementation process to optimize workflows, automate complex tasks and improve customer outcomes

What You'll Bring

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or a related field
  • 5+ years of experience in Python web development
  • 3+ years of SaaS product development experience, including cloud based solutions
  • 3+ years of professional experience with relational databases (e.g., MySQL)
  • 3+ years of experience with Django or another Python framework
  • 2+ years of proven experience building and consuming APIs
  • Hands on experience using AI enhanced IDEs (e.g., Cursor, VS Code) to accelerate delivery, simplify workflows and focus on high value problem solving
  • Experience with agentic frameworks (e.g., AutoGen, LangGraph, CrewAI)
  • Professional experience working with Docker for containerization and AWS services such as EC2, API Gateway and Lambda
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills

Bonus points

  • 2+ years of experience working at a scaling startup
  • Experience working in a customer facing role
