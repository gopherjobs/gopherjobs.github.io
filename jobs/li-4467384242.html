Description

About iCliGo

iCliGo is an innovative platform connecting travelers with independent and highly qualified travel consultants.

Founded in 2014, our Portuguese multinational has grown into a technology-driven company, committed to delivering seamless travel experiences while embracing a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.

About the Role

Our DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) ensures the reliability, scalability, and security of iCliGo’s systems and services.

This professional plays a key role in building and maintaining our infrastructure, automating processes, and enabling fast, safe, and repeatable deployments.

With a proactive mindset, strong problem-solving skills, and a passion for cloud technologies and observability, the DevOps/SRE will help keep our platforms robust and performant while supporting business growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, implement, and maintain cloud-based infrastructure (AWS, Azure, or similar);

  • Automate deployment pipelines, monitoring, and system management tasks;

  • Ensure high availability, scalability, and security of services and applications;

  • Collaborate closely with development teams to integrate CI/CD practices and improve operational efficiency;

  • Monitor system performance, troubleshoot incidents, and proactively prevent outages;

  • Maintain configuration management and infrastructure-as-code using tools like Terraform, Ansible, or similar;

  • Develop and enforce best practices for observability, logging, alerting, and incident response;

  • Contribute to disaster recovery and business continuity planning.

What We’re Looking For

  • Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field;

  • 4+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or systems administration roles;

  • Strong experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP);

  • Hands-on knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automation tools (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, etc.);

  • Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and orchestration;

  • Familiarity with configuration management and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation);

  • Strong understanding of system monitoring, logging, and alerting tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, etc.);

  • Proactive problem-solving skills, excellent communication, and teamwork;

  • Fluent in English.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary based on experience and expertise;

  • Health insurance;

  • 24 days of annual leave (including your birthday off);

  • Remote working model;

  • Travel perks at net rates.
