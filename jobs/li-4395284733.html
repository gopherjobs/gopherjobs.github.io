Description

Workload - 80 - 100% The position aims to deliver high-value solutions in collaboration with key customers and business departments. The ideal candidate should have at least 5 years of professional experience in the .NET stack, strong backend development skills, and end-to-end development lifecycle expertise.

What will you do

  • Build and evolve AI‑enabled applications from early prototypes to production systems
  • Collaborate closely with product managers and analysts during ideation and discovery phases
  • Design, architecture, develop, and maintain high‑quality backend services like APIs, MCPs, databases and messaging systems
  • Rapidly experiment with new ideas and iterate based on feedback and data
  • Participating in DevOps activities: CI/CD, deployment, monitoring, and troubleshooting
  • Take ownership of software components for products emerging from ideation initiatives
  • Continuously explore new development tools, frameworks, and AI‑related capabilities
  • Share learnings and support other teams through knowledge exchange

What You Bring & Who You Are

  • Several years of hands‑on experience as a software engineer using .NET
  • Strong craftsmanship mindset and pride in building quality software
  • Deep experience working across backend components (REST APIs, relational and non-relational databases, messaging queues, etc.), using modern architectural and design patterns
  • Genuine and proven interest in AI‑enabled features and intelligent applications
  • Enjoy working in ambiguous environments where problems are not fully defined
  • Ability to balance speed of experimentation with production quality
  • Team‑oriented, curious, and eager to learn
  • Fluent in English; German is a plus
