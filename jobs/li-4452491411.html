THE OPPORTUNITY NavVis builds mobile mapping systems, hardware and software developed and manufactured in Munich. The System Software turns our devices into products: it runs onboard, brings up the platform, orchestrates the sensors, runs the mapping stack in real time, and gives the operator a UI to control the capture. Every scan a customer delivers starts on the device.

The System Software team owns the on-device software stack across the product line, including NavVis VLX System Software, NavVis MLX System Software, and the variants that follow; the team's deliverable is a working device and a great mapping experience.

We are looking for a Team Lead for the System Software team in the Mapping and Perception (MAP) department. The role spans the full on-device stack, from the mapping UI and Python backend down to the OS and platform layer directly above the kernel, and sits between software and physical devices: your team integrates upstream work from other teams, and you work closely with the hardware teams to turn hardware into a shippable product. You own one of the most consequential cross-team boundaries in the department, combining technical direction with people leadership.

How You Will Make An Impact

  • Deliver the software and user experience across the full stack, from multi-language UI and real-time scan visualization to application-level UI development (Angular, Python backend, application-level ROS nodes) and the Linux platform
  • Lead, mentor, and grow the System Software team across its two squads: set technical direction, coach through regular 1:1s, and drive career development and team performance together with the department lead
  • Ensure strong communication and collaboration within and across teams and departments
  • Shape product direction: work toward product goals with other teams and adapt the System Software as new sensors, form factors, compute platforms, and cross-product features emerge
  • Own software readiness for production launches: define technical direction and align milestones and roadmaps with product management and other teams, from early prototypes to production-ready releases
  • Define team boundaries and shape the hardware and software handover: establish handover conventions per subsystem, including workflows, interface contracts, and best practices
  • Drive reliability, data quality, and scalability of the System Software, including provisioning and update mechanisms that scale with production volumes, and own the team's internal infrastructure (CI pipelines, hardware-in-the-loop tests, stress tests) to catch regressions early and keep quality measurable
  • Uphold engineering quality through the development workflow, release, QA, and code-review processes, contributing hands-on where it matters, and drive AI adoption while balancing speed and quality

What Will Help You Succeed In The Role

  • Master's degree or PhD in computer science, electrical engineering, robotics, or a related field, and 7-10 years of professional software engineering experience on systems that run on real hardware
  • Experience managing a shared technical boundary with another team, for example platform and driver or software and hardware, through interface definitions, handover conventions, defined ownership, and a joint mission, in environments where software engineering intersects with physical device production or hardware integration
  • Strong C++ experience in a production codebase, with solid Python for tooling, scripting, and automation
  • Solid understanding of application development and UI frameworks, enough to give technical direction on UI and application development without being a frontend specialist
  • Demonstrated experience leading or technically mentoring a software team: people development, task coordination, and cross-functional stakeholder communication
  • Excellent written and verbal communication in English, including explaining technical concepts to hardware engineering and production management

Would be an added benefit

  • Experience with UI development and frameworks such as Angular, React, Vue.js, or a comparable modern UI framework
  • Experience with real-time or near-real-time systems: sensor data acquisition, device control, streaming, or live visualization
  • Experience with robotics in general, ideally including SLAM or ROS or a comparable robotics middleware in a production C++ and Python codebase
  • Device provisioning, software roll-out, and OTA update systems, or image-based deployment
  • Experience with GPU or graphics stack integration on embedded platforms (vendor blobs, OpenGL, Vulkan, Mesa) or patching kernel-dependent libraries (NPU, libc, crypto)
  • Experience working with sensor data and sensors, particularly integrating camera or video pipelines
  • Real-time 3D graphics or GPU programming
  • Contributions to open-source software
