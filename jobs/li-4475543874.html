Cisco NSO Developer

Location: Helsinki (Remote EU)

Contract: Freelance 12 months+ extension

Job Summary

Looking for an experienced Cisco NSO Developer to design, develop, and automate network service orchestration solutions across multi-vendor environments. The ideal candidate will build NSO service packages using YANG and Python, integrate NSO with OSS/BSS systems, and deliver scalable, REST/NETCONF-based provisioning workflows supported by robust CI/CD pipelines.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop Cisco NSO service packages using YANG and Python.
  • Automate service provisioning across multi-vendor networks.
  • Integrate NSO with OSS/BSS systems.
  • Build REST/NETCONF-based orchestration workflows.
  • Maintain CI/CD pipelines for network automation.
  • Troubleshoot service deployments and device synchronisation issues.

Required Skills & Experience

  • 10+ years of relevant development experience.
  • Strong hands-on experience in Cisco NSO service development.
  • Strong expertise in:
  • Cisco NSO development
  • YANG data modelling
  • RESTCONF / NETCONF
  • Linux shell scripting
  • Proficiency in a programming language, preferably Python.
  • Experience with multi-vendor network automation and OSS/BSS integration.
  • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
