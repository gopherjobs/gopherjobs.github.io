About Axon

Axon’s mission is bold and simple: Protect Life. We build technology that gives first responders, enterprises and communities the clarity to act in the moments that matter most.

Our Real-Time Operations (RTO) portfolio, powered by Fusus, is transforming how public safety and security organisations detect, assess and respond to incidents. By connecting live video, devices and operational systems, we help customers build a shared understanding of what is happening—and respond with greater confidence.

We’re looking for a Director of Software Engineering to help build and scale the mission-critical software behind these experiences.

The Role

As Director of Software Engineering within Real-Time Operations, you will lead multiple engineering teams delivering secure, reliable and scalable software for public safety and enterprise customers.

You will own the technical direction and engineering execution for your area, translating product priorities into clear plans and measurable outcomes. Working closely with Product, Design and other engineering leaders, you will guide architectural decisions, develop engineering talent and ensure teams deliver software that customers can depend on.

This role requires a leader who combines technical depth with strong people leadership. You will be comfortable moving between long-term strategy and delivery detail—challenging assumptions, making trade-offs and creating the conditions for teams to do their best work.

Your Impact

  • Deliver technology that matters. Lead teams building software that supports real-time situational awareness and incident response.
  • Shape technical direction. Establish a clear technical strategy that balances customer needs, platform reuse and long-term maintainability.
  • Build strong teams. Develop engineering managers and technical leaders, strengthening ownership, collaboration and accountability.
  • Raise engineering standards. Improve reliability, security and delivery quality across systems that span devices, cloud services and user-facing applications.
  • Connect strategy to execution. Partner with Product and Design to turn customer problems into prioritised roadmaps and dependable delivery.

What You’ll Do

Reports to: VP, Software Engineering

Work Location: This role is based out of our London office and follows a hybrid schedule. We rely on in-person collaboration and ask that team members work onsite Tuesdays through Fridays, with the flexibility to work remotely on Mondays, unless there is an approved workplace accommodation. We believe that connection fuels innovation, and our in-office culture is designed to foster meaningful teamwork, mentorship, and shared success.

Role & Responsibilities:

  • Lead and develop multiple software engineering teams, setting clear expectations for technical quality, delivery and team performance.
  • Partner with Product and Design to define roadmaps, sequence investments and make informed trade-offs between new capabilities, platform improvements and technical debt.
  • Guide architecture and system-design decisions across your area, engaging directly with engineers on complex technical challenges.
  • Build engineering practices that strengthen testing, code review, CI/CD, observability and deployment confidence.
  • Own the operational health of your services, using incidents and customer feedback to drive lasting improvements.
  • Hire, coach and develop engineering managers, technical leads and engineers; address performance issues with clarity and candour.
  • Collaborate across Axon to deliver integrated experiences and resolve dependencies between teams.
  • Work with Security, Privacy and Compliance partners to embed customer and regulatory requirements into engineering decisions.
  • Communicate progress, risks and trade-offs clearly, creating alignment without adding unnecessary processes.

What You Bring

  • At least 15 years of total experience in software engineering, including at least 5 years in engineering leadership roles, with experience leading teams and developing engineering leaders.
  • A track record of delivering complex software products through multiple teams, from planning and architecture through launch and operation.
  • Strong technical fluency in distributed systems, cloud services and modern software architecture.
  • Experience building secure, reliable systems where availability, performance and customer trust are critical.
  • Proven ability to balance delivery commitments with the investment needed to improve platform quality and maintainability.
  • Strong judgement in ambiguous environments, with the ability to make decisions, explain trade-offs and adapt as new information emerges.
  • Experience partnering closely with Product, Design and other engineering organisations.
  • A commitment to building inclusive, high-trust teams and developing talent.

Preferred Experience

  • Real-time systems, video streaming, IoT or device-to-cloud architectures.
  • Enterprise security, public safety technology or other mission-critical software environments.
  • Platform modernisation, complex integrations or scaling products across international markets.

Who You Are

  • Mission-driven: You connect engineering decisions to meaningful outcomes for customers and communities.
  • Customer-obsessed: You work backwards from customer needs and stay close to how your software is used.
  • Technically credible: You ask insightful questions, challenge assumptions and help teams make sound architectural decisions.
  • A multiplier: You develop other leaders and create an environment where teams can take ownership and succeed.
  • Candid and collaborative: You give clear feedback, welcome different perspectives and join forces across organisational boundaries.
  • Accountable for outcomes: You bring focus to complex priorities and follow through on commitments.
