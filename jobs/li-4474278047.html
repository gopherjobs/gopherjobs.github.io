Experienced Test Analyst (Leiden, On-site)

Making innovation possible.

Making the world work.

Mission:

The mission of this role is to ensure the accurate execution, analysis, and reporting of EMV and payment-related tests while delivering high-quality technical support to customers and internal teams. The position contributes to maintaining service excellence, strengthening compliance, and supporting the implementation of new payment solutions.

ACTIVITIES

  • Demonstrating exceptional technical knowledge to conduct test sessions in accordance with the related Quality Assurance Plan.
  • Analyzing EMV test data and results, providing insightful feedback.
  • Producing comprehensive test reports and continually striving to minimize issues identified during reviews.
  • Understanding the purpose of tests with respect to customer needs and specificities within the testing scope.
  • Developing technical documentation and actively contributing to the internal knowledge base.
  • Offering technical consulting and debugging services to customers, both remotely and on customer premises when necessary.
  • Providing technical support internally to colleagues.
  • Ensuring the delivery of high-quality service at all times and adhering to the quality process.
  • Expanding your skillset and maintaining acquired skills while actively suggesting improvements.
  • Collecting and disseminating customer feedback to enhance our services.
  • Being the technical point of contact for customers and participating in internal training sessions.
  • Actively participating in sharing technical knowledge with colleagues.
  • Qualification of test tools and coordinating with vendors.

ADDITIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Reviewing and authorizing test reports.
  • Preparing and delivering internal technical training sessions.
  • Providing extensive technical support and analysis to internal teams, customers, and authorities.
  • Engaging actively in the implementation of new services and projects for service improvement.
  • Participating in audit preparations and actual audits.
  • Engaging in technical discussions with authorities such as MasterCard, Discover, RuPay, and others.
  • Autonomy on tasks planning.

JOB EXPERIENCE

  • 3 -5 years of experience in the payment industry.
  • Good English language proficiency (oral and written).
  • Excellent written and spoken communication skills within an international and multi-cultural environment.
  • High degree of accuracy and thoroughness in work.

KNOWLEDGE & SKILLS

  • Strong knowledge in EMV.
  • Working on certification for local schemes like Elo, Ticket etc.
  • Experience in coordinating with Payment schemes.
  • Familiarity with Quality System and procedures.
  • Customer-oriented mindset.

Contract: Full Time
