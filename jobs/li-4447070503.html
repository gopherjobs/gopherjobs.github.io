We are currently seeking a Software Development Senior Specialist to join our team in Guadalajara, Jalisco (MX-JAL), Mexico (MX).

Role: Engineering Support Level 3 - Controls Engineering, Support experience preferably in 4-10 years range

Responsibilities:

  • Investigate and drive support resolution with internal stake holders and other Controls Engineering teams. Serve as point of escalation for support issues from Controls Engineering
  • Work closely with the engineering team to drive resolution of client issues.
  • Look for opportunities to automate existing process and use AI tooling.
  • Manage incidents and Post Mortems. Ensure SLOs are met and clients are kept informed and happy.
  • Create documentation /runbooks and code snippets to share with clients and internal engineering teams

Key criteria:

  • Must be comfortable coding and debugging in Java or Python. Experience of Kubernetes and front end development tools such as REACT an advantage.
  • Must have experience in a support role and role will require shifts including bank holidays. Experience managing incidents.
  • Must be presentable to clients and a clear, empathetic communicator able to talk to clients and stakeholders with confidence.
  • Must be willing to execute on support issues while also building out the function and automating resolution of repeat issues.
  • Good Knowledge of SDLC, CVS, SVN, GIT, Maven, GITLAB, Docker and Jenkins.
  • work experience on SDLC, LINUX system platforms.
  • Good Knowledge of CVS, SVN, GIT, Maven, GITLAB, Docker and Jenkins.Production Support & Incident Management
  • Provide L3 support for complex production issues, including deep analysis, debugging, code-level fixes, and end-to-end validation.
  • Perform detailed Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and implement permanent corrective and preventive actions.
  • Manage incident lifecycle, including outage communication, tracking, escalation, and post-resolution follow-ups.
  • Ensure adherence to SLA commitments with a strong focus on reducing recurring incidents.

Application Support

  • Support Software Delivery Lifecycle (SDLC) operations, including code compilation, builds, artifact management, and software distribution.
  • Troubleshoot issues related to CI/CD pipelines, repositories, and build systems.
  • Create and maintain CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab, Azure DevOps) and ensure build stability.
  • Validate releases to minimize production risks and rollback scenarios.

Infrastructure & Automation

  • Provide infrastructure and platform support across Linux-based environments.
  • Automate repetitive operational tasks using Shell, Python\Java, or Perl scripting.
  • Support configuration management and automation tools such as Chef, Puppet, Ansible.
  • Improve system efficiency by developing automation solutions for provisioning, monitoring, and operational workflows.

Monitoring, Performance & Reliability

  • Utilize tools like Splunk for log analysis, monitoring, and troubleshooting.
  • Continuously focus on performance tuning, scalability, and system optimization.
  • Identify trends, defects, and system inefficiencies to drive proactive improvements.

Database & Systems Expertise

  • Work with relational databases (Oracle, DB2, MS SQL, Sybase) and write optimized SQL queries.
  • Support application servers and middleware such as Apache, Tomcat.
