Description

Back End Engineer, Connected Sports Tech

Location: Remote / hybrid, 1 day a week in Reading

The opportunity

Our client is a century-old global sports brand, recently backed by a leading private equity firm and moving into a digital era. They're building a camera-based auto-scoring product that tracks play in real time and connects to an app for online games, stats and training. You'll build the Golang services and databases behind it, working in a small cross-functional team alongside the hardware engineers.

What you'll be doing

  • Build and maintain back end REST services in Golang
  • Design and evolve the SQL data model behind those services
  • Refine user stories with product and integrate APIs with front end developers
  • Write automated tests, then build and deploy to production
  • Monitor performance and quality in production with the ops team
  • Share knowledge through code reviews and pair programming

What we're looking for

  • 4+ years of commercial experience in Go, or a similar language such as Kotlin or Rust
  • Back end experience on a high-volume consumer product
  • Hands-on experience building REST APIs
  • Strong SQL and familiarity with PostgreSQL
  • Experience with automated testing and CI/CD pipelines
  • Comfort working at a higher level to help shape back end architecture
  • A figure-it-out attitude and comfort with shifting priorities in a startup-style team

Bonus points if you have

  • Kubernetes and containerised deployments
  • Grafana/Loki monitoring and observability
  • Modern front end, or Swift/Kotlin for mobile
  • A background in sports or gaming

What's on offer

  • Bonus of up to 20% based on individual and company performance
  • Pension with 3% employer and 5% employee contributions
  • Private healthcare
  • Cycle to work scheme, with a salary sacrifice EV scheme coming soon
  • Travel to the office paid for
  • You spec your own dev machine
  • Access to 60+ co-working spaces
  • A high degree of autonomy, working directly with a seasoned CTO, CPO and technology investors
