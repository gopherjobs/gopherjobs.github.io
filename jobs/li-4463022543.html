About Us

About this role

As a part of our team, you'll help us shape a platform that works for One — a business owner, an artist, a freelancer.

You will lead our Data squad — the service provider every other squad depends on for data. You'll own the technical direction of the data platform: read models, CDC pipelines, and the analytical warehouse serving all teams. Our data platform is derived from the operational systems of record. You'll run the squad as an internal service: other teams bring requests; you turn them into reliable, auditable data products.

You'll take ownership of:

  • Squad Leadership & Execution: Lead, guide, and grow a high-performing squad of data and backend engineers. As Tech Lead you own outcomes end-to-end, including dependencies on other teams — success means features are live in production, not just tasks closed. You act as the technical reference point for other teams, remaining deeply hands-on by modeling AI-augmented development workflows and shipping production code every week.
  • Data Platform Architecture: Define and evolve the technical blueprints for our high-load data platform, designing robust ETL/ELT pipelines and real-time processing services in Go — the data platform derives from operational sources and never becomes the master.
  • Data Residency & Segregation: Architect storage and data flows that satisfy regulatory residency and segregation requirements, with auditability and retention built in.
  • Event-Driven Systems & Streaming: Design event-driven data flows and CDC pipelines from operational databases into the warehouse; we run NATS, PostgreSQL, and ClickHouse.
  • AI Tooling & Workflow Optimization: Integrate AI agents into the daily engineering workflow, configuring system rules, skills management, and prompt pipelines (Claude, Cursor) to elevate squad productivity.
  • Data Observability & Engineering Craft: Ensure data consistency, reliability, and observability across PostgreSQL and ClickHouse storage layers while mentoring engineers across Go and TypeScript stacks, meeting regulatory-grade auditability and data-segregation requirements.
