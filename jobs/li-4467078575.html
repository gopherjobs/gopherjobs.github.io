Description

SAVR is a fast-growing Swedish fintech company on a mission to reinvent investing. We empower investors with a platform that offers lower fees , greater transparency , and a radically improved experience .

We’re not just building another investment service - we’re reimagining how financial products should work for people, not just institutions. As part of our ambitious, innovative and curious team, you’ll help shape the future of investing in Sweden and beyond.

The Role

As an Engineering Manager, you will lead and coach a cross-functional team of 6–8 engineers across frontend, backend, and AI/ML. Your focus will be on building a high-trust, accountable environment where engineers take ownership of their work and grow their skills, while also contributing to technical initiatives and ensuring reliable delivery. You’ll balance people leadership with hands-on engagement, guiding outcomes and helping individuals grow.

Your Mission

  • Partner with your Product Manager to plan, scope, and deliver roadmap goals that drive customer and business impact.
  • Own selected technical projects, often with suppliers or partners, ensuring smooth execution from requirements to rollout.
  • Build a healthy, high-trust team by setting clear expectations, coaching individuals, and empowering engineers to take ownership of their growth and delivery.
  • Raise the team’s competence and practices by championing effective rituals, continuous learning, and strong communication.
  • Ensure system health and reliability through meaningful monitoring, observability, and post-mortem learning.
  • Promote engineering excellence by driving quality, CI/CD, and operational readiness before launch.
  • Act as a facilitator-leader - guiding trade-offs, unblocking decisions, and creating space for experts to excel.
  • Continuously improve how the team works by introducing better ways of working and helping the org adopt them.

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Co-own backlog, scope, and planning with the Product Manager.
  • Run effective execution rituals (standups, planning, reviews) with transparent risk management.
  • Lead feedback conversations, performance reviews, and growth plans.
  • Partner with the Tech Lead on architecture decisions and technical strategy; contribute code when it’s high leverage.
  • Coordinate work with suppliers/partners and manage integrations.
  • Oversee access management, compliance, and KPI/observability work.
  • Lead by example and contribute to the codebase, such as solving bugs or deliver on features.

Additionally, You Will

  • Promote good engineering practices and constructive code reviews.
  • Foster a learning culture that values feedback and continuous skill development.
  • Influence platform roadmap and tech stack evolution.
  • Balance short-term delivery with long-term sustainability and simplicity.

You’ll Thrive in This Role If You…

  • Multiple years of experience from software engineering.
  • Have experience leading cross-functional engineering teams in a product environment.
  • Can balance hands-on technical contribution with people leadership.
  • Are motivated by coaching, developing others, and raising the competence of your team.
  • Care deeply about reliable systems, observability, and safe, compliant delivery.
  • Demonstrate accountability, empathy, and a growth mindset.
  • Communicate clearly in English and/or Swedish.
  • Are eligible to work in Sweden (Swedish citizen or EU work permit) - we’re unable to offer visa sponsorship.
