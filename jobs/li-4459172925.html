Description

Your Next Chapter Starts Here

We're looking for Senior AI/ML Engineer to join our fast-moving, collaborative team and help build enterprise-level products for both local and global markets. You'll work closely with consultants, product owners, designers, QA, and developers across mobile, backend, and frontend to bring great digital products to life.

How you’ll contribute:

  • Design, develop, deploy, and maintain production-grade machine learning models and AI applications for business needs.
  • Build scalable data, feature, ML, and LLM pipelines from experimentation through production.
  • Develop clean, maintainable, testable, and production-ready Python code.
  • Deploy ML and AI solutions using Azure, AWS, and/or GCP.
  • Establish CI/CD pipelines, containerized deployments, model versioning, experiment tracking, reproducibility, and lifecycle management.
  • Build and optimize LLM applications, RAG architectures, document ingestion pipelines, vector search, and agentic workflows.
  • Implement model and LLM serving APIs, tool/function calling, structured outputs, and integrations with enterprise systems.
  • Develop prompt templates, prompt versioning, evaluation frameworks, and quality measures for LLM-based applications.
  • Implement monitoring, logging, tracing, observability, security controls, guardrails, and hallucination mitigation mechanisms.
  • Optimize models and AI applications for accuracy, performance, scalability, latency, cost, and operational reliability.
  • Troubleshoot production issues and continuously improve ML and AI systems.
  • Collaborate with data scientists, software engineers, cloud engineers, security teams, and business stakeholders.
  • Contribute to technical documentation, coding standards, reusable components, and engineering best practices.
  • Stay current with developments in machine learning, MLOps, generative AI, LLMs, agentic AI, and cloud technologies.

What makes you the one:

  • Bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in machine learning engineering, AI/ML, software engineering, data science, or a related field.
  • Proven experience taking machine learning solutions from experimentation through deployment and production operations.
  • Strong professional experience with Python and software development best practices.
  • Solid understanding of machine learning concepts, including model development, validation, feature engineering, evaluation, optimization, and lifecycle management.
  • Hands-on experience designing and implementing scalable data, ML, and MLOps pipelines.
  • Experience with model serving, REST APIs, microservices, databases, and enterprise application integrations.
  • Practical experience with at least one major cloud platform: Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  • Experience with Git, Docker, CI/CD, automated testing, and deployment automation.
  • Knowledge of model versioning, experiment tracking, reproducibility, monitoring, logging, observability, and production support.
  • Experience optimizing ML and AI solutions for performance, scalability, latency, cost, and reliability.
  • Practical experience developing LLM-based applications, RAG solutions, or agentic AI systems.
  • Familiarity with document ingestion, parsing, chunking, metadata management, embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, and information retrieval.
  • Experience with prompt engineering, prompt templates, prompt versioning, structured outputs, and LLM evaluation.
  • Knowledge of tool calling, function calling, workflow orchestration, and human-in-the-loop processes.
  • Experience with one or more LLM or AI frameworks, such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, or equivalent technologies.
  • Experience developing applications using one or more LLM providers or platforms, such as OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or open-source LLMs.
  • Understanding of LLM-specific risks, including hallucinations, prompt injection, data privacy, access control, security, and the need for appropriate guardrails.
  • Good command of English, with the ability to prepare technical documentation and communicate effectively with technical and business teams.
