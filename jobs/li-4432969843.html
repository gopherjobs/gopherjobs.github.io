Description

About The Opportunity Hi, I'm Oussama Salimi, the Engineering Manager at N26, and I am looking for a Backend Engineer to join the Investments team here at N26.

Why is this role exciting and why should you apply?

  • Democratize wealth: You will build a secure, real-time trading platform powering everything from Crypto and Stocks to ETFs and Managed Funds.
  • High-stakes engineering: Operate at the thrilling intersection of high-speed trading and strict financial regulation where markets move fast.
  • True autonomy: You are an active partner in shaping the product, not just a ticket taker. We trust you to be the owner of the delivery.
  • Get Stuff Done Days (GSDD): Every six weeks, take two days of dedicated innovation time to explore new tech stacks, fix nagging bugs, or prototype outside the roadmap.

In This Role, You Will

  • Write high-quality, performant code (primarily Kotlin) and navigate the codebase fluently.
  • Handle deployment, bug fixing, and incident handling with a high level of independence, taking features from concept to production.
  • Act as a sparring partner for Product Managers during Product Discovery, ensuring technical decisions support the user experience.
  • Question the status quo by asking "why are we building this?" and "is there a simpler way?"
  • Identify knowledge gaps and technical problems, taking active ownership to address them without waiting to be told.
  • Share your learnings from GSDD projects with the wider team, turning individual curiosity into collective knowledge.
  • There will be a lot more too, this is a role that will require a lot of flexibility.

What You Need To Be Successful

  • Proven experience as a Software Engineer taking features from concept to production with minimal supervision.
  • A track record of thriving in dynamic environments where requirements evolve rapidly.
  • A growth mindset: you treat feedback as a tool for engineering excellence and have a specific plan for your own learning.
  • An "evidence over opinion" approach: you couple every critique of a problem with a practical proposal for its solution.

Skills

  • Strong proficiency in backend development, with Kotlin being our default for new services.
  • Experience working with Distributed Microservices and Event-Driven Architecture (Kafka).
  • Good understanding of cloud infrastructure and containerization (AWS, Kubernetes, and Docker).
  • Experience with observability and monitoring tools (Datadog, ELK/OpenSearch).
  • Resilience and proactive communication: you know how to navigate unresponsive stakeholders and unblock yourself.
  • Constructive dissatisfaction: you are a "System Improver" who proactively fixes broken processes.

Interview Process And What To Expect

  • Initial Recruiter Screen
  • Codility Test
  • Pair Coding
  • Systems Design
  • Behavioural Interview

What’s In It For You

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors 🚀.
  • Employee benefits that range from a competitive personal development budget, work from home budget, discounts to fitness & wellness memberships, language apps and public transportation.
  • As an N26 employee you will have access to a Premium subscription on your personal N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members.
  • Additional day of annual leave for each year of service.
  • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, life experiences and family statuses.
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it.
