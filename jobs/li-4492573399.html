Company Description

Synapflo is an AI-native, modular CRM and ERP platform that helps businesses turn conversations into long-lasting customer relationships. We automate sales, admin and customer service conversations across WhatsApp, SMS, LinkedIn, email and website chat, so small and mid-sized businesses can work faster and more accurately without losing the personal touch, with the end goal for clients being payment-in-chat or a booked call or meeting. Our platform includes intelligent lead scoring, live agent hand-off, conversation maturation, personalised outreach, smart inventory, knowledge bases and analytics, all built to get the most out of every interaction. Our mission is to make enterprise-level sales performance accessible to every business: we handle the repetitive work so teams can focus on growth.

Role Description & Responsibilities

We are hiring a Senior Full Stack Engineer (remote, flexible hours) to join Synapflo at the founding stage. You will work directly with our C-suite, product managers and engineering team on the front-end and back-end of Synapflo's unified platform, and help take the product through the final stretch of MVP to launch. This role is unpaid at the outset as we are currently bootstrapped, however once Synapflo secures funding or sufficient revenue, it will convert to a salaried full-time position, with the package based on your contribution and performance. Equity and ESOP can be up for discussion after a 3 month probation period.

What you'll do:

• Build and maintain scalable web applications across front-end and back-end

• Improve user interfaces and the overall product experience

• Turn business requirements into technical solutions

• Bring in the latest tools and technologies where they add real value

• Work with the team to ship reliable, well-integrated features that make SME teams more efficient

Qualifications

• 2+ years of full stack development experience (CRM/ERP experience preferred, but not required)

• Strong back-end development skills in FastAPI and Python

• Front-end experience with Next.js and javascript

• Familiarity with full-stack development practices and DevOps

• Strong grasp of data structures and algorithms (assessed during the technical interview process)

• Experience with background jobs (Celery), containerisation, and deployment

• Strong problem-solving skills, time management and work ethic, and an interest of keeping up with new technologies

• Comfortable working collaboratively in a remote team

Bonus:

• Experience with Meta Business Suite or Meta for Developers

• Experience with Kubernetes and Terraform

• international applications are welcomed, however applicants residing in Singapore will be prioritised
