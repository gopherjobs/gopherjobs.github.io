Company Description It all started when engineer Fred Luddy wrote code that automated a tedious task for his coworker, Phyllis. She cried tears of joy. That moment inspired Fred to build a company that could do that for everyone—freeing people from busywork so they could focus on meaningful work. Today, ServiceNow is the AI control tower for business reinvention. Our ServiceNow AI platform brings together any AI, any data, and any workflow— helping 85% of the Fortune 500® work smarter, faster, and better. We're building an AI-native culture where technology and talent are unstoppable together. And we're just getting started.

Join us to put AI to work for people.

Job Description What you get to do in this role:

  • You will build and operate components of our Kubernetes platform and its core subsystems, taking ownership of well-defined pieces as you grow into broader areas.
  • You will write a lot of Go (controllers, operators, platform services, and the automation around them) and take part in code review with the team.
  • You will take well-scoped features and fixes from design through to production, working with senior engineers on direction and design choices.
  • You will help operate what the team builds: on-call, incident investigation, and contributing to the follow-up work that stops the same failure twice.
  • You will work with the teams that run workloads on the platform, helping them get the most out of it.
  • You will work on a platform that serves regulated markets, where compliance constraints including FedRAMP shape design choices.

Qualifications To be successful in this role you have:

  • Experience using or thinking critically about AI in engineering work, such as AI-powered coding and operational tooling, workflow automation, or how AI changes the way software and infrastructure are built.
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes in production, including deploying workloads and debugging them when they misbehave. Contributions to platform components, tooling, or operators are a strong plus.
  • 2+ years building production software.
  • Programming skills in Go, or solid skills in another systems language and a clear willingness to work primarily in Go.
  • Familiarity with at least one major hyperscaler (AWS, Azure, GCP) and its core compute, networking, and IAM concepts.
  • Working knowledge of containers, CI/CD and GitOps-based delivery, and infrastructure-as-code.
  • Curiosity about how AI changes platform engineering, whether through AI-powered tooling, automating operational work, or rethinking how infrastructure is built and run.

It Also Helps If You Have

  • Exposure to Kubernetes controllers or operators and the operator pattern.
  • Exposure to container networking (CNI), service mesh, or workload identity and mTLS.
  • Exposure to secrets management, certificate lifecycle, or similar core platform subsystems.
  • Experience with observability tooling, including metrics, tracing, and SLOs.
  • Experience with managed Kubernetes (EKS/AKS/GKE), Terraform, or Crossplane.
