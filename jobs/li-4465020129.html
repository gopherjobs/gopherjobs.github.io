Description

We’re looking for a Full-Stack Engineer with focus on frontend to join us at Telia.

I’m Alexander Angerbjörn, Head of ACE Product Engineering Clients , and I’m searching for someone ready to grow with us. We’re a supportive, forward-thinking team focused on developing Telia ACE, a market-leading contact center platform for public and private sector customers.

What you’ll do: You’ll develop and maintain complex web clients, with a strong focus on real-time communication and security.

  • Collaborate within a Scrum team and with other stakeholders, such as architects and site reliability engineers.
  • Develop and maintain complex web clients with a focus on real-time communication and security.
  • Contribute to automation, observability, and deployment using tools such as GitHub Actions, Grafana, Docker, and Kubernetes.
  • Participate in agile development practices, continuously improving processes and delivering value to customers across the public and private sectors.

What will help you thrive in this role: You are bold, curious, and collaborative, ready to challenge the status quo and actively suggest improvements.

  • Proven experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue.js, and other web technologies, combined with a strong interest in secure and complex web-based systems.
  • Experience working in agile teams within large organizations, particularly on complex, long-term product development.
  • A DevSecOps approach, strong communication skills, and the ability to collaborate flexibly within a team.
  • Openness to learning new domains and technologies, with bonus experience in Rust, Qlik, Erlang, CI/CD, and observability.
