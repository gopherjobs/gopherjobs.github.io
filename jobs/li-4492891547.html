About the Role We are seeking a hands-on Technical Program Manager (TPM) / Forward Deployed Consultant to lead end-to-end technical delivery for enterprise banking and fintech clients. Operating embedded on-site, you will combine client-facing program ownership with deep technical execution—bridging Visa, Featurespace, client engineering teams, and third-party vendors from Proof of Concept (POC) through full-scale production.

What You’ll Do

  • Hands-On Featurespace Delivery: Drive multi-phase technical deployments—actively participating in system onboarding, platform configuration, model tuning, technical issue triage, and go-live support alongside Featurespace and client engineering teams.
  • Card Authorization TOM & Regional Rollouts: Define and document target-state technical and operating models, run technical market-readiness checks, and lead rollouts across four APAC markets (Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and Vietnam).
  • Embedded Client Governance: Own end-to-end scope, technical dependencies, milestones, and risk mitigation. Serve as the primary technical escalation and coordination point for the client, maintaining a high-touch engagement rhythm.
  • Engineering & Delivery Cadence: Lead weekly technical syncs with regional markets, build repeatable delivery playbooks, and translate complex technical progress into clear executive updates.

What We’re Looking For

  • 8+ years of experience in Forward Deployed Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Technical Consulting, or hands-on Technical Program Management within payments, fintech, banking, or financial crime technology.
  • Strong Technical Depth: Hands-on familiarity with payment/card authorization lifecycles, system integrations, data/model tuning, and fraud or AML technology stacks.
  • Builder + Orchestrator Mindset: Proven track record managing complex, multi-vendor technical programs while remaining close enough to the tech to unblock engineering and configuration bottlenecks.
  • Client Leadership: Exceptional stakeholder management, technical communication, and executive reporting skills.
