Description

About the Role

We are looking for a Full Stack Technical Lead to resolve production issues on a live, high-traffic hiring product. You will pair deep technical ownership across Java, Python, and React with the ability to lead and mentor engineers through ambiguity. This role blends hands-on engineering with real client impact, keeping complex systems reliable under pressure.

What you will do

  • Analyze and resolve production issues, with particular emphasis on ongoing, in-progress hiring events.
  • Partner closely with client-facing teams to triage and address incoming support tickets.
  • Drive timely issue resolution to meet established SLAs.
  • Monitor production systems and respond quickly to alerts or performance degradation ("going red").
  • Support architecture changes and system integrations that may impact hiring events.
  • Maintain and update event logic in response to changes in dependencies and upstream systems.

Must haves

  • 7+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Java/Spring Boot as the primary backend stack (Event Management Backend, Lobby Backend) — first priority.
  • Django/Python as a secondary backend stack (Event Management Backend, Lobby Backend) — second priority.
  • React for frontend development (Event Management Frontend, Lobby Frontend, IRL Frontend, Jobseeker Frontend).
  • Kafka (Event Management Backend).
  • GraphQL (Event Management Backend, Lobby Backend).
  • SQL or MongoDB (Event Management Backend, Lobby Backend).
  • Build and deployment experience.
  • Experience with at least one of the following: Dagster, Data Lakes, or Amazon Athena .
  • Senior-level engineer able to ramp up quickly and assume ownership across multiple concurrent projects.
  • High level of ownership and autonomy, including taking initiative and owning tasks—specifically merging and deploying code—without requiring hand-holding.
  • Ability to work independently and autonomously.
  • Technical leadership experience , including the ability to lead, shepherd, and onboard other engineers on the team's behalf (senior engineering experience alone is not sufficient for this Tech Lead role).
  • Demonstrated experience overseeing or coordinating the work of others.
  • Clear and concise communication.
  • Ability to manage and navigate ambiguity.
  • Prioritization and strategic thinking.
  • Ability to use data to troubleshoot production issues.
  • Upper-intermediate English level.

Nice to haves

  • Product management experience partnering with GTM teams and engineers to support prioritization.
  • Experience with AI-assisted development tools such as Claude, Cursor, or Windsurf.

Perks

  • Growth without limits: build your skills through mentorship, internal TechTalks, challenging projects, and a dedicated annual learning budget
  • Competitive compensation: get recognition that reflects your skills and impact, with regular performance and compensation reviews
  • Flexibility: work 100% remotely with flexible hours that support focus, autonomy, and a healthy work rhythm
  • Meaningful, modern projects: build impactful products using modern technologies alongside global teams and leading brands
  • Collaborative culture: join a supportive environment with zero micromanagement where ideas are welcomed and contributions are recognized
  • Well-being & support: access local well-being programs and people-focused support tailored to your location
