Description

In a nutshell As a developer working with Salesforce and Python, you'll join the Master Data team. We own the product data everything else runs on — from integrating supplier catalogues, through enrichment and validation, to what a customer sees in the app and what the warehouse needs to plan how a product fits in a tote. Hundreds of colleagues work with the platform every day, handling large volumes of master data across multiple countries, and your job is to make that easier — a validation that catches a bad value before it reaches a customer, a screen that stops taking eight clicks to do one thing. You'll solve data issues and steadily improve the platform. Over time the role grows towards software engineering, deeper into Python and data platform architecture.

Tricks of the trade

  • Ownership: Take ownership of a part of the platform, know it well, and grow into the person others come to for it. You'll propose improvements rather than waiting for them to be assigned, and you try things and iterate quickly
  • Maintain and extend: Design, test, evolve, and refine the core aspects of our master data platform with a creative and analytical approach. Keep it healthy by improving existing flows and integrations while reducing complexity
  • Data modeling and quality: Shape how product information is modeled, and build the validation, enrichment and monitoring that keeps it accurate
  • Work with your users: Build relationships with the operational teams who work daily with the platform, understand their pain points, and turn those into improvements
  • Code Review: Review designs and code of teammates
  • Support: Support existing products and work with other developers to resolve incidents, including occasional on-call shifts

Technologies we use

  • Salesforce: Apex, LWCs, RESTful integrations, Salesforce Connect
  • Deployments: Salesforce DX & Unlocked Packages
  • IDEs: VSCode, Intellij; scratch orgs
  • Python: FastAPI, Pydantic
  • Data: SQL, Snowflake, dbt
  • Version Control: Git, Github
  • CI/CD: TeamCity

We are looking for

  • Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related quantitative field
  • 2+ years of experience with Salesforce implementations including Apex
  • Curious and detail-oriented: you pick things up fast, including technologies new to you, like Python or dbt, and you have the patience to chase an inconsistency until you understand where it came from. Using AI tools effectively in your daily work is good evidence of both
  • Genuinely interested in data and the people who depend on it — models, quality, what happens downstream
  • Solid judgement on tradeoffs between configuration and custom code
  • Applies software engineering best practices to Apex/LWC development e.g. triggers, async apex, testing, access and data lifecycle management
  • English skills are on point (no Dutch required)

Bonus Points!

  • Good command of Python/Java
  • Experience with master data, product data management or data engineering
  • Understanding of DX and unlocked packages
  • Experience with sharing and security setups of Salesforce
  • Salesforce Certifications
