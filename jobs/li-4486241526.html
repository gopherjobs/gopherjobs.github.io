At Capitole, we’re looking for a Backstage Engineer to join an international project for a leading German company in the automotive sector.

We’re looking for someone with strong hands-on Backstage experience who enjoys building, integrating and evolving developer platforms in a large-scale enterprise environment.

What you’ll be doing

  • Develop and enhance features and UI components within Backstage .
  • Build and maintain custom Backstage plugins and integrations with external systems.
  • Work with TypeScript, JavaScript and React to deliver scalable and reusable solutions.
  • Design and implement integrations through REST APIs and asynchronous processes.
  • Contribute to the architecture, scalability and continuous evolution of the platform.
  • Troubleshoot complex technical issues and help improve platform quality, security and reliability.
  • Collaborate with engineering, UX and other stakeholders across an international environment.

What we’re looking for

  • Around 5+ years of experience in software development .
  • Strong hands-on experience with Backstage , including plugin development, integrations or platform customization.
  • Strong knowledge of TypeScript and JavaScript .
  • Experience working with REST APIs and system integrations.
  • Good knowledge of React .
  • Experience with AWS .
  • Good understanding of system architecture and experience working in enterprise IT environments .

Nice to have

  • Experience with Node.js , which will be highly valued.
  • Experience with Kubernetes and AWS Lambda .
  • Experience integrating tools such as SonarQube or other quality, security and monitoring solutions.
  • Experience contributing to Backstage architecture or implementations from scratch.
