Job Overview

We are looking for an enthusiastic and skilled System Development Engineer to join our team and play a critical role in developing, improving, and maintaining software applications that support Eutelsat/OneWeb Satellite Operations Center (SOC) operations. In this position, you'll work closely with software engineers, spacecraft engineers, systems engineers, and operations teams to support mission-critical satellite operations throughout the satellite lifecycle, from launch through decommissioning. This is a unique opportunity to make a significant impact within an organization that values growth, teamwork, and professional development.

Who You Are:

You are an aerospace, astronautical, or spacecraft systems engineer who wants to leverage software to solve challenging operational problems. Whether your background is in mission operations, flight dynamics, spacecraft engineering, mission planning, or satellite ground systems, you are eager to develop and apply software solutions that improve the reliability, automation, and scalability of satellite operations.

You may already have professional software development experience, or you may be looking to expand your software engineering skills while remaining deeply involved in satellite missions and operations. We also welcome software engineers who have a passion for space systems and are excited to learn the operational aspects of managing a global GEO and LEO satellite fleet.

You are collaborative, curious, and comfortable taking ownership in a multidisciplinary environment where software and spacecraft operations come together to support missions from launch through decommissioning.

What You’ll Do:

  • Develop and maintain software applications supporting the Satellite Operations Center (SOC).
  • Design and implement ground system solutions using middleware technologies within virtualized environments.
  • Develop operational automation and orchestration capabilities for satellite operations.
  • Contribute to software prototyping, integration, testing, and operational engineering activities.
  • Perform software system integration and testing involving complex software and hardware subsystems.
  • Develop maintainable and testable software components following engineering best practices and coding standards.
  • Support continuous improvement of software quality, maintainability, testing, and engineering processes.
  • Collaborate with software, systems, spacecraft, and operations engineers throughout the satellite lifecycle.

What You’ll Need:

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or a related technical field.
  • 3+ years of experience in satellite systems, satellite ground systems software, or satellite operations.
  • 3+ years of software development experience using Python, Java, Go, C, or C++.
  • Experience designing and building automated systems.
  • Knowledge of software engineering principles including modularity, maintainability, and testability.
  • Experience with software testing, including unit testing, integration testing, and test automation.
  • Experience developing REST APIs and web services using technologies such as Python, FastAPI, and MongoDB.
  • Experience working in Linux/Unix environments.
  • Experience with relational and non-relational databases.
  • Knowledge of version control and configuration management practices.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with multi-satellite LEO constellations.
  • Experience with Docker, Git, Jira, and Confluence.
  • Experience with satellite or ground system integration and testing.
  • Experience improving CI/CD pipelines and software delivery processes.
  • Familiarity with Generative AI-assisted software development tools and practices.
