Description

About the Company

At Fortil, we bring together engineering expertise, technological innovation and talented people to develop solutions with real-world impact. With more than 2,500 employees across 14 countries, we offer an international environment where autonomy, collaboration and continuous learning are part of everyday work.

About the Role

We are looking for a Cloud Automation Developer the role combines software development, Microsoft Azure, cloud automation and platform engineering to deliver secure and reusable products that simplify the provisioning, configuration, governance and lifecycle management of cloud resources.

The role is remote, once per month in the office

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop reusable Terraform modules, templates and automation workflows for provisioning and managing Azure resources.
  • Build full-stack self-service applications that simplify cloud consumption and recurring operational activities.
  • Develop and maintain backend services and REST APIs.
  • Create frontend applications using JavaScript or TypeScript and frameworks such as React, Angular or Vue.
  • Integrate Azure services with platforms such as GitHub, ServiceNow and identity management services.
  • Design and implement CI/CD workflows for validating, packaging and deploying infrastructure and software changes.
  • Create automated functional, regression and deployment tests for applications, APIs, infrastructure modules and cloud services.
  • Integrate security and governance requirements into cloud delivery patterns, including access management, policy enforcement, secrets handling and auditability.
  • Build monitoring, logging and alerting capabilities into delivered solutions.
  • Produce technical documentation, implementation guidance and operational handover materials.
  • Provide operational support for data processing and analytics service automation.
  • Continuously improve cloud services to increase reliability, performance, security and operational efficiency.
  • Collaborate with application, platform, security and infrastructure teams in an international environment.

Qualifications & Required Skills

  • At least 5 years of relevant professional experience, ideally within an international company or global technical environment.
  • Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Azure and public-cloud architecture.
  • Strong experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
  • Advanced full-stack development skills, covering both backend services and frontend applications.
  • Experience developing backend solutions using Java, Spring and REST APIs.
  • Experience with JavaScript or TypeScript and at least one modern frontend framework, such as React, Angular or Vue.
  • Strong understanding of CI/CD and DevOps workflows using GitHub Actions or an equivalent platform.
  • Experience with cloud security, identity and governance principles.
  • Experience integrating cloud platforms, APIs, identity services and third-party enterprise systems.
  • Knowledge of automated testing and quality engineering practices.
  • Advanced scripting capabilities using PowerShell or an equivalent scripting language.
  • Experience implementing monitoring, logging and production-readiness capabilities.
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • A collaborative, autonomous and results-oriented mindset.
