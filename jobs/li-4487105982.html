About the client

A global provider of information, software and services for professionals in regulated, enterprise B2B industries. With engineering teams across India, the US and Europe, the organization is investing in a shared AI platform that other teams build on, and is raising its delivery standard by making AI-assisted development the default way of working. It is an agile, collaborative environment where engineers own outcomes end-to-end and influence across teams.

About the role

As Principal AI Platform Engineer, you will be the most senior individual-contributor engineer on the Platform Engineering Team. You will set the technical direction for the organization's AI platform and design the production-grade Python services, Web APIs and SDKs that other engineering teams build on. You will also raise the delivery bar by defining how AI-assisted development (e.g., GitHub Copilot) is used across the team. You lead through architecture, standards and influence, not headcount. This is a contract-to-hire engagement.

Responsibilities

  • Own the AI platform architecture end-to-end: target design, technology selection and migration path.
  • Build secure, scalable Python Web APIs (e.g., FastAPI) with authentication, rate limiting and observability.
  • Design, version and publish Python SDKs used by multiple teams, with semantic versioning, deprecation policy and documentation.
  • Set the standard for AI-assisted development: tooling, review practices, guardrails and measured productivity gains.
  • Build, deploy and operate applications on Azure and/or AWS using infrastructure-as-code.
  • Develop LLM and RAG solutions, including prompt design, evaluation frameworks and guardrails.
  • Define testing standards (unit, integration, contract, load) and enforceable quality gates in CI.
  • Instrument services with logging, metrics and tracing; remediate bottlenecks; lead incident reviews.
  • Partner with product, data, security and platform leadership on roadmap, requirements and SLAs/SLOs.
  • Mentor senior and lead engineers and act as technical reviewer of record for significant designs.

Must have skills

  • 12+ years in software development, 8+ of them in production Python.
  • Principal/Staff-level scope: owned the architecture of a platform, service family or SDK used by multiple teams.
  • 6+ years building Web APIs at scale (FastAPI, Django or Flask), including OpenAPI and contract-first design.
  • 4+ years on Azure and/or AWS (Functions, App Service, AKS, Lambda, ECS/EKS) with infrastructure-as-code; Azure preferred.
  • 3+ years of production GenAI: prompt engineering, LLM integration, RAG and evaluation frameworks you designed.
  • AI Agent Development experience.
  • Daily use of AI coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code), with concrete productivity gains and team rollout.
  • 4+ years designing, versioning and distributing SDKs (PyPI or internal registry), including managing breaking changes.
  • 4+ years with CI/CD (Azure DevOps and/or GitHub Actions), pytest and ruff/black/mypy.
  • Enterprise security knowledge: OAuth2/OIDC, JWT, secrets management.
  • Observability with OpenTelemetry, Azure Monitor or CloudWatch, including SLIs/SLOs.
  • 5+ years of SQL and NoSQL data design, plus hands-on vector databases (Azure AI Search, OpenSearch, Pinecone).
  • Elite Python engineer who builds resilient, scalable systems and APIs.
  • Platform architect who reasons about backwards compatibility, versioning, blast radius and total cost of ownership.
  • Quality-driven and execution-centric: well-tested code, automation and fast delivery to production.
  • Fluent professional English, able to explain trade-offs to senior leadership.
  • Influences without authority; comfortable with ambiguity; pragmatic about speed versus technical debt.
  • Based in India, the US or Europe and legally authorized to work there.
  • Willing and able to convert to a direct full-time employee after the 12-month contract; contract-only candidates are not a fit.
  • Willing to relocate to an office on conversion: India (Pune or Chennai), US (Houston, Minneapolis or Atlanta) or Europe (Amsterdam, Netherlands or Bari, Italy). Non-negotiable.

Nice to have skills

  • Experience in regulated B2B information services (legal, tax, healthcare, finance, compliance).
  • Agentic/multi-agent frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel) and MCP in production.
  • Docker and Kubernetes/AKS at scale; multi-region and high-availability design.
  • LLM evaluation and observability tooling (LLM-as-a-judge, tracing, cost/latency optimization).
  • Open-source contributions, patents, publications or conference talks.
  • AWS and GitHub Actions experience.
  • Technical degree from a top-tier Indian institution (IIT preferred; NIT, BITS, IIIT also of interest); a preference, not a gate.
  • Experience at FAANG/Big Tech or comparable high-scale product companies; a preference, not a gate.
