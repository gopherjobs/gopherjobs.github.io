Description

Job Description ConvAI is the specialist voice AI and conversational customer experience unit within Probe Group. We engineer modern enterprise solutions—including our flagship platform, Oration , and an advanced LLM Insights analytics engine—replacing rigid call menus with natural, real-time voice and digital experiences.

The Role We are seeking an experienced Full-Stack Software Engineer to join our ~20-person product engineering team and help scale Oration across New Zealand and Australia. This is a core software engineering position focused on end-to-end product delivery (~70% backend, ~30% frontend) rather than a specialist AI role; prior commercial experience with LLMs or machine learning is not required.

You will work hands-on with Node.js, TypeScript, React, and AWS to deliver new features, bring long-term engineering structure to a fast-built codebase, and build reliable software within a collaborative, remote-first environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Full-Stack Feature Delivery: Design, build, test, and deploy new features and bug fixes across our real-time voice (IVR) and digital chat applications.
  • Codebase Refactoring & Scaling: Introduce sustainable architecture, performance optimisations, and automated testing to a codebase originally built for rapid prototyping.
  • API & Service Integrations: Build resilient REST APIs, database models, and third-party integrations connecting our Node.js / TypeScript backend, React UI, telephony workflows, and Model-as-a-Service (MaaS) APIs.
  • Collaborative Engineering: Participate in peer code reviews, maintain clear technical documentation, and communicate proactively with fellow engineers and internal stakeholders in a remote setting.

Requirements (Must-Haves)

  • 5+ years of commercial software engineering experience in a disciplined development environment.
  • Production depth across our core stack: Proven hands-on experience with Node.js and TypeScript on the backend, React on the frontend, and AWS cloud infrastructure.
  • Strong Engineering Fundamentals: Solid command of relational/NoSQL databases, REST API design, Git workflows, and automated testing.
  • Problem-Solving & Eligibility: Strong lateral thinking, ownership, and proactive communication skills.

Nice To Have (Desirable, Not Essential)

  • Telephony & Real-Time Comms: Experience with IVR, contact-centre platforms, or voice/messaging APIs (e.g., Twilio, Amazon Connect, Genesys, SIP, WebRTC, WebSockets).
  • Applied AI & Speech Tech: Exposure to integrating LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Azure OpenAI), prompt engineering, speech recognition, or RAG pipelines.
  • Legacy Modernisation: Track record of refactoring and modernising fast-built or legacy production applications.

Why Join ConvAI?

  • Supportive, "No-Blame" Culture: Join a tight-knit team of ~20 engineers where mistakes are treated as learning opportunities and open collaboration is the norm.
  • Production AI Exposure: Gain practical experience integrating frontier LLMs and speech technologies into live enterprise products without requiring AI expertise on day one.
  • Remote-First Flexibility & Enterprise Backing: Work remotely across New Zealand or Australia—with optional Thursday team catch-ups at our Ellerslie office in Auckland—backed by the stability and career pathways of Probe Group.
