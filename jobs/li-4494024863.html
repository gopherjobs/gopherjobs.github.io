Description

Lead the Payments tribe of a real-time iGaming platform serving 150M+ players worldwide, and own the reliability, security, and growth of every payment and payout flow. Who We Are At Patrianna, we build a high-performance B2B platform for the global iGaming industry, enabling partners to launch and scale digital gaming products across multiple international markets. Our platform serves as the foundation for multiple gaming brands, providing the core systems that power gameplay, integrations, and platform capabilities across different markets.

"Payments" sits at the core of that platform. Every brand depends on our ability to take payments reliably, pay out redemptions and withdrawals on time, connect new payment methods quickly, and protect players and partners from fraud and abuse.

We operate in a fast-moving scale-up environment where impact matters more than process for its own sake. Teams are trusted to take ownership of the systems they build and the outcomes they deliver. We believe strong engineering leadership starts with technical credibility and ownership of outcomes.

AI-Driven Engineering Culture We are actively building an AI-driven engineering organization with a clear and deliberate stance: artificial intelligence is a core part of how we build and deliver software, not an experiment, not a trend, and not an optional add-on.

AI is embedded into our engineering workflows, from system design and prototyping to refactoring, testing, and code review. We expect engineers to be fully comfortable working in AI-driven environments and to treat AI as a primary productivity multiplier. Strong engineering judgment, architectural ownership, and accountability remain essential: AI amplifies our engineers, and it does not replace responsibility for technical decisions.

About The Role We are looking for an Engineering Manager to lead our Payments tribe . This role combines strong technical expertise with the ability to lead several teams toward clear business outcomes.

You will lead three cross-functional squads of backend, frontend, mobile, and QA engineers (20+ engineers), supported by Team Leads who coordinate day-to-day execution within their squads.

Payments is revenue-critical and security-sensitive. Mistakes cost money and player trust, so this role needs a leader who understands how payment systems fail, who can reason about architecture and technical trade-offs, and who holds the teams to a high bar for correctness, reliability, and security.

During onboarding, you will work closely with engineers to learn the payment architecture, the main payment providers, and the development workflows. This technical immersion builds the system understanding you need to guide the tribe effectively.

Engineering Managers at Patrianna are accountable for the overall performance and outcomes of their teams: delivery reliability, engineering quality, team health, and the professional growth of their engineers.

What The Payments Tribe Owns The Payments tribe owns the money-in and money-out flows for every brand on the platform, across web and mobile. Its work covers:

  • Payment orchestration: a payment orchestration platform plus direct provider integrations, with routing rules, volume allocation, and fallback to alternative providers when a payment fails;
  • Payment methods: cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, pay-by-bank, ACH, and e-wallets;
  • Payouts: redemptions and withdrawals through ACH, push-to-debit, and e-wallet providers, with limits, approval queues, and manual review where needed;
  • Risk and verification: 3DS2, payment method verification, and KYC and fraud checks, built together with the Fraud team;
  • Money correctness: transaction state management, refunds, chargebacks, multicurrency processing, and reconciliation with providers;
  • Operations tooling: the back-office tools that payments operations teams use every day.

What You Will Do

  • Own delivery outcomes: make sure the Payments squads deliver reliable systems and meet commitments by coordinating priorities and execution across squads and with dependent tribes;
  • Own people management: run performance reviews, support career development, give compensation and promotion input, take part in hiring, and handle performance concerns when needed;
  • Own payment provider integrations: lead the integration of new payment providers and payment methods so that brands can launch in new markets on time, and act as the senior engineering contact for providers during technical escalations;
  • Safeguard payment reliability: lead incident response for payment and payout flows, run root cause analysis, and drive improvements in approval rates, payout processing times, and reconciliation accuracy;
  • Protect payment security and compliance: keep exposure of payment data to a minimum, drive security hardening and API abuse prevention, and work with the Security, Fraud, and Compliance teams on controls and audits;
  • Maintain strong engineering standards: make sure the squads apply idempotency, safe retries, clear transaction state models, auditability, and consistency in distributed transactions;
  • Enable effective execution: work with Team Leads and Product Managers so that payment requirements are clear, risks are visible, and squads can execute without unnecessary friction;
  • Improve engineering effectiveness: refine how the tribe plans, coordinates, and releases work as the platform and the number of brands grow;
  • Communicate with stakeholders: report roadmap progress, payment metrics, constraints, and risks to product, operations, finance, and leadership.

What We Are Looking For

  • Money-movement experience: you have built, operated, or led teams that own systems where money correctness matters, such as payments, payouts, wallets, billing, ledgers, or banking;
  • Payment integration experience: you have delivered integrations with payment service providers, acquirers, payment orchestration platforms, or payout providers, and you understand how they fail in production;
  • Engineering leadership experience: a proven track record of leading engineering teams (15–25 engineers, ideally across several teams through Team Leads) and delivering complex production systems while balancing technical leadership and people management;
  • Production operations: experience leading incident response and post-incident improvements for revenue-critical systems;
  • Security mindset: a solid understanding of how to handle sensitive payment and personal data and how to protect payment APIs from abuse;
  • Strong product sense: you can look at a payment initiative and see what is missing, what is risky, and what the engineering implications are;
  • Strong delivery ownership: you produce realistic estimates, align stakeholders around achievable timelines, and communicate scope options, trade-offs, and risks clearly;
  • Strong English communication skills: clear communication in technical, cross-functional, and external vendor discussions.
