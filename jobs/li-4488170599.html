Description

As a Forward Deployed Engineer, you are the link between AI capability and real business outcomes. You work directly with internal teams, learn how their work actually gets done, and build and ship AI-powered solutions that solve their highest-value problems. You are equally comfortable in a stakeholder workshop and in a code editor: scoping a problem, prototyping a solution, and shepherding it through to production.

This role spans the full lifecycle of delivery, including discovery, design, development, deployment, and ongoing iteration. You measure success not by features shipped, but by problems solved and time given back to the business. This is a remote role.

Principal Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Partner with business teams. Work directly with internal stakeholders to understand their workflows, pain points, and goals, and translate them into well-scoped AI opportunities.
  • Build and ship solutions. Design, prototype, and deploy AI-powered applications, integrations, and automations, from retrieval-augmented assistants to workflow agents, that run reliably in production.
  • Own the full lifecycle. Take solutions from first conversation through prototype, pilot, rollout, and ongoing iteration, owning quality, reliability, and adoption along the way.
  • Integrate with enterprise systems. Connect AI solutions to internal data sources and platforms (for example data warehouses, document stores, ERP/CRM, ticketing, and identity systems) in a secure, compliant way.
  • Develop agents and reusable skills. Build autonomous and assistive AI agents, and package common capabilities into reusable skills, tools, and components that other teams and engineers can readily adopt.
  • Make adoption stick. Drive real usage through onboarding, documentation, and hands-on enablement, and track impact against clear business metrics.
  • Close the loop with the platform team. Feed patterns, reusable components, and field learnings back into the team's shared AI platform and standards so each deployment is faster than the last.
  • Champion responsible AI. Apply the organisation's security, privacy, and governance standards to everything you build, and help stakeholders use AI safely and effectively.

Expected areas of Competence:

Proficiency in the following tools and systems:

  • Programming languages:
  • Python
  • JavaScript/TypeScript or a comparable language
  • SQL
  • LLM frameworks and tooling, such as:
  • AI Orchestrators (for example LangChain, LangGraph, Deep Agent, Microsoft Agent Framework)
  • Vector databases (for example Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector, or Azure AI Search)
  • Prompt engineering, Skill development
  • Cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP) and AI services such as Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or Google Vertex AI;
  • APIs and integration with enterprise systems (for example ERP/CRM, document stores, ticketing, and identity);
  • Git and CI/CD pipeline tooling;
  • Containerisation and deployment tooling (for example Docker and Kubernetes);

Competency in the following areas:

  • 10+ years experience
  • Building and shipping end-to-end, production-grade applications rather than isolated scripts;
  • Applying LLMs to real problems through prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, agents, or fine-tuning;
  • Evaluation and observability for LLM systems;
  • Sound judgement around data handling, security, and privacy in an enterprise environment;

Experience/competency in the following areas:

  • Building and shipping production software in a professional engineering role.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder skills: able to run a discovery conversation with a non-technical business team and explain trade-offs clearly.
  • A bias for action and ownership: comfortable with ambiguity, able to scope and prioritise, and motivated by delivering measurable outcomes.
  • Hands-on application of AI/ML or LLMs to real-world problems.

Some experience in the following areas is beneficial, but not essential:

  • An internal platform, solutions engineering, consulting, or forward-deployed role.
  • Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and modern data tooling.
  • Designing agentic systems and building reusable skills, tools, or libraries that other engineers adopt.
  • Enterprise governance, compliance, or regulated environments.
  • Front-end or full-stack development for building lightweight internal tools and interfaces.
