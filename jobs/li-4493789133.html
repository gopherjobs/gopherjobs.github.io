Job Title: Application Architect with Gen AI and Cloud

Location: Remote (Travel to London when required)

Contract with (Inside IR35)

Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced Application / Solution Architect to support the exploration and delivery of innovative AI and Generative AI (GenAI) solutions that address key business challenges.

The successful candidate will work closely with business and technical stakeholders to understand business requirements, identify opportunities for AI and GenAI adoption, and design secure, scalable, and effective technical solutions.

The role involves supporting the transition of GenAI proof of concepts into scalable MVPs, contributing to the technical architecture and ensuring solutions align with organisational security, governance, and compliance standards.

You will be involved in an initial discovery and Alpha phase, undertaking activities such as:

  • Understanding existing business processes and user journeys.
  • Identifying business challenges, pain points and user needs.
  • Conducting technology horizon scanning.
  • Performing gap analysis and assessing existing technical capabilities.
  • Identifying potential AI and GenAI solutions.
  • Supporting the development of solution architecture and technical roadmaps.
  • Working with delivery managers and agile teams to support technical delivery.

Required Skills & Experience

  • Experience working within the public sector is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of application and solution architecture concepts.
  • In-depth knowledge of AWS and Microsoft Azure cloud platforms.
  • Strong experience with Kubernetes, particularly AKS and EKS.
  • Knowledge of AI and Generative AI services and technologies.
  • Experience designing secure, scalable and resilient solutions.
  • Understanding of cloud-native architecture and microservices.
  • Experience with technical systems analysis and solution assessment.
  • Understanding of architecture governance, security and compliance.
  • Experience working in Agile delivery environments.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
