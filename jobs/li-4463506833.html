C++ Software Developer at BairesDev

As a C++ Software Developer, you will build and maintain safety-critical systems to ensure autonomous vehicles operate reliably under degraded conditions. You will ensure system resilience by designing robust software that manages failures in real-time, constrained environments.

What You'll Do:

- Design and develop safety-critical software components using C++ for fail-operational systems.

- Architect resilient system behaviors to maintain safe vehicle operation during hardware or software degradation.

- Implement robust solutions within highly constrained embedded environments to optimize performance and reliability.

- Collaborate with systems engineering teams to define safety requirements and architectural fail-safes.

- Apply a deep understanding of robotics and motion planning to enhance autonomous system reliability.

- Adapt system designs to evolving project requirements within a dynamic systems engineering framework.

What we are looking for:

- 4+ years of experience in Systems Engineering, Embedded Software, or Robotics.

- Proven expertise in developing fail-operational and safety-critical systems using C++.

- Hands-on experience with embedded systems.

- Strong background in robotics or motion planning for autonomous vehicle behavior.

- Proficiency in reliability engineering and designing for system resilience in constrained environments.

- Advanced proficiency in English.
