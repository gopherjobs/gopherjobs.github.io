About the Role

Are you a heavy-hitting Software Engineer who loves solving complex technical problems but hates being buried behind endless, back-office Jira tickets? Do you thrive when sitting at the intersection of deep engineering, production-grade AI architecture, and real-world customer impact?

We are looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE ) to act as the ultimate elite builder-consultant. In this role, you won't just build software in a vacuum - you will be embedded with our most strategic enterprise accounts to bridge the gap between core product capabilities and production-grade reality. You will move beyond high-level architecture to code, debug, map legacy data landscapes, and ship bespoke, mission-critical solutions directly within customer environments.

What You Will Do

  • Embed & Bu ild: Partner closely with enterprise engineering teams and stakeholders to run technical discovery, define requirements, and ship end-to-end solutions.
  • Data Integration & Migration: Own the complexity of moving customer data from legacy systems into modern cloud infrastructures, building robust ETL pipelines and API connectors.
  • AI & Solution Architecture: Architect and deploy production-grade AI workflows, RAG systems, and agentic tools, utilizing AI coding assistants to move fast across polyglot stacks.
  • Product Feedback Loop: Act as the ultimate line of defense in the field. Turn real-world friction and deployment blockers into actionable feedback for our Core Product and R&D teams to shape the future road map.
  • Deliver Rapid Value: Build lightweight custom UIs (React/TypeScript) or low-code automation wrappers where speed is required, ensuring every deployment delivers measurable ROI.

What You

  • Bring The Code: 5+ years of hands-on software development experience with a strong backend focus, ideally in Python, Java, Go, or TypeScript.
  • The Mindset : Proven experience in a customer-facing or field-facing capacity (e.g., as a Solutions Engineer, Tech Consultant, or Early-stage Founding Engineer). You can translate deep tech into business value.
  • The Data Skills: Strong experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure), REST/GraphQL APIs, database schema design, and data validation/reconciliation.
  • AI Literacy: Active, day-to-day use of AI coding tools (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, etc.) and familiarity with modern LLM/vector database setups is highly preferred.
  • Language: Fluency in written and spoken English (C1+). Professional German language skills are a massive advantage but not mandatory.

What’s in It for You?

  • Ultimate Autonomy: A highly entrepreneurial role with flat hierarchies and direct, visible impact on company growth.
  • Flexible Hybrid Culture: Modern office hubs in Germany's top tech cities with flexible remote work setups.
  • Continuous Learning: Deep exposure to frontier AI tech stacks, enterprise systems integration, and commercial scaling.
  • More: Competitive salary package, state-of-the-art equipment, and local wellness/mobility benefits.
