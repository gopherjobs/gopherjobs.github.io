Description

DAS Labs is a 12-year enterprise AI engineering firm based in Rancho Cucamonga, California. We build AI systems for medical, surgical, and home-healthcare clients, and we run our own products: AskVicky, an AI-powered operational discovery platform, and ClawBoss, a governance engine that keeps AI agents deterministic and auditable.

Our core engineering belief is simple: deterministic scaffolding governs probabilistic AI. The language model is never the system. The system is the APIs, the state machines, the validation layers, and the tests that surround it. That is the work this role does.

The role

You will build the deterministic layer of our automation products and client systems: API integrations, workflow engines, data pipelines, and the harnesses that constrain and verify AI agents. You will read a lot of code, much of it produced by junior engineers working with coding agents, and you will be expected to catch what they miss. You will help scope and architect new work before anyone writes a line. This is not a role for someone who wants to prompt a coding agent and ship a dashboard. It is a role for someone who can look at a system, understand why it will fail, and design so it doesn't.

How we work

Small team. High trust. Nobody is waiting to tell you what to do next. If you see a problem, you write it up and propose a fix. If you are stuck for more than a couple of hours, you say so. If you ship a bug, you own it, fix it, and tell the people it affected. We use AI tools every day and expect you to, but we expect you to understand and stand behind everything that ships under your name.

What you'll do

  • Design and build integrations against third-party APIs (scheduling, EHR, CRM, messaging), handling auth, retries, idempotency, rate limits, and failure modes properly.
  • Turn client operational processes into reliable automated workflows with clear state, logging, and recovery paths.
  • Review pull requests from other engineers, including agent-assisted code, and hold the line on correctness, testing, and structure.
  • Write design documents before building. Scope work, identify risks, and propose options with effort estimates.
  • Build and maintain CI/CD, containerized deployments, and the observability needed to know when something breaks before the client does.
  • Work directly with the CTO and, over time, with clients on technical questions.
