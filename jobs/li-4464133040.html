Description

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to maintain and modernize a portfolio of employee lifecycle applications used across candidate acquisition and development. Working primarily in Python/Django with some Java/Spring Boot and React, this person strengthens integrations with enterprise platforms and helps evolve these tools toward AI-enabled, agent-based workflows.

What you will do

  • Maintain and improve key Employee Lifecycle Applications, including Indeed Feedback, Palpatine, Skywalker, Compensation Offer Generator (COG), Demographic Data Collection, and Hindsight.
  • Strengthen and maintain the applications' integrations with Workday and iCIMS to support the candidate and employee lifecycle.
  • Enhance and modernize these applications to improve scalability, security, and user experience.

Must haves

  • 4+ years of experience in software development , with strong hands-on experience with Python/Django - highest priority for back-end development.
  • Hands-on experience with Java/Spring Boot .
  • Strong testing, debugging, and bug resolution skills.
  • Experience integrating applications with LLMs, AI assistants, agents, or other generative AI capabilities.
  • Strong experience with REST APIs and modern authentication and authorization patterns, including OAuth 2.0 , token management, scopes, and least-privilege access.
  • Familiarity with AI security and governance, including sensitive data handling, auditability, guardrails, and user-context versus system-context access.
  • Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript/React .
  • Good JIRA practices and strong technical documentation skills.
  • Upper-intermediate English level.

Nice to haves

  • Experience with Workday and/or iCIMS, particularly with integrating with these systems via APIs. These are among the more common platforms we integrate with, so familiarity with them would be beneficial, but is not required.
  • Hands-on experience developing or supporting MCP servers, tools, resources, or prompts.

Perks

  • Growth without limits: build your skills through mentorship, internal TechTalks, challenging projects, and a dedicated annual learning budget
  • Competitive compensation: get recognition that reflects your skills and impact, with regular performance and compensation reviews
  • Flexibility: work 100% remotely with flexible hours that support focus, autonomy, and a healthy work rhythm
  • Meaningful, modern projects: build impactful products using modern technologies alongside global teams and leading brands
  • Collaborative culture: join a supportive environment with zero micromanagement where ideas are welcomed and contributions are recognized
  • Well-being & support: access local well-being programs and people-focused support tailored to your location
