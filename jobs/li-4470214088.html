Hungry, Humble, Honest, with Heart

The Opportunity Join our team at Nutanix as a Member of Technical Staff 3, where you will play a crucial role in providing a solid Nutanix Cloud Manager (NCM) product to our customers. Your mission will be to build an observability platform that allows our customers to proactively monitor and manage their infrastructure and applications. By building a reliable NCM data platform and driving the adoption of best practices, you will contribute to the success of our products. This is a unique opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies, learn from experienced mentors, and be part of a fast-paced environment where autonomy and ownership are valued.

About the Team The NCM data platform provides an end-end telemetry platform that includes telemetry data collection via agents and a big data pipeline. The NCM data platform is a team of 12 people, spread across India and the US, with a mix of senior and junior engineers. The team is special because it works in a hybrid environment, prioritizing fast deliverables while dedicating time for cross-geo collaboration.

Your Role

  • Design and develop the next generation of Data Platform features for on-prem and cloud.
  • Participate in data modeling discussions and influence data products roadmap to ensure the best solution for requirements.
  • Work across all components of a big data platform such as API gateway, message bus, databases, and database abstraction layers.
  • Propose and drive adoption of best practices for operationalizing data platforms.
  • Design and implement pre-requisite data engineering by authoring Jobs via Apache Spark.

What You Will Bring

  • 3-6 years of experience with a Bachelors or Masters degree in computer science or related streams.
  • Experience with one or more of the following languages: Python, Scala, Java, Golang, C++.
  • Experience working with Database technologies and optimizing workloads.
  • Experience working with Big Data technologies, Kubernetes ecosystem, and microservice patterns.
  • Good understanding and working experience in SaaS technology development best practices.
