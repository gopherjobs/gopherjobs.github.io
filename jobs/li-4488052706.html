Would you like to build the secure engineering foundation for Rabobank's digital asset services? Summary As a Senior DevOps Engineer at Rabobank, you will work within the Digital Assets Services team on the bank’s digital asset platform. You will combine backend development, cloud infrastructure and blockchain technology to build secure and reliable services for custody, trading, payments and tokenisation.

You will integrate specialist digital asset platforms into Rabobank’s regulated technology environment. Your work will help turn architectural designs and emerging use cases into scalable production systems.

At a glance

  • Develop backend services, APIs and platform integrations for digital asset services.
  • Work with Java, Spring Boot, cloud technology, CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes-based platforms and Linux environments.
  • Help establish reliable engineering practices and technical direction within a recently established specialist team.

What will you do as a Senior DevOps Engineer Digital Assets Services?

  • Develop and maintain backend services, APIs and integrations that support digital asset custody, trading, payment and transaction flows.
  • Integrate specialist platforms, including Fireblocks and Trever, into Rabobank’s secure and regulated technology environment.
  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automated deployments, operational tooling and integrations with core banking systems.
  • Implement secure solutions for transaction signing and cryptographic key management.
  • Support services in production by improving observability, reliability and incident management, while contributing to engineering practices and technical direction.

Skills, tools and areas of expertise

  • Backend engineering: you design and develop backend services, distributed systems, APIs and SaaS integrations for complex enterprise environments.
  • Java and Spring Boot: you use backend technologies to create maintainable services and reliable integrations.
  • Cloud and platform engineering: you deploy and operate workloads using AWS or Azure and Kubernetes-based platforms such as OpenShift.
  • CI/CD and Azure DevOps: you create automated delivery pipelines and deployment processes that support dependable platform operations.
  • Linux and production operations: you operate services across cloud and on-premise environments, using observability and incident-management practices to maintain reliability.

Together we achieve more than alone

We believe in the power of differences. By combining people’s differences, we become an even better bank. We are curious about what you will bring to our team Digital Assets Services.

The Digital Assets Services team develops capabilities for digital asset custody, trading, payments and tokenisation. You will join a small, specialised team and work with architects, engineers, business teams and strategic technology partners. Together, you will turn Rabobank’s digital asset strategy into secure, scalable and reliable production systems.

The team is building a greenfield digital asset platform within a major bank. This gives you the opportunity to contribute to the platform’s development, technical direction and engineering culture from an early stage.

Why everyone is welcome at Rabobank

At Rabobank, we are working towards a culture where everyone feels welcome. We value our differences and use them to collaborate more effectively and make better decisions. By being open to different perspectives, we create an environment where colleagues feel heard.

What do you bring?

Must-haves

  • At least seven years of experience as a software engineer, platform engineer or in a comparable technical role.
  • Strong experience in backend development, including designing distributed systems and integrating APIs and SaaS platforms in complex enterprise environments.
  • Practical experience with AWS or Azure, CI/CD pipelines and Azure DevOps.
  • Experience deploying and operating workloads on Kubernetes-based platforms such as OpenShift.
  • Experience operating Linux-based environments across cloud and on-premise infrastructure.
  • Experience supporting production services, including observability, reliability and incident management.
  • Experience with Java and Spring Boot.
  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to take ownership of complex technical solutions while collaborating with other engineers.

Nice-to-haves

  • Knowledge of blockchain technology, tokenisation or stablecoins.
  • Knowledge of cryptographic key management or transaction-signing infrastructure.
  • Experience with institutional custody, trading or digital asset platforms such as Fireblocks or Trever.
