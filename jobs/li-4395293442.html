Workload 80 - 100% The role focuses on enabling fast and agile value generation by leveraging Axpo's cloud capabilities. What will you do

  • Support early‑stage ideation initiatives by rapidly designing and building scalable technical foundations
  • Design and implement cloud‑native platforms and services that enable AI‑enabled products

What You Bring & Who You Are

  • Strong experience as a platform or backend engineer in modern cloud environments, preferably Azure
  • Hands‑on mindset with deep technical curiosity
  • Strong understanding of DevOps practices: CI/CD, infrastructure as code, containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Proven interest in AI‑enabled architectures and how platforms support intelligent products
  • Comfortable working in fast‑moving, exploratory environments
  • Strong collaboration skills and willingness to work closely with product and analytics roles
  • Passion for learning, experimenting, and staying current with modern technologies
  • Fluent in English; German is a plus
