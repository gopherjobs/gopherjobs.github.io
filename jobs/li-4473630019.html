We are partnering with a fast-growing, AI software business in London that is looking to hire multiple Forward Deployed Engineers as it continues to scale its engineering team.

This is an opportunity for a strong full-stack engineer who wants more ownership than a traditional software engineering role. You will work directly with customers, understand complex real-world problems, design solutions and build them end-to-end.

The Role

You will:

  • Work directly with customers and non-technical stakeholders to understand complex workflows.
  • Translate real-world problems into scalable software solutions.
  • Build across the full stack, including frontend, backend, data models and AI/model layers.
  • Own features and product areas from concept through to production.
  • Ship production software quickly and iterate based on user feedback.
  • Work closely with founders and senior engineers on technical and product decisions.
  • Build AI-powered workflows around complex documents, operational processes and enterprise data.
  • Help determine which customer requirements should become scalable product features.

What We're Looking For

  • Around 3–6 years' experience in software engineering or product engineering.
  • Strong full-stack engineering capability.
  • Strong Python experience.
  • Frontend development experience with modern JavaScript/TypeScript technologies.
  • Experience building and shipping production software used by real customers.
  • Comfortable working across frontend, backend, APIs, databases and data models.
  • Strong problem-solving and product judgement.
  • Comfortable speaking directly with customers and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Able to operate effectively in a fast-moving, high-ownership environment.

The technology environment includes:

  • Python
  • TypeScript
  • React
  • Node.js
  • SQL
  • AI / Machine Learning
  • LLMs
  • Document Intelligence
  • Cloud Infrastructure

You do not need experience with every technology listed. Strong engineering fundamentals, learning ability and full-stack ownership are more important.

Nice to Have

  • Early-stage or high-growth startup experience.
  • Founding engineer, early employee or entrepreneurial experience.
  • Forward Deployed Engineering or Solutions Engineering experience.
  • Experience building AI or LLM-powered products.
  • Experience working directly with enterprise customers.
  • Experience building products or features from 0 → 1 .
