Description

FairMoney is a credit-led mobile bank for emerging markets. The company was launched in 2017, operates in Nigeria & India, and raised close to €50m from global investors like Tiger Global, DST & Flourish Ventures.

You will be working as a Engineering Manager (Identity) in our Technology Division, which is now composed of 75+ people in Lagos, Bangalore, Paris, Türkiye, and remotely across the globe.

We are building Engineering centres of excellence across multiple regions and are looking for smart, talented, driven engineers. This is a unique opportunity to be part of the core engineering team of a fast-growing fintech poised for more rapid growth in the coming years.

FairMoney  is a 100% digital bank that is revolutionizing the traditional banking industry. Our mission is to make financial services accessible and convenient for everyone through the use of technology.

As an Engineering Manager (Identity)  at FairMoney, you will lead an engineering squad responsible for building and maintaining reliable identity and KYC solutions that support our products and customers across markets.

You will combine strong people leadership with a hands-on technical background, working closely with engineers, Product, Risk, Compliance, and other stakeholders to deliver secure, scalable, and high-quality solutions. You will play a key role in shaping the squad's technical direction, engineering practices, and overall performance.

If you are an experienced engineering leader who enjoys developing people, solving complex technical problems, and building reliable systems in a financial services environment, we want you to join our team!

We are looking forward to having you work on:

· Leading, mentoring, and developing engineering squads while fostering a strong culture of ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement;

· Providing technical leadership for identity, authentication, KYC, and related backend systems;

· Working closely with Product, Risk, Compliance, and other teams to translate business and regulatory requirements into reliable technical solutions;

· Driving the design, development, and delivery of scalable and resilient distributed systems;

· Ensuring high standards across architecture, code quality, testing, security, reliability, and engineering practices;

· Supporting engineers in solving complex technical challenges and making sound engineering decisions;

· Managing team performance, growth, hiring, and development while ensuring the squad has the right skills and support to succeed;

· Monitoring system performance and reliability, identifying areas for improvement, and driving continuous technical improvements;

· Contributing to engineering strategy and helping shape the technology roadmap for Identity.
