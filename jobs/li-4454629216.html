About The Role AspenView Technology Partners is seeking an API Engineer to join a leading omnichannel enterprise commerce platform serving large hoteliers and multi-property operators in the travel and hospitality industry. You will build and maintain the REST APIs and integrations — on .NET/C# with Apigee and AWS — that power omnichannel payment acceptance, cross-property reconciliation, currency conversion, and split settlement. Success in this role means shipping reliable, well-integrated APIs that meet enterprise standards, while keeping services secure, compliance-aware, and delivered on a steady, predictable cadence.

What You Will Do API Development

  • Build and maintain REST APIs for enterprise, multi-tenant commerce.
  • Follow established contract, versioning, and reuse standards.

Enterprise Integration

  • Integrate ERP and e-commerce systems; manage APIs with Apigee.
  • Support omnichannel acceptance and reconciliation across properties.
  • NET Services
  • Build C#/.NET enterprise services on AWS.
  • Deliver for reliability and enterprise SLAs.

Quality & Security

  • Write automated tests and instrument observability.
  • Apply web-service security (SSL mutual auth, Azure AD) and compliance-aware development practices.

What You Bring Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Experience

  • 3+ years of API engineering experience.
  • Travel & hospitality industry experience required — specifically enterprise hotelier commerce, including omnichannel acceptance, cross-property reconciliation, currency conversion, and split settlement.

Technical Expertise

  • C#/.NET and REST API development.
  • Apigee API management; ERP and e-commerce integration.
  • Enterprise/multi-tenant services on AWS.
  • Strong written and spoken English; LatAm-based and aligned to U.S. (EST) time zone for embedded, client-side collaboration.
  • Nice to have: Kubernetes/Docker, Azure AD, financial-compliance platform experience, and Spanish or Portuguese fluency for LatAm expansion.

Certifications

  • Not required. Certifications in payments or travel/hospitality technology are valued alongside demonstrated delivery experience in these domains.
