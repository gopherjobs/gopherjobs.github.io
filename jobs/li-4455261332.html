Job Summary We are seeking an experienced and highly capable Senior IT Development Manager to lead software development teams in delivering large-scale enterprise and retail technology solutions. The ideal candidate will possess strong expertise in modern software development technologies, application modernization, software architecture design, automated testing, and technical leadership.

This role requires a combination of technical excellence, people management, stakeholder engagement, and customer-facing consulting capabilities. The successful candidate will drive development strategy, mentor development teams, design scalable solutions, and ensure successful project delivery aligned with business objectives.

Key Responsibilities Technical Leadership & Solution Delivery:

  • Lead and manage software development teams delivering enterprise-grade applications and digital platforms.
  • Drive application modernization initiatives, including legacy system transformation, cloud migration, microservices adoption, and API-driven architectures.
  • Define and govern software architecture, coding standards, development frameworks, and technical best practices.
  • Review solution designs and ensure scalability, performance, security, and maintainability.
  • Evaluate and introduce emerging technologies, development frameworks, and industry best practices.

  • DevSecOps adoption

  • Recommend suitable technical solutions based on business requirements.
