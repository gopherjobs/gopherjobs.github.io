-------------【 Job Description】-------------

■Recruitment Background

The company operates one of Japan's premier ad platform businesses, managing high-scale SSP/DSP operations that handle tens of billions of requests per month.

To capitalize on the rapidly growing DOOH (Digital Out-of-Home) market, the company is seeking a Full-Stack Engineer to build a new ad delivery management product from the ground up (0-to-1 stage) and drive the technical expansion of this business unit.

The DOOH market is expanding at an annual rate of 20% to 30%, with further acceleration anticipated as programmatic advertising becomes widespread. While the company currently acts as an agency for premium direct ad sales, its goal is to build an in-house proprietary product to automate and optimize delivery management, enabling the business to scale exponentially.

In this role, you will build the entire technical foundation of the DOOH business end-to-end—ranging from the architecture and development of the delivery management core to digital signage applications and integrations with external media channels.

■Responsibilities

Ad Delivery Management Product Development

・Feature Design & Implementation: Designing and implementing features for product management, inventory management, order placement, delivery configuration, reporting, and role-based access control (RBAC).

・Workflow Automation: Translating operational workflows (Order → Verification → Approval → Configuration → Launch) into smooth software features.

・End-to-End Execution: Developing the entire system from the backend (API and database architecture) to the frontend (admin dashboard UI/UX).

・Operational Efficiency: Streamlining day-to-day operations through template creation, workflow engineering, and automation.

・Iterative Improvement: Driving MVP (Minimum Viable Product) development and continuous iterations based on user feedback.

Dedicated Device & Android Application Development

・Digital Signage Apps: Designing and developing native Android applications tailored for digital signage systems.

・Playback & Scheduling Control: Managing creative asset playback (videos, images) and synchronized delivery scheduling.

・Hardware Integration: Establishing seamless communication with digital signage hardware for remote control and status monitoring.

・Data Ingestion: Capturing logs (impressions, playback counts, etc.) and implementing robust data pipelines to servers.

・Performance Optimization: Optimizing device-level resources, including memory allocation, power consumption, and network efficiency.

External Integrations & SDKs

・API Integration: Designing and building robust API bridges with external media networks.

・System Resilience: Implementing asynchronous processing, retry mechanisms, and advanced error handling.

・Traceability & Auditing: Securing end-to-end traceability and maintaining comprehensive audit logs.

・SDK Development: Authoring external-facing SDKs alongside comprehensive technical documentation.

・Security Standards: Implementing robust authentication/authorization protocols and rate-limiting rules.

Operations Engineering

・Config Templates: Creating reusable delivery configuration templates for frequent workflows.

・Automated Scheduling: Automating real-time inventory updates and scheduling logic.

・Reporting & Alerts: Building automated report generators and communication integrations (Slack, email, etc.).

・Operator-Centric UI: Tailoring inventory, delivery, and reporting interfaces to match operator needs.

Technical Leadership

・Architecture Selection: Owning the technology stack selection and architectural design across the backend, frontend, Android, and infrastructure.

・Quality Assurance: Leading rigorous code and design reviews to maintain code quality.

・Technical Debt Management: Driving continuous refactoring and paying down technical debt.

・Mentorship & Documentation: Guiding and mentoring junior engineers while maintaining high-quality technical documentation and knowledge bases.

Key Advantages & Position Value

・0-to-1 Development in a High-Growth Market: The opportunity to build an ad delivery platform completely from scratch in the DOOH sector, which is currently expanding at an annual rate of 20% to 30%.

・Full-Stack Technical Discretion: Complete technical ownership across the entire stack—backend, frontend, Android, and cloud infrastructure—within a nimble, high-velocity team.

・Cutting-Edge Technical Challenges: Direct engagement with specialized engineering domains, combining digital signage, Android systems, hardware integration, and real-time programmatic ad delivery.
