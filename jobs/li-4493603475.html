Description

About The Role What if your curiosity and creative thinking could make AI systems safer for millions of people? We're looking for AI Red Team Testers to go head-to-head with cutting-edge AI models — probing, prodding, and stress-testing them to uncover weaknesses before they cause real-world harm.

This is your chance to play the role of adversary in one of the most important challenges in tech today: making AI trustworthy. If you love puzzles, think laterally, and enjoy finding the cracks in systems that seem solid, this role was built for you.

Fully remote. Flexible hours. No security or hacking background required.

  • Organization: Alignerr
  • Type: Hourly Contract
  • Location: Remote
  • Commitment: 10–40 hours/week

What You'll Do

  • Design imaginative prompts, scenarios, and conversation flows designed to trip up AI models
  • Attempt to coax AI systems into producing incorrect, biased, unsafe, or inappropriate outputs
  • Document every failure mode you discover with clear, reproducible steps
  • Assess and rate the severity and potential impact of identified issues
  • Collaborate asynchronously with AI safety and research teams to communicate your findings
  • Continuously develop new attack strategies as AI systems evolve

Who You Are

  • A naturally curious, creative thinker who enjoys finding loopholes and solving puzzles
  • Comfortable adopting an adversarial mindset — thinking like someone trying to break the rules
  • Detail-oriented and methodical: you document what you find thoroughly and clearly
  • Strong written communicator who can explain issues in a structured, actionable way
  • Self-motivated and able to work independently on an async schedule
  • No hacking, cybersecurity, or AI background required — if you think creatively and communicate well, you're a strong candidate

Nice to Have

  • Experience in creative writing, debate, philosophy, journalism, or research
  • Prior exposure to AI tools, chatbots, or language models as an end user
  • Background in ethics, psychology, or critical analysis
  • Familiarity with AI safety concepts or previous red teaming work

Why Join Us

  • Work on genuinely important AI safety projects alongside leading research labs
  • Fully remote and flexible — structure your work around your life
  • Freelance autonomy with the satisfaction of meaningful, mission-driven work
  • Contribute directly to the responsible development of AI that impacts the world
  • Potential for ongoing work and contract extension as new projects launch
