Description

Title: Software Quality Engineer

Location: Harrisburg, PA (Currently remote but late it can be onsite)

Contract role

Position Overview

  • The Senior Software Quality Engineer will lead software quality and test automation efforts across enterprise applications and platforms. This role is responsible for establishing quality standards, implementing automated testing solutions, integrating testing into CI/CD pipelines, and promoting quality engineering best practices throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
  • The ideal candidate will have deep experience in software quality assurance, test automation, DevOps practices, and enterprise application testing. They will work closely with development, architecture, business, and operational teams to improve software reliability, security, and delivery quality.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead software quality assurance and quality engineering activities across multiple projects and application teams.
  • Design, implement, and maintain automated testing frameworks and reusable test assets.
  • Integrate automated testing into SDLC processes and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Develop and execute testing strategies for functional, regression, integration, performance, and security testing.
  • Establish quality standards, testing best practices, and governance processes.
  • Monitor quality metrics, analyze defects, and recommend continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Collaborate with technical and business teams to ensure solutions meet quality, performance, and security requirements.
  • Support organizational software quality maturity and process improvement efforts.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.

Skills:

  • 8 years of experience in Software Quality Assurance, Software Testing, or Quality Engineering
  • 7 years of experience working within Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) methodologies including Agile, Scrum, DevOps, and traditional development
  • 7 years of experience designing, implementing, and supporting automated testing solutions integrated into CI/CD pipelines
  • Experience testing enterprise applications, APIs, integrations, web applications, and cloud-based solutions.
  • Experience developing quality metrics, reporting, and quality improvement initiatives
  • Test Automation Framework Design and Implementation, Automated Functional, Regression, Integration, and API Testing
  • Integration, and API Testing, CI/CD Pipeline Integration, Agile, Scrum, DevOps, and SDLC Practices
  • Selenium, Playwright, Azure DevOps, GitHub, Jenkins, or similar DevOps platforms
  • SQL and Data Validation Techniques
