Description

Do you want to be at the forefront of technology for some of the world’s largest airlines? Do you enjoy learning new things in a fast-moving industry? Or perhaps you already have knowledge that you want to build on, while also sharing it with your colleagues.

We have a unique opportunity to offer our consultants exciting assignments within the aviation industry. Do you want to join us on our journey? Don’t wait — get ready for take-off and apply to us today!

To succeed in this role, we believe you have:

  • At least 3 years of experience with Python, as well as at least one additional relevant language, such as C++, Java, or a functional programming language
  • Experience with Linux and Git
  • Experience with cloud technologies, primarily AWS or Azure
  • Basic knowledge of SQL
  • Experience using AI tools such as Claude Code, Copilot, or Cursor in your daily development work
  • A relevant academic degree, for example in computer science, engineering physics, or industrial engineering and management

It’s also beneficial if you have:

  • Rave and domain knowledge within crew planning/management
  • Basic knowledge of DevOps, preferably related to CI/CD, build flows or automation
  • Experience troubleshooting and refactoring in legacy environments
  • Experience with API integrations, test automation, and Bash scripting

We also value experience using AI to support development work. This could include regularly using tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor or Codex for code generation, debugging, test generation, documentation or understanding unfamiliar code. Experience with agentic workflows, AI-assisted code reviews, or sharing AI practices and learnings with your team would be an additional plus. AI experience is considered an advantage, but is not a requirement. More about the assignment: You will be part of a great team working with complex, partly undocumented systems, where technical troubleshooting, system understanding, and problem-solving are key parts of the role. You will work closely with stakeholders and end users to understand business needs and translate them into stable, well-functioning technical solutions.

We believe you are someone who takes ownership from analysis to deployed code, and who enjoys navigating complex architecture with curiosity, persistence, and a structured mindset. You are self-driven, analytical, detail-oriented, and quality-conscious, with a genuine interest in understanding both the technical landscape and the needs of the people using the systems.

About HiQ: Working at HiQ means taking on challenging and varied assignments. As consultants, we work on client assignments that may be delivered either on-site, remotely, or from our own offices, giving us the opportunity to explore new industries, workplaces, and tech stacks. The variety is broad, and the pace of development is high. At HiQ, you get to use and grow your strongest skills, while also exploring new areas and developing new ones. With the right mindset and expertise, you create value both in your assignment and for your colleagues at HiQ.

At HiQ, you’ll find some of the sharpest developers, testers, and security specialists in the industry, alongside project managers, agile coaches, designers, and many others. Our skills and backgrounds may differ, but we share the same drive: to build, improve, and simplify — and to have fun while doing it.

HiQ is driven by culture rather than rules, which means you can be yourself. We take our work seriously, but not always ourselves. What brings us together is our ambition to make the world a little better through technology and communication solutions that make people’s lives easier — for real.

We look forward to receiving your application!
