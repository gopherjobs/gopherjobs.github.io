WHAT YOU WOULD ACTUALLY BE BUILDING

Our client builds a SaaS platform that keeps field workers safe on dangerous sites. Workers wear a device, the platform knows where they are relative to hazard zones, and it alerts before someone walks into one. It runs across rail, highways, construction, aviation, water and energy projects. Trackside, roadworks, airfields. The places where being in the wrong spot at the wrong moment is a fatality rather than a near miss.

That is the product. The software you write stops people being hit by trains and plant machinery.

WHY THE ARCHITECTURE LOOKS LIKE IT DOES

Position data streams continuously from GNSS, cellular, Bluetooth and UWB devices worn by workers in the field, over connectivity that is often poor, at real volume, where a dropped or late message has a safety consequence.

That is why this is microservices with DAPR, event-driven with Kafka and Event Hubs, containerised on Kubernetes and running across two clouds. It is not architecture for its own sake. It is a system that cannot go down.

The other half of the business is connectivity. They put high-speed networking into places that have none, satellite and private 4G and 5G networks including for critical national infrastructure. That is what makes the product work on a site in the middle of nowhere, and it means the software team sits next to people putting real hardware into the ground.
