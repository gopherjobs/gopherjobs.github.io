About The Role Databases are still managed manually. Engineers monitor slow queries, decide which indexes to add, tune connection limits and introduce caching, often without being able to prove whether those changes will improve performance in production.

CAST AI is building an autonomous database administrator that can identify problems, recommend improvements and validate that those improvements work before customers rely on them.

As a Senior Software Engineer on the Database Optimisation team, you will work across two core components:

  • Database Gateway: A high-performance Rust proxy that sits in front of customer databases and provides capabilities such as rate limiting and caching without requiring application changes.
  • Database Agent: A Go-based service that analyses database workloads and produces recommendations such as index suggestions and query improvements.

The focus is not simply producing recommendations. Every recommendation needs to be reliable and validated. A stale read, incorrect cache decision or poor index suggestion can affect a customer’s production system, so correctness is central to the role.

You will help shape the architecture, technical direction and roadmap of the autonomous DBA product.
