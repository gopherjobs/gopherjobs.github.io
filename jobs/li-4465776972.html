Description

About the Role

Our client is seeking a Lead Software Engineer to drive technical architecture, system design, and hands-on execution for their core platform and data engineering initiatives. In this role, you will lead team-level design, build high-performance distributed systems, and optimise data-intensive applications at scale.

You will work closely with lead engineers and platform architects to align team implementations with long-term modernisation goals, while actively mentoring engineers and driving high standards across code quality, reliability, and security.

Key Responsibilities

  • Architecture & System Design: Own team-level design, including high-level and detailed architectures, API contracts, data flows, service interactions, integration patterns, and non-functional requirements (scalability, reliability, performance, security, operability).
  • Architectural Alignment: Partner with lead engineers and architects to align implementation with overall platform architecture, engineering standards, and long-term maintainability.
  • Data Engineering & Analytics: Develop and optimize data-intensive applications and analytics capabilities using Databricks, Spark, and related technologies to process and deliver data at scale.
  • Hands-on Delivery: Serve as an active technical contributor - designing, writing, testing, debugging, and shipping production-quality software across the full SDLC.
  • Performance & Optimization: Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks across services, APIs, data processing workloads, and user experiences.
  • Mentorship & Culture: Guide junior and mid-level engineers through technical direction, code reviews, pairing, design feedback, and knowledge sharing. Model continuous learning, ownership, and clear communication.
  • Engineering Excellence: Drive software quality through automated testing, secure coding practices, CI/CD, observability, performance tuning, and production readiness.
  • Troubleshooting & Reliability: Lead root-cause analysis for complex issues across application, API, data, and cloud layers to implement durable, production-ready solutions.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Experience: 7+ years of software engineering experience delivering scalable, high-performance, and reliable systems in an Agile environment.
  • Distributed Systems: Proven hands-on experience designing and building distributed systems, microservices, RESTful APIs, and cloud-native applications.
  • Core Tech Stack: Strong proficiency in modern stacks, such as Java/Spring Boot, TypeScript/React, and SQL.
  • Data Platforms: Strong experience or deep familiarity with Databricks, Spark, or PySpark for data pipelines and analytics-driven applications.
  • Cloud & DevOps: Hands-on experience building or supporting applications on AWS and/or Azure, including CI/CD, containerization, Kubernetes, observability, and automated testing.
  • Technical Leadership: Demonstrated ability to lead team-level design discussions, evaluate complex technical tradeoffs, and guide implementation through technical influence.

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation and benefits package
  • Flexible hybrid/remote work setup
  • Clear growth trajectories and continuous learning support
  • A collaborative environment driven by technical excellence and autonomy
