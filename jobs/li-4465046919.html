We are hiring for one of our clients, seeking a Senior Software Engineer to work on a contract basis. The position requires strong problem-solving skills and experience with modern software development practices.

Required Skills & Qualifications:

• Bachelor’s or higher degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

• Minimum of 7 years of professional software development experience.

• Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or C++.

• Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).

• Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and system design principles.

• Experience with version control systems, CI/CD pipelines, and Agile methodologies.

• Ability to write clean, modular, and well-documented code.

• Familiarity with databases (SQL and NoSQL) and distributed systems.
