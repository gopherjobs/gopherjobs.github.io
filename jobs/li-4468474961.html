No Prior AI Training Experience Is Required. Key Responsibilities

  • Evaluate AI-generated solutions for complex geospatial problems, debugging, and code development.

  • Collaborate with technical stakeholders to refine documentation and project requirements.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong experience in GIS, remote sensing, spatial analytics, or related geospatial fields.
  • Hands-on experience using AI coding agents for technical or geospatial projects.
  • Proficiency in Python and geospatial technologies.
  • Experience working with complex codebases, debugging, or end-to-end feature development.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication.
  • Ability to clearly document technical workflows for technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Experience collaborating with remote or multidisciplinary teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience using Codex, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, or similar AI coding agents.
  • STEM background with ongoing professional experience in geospatial work.
  • Experience evaluating AI-generated code or technical solutions.
  • Experience with large-scale geospatial datasets and complex spatial workflows.
