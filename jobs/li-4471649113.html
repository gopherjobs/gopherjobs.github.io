Job Overview WHO WE ARE

Relativity is a leading legal data intelligence company building technology that helps users organize data, discover the truth, and act on it with confidence. Our AI-powered, cloud platform, RelativityOne, transforms massive volumes of complex information into actionable insights for litigation, investigations, regulatory inquiries, data breach responses, and other high-stakes legal work where accuracy and trust are crucial.

The world’s largest law firms, corporations, and government agencies rely on Relativity’s legal AI software to securely surface and manage the most relevant and impactful information in their matters. Beyond our commercial impact, we are committed to expanding access to technology and supporting pro bono legal work.

What We Do The Search Infrastructure Team develops and operates the foundational search capabilities behind Relativity Search.

Relativity Search is a suite of applications that empowers customers to find relevant information, then retrieve and present it. Search products cover document ingestion, index building, searching, and search term report execution. Stable, high-performance search directly impacts customer experience and is critical for the organization.

The Search Infrastructure Team builds, maintains, and monitors distributed search infrastructure; improves scalability and query performance; advances automation and operational excellence; and applies data-driven techniques to improve reliability, efficiency, and cost at scale.

About The Role As a Senior Software Engineer on the Search Infrastructure Team, you will help build, operate, and scale the search platform that powers mission-critical customer workflows across RelativityOne.

This role focuses on running petabyte-scale search infrastructure with strong reliability, performance, observability, and operational discipline. You will identify and implement optimizations, drive platform enhancements, define and promote engineering standards, and help shape the future architecture of the service. Working alongside the Lead Engineer and cross-functional partners, you will align technical execution with the long-term search platform vision.
