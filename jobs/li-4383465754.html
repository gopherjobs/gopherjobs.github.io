Mulesoft Architect Job Description:

As a Mulesoft Architect, you will be responsible for designing and implementing scalable, reliable, and secure integrations between systems using Mulesoft's Anypoint Platform. You will collaborate with project teams, stakeholders, and other architects to understand business requirements and translate them into technical designs. Your responsibilities will include:

- Designing and developing Mulesoft integrations, APIs, and services

- Collaborating with stakeholders to gather and analyze requirements

- Creating technical design documents, including diagrams and flowcharts

- Ensuring integrations are scalable, reliable, and secure

- Troubleshooting and resolving technical issues

- Mentoring and guiding team members

Requirements:

- Minimum of 5 years of experience in Mulesoft integration development and architecture

- Strong understanding of integration design patterns and best practices

- Experience with Mulesoft's Anypoint Platform, including API design, management, and deployment

- Strong understanding of RESTful web services, JSON, and XML

- Experience with Agile software development methodologies

- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
