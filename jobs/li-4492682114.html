Description

To apply, you will only have to fill a short form on our website that will take you less than 5 minutes. Job Title: Front-End QA Tester About The Company Our client is a US-based software company that builds customized ERP systems and custom business-management software.

Job Description Each system is tailored to a specific industry, including labor and staffing, maintenance and real estate. With artificial intelligence now speeding up development dramatically, the team needs a dedicated tester who can keep pace and guarantee that new features and bug fixes reach customers with zero complaints.

Responsibilities Of The Role

  • Test new software end to end before delivery to customers, clicking through every screen and button to confirm each business workflow works as intended.
  • Run real operational workflows in the system, such as creating sales orders, the way an actual business user would.
  • Design and execute positive and negative scenarios, checking both expected outcomes and how the system handles bad input, edge cases and errors.
  • Pick up tickets for new features, test them thoroughly, and run regression checks to confirm nothing else broke.
  • Verify bug fixes: confirm the reported issue is resolved and that the fix did not introduce new problems elsewhere.
  • Learn each industry-specific system (staffing, maintenance, real estate and others) well enough to judge whether it behaves the way that business operates.
  • Write clear, reproducible bug reports and give the Project Manager a confident sign-off when a feature is ready to ship.
  • Use artificial intelligence tools and test automation where they speed up or strengthen testing.

Required Experience And Qualifications

  • Proven professional experience in quality assurance or front-end testing of web-based software.
  • Hands-on experience testing customer relationship management, enterprise resource planning or similar business-operations software.
  • A solid understanding of how businesses run day to day (orders, scheduling, staffing, maintenance, invoicing) and how software supports those workflows.
  • A structured, repeatable testing methodology: test planning, scenario coverage, regression testing and bug verification.
  • Experience working from tickets in an agile or continuous-development team.
  • Strong attention to detail and the persistence to test every path, not just the happy path.
  • Ability to work independently and be productive from day one without training.
  • C2 English level.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience with automated front-end and back-end testing.
  • A software engineering background, or the ability to read code and investigate issues beyond the user interface.
  • Practical experience using artificial intelligence tools to generate test cases, automate tests or speed up quality assurance work.
  • Familiarity with industry-specific systems in staffing, maintenance, field services or real estate.
