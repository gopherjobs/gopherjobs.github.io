【About the Service】

The service is a Contract Management Platform that enables contracts to be seamlessly and entirely digitized, from execution to management and AI review. It allows for smooth and secure contract execution through electronic signatures and time stamps compliant with the Electronic Signature Act. In 2023, it became the first general-purpose electronic contract service in Japan to launch a 'My Number Card Signature' feature. Furthermore, by actively adopting AI technology, such as AI contract management and AI review support services, it achieves overall time reduction in contract operations. It is the No. 1 service in the electronic contract market, widely adopted by companies and local governments.*

*Source: Fuji Chimera Research Institute, Inc., "Software Business New Market 2023 Edition" (Electronic Contract Tool FY2022 results), Market Share.
