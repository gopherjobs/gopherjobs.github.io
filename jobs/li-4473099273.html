  • Manage the lifecycle of eligibility and enrollment public Assistance programs.
  • Oversee application performance, reliability, and scalability
  • Coordinate system upgrades, patches, and enhancement
  • Ensure integration with related systems (MMIS, HIX/Marketplace etc.)
  • Lead and manage projects related to eligibility system transformations and modernization
  • Develop project plans, timelines, and budgets
  • Coordinate with vendors, system integrators, and internal IT teams
  • Monitor project risks, issues, and dependencies Business & Stakeholder Engagement
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, policy teams, and operations to gather requirements
  • Translate policy and regulatory requirements into system functionality
  • Act as the liaison between technical teams and business users
  • Manage and mentor application developers, analysts, and support staff
  • Promote Agile/DevOps practices for continuous delivery and improvement
  • Ensure effective workload planning and team performance
  • Ensure accurate eligibility determination logic and data integrity
  • Oversee reporting and analytics related to enrollment, eligibility, and compliance
  • Support data exchanges (e.g., Federal Data Services Hub, state systems)
  • Ensure timely resolution of production issues and incidents
  • Establish SLAs and monitor service performance
  • Drive continuous improvement and process optimization
