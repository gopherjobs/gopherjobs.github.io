About the role

Overall, the team builds a robust, distributed deployment part of the Retail Platform that supports large-scale, low-bandwidth environments. The team is responsible for orchestrating and managing software rollouts across thousands of retail stores using cloud-native and edge-based solutions. 

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design, build, and operate the observability capabilities that provide visibility into deployment workflows, store health, device status, and platform reliability across thousands of geographically distributed retail locations. You will work closely with deployment team, platform, and operations teams to ensure proactive detection of issues, rapid troubleshooting, and data-driven operational excellence.

You will be responsible for

  • Design and implement metrics, logging, tracing, and telemetry solutions across cloud and edge components.
  • Build monitoring pipelines, dashboards, and alerting frameworks that provide actionable insights into deployment health, device status, and store operations.
  • Define and maintain Service Level Indicators (SLIs), Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and reliability metrics for mission-critical deployment services.
  • Create operational analytics for deployment performance, software rollout progress, failure detection, and capacity planning.
  • Integrate telemetry from cloud services, edge devices, deployment agents, and infrastructure components into centralized monitoring platforms.
  • Build automated detection and root-cause analysis capabilities using modern observability practices and tooling.
  • Develop self-service dashboards and reporting capabilities for engineering, support, and operations teams.
  • Drive observability standards, instrumentation practices, and monitoring strategies across the Retail Platform ecosystem.

You will need Mandatory skills:

  • 6+ years of experience in software engineering
  • Strong Java development skills (Spring Framework)
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a data-driven mindset
  • Basic system design skillset
  • Deep understanding of metrics, logs, traces, alerting strategies, and performance monitoring.
  • Experience with modern observability frameworks and standards such as OpenTelemetry
  • Knowledge of event-driven systems and event sourcing patterns
  • Hands-on experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated testing

Nice to Have:

  • Familiarity with SQL databases and distributed systems concepts
  • Knowledge of Kubernetes and Docker
  • Experience in monitoring and observability tools.
  • Hands-on experience with monitoring and visualization platforms such as Prometheus, Grafana, Victoria Metrics or New Relic.
  • Experience troubleshooting complex distributed systems in production environments.
