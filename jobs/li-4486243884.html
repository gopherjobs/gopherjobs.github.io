The opportunity

A leading global systematic investment firm is looking for an experienced platform engineer to join its core compute team. The firm runs high-performance, data-intensive workloads across regions, and this team builds the infrastructure underneath them. You’ll architect and evolve the compute platform, working closely with engineering teams worldwide on infrastructure-as-code, observability and self-healing systems.

Why consider it?

  • Hands-on engineering: you write production code and build internal tooling and APIs, not just run tickets.
  • Real scale: large, global, performance-critical compute supporting research and trading.
  • Influence: you help set best practice and mentor junior engineers.
  • Automation-first culture: incidents lead to architectural fixes, not just firefighting.

What you’ll be doing

  • Architecting and evolving scalable compute platforms using modern infrastructure engineering practices
  • Building automation frameworks for provisioning, configuration management and lifecycle operations
  • Developing internal tooling and APIs that hide infrastructure complexity and boost productivity
  • Driving observability with telemetry pipelines for metrics, logs and traces
  • Taking part in bulk server provisioning, including working with remote hands
  • Partnering with software engineers on scalability, reliability and security
  • Joining on-call and incident response, and turning postmortems into long-term improvements

What we’re looking for

Essential

  • Expert-level Linux administration (RHEL/CentOS/Ubuntu)
  • Configuration management (Ansible, Chef or SaltStack)
  • Python, Go or Rust for infrastructure tooling
  • Infrastructure-as-code (Terraform or Pulumi)
  • Cloud experience (AWS, GCP or Azure) across compute, storage, networking and IAM
  • Monitoring and alerting (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, Zabbix) plus logging and tracing (ELK, Fluentd, OpenTelemetry, Jaeger)
  • Identity and access management (LDAP, Kerberos, OAuth2)
  • Git workflows, test-driven infrastructure and a DevOps mindset
  • A degree in Computer Science, Engineering or similar
