Description

About APADCode APADCode, Inc. is a U.S.-based technology company headquartered in Houston, Texas. We're building AI-powered technical interview technology that helps companies evaluate software engineers based on how they approach problems, write code, and explain technical decisions.

Our platform conducts live, interactive coding interviews and captures the reasoning behind engineers' solutions, helping hiring teams make better-informed decisions.

As we continue improving our interview technology and expanding our technical assessment capabilities, we're looking for experienced software engineers who can bring real-world engineering knowledge into our interview sessions.

About The Opportunity We're looking for skilled software engineers to join our global network of paid technical interview participants.

In this role, as Interviewee, you'll participate in realistic technical interview simulations and authorized assessment sessions, answer engineering questions, solve coding challenges, and explain your solutions in real time.

We want engineers who can think independently, handle unfamiliar technical problems, and communicate their reasoning clearly, just as they would during a professional software engineering interview.

You won't need to manage long-term development projects or maintain production applications. Instead, you'll get paid for participating in scheduled sessions that match your technical background.

Why We're Hiring Building effective technical interview technology requires more than a collection of coding questions. We need to understand how experienced engineers actually approach problems, make technical decisions, and respond to challenging questions.

That's where you come in.

By participating in realistic interview sessions, you'll help us assess the quality of technical questions, evaluate interview experiences, and improve how engineering skills are measured.

We're particularly interested in engineers with hands-on industry experience who can demonstrate practical technical judgment rather than simply memorize interview answers.

Why Join Us?

  • Get paid for your technical expertise. Earn hourly compensation by applying the engineering knowledge and problem-solving skills you've developed throughout your career.
  • No additional full-time commitment. Take part in individual sessions without managing projects, deliverables, or ongoing development work.
  • Work from anywhere. Participate remotely from your preferred location, with opportunities aligned with U.S. business hours.
  • Flexible scheduling. Accept sessions based on your availability and technical specialization.
  • Keep your technical skills sharp. Work through coding challenges, architecture discussions, and engineering problems that test your abilities.
  • Ongoing paid opportunities. Reliable participants who demonstrate strong technical skills may be invited to additional sessions.

What You'll Do

  • Participate as the interviewee in scheduled technical interview simulations and authorized assessments.
  • Solve coding challenges involving data structures, algorithms, and practical software engineering problems.
  • Answer questions related to backend development, frontend development, full-stack engineering, cloud infrastructure, system design, or AI/ML based on your expertise.
  • Explain your approach, reasoning, and technical decisions clearly during live sessions.
  • Discuss potential improvements, trade-offs, performance considerations, and alternative solutions.
  • Provide feedback on interview difficulty, question clarity, and the overall assessment experience when requested.
  • Follow session guidelines, including identity disclosure and assessment requirements.

Required Qualifications Who We're Looking For

  • At least 4 years of professional software engineering experience (every field is acceptable).
  • Strong proficiency in one or more programming languages, including Python, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript, C++, C#, or Go.
  • Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software architecture.
  • Strong analytical thinking and practical problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent spoken English and the ability to explain technical concepts naturally.
  • Confidence solving unfamiliar technical problems under time constraints.
  • Reliable internet connection, webcam, and microphone.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience as a Mid-Level, Senior, Staff, or Principal Software Engineer.
  • Familiarity with technical interview formats used by U.S. technology companies.
  • Strong expertise in backend systems, distributed systems, cloud architecture, full-stack engineering, or machine learning.
  • Previous experience participating in live coding interviews or technical assessments.

If you're an experienced developer who enjoys technical challenges and wants a flexible way to earn additional income, we'd love to connect.
