  • Role Summary

We are seeking a delivery-focused Engineering Manager with 10 to 14 years of professional experience to lead our core data and backend platform teams. This role balances strong people leadership with delivery management. You will not write daily production code or design the systems architecture. Instead, you will focus on team enablement, managing execution timelines, and utilizing your high-level understanding of data and platform architectures to unblock your engineers and ensure seamless delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • People Leadership (50%): Mentor and scale a team of platform and data engineers. Manage performance, guide career development, and foster a collaborative, high-performing culture.
  • Delivery & Platform Management (50%): Oversee execution roadmaps, track project timelines, and ensure platform reliability. Translate business goals into engineering deliverables and remove operational blockers for the team.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Experience: 12 to 14 years of total software engineering experience, with a proven track record in people management and data/backend platform delivery.
  • Technical Literacy: Strong baseline understanding of Python and Go (Golang), enabling you to track project progress, evaluate team updates, and accurately gauge technical delivery times.
  • Data Stack Familiarity: High-level understanding of modern data workflows and ecosystems, specifically:
  • Snowflake (Cloud data warehousing)
  • Cassandra (Distributed NoSQL storage)
  • DBT/ Data Build Tool (Data transformation/modeling)
  • Airbyte (Data integration/pipelines)
  • Systems Understanding: Conceptual familiarity with STL (Standard Template Library) frameworks, microservices, and cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) to understand system dependencies and constraints.
