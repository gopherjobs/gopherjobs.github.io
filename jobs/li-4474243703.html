Description

About ADATAGROUP

ADATAGROUP is a Singapore-based trade-tech company building AI-powered solutions that modernise cross-border trade across Asia-Pacific.

Our platform helps freight forwarders and customs declaring agents automate complex trade and documentation workflows, processing thousands of shipments across the trade lifecycle.

We are a small, high-impact team where engineers have direct ownership of what they build and work closely with technical leadership on AI solutions already in production.

The Role

We are looking for a AI Developer to take ownership of and advance the AI capabilities powering our trade-tech platform.

This is a hands-on engineering role. You will design, build, improve, and deploy LLM-powered systems that process real freight and customs documents for live clients every day.

As a key AI engineer on the team, you will work across document intelligence, customs automation, and trade compliance — from improving extraction accuracy on complex trade documents to developing agentic workflows for customs permit automation and integrating real-time shipment intelligence.

Key Responsibilities

AI Development

  • Design, build, and optimise AI capabilities across document intelligence, customs automation, tariff classification, and trade compliance
  • Build and refine agentic AI workflows for end-to-end trade automation
  • Improve structured data extraction from complex trade documents, including invoices, customs forms, bills of lading, and shipping instructions
  • Evaluate and integrate new AI models, APIs, and techniques as the technology evolves
  • Monitor and improve the accuracy, reliability, and performance of production AI systems

Platform Engineering

  • Develop and maintain Python/FastAPI backend services powering the AI platform
  • Build scalable batch-processing capabilities for high-volume document ingestion
  • Integrate external APIs and data sources to enrich platform intelligence
  • Design validation and quality-assurance pipelines for production AI outputs
  • Write clean, maintainable, well-tested code with appropriate automated test coverage

Collaboration & Ownership

  • Work closely with technical leadership on architecture, AI strategy, and product direction
  • Own technical problems from investigation through deployment and production improvement
  • Triage and resolve client-reported issues together with the operations team
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering best practices
  • Communicate progress, trade-offs, and blockers clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Requirements

Must Have

  • 3+ years of professional software development experience, including at least 1–2 years working with AI/ML or LLM-based applications
  • Strong Python proficiency and experience writing production-grade software
  • Hands-on experience with LLM APIs such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or similar, including prompt engineering, function/tool calling, and structured outputs
  • Experience building REST APIs using FastAPI, Flask, Django, or similar frameworks
  • Experience extracting structured information from unstructured or semi-structured data such as documents, PDFs, web content, or OCR output
  • Ability to work independently in a small, fast-moving team with a high level of ownership
  • Must already have the legal right to work in Singapore. Please note that work pass sponsorship is not available for this role.

Strongly Preferred

  • Experience with intelligent document processing, including PDF parsing, OCR, and extraction from complex multi-format business documents
  • Hands-on experience designing agentic AI systems involving multi-step workflows, tool use, orchestration, or reasoning

Nice to Have

  • Experience with vector databases, embeddings, and RAG architectures
  • Experience with PostgreSQL, cloud object storage, Docker, and AWS
  • Background in freight forwarding, logistics, customs, trade compliance, or supply chain
  • Experience with HS code classification, permit automation, or regulatory workflows
  • Familiarity with React, Next.js, or TypeScript for occasional frontend collaboration
  • Contributions to open-source AI/ML projects
