What you will be working on Under the AI Enablement Office, the Frontier Products Team is a modern team focused on developing software products that aim to make CPF Board and the Government as a whole work better. We work entirely on the cloud using cloud-native technologies.

In this role, you will:

  • Contribute your technical skills to one of our many products and initiatives that aim to make CPF Board and whole-of-government work better, including opportunities where GenAI can add real user value.
  • Explore the field of GenAI, and help prototype standalone products or build our end-to-end GenAI platform stack to enable other GenAI development teams to build products that improve the way we work.
  • Work in a cross-disciplinary team with UI/UX designers and Product Managers to ship usable, safe, and measurable features.
  • Gain hands-on experience with AWS or Azure, including cloud services commonly used to build and deploy AI-enabled systems.

What We Are Looking For We value the diverse skills and perspectives that each intern brings. While you may not need to meet every requirement fully, having some familiarity or budding expertise in the following areas will help you make the most of this opportunity and succeed with our team.

  • Currently pursuing a Degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Has some experience or a keen interest in cloud solutions and GenAI, with basic development knowledge in Java or Python; familiarity with large language models, prompt engineering, evaluation, or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a plus.
  • Interested in developing problem-solving skills and a logical approach to troubleshooting technical issues, including investigating model outputs and system behaviour.
  • Communicates clearly, both in speaking and writing, and is able to explain technical ideas in a simple way, including GenAI concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Works well independently and with others, takes initiative without needing constant supervision.
