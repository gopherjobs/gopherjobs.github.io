Description

Seeking a "Builder-in-Residence" to launch special projects in Germany (remote) . Are you the person who sees a problem in the morning and has an AI-powered MVP running by dinner?

Felix, Gregor and Ivo are looking for a super hands-on, tech-savvy "Product Swiss Army Knife" to join us in our Vienna office. You don't need to be a Senior Dev, but you do need to be a master of the modern stack: AI agents, no-code/low-code, rapid prototyping, and scalable architecture.

We are building a few stealth-mode special projects and need someone who can iterate at lightning speed, work directly with the founding team, and think like an entrepreneur. The vibe: High autonomy, zero bureaucracy, pure building.

Your role: We are looking for a Technical Product Generalist to act as the "execution engine" for a series of special projects. You will sit directly with the Co-Founders and CTO to turn raw ideas into validated products. This is not a role for someone who wants to sit in meetings; it’s for someone who wants to build, break, and ship.

What You’ll Do:

  • Rapid Prototyping: Take a concept from a whiteboard and build a functional MVP using a mix of AI tools, low-code platforms, or light coding.
  • AI Orchestration: Implement AI workflows using tools like OpenAI/Anthropic APIs, LangChain, or Cursor to automate processes and enhance product features.
  • Architectural Design: Help define how different tools and databases talk to each other so our "fast" builds don't crumble when they scale.
  • Iterate & Pivot: Use user feedback to change directions in hours. You should be comfortable "killing your darlings" if the data doesn't support the idea.
  • Bridge the Gap: Act as the glue between the Founders' vision and the CTO's technical roadmap.

Who you are:

  • Your Tech "Toolbox" (Expected Proficiency). You should be a "power user" in most of these categories:
  • AI/LLMs: Cursor, Replit Agent, Claude Artifacts, Midjourney, v0.dev.
  • Automation/No-Code: Make.com, Zapier, Airtable, Bubble, or Webflow.
  • Modern Stack: Knowledge of how APIs, Supabase/Firebase, and Vercel work.
  • Product: Linear, Notion, Figma (enough to wireframe).
  • Your Personality:
  • Entrepreneurial: You’ve likely had a side hustle or tried to start a company before. You care about why we are building, not just what.
  • Tech-Fluent: You can hold your own in a conversation with our CTO about architecture, even if you aren't writing the production backend yourself.
  • Bias for Action: You prefer a "dirty" solution that works today over a "perfect" one that takes a month.
  • Vienna-Based: You want to be in the room where it happens. This is a high-bandwidth, in-person role

Why is this better than a standard Dev role?

  • Founding Team Access: You work directly with the decision-makers every day.
  • Speed: No legacy code. No technical debt. Just building new things.
  • Variety: You won't be stuck on one feature for six months; you'll be launching multiple projects.
