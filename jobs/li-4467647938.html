Location: Philippines – Fully Remote

Job Summary

We are looking for experienced Workforce Management Testers to support testing activities for a WorkForce Software implementation.

Candidates with direct WorkForce Software / WorkForce Suite experience will be prioritized . However, we are also open to candidates with extensive testing experience in UKG/Kronos or other enterprise Time & Attendance / Workforce Management platforms who have strong knowledge of workforce management and payroll processes.

The role will support system testing, integration testing, UAT, regression testing, parallel testing, defect management, and validation of workforce management configurations and integrations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute test cases, test scripts, and test scenarios based on business and functional requirements.
  • Perform testing across:
  • System Integration Testing (SIT)
  • User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
  • Regression Testing
  • Parallel Testing
  • End-to-End Testing
  • Post-deployment validation
  • Validate workforce management configurations involving:
  • Timekeeping
  • Time and Attendance
  • Scheduling
  • Attendance policies
  • Pay Rules
  • Leave and Accruals
  • Payroll interfaces
  • Perform positive, negative, boundary, and end-to-end test scenarios.
  • Validate employee time capture through downstream payroll processing.
  • Test integrations between Workforce Management systems and:
  • Payroll platforms
  • HRIS/HCM systems
  • Time clocks and badge readers
  • Third-party applications
  • Data and reporting platforms
  • Validate data mappings, interfaces, transformation rules, and system outputs.
  • Document testing results, screenshots, evidence, and validation logs.
  • Identify and document system defects.
  • Participate in defect triage discussions with functional and technical teams.
  • Retest resolved defects and perform regression testing where required.
  • Support business users during UAT execution.
  • Maintain accurate test documentation and testing status updates.
  • Support go-live validation and post-production stabilization activities.

Required Qualifications

  • At least 3 years of experience in Workforce Management, Time & Attendance, Payroll, or enterprise application testing .
  • Hands-on experience creating and executing:
  • Test cases
  • Test scripts
  • Test scenarios
  • Test evidence
  • Experience with SIT, UAT, regression testing, and defect management .
  • Strong understanding of Time & Attendance and Workforce Management processes.
  • Experience testing system integrations and validating data flows.
  • Familiarity with defect tracking and test management tools.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.

Platform Experience

Priority will be given to candidates with hands-on experience in:

  • WorkForce Software
  • WorkForce Suite
  • Workforce Time
  • WFS Time & Attendance
  • WFS Scheduling
  • WFS Absence / Accruals

Candidates may also be considered if they have extensive experience with:

  • UKG
  • UKG Pro Workforce Management
  • Kronos
  • Kronos Workforce Central
  • UKG Dimensions
  • ADP Workforce Management
  • Other enterprise Time & Attendance / Workforce Management platforms
