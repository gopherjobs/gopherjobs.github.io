Description

About VinSmart Future:

VinSmartFuture (formerly VinIT) is a technology subsidiary of Vingroup , leading the development of a comprehensive SuperApp and digital ecosystem that serves tens of millions of users across key sectors: payments, healthcare, education, mobility, commerce, and entertainment.

Our mission is to build the "Digital Core" - the foundational technology platform that powers Vingroup's unified digital operations, enabling smart management, data-driven decision-making, and scalable infrastructure on a national level.

Work Location:

  • Hanoi: TechnoPark Tower, Vinhomes Ocean Park, Hanoi
  • HCM: Vincom Dong Khoi, HCM

Job Description:

  • Design, evaluate, and optimize system architectures for applications requiring complex service orchestration, real-time processing, high availability, scalability, and fault tolerance, such as ride-hailing, trip matching, and pricing systems.
  • Define and standardize SOA / Microservices architecture for multi-application, multi-market environments (multi-tenant, multi-region).
  • Analyze and re-engineer existing systems to:
  • Improve scalability, performance, and availability.
  • Ensure observability, tracing, and performance tuning.
  • Enhance resilience and fault tolerance, reduce downtime, and improve user experience.
  • Establish technical standards and collaborate with Development and DevOps teams to ensure consistent implementation following best practices.
  • Work closely with Product Owners and Tech Leads to define system architecture, technical solutions, and technology choices.
  • Research, experiment, evaluate, and propose solutions for application development across both business workflows and platform/non-functional requirements, including but not limited to Infrastructure as Code (IaC), CI/CD, DevSecOps, Integration, Observability, Data Engineering, Data Analytics, MLOps, AI, UI/UX, and Super App development.
  • Contribute recommendations to improve architecture, system performance, and end-user experience (response time, fault tolerance, etc.).

Qualifications:

  • Minimum 3 years of backend development experience and 2 years of system architecture experience.
  • Strong understanding of modern programming languages and development frameworks, such as: Java (e.g., Spring); Scala; Python; Node.js (e.g., NestJS, Express); Go (e.g., Gin, Fiber); Spark (Java, Scala, Python); Flink; Beam
  • Knowledge and hands-on experience with SOA, Microservices, System Integration, and DevSecOps.
  • Understanding of protocols and architectural patterns, including RESTful APIs, JWT/OAuth2, messaging systems, batch and stream processing, multithreading, and singleton patterns.
  • Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, AWS/GCP (EKS/GKE, ECS, Cloud Run, etc.), and Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
  • Experience in database architecture, data modeling, and performance tuning across databases, caching, and messaging platforms, including Redis, Kafka, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and NoSQL databases.
  • Experience designing multi-tenant systems or multi-region deployments.
  • Knowledge of monitoring, logging, and tracing tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, and OpenTelemetry.
  • Strong system thinking, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in Mobility, Payments, Logistics, or Super App domains.
  • AWS/GCP Architect or Kubernetes Administrator certifications.
