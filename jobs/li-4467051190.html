Description

We are looking for a Senior DevOps / SRE / Platform Engineer with strong hands-on experience designing, automating and operating production infrastructure.

This is not a junior or entry-level position nor remote.

You will take technical ownership of cloud infrastructure, deployment pipelines, platform security, observability, reliability and production operations.

A key part of the role is working with AI coding and infrastructure agents such as Claude Code, Codex or equivalent tools. You must be able to instruct AI agents, review and audit their output, identify errors or security risks, and take responsibility for the final production result.

AI-NATIVE DEVOPS

We expect AI agents to be part of the engineering workflow.

You may use tools such as Claude Code, Codex or equivalent systems to accelerate infrastructure development, scripting, debugging, documentation and operations.

However, AI-generated output must never be treated as automatically correct.

You will be expected to:

• Give precise technical instructions to AI agents.

• Review generated code and infrastructure changes.

• Identify security, reliability and architectural issues.

• Validate changes before deployment.

• Understand what the agent has changed and why.

• Maintain human accountability for production systems.

• Build documentation and runbooks that can also be consumed by AI agents through RAG-based systems.
