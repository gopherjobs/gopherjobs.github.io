Description

Shape the Future of AI-Led Engineering at Defaqto

At Defaqto, we're investing in the next generation of software engineering. We're looking for a Principal Software Engineer to act as one of the organisation's most senior technical leaders, driving engineering excellence, influencing architecture across multiple teams, and helping us harness AI to transform how we build products and deliver value to customers.

This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who combines deep technical expertise with strategic thinking. You'll partner with engineering leaders, product teams and senior stakeholders to define technical direction, raise engineering standards and accelerate the adoption of modern development practices.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Defining and evolving engineering standards, architecture principles and best practices across multiple teams.
  • Leading complex technical initiatives and helping teams solve challenging engineering problems.
  • Driving adoption of AI-assisted and AI-led engineering practices to improve productivity, quality and delivery speed.
  • Acting as a technical mentor and coach to senior engineers and technical leads.
  • Influencing technology strategy, platform direction and architectural decisions across the wider business.
  • Remaining hands-on with software design, architecture reviews and technical problem solving.

What We're Looking For

  • Extensive software engineering experience, including experience operating at Principal Engineer, Staff Engineer, Lead Architect or a similar senior technical level.
  • Strong expertise in modern software architecture, cloud-native development and distributed systems.
  • Experience influencing engineering teams without direct line management responsibility.
  • Deep understanding of engineering quality, software delivery, testing, observability and platform engineering.
  • Experience with cloud technologies such as Azure, AWS or GCP.
  • Passion for AI and emerging engineering practices, with practical experience using AI tools to improve software delivery.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence at senior leadership level.

Why Join Defaqto?

  • Opportunity to shape the future of engineering within a leading financial services technology business.
  • Work on meaningful products used across the UK financial advice, wealth management and insurance sectors.
  • Hybrid working with flexibility to balance office and home working.
  • Competitive salary of up to £120,000 plus benefits.
  • A genuine opportunity to influence strategy, technology direction and engineering culture.

Location:

Hybrid - London or Haddenham office, with the flexibility to work remotely for part of the week. You will spend regular time on site with your squads and with product and leadership colleagues.

Right to Work:

Applicants must already hold a legal right to work in the UK without time restrictions and without the need for future sponsorship. We are unable to provide Skilled Worker visa sponsorship.
