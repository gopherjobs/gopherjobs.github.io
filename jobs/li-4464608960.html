PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES INCLUDE

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain high-quality distributed applications within the FORTNA Warehouse Execution System (WES) platform.
  • Participate in the architecture, design, implementation, and delivery of scalable warehouse automation and execution solutions.
  • Collaborate with Product, QA, Infrastructure, and Services teams to deliver reliable and maintainable software solutions.
  • Provide remote support, troubleshooting, and root-cause analysis for both new WES solutions and legacy WCS systems.
  • Contribute to system reliability, performance optimization, scalability, observability, and operational excellence.
  • Develop and maintain RESTful APIs, event-driven integrations, microservices, and enterprise application components.
  • Assist QA and Infrastructure teams in test automation, deployment validation, packaging, and release activities.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering best practices, coding standards, and technical documentation.
  • Support project customization efforts and assist implementation teams during customer deployments.
  • Travel occasionally to customer sites to support solution qualification, testing, and go-live activities when required.
  • Continuously learn and adopt new technologies, tools, and engineering practices, including AI-assisted software development tools.
  • Share technical knowledge with team members and actively contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
  • 5+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong experience with Java (Java 17 or later preferred) and concurrent/distributed application development.
  • Strong understanding of Object-Oriented Analysis and Design (OOAD) , software design patterns, and clean coding principles.
  • Experience developing enterprise applications using modern Java frameworks and technologies (e.g., Spring Boot, Quarkus, Play Framework, Micronaut, or similar), including RESTful APIs and distributed systems.
  • Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, Oracle, or MySQL.
  • Experience with messaging technologies, event-driven architectures , or message brokers (Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ, ActiveMQ, IBM MQ, etc.).
  • Experience with Git and modern build tools such as Maven or Gradle.
  • Experience working with Docker and containerized applications .
  • Familiarity with Linux-based development and deployment environments.
  • Strong troubleshooting, debugging, and problem-solving skills .
  • Good written and verbal English communication skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in Agile/Scrum development environments.
  • Strong collaboration skills and the ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

  • Experience with observability and monitoring tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, ELK, OpenSearch, Splunk, or Datadog.
