General Summary Qualcomm is seeking a Senior Embedded Software Engineer to join the Core Platform Kernel software team. The Core Platform Kernel team develops and deploys real-time operating system (RTOS) software across multiple subsystems on Qualcomm SoCs, supporting Mobile Handset, XR, Compute, Automotive, Cloud AI, and IoT market segments.

The team is responsible for the deployment and evolution of two RTOS platforms at scale: QuRT OS and Zephyr RTOS. As part of Qualcomm's continued investment in open-source software, this role will contribute to our Zephyr strategy and help expand Zephyr deployment across Qualcomm products. We are specifically seeking engineers who can develop high-quality embedded software, contribute to upstream Zephyr components, and work effectively with both Qualcomm engineering teams and the broader open-source community.

This role is well suited for engineers who enjoy deep technical work, solving challenging system-level problems, collaborating across software and hardware boundaries, and building software that benefits both Qualcomm products and the broader open-source community.

What You'll Do As a Senior Engineer, you will:

  • Design, implement, debug, and maintain RTOS and low-level system software components.
  • Independently investigate complex system-level issues involving performance, stability, power, concurrency, and correctness.
  • Develop features from requirements and design through implementation, testing, integration, and deployment.
  • Analyze crash dumps, traces, logs, and hardware behavior to perform root-cause analysis.
  • Collaborate closely with hardware, systems, validation, and software teams to deliver robust solutions.
  • Participate actively in design and code reviews and contribute to engineering standards and technical decisions.
  • Contribute to Qualcomm's engagement with the Zephyr open-source community through code contributions, reviews, technical discussions, and upstream collaboration.
  • Improve software quality through testing, automation, CI, static analysis, and effective debugging practices.
  • Share technical knowledge and help less-experienced engineers develop their skills.

You will be expected to take ownership of significant technical problems, make sound engineering tradeoffs, and deliver high-quality software with a high degree of independence.

QuRT OS QuRT OS is Qualcomm's real-time operating system optimized for the Hexagon DSP, enabling high-performance, low-power workloads including edge AI, modem processing, audio, sensor processing, and other specialized compute workloads.

In The QuRT OS Space, You Will

  • Develop enhancements that expose and take advantage of the capabilities of the Hexagon processor architecture.
  • Design and implement RTOS features with attention to correctness, performance, power efficiency, and maintainability.
  • Own features through requirements, design, implementation, testing, documentation, integration, and support.
  • Diagnose complex interactions between the RTOS, processor architecture, hardware, firmware, and application workloads.
  • Improve CI, automation, debugging, and test infrastructure to increase development velocity and software quality.

Zephyr RTOS The Core Platform Kernel team is a major adopter of Zephyr RTOS across Qualcomm SoCs and subsystems. We maintain a scalable Zephyr platform while contributing improvements back to the upstream community.

This role provides an opportunity to become deeply involved in the Zephyr ecosystem while solving challenging embedded systems problems on Qualcomm platforms.

In The Zephyr Space, You Will

  • Develop and maintain Zephyr subsystems, drivers, platform infrastructure, board support, and architecture components.
  • Contribute high-quality changes to the upstream Zephyr project and work through the upstream review process.
  • Review contributions from internal and external developers and provide constructive technical feedback.
  • Design and implement new Zephyr capabilities with an emphasis on upstream acceptance and long-term maintainability.
  • Participate in architecture discussions and technical reviews within Qualcomm and the Zephyr community.
  • Collaborate with maintainers and contributors from other companies across the Zephyr ecosystem.
  • Enable Zephyr across Qualcomm platforms through board support packages, architecture enablement, driver development, and system software integration.
  • Improve testing, CI infrastructure, release readiness, and software quality across upstream and Qualcomm-maintained components.
  • Develop expertise in one or more Zephyr technical areas and grow toward technical ownership or maintainership of those components.

How You Work: Tools, Automation & Modern Engineering Senior Engineers are expected to be highly effective individual contributors who use modern engineering practices to solve problems efficiently and improve the development environment around them.

In Practice, This Means

  • Applying modern development tools, including AI-assisted tools, when they meaningfully improve productivity or code quality.
  • Using automation, scripting, CI systems, debuggers, profilers, tracing tools, static analysis, and simulation environments to accelerate development and diagnosis.
  • Knowing when automation can solve a problem efficiently and when deeper hands-on debugging or system analysis is required.
  • Identifying recurring engineering problems and improving tools, tests, documentation, or workflows to address them.
  • Maintaining high standards for code quality, testing, documentation, and maintainability.

You are not expected to be an AI expert, but you are expected to be tool-fluent, curious, adaptable, and pragmatic.
