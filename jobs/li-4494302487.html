Description

About The Role We are looking for a Fullstack Tech Lead to lead the technical side of our commerce and content projects. You will lead a team of engineers, keep writing code yourself, and work with clients and product managers to turn their needs into something we can build and ship. The role is fully remote within Latin America.

You will also be the person we count on for our US projects. When the European team is offline, you pick up the harder tasks and problems, and you hand work over between shifts so nothing gets lost. Your working hours will follow US business hours.

Key responsibilities:

  • Lead the technical design, delivery and long-term evolution of storefronts, APIs, microservices and integrations on Adobe Commerce, Shopify, Next.js, Contentful and PIM systems.
  • Write production code, mostly on the hardest parts, and review the code of others.
  • Set and keep standards for testing, code review and CI/CD in GitHub Actions, Jenkins and Azure DevOps.
  • Own API design (REST, GraphQL or gRPC), data flow and state management between frontends, backend services and third-party systems such as ERP, payments and order management.
  • Cover our US projects while the European team is offline: handle complex tasks and incidents, and run the daily handover so each shift knows the status, open risks and next steps.
  • Mentor engineers on both frontend and backend, unblock them on technical problems, help hire new people and build an inclusive team culture.
  • Join client meetings to explain trade-offs, run technical workshops and help with estimates.
  • Keep an eye on technical debt, upgrades and code we inherit from other vendors, look for ways to make our delivery smoother, and try new tools, including cloud services and AI assistants, where they help the team.
  • Work closely with product managers, UX/UI designers, DevOps and solution architects to agree on what is feasible, set clear milestones and keep the scope realistic.

What we look for Core technical expertise:

  • 7+ years of fullstack web development, including 2+ years leading engineers or technical delivery.
  • Delivered production commerce or content platforms for clients, including at least one platform migration, upgrade or takeover of existing code.
  • Advanced JavaScript/TypeScript with React and Next.js. Vue is a plus.
  • Strong backend experience in PHP and/or Node.js, .NET or Python. PHP is a must if you will lead Adobe Commerce work.
  • Hands-on work with at least two of Adobe Commerce, Shopify, Contentful or a PIM.
  • Solid SQL (MySQL/MariaDB or PostgreSQL) with good data modelling and query tuning, plus Redis and a search engine such as OpenSearch or Algolia.
  • Experience building APIs (REST or GraphQL) and connecting systems such as ERP, payments and order management.
  • Docker and Kubernetes, in the cloud or on-premises; CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions, Jenkins or Azure DevOps; working experience with at least one of AWS, GCP or Azure.
  • Automated testing across the stack (for example Jest, Playwright, Cypress or PHPUnit).
  • A good grasp of distributed systems: microservices, message and event patterns, caching, authentication and authorization, and how an API changes over its lifetime.

Leadership and delivery

  • A record of mentoring engineers, running code reviews and helping a team agree on technical decisions.
  • Practical judgment: you balance clean architecture with deadlines, scope and maintainability, and you explain the trade-offs clearly to clients and to colleagues who are not engineers.
  • Used to working with teams in other time zones: you write clear handovers and act on your own when colleagues are offline.
  • Fluent Spanish and English, written and spoken. Spanish is mandatory.

Nice to have

  • Adobe Commerce certification, or Shopify Plus or Hydrogen experience.
  • Headless or composable commerce setups, including Hyvä and commercetools.
  • Terraform, Helm, and monitoring and observability tools such as Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana, Application Insights or OpenTelemetry.
  • Experience with estimates, RFPs and client workshops.
  • Everyday use of AI coding assistants such as Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or Cursor, with clear rules for reviewing generated code and handling client data.
  • AI in the delivery process: pull request summaries, test generation and incident triage, always with a person approving.
  • Building AI features for stores, such as search, product data enrichment in a PIM, or draft content in Contentful.

What we value

  • Clean architecture, good performance and software that stays reliable.
  • Practical decisions that balance the ideal design with client timelines and business goals.
  • Curiosity about new commerce and frontend technologies.

How we work

  • Fully remote within Latin America.
  • Collaborative, multicultural teams across time zones.
  • A culture of ownership, open communication and continuous professional growth.
