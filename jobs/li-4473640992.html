Responsibilities The Senior Forward Deployment Engineer works inside a customer's development program as software developer, systems engineer, and deployment lead in one role, and ships production code. This person determines and develops approaches to complex assignments, leads major deployment engagements using in-depth experience to influence outcomes, diagnoses problems deep within the protocol stack (Layer 1 to Layer 3), modifies software, validates fixes, and delivers controlled releases during a customer's early development, integration, and regression cycles.

This is a customer-facing software engineering role where you will embed directly within a customer environment to customize, integrate, and deploy technology into real-world production systems, leading complex, high-impact problems.

Core Responsibilities

  • Lead major or large-scale deployment engagements, coordinating across Keysight and customer engineering teams
  • Own technically complex deployment issues from first report to validated fix, and serve as the go-to technical resource for deployment issues within the account or program
  • Work onsite or closely with wireless customer development teams during major feature introductions
  • Analyze signaling flows, traces, timing behavior, and performance data to isolate failures across Keysight and customer implementations
  • Write and modify application and protocol-stack software, plus the supporting software and firmware the customer environment requires
  • Develop and extend Python APIs that customer teams integrate against
  • Build automation workflows inside the customer's workflows
  • Create diagnostic instrumentation when existing tools are insufficient
  • Develop, validate, and deploy controlled patches, feature flags, and interim releases under Keysight's release process
  • Run rapid prototyping loops directly with customers to test and iterate on new features based on live feedback
  • Act as a feedback loop to the core product and engineering teams, translating field problems into engineering requirements and defects for product management and R&D

Qualifications Typically requires a Bachelor's or Master's degree (or equivalent) and 6-7 years of relevant software/systems engineering experience.

  • Strong software-development experience across multiple software stacks
  • Preferred expertise in at least one domain (PHY/L1, MAC/RLC/PDCP and L2, or RRC/NAS and L3), plus working knowledge of adjacent layers and end-to-end 4G, 5G, and 5G-Advanced system behavior
  • Proficiency in C/C++ and Python (including API development), Linux, embedded or real-time software, and automated testing
  • Experience debugging protocol interactions through logs, traces, packet captures, call flows, and timing analysis
  • Familiarity with 3GPP specifications and the practical differences among standards compliance, interoperability, and implementation behavior
  • Experience in design and test instrumentation and test emulation tools
  • Ability to produce production-quality code that ships in a customer's build
  • The judgment and customer presence to operate inside confidential development programs
  • Excellent communication skills; able to explain technical trade-offs to both engineers and non-technical stakeholders
  • Willingness to relocate or travel to a specific customer site as required by the assignment
  • Extensive experience and breadth and depth of knowledge across multiple protocol layers and software stacks, with demonstrated ability to work independently
  • Demonstrated ability to lead technical discussions with customer
