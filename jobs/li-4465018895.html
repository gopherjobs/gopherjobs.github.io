📋 What You'll Architect As a Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer , you will help organisations transform complexity into measurable outcomes by combining technology, data, intelligence, and human decision-making.

This role is both technical and consultative, perfect for someone who can combine full-stack development , cloud-native engineering , backend API design , and modern software delivery practices . We're looking for professionals who can build scalable products that support enterprise-grade AI and data platforms.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications supporting AI and data-driven business solutions.
  • Build modern frontend applications using React 18 and TypeScript.
  • Develop scalable backend services and APIs using FastAPI, Python, Node.js, and TypeScript.
  • Design and implement integrations with enterprise systems and data sources through APIs and connectors.
  • Work with PostgreSQL, Amazon RDS, and Amazon DynamoDB to support application persistence and performance.
  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of a shared NX monorepo architecture.
  • Implement and maintain automated testing, quality controls, and regression testing practices.
  • Support CI/CD processes through GitHub Actions, Sonar, Dependabot, and modern DevOps practices.
  • Collaborate closely with AI Engineers, Cloud Engineers, Architects, Product teams, and business stakeholders.
  • Contribute to technical standards, architecture decisions, code reviews, and engineering best practices across projects.
