Description

About Us Semeru67 Foundation expands early-childhood literacy and fosters community self-reliance through accessible, multi-lingual digital applications and educational infrastructure. We build scalable technology designed specifically to empower low-resource populations, early learners, rural farmers and micro-entrepreneurs.

Current Focus Areas

  • Foundational Education
  • Community Empowerment

Current Projects

  • QRA Cards Tactile, physical flashcard sets designed for hands-on, offline learning in classrooms and homes. Printed with high-contrast visuals and phonetic guides, QRA Cards allow children to master basic alphabets, phonics, and vocabulary without needing digital devices.
  • QRA App A lightweight, mobile-first flipcard application built for low-cost smartphones. Operating offline-first, the app pairs phonetic audio clips with visual flipcards. Powered by a dynamic backend, the application easily adapts to new languages and step-by-step learning modules via simple data updates.
  • Street Vendor Locator A community-driven digital map connecting local street hawkers, informal vendors, and small agricultural producers with urban consumers. This tool helps micro-entrepreneurs increase their visibility, track daily locations, and stabilize their household income.

Future Plans - Expanding our Impact

  • Digital Library & Expanded Learning Modules. Developing next-generation interactive stories, decodable readers, and specialized vocabulary modules to expand the QRA App learning ecosystem.
  • Community Library Book Drives. Distributing physical children’s books, storybooks, and literacy resources to underfunded community libraries and local learning centers.
  • Educational Teaching Aids. Providing physical learning kits, math blocks, and hands-on teaching aids to rural educators and early-childhood tutors.
  • Nourish to Learn. Providing nutritious meals and healthy snacks for children, directly addressing the link between nutrition and cognitive development.
  • Mobile-First Interactive Presentation Tool. A responsive presentation engine optimized for low-end mobile screens. Enables field educators, community leads, and volunteers to deliver interactive workshops and training sessions anywhere, even with limited internet connectivity.
  • Integrated Education & Community Portal. Developing comprehensive, all-in-one digital hub built to support learners, educators, and field teams worldwide

The Role We are seeking an empathetic, structured, and modern Agile Coach / Scrum Master to join our team. In a volunteer-driven, highly asynchronous environment, standard rigid Scrum frameworks can cause friction and burnout. Your core mission is to adapt Agile principles to fit our flexible 10-hour/week volunteer model—helping teams maintain momentum, eliminate blockers, establish clear sprint goals, and foster a healthy, continuous-improvement delivery culture.

Key Responsibilities

  • Agile Framework Adaptation Tailor Agile/Scrum/Kanban practices to fit a lightweight, asynchronous, remote volunteer environment.
  • Sprint Facilitation & Execution Facilitate core cadence events—monthly Sprint Planning, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives—keeping live calls tight and focused.
  • Asynchronous Standups & Flow Setup and maintain lightweight async standup routines to keep visibility high without forcing daily live meetings.
  • Blocker Removal & Team Health Proactively identify organizational dependencies, friction points, and volunteer capacity bottlenecks, helping team leads clear paths to execution.
  • Coaching & Continuous Improvement Guide Program Managers, Tech Leads, and team members on writing clear user stories, setting realistic sprint commitments, and running productive retrospectives.

What We Are Looking For

  • Agile Expertise Solid understanding of Scrum, Kanban, and Agile principles with the practical wisdom to know when to adapt rules for volunteer settings.
  • Async Facilitation Skills Experience driving alignment through written documentation, Notion databases, and structured async communication.
  • Empathy & Engagement Outstanding interpersonal skills; adept at motivating uncompensated volunteers through purpose, clarity, and psychological safety.
  • Pragmatic Problem-Solving Ability to trim unnecessary process overhead and focus purely on delivery velocity and team satisfaction.
  • Open & Respectful Mindset Committed to fostering an inclusive, cross-cultural, and continuous-learning environment.

Time Commitment

  • Flexible, asynchronous schedule (~10 hours/week or ~40 hours/month).

What You Will Gain

  • Verified International Experience A formal Certificate of Volunteer Service and verified portfolio deliverables in an international organizational setting.
  • Endorsements LinkedIn endorsements after 100 hours or 3–6 months.
  • Tangible Impact Direct experience of building digital public goods and educational initiatives changing real lives.
