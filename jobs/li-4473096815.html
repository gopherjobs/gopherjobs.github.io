Job Description – Along-side Pre Sales technical and Sales at foreign markets (such as Australia, Thailand, Singapore, EU,..) to provide broad expertise in digital services to customers (Ecommerce, Fintech, … business). Participating in the construction of system architecture, building and ensuring architectural standards in accordance with business or development requirements

– Designing, describing, and managing solution engineering to match it with specific business problems of customers

– Leading the practice and introducing the overall technical vision to clients

– Transferring effectively project details to Delivery Department to ensure implementations are in line with client requirements

Requirements – As a solution architect, you are required to code and design architectures to fit with business requirements and help customers succeed in their business

– Solid experienced at least 1 top programming language, such as Java, PHP, Golang, C# .NET, Python, etc

– Having a DevOps mindset and DevOps skills

– Experienced in microservices architecture design

– Experienced in API development design for Web and Mobile applications

– Experienced in designing and implementing CI/CD Pipelines

– Experienced working with Containerization, Virtual Machines such as Docker, VMWare, and Orchestrators such as Kubernetes Engine

– Experienced working at least 1 Public Cloud such as AWS, GCP, MS Azure, etc.

– Experienced with both database and application systems from On-Premises to Cloud

– Experienced working with SQL, NoSQL Databases such as MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle DB, MongoDB

– Good English in written and verbal communication skills

– Knowledge of enterprise-level application ecosystems (ERP, OMS, CRM, WMS, etc.) (nice to have)

– Ability to generate innovative ideas for applying AI in company workflows and products, along with leading and training team members on essential AI skills and knowledge.

Benefits – Attractive salary package, up to 4000$

– Salary review twice a year

– Flexible working hour (between 7:30am – 7:30pm on staff’s preference)

– Premium health care up to $3,000/year

– Working in One of the largest Ecommerce Agencies in South East Asia – Professional English environment

– Free English, Japanese and professional training packages

– Firm’s Certified Qualifications Sponsorship For Career Development – Annual company trip inside or outside Vietnam

– Other fun activities: happy hour, quarterly team building, football club, yoga club, swimming club, charity activities, etc.

– Free entertainment parties: Birthday party, Anniversary party, Sum-up Party, Year-End Party, etc.
