Description

About the Role

We are looking for a Software Engineer with experience in Java and MuleSoft to own backend services and platform-level engineering supporting a Case object ecosystem. This person strengthens service logic, data processing, and platform reliability alongside Salesforce Service Cloud integrations. Production troubleshooting, incident support, and on-call rotation are part of the role.

What you will do

  • Own backend Java services and platform-level engineering supporting the Case object ecosystem, including service logic, data processing, and platform reliability for case-related features.
  • Contribute to active engineering efforts across daemons owned by CES CS/Ops (integration layer focus) and Mulesoft capabilities owned by CES CS/Ops (integration layer focus).
  • Support stakeholders and their reps in Service Cloud, partnering with CS Ops, BizOps, JS Ops, FinSys, and other orgs on integration-related issues affecting Cases and case workflows.
  • Diagnose and resolve API/integration failures impacting downstream Salesforce Service Cloud processes.
  • Participate in the DFR (Developer First Responder) rotation and Ops Review process as a core, non-optional responsibility.
  • Triage critical alerts related to backend service/platform health and monitor platform health KPIs for core Java services.
  • Handle "keep-the-lights-on" injects, track open incidents, and document findings in Confluence.
  • Keep sprint data current, including availability, carryover, and newly assigned points.
  • Maintain transparency on work status via tickets, standup, and Slack; follow established engineering and DevOps processes; collaborate effectively across the team and partner orgs; and complete assigned work reliably and on schedule.

Must haves

  • 3-5 years of experience as a software engineer.
  • Experience with Java .
  • Experience with Mulesoft .
  • Backend software engineering experience.
  • Hands-on API/integration experience .
  • Production troubleshooting and incident support experience.
  • Experience with production monitoring, alert triage, and service/platform health.
  • Experience in diagnosing and resolving API/integration failures.
  • Upper-intermediate English level.

Nice to haves

  • Salesforce Service Cloud integration experience.
  • Enterprise integration / middleware experience.
  • Message-based or asynchronous integration experience.
  • Data processing / data transformation experience.
  • CI/CD and DevOps experience.
  • Monitoring / observability tools.
