Job Description

The Senior Technical Product Manager is someone who is structured, analytical, and tech native, and can act as a bridge between our products and our tech teams. We are looking for someone who can be trusted to take the idea for a complex technical solution and diligently yet pragmatically do everything that is needed to develop it and get it across the line, analysing, escalating, designing, proposing, and coordinating a range of client and internal stakeholders.

You will report to our VP of Product, sit in our Product team and be the main product counterpart for our infrastructure tech team, owning the new and existing features and solutions they build. You will also work closely with the rest of the Product team, Customer Success and colleagues across the company. Around 90% of your time will be internal, with the remaining 10% client-facing: capturing requirements, onboarding clients and troubleshooting technical issues.

There is a lot to learn about the real estate industry, our clients and our products, so you will start with smaller, well-defined pieces of work and take on more as you ramp up. Within a year, we expect you to own a portfolio of technical features and solutions end to end, run client calls and POCs, and be a trusted right hand in delivering our technical roadmap.

About Restb.ai

We are a Barcelona-based scale-up, and world leader in generating AI insights for the real estate sector. We use computer vision, machine learning and large language models to examine real estate photos and listing data and turn them into insights that support property valuations across many subsegments and in 35 countries.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Own a portfolio of features and solutions built by our infrastructure tech team, both new and existing, from initial idea through release and ongoing improvement.
  • Capture and clarify requirements from clients and internal stakeholders, and turn them into clear, well-structured specs.
  • Act as the primary interface between Product and the infrastructure team for your features, coordinating delivery and keeping everyone aligned.
  • Analyse data and test outputs (including LLM and computer vision results) to validate that solutions work as expected and deliver real value.
  • Design pragmatic solutions and propose trade-offs between scope, quality and time.
  • Spot risks and blockers early, and escalate them with a clear view of the options.
  • Support clients with technical onboarding, integration and troubleshooting.
  • Get hands-on with LLMs and other tools to prototype ideas, automate tasks and test hypotheses.
  • Write clear documentation, such as specs, release notes and client-facing guides.
  • Over time, lead client calls, requirements workshops and POCs.
  • Continually improve the way things are done around you.

Desired skills and experience

  • Bachelor’s degree with a quantitative focus (Engineering, Statistics, Business Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, Maths, Economics, etc.), or equivalent experience.
  • 4+ years in technical product management, engineering, solutions or technical consulting roles, including leading delivery of complex technical projects. Experience in real estate is a plus, but not required.
  • Ability to truly understand and manipulate data. Someone who looks at data and sees a story and actionable insights.
  • A pragmatic, structured problem solver who uses a hypothesis-driven approach to get quickly to the best answer, backs up recommendations with data, and can clearly articulate the risks of each decision.
  • A track record of shipping solutions that delivered measurable impact, and of influencing stakeholders and leadership without direct authority.
  • Technically curious and comfortable around code: you don’t need to be a developer, but you understand how software gets built and can hold your own in technical discussions with engineers. Everything else can be learned on the job.
  • A strong and proactive communicator, with an active awareness of accountability and risk and how to escalate accordingly.
  • A fast learner with a high bar and a strong growth mindset: you take ownership, seek out candid feedback, and get things done.
  • C1-level English, with strong written and oral communication. This role is in English, resumes in other languages won’t be considered.
  • This is a hybrid role based in Barcelona, with at least 3 days a week in our office (many of the team come in more). Visa support is available for the right candidate.

Our hiring process

  1. Intro call, HR (30 min): your background, what you're looking for, and salary range for the role.
  2. Fit/behavioural interview, VP of Product (45 min): your experience, your values, and how you work. An opportunity for you to find out more about the role and the company.
  3. Live case exercise, panel (60 min): a short, realistic problem you'll work through live, so we can see how you think, communicate, and present. Closely directed, challenging, and fun!
  4. Informal team interviews: meet some of the people you will work with across product, engineering, and customer success.
  5. Offer.
