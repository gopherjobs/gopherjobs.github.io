Application Security Analyst (AI Training) About The Role Your instinct for spotting a real exploit versus a theoretical risk is exactly what's missing from most AI systems — and that's where you come in.

At Alignerr, we partner with the world's leading AI research labs to build and train cutting-edge AI models. We're looking for experienced application security professionals to help AI systems learn how to think like a security engineer: assessing vulnerabilities, prioritizing real risk, and recommending practical remediations.

This is a remote, flexible, contract role. You set your schedule and work on your own terms.

What You'll Do

  • Analyze realistic application security scenarios across code, APIs, and system behavior
  • Identify and classify vulnerabilities including authentication flaws, injection risks, access control issues, and business logic bugs
  • Evaluate secure coding practices and assess the quality of remediation strategies
  • Build and validate security-focused reasoning datasets that teach AI to accurately assess application risk
  • Apply your real-world instincts to distinguish genuinely exploitable issues from noise

Who You Are

  • 2+ years of experience in application security, product security, or software security roles
  • Solid working knowledge of the OWASP Top 10 and common production vulnerabilities
  • Strong ability to assess real-world exploitability — not just theoretical risk
  • Clear, structured written communication when explaining security findings and reasoning
  • Comfortable working independently and asynchronously on task-based assignments

Nice to Have

  • Experience with penetration testing, secure code review, or threat modeling
  • Familiarity with bug bounty programs or vulnerability disclosure processes
  • Background in API security, cloud-native applications, or DevSecOps

Why Join Us

  • Work directly on frontier AI systems with top research labs
  • Fully remote and flexible — work on your own schedule, 10–40 hours/week
  • Freelance autonomy with meaningful, intellectually engaging work
  • Your security expertise shapes how the next generation of AI reasons about risk
  • Potential for ongoing work and contract extension
