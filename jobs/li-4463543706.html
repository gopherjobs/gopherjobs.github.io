👤 Your role

  • You will lead the technical design and implementation of new partner integrations and platform features, blending technical expertise with product management to own the end-to-end delivery and business impact of your work.
  • You will own the full lifecycle of your services—from development and deployment to production monitoring, alerting, and incident response.
  • You will actively manage our AWS cloud infrastructure, writing and maintaining Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) to ensure environment parity and security.
  • You will evolve our integration strategy, championing a platform-first mindset that drives synergies across our integration ecosystem, while ensuring high-performance, secure communication between internal and external systems.
  • You will leverage cutting-edge AI-powered tools across the entire software development lifecycle—from system design and code generation to infrastructure automation, log analysis, and performance monitoring—to drive innovation and efficiency.
  • You will provide technical support and create high-quality documentation to ensure the long-term maintainability of our connectivity layer.

  • Observability: Strong experience setting up and maintaining monitoring and alerting solutions (e.g., New Relic, Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, or Datadog).
  • Technical Documentation: Ability to translate complex system designs into clear, actionable documentation.
  • Solid experience with modern backend languages (e.g., PHP/Laravel and Node.js/TypeScript).
  • Expertise in database design (MySQL, NoSQL) and optimizing queries for large-scale data.
  • AI-based automation: Proficiency in integrating and leveraging AI-assisted programming and agents in your daily workflow.
  • Fluency in English, with strong communication skills for customer and partner-facing situations.
  • Experience with containerization (Docker).
