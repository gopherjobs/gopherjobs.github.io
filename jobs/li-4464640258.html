About Us Forage is building the modern payments stack that powers inclusive commerce. Our technology enables grocers, delivery platforms, and point-of-sale systems to seamlessly accept EBT payments both online and in-store. Beyond infrastructure, we’re helping SNAP EBT shoppers stretch their grocery budgets every week, making healthy food more affordable and accessible to the 42 million Americans on food assistance.

Backed by leading fintech investors, Forage is a fast-growing startup with a clear vision and real-world impact, feeding tens of thousands of families daily. Our team is made up of kind, driven individuals who take ownership, move quickly, and collaborate closely. We value humility, curiosity, and a shared commitment to making a difference.

We’re not just building payments infrastructure — we’re helping feed tens of thousands of families each day, and transforming grocery access for millions more. Watch our story and see why we do what we do . The Role We're looking for Software Engineers who are passionate about building high-quality products that solve meaningful customer problems.

As a Software Engineer at Forage, you'll work across the product development lifecycle—from shaping requirements and designing technical solutions to implementing, testing, deploying, and iterating on features. You'll collaborate closely with Product, Design, Solutions Engineering, and fellow engineers to deliver reliable, scalable systems that directly impact our customers.

We hire engineers across multiple levels, from mid-level individual contributors to senior and staff-level technical leaders. The exact level will be determined through our interview process based on experience, technical expertise, and demonstrated impact.

What We're Looking For

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 3+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Experience building and shipping production software in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong understanding of software engineering fundamentals, including system design, testing, and debugging.
  • Experience working with cloud infrastructure, ideally AWS.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to work effectively across engineering, product, and business teams.
  • Passion for building products that deliver exceptional customer experiences.

Preferred Technical Experience Experience with some or all of the following:

  • Python
  • Django
  • TypeScript
  • React
  • React Native
  • HTML/CSS
  • REST APIs
  • Distributed systems
  • AWS cloud services
  • CI/CD and modern development practices

Leveling Expectations Intermediate Engineer (L2–L3)

  • Independently delivers features and projects.
  • Contributes to technical design discussions.
  • Collaborates effectively across teams.

Senior Engineer (L4)

  • Leads medium-to-large projects.
  • Drives technical decision-making within their domain.
  • Mentors other engineers and improves engineering practices.

Staff Engineer (L5)

  • Owns cross-functional technical initiatives.
  • Influences architecture and engineering strategy.
  • Raises the effectiveness of the broader engineering organization through leadership and mentorship.
