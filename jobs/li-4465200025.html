Description

At boost.ai, we’re redefining how people interact with companies. As a Gartner Magic Quadrant Conversational AI Challenger, we empower organizations around the world to deliver next-generation customer experiences through intelligent chat and voice automation.

Our Core Engineering team builds and maintains the platform and APIs that boost.ai's chat and voice automation run on, the foundation every other engineering team builds on top of.

We are currently seeking a Core Support Engineer to join our Platform team. This is a great opportunity if you enjoy problem-solving, collaboration, and working at the intersection of technology, documentation, and customer impact.

What you’ll do

As part of the Core Engineering team, you'll:

  • Own technical cases escalated from Operations, reproduce, investigate, and assess severity, then hand off to the responsible team once confirmed.
  • File bug reports and work with team leads on ownership and timeline.
  • Track bug root causes, avoidable or not, how critical, in collaboration with Customer Success.
  • Fix documentation and usage gaps at the source, and help build out Core Engineering's knowledge base.
  • Maintain our API and platform documentation.
  • Own database performance monitoring on a regular cadence, using our RDS analytics tooling to catch outlier queries and resource patterns.
  • Build and maintain your own tooling, using AI coding assistants, to speed up tickets, tracking, and DB monitoring.
  • Suggest improvements to Core APIs and the platform based on patterns you see in tickets and logs.
  • Act as the go-to person for how Core Engineering's APIs and platform work, mostly for internal teams, occasionally direct with customers.
  • Grow into a development role over time, if you show the aptitude for it, a path several people from our support teams have already taken.

You’ll thrive in this role if you have:

  • Advanced problem-solving and troubleshooting skills
  • Strong collaboration, communication and organizational skills
