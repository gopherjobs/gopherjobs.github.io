Description

Who We Are: Alpaca is a US-headquartered, global leader in agent-first brokerage infrastructure for stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, fixed income, 24/5 trading, and more.

Amongst our subsidiaries, Alpaca is a licensed financial services company, serving hundreds of financial institutions across 40 countries with our institutional-grade APIs. This includes broker-dealers, investment advisors, wealth managers, hedge funds, and crypto exchanges, totalling over 10 million brokerage accounts.

Our global team is a diverse group of experienced engineers, traders, and brokerage professionals who are working to achieve our mission of opening financial services to everyone on the planet. We're deeply committed to open-source contributions and fostering a vibrant community, continuously enhancing our award-winning, developer-friendly API and the robust infrastructure behind it.

Alpaca is proudly backed by $400 million in funding from top-tier global investors including Portage Ventures, Spark Capital, Tribe Capital, Social Leverage, Horizons Ventures, Opera Tech Ventures, SBI Group, Derayah Financial, Unbound, Peak XV, Elefund, and Y Combinator. Our Team Members: We're a dynamic team of 400+ globally distributed members who thrive working from our favorite places around the world, with teammates spanning the USA, Canada, Japan, Hungary, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK, and beyond!

We're searching for passionate individuals eager to contribute to Alpaca's rapid growth. If you align with our core values—Stay Curious, Have Empathy, and Be Accountable—and are ready to make a significant impact, we encourage you to apply.

Your Role: We're seeking an Engineering Manager for Canada to establish Alpaca's engineering presence in the Canadian market from the ground up. In the near term you will be very hands-on: architecting and building critical financial infrastructure yourself, hiring the first few engineers, and shaping the technical strategy for one of North America's largest and most sophisticated financial markets.

You will be the strategic technical leader for Canada operations. For the near term this is not a pure engineering management role: you will work with a small founding team, spend most of your time designing and writing production code, and stay close enough to the day-to-day work to make sound architectural calls. You will act as the key liaison between engineering and executive leadership, and work directly with finance, operations, product, legal, compliance, partners, and regulators (CIRO).

What you build here becomes the foundation for launching in Canada and for scaling to similar international markets. Success is measured by a working, compliant Canadian brokerage platform that partners can launch on, and an engineering organization that can run and grow it.

Location: Remote within Canada. Collaboration with APAC, Americas, and EMEA teams will occasionally require meetings outside regular working hours.

What You'll Walk Into

You won't be starting from a blank page. You'll own a defined build:

  • An emerging Canadian brokerage solution: an MVP for localized infrastructure and a Canadian Ledger instance, account creation and KYC, funding and foreign exchange (CAD), settlement, corporate actions, compliance and regulatory reporting, trading, and reporting.
  • A highly regulated environment: CIRO requirements, data residency, and disaster recovery and business continuity expectations shape architecture from day one.
  • A global core platform: you'll work with Alpaca's core teams to extend the shared framework for Canadian markets, currencies, and regulatory requirements, rather than forking it.
  • Partners waiting to launch: the platform must scale across multiple Canadian fintech partners, not just the first one.
  • A small founding team to build: a few engineers to start. Hiring standards, engineering culture, and practices for Canada are yours to establish, and you will write code with them.

Global collaboration
