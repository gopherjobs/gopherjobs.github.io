Description

A detail-oriented and experienced technical leader who thrives at the intersection of hands-on engineering and team leadership. You explore new technical frontiers, discover innovative solutions, and keep your technical skills as your foundation. This is a hands-on role — you will be expected to write code, conduct code reviews, and be deeply involved in day-to-day technical decisions. As a team player, you know when to ask for help and when to offer it.

What You'll Do

  • Drive digital transformation as the primary focus — design and deliver innovative ideas and solutions that go beyond market trends, align with stakeholder needs, and build flexible systems that scale up and out with demand
  • Work closely with the Head of Engineering, Head of Product, and other stakeholders to define the product roadmap and delivery timeline for each release
  • Lead one or more development squads within the Supply Chain tech team, providing technical guidance, mentorship, and direction to team members
  • Champion innovative ideas and solutions that enable the team to tackle complex problems effectively
  • Provide user story backlog sizing and recommendations, leveraging technical expertise to ensure backlog items are well-defined and sprint-ready
  • Uphold and enforce code quality standards to meet the team's engineering goals
  • Actively participate in designing and building complex systems and architectures

Open to remote candidates. Requirements

  • Strong communication skills in English, with the ability to effectively engage cross-functional teams and stakeholders across the organization
  • Proven experience and in-depth knowledge of backend OOP languages, preferably Java, Golang, Node.js or other open-source technologies
  • Some experience in programming languages like React, Typescript or Javascript.
  • Experience with relational database querying using SQL, MySQL, or PostgreSQL
  • Familiarity with GitOps environments such as ArgoCD, Kubestack, or similar tools
  • Experience with automated code deployment tools such as CircleCI, Git Actions, GitLab CI/CD, or similar
  • Experience with container technologies Docker and Kubernetes
  • Proven leadership experience, with a track record of guiding and growing engineering teams
  • Experience with complex systems and a solid understanding of open API standards and service-oriented architecture

Nice to Have

  • Experience in supply chain domains such as logistics, warehouse management, procurement, or inventory systems is a plus but not required
