Description

Why OpenRelay?

OpenRelay is GPU compute and LLM inference for people who ship. Customers rent GPU machines and call inference APIs; providers plug their hardware into our network and get paid for it. We run a distributed fleet on real hardware, not a reseller skin over someone else's cloud. Small team, high bar, and room for A-players to own things that are live in production.

No Degree Required, Show Us What You've Built

We care about what you can do, not diplomas. The profile we want most is simple: you built something on the web, and then you got strangers to use it. That last part is everything. Plenty of engineers can ship a site. Figuring out how people actually find it, ranking for a query that matters, writing cold email that gets replies, launching somewhere and converting the traffic, is the rarest and most valuable skill you can bring us. Show us what you built and the users you got on it: real traffic, real signups, real revenue.

What You'll Build

You'll own features end-to-end in one of three tracks (but at a startup, you'll touch all of them).

Fullstack Engineering

- Own and ship production features across the OpenRelay platform: the customer dashboard, the marketing site, the docs, and the internal ops portal

- Work across the frontend, API, and data layers in a pnpm monorepo, React and TypeScript on top of Go services

- Design and maintain APIs, background jobs, and workflows that customers depend on to launch and run GPU workloads

- Use agentic engineering tools (Claude Code, autonomous loops) to build the machine that builds the machine: configure the repo, CI, and workflows so features can be built, tested, and shipped autonomously

Platform and Infrastructure Engineering

- Work on the Go control plane that schedules customer workloads onto a distributed fleet of provider machines

- Build against the systems that keep it honest: scheduling, health reconciliation, metering, and billing

- Touch the real infrastructure layer: AWS, Terraform, Docker, Linux, networking, and GPU hosts

Growth Engineering

- Own programmatic SEO: generate and optimize pages targeting high-intent GPU, inference, and model queries

- Build automated outreach and SDR systems with agentic tooling

- Design and maintain automation workflows for scalable content production

- Run A/B tests and conversion rate optimization experiments with PostHog

- Build and test landing pages, and kill the ones that do not work

You'll own features from design through deployment and be expected to ship, test, and maintain production code.
