We’re looking for a Senior Backend Engineer (Python) who will join our team.

What will you do:

  • Design and build backend services in Python (FastAPI)
  • Implement async job execution and status tracking (Redis/Celery/Kafka)
  • Integrate with LLM services (internal & external)
  • Build and maintain external MCP servers that expose our platform to third-party AI agents and clients
  • Develop ETL pipelines and ensure data quality across ingestion, transformation, and storage
  • Ensure stability, observability, and cost-efficient inference

About you:

  • 5+ years Python backend experience
  • Strong FastAPI experience
  • Solid PostgreSQL + S3 (or equivalents)
  • Experience with async task queues (Celery, Redis, Kafka)
  • Production experience integrating with microservices: long-running jobs, retries, failure handling
  • Solid hands-on experience with agentic AI: building MCP servers, implementing agents, and working with various LLMs and other generative models
  • Genuine enthusiasm for agentic AI and keeping up with how the field evolves
  • Basic observability skills (logging, monitoring, alerting)
  • Experience in cross-functional teams (frontend, ML)
  • Experience with AWS infrastructure, Docker, Kubernetes

It would be great if you have:

  • Familiarity with LangChain or agentic workflows
  • Marketing technology background (ad platforms, creative tools)
  • Experience with StarRocks (or other OLAP databases, ex. Clickhouse)
