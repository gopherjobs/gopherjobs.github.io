  • Comfortable with REST API design, SQL, and microservices architecture

  • Cloud experience (any major provider), Git, and Agile ways of working
  • A collaborative mindset — happy to pair program and work cross-functionally

Nice to have

  • Familiarity with JavaScript/React (handy for working with their frontend team)

Why join them
