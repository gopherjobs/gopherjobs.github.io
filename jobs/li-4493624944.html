Software Engineer - AI Infrastructure | Sweden | Remote-first

I'm partnering with a well-funded, fast-growing Swedish AI infrastructure company building the compute infrastructure powering the next generation of AI.

As demand for AI continues to grow, they're scaling their high-performance GPU infrastructure and looking for a Software Engineer to help build the software layer behind it.

You'll join a small, senior engineering team as an early hire, taking ownership of the applications, backend services and automation that make this infrastructure accessible, manageable and scalable for enterprise customers.

It's an opportunity to work across product engineering, backend development and systems, with genuine influence over the architecture and direction of the platform.

You'll work across the software powering the company's AI infrastructure:

  • Customer-facing platform: Building the applications and interfaces customers use to manage compute resources, monitor usage and interact with the infrastructure. Primarily Go on the backend and TypeScript on the frontend.
  • Backend services: Developing the APIs and services that underpin the company's GPU compute offering and AI-related products.
  • Infrastructure automation: Building software, tooling and APIs to automate operational workflows, replacing manual processes with scalable solutions.
  • Systems and integrations: Working closely with infrastructure and operations teams to connect software with the underlying compute, storage and networking environments.
  • Production ownership: Taking responsibility for what you build, from initial design through to deployment, monitoring and ongoing improvements.

What we're looking for

  • Several years of commercial software engineering experience.
  • Strong experience in at least one of Go, Python, Rust or TypeScript . Go is the primary language, but willingness to learn it is more important than having used it already.
  • Experience building backend services, APIs or full-stack applications.
  • Comfortable working with production systems and taking ownership from development through to deployment.
  • Familiarity with containers, CI/CD and cloud or self-hosted environments.
  • A problem-solving mindset and the ability to take ambiguous challenges through to practical solutions.
  • Someone who enjoys working in a small, collaborative engineering team and having a genuine impact on what gets built.

Particularly interesting: Experience with Kubernetes, Terraform, Linux, infrastructure automation, monitoring and observability, distributed systems or high-performance computing.

Why join?

  • Early influence: Join a small engineering team where your decisions will genuinely shape the software architecture and engineering practices.
  • Interesting problems: Work at the intersection of software engineering, AI and high-performance computing.
  • Real ownership: Build software that directly supports the infrastructure enterprise customers depend on.
  • Growth: Join a well-funded business operating in one of the fastest-growing areas of technology.
  • Great team: Work alongside experienced engineers across software, infrastructure and operations.

The details

  • Location: Based in Sweden.
  • Working arrangement: Remote-friendly, must be based in Sweden!
  • Type: Full-time, permanent.
  • Language: Strong English communication skills.
