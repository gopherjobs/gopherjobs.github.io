About The Senior Software Engineer Role 🔍 As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll work across the stack: building features, designing and maintaining the APIs and services behind them, and increasingly building the AI-powered parts of the product.

Engineering at Motion is roughly three teams: Platform (cloud infra, DBs, centralised services and APIs), Harness (our agentic harness Runneth, the VM it runs on, and the primitives that the Runneth experience is built on), and Runneth itself (everything customer-facing). Where you land depends on what we need most when you start and where you’re able to contribute the most impact. What we do know: you'll be building AI-powered features across the product, and you'll own what you ship.

| Our tech stack : React, Node.js with Express, TypeScript, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Azure.

  • A track record of shipping things people use.

  • Real depth somewhere in the stack, you’re an expert at something. We'll ask you about an initiative or feature that you personally owned and built.

  • Any background: self-taught, bootcamp, degree, founder. We love talent from everywhere!
  • and you'll be a strong fit if you align with these qualities:
  • A strong sense of ownership: you make decisions, stand behind them, and fix things quickly when they're wrong.
  • A pragmatic mindset. You optimize for impact over perfection, care more about shipping real customer value than polishing every edge, and improve things over time.

You're probably not the right fit if

  • You do your best work with structure: a defined spec, a predictable calendar, 2 week sprint cycles
  • You struggle to context switch: You can only handle one focus at a time, switching between live issues and your work is frustrating, you tend to ignore Slack.
  • You want a steady, predictable pace: a roadmap that's set in advance and stays the course.
  • You're looking for a leadership role or a specific title
  • You're cautious or sceptical about AI, in general or as part of engineering. We're betting hard on agentic coding being the future; if that feels like a mandate rather than an unlock, it won't be a fit.
