Description

LanguageWire is a global leader in language and content services, dedicated to helping brands achieve their full global potential. Since our founding in 2000, we have continuously evolved and developed unique technology to facilitate seamless multilingual communication. Our worldwide network of language experts, coupled with an intuitive online platform, makes it effortless for brands to translate and manage multilingual content. 

The role you'll play

We're launching a new team dedicated to modernizing and expanding one of our customer-facing tools — and we're looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to help build it from the ground up. This isn't a legacy product handoff: you'll be joining at the very start, shaping both the technical direction and the team culture from day one. 

The current stack is Vue.js on the frontend and Python on the backend, but we're planning a migration to .NET in the near future. Because of this, we're looking for someone comfortable working across all three — Vue, Python, and .NET — who can help drive that transition while continuing to ship new features. As the senior engineer, you'll take a leading role in architecture decisions, technical direction, and mentoring as the team grows. 

The team you'll be a part of

You'll be one of the founding members of a brand-new team, led by our Tech Team Lead. We're actively growing the team and expect to bring on a Product Manager and one additional Engineer alongside you. This means you'll have real influence over how the team is structured, how we work, and the technical foundations we build on — it's a rare chance to help define a team's culture and practices from the very beginning, rather than stepping into something already set. 

If you want to make a difference, make it with us by...

  • Developing and maintaining applications, including front-end interfaces, back-end services, and databases

  • Contributing to the architecture, design and implementation of new features and improvements

  • Implementing best practices for code quality, testing and deployment automation

  • Ensuring high-quality software through active participation in code reviews

  • Actively engaging in sprint retrospectives, refinements, daily stand-ups, and other team processes

  • Taking full ownership of the products and solutions you develop, eliminating bugs early in the process through TDD/BDD and other tests

  • Leveraging AI-assisted development tools and workflows to increase engineering velocity, while maintaining code quality and rigor

  • Sharing knowledge and supporting fellow developers, QA professionals, and support teams

  • Supporting team leaders with your expertise and assist in customer meetings

  • Owning CI/CD pipelines to ensure seamless operations

In one year, you'll know you were successful if...

  • You are fully integrated as part of the team and have built strong working relationships.

  • You actively contribute to maintaining a positive, collaborative, and knowledge-sharing team atmosphere.

  • You take full ownership of products and solutions, having delivered several major features to production with measurable impact.

  • You share your expertise by mentoring mid-level and junior developers, helping them grow and succeed.

  • You contribute to improving team processes, code quality, and technical decision-making.
