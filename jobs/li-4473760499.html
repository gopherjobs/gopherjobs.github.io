WHAT YOU WOULD ACTUALLY BE BUILDING

Our client is building the AI system of action for UK lettings and estate agencies. It sits on top of the agency's CRM and runs the operational work: prospecting, viewings, maintenance, compliance and arrears.

A lot of that work runs through their voice agent, which picks up the phone when the branch can't. Every one of those agents depends on the right data, in the right shape, at the right time.

That data platform is yours to own.

WHY THE DATA PLATFORM MATTERS HERE

The product is built on agentic pipelines. The quality of what the agents do comes down to the data underneath them: what they can retrieve, how fresh it is, and whether you can measure when they get it wrong.

The stack spans Postgres, MongoDB and Qdrant on Google Cloud, with data coming in from agency CRMs and live operational workflows. You will know what it takes to make that reliable as the business scales across agencies.

THE ROLE

  • Lead and grow a small data team while staying hands on
  • Work directly with engineering leadership and the founders on data strategy
