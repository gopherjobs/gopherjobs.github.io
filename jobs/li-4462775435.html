Czym będziesz się zajmować?

PROJECT

We are looking for a Senior Java Developer with 7+ years of experience to join our high-performing team developing modern, large-scale backend systems for the travel and transportation industry.

If you have a strong background in Java development, cloud-native architecture, and enterprise-grade application design, this is your opportunity to take part in building the technology behind global travel experiences.

As a Senior Java Developer , you will lead a team to design, implement, and optimize backend services and APIs that power mission-critical systems used by millions of travelers worldwide. You’ll play a key role in shaping system architecture, ensuring performance, reliability, and scalability — while mentoring developers and contributing to a culture of engineering excellence.

YOUR ROLE

  • Design, develop, test, and debug complex software solutions
  • Design and build prototype applications
  • Research best practices, new tools, and technology.
  • Work closely with technical staff to understand problems with software and resolve them
  • Work closely with the Product team to understand business requirements
  • Assist in the development of software technical documentation
  • Support SRE in our effort to evolve CI/CD processes and stability

Kogo poszukujemy?

📍 Kraków (Hybrid - 2 days office / 3 days remote)

MUST HAVE

  • 7–9 years of experience in backend software development.
  • Strong proficiency in Java and related Java EE technologies.
  • Deep knowledge of Spring , Hibernate , and RESTful web services .
  • Solid grasp of OOP principles and design patterns.
  • Experience working with XML and relational databases (Oracle, SQL).
  • Familiarity with Linux-based development environments.
  • Experience with build tools like Ant, Maven, or Gradle.
  • Hands-on experience with Apache Camel and/or ServiceMix .
  • Strong communication and team leadership skills.

NICE TO HAVE

  • Experience working in Agile/Scrum development teams.
  • Knowledge of JMS, JBoss, JMX, Drools .
  • Understanding of TDD and experience with JUnit/unit testing .
