Job Description

Salary Range - £63,500 - £75,500

A Senior Platform Engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the platforms, tooling, and infrastructure that enable product and delivery teams to build, deploy, and operate software reliably and efficiently. They define and champion good practice through technical standards, evolving conventions, and shared tools and platforms that remove friction and empower teams to move faster and more safely.

A key aspect of Senior Platform Engineering is long‑term thinking: designing systems that remain resilient, scalable, and maintainable as time passes. Balancing immediate delivery needs with future sustainability ensures the continued success of the individual, the team, and the organisation.

A member of the Platform Team builds internal products and automation to support cross functionally. They focus on improving reliability, observability, deployment, and operational processes, while clearly documenting standards and enabling self‑service capabilities for engineering teams.

Responsibilities

As a Senior Platform Engineer at Inflo, you will:

  • Write high quality, maintainable code
  • Maintain and improve platform performance, reliability, and resilience
  • Contribute to and maintain technical designs and architecture
  • Bring innovation to platform and delivery practices
  • Optimise systems and services for deployability, operability, and scalability

Core values in action

  • Science – A Senior Platform Engineer demonstrates Science by taking a data‑driven approach to designing, improving, and operating platforms, using evidence, monitoring, and experimentation to make informed technical decisions.
  • Ownership – They demonstrate Ownership by taking accountability for the reliability, security, and maintainability of the platforms they build, proactively identifying issues and driving solutions through to completion.
  • Openness – They demonstrate Openness by sharing knowledge, documenting standards, and collaborating transparently cross functionally and welcoming feedback and diverse perspectives to improve outcomes.
  • Agility – They demonstrate Agility by adapting quickly to changing requirements, iterating on platform capabilities, and continuously improving tooling and processes to support fast, safe delivery.

Success within the first 12 month

  • You take ownership of proposed features, and are responsible for the successful end-to-end lifecycle from inception to delivery, directing the work of others as required.
  • You actively lead team ceremonies, offering strategic suggestions to improve working practices.

We maintain an optimal balance, embracing the agility and collaborative spirit of a start-up, unencumbered by rigid corporate structures, yet with the stability, resources, and professionalism of an established, international company.
