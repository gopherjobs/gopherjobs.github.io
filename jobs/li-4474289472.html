Description

We are hiring a Senior Data Engineer for a 6–12 month onsite assignment in Gothenburg, Sweden, with a leading global IT services company working for a major automotive client.

About the role

You will build and maintain data products and pipelines, with a strong focus on data quality, reliability, privacy compliance and high system uptime, supporting data-driven insights and decision-making.

Key responsibilities

  • Build, run and improve data pipelines through automation, scalability and performance enhancements

  • Ensure data quality, accuracy and reliability across all data solutions

  • Monitor pipeline performance and quickly resolve issues to minimise downtime

  • Implement testing, validation and monitoring processes

  • Work with privacy, legal and security teams to ensure data is handled according to regulations

  • Collaborate with analytics, product and engineering teams to deliver business-ready datasets

  • Maintain clear documentation of data flows, architectures and privacy processes

Requirements

  • 8+ years of experience as a Data Engineer, preferably with large-scale or sensor-based data

  • Strong SQL and at least one of Python, Scala or Java

  • Hands-on experience building and managing ETL/ELT pipelines on Azure, AWS or GCP

  • Understanding of data privacy principles and experience delivering compliant data solutions

  • Strong focus on data quality, monitoring and operational reliability

Nice to have

  • GDPR and data governance experience

  • Airflow, Databricks or Grafana

  • Kafka or other streaming technologies

Assignment details

  • Location: Gothenburg, Sweden (onsite)

  • Duration: 6–12 months

  • Start: Immediate

  • Employment: contract on Ampstek payroll

Work authorisation

Applicants must hold Swedish citizenship or a Swedish permanent residence permit (PUT). Sponsorship is not available for this role.
