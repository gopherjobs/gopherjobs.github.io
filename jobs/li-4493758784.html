✨The core mission of the Data Engineering Manager: In your first 6 months, you'll step into the day-to-day leadership of a 5 person data engineering squad, help drive our Snowflake-to-BigQuery migration across the finish line, and establish delivery rhythms, SLOs, and quality gates that keep our pipelines boringly reliable. Over the next year, you'll focus on execution and technical maturity: rolling out data contracts with upstream service teams, optimizing our GCP infrastructure, and ensuring our data platform reliably feeds HTB's AI/ML and Analytics workloads.

🍺 The fellowship you'll be joining: You'll lead a focused squad of 5 Data Engineers building and operating streaming and batch pipelines (Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Kafka, Airflow) and maintaining our BigQuery/Bigtable core platform. You will report to the Director of Platform and work closely alongside the Analytics Engineering, BI, and AI/ML infrastructure teams (self-hosted GPU inference, LLM platform) within the same department. Your key day-to-day partners outside the squad: Software Development, Infrastructure, Product Engineering, and Analytics Engineers who rely on your team's pipelines and data availability to build downstream models and products.

⚔️ Technology tools & weapons you'll be using:

  • Google Cloud Platform: BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow (Apache Beam), Bigtable, Vertex AI Feature Store
  • Distributed processing: Apache Flink / Spark
  • Streaming & messaging: Kafka (Aiven), Pub/Sub with Avro schemas and schema registry (Karapace)
  • Orchestration & transformation: Airflow, Python, SQL
  • Governance & DevOps: Data contracts, CI/CD quality gates, infrastructure-as-code

🚀 The adventures that await you after becoming Data Engineering Manager at Hack The Box:

  • Manage, mentor, and grow a squad of 5 data engineers, running 1:1s, sprint ceremonies, code/architecture reviews, and hiring to fill key skill gaps
  • Own the sprint-to-sprint delivery of the data engineering roadmap: break down quarterly goals into executable tasks, manage dependencies, and keep delivery commitments transparent
  • Execute the consolidation onto our GCP-native architecture (BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Bigtable), completing the migration off legacy warehouses and CDC pipelines
  • Implement practical data governance across engineering teams: enforce schema contracts, compatibility tiers, and CI/CD quality gates between upstream producers and the platform
  • Maintain strong operational discipline: monitor SLOs for data freshness and pipeline reliability, track BigQuery compute/storage costs (FinOps), lead incident response, and enforce documentation standards
  • Collaborate closely with the AI/ML team to ensure low-latency, reliable data ingestion for feature stores, model training, and AI product telemetry
  • Communicate technical trade-offs, project status, and infrastructure needs clearly to department leadership and peer engineering managers

🏆 Skills, knowledge, and experience points required to unlock the role of Data Engineering Manager at Hack The Box:

  • 5-7+ years in data engineering, including 1-3+ years directly managing or tech-leading a squad of 3+ data engineers in a product or SaaS environment
  • Proven track record building and operating a modern cloud data platform — ideally GCP (BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow); strong experience with another major cloud data stack also counts
  • Strong hands-on fluency with streaming and batch architectures: Kafka or Pub/Sub, schema management (Avro/Protobuf, schema registries), Python, and SQL
  • Experience implementing data quality and governance practices that engineers actually adopt: schema validation, CI/CD gates, lineage, and alerting
  • Pragmatic engineering leadership: able to jump into architecture discussions and PR reviews with your team while keeping sprints unblocked and stakeholders informed
  • Bonus XP: exposure to ML/AI data infrastructure (feature stores, model telemetry), cybersecurity or gaming/community-driven products, ClickHouse
