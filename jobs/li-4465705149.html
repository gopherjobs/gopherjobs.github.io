Description

  • Strong knowledge of system design, distributed systems, scalability, data modeling, APIs and event-driven architecture
  • Product-based / SaaS / startup experience is highly preferred
  • Experience in at least 2 of these areas: SaaS, Marketplace/Platform, Fintech/Payment, AI/LLM
  • Experience building or integrating AI into a product is a strong plus
  • Strong business and product mindset
  • Comfortable working directly with PMs, CTOs and international engineering teams
  • Fluent English – you’ll work directly with teams in the US
  • Hands-on mindset, you should still be comfortable reviewing code, designing systems and solving complex technical problems

💡 Tech stack: Node.js, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, AWS, Docker or equivalent technologies.

Node.js is not mandatory - strong Backend and Architecture fundamentals matter more.
