Job Title: Full Stack Developer — Backend Focus

Location: (Only in Canada) Remote / Flexible

⚠️ IF YOU HAVE ALREADY APPLIED, PLEASE DO NOT REAPPLY. We will review all applications.

⚠️ Canadian Citizens or Permanent Residents only!

⚠️ This is NOT a salaried position/employment. It is a Temp-Contract: Project-Based / Fixed-Term / Intern might be considered. Only apply if you are comfortable with this setup.

Overview:

We are seeking a proactive Full Stack Developer with a stronger backend focus and the ability to support technical project delivery.

The preferred candidate will contribute hands-on to the development of technical requirements.

You will work directly with the technical delivery lead/project coordinator, company owner and collaborate with the architect, developers, designers, DevOps, and other contributors to ensure architectural, functional, backend, data, workflow, and user-experience requirements are implemented correctly.

The selected candidate must be capable of contributing technically, particularly across backend design and development, APIs, databases, integrations, system logic, and full-stack implementation.

All source code and IP will be owned by the company.

Responsibilities:

  • Develop and maintain frontend and backend SaaS features using TypeScript, Next.js, React, NestJS, and PostgreSQL.
  • Build responsive, pixel-accurate UI components from Figma designs and existing design systems.
  • Develop REST APIs, database structures, complex workflows, search, filtering, and business logic.
  • Implement authentication, role-based permissions, data security, and English/French internationalization.
  • Integrate AI-assisted features, APIs, matching/ranking logic, and third-party services in collaboration with existing contributors.
  • Write automated tests, troubleshoot integration issues, and support CI/CD, deployment, and production readiness.
  • Collaborate with the development team to independently deliver assigned features from requirements through testing and completion.

Skills / Core we need:

  • Strong TypeScript, React, and Next.js (App Router, Server Components, Server Actions).
  • NestJS, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, and Drizzle ORM or equivalent.
  • Figma-to-code implementation, responsive UI, and accessibility.
  • Authentication, OAuth/OIDC, role-based permissions, and secure data handling.
  • Automated testing (Vitest, Playwright or similar), Git/GitHub, and CI/CD.
  • Practical experience integrating AI/LLM APIs into software applications.
  • Strong problem-solving, debugging, documentation, and independent feature-delivery capabilities

Nice-to-Have / Preferred:

  • Python/FastAPI, AI/ML workflows, embeddings, semantic search, and recommendation/matching systems.
  • Experience with SaaS marketplaces, complex business workflows, and subscription/payment integrations.
  • Docker, AWS, background jobs, caching, and production deployment.
  • Experience with internationalization (EN/FR), performance optimization, and scalable application architecture.

Deliverables / Expected Output:

  • Functional, integrated, and tested frontend/backend features aligned with approved designs and requirements.
  • Secure APIs, database changes, workflows, AI integrations, and automated tests.
  • Maintainable code, clear documentation, completed development tasks, and deployment support.

Education / Experience:

  • 1-4 years of relevant full-stack development experience preferred; equivalent demonstrated capability considered.
  • Proven experience independently developing, integrating, testing, and delivering production SaaS features.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with architecture, frontend, backend, AI/ML, and DevOps contributors.

Notes:

  • This is a temporary, project-based independent contractor engagement , not a salaried or employment position. Compensation is a mutually agreed fixed fee for defined project deliverables, not hourly or annual pay.
  • Payments are stage-based, with the majority deferred until final project completion, acceptance, and handover.
  • All work must be original or use properly authorized third-party components. All project-specific source code, documentation, designs, configurations, and deliverables, including associated intellectual property rights, will belong to the company under the applicable agreements.
  • NDA and IP assignment agreements are required before confidential project details are disclosed.
  • The role combines hands-on full-stack development with technical coordination, integration, and delivery support.
  • Material product, technical, and architectural decisions require appropriate review and approval. The contractor must follow established project requirements and architecture.
  • Shortlisted candidates may be required to complete a technical challenge as part of the selection process.

Eligibility

Candidates must be Canadian Citizens or Permanent Residents and must be legally eligible to perform independent-contractor services in Canada.
