🟢 Dev.Pro is a US-based software development company helping tech companies build their products and grow their business. We're remote-first, and we work with independent contractors in over 55 countries, so you'd be collaborating in a multicultural environment on some really interesting projects.

We invite a Senior Android Software Engineer with proven technical and team leadership experience to join our team. You'll be hands-on throughout the full software development life cycle - designing, coding, testing, and maintaining complex Android applications for a leading payments technology company. In an agile environment, you'll collaborate with cross-functional teams, guide and coordinate developers, drive engineering best practices, ensure code quality, and support technical decision-making.

🟩 What's in it for you:

  • Work with a Fortune 500 fintech leader driving innovation in payments across 100+ countries
  • Own the full SDLC, delivering secure, high-performance Android applications from design to deployment
  • Combine hands-on development with technical and team leadership, mentoring engineers and driving successful delivery

✅ Is that you?

  • 7+ years of Android development experience
  • Previous experience as a Team Lead, Tech Lead, or in a similar leadership role
  • Expertise in native Android development with Java and Kotlin
  • Strong knowledge of MVVM and modern Android architecture patterns
  • Hands-on experience with Android libraries and integrations
  • Knowledge of secure coding practices, including build obfuscation and encryption
  • Experience with encryption frameworks and utilities like HTTPS, TLS, SHA-256, and Cipher Block Chaining
  • Proven ability to lead technical discussions, guide and mentor developers, coordinate team activities, and support technical decision-making
  • Ability to prioritize tasks, manage competing priorities, and support efficient team delivery
  • Experience conducting code reviews and maintaining engineering standards
  • Excellent communication skills for documentation, planning, and cross-functional collaboration
  • Strong critical thinking, problem-solving, and attention to detail
  • High ownership, resourcefulness, self-motivation, and a collaborative mindset

Desirable:

  • Experience with third-party device integration (watches, payment terminals, etc.)
  • Understanding of client-server full-stack architecture
  • Understanding of Pub/Sub and Producer/Consumer messaging patterns

🧩 Key responsibilities and your contribution In this role, you'll drive Android development, combining technical expertise and leadership to deliver high-quality solutions.

  • Develop, maintain, and enhance Android applications based on business needs and technical designs
  • Own feature development from requirements to delivery, ensuring performance, security, and reliability
  • Work with developers, architects, testers, and analysts on technical designs for feature groups
  • Collaborate with product owners, BAs, and stakeholders to refine requirements and break down tasks for efficient delivery
  • Coordinate team activities, prioritize tasks, and ensure team alignment to support timely delivery
  • Guide and mentor developers, helping the team solve complex engineering challenges
  • Contribute to technical decision-making and promote consistent engineering standards across the team
