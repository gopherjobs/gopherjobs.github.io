【Job Requirements】

Please note that this is NOT an English-speaking role. Since our projects are located within Japanese companies, communication with local teams in Japanese is essential for daily operations. We will strictly contact candidates who meet the following qualifications:

[Must Qualifications]

  • Daily conversation level in Japanese (Equivalent to JLPT N4 or higher)
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Information Technology, Statistics, or Mathematics (Other Science/STEM fields are also welcome)
  • At least 1 year of experience in web or mobile application development
  • Basic knowledge of DBMS

[Preferred Qualifications]

  • Experience in developing applications using AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Azure
  • Experience in creating documents in Japanese

【Benefits & Characteristics】

  • Opportunities to work at major companies or startup companies using cutting-edge technology
  • Global IT engineers from about 60 countries are active in our company.
  • We recruit Global IT engineers for a full-time position.
  • Full support system such as Visa sponsorship, Housing allowance, and Housing search support
  • Commuting allowance (Maximum: 30,000 yen per a month), Social insurance, and Severance pay (if you have worked for more than 3 years)
  • Practical Japanese classes (for JLPT classes, casual classes for your family, etc.), Business manners, and technical skills training
  • The speed of selection from an interview to a job offer is about one week.

【About the Role & Projects】

At Human Resocia, we have business with 5,200 clients, including major automobile manufacturers, major electronics manufacturers, and major IT companies, so we can assign you to projects that fit your experience. You will be working on projects by clients.

There are many projects related to web application development, especially using Java, C#, PHP, Python, Node.js, JavaScript (React, Angular, Vue), etc.

Also, there are various roles such as back-end, front-end, and full-stack.

Recently, the number of mobile application developments using Java, Swift, Kotlin, React Native, and Unity are increasing.

[Project Examples]

  • Cloud application development on AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Azure
  • Metaverse and IoT development for smart cities
  • Disaster prevention information system and mobile app development
