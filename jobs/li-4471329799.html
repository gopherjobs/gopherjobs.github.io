Why This Organisation

Our client is a next generation cloud security platform company that uses runtime context to identify and prioritise critical risks across cloud infrastructure. Unlike traditional security tooling, they take a proactive approach — using real time runtime data for risk prioritisation and posture insights so engineering teams can focus on what actually matters. With eBPF powered sensors, agentless cloud posture discovery, real time threat protection and integrated API security, their platform delivers end to end cloud infrastructure protection across all major cloud providers.

The Role and What It Can Do For Your Career

This is a Backend Engineer position within a high performing engineering team working across all major cloud providers with a cutting edge technology stack. You will take full ownership of the lifecycle of projects from initial design through to deployment, monitoring and maintenance in production. You will contribute to technical and architectural designs, perform reliability and performance analysis and work closely with teams across Frontend, DevOps and Product to deliver high quality features.

The career value here is significant. You will be working on distributed systems problems across cloud native infrastructure in a domain cloud security, that is growing faster than almost any other area of technology.
