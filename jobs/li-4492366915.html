Summary

We are looking for a Senior AI/ML Engineer to design and build production-grade AI systems, with a strong focus on LLMs, RAG pipelines, and scalable ML infrastructure.

This is a highly client-facing / hands-on engineering role focused on turning AI prototypes into reliable, scalable production systems used in real-world applications.

Important Requirements (Must-Have)

  • Must be able to work in or overlap with EST (US Eastern Time) hours

Strong Fluent English communication skills (clear, structured, and responsive communication is essential)

Ability to learn new various fields

  • Comfortable actively participating in technical discussions and explaining design decisions

  • Reliable availability and responsiveness during agreed working hours

Compensation

  • Monthly budget: $2,000 – $4,000 USD

  • Long-term engagement potential based on performance

  • Contract-based, remote-friendly role

What You’ll Work On

  • Designing and implementing RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines

  • Integrating and optimizing LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, open-source models, etc.)

  • Building and maintaining vector search and embedding systems

  • Improving latency, cost efficiency, and response quality in production systems

  • Developing evaluation frameworks for retrieval accuracy, hallucination, and performance metrics

  • Deploying scalable AI services on AWS / GCP / Azure

Required Skills & Experience

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience with strong AI/ML production exposure

  • Hands-on experience with LLMs and prompt engineering

  • Strong experience with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS, Chroma, etc.)

  • Proven experience building RAG-based systems in production

  • Strong Python backend engineering skills

  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS / GCP / Azure)

  • Understanding of ML evaluation metrics and trade-offs (precision/recall, latency, cost)

Nice to Have

  • Experience with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar frameworks

  • Experience fine-tuning transformer models

  • Background in search, NLP, or recommendation systems

  • Experience with distributed systems or high-scale APIs

  • Experience in enterprise SaaS, healthcare, or finance AI systems

What We’re Looking For

We are looking for engineers who understand both AI models and real-world system constraints. The ideal candidate can balance accuracy, latency, and cost while building maintainable production systems. Strong communication and reliability are just as important as technical depth.
