This position combines Product Owner, Data Analyst and Scrum Master responsibilities. It provides end-to-end ownership, from requirements analysis and backlog management to Agile delivery and continuous improvement

👉Key Responsibilities:

  • Manage and prioritise the product backlog in collaboration with stakeholders.
  • Facilitate Scrum ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews and retrospectives. Work with developers, UI/UX specialists and business users to deliver effective data solutions.
  • Track delivery progress, risks, dependencies and impediments.
  • Gather and analyse business and functional requirements, translating them into clear user stories, acceptance criteria and process flows.
  • Analyse data collected in GCP/BQ to identify relationships, trends, issues and opportunities.
  • Act as a subject-matter expert on business requirements, data functionality and expected outcomes.
  • Support testing by ensuring that requirements and acceptance criteria are complete and testable.
  • Promote continuous improvement of the product and delivery process.

👉Required Skills:

  • Experience as a Product Owner, Business Analyst, Data Analyst or Scrum Master, ideally spanning more than one of these roles.
  • Experience in gathering and documenting requirements and writing user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Ability to work effectively with business users, developers, testers and data specialists.
  • Practical experience in managing and prioritising a product backlog using JIRA.
  • Experience working in an Agile or Scrum delivery environment.
  • Ability to design processes and data flows.
  • Strong SQL skills and experience in analysing and validating data.
  • Ability to communicate in English, both verbally and in writing.

Nice to have

  • Experience in supporting user acceptance testing and defect management.
  • Understanding of data modelling, data pipelines or data-refinery environments.
  • Experience with GCP, BigQuery and Confluence.
  • Experience in a large, complex or matrix organisation.
  • Understanding of data management principles, including data quality, lineage, data protection and data retention.
