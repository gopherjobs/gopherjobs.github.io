Description

We’re partnering with a well-known NZ product business that’s in serious growth mode and tackling genuinely complex platform and product challenges at scale. They’re looking for a Staff Engineer who loves being hands-on, can simplify the gnarly stuff, and enjoys lifting the technical capability of the people around them.

The Role You’ll sit at Staff level, working across priority initiatives while also having space to drive improvements you believe will materially move the needle. You’ll be a trusted technical voice, helping teams make good calls across architecture, delivery, and quality, and mentoring others along the way.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Owning and delivering high-impact engineering work end-to-end (design through to production)
  • Helping shape technical direction and making pragmatic trade-offs with the business context in mind
  • Working across a modern full-stack environment (primarily Python and TypeScript/React, plus some C#)
  • Untangling complex problems and landing simple, scalable solutions
  • Providing guidance across teams via mentoring, reviews, and strong technical communication
  • Navigating healthy technical debate and helping teams align on approach

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong full stack engineering capability, with depth in backend and confidence across modern frontend
  • Proven experience operating at senior/staff level (or ready to step into it) with broad influence across teams
  • A track record of solving complex problems with clean, scalable solutions
  • Great communication and people skills (mentoring, reviewing, influencing without ego)
  • Comfortable with change, and motivated by purpose-led work

Important To be considered, you must already have the legal right to work in New Zealand (NZ citizen/resident or an existing valid work visa). We can’t support visa sponsorship for this one.
