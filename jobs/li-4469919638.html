we are looking for a Quality Assurance Automation Engineer- English speaking.

Reporting Line

Software Engineering Manager – QA Automation

Role Overview

The Quality Assurance Automation Engineer develops automated tests and engineering tools that make software easier to test and releases safer to ship.

Working closely with Engineering, QA, Product and Release Management, this role improves testability, identifies risks early, and provides clear evidence about product quality and release readiness. The engineer will contribute to our test automation ecosystem, build tooling that enables efficient testing, integrate quality checks into CI/CD pipelines, and support the responsible adoption of AI-assisted development tools.

Key Responsibilities

Test Automation and Architecture

Design, implement, and maintain automated UI, API, integration, mobile, and end-to-end tests.

Build scalable automation frameworks.

Decide what to automate, at which layer, and what not to automate, based on risk and real production usage rather than volume.

Lead the consolidation of legacy suites into our current framework, retiring redundant coverage without losing protection.

Support multiple applications, clients, configurations, feature flags, and environments, including per-client pipelines.

Improve framework maintainability, execution speed, and reliability. Keep suites fast, stable, and cheap to maintain.

Maintain traceability between requirements, risks, automated tests, defects, and results in Jira and Confluence.

Test Enablement and Tooling

Design and build internal tools that enable developers and QA engineers to test software more effectively.

Develop utilities for test-data generation, environment setup, configuration, debugging, reporting, and local test execution.

Create reusable libraries, scripts, command-line tools, and CI integrations that simplify testing workflows.

Improve application testability by introducing appropriate test hooks, service abstractions, mocks, and diagnostic capabilities.

Integrate supporting services into the automation ecosystem, such as email/SMS testing (e.g. Mailosaur) and payment or third-party sandboxes.

Identify recurring testing challenges and solve them through reusable engineering solutions rather than one-off workarounds.

Document and support the adoption of testing tools across engineering teams.

Collaboration and Quality Engineering

Work with developers to improve application testability, observability, and error handling.

Contribute to risk-based test strategy with QA and Product, and challenge requirements and acceptance criteria that are ambiguous or incomplete.

Contribute to technical design discussions and promote shared ownership of quality.

Ensure the right checks run at the right time: on merge requests, deployments, regression cycles and releases.

Own failure investigation for your scope across application code, automation, infrastructure, configuration and data, and drive root causes to closure with the teams involved.

Review automation and tooling code and support other engineers in developing and debugging tests.

Make quality visible through metrics that matter: critical-flow coverage, stability, flaky-test rate, failure categories, and execution time.

Coach QA engineers transitioning into automation through pairing, code review, and shared standards.

CI/CD, Release Readiness, Investigation, and Reporting

Integrate automated tests and quality tooling into GitLab CI or equivalent CI/CD platforms.

Maintain appropriate test suites for merge requests, deployments, regression runs, and releases.

Participate in release regression and UAT cycles across staging and pre-production, and provide clear go/no-go evidence to the Release Manager and stakeholders.

Investigate failures across application code, automation, infrastructure, configuration, and test data.

Use Currents dashboards, Playwright traces, logs, screenshots, and videos to investigate failures and identify flaky tests.

Communicate test outcomes, risks, blockers, coverage gaps, and environment limitations clearly and early, with proposed mitigations.

Track and report meaningful indicators such as critical-flow coverage, execution stability, flaky-test rate, failure categories, and execution time, contributing to the team's QA dashboard.

Continuous Improvement

Research and introduce tools and practices that improve testing effectiveness and developer experience.

Contribute to performance and load testing using tools such as k6, JMeter, or Locust.

Use AI-assisted development tools (Claude Code, etc) to work faster on test development, tooling, analysis and documentation, while critically validating their output and respecting security, privacy, confidentiality and intellectual-property policies.

Critically review and validate AI-generated output while respecting security, privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property policies.

Share effective quality-engineering and AI-assisted development practices with the wider team.

What success looks like in the first year

Critical user journeys for your assigned products/initiatives are covered by stable suites running on every cycle and release.

Flaky-test rate and execution time on those suites are measurably down, with the data to show it.

At least one reusable tool or library you built is adopted by other teams.

Release readiness for your scope is evidenced by data, not by manual sign-off alone.
