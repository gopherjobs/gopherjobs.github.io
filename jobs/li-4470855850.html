About TAMM

TAMM is a unified digital ecosystem platform and portal that brings together over 1,000 government and private sector services in Abu Dhabi, UAE.

TAMM Operating System ( TAMM OS ) is a specialized, low-code software development and technology platform developed by the Abu Dhabi government, it functions as a backend ecosystem designed exclusively for government entities.

About the Role

The TAMM OS Developer is responsible for designing, developing, and deploying digital government services and workflows on the TAMM platform.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build responsive user interfaces aligned with TAMM Design Language System (DLS) standards.
  • Integrate TAMM services with enterprise applications, databases, and APIs.
  • Configure workflows, business processes, forms, and automation components.
  • Support application lifecycle management, releases, and compliance with government standards.
