As a TypeScript Developer (fullstack), you'll be at the forefront of creating custom software solutions tailored to meet unique customer needs. Your primary focus will be on contributing to the development of enterprise applications specializing in data analysis and reporting. You'll collaborate closely on the design and improvement of our TypeScript-based frontend, working with Angular and integrating seamlessly with our Java backend. We're looking for someone who thrives on problem-solving, welcomes challenges, and is dedicated to continuous learning and growth.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Craft custom software solutions to fulfil specific customer requirements, ensuring top-notch quality in all deliverables.
  • Play a key role in enhancing and maintaining our enterprise application geared towards data analysis and reporting.
  • Work collaboratively on the design and development of our TypeScript-based frontend, utilizing Angular and seamlessly connecting with our Java backend systems.
  • Take an active part in shaping the entire development lifecycle, from understanding requirements to implementing, testing, and documenting solutions.
  • Utilize your expertise to identify and resolve technical issues efficiently, ensuring smooth project progress.
  • Stay updated on the latest technologies and trends to improve development practices continuously.

Qualifications and Skills:

  • Hold a degree in (business) informatics or a related field, providing a strong foundation in software development principles.
  • Demonstrated experience in TypeScript development, with a solid grasp of the Angular framework.
  • Familiarity with Java and JavaScript programming languages; additional proficiency in these areas is a plus.
  • Ability to design and implement user interfaces for the web, using JavaScript libraries to enhance functionality.
  • Experience with testing frameworks like Selenium or Playwright is beneficial, with a willingness to expand your knowledge in this area.
  • Basic understanding of SQL, JSON, Git, and Maven to facilitate collaboration within development teams.
  • Fluent in German, with excellent communication skills for effective interaction with German-speaking stakeholders.
  • Comfortable with remote work setups, showing self-motivation and accountability in a distributed team environment.

If you're a proactive developer who loves TypeScript and enjoys tackling challenges head-on, we encourage you to apply and join our dynamic team shaping innovative software solutions.
