Job Description

Role Overview

We are seeking a highly experienced Principal Engineer to lead the design and evolution of the enterprise-scale platform architecture that powers Freshworks’ multi-tier SaaS products. This role will define the architecture strategy for a major platform domain, drive modernization initiatives, and establish best practices across service design, reliability, engineering quality, and cost.

The ideal candidate combines deep technical expertise in distributed systems, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, microservices, event-driven design, and large-scale production operations with strong leadership and stakeholder management capabilities — and remains genuinely hands-on, writing production code and leading deep debugging when it matters.

Key Responsibilities

Architecture & Strategy

  • Define and own the platform architecture roadmap for your domain, including the target state and a realistic path to it.
  • Design scalable, secure, and cost-efficient architectures supporting high-throughput, low-latency, multi-tenant workloads.
  • Establish architecture standards, design principles, decision records, and review practices.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies and own build-versus-buy decisions with measurable outcomes.

Platform Engineering

  • Architect and build cloud-native services and APIs that scale with global growth.
  • Design service decomposition, API contracts, messaging and event-driven flows, caching strategies, and database-layer design.
  • Treat multi-tenancy as a first-class design concern: isolation models, noisy-neighbour control, per-tenant limits, and cost attribution.
  • Lead platform modernization — monolith decomposition, migrations, and deprecations carried through to completion.

Reliability, Performance & Operations

  • Drive platform reliability, availability, performance optimization, and observability.
  • Take ownership of complex production issues in distributed SaaS environments, leading deep root-cause analysis and implementing permanent preventive safeguards.
  • Define SLOs, failure modes, resilience patterns, and capacity planning for the systems you own.

Engineering Excellence & Quality

  • Write clean, maintainable, high-performance production code and build proof-of-concepts that settle architectural questions.
  • Establish rigorous engineering standards through code reviews, automated testing, and CI/CD integration.
  • Identify architectural bottlenecks and technical debt, and drive practical, iterative refactoring without compromising business delivery.

Security, Privacy & Compliance

  • Ensure platform designs and team practices align with information security standards and regulatory requirements.
  • Design for tenant isolation, access control, auditability, and data residency from the outset.

Technical Leadership

  • Provide architectural guidance across multiple engineering teams.
  • Lead architecture reviews, design discussions, and technical decision-making.
  • Mentor senior and staff engineers, raising the bar through reference implementations and sponsorship rather than authority.
  • Influence engineering strategy and long-term technology investments; articulate complex trade-offs to both technical panels and executive stakeholders.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related discipline — or equivalent practical experience.
  • 14+ years of experience in software engineering, platform engineering, or systems architecture within modern distributed SaaS environments.
  • Expert-level backend engineering in Java, Python, Go, or equivalent — currently hands-on.
  • Proven experience designing and operating enterprise-scale, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, with real numbers for throughput, latency, availability, and behaviour under failure.
  • Strong understanding of distributed systems fundamentals: consistency and idempotency, retries and backpressure, concurrency, caching, and database design and optimization.
  • Demonstrated ownership of large-scale migrations or modernization initiatives with measurable outcomes.
  • Advanced production debugging skills — Linux internals, profiling, log analysis, and telemetry in distributed cloud environments.
  • Experience leading cross-functional architecture initiatives in SaaS or product organizations.

Good to Have

Data Platform & Analytics

  • Batch and real-time data ingestion, transformation, and serving architectures; streaming and CDC (Kafka, Kinesis, Debezium).
  • Lakehouse and modern data platform technologies such as Delta Lake, Iceberg, Databricks, Snowflake, and cloud-native data services.
  • Reusable data models, semantic layers, and curated data products enabling self-service analytics.
  • Data governance practice — metadata management, cataloging, lineage, and data quality frameworks.
  • Feature and training data architectures supporting AI/ML initiatives.

Adjacent Experience

  • ClickHouse, OpenSearch, or similar analytical and search engines.
  • AI-native product capabilities — RAG, agentic workflows, and LLM integration in production.
  • FinOps practice: cost attribution, showback, and efficiency guardrails at platform scale.
