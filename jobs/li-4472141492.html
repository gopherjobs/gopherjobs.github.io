Description

Role Description The Senior Software Architect with 8+ years of experience at Redacto is a full-time, on-site role based in Bengaluru. This position is responsible for defining and evolving the overall software architecture of Redacto’s data privacy platform, ensuring scalability, security, and performance.

This is a onsite job (No remote)

What you'll do

  • Own

the entire platform architecture across the Consent, Discover, Vendor, Privacy Governance, and Breach Management modules — from high-level system design to critical component decisions.

  • Drive

R&D: research, prototype, and productionize emerging capabilities — AI/LLM-driven classification, privacy-enhancing technologies, and next-gen data discovery.

  • Design

for hybrid and split-plane deployments — SaaS control plane with Kubernetes-based data planes running inside customer data centers and clouds.

  • Define

architecture standards, design-review processes, and ADRs; ensure consistency, security, and quality across engineering teams.

  • Partner

with product and founders on feasibility, technical trade-offs, and the long-term platform roadmap.

  • Ensure

scalability, multi-tenancy, performance, and reliability as the platform grows across large regulated enterprises.

  • Translate

DPDPA and regulatory requirements into concrete architectural patterns — data residency, isolation, auditability, and privacy by design.

  • Mentor

tech leads and senior engineers; raise the technical bar without owning people management.

Skills

  • Architecture:

Distributed systems, microservices, event-driven design, API-first design; multi-tenancy, data partitioning, and isolation models; HLDs, LLDs, C4 views, ADRs.

  • Systems & Data:

Deep backend expertise (Python, Go, Java, or similar); SQL/NoSQL at scale; streaming and messaging (Kafka or equivalent); data pipelines, CDC, and large-scale scanning/classification workloads.

  • AI & R&D:

Practical experience applying ML/LLMs to product problems — classification, entity recognition, retrieval; ability to evaluate build-vs-buy and model trade-offs.

  • Cloud & Deployment:

Kubernetes, containers, Helm, IaC (Terraform); designing systems that run as SaaS, on-prem, and hybrid; cloud-agnostic patterns across AWS/GCP/Azure.

  • Security & Privacy:

Encryption at rest/in transit, KMS, secrets management, zero-trust; privacy-by-design, data minimization, auditability; familiarity with DPDPA, GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001.

Qualifications

  • Strong expertise in Software Architecture and Software Design, with experience shaping complex, distributed systems.
  • Proficiency in Software Development and Programming, including hands-on work with modern languages and frameworks like GoLang.
  • Deep understanding of Design Patterns and their application in building maintainable, scalable software solutions.
  • Experience designing secure, high-availability platforms, ideally in data privacy, security, or compliance-focused products.
  • Ability to lead technical discussions, mentor engineering teams, and make well-reasoned architectural decisions.
  • Solid knowledge of cloud-native architectures, microservices, APIs, and modern DevOps practices.
  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross-functional stakeholders.
