About The Team The Database Engineering team is a distributed team responsible for providing 24/7 operational support for customer environments. The team also evaluates, adopts, and deploys new technologies and services into live production environments, ensuring they are reliable, scalable, secure, and production-ready.

About The Role Do you want to work for a company that works hard, has fun, and continues to experience significant growth? Do you want to be part of an organization where your contributions directly help scale and sustain that growth?

At Workday, we are looking for a Senior Site Reliability Engineer who thrives in a fast-paced, challenging environment and enjoys solving complex technical problems at scale.

In this role, you will support large-scale MySQL and open-source database environments across production and development systems, participate in an on-call rotation for critical production issues, drive automation to improve availability and scalability, and help architect both existing and new database services in our production environment.

About You Basic Qualifications:

  • 7+ years of experience managing databases for enterprise cloud applications at scale.
  • 3+ years of experience working with MySQL and/or PostgreSQL database environments in private and public cloud platforms, including AWS and/or GCP.
  • Experience with managed cloud databases (AWS Aurora, Google Cloud SQL) and core architecture principles (design, replication, clustering, HA/DR, backup/restore).
  • Background as a Database Engineer/Architect/DRE operating Linux-based, clustered open-source and NoSQL technologies (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis, Riak).
  • Proficient in Python or Go, with secondary scripting experience (Shell, Ruby).
  • Proven experience with Linux operating systems and containerised environments.
  • Hands-on experience with containerisation (Kubernetes, Docker), CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure-as-code tools (Terraform, Ansible, Chef, Git, Jenkins, Argo).
  • Hands-on experience supporting mission-critical production workloads on MySQL, PostgreSQL, or other open-source databases.
  • Proven track record supporting mission-critical, high-availability production environments.

Other Qualifications:

  • Deep knowledge of MySQL GTID, group replication, and Galera HA solutions. Familiarity with ecosystem tools like Orchestrator, Percona (MHA/Galera), Vitess, Ceph, Gluster, and Pure Storage.
  • Solid understanding of IaaS capacity management principles across AWS, GCP, and private cloud environments.
  • Proficiency with GitHub, Jira, and Confluence, alongside excellent technical documentation and communication skills.
  • BS/MS in Computer Science, a related technical field, or equivalent experience.
