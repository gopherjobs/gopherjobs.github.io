Description

FairMoney is a credit-led mobile bank for emerging markets. The company was launched in 2017, operates in Nigeria & India, and raised close to €50m from global investors like Tiger Global, DST & Flourish Ventures.

You will be working as a Senior Software Engineer (Identity) in our Technology Division, which is now composed of 75+ people in Lagos, Bangalore, Paris, Türkiye, and remotely across the globe.

We are building Engineering centres of excellence across multiple regions and are looking for smart, talented, driven engineers. This is a unique opportunity to be part of the core engineering team of a fast-growing fintech poised for more rapid growth in the coming years.

FairMoney  is a 100% digital bank that is revolutionizing the traditional banking industry. Our mission is to make financial services accessible and convenient for everyone through the use of technology.

As a Senior Software Engineer (Identity)  at FairMoney, you will work as part of our Identity engineering squad, building reliable and scalable systems that support identity verification, KYC, authentication, and other critical customer onboarding processes.

You will take ownership of complex backend projects, contribute to technical decisions, and work closely with other engineers and cross-functional teams to deliver secure, high-quality solutions. You will also have the opportunity to leverage AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering productivity and delivery.

If you are an experienced backend engineer who enjoys solving complex problems, building distributed systems, and working on products that have a direct impact on financial services, we want you to join our team!

We are looking forward to having you work on:

  • Designing, developing, and maintaining scalable backend systems for Identity, KYC, authentication, and related services;

  • Taking ownership of complex technical projects from design through implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance;

  • Building reliable distributed systems and services that can support millions of customers across multiple markets;

  • Working closely with Product, Risk, Compliance, and other engineering teams to deliver effective identity and KYC solutions;

  • Contributing to technical architecture, engineering standards, code reviews, and best practices within the Identity squad;

  • Identifying and resolving performance, scalability, reliability, and security challenges across our systems;

  • Using AI-assisted development tools and modern engineering practices to improve productivity and software delivery;

  • Mentoring other engineers and contributing to a culture of knowledge sharing and continuous improvement

🔧 Our Technical Stack

  • Our customer-facing product is an Android application;
  • It communicates with Ruby on Rails or Go-lang backends (Sidekiq, RSpec);
  • Our ML pipeline is API-Flask (Python);
  • We are hosted on AWS: Kubernetes, SNS, SQS, Lambdas, RDS Aurora MySQL and PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, S3, and Kinesis;
  • Prototype of a data warehouse on GCP (GCS, BigQuery, Cloud Composer);
  • We run our CI/CD pipeline with GitHub Actions
