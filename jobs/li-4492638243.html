Description

Sinch is a global customer engagement platform that provides communication services for businesses across messaging, voice, and email. Sinch's services are used by more than 150,000 businesses, including many of the world's largest tech companies.

ABOUT SINCH

Sinch is pioneering the way the world communicates. More than 150,000 businesses - including Google, Uber, Paypal, Visa, Tinder, and many others - rely on Sinch's Customer Communications Cloud to power engaging customer experiences through mobile messaging, voice, and email.

Whether you need to verify users or craft omnichannel campaigns, Sinch makes it easy. Our AI-infused Super Network, APIs, and applications ensure you can connect with your customers reliably and securely, at every step of their journey.

At Sinch we "Dream Big", "Win Together", "Keep it simple", and "Make it Happen". These values are our foundation!

DESCRIPTION

Do you want to grow as an engineer by building systems that handle millions of messages every day? In this role, you will work on backend applications that need to be fast and reliable, and you will learn from experienced engineers along the way.

You will collaborate with the team, share knowledge, and help build reliable, high-quality software in a collaborative engineering environment.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Design, develop and maintain backend services, taking ownership of technical solutions and contributing to architectural decisions;

  • Drive the development of scalable, reliable, and distributed backend systems;

  • Work with Java, Spring, Kafka, RabbitMQ, Redis, and MySQL to enhance our products, ensuring security, efficiency, and responsiveness;

  • Maintain and improve internal tools for monitoring, debugging, and optimizing applications;

  • Contribute to the observability, reliability, and fault tolerance of backend services;

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Software Engineers, UX Designers, and Product Managers;

  • Ensure high standards of code quality, security, testing, and software development best practices.

REQUIREMENTS

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Systems Analysis, or related fields;

  • Advanced/fluent English.

  • At least 8 years of professional experience in backend development using Java/JVM;

  • Strong experience with Spring Framework/Spring Boot;

  • Experience with SQL databases, preferably MySQL;

  • Knowledge of NoSQL/key-value databases such as Redis;

  • Strong Knowledge of asynchronous messaging technologies such as Kafka and/or RabbitMQ;

  • Strong understanding of microservices and distributed applications;

  • Problem-solving skills and experience troubleshooting backend applications;

  • Experience working with cloud environments, preferably AWS;

  • Experience with containerized environments using Docker and/or Kubernetes;

  • Experience with logging, monitoring, and observability tools;

BIG PLUS!

  • Experience with Kotlin;

  • Experience with Reactor;

  • Knowledge of gRPC/Protobuf;

  • Experience with event-driven architectures;

  • Experience working with high-scale distributed systems.
