Job Description

  • Own and evolve our AWS and Kubernetes infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), ensuring scalability, reliability, and security by design
  • Design, automate, and optimize CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI), embedding security controls (e.g., SAST/DAST) directly into development workflows
  • Lead monitoring and observability initiatives (e.g., Datadog), proactively identifying system risks and driving incident response and resolution
  • Define and implement cloud security best practices, including IAM, RBAC, vulnerability management, and infrastructure hardening across environments
  • Drive security initiatives such as patch management, threat detection (e.g., GuardDuty, Falco), and credential leak prevention across systems and repositories
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering teams, contributing to architectural decisions and leading security audits and compliance efforts

Qualifications

  • You bring 5+ years in DevOps/SRE and 3+ years in security-focused roles, with a proven track record in securing cloud-native systems at scale
  • You have deep hands-on experience with AWS and Kubernetes in production environments, including strong knowledge of IAM and RBAC models
  • You are highly proficient in Terraform and CI/CD tooling (GitLab CI), and comfortable coding in at least one language (Go, Python, or JavaScript)
  • You have solid experience with data storage systems (e.g., PostgreSQL) and a working understanding of data pipelines/ETL processes (e.g., AWS Glue)
  • You have strong expertise in cloud security, monitoring, and observability, including tools like Datadog, GuardDuty, Falco, or similar
  • You are a proactive problem-solver with strong communication skills, comfortable working in Agile environments, and fluent in English
