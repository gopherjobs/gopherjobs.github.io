Job Summary As a Principal Engineer in our SASE group, you will be a key leader in designing, developing, and delivering next-generation technologies for our SD-WAN team. You will be part of a world-class engineering team working on groundbreaking technologies and solving complex problems. We are seeking a passionate leader who takes ownership, brings new ideas to software development, and thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Implement URL filtering, anti-malware, SSL/TLS decryption, and Cloud Access Security Brokers for secure Direct Internet Access.
  • Work with 802.1X, RADIUS/TACACS+, and Identity Providers (IdPs) for authentication.
  • Manage IPsec tunnels and public key infrastructure (PKI).
  • Wrap and parse packets for VXLAN, GENEVE, GRE, and IPsec.
  • Perform Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) including stream reassembly and Layer 7 Application Identification (AppID).
  • Design, develop, and implement highly scalable software features for our SD-WAN solution.
  • Architect and implement large-scale distributed systems, ensuring high availability and performance.
  • Lead database design and implementation for custom use cases, including graph, time-series, and in-memory databases.
  • Proactively collaborate with development and quality assurance teams to achieve the best product quality.
  • Work closely with DevOps and Technical Support teams to troubleshoot and resolve complex customer issues.
  • Suggest and implement improvements to the software development lifecycle and team processes.
  • Mentor and coach junior team members, fostering a culture of technical excellence and applied learning.

Qualifications Required Qualifications

  • M.E./B.E. degree in Computer Science/Engineering or equivalent experience.
  • 9+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Experience implementing URL filtering, anti-malware, SSL/TLS decryption, and Cloud Access Security Brokers.
  • Experience working with 802.1X, RADIUS/TACACS+, and Identity Providers (IdPs) for authentication.
  • Experience managing IPsec tunnels and public key infrastructure (PKI).
  • Proficiency in packet wrapping and parsing for VXLAN, GENEVE, GRE, and IPsec.
  • Experience with Deep Packet Inspection (DPI), stream reassembly, and Layer 7 Application Identification.
  • Extensive hands-on programming skills in C/C++, Python, and Go.
  • Expertise in control plane, data plane, and security features of SD-WAN platforms, including multi-core/multi-process programming.
  • Deep knowledge of Layer 2/3 networking and routing protocols such as BGP, OSPF, IPSec, QoS, and NAT.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with DPDK for high-performance network packet processing.
  • Familiarity with machine learning concepts and their application.
  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCE, Kubernetes) and virtualization technologies (ESXi, KVM, Hyper-V).
  • Knowledge of application layer protocols (e.g., HTTP, SMTP, SIP) and TLS/SSL internals.
