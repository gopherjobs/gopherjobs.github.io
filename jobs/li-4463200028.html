Description

Software - Intern - Americas Delivery Mexico (ADMX)

About the Opportunity

Are you passionate about a career in technology? Together with our other delivery centers

around the world, we provide design, development, and sustainment services for our

clients, both pre- and post-implementation. As an intern, you’ll get the chance to work on

projects that will build on your technology and business skills, support organizations

undergoing large-scale transformation, and help our clients achieve a higher level of

service and business value.

You will be based in Queretaro, Guadalajara, or Monterrey and be a part of the ADMX

(Americas Delivery Mexico) team, serving our clients with international operations.

Deloitte Consulting ADMX is a member of the Global Delivery Network which has

presence across the world with Delivery centers in the United States, Romania, India,

Spain, China, and the Philippines.

We provide consulting services to help our clients achieve a higher level of service in

operational efficiency and business value. We are a team of professionals passionate

about serving clients with distinction and learning, and we are driven by our purpose:

making an impact that matters for our clients, our people, and society.

Work you’ll do

As a Software Intern, you will participate in a full-time, intensive training program lasting

up to 90 days. This program covers specific technologies, consulting skills, and includes

a shadowing period with our experts at Deloitte. Upon successful completion and based

on your performance, you may be eligible for a full-time offer as an Analyst.

Additionally, you will be involved in the following activities:

The software engineering intern has an engineering mindset when it comes to product

development. They like to take a systematic, disciplined, and quantifiable approach to the

design and development of end-to-end products/applications. They are seen as a builder

and innovator. As part of Americas Delivery Mexico, they are part of a cohort of Mexico

and US based software engineers, with a distinct cultural construct, collaboration

platform, developer tools, autonomy, firm processes, etc. They work with a team to

develop the mobile interface that employees and customers use to monitor, plan, access,

track, and view its products.

The team

Our ADMX team is comprised of deeply passionate and experienced professionals who

guide clients through their most complex challenges. We collaborate with our clients to

develop leading practices and support organizations in achieving excellence through

implementing solutions that transform how they organize, develop capabilities, and

deploy leading technology.
