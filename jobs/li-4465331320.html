Company Description

Chisholm Clarke is a New Zealand-based legal search firm known for confidential, expert career guidance for lawyers and legal professionals. In addition to tailored job search support, the firm provides detailed market insights, practical advice, and private recruitment services for individuals at all career stages. Chisholm Clarke contributes to the legal community through initiatives such as the “What a Lawyer” podcast, the “New Lawyer Fund,” graduate interview workshops and sponsorship of an annual charity golf tournament for (yep, you guessed it) Lawyers. Since its founding in 2022, the firm has received multiple industry awards, including Service Provider of the Year (2024 and 2025) to the legal industry and Seek’s 2025 Agency of the Year, and is recognized as a “Recommended Recruiter” by Sourcr.

Role Description

Through the success of the What a Lawyer Podcast, an opportunity has presented itself to go one step further, to provide more meaningful conversations and a place to house everything.

The Application Developer is a contract role to build an MVP, will be based on-site in Christchurch and will be responsible for architecture, development, testing, and maintaining this software application. Once the current .html screen designs and build readiness brief has been understood, the day-to-day tasks include:

  • Writing clean, efficient code
  • Implementing application features
  • Managing integrations between internal systems and third-party platforms,
  • Rigorous testing and deployment
  • Ensuring data quality, security and storage within chosen tech support systems.

The Application Developer will also contribute to user interface enhancements, performance optimisation, and general best practices across web-based tools used by the firm and its users.

Qualifications

  • Strong software development and programming skills, with experience in building and maintaining production-ready applications
  • Practical experience working with databases, including data modeling, queries, and performance optimisation
  • Proficiency with Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) and related front-end technologies to support responsive, user-friendly interfaces
  • Experience with system and API integration, connecting applications with external services and internal tools
  • Familiarity with common web development frameworks and languages (e.g. Next JS, React, Vercel, Supabase, Github, Stripe etc)
  • Understanding of secure coding practices, data privacy considerations, and basic DevOps or deployment workflows
  • Ability to work collaboratively with non-technical stakeholders, translate business needs into technical requirements, and communicate clearly and efficiently
  • Relevant tertiary qualification in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or equivalent practical experience

For the right candidate, ongoing support work will be available after the initial MVP build for debugging, hosting and security checks, then the remainder of the build to complete the full app.
