Description

We are recruiting for a Senior Software Engineer with strong Haskell or PureScript experience to join a high-calibre technology team based in Dublin.

This is an exciting opportunity to work on the software and engineering platforms behind next-generation streaming and digital experiences , operating at significant scale and supporting millions of customers.

The Dublin engineering organisation is focused on solving complex technology challenges across high-performance backend services, distributed systems, streaming platforms, automotive technology, advertising technology, data science and analytics .

Haskell or PureScript is the primary language requirement. Scala experience is not required. The team is looking for strong functional programmers who can bring deep expertise in Haskell or PureScript and apply that knowledge to challenging distributed-systems problems.

The successful candidate, if they not already have experience with Scala, will be gain significant exposure to Scala while in the role, including the Typelevel ecosystem and modern functional Scala libraries .

This makes the role particularly interesting for experienced Haskell or PureScript engineers who want to move into Scala without having to compromise on their functional programming background.

The Technology

You’ll be working on large-scale software platforms where performance, resilience, scalability and correctness are critical.

The engineering challenges include:

  • High-throughput backend services supporting millions of users.
  • Distributed systems designed for high availability and resilience.
  • Streaming and real-time services.
  • APIs and service-to-service integrations operating at significant scale.
  • Cloud-native infrastructure and containerised workloads.
  • Data-intensive systems supporting analytics and decision-making.
  • Technology platforms supporting connected and automotive experiences.
  • Advertising technology and high-scale digital services.
  • Observability, monitoring and production operations across critical systems.

The engineering culture has a strong emphasis on functional programming, strong typing, concurrency, composability and reliable software design .
