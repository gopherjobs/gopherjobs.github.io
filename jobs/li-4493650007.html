Kreditz is transforming how businesses assess credit and risk using real-time data and NextGen Credit Scoring solutions. About The Position Kreditz is looking for a Senior Software Engineer, Backend, to join our Tech team.

You will design, build and operate the backend services behind our platform as we continue to modernise our infrastructure. You will shape backend architecture, define clear contracts between services, and keep our platform robust, secure and efficient.

We operate in a regulated environment. Kreditz AB is a licensed account information service provider under PSD2, supervised by Finansinspektionen, and Kreditz Data Sweden AB holds a credit reference licence from IMY. Security, auditability and data protection are part of how we build, not an afterthought.

How We Work With Ai AI agents are part of how we build software every day. We expect you to use them to move faster, and to remain fully accountable for what ships. At Kreditz, seniority means judgement: breaking problems down, specifying the right thing, reviewing generated code critically, and knowing when not to trust it.

RESPONSIBILITES

  • Drive the modernisation of our platform, moving existing functionality to new services step by step while keeping production stable
  • Design, develop and operate backend services and APIs, primarily in Go
  • Define and evolve backend architecture, including service boundaries and API contracts between microservices
  • Build and deploy services on AWS using ECS, Fargate and Service Connect, with infrastructure as code in Terraform and CI/CD in GitHub Actions
  • Own your services in production, including observability, incident handling and continuous improvement
  • Build security, auditability and data protection into designs from the start
  • Review code, both your colleagues’ and AI-generated, and raise the quality bar across the team
  • Work with event-driven and serverless technologies (SQS, SNS, Lambda)
  • Help shape how the team uses AI agents in its development workflow
  • Document architecture, processes and integrations
  • Participate in an on-call rotation as we develop our operational model

WE ARE LOOKING FOR An experienced engineer who combines strong backend fundamentals with architectural thinking, and who enjoys taking ownership in a fast-moving, regulated environment.
