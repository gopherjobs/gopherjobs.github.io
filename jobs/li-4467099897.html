About The Role We’re looking for an experienced backend software engineer to help drive and evolve our product. Our technology is new and rapidly evolving: you’ll be an early contributor with a substantial impact on the architecture and be solving multiple exciting technical challenges!

What You’ll Do

  • You'll design, extend and optimize a large-scale, highly scalable, and fault-tolerant system that handles large graph datasets at enterprise scale. Our product is processing billions of events from hundreds of thousands of endpoints in real-time with sub-second latency requirements
  • You'll architect and implement Security products to protect customers data
  • You'll be solving real-world scaling problems that require thorough performance analysis and troubleshooting skills
  • You get to work with a modern and constantly evolving microservices-based software stack which includes Go, Kubernetes, BigTable, BigQuery, Spanner, Redis, Docker, Etcd
  • You'll write secure-by-design hardened software that must withstand real-world attacks, while processing untrusted data and communicating with hundreds of thousands of endpoints over the internet

Who You Are

  • You have a strong track record in building backends that are actively used at scale. You contributed significantly to and extended their architecture.
  • You have experience developing large applications in Go.
  • You used databases at a very large scale. Knowledge of the GCP services is a plus
  • You have experience with profiling and optimizing the distributed code that uses large databases and streaming platforms.
  • If you have experience in building connectors that integrate at API level with SaaS applications commonly used in the enterprises let us know
  • You have hands-on experience with microservices and Kubernetes
  • You have excellent verbal and written communication skills in English and you can be available to frequently communicate with distributed teams in Europe and US
  • You thrive in fast paced environments tackling ambiguous challenges and you enjoying shipping with velocity
  • You have a customer centric mindset and enjoy direct feedback from customers
  • You are based in Bangalore for the India location (Remote for EU based talent)

Joining Cyberhaven means joining the team building Data Security for the Agentic Enterprise. Traditional tools fall short. Cyberhaven traces the full lifecycle of your data, adapting protection to changing context, so protection moves with the way people actually work, not against it. AI Changed Work. We Protect It.

Backed by $250M from leading investors like Khosla and Redpoint, our team includes leaders who built industry-defining technologies at CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Meta, Google, and more. This role lets you shape the future of data security, working alongside people driven to protect workflows, not just data, for customers who cannot afford to slow down to stay safe.
