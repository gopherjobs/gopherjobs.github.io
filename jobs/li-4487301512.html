About:

We believe clear, actionable data changes lives. For a decade, our mission-driven organization has partnered with school districts, municipalities, and education non-profits across the country to deliver custom data visualization dashboards that empower leaders to make sound, student-centered decisions.

We are seeking an experienced Senior Full Stack Engineer to report directly to the Director of Technology and scale our core Partner Portal. In this role, you will refactor and re-engineer foundational web applications, implement high-impact features, and shape a secure, intuitive experience for decision-makers.

Work Set-up: 100% Work from home

Work Schedule: Night Shift - Monday to Friday, 11:00 PM - 8:00 AM PH Time

Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Architect & Re-Engineer: Lead refactoring initiatives across the Partner Portal to support growth, optimize full-stack performance, and maintain clean architectural patterns.
  • Build End-to-End Features: Partner closely with the product innovation team to translate Figma designs into responsive, accessible interfaces backed by robust APIs.
  • Uphold Quality & Security: Write clean, modular, tested code with meticulous attention to detail, automated testing, documentation, and rigorous data confidentiality standards.
  • Modernize Tech Stack: Work across a modern Python/JS ecosystem while expanding into containerized environments (Docker) and exploring backend microservices in Go.

Skills and Qualifications:

  • 4+ years of Full-Stack Development building web applications backed by Python (Flask, SQLAlchemy, Django, or FastAPI) and SQL (MS SQL Server).
  • 4+ years of Frontend Engineering using modern JavaScript frameworks ( Vue, React, etc. ), semantic HTML, and modern CSS.
  • Design-to-Code Fluency: Proven ability to translate Figma mockups into pixel-clean, interactive user interfaces.
  • Containerization & APIs: Hands-on experience integrating 3rd-party APIs (SSO, data feeds) and working with Docker / Docker Compose .
  • Testing & Maintainability: A disciplined approach to unit/integration testing, clear code documentation, and version control.
  • Remote Independence: Thrives in an autonomous, collaborative, remote-first setup with strong written communication skills (Slack, Zoom).
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree required.

Not Required But Bonus Points If You Have:

  • Experience or interest in learning Go .
  • Background in EdTech or handling sensitive student data.
  • Experience with cloud/DevOps, remote debugging (Docker exec, Flask CLI, SSH), and Unix environments.
  • A pragmatic approach to integrating AI tools into development workflows without sacrificing code quality or understanding.
