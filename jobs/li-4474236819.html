CloudOps Engineer

Remote (Ecuador)

Contract

The CloudOps team is responsible for the stability, performance, security, and continuous improvement of Client's cloud platforms and production environments. We partner closely with Engineering, Security, Support, and Product to keep customer-facing systems healthy, automate repeatable operations, and continuously raise the bar on reliability and operational excellence. This role is part of a collaborative, improvement-minded team that values clear communication, documentation, and pragmatic problem-solving.

About The Role:

We are seeking a CloudOps Engineer to support our Cloud Operations team. In this role, you will contribute to the strategic direction and execution of our cloud initiatives, ensuring the health and reliability of our production environments while driving transformational change.

Primary Objectives:

Support the reliability, performance, and security of cloud‑based infrastructure

Assist in managing production and non‑production environments

Contribute to infrastructure automation and standardization using Infrastructure as Code

Help ensure SLA compliance and adherence to security and data‑handling policies

What You Will Be Doing:

Deploy, manage, and support cloud infrastructure with an emphasis on AWS and Terraform

Maintain application infrastructure supporting customer‑facing systems

Assist in modernizing and improving legacy infrastructure using cloud‑native services

Support production and non‑production environments for deployment, testing, and troubleshooting

Participate in an on-call rotation to support production systems and incident response as needed

Diagnose and resolve infrastructure and application issues related to performance, reliability, and scalability

Follow established change management and operational procedures

Contribute to documentation, runbooks, and operational guidelines

Monitor systems using observability tools and escalate issues as needed

Collaborate closely with CloudOps, Engineering, and other teams to support platform stability

What We Are Looking For:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent practical experience

2–4 years of experience in cloud engineering, DevOps, or a related role

Practical experience with at least one major cloud platform (AWS preferred; Azure or GCP acceptable)

Working knowledge of Infrastructure as Code concepts (Terraform experience strongly preferred)

Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus, New Relic)

Understanding of basic security best practices in cloud environments

Strong troubleshooting and problem‑solving skills

Ability to work collaboratively and communicate clearly with teammates

What will Make You Stand Out:

Hands‑on experience with Terraform in real production environments

Exposure to re‑architecting or improving existing infrastructure

AWS certifications or active progress toward certification
