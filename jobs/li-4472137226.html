Description

Qliro is all about safe and simple payments. We are a payment provider who loves to help customers purchase online without even thinking about the payment part. We are also a dedicated payment partner to our merchants who serve customers every day. Since our start in 2014 we’ve grown into becoming one of the most loved payment providers in the Nordics, working with some of the largest e-commerce companies to provide safe and simple payments. Flexible payments is building the products that enable our merchants' customers to pay in a flexible, easy and secure way. We are working on greenfield projects in a highly modern microservices and cloud environment, mainly backend. This is an international team characterised by a high seniority.
