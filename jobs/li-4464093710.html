About Reonic

At Reonic, we're building the AI-native operating system for renewable installers.

We're a well-funded VC-backed startup reshaping how the world uses energy. With top-tier people, great investors, and massive goals ahead. We work hard and move fast, to build a company that lasts, and that brings lasting change.
