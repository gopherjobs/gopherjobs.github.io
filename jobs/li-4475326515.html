About The Role Yuno is looking for a Staff Engineer to be the senior technical leader for Trust & Vault — the domain that secures card data and optimizes how it's used across every transaction on the platform. You'll architect the foundational systems behind PCI-compliant card vaulting and tokenization, card authentication (3DS, network tokenization), and the optimization capabilities that lift authorization and acceptance rates, and raise the technical bar for the pod that builds them. This is a hands-on leadership role in a zero-tolerance-for-failure environment. You'll design and build bank-grade, resilient capabilities, champion a "You Build It, You Run It" culture where your team owns its services from code to production, and turn the hardest problems in card-data security and payment optimization into elegant, maintainable architecture. What This Domain Owns Card vaulting & tokenization — tokenize, store, and retrieve raw card data within the PCI boundary Authentication — 3DS / SCA and network tokenization to authenticate cardholders and strengthen every transaction Payment optimization — lift authorization and acceptance rates through network tokens, account updater, and intelligent use of vaulted data Account updater & portfolio migration — keep stored card credentials current to reduce declines, and migrate card portfolios between vaults and processors How AI Shows Up in This Role The tokenization, authentication, and vaulting layer you own is foundational infrastructure for an AI-native platform — the secure card-data foundation every transaction, including agent-driven flows, depends on AI is our default execution layer: you're expected to use AI-assisted tooling fluently across design, implementation, testing, and review, and to shape how the domain adopts it Your Contribution Will Be Domain architecture and technical leadership — own the architecture of Trust & Vault across vaulting, authentication, optimization, account updater, and portfolio migration; set the technical direction with versioned APIs and event-driven design, and raise the bar for the pod Card-data security and vaulting — architect the systems that tokenize, store, and retrieve raw card data within the PCI boundary, safely and at scale Authentication — build and evolve authentication capabilities (3DS/SCA, network tokenization) that strengthen transactions without adding friction Payment optimization — design capabilities that lift authorization and acceptance rates — network tokens, account updater, and smart use of vaulted data — turning secure card data into better payment outcomes Bank-grade, resilient systems — design headless, PCI-compliant capabilities engineered for a zero-tolerance-for-failure environment, where feature velocity never compromises reliability or compliance You Build It, You Run It — champion a YBIYRI culture: your team designs for multi-tenancy, elastic scaling, and automated observability, and owns its microservices in production Mentorship and cross-functional collaboration — mentor engineers and partner with Product, the CTO Office, and Staff Engineers across domains to eliminate silos and deliver complex, cross-team work What Success Looks Like Within your first 6–12 months, you've led the architecture of at least one core Trust & Vault capability (vaulting, authentication, or optimization), your pod owns its services in production under a YBIYRI model, and you're the technical voice Yuno trusts on card-data security, authentication, and PCI-compliant design.
