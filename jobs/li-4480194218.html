Position Overview Senior Software Engineers are members of our product teams, responsible for creating, enhancing, and supporting common features. Working side-by-side with Product Owners, Software Test Engineers, and Business

Stakeholders are actively involved in the complete software development life cycle in an agile environment, including technical design, hands-on coding, unit testing, integration testing, performance tuning, maintenance, test automation, deployments, and upgrades.

They also work closely with DevOps, DBAs, and Systems Engineers to make deployments easier to improve team velocity and overall quality of delivery. They design, develop, and contribute to some of the most important functionality happening in the product category, and also mentor, coach, and empower technical team members while still taking a very active role in developing solutions.

Required Experience

  • 5+ years of hands-on experience in mobile front-end development, with strong proficiency in client-side technologies such as HTML, JavaScript, CSS, and asynchronous data fetching.
  • Strong understanding of mobile UX best practices and responsive design principles.
  • Experience building reusable components and front-end libraries for future use.
  • Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams and stakeholders to design and ship new features.
  • Expertise in front-end technologies, including JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, React, Angular, and CSS.

Nice To Have
