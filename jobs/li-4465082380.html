🚀 Join a Global Technology Team as a Software Engineer (Java / Backend Focus)!

We are looking for a Software Engineer passionate about building scalable, high-performance solutions to join a global technology team. This role is primarily focused on Backend Development (60%) , with additional involvement in Frontend initiatives (40%) , offering the opportunity to work with modern cloud-native technologies, distributed systems, and large-scale applications.

🌎 Location & Benefits

📍 100% Remote within Colombia

📄 Permanent / Indefinite-term Contract

🕐 Monday to Friday | 40 hours per week

💰 Salary: Up to COP $8,000,000/month

✅ Full legal benefits package

🎯 What You'll Be Doing

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable software solutions.
  • Build and enhance backend services using Java and Spring Boot .
  • Develop and integrate RESTful APIs and microservices.
  • Participate in architecture discussions and technical decision-making.
  • Implement automated testing strategies across multiple testing layers.
  • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation.
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize application performance and reliability.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an agile environment.
  • Support frontend development initiatives when needed.

✅ What We're Looking For

  • 5+ years of experience developing high-quality software solutions.
  • Strong knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and Java .
  • Hands-on experience with:
  • Java
  • Spring Boot
  • Docker
  • Kubernetes (K8s)
  • Experience with RESTful architectures and microservices .
  • Strong background in test automation, including unit, integration, regression, and load testing.
  • Experience with AWS cloud services.
  • Knowledge of GitLab CI/CD and Terraform .
  • Experience with PostgreSQL or other relational databases.
  • Understanding of scalable, low-latency, distributed systems.
  • Experience with Kafka , AWS SQS , New Relic , and Splunk .

⭐ Nice to Have

  • Frontend development experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, or React .
  • Experience working with high-traffic customer-facing applications.
  • Cloud-native and distributed systems experience.
  • Strong communication skills and experience collaborating with global teams.
