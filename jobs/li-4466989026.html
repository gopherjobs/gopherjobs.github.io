Description

As a Backend Developer at Logicc, you'll play an active role in shaping the future of AI applications. Working in our agile team, you'll see the direct impact of your work on our users while helping drive one of the most exciting technology fields of our time.

If you don't meet every qualification listed below, we still encourage you to apply. We value curiosity, integrity, and a high level of initiative more than checking every box.

Our working language is English - German is not required.

Tasks

  • Develop and enhance internal tools and backend services using Go, NodeJS or TypeScript

  • Design, maintain, and optimize PostgreSQL databases, including schema design, migrations, and performance tuning

  • Build and maintain data synchronization processes across multiple systems

  • Design and optimize agent-first development workflows and CI/CD pipelines using GitLab

  • Collaborate closely in a cross-functional team

Requirements

  • 3+ years of professional experience in backend development using Go, NodeJS or TypeScript

  • An open mindset toward AI-assisted software development and hands-on experience with coding agents

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills (English is our working language)

Nice-to-have:

  • Experience with TypeScript and Vite or similar frontend frameworks

  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and Docker-based deployments

Benefits

  • Subsidized Germany Ticket (Deutschlandticket)

  • Wellpass membership

  • Opportunity to shape products in a fast-growing startup

  • High degree of ownership

  • Team events

  • Salary of 50.000 - 70.000€ annually, based on your experience

You'll become part of our cross-functional product team. As a young startup, we keep communication simple, decisions fast, and your ideas truly matter.

Our day-to-day work is pragmatic, with short daily stand-ups and direct communication. We value clean, maintainable code and scalable architecture. While our core team is based in our Hamburg office, we also work remotely across Germany.

You'll be joining an ambitious, collaborative environment where building a great product and making meaningful progress together are at the heart of everything we do.
