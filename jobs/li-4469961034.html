Distinguished Enterprise Architect, Flights Our Technology team is the backbone of our company: constantly creating, testing, learning and iterating to better meet the needs of our customers. If you thrive in a fast-paced, ideas-led environment, you’re in the right place.

Why This Job’s a Big Deal As a Distinguished Enterprise Architect for Flights, you’ll define and evolve the architecture that powers Priceline’s end-to-end flight experience—from search and discovery through shopping, pricing, availability, booking, and post-booking experiences.

This role raises the architectural bar for Priceline’s Flights portfolio—building reliable, scalable, secure, and cost-effective foundations that enable rapid innovation across the flight journey. You’ll connect Product, Technology, Data, AI/ML, and third-party supplier capabilities to create seamless, high-performing flight experiences at scale. Your work will support today’s priorities while creating the architectural foundation for future innovation. You’ll operationalize data, measurement, and experimentation, using A/B testing, feature flags, and other modern capabilities to continuously improve the Flights experience.

In This Role, You Will Get To

  • Define and evolve the Flights target-state architecture aligned to Priceline’s long-term business and technology objectives, with a pragmatic, phased roadmap and clearly defined interim milestones.
  • Design complex, distributed systems supporting flight search, shopping, pricing, availability, booking, and post-booking experiences that are reliable, scalable, secure, and cost-effective—grounded in explicit trade-offs and consistent with Priceline’s infrastructure and operations standards.
  • Partner with Product and Technology leaders to design end-to-end Flights solutions across internal platforms, airline and supplier systems, and third-party technologies—defining integration patterns, API/data contracts, clear ownership boundaries, and SLAs.
  • Champion engineering best practices across key domains, including software design, APIs, DevOps/CI/CD, QA, SRE/observability, cloud, data, AI/ML, security, and experimentation, partnering with domain experts to unlock innovation and operational efficiency.
  • Drive AI/ML-driven capabilities across the flight journey — personalized search/shopping ranking, generative and agentic booking assistance, pricing and demand-forecasting models, and anomaly detection for booking/pricing failures — partnering with Data/AI and Product leaders.
  • Partner with Flights business leaders, Product leaders, and Enterprise Architects to drive well-founded technology choices and ensure scalable, reusable, and platform-aligned architectural patterns across the portfolio.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies, architectural patterns, and travel industry capabilities to shape strategic roadmaps—leading build/buy/partner analyses, proofs of concept, and TCO/ROI assessments to de-risk decisions.
  • Define and evolve architecture supporting flight supplier integrations, content, pricing, availability, and distribution, ensuring solutions can scale as the Flights ecosystem and partner landscape evolve.
  • Enable data-driven product development by establishing architectural patterns for analytics, experimentation, A/B testing, feature flags, and AI/ML capabilities across the Flights experience.
  • Lead architecture governance, codifying standards and guardrails, managing technical debt, ensuring compliance, and minimizing architectural and operational risk.
  • Coach senior and staff engineers, uplevel engineering and architectural practices, and cultivate a culture of clarity, simplicity, scalability, and continuous improvement across the Flights organization.

Who You Are

  • Strategic thinker and clear communicator who can influence executives, peers, and engineers.
  • 12+ years in software engineering and architecture, with significant experience in Growth/MarTech, cloud, microservices, experimentation, data pipelines, AI/ML (including generative AI), and privacy-by-design.
  • Hands-on software engineering background with fluency in API design (gRPC/REST/GraphQL), DevOps/CI/CD, QA, and observability/SRE practices.
  • Proficient in the Node.js ecosystem and Java/Spring ecosystem.
  • Cloud-native experience—ideally GCP (GKE, BigQuery).
  • Familiar with key MarTech platforms and concerns—marketing analytics, headless CMS, CDPs, SEO/SEM, and Core Web Vitals.
  • Bias for clarity and simplicity—reducing complexity and enabling teams to move faster and smarter.
  • Analytical problem-framer who reduces ambiguity into clear, actionable components.
  • Illustrated history of living the values necessary to Priceline: Customer, Innovation, Team, Accountability, and Trust.
  • The Right Results, the Right Way is not just a motto at Priceline; it’s a way of life. Unquestionable integrity and ethics are essential.
