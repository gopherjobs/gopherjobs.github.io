Description

Our Deloitte AI & Engineering team works to transform technology platforms, drive innovation, and help make a significant impact on our clients' success. Work you'll do As a Senior Engineering Management Specialist - DevOps Engineer on the Industry Solutions team, you will be responsible for...

  • Deploy, manage, and optimize AWS cloud infrastructure supporting the client, including self-managed and non-self-managed Red Hat OpenShift clusters
  • Build and maintain continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines using GitLab Runners to support automated build, test, and deployment activities
  • Monitor platform health, performance, and security using observability and security tools, and address issues affecting reliability and availability
  • Manage cloud services and components including AWS Batch, AWS Secrets Manager, Amazon Simple Notification Service (Amazon SNS), Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3), AWS CloudTrail, Amazon Route 53, Amazon CloudFront, Application Load Balancer, AWS Lambda, and AWS Step Functions
  • Collaborate with development, quality engineering, and infrastructure teams to troubleshoot issues, improve workflows, maintain documentation, and support ongoing platform operations and enhancements
