Description

What You Will Do As Fullstack Engineer Embark on our journey We are in an exciting transition to have more focus on our digital product innovation to create scalable best in class customer journeys. Our department Group Tech focuses on developing, innovating, and driving forward digital products right across the Postcode Lottery Group. This includes things like developing websites, mobile apps, and internal software. By collaborating across each of the Postcode Lottery countries, we see benefits of using the same technology and blueprints to build our local products which will mean faster and more efficient development.

This is an exciting opportunity to work at the heart of our transformation, solving complex problems and delivering products that reach millions of players across the Postcode Lottery Group.

We are looking for a Fullstack Engineer for our Sales Flow Platform Team . You'll work on the purchase experience: every step a player takes to buy a lottery ticket or subscription, across every one of our lotteries. The purchase flow is becoming a global engine with local levers. The core steps, payment processing and fraud prevention are shared once across all lotteries, while each market configures its own funnel order, offers, copy and payment methods without forking the engine. The Sales API behind it is serverless on AWS, and we are looking for two engineers with hands-on serverless experience.

What You'll Do

  • Build and run the purchase journey end to end: product selection, recognising new and returning players, checkout, payment and confirmation.
  • Keep the purchase session correct across steps: idempotent submission so nobody buys twice, session recovery and abandonment.
  • Integrate payment providers, such as Apple Pay and Trustly, once, and offer each one to every lottery that needs it.
  • Help make the flow configurable per country and campaign through governed extension points rather than forks, so each market can test and optimise its own flow.
  • Work across the serverless Sales API (Node.js Lambda functions with API Gateway, EventBridge, Step Functions, DynamoDB and AWS CDK) and the purchase flow's web service on our shared Next.js framework.
  • Own the operational health of what you run: observability, reliability, deployment and on call.
  • Hands-on with continuous delivery and continuous deployment, working in a team where automated pipelines let you see your code go live, often several times a day.

About You You'd describe yourself as follows:

  • Hands-on experience building and running serverless systems, ideally on AWS.
  • Write fluent TypeScript and use the type system as a design tool, on client and server.
  • Work in modern React and Next.js, and understand rendering strategies and the server and client boundary.
  • Build Node.js services with sound HTTP semantics, API design and error handling, and can reason about a request end to end.
  • Read and write infrastructure as code rather than clicking in a console.
  • You build it and you run it: you instrument before you ship, write the runbook, investigate incidents and take part in on call.
  • You test at the level that fits the risk, including contract testing where teams depend on each other.
