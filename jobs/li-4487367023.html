Role: At ClearRoute, the Technical Delivery Manager is the operational backbone of an engagement: running governance, managing risk, coordinating delivery teams, and keeping clients and ClearRoute aligned. Unlike a pure project manager, we are looking for a Technical Delivery Manager who is engineering-literate and leads delivery in an engineering-first way, holding the team to strong Agile and engineering-excellence practices.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Establish and run the Delivery Playbook governance structure and cadence (kickoff, planning, execution, closure)
  • Facilitate communication between client and ClearRoute; maintain a clear view of who is working on what and who the stakeholders are Capture delivery data, RAG status, risks and insights for governance reporting (bi-weekly and monthly delivery reviews)
  • Manage engagement risk to protect ClearRoute's interests; identify and decouple dependencies
  • Coordinate workstreams, coach the team, ensure quality of deliverables and adherence to Agile practices
  • Run the Delivery Health Check Toolkit regularly to gauge team health/performance
  • Own engagement closure: sign-off of deliverables, engagement review, case study and IP capture

Required Experience:

  • Proven track record managing software delivery engagements end-to-end (on time, on scope, on budget), ideally in a consultancy or client-facing services environment.
  • Strong working knowledge of Agile delivery methodologies (Scrum/Kanban) and modern engineering practices (CI/CD, DORA metrics, quality engineering), with enough technical literacy to challenge and guide engineering teams.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, including the ability to build trust with senior client stakeholders and economic buyers.
  • Demonstrable risk and dependency management capability, with sound commercial judgement to protect delivery and margin.
  • Experience running delivery governance and reporting — status, RAG, risk logs — and presenting to client and internal leadership.
  • Ability to coach and coordinate multidisciplinary teams, and to operate pragmatically by adapting process to context rather than applying it rigidly.

Desirable Experience:

  • Background as a hands-on software engineer or technical lead before moving into delivery management.
  • Experience across multiple engagement models (Time & Materials, Capability/squad-based, and Outcome/OKR-based).
  • Familiarity with cloud, DevOps, and test-automation domains
  • Experience setting up engagements from scratch — onboarding teams onto client systems, co-creating backlogs, and establishing delivery cadence.
