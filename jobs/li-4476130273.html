Job Summary We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design, build, and own production backend services as part of a major platform modernization initiative. The team is moving core backend capabilities away from a third-party CMS platform and into independently owned services, starting with authentication and identity.

In this role, you will build scalable backend services that support a new headless frontend and other digital products, define APIs, lead service migrations, and ensure strong reliability and observability in production. The ideal candidate has strong Java and Go experience, hands-on experience with AWS and GCP, deep knowledge of authentication and distributed systems, and the autonomy to make sound technical decisions in complex environments. The team also operates with an AI-first development approach, leveraging AI coding assistants and agentic workflows to improve engineering productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and own production-grade backend services that replace capabilities currently provided by third-party CMS platforms.
  • Lead the design and implementation of a new authentication and identity service, including secure session and token management.
  • Define and implement APIs that support the headless frontend and other digital products.
  • Design solutions using REST, GraphQL, and/or gRPC and distributed systems principles.
  • Plan and execute service migrations from vendor or legacy platforms with minimal disruption to existing users and systems.
  • Build and operate cloud-native services across AWS and Google Cloud.
  • Ensure backend services meet requirements for reliability, scalability, performance, and observability.
  • Implement and maintain effective logging, metrics, and distributed tracing for production services.
  • Collaborate closely with the Platform Engineering Manager and other engineering stakeholders on technical decisions and implementation strategy.
  • Mentor a mid-level engineer and contribute to engineering standards and best practices.
  • Leverage and help shape AI-assisted and agentic development workflows across the team's engineering process.
  • Continuously evaluate and improve backend architecture, service reliability, and development practices.

Qualifications And Job Requirements

  • 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with senior-level ownership of production services.
  • Strong professional experience with Java and Go, including production experience with both.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS and Google Cloud, including managed services such as Cloud Run, GKE, Pub/Sub, Lambda, RDS, or equivalent technologies.
  • Strong understanding of API design and distributed systems, with experience using REST, GraphQL, and/or gRPC.
  • Strong experience with authentication and identity systems, including OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SSO, session management, and token management.
  • Experience migrating services or capabilities away from a third-party vendor, CMS, or legacy platform.
  • Strong understanding of production observability and reliability, including logging, metrics, and tracing.
  • Ability to design and deliver scalable, maintainable backend services with a high degree of autonomy.
