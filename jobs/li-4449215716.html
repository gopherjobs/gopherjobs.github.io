About The Team We build the tooling and platform capabilities that power the next generation of AI agents on the Cloudflare Developer Platform. Our goal is to make it easier for agents to interact with the web, and to be observed and controlled while they do it — without giving up performance, scalability or security.

Browser Run is the centre of that work, and it is the product this role owns. It runs full browser sessions on Cloudflare’s global network and lets developers drive them with code or with an agent: a Chrome DevTools Protocol endpoint that any Puppeteer, Playwright or CDP client can connect to from anywhere, Workers bindings, and a REST API for the common jobs — screenshots, PDFs, Markdown extraction, scraping and crawling. On top of that sit session recording and replay, live debugging, and a path for a human to step in when an agent gets stuck.

It is used by external developers building agents, crawlers and scrapers, and by other Cloudflare teams who need a browser they can trust. It is also a billed product on a steep growth curve, which makes capacity, cold-start latency, concurrency limits and abuse protection product problems rather than merely operational ones.

The team’s wider portfolio includes an agent-first browser built on Workers rather than containers, a managed CI platform for the agent development lifecycle, visual regression testing delivered as a service, a pre-rendering service, URL Scanner, and CDNJS. Browser Run is the focus of this role today; there is scope for more of that portfolio to come into it as the role and the team grow.

Browser Run went from an internal rendering tool to a public, billed product, and the surface has not stopped moving — the CDP endpoint, MCP client support, session replay and the REST API all landed inside a year. That pace is the point, and it is the thing this role has to make sustainable.

We are a tight-knit team that communicates and collaborates regularly. We celebrate our achievements, support each other when we make mistakes, and hold each other and our work to the highest standard.

About The Role This is a hands-on engineering management role. You will own Browser Run engineering end to end — the people, the roadmap, the reliability, and the story we tell customers about it. You will report to the Senior Engineering Manager for AI Agents Tooling & Platform and partner closely with Product Management, Developer Relations, and platform teams across Cloudflare.

Browser Run is the primary focus. The team owns other products, and there is scope for some of them to move into your remit as the role and the team grow, but we are hiring someone to make Browser Run excellent first.

You will not be writing most of the code. You will be expected to understand it well enough to review a design document, challenge an architecture, and know when an estimate is wrong.

What you’ll do

  • Manage, coach and grow the team of systems engineers behind Browser Run — 1:1s, career development, performance conversations, and hiring.
  • Own the Browser Run roadmap and delivery, and give the team and the wider organisation clear visibility into what is being worked on, in what order, and what we are deliberately not doing.
  • Partner with Product Management to set direction: shape the roadmap, pressure-test scope, and make the trade-off calls that keep an incubation product shipping.
  • Own Browser Run’s operational health — browser fleet capacity, session reliability, cold-start latency, concurrency limits, on-call rotations, alert quality and incident follow-through. External developers and other Cloudflare teams depend on this service.
  • Keep the automation surface honest: Puppeteer and Playwright compatibility, CDP behaviour that matches what developers expect from a local Chrome, and a credible story for Chrome version upgrades.
  • Develop leaders. Identify and grow the engineers who will run day-to-day delivery for an area of the product, and hand that work to them.
  • Resolve cross-team dependencies with the Workers, Developer Platform, Security and Billing teams, and bring contentious technical decisions to closure quickly and calmly.
  • Own the commercial mechanics of a billed service, including the unglamorous parts: usage metering, quotas, plan limits, abuse protection and pricing input.
  • Represent Browser Run externally through launch blog posts, developer documentation, conference talks and direct customer conversations.
